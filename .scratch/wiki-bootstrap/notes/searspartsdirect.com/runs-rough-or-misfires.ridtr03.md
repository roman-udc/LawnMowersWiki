## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "riding mower runs rough or misfires"
- alias: "engine runs rough after hitting a rock or stump"
- alias: "engine stalls when I try to drive the mower"
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, sheared-flywheel-key, fouled-spark-plug, old-fuel, low-compression
- fix: tune up the engine each season to keep it running smoothly.
- fact: poor-running engine may stall when driving; uneven cuts if the engine doesn't run smoothly while mowing.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: carb mixes gas with air before the cylinder; unbalanced mix means no start, or rough running and stalling when you drive; damaged carb may leak gas; impurities in gasoline clog it; seals and gaskets wear out and leak gas.
- fix: rebuild (carburetor rebuild kit for a clogged or leaking carb) or replace.

## page: procedures/replace-the-carburetor
- fact: replace if symptoms above (won't start, runs rough/stalls, leaks gas); pointer to "How to replace a riding lawn mower carburetor".

## page: procedures/rebuild-the-carburetor
- fact: use a carburetor rebuild kit to refurbish a clogged or leaking carburetor; pointer to "How to rebuild a riding lawn mower carburetor".

## page: causes/sheared-flywheel-key
- fact: small metal rectangle keeping crankshaft and flywheel aligned when the flywheel nut is tightened; designed to shear if the blade hits an object like a rock or stump hard enough to slip the flywheel out of alignment, protecting costlier parts.
- symptom: damaged key disrupts ignition timing: slightly sheared: engine runs rough or misfires; badly broken: engine stops and won't start.
- fix: replace the key.

## page: procedures/replace-the-flywheel-key
- fact: pointer to "How to replace the flywheel key on a riding lawn mower"; replace if broken or sheared.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- confirm: check for fuel, oil or carbon deposits on the plug; replace if fouled.
- fact: won't spark if coated with carbon or oil residue or cracked ceramic insulator; a 2-cylinder engine runs rough when one plug goes bad.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- fact: replace if damaged or coated with residue; pointer to its guide.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: bad fuel causes a rough-running engine; drain the tank and refill with fresh gasoline if fuel is over 6 months old; use fuel stabilizer to keep gas fresh.

## page: causes/low-compression
- fact: low cylinder compression causes rough running; if found, check and adjust valve lash.

## page: procedures/check-compression
- fact: Source only says check cylinder compression, and adjust valve lash if low (no numbers).

## page: procedures/tune-up
- fact: tune-up is routine maintenance: replace engine oil, oil filter, air filter, fuel filter and spark plug; check the ignition, carburetor, battery, throttle and choke controls; clean the engine cooling fins. Engine performance suffers without regular tune-ups.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: engine fuel filter screens impurities going to the carb; clogged filter blocks gas flow so engine won't start; a damaged filter lets impurities clog the carburetor. Replace if damaged or clogged.
