## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "mower vibrates when I engage the blades"; "riding mower deck vibrates"
- causes-named: bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Vibration on blade engagement suggests a problem in the blade drive system: unbalanced blade, worn pulley or mandrel, bad blade belt, or debris caught inside a pulley. Loose nuts or bolts on pulleys, mandrels or blades can also cause it.
- fix: Remove the mower deck and re-tighten all fasteners; check pulleys, mandrels and blades for wear; replace worn or damaged components.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fact: "Mandrel assembly" (spindle) attaches to the deck and spins the blade. A damaged mandrel causes deck vibration and uneven cutting; a blade won't move if the mandrel locks up. Examine regularly; replace damaged ones.
- fact: Repair guides referenced: how to replace a mandrel assembly on a riding lawn mower.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: A bent blade won't cut evenly and may cause deck vibration. Dull blades leave ragged edges. Inspect regularly; replace if damaged, bent or dull.
- fact: Repair guides referenced: replace a riding lawn mower blade; replace a cutting blade on a zero-turn riding mower.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: Blade belt spins the blades; worn or broken belt won't spin them (no cutting); worn belt can also cause deck vibration or uneven cuts. Examine regularly; replace if worn or broken.
- fact: Repair guides referenced: replace the blade belt on a zero-turn riding mower; replace a riding lawn mower blade drive belt.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Deck idler pulley tightens the blade belt against the blade pulleys to spin the blades when the blade engagement lever is shifted to engage. Replace if blades don't spin; a worn idler pulley can also cause uneven cuts and deck vibration. Replace if worn or broken.
