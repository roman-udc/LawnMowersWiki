## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- fact: Fuel filter keeps dirt out of the carburetor. Clogged/dirty filter lets too little fuel reach carburetor: engine runs rough or stops. Replace with a manufacturer-approved part when tuning up the tractor each year.
- brand: Procedure works for common Briggs & Stratton engines powering Craftsman, MTD, Husqvarna, Murray, Toro, Troybilt, Snapper, Yard-Man, Ariens, Poulan and John Deere riding mowers and lawn tractors.
- difficulty: 2 (Source scale); time: 15 minutes or less
- tools: 2 hose pinch-off pliers, pliers, shop rag, work gloves
- safety: Level surface, well-ventilated area, parking brake set, ignition off and key removed, engine cool, work gloves. Source warning: repairs can be hazardous; proceed only if confident; some repairs for qualified technicians.
- step 1: Disconnect the spark plug wire (lift hood). Tip: disconnect both wires if the engine has 2 cylinders.
- step 2: Remove the lower dash fastener and pull off the lower dash. Clamp hose pinch-off pliers on the fuel line on each side of the filter. Put shop rag under filter. Release hose clamps and remove hoses from both sides. Wipe up spilled fuel.
- step 3: Connect hoses to new filter with the hose clamps; release the pinch-off pliers; reinstall lower dash with fastener.
- step 4: Reconnect spark plug wire(s) (both if 2 cylinders); lower hood.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- symptom: Engine runs rough or stops running completely (problems/engine-runs-rough).
- fix: Replace; do so annually at tune-up.

## page: procedures/tune-up
- fact: Replace the fuel filter each year when tuning up the lawn tractor.
