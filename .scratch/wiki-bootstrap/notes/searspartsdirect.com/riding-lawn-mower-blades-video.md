## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- tip: Keep blades clean and sharp, and replace often.
- tip: Universal blade removal tool holds blade in place while removing/replacing the blade bolt; easier and safer than a block of wood or your hand.
- tip: Keep deck clean and use a deck lubricant such as Mo-Deck spray for best mulching and discharge.
- fact: Check owner's manual for compatible blades; not all blade types fit every mower.

- suggested slug guides/blade-types (borderline product info, taxonomy hinted mower-parts-that-wear-out): 
- fact: High-lift blade (also called bagging or 2-in-1): curved tips lift clippings and help blow them out the side discharge chute or into the bag.
- fact: Mulching blade: contoured shape swirls clippings in deck; long cutting edges recut clippings into mulch. Must cover the discharge port with a mulching plug (mulching kit includes blades and plug); replace blades when worn.
- fact: 3-in-1 blade: combines high-lift and mulching; use for side discharge, bagging or mulching; use optional mulching plug on discharge port for mulching.
- fact: Ninja blade: 6 cutting surfaces, finely cuts clippings to mulch, helps lift when bagging; only for compatible Snapper Ninja mowers.
- fact: Slotted blade (aftermarket 3-in-1 and mulching): slots on the upward-facing side control airflow so clippings are recut more, finer mulch.
- fact: Sand blade: low-lift fins move grass and sand out the side discharge chute; helps prevent deck wear on sandy ground.
- tip: Check owner's manual before choosing specialty blades.
