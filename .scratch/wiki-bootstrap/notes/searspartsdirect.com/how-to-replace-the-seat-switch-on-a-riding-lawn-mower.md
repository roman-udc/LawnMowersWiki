## page: procedures/replace-the-seat-switch
- fact: Applies to most riding mowers; Source names Craftsman, MTD, Troybilt, Husqvarna, Murray, Snapper, Cub Cadet and Ariens lawn tractors and riding mowers.
- fact: The seat switch is a safety device that shuts off the engine if it does not detect someone in the seat when the tractor starts moving. When broken it will not sense the operator and will stop the engine when you release the brake.
- tip: Replace a broken switch with a manufacturer-approved part. If unsure the switch is the problem, Source links a troubleshooting video for testing it.
- difficulty: 1 (Source scale; Difficulty: 1)
- time: 15 minutes or less
- tools: wrench set, slot screwdriver, work gloves
- safety: Source warning: repairs can be hazardous; use proper tools and safety equipment; do not proceed unless confident; some repairs should be done by a qualified technician.
- step 1: Disconnect the battery: park on a level surface, set the parking brake, turn the ignition off and remove the key, wear work gloves, lift the seat, remove the bolt connecting the negative cable to the battery and tuck the cable away so it does not touch the battery post.
- step 2: Remove the seat: remove the retaining bolt and washer from the seat bottom; press the seat adjustment lever and slide the seat fully forward; release the locking tab and pull the seat off the bracket while supporting the seat adjustment lever; remove the seat adjustment lever.
- step 3: Remove the old switch: disconnect the wire harness from the switch; rotate the switch counterclockwise to release it and pull it off the seat.
- step 4: Install the new switch: push it into the seat bottom and rotate clockwise to lock; connect the wire harness.
- step 5: Reinstall the seat: reinstall the adjustment lever and support it while snapping the seat back into the bracket; reinstall the retaining bolt and washer.
- step 6: Reconnect the negative cable to the negative battery terminal; lower the seat.

- symptom: problems/engine-starts-then-dies (engine stops when the brake is released)
