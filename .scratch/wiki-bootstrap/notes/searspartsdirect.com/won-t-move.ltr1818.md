## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "riding mower won't drive forward"
- alias: "slows down going uphill"
- alias: "engine dies when I release the brake"
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, safety-key-missing, bad-safety-switch, dirty-carburetor, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- symptom: won't drive forward or slows uphill - check drive belt; slipped belt - reinstall; damaged or broken - replace
- symptom: engine dies when brake released - seat switch may be unplugged or broken
- fact: check the transaxle freewheel control; if in freewheel position, shift to engage the transaxle
- fact: purge air from the transaxle before first use and after any transaxle repair
- fact: engine sputters then stalls when engaging drive - tune up the engine; rebuild or replace carburetor if clogged and not feeding the engine
- fix: replace the ground drive belt if worn or broken

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fix: replace the ground drive belt if worn or broken; reinstall if slipped out of place
- fact: repair guides referenced for zero-turn and riding mower ground drive belt replacement (not in this source)

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: seat switch kills the engine if it doesn't detect you in the seat when you release the brake; stops the engine if its wire harness is unplugged (anti-tamper)
- confirm: when engine dies on brake release, check that the seat switch wire harness is plugged in
- fix: replace the seat switch if it's plugged in but doesn't recognize you in the seat
- fact: worn or broken seat may not engage seat switch - engine shuts off on brake release; replace seat if damaged or worn

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: carburetor mixes gas and air; unbalanced mix means engine won't start or runs rough and stalls when driving; damaged carburetor may leak gas; impurities in gasoline clog it; seals and gaskets wear out and leak gas
- fix: use a carburetor rebuild kit to refurbish a clogged or leaking carburetor; or replace

## page: procedures/tune-up
- fact: tune-up is routine maintenance; poor-running engine may stall when driving; uneven cuts if the engine doesn't run smoothly
- step 1: replace engine oil, oil filter, air filter, fuel filter and spark plug
- step 2: check the ignition, carburetor, battery, throttle and choke controls
- step 3: clean the engine cooling fins
