## page: guides/winterizing-and-storage
- fact: needed: engine tune-up kit; lubricants (multi-purpose grease, mower deck spray); replacement belts and blades if needed; brushes, scraper, soapy water, rags.
- step 1 (wash under deck, no washout port): park near faucet, stop engine, clean under deck with hose and spray attachment.
- step 2 (washout port): park on clear level lawn near faucet with discharge chute pointed away from people, pets, house, vehicles; parking brake, engine off; remove bagger chute or mulch cover; connect hose to washout port, water on; start engine, throttle fastest, engage blades for several minutes; blades off, engine off; water off, disconnect hose; start tractor, move to dry area on concrete or asphalt, engage blades several minutes to dry underside.
- step 3 (deck for storage): park near storage, let engine cool. Many owner's manuals recommend removing deck and storing disconnected; follow manual. Disconnect fasteners, pull deck out; scrape and wipe underside; replace damaged or dull blades now; spray underside with Mo-Deck spray; remove mandrel pulley covers and clean pulleys; if grease fittings on mandrel housings, fill with grease gun until resistance (full), wipe residual; examine deck belt and replace if necessary; wash top of deck and pulley covers with soapy rag, wipe with clear water, dry; touch up nicks by light sanding and touch-up paint; reinstall pulley covers once completely dry.
- step 4 (engine fuel): two options: run engine until all gasoline in tank and fuel system burned and it dies; or add fuel stabilizer and run engine 10 minutes so stabilized fuel reaches carburetor. Change engine oil so fresh oil in spring.
- step 5: lube pistons: remove spark plugs, pour 1 ounce of oil into each spark plug hole, run engine 5 seconds to distribute (coats piston and cylinder against corrosion); gap and install new spark plugs.
- step 6: filters: replace fuel filter per owner's manual if equipped; clamp fuel lines to prevent spills if engine wasn't run dry. Air filter: remove cover, pull pre-filter and paper filter, wipe base with damp cloth without letting dirt into carburetor intake port; replace pre-filter if holes/damage, else wash in soapy water, rinse, pat dry with paper towels; tap paper filter on ground; replace if too dirty or damaged; reinstall.
- step 7 (battery): disconnect black cable first, then red; remove battery, wipe with clean wet rag, dry thoroughly; wire brush terminals and cable ends until metal bright; coat terminals with grease or petroleum jelly; place battery back but do NOT reconnect cables (connected battery in storage causes corrosion and power leakage); position cables so they don't touch terminals; fully charge battery if you have a charger; may still need charging in spring.
- step 8 (tractor): blow clippings/debris off with leaf blower or compressed air; wipe with damp cloth; sand scratches lightly with fine grit and apply touch-up paint; clean debris from under tractor, especially the steering plate below brake pedal (debris restricts brake pedal movement); check ground drive belt and replace if worn/damaged (manual shows routing over pulleys and belt keepers); check tire air pressure and fill; replace damaged or tread-bare tires.
- step 9 (store): push to dry spot indoors preferably; don't store indoors with gasoline in tank if fumes may reach open flame or spark. Outside: move deck under tractor and cover both with protective lawn tractor cover that doesn't retain moisture; don't use plastic cover (doesn't breathe, condensation, rust).
- tip: if too much work, oil change and spark plug replacement can be put off until spring.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- fact: clean deck underside before storage to prevent off-season rust; washout port procedure above.

## page: guides/battery-care-and-charging
- fact: store battery with cables disconnected, clean terminals and coat with grease/petroleum jelly; fully charge before storage.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: avoid gummy deposits: either run engine dry or add stabilizer and run 10 minutes to reach carburetor.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- fact: winter prep: 1 oz oil in each cylinder, run 5 seconds, install gapped new plugs.

## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- fact: change oil before storage so fresh oil in spring.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: pre-filter replace if holes, else wash in soapy water, rinse, pat dry; paper filter tapped on ground, replace if too dirty/damaged.
