## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-oil
- alias: "riding mower leaking oil"
- causes-named: overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket
- confirm: check oil level when the engine leaks oil.
- fix: if overfilled, drain oil to the full mark on the dipstick. A leaking head gasket or sump gasket can also leak oil; replace damaged engine gaskets.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "gasoline stains on pavement or garage floor"
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, bad-fuel-pump
- confirm: if you see gasoline stains on pavement or garage floor, investigate the source of the leak.
- fix: rebuild or replace the carburetor if it leaks gas; check the fuel pump for leaks and replace if damaged.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fix: drain oil to the full mark on the dipstick.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- symptom: oil leak plus engine can't reach normal operating speed or mower struggles to climb a hill.
- fact: the head gasket seals the cylinder head to the top of the engine; air leaking past a damaged gasket reduces cylinder compression, causing power loss; a blown gasket leaks oil because it doesn't seal the head mounting surfaces.
- fix: replace the head gasket if it leaks.

## page: causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat
- fact: seals and gaskets in the carburetor eventually wear out and can leak gas; use a carburetor rebuild kit to refurbish a clogged or leaking carburetor.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: impurities in gasoline can clog the carburetor, preventing starting, causing rough running and stalling when driving.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: moves fuel from the tank to the engine; if the engine won't start or quits after starting, the pump might not be working. A cracked pump leaks gasoline.
- fix: replace if it leaks or doesn't pump fuel to the carburetor.

## page: procedures/replace-the-carburetor
- fact: carburetor mixes gas with air before the cylinder; imbalance means no start or rough running and stalling when driving; a damaged carb may leak; replace if it causes these symptoms.
- fact: Source links repair guide "How to replace a riding lawn mower carburetor".

## page: procedures/rebuild-the-carburetor
- fact: use a carburetor rebuild kit for a clogged or leaking carb. Repair guide link: "How to rebuild a riding lawn mower carburetor".

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- fact: drain to the dipstick full mark.
