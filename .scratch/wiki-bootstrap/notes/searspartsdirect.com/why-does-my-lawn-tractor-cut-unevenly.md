## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "lawn tractor cuts unevenly"
- alias: "grass uneven or not completely cut"
- causes-named: unlevel-deck, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, wrong-tire-pressure, incorrect-path-overlap
- tip: Level the deck (essential for smooth even cut).
- tip: Use sharp, straight blades; inspect before every use for sharpness, straightness and chips. Replace blades at the start of each cutting season or whenever damaged.
- tip: Clean the deck: clippings buildup under the deck interferes with airflow and clipping movement, hindering blade lift.
- tip: Slow down: the taller and thicker the grass, the slower you should go so blades can lift and cut.
- tip: Mow at full throttle: ground speed slower but engine at full throttle for the right blade speed.
- tip: Check tire air pressure: over- or under-inflated tires make the tractor travel unevenly; see owner's manual (look up by model number).
- tip: Overlap passes: the blade extends only to the deck edge; overlapping by just an inch ensures the blade reaches every grass blade.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: Leveling is essential for an even cut; links to a Sears PartsDirect video on adjusting/leveling the deck.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Inspect before every use; replace at start of each season or any time damage found.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Clippings buildup hinders lift; links to tips for preventing clippings sticking.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Over- or underinflation causes uneven travel; consult the owner's manual for correct pressure.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Full throttle for right blade speed.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Slow for taller, thicker grass.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Overlap by about an inch.
