## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- tools: blade removal tool: a clamp that holds the blade in place while loosening/tightening the blade bolt on walk-behind or riding mowers. A block of wood between deck and blade can work in a pinch but can slip and let the blade move.
- brand/part: Sears PartsDirect/lawn mower blade removal tool 490-850-0005
- step 1: follow the owner's manual to prepare. Walk-behind: drain the gas tank, disconnect the spark plug wire, tilt the mower. Riding: disconnect the spark plug wire and remove the mower deck.
- step 2: place the blade removal tool over the blade and the side of the mower deck.
- step 3: tighten the clamping screw firmly onto the deck so the tool doesn't move.
- step 4: remove the blade bolt and pull off the blade.
- step 5: install the new blade and tighten the bolt. Some manufacturers recommend 40-50 ft.-lbs. blade bolt torque; check the owner's manual; if none is specified, tighten firmly.
- step 6: loosen the clamping screw and remove the tool.
- step 7: reassemble per owner's manual.
- safety: tool makes removal safer and easier.
