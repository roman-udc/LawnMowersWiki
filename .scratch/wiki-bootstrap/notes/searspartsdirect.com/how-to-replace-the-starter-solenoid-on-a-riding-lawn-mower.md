## page: procedures/replace-the-starter-solenoid
- fact: When the ignition key is turned to start, the starter solenoid sends current to the starter. If you don't hear the internal solenoid contact click when you turn the key, replace the solenoid because it isn't working.
- brand: Procedure works for common Craftsman, MTD, Troybilt, Toro, Husqvarna, Murray, Cub Cadet and Ariens riding mowers and lawn tractors. Use a manufacturer-approved part.
- difficulty: 3 (Source scale); time: 30 minutes or less.
- tools: wrench set; work gloves.
- safety: Source warning: repair or maintenance of power equipment can be hazardous; use proper tools and safety equipment; don't proceed unless confident you understand all steps; some repairs should only be done by a qualified technician. Wear work gloves.
- step 1: Remove the battery: ignition off and key out; lift the seat; remove the bolt connecting the negative cable to the battery; lift the insulating cover and remove the bolt connecting the positive cable; lift the battery out of the battery box.
- step 2: Remove the battery box: release the seat switch wire harness clip from the seat bracket to give slack; release retaining tabs and lift the box out of the mower body; set it on the fender.
- step 3: Remove the solenoid: note where wires connect; remove mounting nuts from terminal posts and pull wires off the posts; pull wires from the coil spades; remove the solenoid mounting bolt from the frame; rotate the solenoid forward and pull it out.
- tip: Take a photo of the connections to reconnect correctly.
- step 4: Install the new solenoid: transfer the mounting clip from old to new; position it on the frame and install the mounting bolt; connect wires to the coil spades; install wires on terminal posts with the mounting nuts.
- step 5: Reinstall the battery box: position the box in the opening, align wire harness and battery cables; lower it into the mower body and engage retaining tabs; reconnect the seat switch wire harness clip to the seat bracket.
- step 6: Reinstall the battery: lower it into the box; with the insulating cover on the cable, reconnect the positive cable to the positive terminal; reconnect the negative cable to the negative terminal; lower the seat.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: no click from the solenoid contact when the key is turned to start.
- fix: Replace it (see procedure).

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- confirm: Listen for the internal solenoid contact click when the key turns to start; no click means replace.
