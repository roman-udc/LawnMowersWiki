## page: procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly
- fact: The mandrel assembly (the spindle) attaches to the deck and spins the blade. This guide's mandrel assembly includes the blade shaft and bearings; other mandrel assemblies include different parts, so check the parts diagram for your model.
- fact: Covers common Craftsman, MTD, Troybilt, Husqvarna, Ariens, Cub Cadet and Snapper riding mowers and lawn tractors.
- symptom: Replace with a manufacturer-approved part if the blade shaft vibrates or doesn't spin freely inside the mandrel. Bearings wear with normal use; an unbalanced blade speeds bearing wear, as does hitting rocks.
- difficulty: 3 (Source scale, no max stated)
- time: 30 minutes or less
- tools: wrench set, pliers, wood block, torque wrench (optional), work gloves
- safety: Source warning: repairs on power equipment can be hazardous; use proper tools and safety equipment; don't proceed unless confident; some repairs should be done only by a qualified technician. Warning: the spring-loaded attachment lift lever exerts more tension after the rear lift links are disconnected, so don't use the lever until the deck is reinstalled.
- step 1: Disconnect the spark plug. Park on a level surface, set the parking brake, ignition off, remove the key, wear work gloves. Lift the hood and disconnect the spark plug wire (both wires if the tractor has 2 cylinders).
- step 2: Remove the mower deck. Lower the deck to its lowest position; disengage the blades. Roll the blade belt off the engine pulley and out of the belt keepers. Remove the front lift link support retaining pin and washer and detach the support from the deck. Remove the left suspension arm retaining clip and washer, detach the arm from the frame. Remove the left rear deck bracket retaining clip and washer, release the bracket from the deck. Repeat on the right for the suspension arm and rear deck bracket. Pull the deck out far enough to reach the blade cable; release the locking tabs and pull the cable out of the deck bracket; disconnect the blade cable spring from the idler arm and move the cable aside. Pull the deck out from under the frame.
- step 3: Remove the mandrel assembly. Wood block between blade and deck to hold the blade. Remove the pulley mounting nut and detach the pulley. Pull the block, flip the deck over, replace the block. Remove the blade mounting bolt and pull the blade. Remove the block, flip the deck upright. Remove the mandrel mounting bolts and pull the mandrel out from under the deck.
- step 4: Install the new mandrel. Thread the mounting bolts into the new mandrel's holes to start the threads, then remove them. Position the new mandrel in the deck opening and install the mounting bolts, tighten firmly. Wood block between blade shaft/pulley and deck; reinstall the pulley with the blade belt attached properly; tighten the nut firmly. Flip the deck over; with the correct side of the blade facing up, attach the blade to the new shaft and hand tighten the bolt; block in place; tighten firmly; flip upright.
- step 5: Reinstall the deck. Push the deck near the frame; connect the blade clutch cable spring to the idler arm; push the cable retainer into the deck bracket and engage locking tabs; position the deck under the frame; reconnect front lift link support (washer and clip); reconnect left suspension arm and deck bracket (washers and clips); repeat on the right; route the blade belt through the belt keepers and onto the engine pulley; check routing over all pulleys and keepers and adjust if needed.
- step 6: Reconnect the spark plug wire(s) (both if 2 cylinders) and lower the hood.
- tip: Use new mounting bolts. The lower side of the blade is clearly labeled for easy installation. Refer to the owner's manual for blade belt routing.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: Blade shaft vibrates or doesn't spin freely inside the mandrel; bearings wear from normal use, accelerated by an unbalanced blade or hitting rocks.
- fix: See procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Blade has a labeled lower side; attach with the correct side facing up (i.e., label identifies correct orientation).

## page: causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing
- fact: Blade belt must be routed through all belt keepers and over all pulleys on reinstall.
