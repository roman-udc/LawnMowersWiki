## page: procedures/jump-start-the-battery
- tools: jumper cables, battery, safety glasses, work gloves.
- fact: Ideally use a battery charger; repeatedly jumping can eventually damage the charging system. OK to jump occasionally only if the mower has a 12-volt battery. 6-volt battery: must use a charger. Check the owner's manual for voltage.
- safety: Batteries contain acid and generate explosive gases: wear eye protection and work gloves; don't lean directly over a battery when connecting cables; keep sparks and flames clear; no smoking near a battery.
- safety: Jump with an automobile, not another riding mower (can damage the other mower's electrical systems).
- safety: Never jump from a running engine; shut the vehicle's engine off.
- step 1: Park the mower on a level surface, set the parking brake, follow the owner's manual to prepare for starting (on many mowers shut off the blades before starting the engine).
- step 2: Park the vehicle next to the mower and shut off its engine. Open the vehicle hood and lift the mower seat to access both batteries.
- step 3: Connect one end of the red cable to the positive terminal of the mower battery.
- step 4: Connect the other end of the red cable to the vehicle's positive terminal. Don't let the free end of the red cable touch any metal (sparks, could damage the good battery).
- step 5: Connect the black cable to the vehicle's negative terminal.
- step 6: Connect the other end of the black cable to the metal mower frame, away from the fuel tank and battery.
- step 7: Turn the key and start the mower.
- step 8 (disconnect, reverse order): Black cable off the mower frame first, then black off the vehicle battery, then red off the vehicle battery, then red off the mower battery.
- step 9: Fully charge the mower battery as soon as possible with a charger. If it won't charge, replace it.
- difficulty: Video guide (Sears PartsDirect); basic.
