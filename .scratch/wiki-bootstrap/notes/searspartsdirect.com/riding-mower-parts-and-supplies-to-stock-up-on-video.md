## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact: Six items to keep on hand: blades (wear fast with frequent mowing; rocks/foreign objects damage them); mandrels (damaged by rocks, stumps, foreign objects; keep an extra set); belts (blade and ground drive belts wear quickly in season); mower-deck spray (nonstick treatment bonding to metal and plastic, keeps clippings from sticking under deck); fuel stabilizer (keeps gasoline fresh, avoids starting problems); tire sealant (fixes flat tires, seals punctures up to 1/4-inch diameter).
- fact: Nice-to-have, especially with a big lawn: air filters, spark plugs, engine oil, oil filter, blade removal tool, flywheel keys.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Stocking parts avoids downtime spring through fall.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Fuel stabilizer keeps gasoline fresh to avoid engine-starting problems.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
