## page: procedures/repair-a-flat-tire
- fact: Small object embedded in tread can often be repaired with tire sealant.
- tools: pliers, work gloves, safety glasses
- parts: tire sealant (front tire / rear tire if replacement needed)
- step 1: Find the leak. If a nail, screw or other object is stuck in the tire, do NOT pull it out before adding sealant (sealant may not seal the hole). If leak location unknown: put air in tire and spray soapy water; leaking air makes bubbles.
- step 2: Confirm leak is in the tread area (sealant can fix tread holes). Sealant cannot fix sidewall holes; replace the tire.
- step 3: Wear work gloves and eye protection. Check the chart on the sealant bottle for amount by tire size (example in video: 16 ounces).
- step 4: Rotate wheel so valve stem is at the 5 o'clock position.
- step 5: Remove cap and valve stem core.
- step 6: Open sealant bottle, connect the supplied hose to the bottle tip, connect other end to valve stem.
- step 7: Squeeze bottle to inject the right amount.
- step 8: Disconnect hose, wipe residue, reinstall valve stem core.
- step 9: Fill tire to recommended pressure, reinstall valve cap.
- step 10: Now pull out the stuck object, if any.
- step 11: Drive the mower 3 to 5 minutes to distribute sealant evenly inside the tire.
- fix: If sealant doesn't fix leak or hole is in sidewall, replace tire (front or rear tire replacement videos). On some tires an inner tube can be installed instead of replacing the tire.
- difficulty: easy (video DIY; no rating stated)

- fix: Tread holes: sealant; sidewall: replace tire.
