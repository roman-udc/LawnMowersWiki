## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- fact: Charge at the start of the mowing season, before winter storage (fully charged), or if unexpectedly dead (e.g. ignition left on).
- tools: battery charger, safety glasses, work gloves
- fact: Most riding mowers use 12 V batteries; some models built before 1980 use 6 V. Use a charger matching battery voltage and with output of 10 amps or less; more than 10 amps can damage the battery. Battery can be charged without removing it from the mower.
- step 1: Unplug the charger from the outlet.
- step 2: Lift the seat; connect red charger cable to the red positive terminal.
- step 3: Connect black charger cable to the black negative terminal.
- step 4: Set charger to 12 volts and 10 amps or less; plug in.
- step 5: At 10 amps it should fully charge in one hour; longer at lower amp settings. Some chargers shut off automatically at full charge; unplug when it indicates full.
- step 6: Disconnect charger cables; lower the seat.
- safety: wear work gloves and eye protection.

## page: guides/battery-care-and-charging
- fact: 12 V standard (6 V on some pre-1980 models); charger 10 A or less; charge before spring start and before winter storage.
