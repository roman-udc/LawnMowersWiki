## page: problems/battery-keeps-dying
- alias: "dead battery on my riding mower"
- alias: "battery drains when I run the tractor"
- alias: "charged the battery but it loses charge quickly"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, bad-alternator
- fact: Battery can lose charge in the off-season; charge fully before winter storage; may need charging again before starting in spring.
- fact: If it charges on an external charger but drains while running the tractor, the alternator could be at fault.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- symptom: problems/battery-keeps-dying, problems/engine-wont-crank (engine makes no noise when starting)
- confirm: A good indicator the battery needs replacing is that it loses charge quickly after charging.
- fix: Replace the battery if it won't charge.
- tip: Source links a video on jump-starting the mower safely when the battery is dead and you need to mow right away.

## page: causes/bad-alternator
- symptom: problems/battery-keeps-dying
- fact: The alternator attaches to the engine under the flywheel, generates power while the engine runs and recharges the battery.
- confirm: Measure DC voltage at the battery with a multimeter with the engine running; should read between 13 and 14 volts.
- fix: If not 13-14 V, you may need to replace the alternator.
