## page: procedures/replace-the-starter-motor
- fact: starter motor spins flywheel to start engine when key turned. If you hear click from starter solenoid but motor won't run, confirm the problem (troubleshooting video). Replace faulty starter with manufacturer-approved part.
- brand: guide covers Craftsman, Husqvarna, MTD, Troy-Bilt, Murray, Poulan, Ariens, Snapper, and John Deere riding mowers and lawn tractors.
- difficulty: 3; time: 30 minutes or less.
- tools: wrench set, nut driver set, work gloves.
- safety: let engine cool; park on level surface, parking brake set; work gloves; ignition off and key out; Source warns repairs can be hazardous, only proceed if confident; some repairs only by a qualified technician.
- step 1: disconnect battery: lift seat; remove bolt connecting negative cable to battery and tuck cable away so it doesn't touch the battery post.
- step 2: remove engine blower housing: lift hood; remove air duct mounting screws and pull off duct; turn each air filter cover retaining knob counterclockwise, pull off cover; remove air filter; remove air filter housing screw; remove front and rear blower housing mounting bolts; lift off blower housing.
- step 3: remove old starter: remove lower dash fastener and pull off lower dash; remove starter motor wire mounting nut and pull off wire; remove starter motor mounting bolts (a wire retainer comes off with the left bolt); pull off starter.
- step 4: install new: position on engine, attach with right mounting bolt; position wire retainer and left bolt, route wires through retainer, tighten left bolt; attach starter wire with mounting nut; reinstall lower dash and fastener.
- step 5: reinstall blower housing: position on engine, align the air diverter in the housing; reinstall mounting bolts; air filter housing screw; air filter and cover; air duct with screws; lower hood.
- step 6: reconnect negative cable to negative battery terminal; lower seat.
- tip: rear blower housing bolts are longer than front ones.
