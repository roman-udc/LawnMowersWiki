## page: procedures/tune-up
- fact: tune up the engine at least once a year; tune-up = change oil, oil filter, air filter, spark plug, fuel filter. Helps engine last longer and perform better.
- parts: oil, oil filter, air filter (this engine: foam pre-filter plus air filter, replace both), spark plug (two if the engine has 2 cylinders), fuel filter; many mowers have a sticker under the hood listing these part numbers.
- tools: drain tube (if lost; or drain hose), drain pan or container with a spout or cover (to carry old oil to a collection facility), spark plug wrench, gap tool, strap wrench (oil filter), pinch-off pliers (two), shop rag, work gloves.
- brand/part: sears/oil: searspartsdirect product id-40048
- brand/part: sears/drain tube: id-148456
- brand/part: sears/oil filter: id-696854
- brand/part: sears/air filter: id-793569
- brand/part: sears/spark plug: id-rc12yc
- brand/part: sears/spark plug wrench: id-89838
- brand/part: sears/fuel filter: id-691035
- brand/part: sears/MO-DECK non-stick deck spray: id-mo-deck (mentioned in the power-loss article, not here)
- step 1: gather supplies.
- step 2: run mower a few minutes in a well-ventilated area to warm oil; park on level surface; set parking brake; remove ignition key; lift hood; disconnect spark plug wire(s).
- step 3 (oil change, video mower): remove the fastener from the lower right dash cover; slide cover up slightly to release locking tabs, remove. Remove the oil drain valve cap, install drain tube, pan under it. Push in and turn drain valve counterclockwise to unlock, pull outward to open. Remove the dipstick (keep debris out of the opening). Drain until it stops. Push valve in and turn clockwise until the pin locks. Remove drain tube, reinstall cap and dash cover.
- step 4 (add oil): some oil stays in the engine so you may add less than the manual's capacity. Pour through dipstick tube stopping several ounces short of manual amount; wait a minute; wipe dipstick, push all the way in and lock, unlock and pull; if not at top of full mark add a little, wait, repeat; reinsert dipstick.
- step 5 (air filter): turn cover retaining knobs counterclockwise, pull cover off; release filter from intake tube and pull out; slide new pre-filter over new air filter, attach to intake tube, slide into housing, reinstall cover, turn knobs clockwise to lock.
- step 6 (spark plug): remove with spark plug wrench; set the gap on the new plug with a gap tool to the manual's spec (for the video engine, .03 inches); thread in and tighten with wrench; don't reconnect the wire yet.
- step 7 (fuel filter): clamp fuel line both sides of the filter with two pinch-off pliers; rag under filter; release hose clamps and pull lines off; remove filter; wipe spills; connect lines to new filter; release the pliers; reinstall lower left dash with fastener; reconnect spark plug wire and lower hood.
- step 8 (recheck oil): run engine a few minutes in a well-ventilated area, shut off, wait a minute for oil to settle in the sump, recheck, add to the full mark.

## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- step: warm the oil by running a few minutes; level ground; parking brake; remove key; disconnect plug wire(s); drain via drain valve and tube (see tune-up steps 3-4); refill in stages to avoid overfilling.
- tip: to avoid overfilling stop several ounces short of full, wait a minute, check dipstick, add a little at a time.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step: turn cover retaining knobs counterclockwise; pull filter off intake tube; new pre-filter slides over new air filter; reinstall; knobs clockwise.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- step 1: remove with spark plug wrench.
- step 2: gap the new plug per owner's manual (video: .03 inches).
- step 3: thread in and tighten with the wrench; reconnect wire last.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- step 1: pinch-off pliers on the fuel line both sides of the filter; rag under.
- step 2: release hose clamps, pull lines off, remove filter, wipe spills.
- step 3: connect lines to the new filter; release pliers.
- tools: two pinch-off pliers, shop rag.
