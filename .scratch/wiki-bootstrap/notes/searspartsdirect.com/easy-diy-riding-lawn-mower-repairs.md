## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact: nine simple repairs for most beginners, 15 to 45 minutes each: worn/damaged tire; broken seat switch; cracked/broken seat; bad spark plug; clogged air filter; bad fuel filter; failed ignition switch; bad ignition coil. (Source says nine but lists eight headings.)

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: riding mower tires wear over time; damage from normal use; front and rear tire replacement guides exist.

## page: procedures/replace-a-tire
- fact: separate guides for front and rear tire replacement.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- symptom: riding mower shuts off after you release the brake -> possibly broken seat switch (safety device preventing running if no one on seat).

## page: procedures/replace-the-seat-switch
- fact: replacing seat switch is easy per Source.

## page: procedures/replace-the-seat
- fact: seat holds and contacts the seat switch; if foam cushion missing or seat cracked/broken, seat may not contact switch and engine shuts off when brake released.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: engine runs rough or hard to start. Replace spark plug annually.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- tip: annual replacement.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: air filter traps dirt and debris keeping fuel going to carburetor clean (as stated); buildup makes engine run rough or stall.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: replacement is an easy repair.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: fuel filter prevents debris in gasoline entering carburetor; clogged -> engine runs rough or stops.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- fact: easy repair.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: engine won't stop when key turned off, or won't start when key turned on.

## page: procedures/replace-the-ignition-switch
- fact: easy repair.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: ignition coil (spark coil) produces energy for spark at plug; quick easy replacement.

## page: procedures/replace-the-ignition-coil
- fact: easy repair.
