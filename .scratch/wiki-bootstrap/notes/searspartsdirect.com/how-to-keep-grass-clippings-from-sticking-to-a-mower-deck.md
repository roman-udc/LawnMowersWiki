## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- fact: clippings caked under the deck disturb airflow, dull the cut, drag performance, and over time cause rust and corrosion.
- tip: spray stuck clippings out through the discharge chute with a garden hose; if the mower has a washout port, follow the manual to flush the deck; otherwise add a deck washout port kit.
- tip: spray lubricant (e.g., MO-DECK) makes a slick surface that prevents buildup and rust and speeds cleanup; scrape off dried clippings first so it coats evenly.
- tip: mow only when dry; wet cut grass turns into a thick paste.
- tip: cut no more than 1/3 of the grass height; shorter clippings blow away more easily.
- brand: Sears PartsDirect product mentions: MO-DECK, deck washout port kit.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: grass buildup under deck blocks the airflow needed for clean cut and discharge (see cleaning guide above).
