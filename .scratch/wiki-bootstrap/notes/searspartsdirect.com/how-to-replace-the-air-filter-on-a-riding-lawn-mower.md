## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: air filter traps dirt from air going into the carburetor; clogged filter starves carburetor of air for combustion mix.
- difficulty: 1 (Source scale); time: 15 minutes or less
- tools: work gloves
- parts: replacement air filter (manufacturer-approved), foam pre-filter
- step 1: park on level surface, set parking brake; ignition off, remove key; let engine cool; wear work gloves; lift hood and disconnect spark plug wire (both wires if 2 cylinders).
- step 2: rotate air filter cover retaining knobs counterclockwise to release; pull off cover; release filter from intake tube; pull out filter and foam pre-filter.
- step 3: slide foam pre-filter onto new air filter; connect filter to intake tube and slide into housing; reinstall cover and rotate knobs clockwise to lock.
- step 4: reconnect spark plug wire(s) (both if 2 cylinders); lower hood.
- tip: replace the foam pre-filter whenever replacing the air filter.
- safety: Source warning: repairs can be hazardous; use proper tools and safety equipment; some repairs only by a qualified technician.
- brand: guide covers common Briggs & Stratton engines powering Craftsman, Husqvarna, John Deere, Ariens, Toro, MTD, Troybilt, Murray, Snapper and Cub Cadet riding mowers and lawn tractors.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: dirty air filter could be the problem if engine stalls after starting, runs rough, or sputters (problems/engine-starts-then-dies, problems/engine-runs-rough).
- fix: check regularly, replace if dirty.
