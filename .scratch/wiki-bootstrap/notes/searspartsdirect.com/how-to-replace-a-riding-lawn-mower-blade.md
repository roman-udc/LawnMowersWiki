## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- fact: Applies to Craftsman, MTD, Troybilt, Husqvarna, Toro, Murray, Ariens, Snapper and Weed Eater lawn tractors and riding mowers. Replace with a manufacturer-approved part if blade is dull or bent.
- difficulty: 3
- time: 30 minutes or less
- tools: wrench set, wood block, torque wrench (optional), work gloves
- safety: Repairs can be hazardous; use proper tools/safety equipment; some repairs should be done only by a qualified technician. After disconnecting rear lift links, the spring-loaded attachment lift lever exerts more tension - don't use it until the deck is reinstalled.
- step 1: Park on level surface, set parking brake, ignition off, remove key, wear work gloves, lift hood, disconnect spark plug wire (both wires if 2 cylinders).
- step 2: Remove deck: lower deck to lowest position; disengage blades; roll blade belt off engine pulley and out of belt keepers; remove front lift link support retaining pin and washer and detach support; remove left suspension arm retaining clip and washer and detach from frame; remove left rear deck bracket retaining clip and washer and release bracket; repeat on right side; pull deck out far enough to reach the blade cable; release locking tabs and pull cable out of deck bracket; disconnect blade cable spring from idler arm and move cable aside; pull deck out from under frame and flip over.
- tip: On some tractors the blade can be replaced without removing the deck.
- step 3: Put a wood block between blade and deck to stop rotation; remove blade mounting bolt; pull off blade.
- step 4: With the correct side of the blade facing up, attach to blade shaft, hand-tighten bolt; replace wood block and tighten firmly; remove block; flip deck upright.
- tip: The lower side of the blade is clearly labeled for easy installation.
- step 5: Reinstall deck: push near frame; connect clutch cable spring to idler arm; push cable retainer into deck bracket and engage locking tabs; position under frame; reconnect front lift link support (washer and retaining clip); reconnect left suspension arm and deck bracket (washers and clips); repeat on right; route blade belt through belt keepers and onto engine pulley; check routing over all pulleys and through all keepers; see owner's manual.
- step 6: Reconnect spark plug wire(s) (both if 2 cylinders) and lower hood.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Dull or bent blade tatters grass tips rather than a clean cut; a bent blade creates vibrations that can damage the mandrel assembly.
