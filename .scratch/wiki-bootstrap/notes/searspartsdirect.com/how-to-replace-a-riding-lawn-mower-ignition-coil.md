## page: procedures/replace-the-ignition-coil
- fact: applies to Briggs & Stratton engines powering Craftsman, Husqvarna, Toro, MTD, Troybilt, Murray, John Deere, Snapper, Ariens, Poulan and Weed Eater riding mowers and lawn tractors
- difficulty: 3; time: 30 minutes or less (Source's scale not defined beyond "Difficulty: 3")
- tools: wrench set, feeler gauges, work gloves
- parts: manufacturer-approved riding lawn mower ignition coil
- safety: repairs to power lawn-care equipment can be hazardous; use proper tools and safety equipment; follow all instructions; don't proceed unless confident; some repairs only by a qualified technician
- step 1: disconnect the battery: park on level surface, set parking brake; ignition off, remove key; wear work gloves; lift seat; remove bolt connecting negative cable to battery and tuck cable away so it doesn't touch battery post
- step 2: remove engine blower housing: lift hood; remove air duct mounting screws, pull off air duct; turn each air filter cover retaining knob counterclockwise, pull off cover; remove air filter; remove air filter housing screw; remove front and rear blower housing mounting bolts and lift off housing
- step 3: remove old coil: disconnect spark plug wire and pull it out of engine bracket; turn flywheel so magnet points away from coil; remove coil mounting screws; lift coil and disconnect kill wire; remove coil
- step 4: install new coil: transfer spark plug boot from old coil to new; connect kill wire to new coil; position coil on engine and loosely install 2 mounting screws; pull coil away from flywheel and tighten mounting screws; turn flywheel so magnet aligns with coil
- step 5: set air gap: insert feeler gauge; correct size for most engines is 0.011 inch; holding gauge in place loosen mounting screws so magnet pulls coil to it; tighten screws; turn flywheel by hand and remove gauge; route spark plug wire through engine bracket; connect wire to spark plug
- step 6: reinstall blower housing: position housing on engine, align the air diverter in the housing; install mounting bolts; air filter housing screw; air filter and cover; air duct with mounting screws; lower hood
- step 7: reconnect negative cable to negative terminal; lower seat

## page: procedures/test-the-ignition-coil
- confirm: use a spark plug tester to check for spark when you turn the key; no spark: ignition coil likely isn't working

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: no spark at spark plug tester when key turned
- fact: ignition coil produces the energy that creates the spark at the plug
- fix: replace with manufacturer-approved coil
