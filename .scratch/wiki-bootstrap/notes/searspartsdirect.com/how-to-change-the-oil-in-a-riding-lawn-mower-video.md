## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- fact: change oil as often as the owner's manual recommends, at least once a year; oil gets dirty with particles that abrade engine parts.
- tools: strap wrench (oil filter), drain pan or container with a spout or cover (to carry old oil to a collection facility), drain tube, work gloves.
- parts: engine oil of the type and amount in the owner's manual (demo mower used 10W-30, which lubricates well at typical mowing-season temperatures); oil filter; oil drain tube (if the original is lost).
- brand/part: Sears PartsDirect product links in the article (not OEM numbers): oil 3fh63ys75d-0071-071 (id-40048); drain tube 8751fh9w66-0071-917 (id-148456); oil filter 4b2uojtrax-0071-500 (id-696854).
- safety: run the mower in a well-ventilated area to warm the oil; park on level surface, set parking brake, remove ignition key, lift hood and disconnect the spark plug wire (all wires if more than one cylinder).
- step 1: gather supplies.
- step 2: warm oil by running engine (ventilated area); park level, brake on, key out, disconnect spark plug wire(s).
- step 3: (demo mower) remove fastener from the lower right dash cover; slide cover up slightly to release locking tabs and remove it.
- step 4: remove the oil drain valve cap and install the drain tube; put the drain pan under the tube.
- step 5: push in and turn the drain valve counterclockwise to unlock, then pull outward to open. Remove dipstick, keeping debris out of the opening. Let oil drain until it stops completely.
- step 6: push drain valve in and turn clockwise until the pin locks; remove drain tube; reinstall valve cap and dash cover with fastener.
- step 7: (filter) remove fastener from lower left dash cover and pull the cover off to reach the filter; move drain pan under the filter.
- step 8: turn the filter counterclockwise with a strap wrench to unlock; remove; wipe oil spills off the frame.
- step 9: lubricate the new filter's seal with a light coat of new oil; position on engine; turn clockwise to lock; pull pan out; reinstall dash cover and fastener.
- step 10: (refill) pour clean oil through the dipstick tube, stopping several ounces short of the full amount specified (some oil remains after draining, so you may add less than the manual amount); wait a minute for oil to settle in the sump.
- step 11: wipe dipstick with a shop rag, push it all the way in and lock it, then unlock and pull out; if oil doesn't reach the top of the full mark, add a little, wait a minute, recheck; repeat until at the top of the full mark; reinsert dipstick.
- step 12: reconnect spark plug wire, lower hood, run the engine a few minutes in a well-ventilated area, shut off, wait a minute, recheck level and add oil if needed to reach full.
- tip: to avoid overfilling add oil gradually and recheck.
- brand: dash cover and drain valve steps are specific to the demonstrated mower (video by Sears PartsDirect).
