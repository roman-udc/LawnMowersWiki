## page: procedures/replace-a-tire
- fact: For a tire too damaged to repair. Applies to Craftsman, MTD, Husqvarna, Murray, Troybilt, Toro, Snapper, Yard-Man, Weed Eater, Ariens, John Deer (sic) and Southern States lawn tractors and riding mowers.
- difficulty: 4 (Source scale); time: 60 minutes or less.
- tools: floor jack or bottle jack, jack stand, slot screwdrivers, pry bars, pliers, valve stem core removal tool, liquid detergent, water, wood blocks, work gloves, safety goggles, compressed air supply, anti-seize compound. Optional per tip: ratcheting tension straps.
- parts: new tire; manufacturer-approved part.
- safety: Source warning: repairs can be hazardous; use proper tools and safety equipment; some repairs for a qualified technician only.
- step 1: Park on level surface, parking brake on, ignition off, key out; gloves and goggles; lift hood and disconnect spark plug wire; lower hood. Tip: disconnect both wires if engine has 2 cylinders.
- step 2: Block front tires so mower can't roll; jack under frame to raise rear wheel; jack stand under frame; remove jack.
- step 3: Pull off axle cover; remove retaining ring then washer; pull tire off axle; remove the square key.
- step 4: Remove valve stem cap and valve stem core (lets remaining air out); break the bead and pry tire off rim on one side, then the other.
- step 5: Clean wheel rim; lay new tire flat; lubricate top bead with liquid detergent; pry rim into top side of tire; flip; lubricate bead; pry remaining side onto rim. Warning: use blunt tools to pry to avoid damaging the bead.
- step 6: Fill with air to seat the bead; reinstall valve stem core; fill fully. If tire won't fill, install ratcheting tension straps around outer edge of tread on each side, tighten to force beads outward, fill to seat bead, reinstall core and fill with just enough air to keep beads seated, carefully release and remove straps. Tip: or use one strap around center of tread.
- step 7: Slide wheel on axle aligning key slots in rim and axle; apply anti-seize to square key; push key into rim; reinstall washer, snap retaining ring on axle; push axle cover onto wheel.
- step 8: Jack under frame, raise, pull jack stand, lower mower, remove wood blocks from front tires.
- step 9: Raise hood, reconnect spark plug wire (both if 2 cylinders), lower hood.

## page: procedures/repair-a-flat-tire
- fact: If puncture is only in the tread, it can be fixed with tire sealant (Source points to a separate video; no steps here).

## page: problems/flat-tire
- fact: Tire too damaged to repair = replace (procedures/replace-a-tire); tread puncture = sealant.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: Tire too damaged to repair requires replacement.

## page: causes/missing-axle-key
- fact: Square key sits between wheel/axle key slots; apply anti-seize to it on reinstall.
