## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- fact: applies to Craftsman, Husqvarna, Murray, MTD, Toro, Troybilt and Ariens riding mowers and tractors.
- difficulty: 1 (Source scale); time: 15 minutes or less.
- tools: spark plug wrench (or deep socket and ratchet), gap tool, work gloves
- fact: replace the plug once a year or as often as the owner's manual specifies; replace with a manufacturer-approved part if fouled or ceramic insulator cracked.
- symptom: broken or fouled plug makes the engine run rough or hard to start.
- step 1: park on level surface in a well-ventilated area, set parking brake; ignition off, remove key; let engine cool; wear work gloves; lift the hood and disconnect the spark plug wire (both wires if 2 cylinders).
- step 2: remove the old plug with a spark plug wrench (or deep socket and ratchet).
- step 3: set the gap with a gap tool to the owner's manual spec (for the engine in the Source, .03 inches); thread the new plug in and tighten with the wrench firmly but don't overtighten, so it removes easily next time.
- step 4: reconnect the spark plug wire(s) (both on 2-cylinder engines) and lower the hood.
- safety: repairs can be hazardous; some should be done by a qualified technician.
