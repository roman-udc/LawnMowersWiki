## page: problems/loose-steering
- alias: "steering wheel has too much play"
- alias: "front wheels point outward"
- alias: "front wheels wobble or don't align"
- causes-named: loose-steering-linkage, bad-front-axle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: steering wheel has too much play; wheels don't turn with the steering wheel; steering slips when turning; front wheels point outward; front wheels wobble or don't align
- fix: check tie rods, steering shaft and sector gear assembly; replace damaged parts; also check the wheels and front axle - a bent or worn wheel spindle or worn front axle won't allow proper steering

## page: causes/loose-steering-linkage
- fact: tie rod - if steering is loose or wheels don't turn with the steering wheel the tie rod may need replacing (remove and replace)
- fact: steering kit - if steering is loose or slips when turning the wheel, the complete steering kit may need replacing
- fact: check tie rods, steering shaft, sector gear assembly

## page: causes/bad-front-axle
- fix: replace the front axle if the front wheels point outward or steering is loose

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: bent or damaged wheel spindle prevents proper steering; front wheels wobble or don't align
- fix: check wheel spindles for damage; replace the spindle assembly if damaged
- fact: this is the front wheel spindle, not a blade spindle
