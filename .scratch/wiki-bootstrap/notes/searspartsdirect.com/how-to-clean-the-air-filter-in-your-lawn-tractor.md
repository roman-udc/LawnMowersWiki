## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: Dirt blocks airflow to the carburetor and clogs cooling fins, making the engine overheat. Owner's manual has model-specific instructions.
- fact: General rule: tap out or replace the paper filter after every 2 to 3 mowing sessions; wash the foam pre-filter every 25 hours of use (or once a year, whichever first).
- step 1: Work where dirt won't blow or fall into the tractor. Wipe loose dirt and debris from near the filter cover.
- step 2: Remove the wing nuts holding the filter cover; set aside cover and wing nuts.
- step 3: Before removing the air filter, wipe dirt from the rubber seal surrounding the filter with a rag, without pushing dirt under the element. Remove the filter and the foam pre-filter if present.
- step 4: Foam pre-filter: wash in diluted liquid dish detergent and pat dry with a paper towel or clean rag. If washing doesn't remove dirt or the foam is damaged, install a replacement pre-filter.
- step 5: Paper filter: tap gently on your hand or a flat surface to remove loose dirt. If still dirty after tapping, or ripped, install a replacement.
- step 6: Wipe dirt from the areas the filter rubber seal touches; install pre-filter and filter; position the cover and tighten the wing nuts to secure it; don't overtighten.
- safety: Warning: don't use compressed air or any petroleum products (gasoline, oil, kerosene) to clean the paper filter; they can damage it.
- tools: rag, wing nuts (hand), dish detergent, paper towel.
- parts: Replacement air filter and foam pre-cleaner (no part numbers in Source).

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Dirt blocks airflow to the carburetor; clean/replace as above.
