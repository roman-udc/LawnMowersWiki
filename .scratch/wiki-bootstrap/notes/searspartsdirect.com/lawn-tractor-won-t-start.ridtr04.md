## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "engine spins but won't start when I turn the key"
- fact: engine is missing one of fuel, compression or spark; check the basics first (Source links a video "riding mower engine spins but won't start").
- causes-named: old-fuel, bad-ignition-coil, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: causes listed across the article: stale gas, ignition switch, ignition coil (no spark from a known good plug after removing the kill wire), carburetor, air filter, fuel filter.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "click when I turn the key"
- alias: "mower seems completely dead"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor, seized-engine, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground
- fact: a click on key turn means dead battery, bad starter solenoid, failed starter motor or seized engine (video linked). Completely dead: check battery, ignition switch, interlock switches, starter solenoid and wiring; ignition switch problems can stop the starter getting current.

## page: problems/battery-keeps-dying
- fact: if you constantly have to charge the battery, a bad battery or failed alternator could be the cause. If the battery won't charge fully within 8 hours on a charger, probably replace the battery; if the battery is okay, blame shifts to the alternator.
- causes-named: bad-battery, bad-alternator

## page: causes/bad-battery
- symptom: no noise at all when trying to start (problems/engine-wont-crank); battery loses charge quickly after charging.
- confirm: battery won't charge fully within 8 hours connected to a charger.
- fix: replace the battery.

## page: causes/bad-alternator
- fact: alternator attaches to the engine under the flywheel and generates power while the engine runs; it also recharges the battery.
- confirm: check DC voltage at the battery with the engine running; should read between 13 and 14 volts; if not, may need to replace the alternator.
- symptom: battery drains while running the tractor, then it won't start.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fact: supplies power to the engine's electrical components.
- symptom: doesn't turn over with the key in On, or doesn't shut off with the key in Off.
- fix: replace; repair guide: how to replace a riding lawn mower ignition switch.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: produces the energy for the spark at the plug.
- confirm: remove the kill wire; if there is no spark from a known good spark plug, the coil is the likely cause.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: impurities in gasoline can clog the carburetor, preventing starting; clogs can also make the engine run rough and stall when driving. Seals and gaskets in the carburetor eventually wear out and leak gas. Use a carburetor rebuild kit to refurbish a clogged or leaking carburetor.

## page: procedures/rebuild-the-carburetor
- fact: use a rebuild kit for a clogged or leaking carburetor (repair guide linked; no steps here).

## page: procedures/replace-the-carburetor
- fact: carburetor mixes gas with air before it goes into the cylinder; unbalanced mix means no start, or rough running and stalling when driving; a damaged carb may also leak gas; replace if it causes any of these symptoms.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: coil-activated switch that activates the starter motor; when the key is turned to start the solenoid sends current to the starter motor and you hear it click.
- confirm: replace if it doesn't click when it gets power from the ignition switch, or if it clicks but doesn't send current to the starter motor to spin the engine.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: air filter traps debris from air entering the carburetor; a dirty filter could be the problem if the engine stalls after starting or runs rough or sputters, since the carburetor doesn't get enough air. Inspect regularly and replace when dirty.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: spins the flywheel to start the engine. If the starter motor runs but doesn't spin the flywheel, check the starter drive gear for damage; a defective drive gear won't mesh with the flywheel teeth. Replace the starter if the drive gear is damaged. If the starter doesn't run when activated, it may be defective, but first check other failures that prevent it running: a locked-up engine, or poor current flow to the motor. Replace if it won't run and other causes are eliminated.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: brake interlock switch detects whether the brake pedal is fully pushed down; the parking brake holds the pedal fully depressed, so the switch also recognises the parking brake being set; it prevents starting without the brake pushed in or parking brake set. Replace if the engine won't start because the switch doesn't detect the pedal fully depressed or parking brake set.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: engine fuel filter screens impurities from gasoline going to the carburetor. Clogged = gas can't flow, no start; damaged = impurities reach and clog the carburetor. Replace if damaged or clogged.

- fact: locked-up engine can prevent the starter spinning; a click with no crank can mean a seized engine.
