## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "lawn mower pulls to one side while cutting"
- alias: "mower veers toward the bushes"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, loose-or-seized-wheel-bearing, bad-tire, unlevel-deck, worn-drive-belt, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fact: common causes: tire issues, bent/misaligned deck, slipping drive wheel, tricky terrain.
- fact: if pull disappears or switches sides when you mow the same area in another direction, it's terrain, not mechanical. Slopes/dips shift weight; wet or soft ground lets one wheel sink; thick grass patches slow one wheel more.
- tip: changing mowing pattern balances wear and gives a more even cut.
- fact: checklist order: tire pressure, wheels, level deck, drive system (self-propelled only), terrain.
- pro: if still veering after the easy checks, the problem may be deeper in the drive system or chassis.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: check first, especially on riding mowers and self-propelled walk-behinds with inflatable tires. Low tire affects traction and balance; low side drags or sinks into soft ground and the mower veers that way.
- confirm: check all four tires (or both on a two-wheeled walk-behind) against the recommended PSI in the manual or stamped on the tire sidewall. Even a few pounds of difference can affect steering.
- fix: top off the low tire to match; inspect for slow leaks or damage.

## page: causes/loose-or-seized-wheel-bearing
- fact: wheel bearings can wear or seize and axles can bind, especially after rough terrain use; a dragging or sticking wheel favors one side.
- confirm: spin each wheel by hand; look for wobble, cracks, bent rims, resistance when turning.
- fix: replace a wheel that looks bad.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: damaged wheels (wobble, cracks, bent rims) cause pulling; replace the wheel.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- symptom: deck with one side lower than the other leans the mower and pulls it off course; may also show uneven cut or one side scalped.
- confirm: park on flat surface; measure deck height side to side, from blade tip or bottom edge of the deck to the ground.
- fix: adjust deck leveling arms or linkages per service manual; level deck distributes weight evenly, reduces pull.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- step 1: park on flat surface.
- step 2: measure from blade tip or bottom deck edge to the ground on both sides.
- step 3: if uneven, adjust deck leveling arms/linkages per the service manual.
- fact: article links a Sears guide for leveling a riding mower deck.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fact: self-propelled drive: check drive wheels for uneven tread wear or one wheel slipping more; inspect drive cables (a stretched or uneven cable gives one wheel more power, esp. on variable-speed models); some use separate gear assemblies or a belt-driven transmission that can wear unevenly or develop tension issues on one side.
- fact: slipping, loose or misaligned drive belt gives inconsistent power and veering.
- fix: adjust cable tension or replace wheels; otherwise look deeper at drive system wear.

## page: causes/bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fact: uneven drive cable tension (stretched cable) sends more power to one wheel (self-propelled).
