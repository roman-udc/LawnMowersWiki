## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact engine tune-up parts (after every season): air filter (prevents abrasive particles entering cylinders causing wear and oil contamination); oil filter (oil lubricates, cleans, prevents corrosion, improves sealing, cools by carrying heat away; filter removes contaminants); spark plug (replace once a year or as owner's manual specifies); fuel filter (screens dirt and rust particles from gasoline before carburetor; paper integrated in cartridge; replace per owner's manual).
- tip: engine parts may be on a separate parts list from frame and deck; look up with engine model number; part numbers may be listed on underside of tractor hood.
- fact deck parts: mower blade (one sharp edge on top side; upward angle blows grass up and out the discharge chute; dull, bent, corrode; replace once per season or when dull or damaged); blade belt (attaches to engine pulley and spins blades; wears and can slip after a season; replace at end of each cutting season or when excessively worn); mandrel pulley (mandrel = housing, bearings, blade shaft, with pulley on top; bearings often wear after a season).
- fact frame/body parts: ground drive belt (replace yearly; connects engine crankshaft to transaxle pulley to drive rear wheels); head lights (look up part by model number; lift hood, remove socket from housing, replace bulb); tires (replace when tread thin; tire sealant can fix small leaks instead of replacing).
- fact repair parts: flywheel key; starter solenoid; battery; alternator; seat switch; starter motor (see below).
- tip: grease moving parts and keep deck clean as well as replacing parts.
- pro: if not confident, schedule a Sears Technician.

## page: procedures/tune-up
- fact: engine tune-up parts after every season: air filter, oil filter, spark plug (once a year), fuel filter.

## page: causes/sheared-flywheel-key
- fact: flywheel key = small rectangular metal piece keeping crankshaft and flywheel aligned when flywheel nut tightened; shears if blade hits an object hard enough to make flywheel slip out of alignment, protecting expensive engine parts.

- fact: replace broken or sheared key (video referenced).

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: sends current to starter when key at start; if no internal contact click when turning key, diagnose and replace solenoid if not working.

## page: procedures/replace-the-starter-solenoid
- fact: step-by-step video referenced.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: battery provides current to starter motor; replace when weak or dead and won't recharge.

## page: causes/bad-alternator
- fact: alternator = set of wire coils under the engine flywheel generating electricity to power tractor when running and recharge battery; replace when it doesn't recharge battery as engine runs.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: seat switch shuts engine off if no one detected in seat when tractor starts moving; broken switch won't sense you and stops engine when you release brake. Replace.

## page: procedures/replace-the-seat-switch
- fact: replace broken seat switch (video).

- fact: spins flywheel when key turned; solenoid clicks but no starter run -> replace starter.
