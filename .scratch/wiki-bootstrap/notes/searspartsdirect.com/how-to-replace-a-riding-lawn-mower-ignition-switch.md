## page: procedures/replace-the-ignition-switch
- brand: basic procedure works for Craftsman, MTD, Murray, Toro, Troybilt, Husqvarna, Poulan, Weed Eater and Yard-Man riding mowers and lawn tractors.
- difficulty: 1 (Sears scale); time: 15 minutes or less.
- tools: wrench set, work gloves.
- parts: manufacturer-approved replacement ignition switch.
- safety: Sears warning - repairs to power lawn-care equipment can be hazardous; use proper tools/safety equipment; don't proceed unless confident; some repairs only by qualified technician.
- step 1: Disconnect battery: park on level surface, set parking brake; ignition off, remove key; wear work gloves; lift the seat; remove the bolt connecting the negative cable to the battery and tuck the cable away so it doesn't touch the battery post.
- step 2: Remove switch: lift the hood to access back of switch; release the locking tabs and push switch out through the front of the dash; disconnect wire harness on back of switch (if hard, wiggle the plug while pulling).
- step 3: Install new switch: connect wire harness to back of the new switch; push switch into dash and engage locking tabs; lower the hood.
- step 4: Reconnect battery: reconnect negative cable to negative battery terminal; lower the seat.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: riding mower won't start when key turned to On, or won't stop when key turned to Off.
- confirm: with multimeter test whether electricity travels between the S and B terminals; if not, switch is the problem.
- fix: replace with manufacturer-approved part.
