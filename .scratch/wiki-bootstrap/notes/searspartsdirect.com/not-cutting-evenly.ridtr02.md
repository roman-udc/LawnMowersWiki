## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "riding mower not cutting evenly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, mowing-too-fast, dull-or-bent-blade, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing (mandrel), worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bent-deck-or-deck-shell, engine-runs-rough (tune-up)
- confirm: Look at the cut pattern. Slanted stair-step pattern: inflate tires to manufacturer recommendation and level the deck. Alternating stripes of taller and shorter turf: slow down (mowing too fast); if slowing doesn't help, sharpen or replace blades. Ragged or tattered grass tips: sharpen or replace blades.
- fix: Vibration: replace unbalanced blades and worn mandrels. Replace a worn blade belt (won't spin blades correctly). Replace deck idler pulleys that don't spin freely. Replace the deck if bent. Tune up the engine if it doesn't run smoothly while mowing.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: Mandrel assembly attaches to deck and spins the blade; blade won't move if the mandrel locks up; a damaged mandrel causes deck vibration and uneven cutting. Examine regularly; replace damaged mandrels.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- symptom: Tilted deck gives uneven cuts.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- step 1: Park the tractor on a flat surface and inflate all 4 tires evenly. step 2: Lower deck to cutting position and position blades sideways. step 3: Measure height of the cutting blade outer edges. step 4: Adjust mower deck hangers to level the blades. (Source links a leveling video for detail.)

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Bent blade won't cut evenly and may cause deck vibration; dull blades leave ragged edges. Inspect regularly; replace if damaged, bent or dull.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Blade belt spins the blades; worn or broken belt won't spin blades (no cutting); worn belt can cause deck vibration or uneven cuts. Examine regularly, replace if worn or broken.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Deck idler pulley tightens the blade belt against the blade pulleys when blade engagement lever is shifted; if blades don't spin check it; worn idler causes uneven cuts and vibration. Replace if worn or broken.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- symptom: Deck houses blades, mandrel pulleys, blade belts; damage causes uneven cutting; severe rust can corrode holes. Replace if damaged or heavily rusted.

## page: procedures/tune-up
- fact: Engine tune-up is routine maintenance: replace engine oil, oil filter, air filter, fuel filter and spark plug; check ignition, carburetor, battery, throttle and choke controls; clean engine cooling fins. A poorly running engine may stall when driving and cut unevenly.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Inflate tires to manufacturer's recommendation (stair-step cut pattern).

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Alternating taller/shorter stripes: slow down.
