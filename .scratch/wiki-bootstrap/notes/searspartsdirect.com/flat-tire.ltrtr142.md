## page: problems/flat-tire
- alias: "flat tire on my riding mower"
- causes-named: bad-tire
- tip: Check tractor tires regularly.
- fact: When a tire goes flat you can often repair it with tire sealant; sometimes install an inner tube to fix leaks sealant can't; replace the tire if too damaged to repair.

## page: procedures/repair-a-flat-tire
- fix: Tire sealant first; an inner tube for leaks sealant can't fix; replace if too much damage. (Links to a Sears PartsDirect repair video; no steps in this Source.)

## page: procedures/replace-a-tire
- fact: Front: replace a damaged front lawn tractor tire with a new one of the same size as the other front tire. Rear: needs deep tread for traction to move the mower through grass; replace a worn or damaged rear tire with one the same size as the other rear tire. Links to repair guides for front and rear tires (no steps here).

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: Riding mower tires are durable but wear out over time or can be punctured.
