## page: procedures/replace-the-carburetor
- fact: carburetor blends air and fuel; water damage, dirt, debris or gummed-up gasoline can cause surging or hard starting
- fact: guide covers Briggs & Stratton engines powering Craftsman, Troybilt, MTD, Husqvarna, Murray, Snapper, Weed Eater, Toro and Ariens tractors/riding mowers; use a manufacturer-approved part
- tip: if the carb isn't beyond repair, rebuild it instead (Source links a rebuild guide)
- difficulty: 3 (Source scale); time: 45 minutes or less
- tools: wrench set, nut driver set, pliers, hose pinch-off pliers, shop rag, work gloves
- safety: repairs to power lawn equipment can be hazardous; use proper tools/safety gear; only proceed if confident; some repairs only by a qualified technician
- step 1: disconnect the battery: park on level surface in well-ventilated area, set parking brake, ignition off and key out, let engine cool, wear gloves, lift seat, remove bolt connecting the negative cable to battery and tuck cable away so it doesn't touch the post
- step 2: remove engine blower housing: lift hood; remove air duct mounting screws and duct; turn each air filter cover retaining knob counterclockwise and pull off cover; remove air filter; remove air filter housing screw; remove front and rear blower housing mounting bolts and lift off housing
- tip: remove the hood for more room
- step 3: disconnect the fuel line: clamp the line with hose pinch-off pliers; shop rag under the carb end to catch drips; release the clamp and pull line off carb; wipe up spilled fuel
- step 4: remove the carburetor: remove air intake mounting nuts; detach breather tube and remove air intake; disconnect wire from the fuel solenoid; photograph the carb linkage connections for reassembly; remove carb mounting studs; disconnect throttle rod and throttle spring; disconnect choke rod; pull off carb; remove intake gasket on the engine and clean the mounting surface
- step 5: install new carb: position near engine, reconnect choke rod; connect throttle rod and spring; install new intake gasket on engine; attach carb with mounting studs and tighten firmly; connect fuel solenoid wire; attach breather tube to air intake and reconnect intake to carb; install intake mounting nuts
- step 6: connect the fuel line to carb; remove the pinch-off pliers
- step 7: reinstall blower housing: position it, align the air diverter in the housing; install mounting bolts; install air filter housing screw; install air filter and cover; install air duct with its screws; lower hood
- step 8: reconnect negative cable to negative battery terminal; lower seat

- symptom: water damage, dirt, debris or gummed-up gasoline in the carb: engine surges or hard to start
