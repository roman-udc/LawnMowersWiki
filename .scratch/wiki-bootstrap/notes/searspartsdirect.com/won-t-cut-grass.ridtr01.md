## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "riding mower runs but blades won't cut grass"
- alias: "blades won't spin"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: if the engine runs but blades don't cut, check the blade belt first (replace if worn or broken). If the belt is OK, check the blade belt idler pulley, blade clutch cable and clutch lever assembly (tractors with manual blade actuators). On mowers with a PTO switch, check the PTO switch and electric clutch. Failed mandrel pulleys can also stop blades spinning properly.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: the blade belt spins the blades; a worn or broken belt won't spin them. Worn belt can also cause deck vibration or uneven cuts. Examine regularly; replace if worn or broken.
- fact: Source links repair guides: replace blade belt on a zero-turn riding mower; replace a riding mower blade drive belt.

- fact: guide links as above; no steps in Source.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fact: Source calls it the mandrel assembly: attaches to the deck and spins the blade; blade won't move if the mandrel locks up; damaged mandrel causes deck vibration and uneven cutting. Examine regularly; replace damaged mandrels. Repair guide: replace a mandrel assembly on a riding lawn mower.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly
- fact: mandrel assembly replacement guide link only.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: deck idler pulley tightens the blade belt against the blade pulleys to spin the blades when the engagement lever is engaged; a worn idler pulley can stop blade spin and also cause uneven cuts and deck vibration. Replace if worn or broken.

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: the blade engagement lever pulls the blade clutch cable to spin blades when shifted to engaged; blades won't spin if the lever breaks (examine and replace if broken).
- fact: the blade clutch cable tightens the blade belt; blades won't spin when the lever is pushed if the cable is worn or broken; examine the cable and replace if worn or broken. Repair guide: replace a blade clutch cable on a riding lawn mower.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: the electric clutch mounts to the engine crankshaft and controls the blades; blades won't spin if it is defective; replace if it won't engage the blades when activated. Repair guide: replace the electric clutch on a zero-turn riding mower.
