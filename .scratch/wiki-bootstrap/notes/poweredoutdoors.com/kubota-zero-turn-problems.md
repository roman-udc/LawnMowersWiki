## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Kubota zero turn won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: Engine needs air, fuel and spark; battery, starter solenoid and switches can also fail.
- fix: empty tank -> fill fresh; old fuel -> empty, fresh fuel + additive like Sea Foam; plugs -> replace dirty/damaged; air filter -> clean or replace; fuel filter -> replace; fuel line -> remove, clean, replace; carb -> remove and clean, rebuild or replace; battery -> charge, replace if won't hold charge, secure cables, remove corrosion.
- brand: Models named: Z121, Z231, Z421, Z422, Z726, Z781 and other zero turns.
- safety: Park on flat level surface, remove ignition key, remove spark plug wire before repairs.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, bad-ignition-coil, fouled-spark-plug
- fix: wrong oil level -> add or remove oil to engine manufacturer's level; consult a mechanic if that doesn't solve it (possible extensive engine damage). Plugged deck -> scrape deck, sharpen blades. Ignition coil with break in continuity -> replace. Spark plugs: new if dark, damaged, worn; wire brush if good and slightly dirty. Cooling fins: clean, replace damaged fins.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- causes-named: transmission-bypass-levers (unmapped), worn-or-stretched-belt (drive pump belt), bad-belt-tensioner-spring / worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing (tensioner pulley), missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Hydrostatic transmission pumps fluid to move wheels; pressure is so great you can't free-roll the zero turn without bypassing the transmission. Make sure bypass levers aren't bypassing; check belt, pulleys, tensioners; oil fresh and full.
- fix: Put transmission bypass levers (one near each drive wheel) in operating position. Worn pump drive belt -> replace and confirm seated around pulleys. Bad tensioner pulley -> replace, grease tensioner arm. Broken/missing idler arm spring -> replace. Low/old hydraulic oil -> change at Kubota's interval, keep full; some transmissions are unserviceable, see Kubota dealer.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fix: loose/missing engine mount bolts -> secure/replace. Failing clutch -> replace bad electric clutch; on manual-engagement clutch replace worn cable, lever, bushings. Blades -> replace damaged, balance. Material around blade shaft -> remove, inspect spindle and housing. Spindle bearings -> replace when worn. Belt -> replace worn/loose. Pulleys -> replace damaged ones or those with restriction when turned by hand or wobble from bad bearing. Debris -> clean all crevices.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud (missing engine guard), clogged-air-filter, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast (overworking)
- fact: Engine oil keeps engine cool: use air-cooled engine oil of right viscosity that is high in zinc; keep level full. Check cooling fins, engine shroud, engine guard, air filter, deck, blade condition.
- fix: Wrong oil -> drain, refill fresh; use 10W-30 in most conditions (different viscosity possible in very warm conditions). Missing engine guard -> replace/secure. Plugged deck -> scrape. Dull blades -> sharpen/replace. Overworking -> slow ground speed, avoid wet grass, double or triple-cut an overgrown lawn.
- note: Source's overheating table has misaligned rows (low-oil and fins rows carry unrelated fuel/carburetor text); ignore those.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- fact: Smokes when air filter plugged, oil not correct, or bad gasket lets oil seep onto hot surface like the muffler; unexplained smoke may be worn piston ring or burnt valve.
- fix: Too much oil -> remove a little from the crankcase, check air filter. Low oil -> technician inspect; likely damaged. Gasket -> locate and repair.
- pro: Small engine technician for internal tests.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "won't drive straight", "pulls to one side"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, tracking adjustment
- fix: Tires filled to manufacturer spec and equal both sides. Replace bad dampers. Tracking: adjust the speed adjustment bolt so each tire moves at same speed (owner's manual).

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: Adjust speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed so both tires rotate at same speed; see owner's manual.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "running rough and sputters", "bogs down"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Look at fuel flow, airflow, intermittent spark, and extra engine load (fast ground speed, plugged deck, dull blades). Throttle not at fastest speed with blades engaged causes sluggishness.
- fix: Slow ground speed in tall/thick/wet grass; throttle at highest position when engaging blades; scrape deck; sharpen blades; plug wire-brush clean or new if very dark/worn/damaged.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- causes-named: bad-pto-switch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, bad-pto-clutch, worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, blown-fuse
- fix: Belt worn, cracked or shiny glazed -> replace. Belt falls off pulleys -> replace worn idler arm, spring, bad pulleys, stretched belt. Clutch -> replace; manual engagement clutch replace worn cable, lever, springs, linkages. Battery: test, charge if under 12.7 V on 12-volt battery, replace if won't hold. Fuse: same capacity.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: Hard to find as gas evaporates; common: carburetor and dry cracked fuel lines; plastic components degrade and seals dry out.
- fix: Replace gasket between carb bowl and carb; clean carb (stuck float/needle) and replace damaged parts; replace tank leaking at seams; replace shut-off valve; weak filter new; pump new; gas cap (on some caps only the rubber seal can be bought).

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Click = suspect solenoid; also check power to solenoid: battery, cables, terminals, ground; then starter motor.
- fix: Replace damaged cables, remove corrosion, secure loose. Battery below 12.7 V on a 12-volt: charge; replace if it won't hold. Ground: remove corrosion; ground cable from battery and from starter solenoid must contact well (a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded). Test solenoid and replace if faulty. Replace bad starter motor.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, unlevel-deck, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: First check tire pressures (low tire unlevels deck). Then top and underside of deck.
- fix: Blades extremely worn where sails are thinner than center or ends rounding -> replace. Blade installed with correct hardware, sails pointing up toward deck. Bent spindle -> replace and inspect housing. Belt stretched/worn/cracked/glazed -> replace; rotate pulleys by hand slowly and replace if restriction, bearing noise or wobble. Scrape deck. Equalize tires left/right. Level deck side-to-side; deck sits slightly lower in front than rear; spec in operator's manual. Throttle highest when engaging blades. Slow down in thick/wet/tall grass; too fast can push grass over instead of cutting. Overlap paths; use a small push mower for ditches and steep grade changes.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Weak hydrostatic transmission when hydraulic oil old or tank low, or drive belt/tensioner pulley worn or broken. Replace belt, replace tensioner pulley and grease arm, change oil at intervals and keep full.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Keep mower clean, store dry to prevent corrosion; routine maintenance and fresh gas reduce problems.
