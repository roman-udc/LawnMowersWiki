## page: problems/engine-dies-when-hot
- alias: "John Deere lawn mower quits when hot / shuts off when hot"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, old-fuel, dirty-carburetor, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-ignition-coil, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- safety: remove spark plug wire; wait for engine to cool and moving parts to stop.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- tip: check before each use; author starts season with new filter and cleans several times; more often if heavy use or dusty.
- step paper: remove; wipe housing; remove foam pre-filter if present; tap; light test; reuse if light visible; replace if no light, extremely dirty, oil-covered, damaged.
- step foam pre-cleaner (used with paper primary): DO NOT oil a foam pre-cleaner (can damage paper filter); inspect (brittle, torn, dark spots = replace); wash mild detergent and water; rinse until soap gone and water clear; squeeze, lay flat to dry (sun speeds); put onto paper filter.
- mistake: oiling a pre-cleaner.

- see above.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: old gas leaves varnish building up over time; mower quits after running a while.
- fix: siphon and dispose properly; mix fresh gas with stabilizer/cleaner (Sea Foam Motor Treatment); refill.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: deposits plug fuel jet, stick small parts; quits when hot.
- confirm: confirm fuel to carb (else check filter, line, pump); remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in intake, start; sluggish then shut down after cleaner burns off = carb.
- fix: clean it, shop clean, or install new carburetor.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: use air-cooled engine oil with high zinc concentration (zinc described as cooling agent); differs from car oil. Most small engine makers recommend SAE30 or 10W-30; may need 20W-50 in higher ambient temperature; use viscosity chart (Kawasaki Engine Oil Viscosity & Temperature chart referenced).
- fix wrong oil: drain, refill with manufacturer-recommended viscosity for ambient temp.
- fact low oil: oil heats and thickens, increases friction; quits when hot. Usually an oil change won't fix once shut down; likely internal damage; mechanic tests. Check oil before each mowing; low oil may mean burning oil or leak.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: crankshaft and rod push through excess oil and can't rotate freely; raises crankcase pressure, loads internal parts, quits when hot; smoke from excess oil can clog air filter.
- fix: remove spark plug wire; drain a little: drain plug (quickly remove and replace); oil filter (rag ready); oil fill hole on push mowers (tip mower, carburetor and air filter on high side); oil extractor/evacuator; turkey baster. Recheck level. Replace air filter if oil on it.

- step: five methods above.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: clean annually, check periodically; clogged/broken fins or debris around engine can shut off mower.
- fix: remove debris around fins, replace broken fins, clear under shrouds, confirm heat shield securely in place.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: stops working when hot; windings separate and short; insufficient voltage.
- confirm: confirm good plug first; ohmmeter for continuity break; replace.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: vent equalizes pressure; plugged = vacuum; shuts off after running a while.
- fix: replace cap if mower runs with cap loosened but quits shortly with cap installed.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: clogged deck + dull blades load engine, bog and quit. Sharpen blades, scrape deck, avoid mowing wet.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: dull blades magnify load.
