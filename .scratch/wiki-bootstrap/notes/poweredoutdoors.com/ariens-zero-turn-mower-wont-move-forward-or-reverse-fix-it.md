## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Ariens zero turn won't move forward or reverse"
- alias: "mower moves slowly or weakly"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- brand: Ariens zero turn has two pumps and wheel motors (left and right); hydrostatic transmission pumps fluid to wheel motors. Source lists 7 reasons.
- safety: Follow operator's manual: engine off, remove key, wait for all parts to stop moving and cool before working on a hydrostatic transmission.
- pro: Consult a professional if lacking skills/knowledge/condition to repair safely.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: zero turn won't move (problems/mower-wont-move) after the mower was recently pushed/free-rolled.
- fact: Fluid is under so much pressure the mower cannot be moved manually without the bypass lever. Bypass levers are pulled out to "free-roll" (used when mower has mechanical problems/engine no longer runs).
- confirm: Check the transmission bypass levers near each wheel; pulled out = bypass, pushed in = transmission engaged.
- fix: Push both bypass levers back in so the transmission powers the wheels.
- safety: Park on level surface with parking brake engaged when using bypass levers; bypass on a hill can let the mower roll downhill.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- symptom: zero turn won't move; hydraulic pump is driven by a v-belt off the engine; loose or fallen-off belt = no movement.
- confirm: Make sure belt is seated correctly on pulleys; look for worn, stretched or broken belt.
- fix: Replace worn/stretched/broken belt. If belt fell off, check tensioner pulley and idler arm spring.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Source frames "bad tensioner pulley" (not spring): pulley often hard plastic with a center bearing; pulley can break or bearing fail, so belt stops rotating or falls off. Check after pump belt found not worn.
- fix: Replace bad pulley with a new one. Keep the tensioner arm greased so it moves and doesn't seize.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: Idler spring puts tension on the drive belt.
- fix: Replace spring if broken, missing, or stretched.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: weak movement, runs slowly or not at all (problems/mower-wont-move).
- confirm: Check level when oil is cool against the "full" mark on the transmission overflow tanks; check for hydraulic fluid leaks.
- fix: Add hydro oil until full level (cool). Change oil and filters after initial 75-hour break-in, then every 400 hours.
- brand: Some entry-level Ariens zero-turn transmissions are sealed and unserviceable (cannot change oil). Check operator's manual or Ariens dealer if unsure.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- fact: Low, old or bad fluid doesn't lubricate well; friction overheats fluid. Hot fluid can also indicate extensive transmission damage.
- symptom: mower runs fine cold but stops running when hot.
- pro: Take to dealership; experienced mechanic checks transmission for damage and whether repair or replacement is needed.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- symptom: mower moves slowly or not at all after changing hydraulic fluid.
- fix: Must bleed air after changing fluid. On most Ariens zero-turns, raise the drive tires off the ground and let them spin forward and backward.
- step 1: Place mower on level surface; chock wheels to prevent rolling forward.
- step 2: Raise the rear drive tires off the ground.
- step 3: Let them spin forward and backward until no excessive noise and wheels move at normal speed.
- step 4: Refer to operator's manual for detailed steps.

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1: Level surface, chock wheels.
- step 2: Raise rear drive tires; spin forward and backward until quiet and normal speed (most Ariens zero-turns). Consult manual.

## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Change hydraulic oil and filters after 75-hour break-in, then every 400 hours (Ariens). Some entry-level models sealed/unserviceable. Bleed air afterward.
