## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "PowerSmart lawn mower won't start"
- fact: won't start when engine lacks air, fuel or spark.
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter (fuel strainer), dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter
- safety: follow operator's manual; remove spark plug wire.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: PowerSmart has a small fuel tank; easy to forget to check when frustrated. Fill (4-stage PowerSmart) with fresh gas min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down from 30 days; ethanol attracts moisture, varnish and gummy deposits.
- tip: min 87 octane; never more than 10% ethanol, ethanol-free best; consume within 30 days; stabilizer must be added to fresh fuel (won't reverse old fuel); store in approved container away from moisture/combustibles.
- fix: drain with fuel siphon pump; fresh gas + additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment).

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: loosen/remove cap and start; if it starts but stops after cap replaced and runs a while, cap bad. Replace.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: shut off supply (shut-off valve if any) or crimp with fuel hose pinch-off pliers; remove line end at carburetor into container lower than tank (no uphill without pump); turn on; good flow = line OK.
- fix: if poor flow, with flow off remove line from mower (if no shut-off valve may have to drain the tank); carb cleaner then compressed air, repeat; reinstall; new line if can't clear or if damaged/cracking with age.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: PowerSmart has a fuel strainer (small part attached to tank straining fuel before line). Check after line OK: drain tank, remove strainer, inspect; clean screen or replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in intake; starts, runs, shuts down -> disassemble and clean.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace annually; check and clean through season.
- step paper: remove, wipe housing without dirt into intake, tap against hand; replace if very dirty, damaged or wet.
- step foam pre-filter (fits around paper filter): wash in warm water and mild detergent; rinse until clear; dry completely before placing around paper; replace if torn or worn.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: dirty tip, cracked porcelain, burnt electrode; clean deposits; replace if very dark or damaged. Gap per operator's manual; wrong gap or loose wires cause no-start.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: confirm plug good first; ohm meter continuity; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fact: rope unstrung; broken pulley, springs or clips. Sometimes restringing is enough; else replace parts. Price full recoil assembly first - may be better than replacing components.
