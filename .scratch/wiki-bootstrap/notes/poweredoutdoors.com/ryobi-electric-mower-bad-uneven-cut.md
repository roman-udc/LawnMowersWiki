## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "RYOBI electric mower bad or uneven cut", "leaves lawn looking bad"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure (damaged/loose wheel), dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, wrong-cutting-height, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, mowing-too-fast, uneven lawn (unmapped)
- brand: Electric zero-turn models may also have low tire pressure or unlevel deck.
- safety: Park on flat level surface, remove battery pack(s) and safety key; wait for all moving parts to stop before working under deck.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fix: Walk-behind: tighten loose wheel hardware, replace damaged wheel. Zero-turn: check drive wheel pressures and add air; inspect castor wheels and replace if damaged. A damaged/low tire makes deck sit lower on that side.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: worn blade tears grass tips; lawn turns brown a day or two after; on wide multi-blade decks worn blades leave a strip between blades.
- fix: Sharpen with file if still good. Replace if extremely worn, gouged, rounded ends, or sail thinner than center.
- fact: Balance: equal weight both ends; unbalanced by sand wear or by removing more metal from one end when sharpening; balancer or nail 3/4" to 1" out of wall, remove metal from end hanging lower.
- confirm: Bent blade: remove and lay over a new RYOBI blade; even slight bend causes bad cut and vibration.
- mistake: Never straighten a bent blade (weakened; can break and fly out, injuring people, pets).
- tip: Walk lawn before mowing to remove rocks, stumps, toys.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail (high side that creates air movement to toss grass) must face up. Facing the ground can damage the blade motor, beats and tears grass, may hit high spots.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- fact: Blade not tight won't rotate, so mower won't cut. Tighten blade bolt to torque spec in operator's manual (no value given).

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- fact: Deck set too low can't handle the grass. Raise deck for thick tall grass to avoid overworking motor. Double or triple cut: first cut at highest setting, lower for each additional pass.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Plugged deck lacks airflow area; deck and blade create suction lifting grass. Also wears the motor. Clean with deck scraper or wire brush; avoid cutting wet grass.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Adjust ground speed to conditions; too fast pushes grass over; thick/tall/wet grass heavily loads; motor can overload and shut off.

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- fact: Remove battery pack(s) and safety key before working under deck.
