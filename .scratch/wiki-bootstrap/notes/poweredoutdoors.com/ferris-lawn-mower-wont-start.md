## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: won't start when it can't get air, fuel and spark: old fuel, clogged fuel components, dirty carb, plugged air filter, bad plug/ignition switch/coil, bad battery, faulty charging, bad fuel cap.
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, faulty-charging-system, incorrect-starting-procedure
- safety: remove ignition key, unplug spark plug wires, set parking brake before work. Gas-powered Ferris only; diesel has a separate guide.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- symptom: key turned and mower doesn't start or even turn over: ignition switch or coil; bad battery; safety switch.
- causes-named: bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: may have forgotten to refill or developed a leak. Check level and leaks; fill with fresh fuel.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- brand: Ferris requires unleaded, min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol.
- fact: gas can break down in as little as 30 days; ethanol and moisture separate and sink to tank bottom, degrade fuel system, gum up, cause blockages, make engine run hot.
- tip: if not used in 30 days add Sea Foam to stabilize and reduce moisture.
- fix: drain with a fuel siphon, add fresh fuel with additive.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: remove cap and start; if it runs, reinstall and keep running; shutdown suggests plugged cap.
- fix: try cleaning the cap to get it to vent; if that fails buy a new Ferris cap.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: remove, clean the tip; very dark, burnt electrode or broken porcelain: replace. Check carbon buildup/cracked porcelain; correct gap; wires secure.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: dirty or missing filter risks engine damage including overheating, costly repair or engine replacement.
- tip: new filter each season; check more in dry dusty conditions.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: PAPER filter:
- step 1: remove from housing.
- step 2: wipe housing with dry clean rag; no dirt into intake.
- step 3: tap against solid surface; do not use compressed air (damages filter).
- step 4: hold up to light.
- step 5: light visible: reuse; else replace; reinstall and attach cover.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace; arrow on the side points in direction of fuel flow: toward the carburetor and away from the tank.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fact: usually from running old fuel; gummy deposits.
- confirm: fuel shut-off valve at the bottom of the tank or pinch pliers; with flow stopped remove the end furthest from tank into a container placed lower than the tank (fuel cannot flow uphill without a pump); start flow and check.
- fix: carb cleaner, compressed air, repeat; replace if not cleared. Replace lines if dry and cracking even with no clog.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: old gas degrades pump; can't build pressure.
- confirm: check for leaks; verify flow to pump; disconnect hose from carb into container, start; need constant or pulsating flow; else replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fix: clean with carb cleaner incl. float bowl and needle (mechanically inclined), or local small engine mechanic, or replace if too dirty.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter about 12.7 volts; charge if lower; replace if no hold.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: baking soda solution of 2 cups water and 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda; wire brush.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: switches block start without parking brake engaged; one disengages the deck and shuts off if operator absent; consult manual for others.
- confirm: test with multimeter; temporary bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: never operate with a switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: multimeter test; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: after plug verified, check continuity with multimeter; replace on break.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: contributes to weak battery; bad stator or alternator possible; test with ohmmeter.
- pro: Ferris dealer or small engine shop; guessing is costly since electrical parts are usually non-returnable.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Ferris safety features require specific start steps; read operating manual.
