## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Cub Cadet won't move forward or reverse"
- alias: "engine starts but mower isn't moving"
- causes-named: brake-hung-up (simple: brake left on), bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: can be as simple as leaving the brake on or forgetting the transmission valve was bypassed to push the mower; transmission bypass lever engaged; pump belt loose, worn or broken; tensioner pulley worn; spring stretched or missing; hydraulic oil hot, old or low; air trapped in hydraulic system
- safety: follow operator's manual safety instructions before work
- fact: non-transmission items if engine sputters or shuts off so you can't drive: battery and charging system, plugged fuel filter and fuel lines, plugged air filter, dirty carburetor

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: Cub Cadet hydrostatic mowers use transmission fluid pumped to turn the wheels; a transmission bypass lever lets operator push the mower manually; must be disengaged afterwards or the wheels won't be driven (problems/mower-wont-move)
- fact: zero-turn has a bypass lever per transmission (left and right); riding mower/lawn tractor has one bypass lever at the rear

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- confirm: check belt hasn't fallen off and sits correctly on pulleys; worn, cracked or broken belt must be replaced
- fact: a belt that can't grip the pulley won't drive forward or reverse

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: tensioner pulleys often hard plastic with a bearing in the center; pulley can break or bearing fail; replace with a new pulley
- tip: keep the tensioner arm greased so it moves and doesn't seize

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: idler spring puts tension on the drive belt; replace if broken, missing or stretched

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: some Cub Cadet riding mower transmissions are sealed and unserviceable (cannot change hydraulic oil); check operator's manual whether regular service is required
- fact: serviceable transmissions need hydraulic oil changes at Cub Cadet's intervals; first change comes at a sooner interval than later ones
- fix: when low, add hydro oil until fluid reaches full level while hydraulic oil is cool; also check for leaks as a reason for low level
- fact: old or low oil can make mower not move or seem very weak

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- fact: bad or low fluid can't lubricate efficiently, raising friction and overheating; hot fluid can cause more extensive damage
- pro: take to Cub Cadet dealer if hydraulic pump leaks; if mower runs fine cold but stops when hot, have experienced mechanic check transmission

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: must bleed air after changing hydraulic fluid
- fix (most Cub Cadet mowers): put mower on a flat surface; chock front wheels; raise rear drive tires off the ground and let them spin forward and backward until there's no excessive noise and wheels move at normal speeds; follow operator's manual for model-specific steps

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1: place mower on flat surface
- step 2: secure front wheels with wheel chocks to prevent rolling forward
- step 3: raise rear drive tires off the ground
- step 4: let them spin forward and backward until no excessive noise and wheels move at normal speeds
- tip: refer to operator's manual for model-specific steps

## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- fact: only on serviceable transmissions; intervals per Cub Cadet; first change sooner; check level with oil cool; check for leaks; must purge air afterwards
