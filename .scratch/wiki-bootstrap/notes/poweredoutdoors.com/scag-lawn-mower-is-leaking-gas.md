## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "smell of gas in my storage area"
- symptom: gas smell; fuel evaporates so may leave no sign besides the smell
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, cracked-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal
- safety: ventilate the area before working; gas fumes are dangerous
- pro: if unsure, SCAG dealer or repair shop

## page: causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat
- fact: old gas sitting in the carb leaves varnish/gummy deposits that make small parts stick so gas runs out
- symptom: gasket (between bowl and carb, like a rubber band) dries, becomes brittle from heat/cool cycles next to the engine
- fix: clean around carb; remove screw at bottom of bowl (rag for gas); lower bowl; swap gasket; reinstall bowl, tighten screw
- symptom: leak near the air intake port after gasket fixed or ruled out: stuck float (float regulates fuel into bowl; stuck means overflow)
- fix: disassemble and repair/clean float; may need rebuild kit or replacement
- symptom: float fine but float needle stuck (works with float to keep gas flowing into bowl)
- fix: rebuild the carb; temporary: tap with rubber mallet or rubber tool handle (works once or twice), eventually replace float needle

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-filter
- symptom: old fuel degrades/softens plastic housing, leaks at seams
- fix: replace; careful with soft plastic, ends can break off in the fuel line
- tip: replace annually during routine service

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- symptom: degrades like the filter; check seams
- fix: replace on a leak

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-tank
- fact: SCAG tanks are high-density polyethylene, can leak at seams
- fix: best to replace tank

## page: causes/bad-shutoff-valve
- fact: SCAG shut-off valve often behind the seat
- fix: replace when leaking (valves prone to leak with time)

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-line
- symptom: older lines dry, crack; leaks from cracks and punctures around hose clamps
- confirm: follow line from tank to carburetor
- fix: replace leaking line (match diameter); replace dry/cracked lines before they leak; replace pinch-style clamps with worm gear clamps (less likely to puncture)

## page: causes/bad-gas-cap-seal
- symptom: seal dries out; fuel sloshes out around cap in operation; evaporates leaving only fumes
- confirm: carefully rock the mower back and forth to splash fuel around cap; wet area outside the tank means replace cap
