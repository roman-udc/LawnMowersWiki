## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "click, click, click when I turn the key"
- alias: "Kubota mower clicks but won't turn over"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor, faulty-charging-system
- fact: Clicks and won't turn over when wires/cables are loose or corroded, battery weak, ground bad, starter solenoid bad, or starter motor failed.
- safety: Always disconnect the negative (black) battery cable before electrical repairs. Consult a mechanic if not comfortable with the electrical system.
- pro: Charging system diagnosis: have a mechanic familiar with it test.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fact: Check cables/wiring/terminals before the battery; vibration loosens them. Verify battery-to-solenoid and solenoid-to-starter-motor cables are secure. Corrosion interferes with continuity.
- fix: Replace cables/wiring that keep coming loose or are broken. Disconnect the battery and remove corroded components; clean with a wire brush and baking soda mix (2 cups water, 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda). Repair/replace corroded wiring. Add dielectric grease to protect terminals and wiring. Replace damaged terminals.
- tip: Keep the mower dry and store in a dry location to reduce corrosion.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Weak battery may not turn over the engine.
- confirm: Multimeter red/black probes to same-color terminals. On a 12-volt battery, reading is 11.5-12.7; near 11.5 is almost dead, 12.7 is fully charged.
- fix: Charge on a battery charger; if it won't hold a charge replace it. If it charges but keeps dying, check the charging system.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Wear safety gear (eyes and skin; acid or electrical shock).
- step 2: Get access to the battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed to uncover); leave the battery in its casing with terminal cables attached.
- step 3: Connect charger cables red first: red clamp to positive terminal, black clamp to negative.
- step 4: Touch only the rubber coating of the cables and clamps.
- step 5: Set voltage (usually 12 volts) and amps; more amps charges faster; start with two amps and work up to no more than 10 amps.
- step 6: If the charger has a gauge, keep it connected until fully charged.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- confirm: Check the black ground cable from the battery to the frame: good contact, free of corrosion. Also check the solenoid ground; a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded.
- fix: Replace a damaged ground cable; remove corrosion at battery and solenoid grounds.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Clicking or humming and no start, once battery, cables and connections are good.
- fact: Solenoid is an electromagnetic switch actuated to engage the starter motor. Most are mounted on the starter but needn't be; follow the positive wire from the battery to find it. Failure causes: internal spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; a bad ground, weak starter or bad battery can also kill it.
- confirm: Test with a volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches. If the mower starts when bypassing the solenoid, replace it.

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches.
- fact: Bypass starts the mower = bad solenoid. (Source links a separate Kubota solenoid test.)

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: If battery, cables, wiring, ground and solenoid are good and it still won't start, suspect the starter; can be removed and tested.
- pro: Starters are pricey; have the local dealer confirm before replacing, or take the starter to a repair shop specializing in starters/alternators to test.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Bad charging system won't keep the battery charged, causing a weak battery that won't start the mower. Check with a volt-ohm meter. Could be bad stator/alternator, regulator or other electrical problem.
- pro: Hard to troubleshoot; mechanic recommended; wrong electrical parts can't be returned.
