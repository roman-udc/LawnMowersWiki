## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "mower won't turn over, just humming or clicking"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor, bad-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Clicking or humming when key turned and mower doesn't start; a wire gets hot and smokes or melts.
- fact: Solenoid is an electromagnet on/off switch engaging the starter motor. Usually mounted to the starter motor but may be frame-mounted closer to the battery. Find it by following the red positive cable from the battery. Round or square, 3 or 4 posts; some attach at the top of the starter. Wiring schematics vary (may include lights, 12-volt ports).
- fact: Inside: spring and copper plate; fails if spring weakens or copper plate corrodes. Can also fail due to weak starter, bad battery, or bad ground.
- confirm: Likely bad when starter turns the engine over with the solenoid bypassed.
- mistake: Before replacing, check for loose or corroded wires or bad ground.

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- safety: When working on the electrical system, remove the black (negative) cable first, then the red, from the battery before repairs. Follow Kubota operator's manual.
- tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches, needle-nose pliers (if screwdriver doesn't work), battery charger (optional).
- step 1: Battery full charge: at least 12.3 volts on voltmeter.
- step 2: Set parking brake, neutral, key on.
- step 3: Bypass: lay a long screwdriver across the solenoid touching the battery cable and the starter cable; spark is common; if the engine turns over it is probably bad; needle-nose pliers can be used instead.
- step 4: Bench test for starter-mounted solenoid: remove starter; charger negative (-) clamp to starter case, positive (+) clamp to the big post and exciter wire on the solenoid.
- safety: Always wear safety gear.
