## page: procedures/replace-the-deck-belt
- fact: steps are a guide for most Craftsman models; consult the model-specific operator's manual.
- parts: buy the correct belt; read the part number off the old belt or look up by model number at SearsPartsDirect.com. Auto parts store matches perform worse and last shorter than OEM belts; wrong belt type can be a safety problem.
- fact: PUSH MOWER
- tools: screwdriver set, ratchet or socket wrench set, block of wood, work gloves, safety glasses.
- step 1: work somewhere good; author prefers push mower on a workbench (secure it so it can't fall); ground is fine. Let a recently run engine cool about 10-15 minutes to avoid burns and fumes.
- step 2: gas push mower: unplug spark plug boot and seal the fuel tank so gas doesn't seep when tipped: tape packaging tape around cap and the cap vent hole, or lay a thin sandwich bag over the opening and tighten the cap on it. Battery push mower: remove the battery.
- step 3: tilt mower back on its handle; secure the handle (strap to workstation or weight with something soft and heavy). Wedge a block of wood between blade and deck to keep the blade from moving.
- step 4: with glove, hold the blade where the block wedges; loosen blade bolt; remove bolt, blade and blade support from the crankshaft.
- step 5: remove bottom drive cover screws and debris shield bolts; remove shield by gently twisting it off the crankshaft pulley.
- step 6: pull one end of the belt off the crankshaft pulley, then remove the crankshaft pulley.
- step 7: release/unstrap handle and stand mower upright; remove drive cover mounting screws on top; if there is a height adjustment lever, release it before pulling the drive cover; remove cover.
- step 8: unhook the keeper securing the belt to the top of the transmission; remove old belt; place new belt over the transmission pulley and through the opening of the deck.
- step 9: reverse order: reattach belt keeper on top of the transmission drive pulley; replace drive cover, reconnect height adjuster lever, screws; tip back and secure handle; wrap new belt around crankshaft end and replace crankshaft pulley (indentations on the pulley must be aligned); roll belt onto pulley so it sits securely; replace debris shield and bolts and bottom drive cover screws; replace blade, blade support and bolt.
- tip: check blade condition before reinstalling; sharpen or replace.
- step 10: remove wood block and bag/tape from fuel tank; reconnect spark plug (gas) or battery.
- fact: RIDING MOWER (takes longer; deck must come off)
- tools: socket wrench set, screwdriver set, pliers, work gloves, safety glasses. OEM Craftsman belt recommended; aftermarket matches cause more problems and can be a safety concern.
- safety: remove ignition key and spark plug wire; lower deck to lowest position; blades disengaged; parking brake set.
- step R1: keep owner's manual handy; diagrams/manuals available at SearsPartsDirect.com by model number. Remove belt from engine pulley, releasing it from its keepers.
- step R2: remove front link support retaining pin and washer under the engine pulley; detach support rod from the deck.
- step R3: left side: remove clip to detach suspension arm; remove clip and washer on left rear deck bracket to release bracket from deck. Repeat on right side.
- step R4: before sliding deck out, pull blade cable (PTO cable) out of the deck bracket by removing the cotter pin; disconnect the blade cable spring from the idler arm. Verify nothing else attaches deck to mower; slide deck out.
- step R5: remove belt covers (hex screws). Note belt routing first (a label may show it; author photographs it). Remove old belt from idler and spindle pulleys; may need to loosen the nut on the left idler pulley.
- step R6: install new belt around outer spindle pulleys then the idler pulleys; retighten left idler nut if loosened; reinstall belt guards.
- step R7: slide deck back under, secure brackets, rods, blade cable and spring; pull the right side of belt onto the PTO pulley, rolling the narrow side of the belt up and into the pulley while slowly rotating the pulley until it slides into place; rotate belt by hand to verify seating in every pulley; reinstall spark plug boots.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: deck belt wears over time and is ineffective turning the blades; failing belt hurts cut quality; owners often notice only when lawn looks worse.
