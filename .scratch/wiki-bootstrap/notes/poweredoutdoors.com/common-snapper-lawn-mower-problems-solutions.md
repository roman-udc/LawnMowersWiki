## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Snapper mower won't start"
- fact: engine isn't getting the air, fuel or spark needed for combustion; check fuel in tank and battery not dead
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter
- fact (table): empty tank: fill with fresh fuel; bad/old fuel: drain tank, refill fresh, use additive like Sea Foam to stabilize and help clean fuel system; clogged fuel-cap vent creates vacuum restricting flow: clean cap/vent, replace if needed; spark plug excessively dirty/broken/bad connection/wrong gap: replace, secure connection, gap to manufacturer spec; plugged air filter (dirt and grass): clean, replace if bad; plugged fuel filter: replace; bad/leaking fuel pump no longer makes pressure: replace; blocked fuel lines: carb cleaner and compressed air, replace line if necessary; dirty carburetor (bad fuel, ethanol deposits): clean components, replace if needed; bad battery/loose cables/corroded terminals: charge, replace if won't hold charge, tighten cables, clean corrosion; bad safety switch: test and replace; bad ignition switch or coil: test and replace bad switch; broken recoil on pull-start: check if it can be restrung or parts (pulley) replaced, replace assembly if needed

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Snapper mower loses power and dies"
- alias: "Snapper dies while mowing"
- fact: mower can die when engine overheats (plugged cooling fins, low oil creating friction and heat, plugged deck adding extra draw on engine), or when fuel/air restriction develops (old fuel clogging fuel system, plugged air filter)
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor
- fact (table): bad fuel: drain, refill fresh, stabilizer like Sea Foam; plugged air filter: clean/replace; cooling fins packed with dirt, oil, grass: clean and replace broken fins; clogged fuel lines: carb cleaner and compressed air; dirty carburetor: clean all components, replace if needed

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- fact: smoke is oil burning off a hot component like the muffler or inside the cylinder, or burning more fuel than air in combustion
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- fact (table): plugged air filter: clean/replace; too much oil in crankcase builds pressure and lets oil into cylinder through valve train: drain a little oil to manufacturer's recommended level; low oil raises friction and extreme heat: take to small engine repair shop to inspect damage, you can try adding oil but often too late; internal problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket): take to small engine repair shop
- pro: internal engine problems go to a small engine shop

## page: problems/engine-loses-power
- fact: (Source heading "Weak Hydrostatic Transmission" is a drive-power issue; see problems/mower-wont-move). Dying-under-load causes listed under engine-starts-then-dies.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Snapper won't move or go forward"
- alias: "weak hydrostatic transmission"
- fact: hydrostatic transmission can feel weak from bad hydraulic belt or pulley, old hydraulic oil, or low oil in hydraulic tank; some transmissions are non-serviceable (sealed, oil can't be changed): take to repair shop or Snapper dealership
- fact: check drive release levers; if not in operating position mower will not move
- causes-named: (won't-move table) drive release lever wrong position, missing-axle-key, worn-drive-belt, bad tensioner pulley (worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing), missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact (table): drive release lever lets hydrostatic mowers roll freely; must be in operating position; riding mower may have small key in the axle that fell out: replace key; drive belt fallen off or worn: replace worn/cracked belt, make sure it is securely around pulleys; tensioner pulley bearing fails: replace; idler arm spring broken/missing can't tension drive belt: replace; low/old hydraulic oil: change at Snapper's recommended intervals and check level periodically; hot hydraulic fluid can signal transmission damage: let cool; if it runs fine cold but weak when hot, have it checked at Snapper dealer; air must be bled after changing hydraulic fluid or the mower can't move at normal speeds: follow Snapper's owner's manual procedure
- fact (weak transmission table): bad drive belt makes pump inefficient: replace frayed, cracked or worn belt; bad tensioner pulley fails to keep belt tension: replace pulley and grease the tensioner arm
- pro: sealed hydraulic systems: Snapper dealership

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, loose-steering-linkage (worn steering parts)
- fact (table): unequal tire pressure pulls to one side: fill to manufacturer's spec, tires on each side need equal pressure; bad dampers on zero-turn make mower jerk to one side going forward: replace damper; zero-turn tracking: when one tire moves faster than the other mower pulls: adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed per owner's manual; worn/loose steering components in riding mower (worn bushings, washers, gears) won't steer one way: inspect and replace failed parts

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: mowers vibrate naturally; vibration intensifies with loose/broken parts, worn linkages, bad clutch or debris; even a tiny item can cause significant vibration; deck causes: unbalanced blades, worn spindle housing and pulley bearings, worn belt, bent brackets
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact (table): loose/missing engine mounting bolts (bolts reduce vibration): secure and replace missing; bad clutch (bearing failure, bearing falls out of clutch; manual clutch bushing and linkages can fail): replace clutch; unbalanced/damaged blades: replace damaged, balance before installing; string or long weeds wrapped around blade shaft: remove, check spindle for damage; bad spindle housing bearing: replace bearing or whole spindle housing assembly; worn pulleys or deck belt: replace; debris lodged anywhere: check deck and mower for stuck items, "don't overlook the small stuff"

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Snapper mower bad or uneven cut"
- fact: not always the deck; a low tire can keep mower from sitting level; check tire pressure first, then deck
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down (blade installed wrong), clogged-deck-chute (clogged deck), wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed (engine speed too low), mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell, operator error
- fact: Source table lists: worn blade, bent blade, blade installed wrong, clogged deck; uneven tire pressures, unleveled deck, bent spindle/bearing, worn deck pulleys or belt; engine speed too low, ground speed too fast, damaged deck shell, operator error

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- fact: strong gas odor in garage/shed; fuel evaporates so may leave no wet spot; leaking gas wastes fuel and kills grass; poor to store a leaking mower in an unventilated area
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-primer-bulb, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact (table): dirty/failed carburetor (old-gas residue): clean and replace damaged parts; old/plugged fuel filter: replace; fuel pump leaks at seams: replace; metal tank rust hole or poly tank seam leak: replace tank, rust hole sometimes repairable if no replacement; metal and plastic shut-off valves prone to leak: replace; rubber fuel lines dry and crack: replace; primer bulb cracks or poorly attached to hose: replace; gas cap seal fails so gas splashes out: replace gas cap

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Snapper blades won't turn on or engage"
- fact: deck belt worn or off; PTO switch bad; clutch failed; manual clutch linkage/cable worn; battery fails to power electric clutch solenoid
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt (worn deck belt), worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact (table): worn deck belt can't grip pulleys: replace if worn, cracked or shiny; belt coming off: worn idler arm and spring may not tension, belt stretched, pulleys not parallel to deck: replace worn parts/stretched belt, check routing; bad PTO switch fails to let the battery energize clutch: replace; bad clutch can't transfer power: replace; worn clutch cable/lever/spring/linkages on manual clutch: replace; weak battery can't power clutch: test and charge if 12 V battery reads less than 12.7 volts; seat switch not sensing operator blocks engagement (Source's solution says "Replace a bad battery" in this row, apparently a typo for the switch); blown fuse: replace with same amperage, have a mechanic find root cause if repeats

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: fuel breaks down over time, becoming less efficient and prone to clog fuel system and cause engine damage
- fix: drain tank, refill with fresh fuel, stabilize with a fuel additive such as Sea Foam

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: vent in cap can clog, tank forms vacuum restricting fuel flow; clean cap and vent, replace cap if needed

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: low or old hydraulic oil fails to lubricate, system seems weak; change at Snapper intervals; check level periodically

## page: causes/missing-axle-key
- fact: riding mower may have a small key in the axle that can fall out of place; mower won't move; replace key
