## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- symptom: May have a fuel leak, a faulty fuel gauge, or you forgot to add fuel.
- fix: Check for a leak; repair/replace faulty parts; add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Unleaded, minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol; always low-ethanol or ethanol-free; high ethanol can cause significant engine damage.
- fix: Drain with a manual siphon pump (inexpensive); add fresh gas plus additive that cleans the fuel system, reduces moisture, stabilizes.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: Old gas is often the main culprit when a Kubota zero-turn runs sluggishly, won't start or dies. Ethanol attracts moisture; leaves varnish and gummy deposits, restrictions, component failure.
- fact: Gas begins to break down as soon as 30 days; buy only what you'll use in 30 days; otherwise stabilize with Sea Foam Motor Treatment (author adds it to all small-engine fuel).
- fix: Drain tank, refill fresh with additive.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Replace once a year; more often after a dirty tank of fuel (better to drain dirty fuel).
- fix: Replace; arrow on filter points in the direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom: Sticky deposits from old fuel narrow the line.
- fix: Remove line, carb cleaner, then compressed air; if it won't clear buy new line of right diameter and length (cut to size). Also replace lines that are dry and starting to crack.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Pump works against gravity to lift fuel to the carburetor. Most Kubota zero-turns use a vacuum-style pump; fuel degrades it over time; replace if it can't draw fuel with engine vacuum or leaks.
- step 1: Shut off fuel at the shut-off valve under the tank or pinch line with clamps.
- step 2: Remove hose from pump inlet port into a container lower than the tank; open fuel flow; flow means pump is getting fuel (no flow: restriction before the pump). Shut off fuel; refit hose.
- step 3: Remove hose from carburetor into a container; open fuel flow and start engine; watch for steady or pulsating flow; then shut off and reattach.
- fix: Replace pump if no constant or pulsating flow.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: Can't regulate air and fuel; old fuel clogs passages and leaves varnish making parts stick.
- fix: Clean, replace stuck/damaged parts; or take to a small engine shop or replace the carburetor assembly.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: Tank vents through the cap on Kubota zero-turn so tank pressure equals outside; plugged vent makes a vacuum.
- confirm: Run with and without cap. If it won't start with the cap on, loosen the cap (you may hear vacuum release); if it then starts, venting problem; tighten and run to see it shut off again and won't restart until cap loosened.
- fix: Replace the fuel cap.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: Loose wires, wrong gap, fouled plug cause intermittent running/starting.
- fix: Inspect for carbon buildup or cracked porcelain; replace if damaged, worn or very dark; otherwise clean tip with small wire brush; gap correctly; secure wires.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Replace annually, check regularly. Plugged filter may cause no start, overheating or smoke.
- step (paper): remove; wipe housing and cap with dry cloth without letting dirt into intake; tap on solid surface; hold to light; reuse if light shows, else replace if no light, very dirty or damaged; reinstall.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: Disconnect battery, remove corroded components, clean with baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda) or cola plus small metal brush; make sure all wiring and components are secure.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fix: Multimeter; under 12.7 V put on charger; replace if it won't hold a charge.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- symptom: Operator presence control system; a defective switch prevents starting.
- confirm: Multimeter test; temporary bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: Never operate or run with a switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: Key turned, fails to start; multimeter test; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Click or hum when turning the key; a wire attached to the solenoid gets hot, smokes or melts.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- symptom: Not the main reason but contributes to a weak battery. Bad stator or alternator among other parts.
- pro: Take to local Kubota dealer; electric parts are expensive and probably non-returnable.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- symptom: Choke restricts airflow for cold starts; wrong lever position can prevent starting.

## page: procedures/start-a-zero-turn-mower
- step 1 (cold start): Choke lever to full choke.
- step 2: Throttle halfway between slow and fast.
- step 3: Turn key to start, release when engine starts.
- step 4: Move choke lever to off.
- step 5: Idle at half throttle a few minutes before going to full throttle to mow.
- fact: Safety interlock: sit in the operator's seat, brake engaged, PTO off. Check model procedure in the operator's manual.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- symptom: Kubota won't start if safety interlock requirements not met (seat occupied, brake engaged, PTO off) or choke wrong.
