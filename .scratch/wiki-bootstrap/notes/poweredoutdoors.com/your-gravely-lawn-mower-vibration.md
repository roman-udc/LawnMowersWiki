## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Gravely mower vibration", "feels like riding a jackhammer"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring (idler bracket/spring), debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: Remove ignition key, disconnect spark plug wires, make sure mower can't move.
- note: Text is nearly identical to the Simplicity vibration article; same facts, Gravely-branded.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: Engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) sit under the engine and absorb some vibration. Tighten loose; replace worn, broken, missing. Push mower: check engine bolts when vibration appears once blade engaged.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: Electric clutch: ball bearing can fail and shake mower. Manual engagement clutch (belts and pulleys): bushing and linkages can fail. Push mower has no clutch; lever engages blade; check lever for loose/worn parts.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Wrong or unbalanced blades vibrate. Author prefers OEM; aftermarket with matching center hole and length may still not fit; lay on OEM blade to compare. Imbalance from normal wear or uneven sharpening; balance with a balancer before installing.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: Wire, string, long weeds; remove, then inspect spindle and housing.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Gloves; rock blade ends up and down; movement or knocking = remove blade and spindle housing, inspect and repair.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: Slack makes belt not seat; replace if stretched, cracked, glazed; if fine, check routing and tension.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Pulley should be flat and parallel; rotate by hand, feel restriction/noise. Some pulleys use only sealed bearings: replace pulley and bearing assembly.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Loose/worn idler bracket and spring (hole in bracket enlarges, spring stretches) = vibration; replace. Also check bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage and replace if worn.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Even a wood chip can cause extreme vibration; debris under pulleys and on the tensioner adds slack. Remove spindle covers, clean deck regularly; clean after each mowing (also prevents corrosion).
