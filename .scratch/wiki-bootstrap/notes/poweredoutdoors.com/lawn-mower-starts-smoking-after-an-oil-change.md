## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "mower starts smoking after oil change"
- causes-named: spilled-oil-on-engine, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- safety: take precautions around the hot engine.

## page: causes/spilled-oil-on-engine
- symptom: smoke rolling off mower soon after an oil change; oil burns off hot engine or muffler.
- fix: find and wipe off the excess oil.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: bluish-white smoke as oil enters the cylinder through the valve train and burns; pressure builds in the crankcase.
- fact: thick smoke can plug the air filter causing running issues; check and clean/replace air filter and spark plug.
- confirm: remove dipstick, wipe clean, reinsert, pull out and read; oil should be within the full lines.
- fix: remove excess: loosen and quickly tighten the drain plug to let a little out; or draw from the oil filter or the oil fill with a turkey baster or oil evacuator; recheck, adjust until within full lines.

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step 1: read dipstick (wipe, reinsert, withdraw) for level.
- step 2: loosen and quickly retighten drain plug to release a little oil, or extract via oil fill/filter area with turkey baster or oil evacuator.
- step 3: recheck dipstick; add or remove a little until within full lines.
- tools: turkey baster or oil evacuator, clean cloth.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- symptom (low oil): friction builds, engine overheats, smoke when heat burns oil and melts parts; engine damage likely.
- pro: have a small engine mechanic look at it.
- fact (wrong oil): wrong viscosity can burn and smoke; always use the engine manufacturer's recommended viscosity.
- fact: use air-cooled engine oil, high in zinc (acts as cooling agent); regular car oil has little zinc because car engines are liquid-cooled.
- fact: most mower manufacturers recommend SAE30 or 10W-30; in higher temperatures may need 20W-50; ambient temperature affects viscosity choice (Kawasaki Motors publishes a viscosity vs temperature chart).

## page: guides/engine-oil-guide
- fact: air-cooled engine oil with high zinc; SAE30 or 10W-30 typical; 20W-50 in higher temps; follow engine manufacturer; Kawasaki chart referenced.
