## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "lawn mower won't turn over"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, bad-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-wiring-or-ground, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fix: start with the battery, then electrical and starter problems
- safety: caution with electrical system to avoid electrocution; follow safety guidelines; professional if unsure

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: voltmeter; fully charged 12 volt reads about 12.7 volts; charge if lower
- fact: leaving the key on and a bad charging system can drain it (links to own article)
- tip: keep charged especially during storage; in cold an uncharged battery can freeze and go bad
- fix: replace if it won't hold a charge; bring old battery to avoid core fee

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed); don't remove from casing
- step 2: positive (red/plus) charger cable first on the positive terminal
- step 3: negative (black) on negative terminal
- step 4: touch nothing that isn't rubber coated
- step 5: 12 volts; start at 2 amps to no more than 10 amps; slow charge best
- safety: protective gear for eyes and skin

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: vibration breaks continuity; moisture corrodes terminals
- fix: baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); secure loose wires; replace damaged or severely corroded wires, connections or terminals

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probe on each prong, resistance; near 0 good, infinity bad
- fix: replace with same amperage; repeated blowing - repair shop or dealer

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: key or toggle switch does nothing; won't turn over
- confirm: multimeter on resistance; prongs "B" (battery) and "S" (starter solenoid); key in start position; probes on B and S; good = near 0 ohms; bad = infinite

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence system prevents start unless conditions met - e.g. brake engaged on ride-on, bail lever held against the handle on push mower
- confirm: test with multimeter; temporary bypass for troubleshooting only
- safety: never operate without safety switch or with it bypassed

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch initiates the starter motor; fails when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can cause failure
- confirm: battery must be fully charged before testing

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid; remove and test
- pro: author recommends a starter/alternator specialist shop test and rebuild before buying a new one (opinion)
