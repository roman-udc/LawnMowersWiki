## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "leaves strips of grass", "scalps my lawn", "bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- tip: Check tire pressure first to avoid unneeded deck adjustments you'd have to reverse.
- safety: Remove ignition key, disconnect spark plug wires, set parking brake.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Low tire sits that side closer to ground, deck uneven. Pressure must be equal both sides; recommended pressure is on the tire sidewall.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: dull blades beat up grass; turns brown a couple of days after; blade ends thin and rounded leave strips between blade passes.
- fact: Sharpen about every 25 hours; more often in sandy or gravel conditions. Replace if ends rounded or sail thinner than center.
- confirm: Bent blade - remove and lay on new blade; gaps = replace. Or measure on flat surface ground to tip, rotate 180 degrees, difference over 1/8" = replace.
- mistake: Never straighten and reuse; weakened, can crack.
- fact: Impact with tree root, rock etc. can also damage spindle; check spindle and housing.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: Sail is the high side creating airflow under deck to lift grass; replace when thinner than center of blade.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Common on newly installed blades (author saw customer cases). Sail/fin must point up to deck. Some blades stamped "BOTTOM" or "THIS SIDE DOWN"; remember "Sail Up". Upside down tears and bruises grass; sail can hit ground and damage spindle housings.
- fact: Hardware: bolt and washer or nut and washer etc.; check manual/parts diagram for right parts and order; concave washer cupped side faces the blade.
- fact: Blades must be balanced (new blades already are); balancer, or nail 3/4" to 1" out from wall; remove metal from the end hanging lower. Unbalanced blades vibrate and damage deck and engine.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Deck uses blade-created airflow to form suction lifting grass; packed deck reduces airflow, bad cut, engine works harder and may shut down.
- tip: Scrape regularly with deck scraper, wire brush or putty knife. Don't cut wet grass. Silicone or Teflon spray on deck underside reduces sticking (won't stop all).

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: Level side to side; front may need to be even with or slightly lower than rear depending on model; see manual. Summary table says level side-to-side and front-to-back.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Gloves, rock blade ends up/down; knocking sound or movement = take apart spindle housing; replace any failed part.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: frayed, cracked, stretched, glazed shiny belt slips on pulleys; can't turn blades at fast speed. Replace.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Pulleys should sit securely and parallel to deck; spin each slowly, feel for restriction, listen for bearing noise; replace. Some pulleys allow bearing replacement; most idlers have sealed bearings so replace whole assembly.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Blade tip speed affects cut; run at full throttle with deck engaged. If power not what it was, small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Thick, tall or wet grass loads the engine; slow down; too fast pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Overlap paths; rushing with spaced paths leaves strips (author's own mistake).

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Hitting fence post, tree, building can tweak deck shell; repair or replace. If unrepairable get price for replacement shell and complete deck assembly, plus labor to swap components.

## unmapped
- tip: Uneven lawn (molehills etc.) and wide decks (52" or 60") scalp; author rolls lawn with heavy roller; small push mower better for wavy areas and ditches. Lawn-care-ish; suggest fold into problems/uneven-cut as minor note.
