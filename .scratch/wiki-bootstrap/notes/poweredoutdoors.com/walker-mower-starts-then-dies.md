## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Walker starts then dies"
- alias: "stops running"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, bad-safety-switch, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-deck-chute
- safety: remove spark plug wire and ignition key before repairs.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol (corn or other high-starch plant) pulls in moisture, corrodes the fuel system; when mixture separates and evaporates leaves gummy deposits.
- tip: min 87 octane; no more than 10% ethanol (E10); avoid E15 and E85; fresh gas used within 30 days; additive; store away from combustibles and dry.
- fix: drain tank with fuel siphon pump into approved gas container; fill with right gas plus stabilizer. Author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment each tank (stabilizes, reduces moisture, cleans fuel system; "safe petroleum-based").

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: inline filter between fuel lines; when plugged mower runs sluggishly and dies. Replace; arrow on housing in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: use fuel shut-off valve to start/stop flow and check each section.
- fix: turn off fuel, remove line, spray carb cleaner, blow compressed air; replace line if can't clear.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: most Walker pumps are vacuum pumps (engine vacuum draws fuel from tank to carburetor); fail from wear or fuel degradation.
- confirm: confirm flow to pump; then turn off fuel, remove line from carburetor into container, turn on fuel, start; steady or pulsating flow = pump works; poor flow = replace.
- brand: Walker with electronic fuel injection pump: use a fuel pressure gauge; spec in operator's manual.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: fuel flows to carb; remove air filter; spray carb cleaner into intake; start; starts, runs fine, then shuts down = carburetor.
- fix: remove and clean, or take to small engine shop.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: dust and grass clippings wear the engine if filter missing; replace annually (average homeowner), more often commercial/heavy use; check before each use and clean (a minute or two); plugged filter can overheat engine.
- step (paper): remove element; wipe housing with clean dry cloth, none into intake; tap on a hard surface; don't use compressed air (damages paper); hold to light; replace if no light or damaged; reinstall and cover.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: plugged fins let engine overheat after running a while. Remove dirt and replace broken fins; clean around block and shroud; heat shield correctly installed for air circulation.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: check oil with dipstick before each use. Low oil -> friction, heat in crankcase, burning oil and parts, overheat, shutdown.
- fix: adding oil rarely fixes it; significant damage likely; experienced small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: excess oil builds crankcase pressure. Remove a little: drain plug, oil filter, or oil fill area with oil evacuator or turkey baster; repeat until correct.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: clean dirty plug with wire brush; replace if very dark or damaged; gap per engine manufacturer; wires secure.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: windings separate and short when hot; ohmmeter continuity; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Walker has an operator presence control system; switches prevent starting unless conditions met like brake engaged. Failing switch causes no turnover. Seat switch, brake switch, PTO switch may be bad.
- confirm: multimeter; or temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only. Check manual for additional switches.
- safety: never operate without or with bypassed safety switch.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: leaving choke on after warm-up makes mower die; set choke lever to open/off.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: mower shuts down and won't restart until you loosen cap to let air in.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: plugged deck strains engine, overheats, shuts down; dull blades magnify. Scrape deck regularly and sharpen blades; deck uses blades and under-deck area to create airflow.
- safety: protect hands, remove ignition key, disconnect spark plug wire.

## page: problems/battery-keeps-dying
- alias: "Walker battery not charging"
- fact: run at high engine speed; do not idle long (engine power needed to charge battery). If battery needs continual charging with good tight battery and wiring, suspect charging system; link to testing article.
- causes-named: faulty-charging-system, bad-battery
- pro: replace bad battery; charging system faults -> experienced mechanic (too many components; electrical parts typically non-returnable).
