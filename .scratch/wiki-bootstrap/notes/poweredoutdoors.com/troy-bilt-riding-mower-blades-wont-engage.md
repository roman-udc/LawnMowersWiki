## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Troy-Bilt blades won't turn on"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- safety: Remove ignition key and spark plug wire(s), wait for parts to stop before reaching under deck.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Cracks, fraying, shiny glazed look; sits deep in pulley grooves; slips so blades don't turn, or spin slower and cut worse (fast blade speed needed for suction).
- symptom: Belt off pulleys: bad pulley, worn tensioner arm, missing spring.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: Arm and spring hold idler pulleys inline; spring can break or stretch; bracket hole can wear; belt vibrates off.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Rotate pulley slowly by hand: resistance or bearing noise; grab each side and rock for play; good pulley sits securely. Replace pulley assembly.

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fix: Usually a knob; check continuity; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: Transfers engine power to blades by engaging drive belt; replace if faulty.

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: Manual clutch models: check lever, cable, spring, bushings, linkages; replace worn.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter ~12.7 V for fully charged 12 V; charge if lower.
- fix: Charge with gear on; battery under hood or seat, in casing; positive (red) first, then negative; touch only rubber; 12 V; 2 amps up to no more than 10; slow charge best. Replace if no hold; core fee typically $15-$25 without old battery.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Seat switch under seat (operator presence); blades won't run without operator. Test with multimeter or bypass temporarily; never run without it.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: Same size fuse; if recurring, Troy-Bilt dealer or shop.
