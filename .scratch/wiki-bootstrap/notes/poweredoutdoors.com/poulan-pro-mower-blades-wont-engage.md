## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Poulan Pro blades won't engage", "blades won't turn on"
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- safety: follow operator's manual; always remove ignition key and spark plug wires; wait for all parts to stop moving.
- pro: repeated blown fuses: service dealer/repair shop for root cause.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- confirm: cracks, wear, fraying, shiny glazed appearance, belt deep in pulley grooves.
- fix: replace; even if blades rotate, reduced speed hurts cut quality (fast blade speed makes the suction that lifts grass).

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Tensioner arm and spring hold idler pulleys in line; a pulley on one side of the bracket, spring on the other. They wear causing the belt to vibrate off. Spring can break or stretch; the bracket hole can wear. Belt-off causes: bad pulley, worn tensioner arm, missing spring.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: rotate each pulley by hand, feel for resistance/bearing noise; grab each side of the pulley and check for rocking play. A good pulley sits securely.
- fix: replace the pulley assembly; you may be able to replace the bearings in a Poulan Pro idler pulley.

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: usually a knob letting battery voltage power the clutch; check continuity; replace if broken.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: transfers engine power to blades by engaging the drive belt; replace worn/faulty clutch.

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: manual-engagement mower has clutch lever and cable; check lever, cable, spring, bushings, linkages; replace worn parts.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Battery powers the clutch solenoid; weak battery can't.
- confirm: multimeter; fully charged 12 V reads about 12.7 V; charge if lower.
- fix: replace if it won't hold a charge (hardware/automotive store or Poulan Pro dealer). Bring old battery; core fee typically $15-$25.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- safety: protective gear for skin and eyes.
- step 1: Access battery (screwdriver to uncover; under hood or seat); don't remove from casing.
- step 2: Charger positive (red/plus) cable on positive terminal first.
- step 3: Negative (black) on negative terminal.
- step 4: Touch only rubber-coated parts.
- step 5: 12 V; start at 2 amps up to no more than 10; slow charge is best.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Seat switch under the seat senses the operator; blades won't engage without operator in seat; bad switch blocks blades.
- confirm: multimeter, or temporarily bypass to identify.
- safety: don't operate without the safety switch installed.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fact: no battery power to clutch may be a blown fuse; replace with the same capacity fuse.
