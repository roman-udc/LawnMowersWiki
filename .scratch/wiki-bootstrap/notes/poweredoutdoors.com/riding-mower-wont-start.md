## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "riding mower won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, wrong-fuel, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, bad-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, blown-fuse, faulty-charging-system, incorrect-starting-procedure
- confirm: before diagnosing verify: fuel shut-off valve open; enough fresh fuel; parking brake or bail lever engaged; PTO switch off; choke closed/on for a cold start.
- confirm (fuel test): remove the air filter, spray carb cleaner into the air intake (author uses carb cleaner, not starter fluid), try to start; if it tries/starts, fuel system problem (dirty carb, clogged fuel filter/lines, fuel pump if fitted); if it does not try, most likely spark plug.
- confirm (air test): inspect filter; choke lever ON = choke plate closed; OFF = plate open (on some models, fast throttle position); if not moving check linkage and cable, free with carb cleaner, replace bad cable.
- confirm (spark test): boot off plug, spark plug tester on wire, other end on plug still installed; crank; glow = spark; no glow: bad plug/coil; electric start also battery, wiring, solenoid.
- safety: always remove ignition key and spark plug wires before work.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: riding mower requires unleaded gasoline minimum 87 octane and ethanol 10 percent or less (often sold as Regular or E10); read the pump decal for octane and ethanol percentage.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: combustion properties best in first 30 days; ethanol attracts moisture causing rough running or no running; gummy substance blocks the fuel system.
- fix: drain and flush the tank; add fresh fuel with stabilizer/cleaner like Sea Foam.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: like a travel mug lid with a clogged air hole.
- confirm: remove the cap, start and run (avoid letting contaminants into the fuel system); if it runs fine, refit the cap and let it run; if it bogs and shuts off, cap is the cause.
- fix: try to clean; otherwise replace.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: riding mower may have one or two plugs; check gap, connection, damage, carbon buildup, cracked porcelain. Replace and gap properly; wires secure.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: fully plugged filter means no start; running an extremely dirty filter can cause extensive engine damage: overheating, and it may draw air and oil out of the crankcase because that's the only air it can find.
- fix: remove and check; tap paper filter on a hard surface; don't use an air compressor; hold to light; reuse if light shows, else replace.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: most riding mowers use a vacuum pump; gas degrades plastic and internals; check for leaks (replace if any). Otherwise test: stop/start flow with the shut-off valve or clamps; confirm fuel to the pump; check for steady or pulsating flow from the outlet; replace if not.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- tip: author replaces the inline filter at the start of every season; it is one of the least expensive service parts.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: clogs not visible; check each section with shut-off valve or clamps.
- fix: carb cleaner then compressed air; replace if dry/brittle or won't clear.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fix: take apart and clean with carb cleaner; replace if too dirty or parts failed (rebuild or new).

## page: causes/bad-battery
- fix: test with a multimeter; need about 12.7 volts; charge if less; replace if it won't hold a charge.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: baking soda solution 2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda; wire brush.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: a switch disengages the deck when the operator leaves the seat; another prevents starting without the parking brake; more per the manual.
- confirm: multimeter, or temporary bypass.
- safety: never operate without a working safety switch; never run with one bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: key does nothing or feels wrong; multimeter; replace.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; a wire on the solenoid gets hot, smokes or melts.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: location varies; start around the battery (under or next to it); may also be behind the dash.
- fix: replace with same amperage; if it keeps blowing go to a shop or dealer for the root cause.

## page: procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse
- fact: same amperage replacement; fuse block near battery or behind dash.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: contributes to a weak battery; bad stator or alternator or other items. Have a shop check; guessing is expensive and parts stores won't take returns on electrical parts.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: special procedures (safety, throttle and choke); refer to the manual.
