## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Source summary: gather supplies and tools, remove hydraulic filters, drain oil, install new filters, fill with fresh hydraulic fluid, remove air from the system. Author: if you can change engine oil you can probably change hydraulic oil and filter.
- fact: a dealer service also checks starting system, safety switches, charging system, belts, pulleys; ask for a general check at scheduling.
- fact: oil: Exmark recommends Exmark Premium Hydro Oil. Mobil 1 15W-50 can substitute (not on Yanmar diesel models) but oil must then be changed twice as often: if Exmark says 500 hours, change every 250 hours with Mobil 1. Lazer Z with Yanmar diesel uses Ruby Tran Fluid. Z-Spray models use 5W-40 synthetic hydraulic oil.
- fact: Quest E-Series and some S-Series (Quest S 42") have sealed, non-serviceable hydraulic systems; contact the Exmark dealer for problems.
- fact: you only change the oil that drains from the filters; never change the oil in the whole hydraulic system unless it is contaminated or has become very hot (doing so can introduce contaminants).
- tools: oil drain pan, new filters, hydro oil, socket (Lazer), jack stands/jacks (rear wheels off ground), cardboard under the mower, rag.
- safety: remove key, engage parking brake, wait for moving parts to stop; engine and transmission can be hot.
- step 1 (Radius): park on a flat surface (cardboard under to catch spills); key out; parking brake on.
- step 2: filters are near each rear wheel behind a guard (skid plate); remove guards and clean around filters first (do not introduce dirt).
- step 3: oil pan under one filter, unscrew, let drain; repeat for the other; take old oil to a recycling center.
- step 4: thin layer of hydro oil on the new filter's rubber seal; install turning clockwise; once it contacts the adapter turn about one more full turn.
- step 5: loosen the vent plug on each transaxle; fill through the expansion reservoir (small plastic tank on top behind the seat) until oil comes out of the vent plugs; tighten plugs; continue filling to the "full cold" line on the reservoir.
- step 6: purge air with drive tires free: raise rear on jack stands so wheels are off the ground; start, about 1/2 throttle; release parking brake; with bypass valve open, slowly move motion control levers forward and reverse about 6 times; close bypass valve and repeat about 6 times; with engine off and hydros cooled check the level and add oil to "full cold". Repeat purge if needed until tires move at normal speed and noise seems right.
- step L1 (Lazer Z E/S/X): park flat (cardboard), key out, brake on; L2: raise rear with jacks so wheels are off ground; L3: remove pump drive belt; L4: clean around the filters; L5: oil pan, unscrew each filter with a socket, drain, mark used oil containers for recycling; L6: thin oil film on seal, install clockwise, torque to 14 ft-lb; L7: slide seat all the way forward, clean around the hydraulic caps on left and right behind the seat, remove caps and fill with Exmark Premium Hydraulic Oil until the dipstick reads full, replace caps; L8: reinstall pump drive belt; L9: purge: start, full throttle, release parking brake, hold speed levers all the way forward for one minute, shut off, let hydros cool, check oil level.
- fact: release of the hydraulic drive for pushing differs per model (article says details "coming soon").

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1 (Radius): rear wheels off ground on jack stands; start at about 1/2 throttle; release parking brake; bypass valve open; levers forward/reverse about 6 times; close bypass; repeat about 6 times; check oil with engine off and cool; add to full cold.
- step 2 (Lazer Z): full throttle; levers all the way forward for one minute; shut off, cool, check level.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Exmark hydro service intervals and quantities (model / series / deck / oil / amount / first change / interval):
  - Viking 36", 48": Exmark Hydro Oil, 2.4 qt, first 250 h, every 500 h.
  - Turf Tracer S 36", 48", 52" and X 52", 60": 2.4 qt, 250 h, every 500 h.
  - Vantage S 36"-60" and X 48"-60": 2.4 qt, 250 h, every 500 h.
  - Quest E 42", 50": 4.8 qt, sealed. Quest S 42": 4.8 qt, sealed. Quest S 50", 60": 4.8 qt, first 75 h, every 400 h.
  - Pioneer E 48", 52" and S 44"-60": 4.8 qt, first 100 h, every 500 h.
  - Radius E/S/X 48", 52", 60": 4.8 qt, first 75 h, every 500 h.
  - Lazer E/S 48"-72" and X 52"-72": 3.2 qt, first 250 h, every 500 h.
  - Lazer Diesel 60", 72": Exmark Hydro Oil, 5.5 qt, 250 h, every 500 h.
  - Lazer Diesel (Yanmar) 60", 72", 96": Ruby Trans Fluid, 15 qt, first 200 h, every 800 h.
  - Staris E 32", 36", 44" and S 48"-60": 3 qt, 250 h, every 500 h.
- brand: hydrostatic drives and filters by model: Quest S-Series Hydro Gear ZT-2200 (no filter listed); Radius E-Series Hydro-Gear ZT-2800; Radius S-Series ZT-3100; Radius S-Series Rear Discharge ZT-3400; Radius X-Series ZT-3400 (all Radius: Hydro Gear filter x2); Lazer Z E-Series and S-Series Parker Unitized Pump 12 cc and wheel motor system; Lazer Z X-Series Parker unitized pump 16 cc; Lazer Z Diesel Kanzaki KPS-18T trans 18 cc pumps and Hydro Gear HGM-18 motors (Lazer: Exmark filter x2); Navigator Hydro-Gear 12 cc pumps, Hydro-Gear H Series motors (Exmark filter x1); older Lazer Z, Z HP, Z AS, Z CT (Exmark filter x1).
- brand/part: Hydro Gear 52114 (spin filter, Radius, qty 2) [from the Source's product link name "Hydro Gear 52114 Spin Filters"]
- brand/part: Exmark 116-0164 (Hydro Filter Lazer, qty 2)
- brand/part: Exmark 109-4180 (25 micron filter; Navigator and older Lazer Z, qty 1)
