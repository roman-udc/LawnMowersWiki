## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Wright mower vibrates and shakes"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade (wrong/unbalanced blades), debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: always some vibration; severe vibration indicates a problem
- safety: remove ignition key and spark plug wires; follow Wright manual

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) under engine absorb much vibration; install new bolt if missing, damaged or worn

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: ball bearing may fail on an electric clutch causing shake; replace the clutch

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: author uses OEM blades; for aftermarket confirm blade length and center hole size, but dimensions alone don't guarantee fit; lay on top of OEM blade for exact match; slight differences can keep the blade from sitting securely on the spindle, hit the deck shell, vibrate and cut poorly
- fact: unbalanced blade is heavier on one end, wobbles, vibrates at high speed; from normal use (dirt wears unevenly) or sharpening removing more metal from one end; balance with a blade balancer before installing

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: wire or string in grass wraps around spindle; inspect and remove; check spindle and housing for damage

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fact: impact may bend spindle, or housing bearings wear
- confirm: remove spark plug wire; gloves; grab each end of blade and rock up and down; should feel secure; movement or knocking: remove blade and spindle housing, inspect bearing or spindle

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: replace if stretched, cracked or glazed; otherwise check routing and tension

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: pulleys securely attached with no wobble; one side higher than other suggests bad bearing; rotate slowly by hand, feel restriction, listen for noise; replace bearing

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: idler bracket hole where spring attaches wears larger; spring stretches; replace; check bearing and bushing in engagement linkage

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: wood chip-sized debris can cause extreme vibration; remove spindle covers and clean deck regularly; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat; buildup affects tensioner leaving slack
- tip: clean after each mowing (prevents vibration and corrosion from moisture in debris)
