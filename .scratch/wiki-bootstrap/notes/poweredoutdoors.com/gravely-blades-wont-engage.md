## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "I pull the PTO switch and nothing happens"
- alias: "blades won't spin"
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- safety: never reach under the deck until you remove the ignition key, remove spark plug wire(s) and wait for parts to stop moving

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: worn belt slips; blades won't turn or turn slowly
- confirm: look for cracks, fraying, shiny glazed look; belt sitting deep in the pulleys is also wear
- fix: replace the belt; belt grip affects blade speed, and fast blade speed creates suction under the deck for an even cut
- fact: if belt fell off, look for stretched belt, bad pulley, worn tensioner arm or missing spring

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: tensioner arm and spring hold the idler pulleys in line; pulley one side of bracket, spring other
- symptom: worn spring hole in bracket or worn spring lets belt loosen and vibrate off pulleys

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: bearing wear lets pulley wobble, one side higher off the deck; belt rolls off
- fix: replace pulley with bad bearing

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: usually a knob that lets battery voltage power the clutch
- confirm: check continuity
- fix: replace switch with a break in continuity

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: PTO clutch transfers engine power to the blades by engaging the drive belt
- fix: replace a worn or faulty clutch

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: electric clutch uses battery power; weak battery can't power the clutch solenoid
- confirm: multimeter; fully charged 12-volt battery reads about 12.7 volts; charge if lower

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- tools: battery charger, protective gear, screwdriver
- step 1: access battery (under hood or under seat); screwdriver may be needed; don't remove battery from casing
- step 2: connect charger positive (red/plus) first to positive terminal
- step 3: connect negative (black) to negative terminal
- step 4: don't touch anything without rubber coating
- step 5: set 12 volts; start at 2 amps up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge best
- fact: if it won't hold a charge replace it; bring the old battery, core fee averages $20

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: seat switch under the seat senses operator; blades won't turn without operator in the seat
- confirm: test with multimeter, or temporarily bypass for diagnosis
- safety: do not operate without the safety switch installed

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- symptom: no battery power to the clutch
- fix: replace with same-amperage fuse
- pro: if fuses keep blowing, take to Gravely dealer or repair shop
