## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Exmark bad or uneven cut / strips of uncut grass / brown tips after mowing"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, wrong-tire-pressure, blade-installed-upside-down, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: table in Source (cause -> fix): worn blade -> sharpen/replace; low tire pressure -> correct pressure; bent blade -> replace; blade incorrectly installed -> sail point upward; clogged deck -> scrape; deck not level -> level side-to-side and front-to-back; spindle/housing damage -> replace; bad belt or pulleys -> replace; engine speed low -> full throttle; ground speed too fast -> adjust; incorrect paths -> overlap; deck shell damaged -> repair/replace.
- fact: check tire pressures FIRST (before deck adjustments) since low tire shifts deck height; skipping may cause unnecessary deck adjustments.
- brand: Exmark known for cut quality; patented baffle system controls airflow (patent lawsuit won against a major manufacturer).
- fact: uneven lawn can cause scalping (high spots, shallow ditches); wide decks can't follow dips; use a smaller deck like a push mower for ditches.
- pro: leveling best done at Exmark dealer (author's preference); engine power loss at full throttle -> dealer/small engine shop.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact worn: dirt and debris wear blades dull and thin (normal). Thick turf wears blades slower than thin turf with exposed dirt. Look for strips of uncut grass or brown tips a couple of days after mowing. Sharpen when dull; replace when sail is thinner than the center of blade.
- confirm bent method 1: remove blade and lay on a new blade; no bends/gaps = not bent.
- confirm bent method 2: park on flat level surface; measure ground to blade tip on one side; rotate 180 degrees; measure other side; more than 1/8" difference = replace. Repeat for each blade.
- fact: bends come from hitting rock, stick, toy; walk yard first; even slight bend gives uneven cut.
- safety: never attempt to straighten a bent blade - weakens metal, can break off and fly out of deck.
- tip: before removing blade, check spindle housing: work gloves; rock blade ends up and down; play or knocking = remove spindle housing and check bearing/spindle.
- fact: unbalanced blade: sharpened blades must be balanced (new blades need not); use blade balancer; or hang blade on nail sticking out of wall 3/4" to 1" and remove metal from the end that hangs lower until level. Unbalanced blade damages spindle assembly and strains engine; causes vibration.

## page: procedures/measure-a-mower-blade
- step: method 2 above (1/8" difference limit; measure ground to tip, rotate 180).

## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- fact: balance sharpened blades; never straighten bent blades; torque 50-60 ft-lb with wrench, no impact gun (over-tightening damages spindle); hardware: some Exmark blades use washer and bolt, others washer, bolt and splined bushing.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- confirm: gauge in each drive tire; same pressure in each; fill to tire manufacturer's pressure on sidewall; add air as needed.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: blade sails facing the ground tear grass (uneven cut) and impact ground damaging spindle housings. Install with sail (high side) upward ("Sail Up!"). Exmark OEM blades have part number on the bottom of blade; some aftermarket blades stamped "BOTTOM" or "THIS SIDE DOWN".

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Exmark uses the area under deck and air from blades to create suction that stands grass up for even cut; plugged deck reduces air space; also strains engine.
- tip: scrape deck regularly; don't mow wet; silicone or Teflon spray on clean deck, let dry before using (reduces buildup, doesn't prevent all, reduces scraping frequency).

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: use owner's manual specs; Exmark has many deck models/series with different measure/adjust steps; level side-to-side and correct rake front-to-rear.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: heavy gloves; rock blade up and down; knocking or movement; remove spindle housing and inspect; bad spindle/bearing makes blade sit unlevel leaving gaps between blade tips.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: worn/loose belt can't drive blades at speed. Inspect for shiny glazed look, fraying, cracking; replace. Author uses OEM belts only.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: hand spin each pulley; restriction or bearing noise = replace; pulley not flat/parallel to deck = replace.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: run full throttle with deck engaged.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: thick, tall or wet grass loads mower; slow down.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: fewer passes finish sooner but leave uncut strips if paths not overlapped.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: damaged deck shell (hit an obstruction); repair or replace; ask dealer to quote complete deck assembly too since labor to swap components may make new deck worthwhile.
