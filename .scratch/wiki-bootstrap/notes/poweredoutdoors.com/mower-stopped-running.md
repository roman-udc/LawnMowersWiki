## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "mower stopped running"
- fact: stops when air, fuel or spark is missing; reasons ordered easiest/most common to harder
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, engine-overheats

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: fill with unleaded, 87 octane or higher (regular; shown on pump label), no more than 10% ethanol

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: shelf life about 30 days; stabilizer (Sea Foam) if it can't be used within 30 days; small engine makers recommend no more than 10% ethanol; ethanol-free is best for fuel system longevity
- fix: drain tank, fill with fresh gas plus Sea Foam (cleaner and stabilizer)

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: engine can't get clean air, hunts for it wherever it can, may overheat and pull air and oil out of the crankcase
- step (paper): remove filter; clean cloth wipe of housing without dirt into intake; tap on solid surface; hold to light, replace if no light visible
- step (foam): remove without knocking dirt into intake; wipe housing; wash with dish soap removing dirt/oil; rinse until soap gone, air dry; lightly coat with filter oil, squeeze out excess (don't oversaturate); replace if dark spots or dry and brittle

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: remove cap and let mower run; if it runs well, replace cap and see if it goes sluggish or shuts off
- fix: replace cap if vent can't be unclogged

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: gummy hard deposits from bad fuel; dirty carb can't let fuel flow through the stem to the jet
- confirm: spray carb cleaner into air intake and start; if it won't stay running, clean or replace

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace dirty clogged filter

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: clamps to stop fuel flow, remove lines to check flow
- fix: carb cleaner then compressed air; replace dry/cracked or uncleared lines (take correct diameter and length to hardware store or dealer)

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: smoke; smoke can clog the air filter so the engine seeks air elsewhere and stops; can cause internal engine damage, even engine replacement
- fix: remove excess oil down to the dipstick fill line per manufacturer
- tip (methods): drain plug: loosen then quickly retighten to let a little out; remove the oil filter (spin off, cloth ready); oil evacuator through the fill tube; turkey baster (don't reuse for food)

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step: drain plug method: loosen the plug and quickly tighten so only a small amount drains
- step: oil filter method: spin off the filter, have a cloth to catch oil
- step: oil evacuator: vacuum oil via tube in the oil fill
- step: turkey baster: sucks a small amount; don't use for food afterwards

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- symptom: low oil means friction and heat that can melt internal parts; engine stops
- confirm: look for oil leaks
- fix: changing oil/filter is possible but if engine overheated and stopped, adding oil probably won't help; likely internal damage
- pro: small engine repair shop to inspect for internal damage

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- fact: any overheat stops the mower: wrong oil type, clogged engine fins or housing, engine working harder due to cutting conditions; author links 7 causes overheating article
