## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Simplicity zero turn won't move forward or reverse / moving slowly"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- safety: Follow operator's manual; remove spark plug boot(s) and ignition key.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- fact: Simplicity has two bypass levers (left and right transmission) near each drive wheel. Must be in DRIVE/CLOSED position; PUSH/OPEN position lets it free-roll but won't drive. Transmission pressure is so great the mower can't be pushed without bypassing.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fact: Check belt hasn't fallen off and is positioned on pulleys; replace worn, cracked or broken belt.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Tensioner pulleys often hard plastic with center bearing; replace bad pulley; keep tensioner arm greased so it can move.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: Idler spring tensions the belt; replace if broken, missing or stretched.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- brand: Some Simplicity zero-turn transmissions are sealed and unserviceable (no oil/filter change); see operator's manual. If non-serviceable but oil tanks accept fill, add oil to each tank (left and right) to FULL COLD level.
- brand: Serviceable: change per Simplicity intervals; first change sooner. Simplicity recommends 20W-50 oil in the transmissions. Change hydraulic oil and filter after the first 100 hours, then every 400 hours.
- fix: When low, add oil to FULL COLD level with oil cool; check for leaks.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- fact: Bad or low fluid can't lubricate, overheats; can cause more extensive damage.
- pro: Simplicity dealership if hydraulic pump leaks; experienced mechanic to check the transmission if mower runs fine cold but stops when hot.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: Bleed air after changing or adding fluid.

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1: Flat surface; chock front wheels so mower can't roll forward.
- step 2: Raise rear drive tires off ground on jack stands.
- step 3: Put bypass levers in PUSH/OPEN.
- step 4: Start engine and release parking brake.
- step 5: Move speed levers forward and backward about 6 times.
- step 6: Stop engine, engage parking brake.
- step 7: Close bypass levers to DRIVE/CLOSED.
- step 8: Move speed levers forward and backward about 6 times.
- step 9: Repeat as needed until no excessive noise and wheels move at normal speed. Air removal lowers oil level: check and top up.
- fact: See operator's manual for model-specific steps.

## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- brand: Simplicity: 20W-50; oil and filter at first 100 hours then every 400 hours; FULL COLD level.
