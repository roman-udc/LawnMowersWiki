## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "John Deere mower isn't getting gas"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Bad fuel has clogged the system, causing restrictions in filter, lines, pump or carburetor.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- fact: Dirty carburetor test: if it starts on carb cleaner and won't keep running, carb needs cleaning; cap test (run with/without cap) for fuel starvation.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: Fuel sitting a long time: ethanol and moisture separate, sticky deposits cause blockages and premature fuel component failure. Same moisture/sediment issues apply to a diesel-powered John Deere mower.
- fix: Siphon the tank into a gasoline-rated container (plastics degrade quickly and leak); take old fuel to an authorized recycling center; refill with gas plus stabilizer (author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment: stabilizes up to two years, cleans fuel system).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Replace annually at routine service.
- fix: Replace; arrow on the plastic filter must point in the direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: Start/stop fuel flow with the shut-off valve at the bottom of the tank (or hose pinch-off pliers); check each section by starting/stopping flow to see if fuel still runs out.
- fix: Remove clogged section; carb cleaner to loosen; blow out with compressed air; repeat; replace if you can't clear it or it's dry and cracked.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Used when the carburetor sits higher than the tank. Most use a vacuum pump that builds pressure off the crankcase.
- step 1: Confirm fuel reaches the pump: stop fuel flow, remove line from pump inlet port into a container lower than the tank, restart flow; fuel out = flow confirmed; if not, find blockage in lines/filter.
- step 2: Reattach line to inlet; remove line from carburetor into a container; start fuel flow and start the mower; expect steady or pulsating flow; if not, replace pump.
- fix: Replace if cracked or not working.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: Remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into the air intake, start; if it starts and won't continue to run, remove and take carb apart to clean. Dirty carb clogs small parts including the fuel jet.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: Run mower with and without the cap; then reinstall and see if the fuel issue returns.
- fix: Author replaces a clogged-vent cap so the problem doesn't return; cleaning is possible.
