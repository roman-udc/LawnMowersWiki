Note: Hustler lineup per Source: walk-behind, zero-turn, stand-on; mostly gas, some diesel models (article covers gas only).

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Gas Hustler mowers use unleaded gasoline, minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol. All use four-stroke engines requiring unleaded.
- brand: Hustler; diesel models exist - check owner's manual if unsure of fuel type.
- fact: Ethanol is plant-based, added to make gasoline more environmentally friendly; okay in most vehicles but not good for small engines. Never use E15 (15%) or E85 (85%) in a small engine. Lower ethanol is better.
- fact: Ethanol attracts moisture; solution separates from gasoline and sinks to the tank bottom; can cause significant engine damage when it runs through the mower.
- fact: Old fuel leaves gummy residue that degrades/clogs fuel system components, causing sluggish running or no run.
- fact: Ethanol-free gas is even better (does not break down like ethanol fuels); more expensive in canisters at hardware stores; cheaper at fuel stations; sometimes sold as recreation fuel or REC-90; find stations at pure-gas.org.
- tip: buy only a month's worth; many problems come from wrong gas or stale gas. Store in a dry area to keep water out.
- tip: author buys best quality low-ethanol gas if affordable.
- brand: author's own practice: 10% ethanol unleaded plus fuel additive.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Gas can begin to break down as soon as 30 days after purchase; buy only what you can use within a month; stabilize if more.
- fact: Author prefers Sea Foam Motor Treatment: stabilizes fuel for up to two years (per author), reduces moisture, acts as fuel system cleaner. Use when storing excess fuel and when storing the mower with fuel over the off-season. Add to each tank in case fuel isn't used before breaking down.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Many Hustler problems stem from wrong type of gas or stale gas (see guide above).

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breakdown from ~30 days; gummy deposits clog fuel system.
