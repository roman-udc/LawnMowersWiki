## page: guides/winterizing-and-storage
- fact: Winterize to protect the fuel system, battery and wiring harness; remove debris and moisture to prevent rust and corrosion of deck, electrical components and other parts.
- tools: socket or spanner wrench set; rags/paper towel; gas container (if emptying tank); fuel stabilizer; rubber and work gloves; engine oil and filter (per owner's manual); oil drain pan; filter wrench; air filter, fuel filter, spark plug; hydraulic oil and filter if needed; deck scraper or wire brush.
- step 1: Change engine oil and filter if dirty or about 50 hours since last change (see procedure below).
- step 2: Change hydraulic transmission oil and filter if the system is serviceable (not sealed; non-serviceable often on entry-level mowers) and the interval is reached; see manual.
- step 3: Disconnect spark plug boot(s) so the engine can't start while working under deck.
- step 4: Replace spark plug(s) (may be more than one), air filter, fuel filter (or confirm air filter reusable).
- step 5: Inspect and scrape the deck (details below).
- step 6: Fuel: gas breaks down over 30 days; don't store untreated fuel. Option 1: siphon out as much as possible, run the mower until it shuts off. Option 2 (author's choice): add stabilizer (Sea Foam Motor Fuel Treatment) per bottle directions; run engine a few minutes to circulate; these additives stabilize fuel for up to 2 years. Untreated gas attracts moisture and gums the system.
- step 7: Battery so it doesn't freeze. Option 1: fully charge (a fully charged battery will not freeze). Option 2: remove and store in cool dry place away from combustibles: gloves and eye protection; remove brackets/tie-downs; adjustable wrench turn black negative terminal counterclockwise to remove first, then red positive; clean corrosion with wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda).
- step 8: Wash: garden hose; pressure washer OK but not high pressure around deck and bearings (water pressure can damage bearings). Dry fully (sun, light breeze, leaf blower); never store wet; moisture in electrical components increases corrosion and failed parts.
- step 9: Lubricate cables; grease all grease zerks.
- step 10: Store in a cool dry place; tarp or drop cloth indoors for dust; waterproof (marine-grade) cover if outdoors.
- step 11: Rodent prevention: wires are coated with soy-based material rodents chew; author has seen harnesses damaged by mice/squirrels. Uses Grandpa Gus's Mouse Repellent (pouches in mower, spray on wires), says safer around pets and better smelling than mothballs; cheap compared with electrical repair.
- mistake: Don't just push it into a corner and ignore until spring.

## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- step 1: Park on flat surface (newspaper/cardboard under it). step 2: warm oil by running a couple of minutes. step 3: shut off, remove key, disconnect spark plug wire(s); careful with hot engine. step 4: remove drain plug, drain into pan. step 5: replace oil filter. step 6: replace drain plug, refill to full per manufacturer; don't overfill or underfill (extensive internal damage).

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- fact: Replace spark plug(s) in winterizing; Cub Cadet may use more than one.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: Replace air filter at winterizing, or verify it's in good condition and reusable.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- fact: Replace fuel filter at winterizing.

## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- fact: With deck access, replace blades with new set (author's habit) or sharpen if in good shape; remove blades to scrape deck with deck scraper or metal brush; reinstall deck if removed.
- confirm (deck top): turn each pulley slowly by hand for restriction/sound (bad bearing, replace pulley); belt not worn, shredded, stretched, glazed; brackets in position and good; clear debris from under pulleys, pulley grooves, idler bracket movement (all can make belt come off).
- confirm (deck bottom): gloves; grab each blade end and rock up/down for play or knock = spindle housing problem; remove housing, check bearing/spindle; may need complete spindle housing assembly.

## page: guides/battery-care-and-charging
- fact: Winter: fully charged battery won't freeze; or remove and store cool and dry.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Gas breaks down over 30 days; stabilizer (Sea Foam) up to 2 years per author; or siphon and run dry.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- fact: Hose off; pressure washer carefully (not high near deck/bearings); dry completely before storage.
