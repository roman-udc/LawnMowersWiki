## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Kubota mower cuts uneven", "bad cut", "strips of uncut grass", "scalping"

- fact: Summary table: worn blade (sharpen/replace); low tires (correct pressure); bent blade (replace); blade installed wrong (reinstall with sail point upward); clogged deck (scrape); unlevel deck (side-to-side and front-to-back); damaged spindle/housing (replace); bad belt/pulleys (replace); engine speed too low (full throttle); ground speed too fast; wrong paths (overlap); damaged deck shell (repair/replace); uneven yard/high spots (smaller deck mower / roll the lawn).
- fact: Kubota blades also unbalanced/incorrect install; unlevel yard is a non-mower cause.
- tip: Check tire pressure first before touching the deck.
- pro: If unsure how to repair safely use a Kubota dealer or repair shop. Engine lacking power at full throttle: take to Kubota dealer/small engine shop.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Low tire throws off the level of the mower; check before deck adjustments or you may have to reverse adjustments later.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: Sail is the higher side of the blade, creating air movement under the deck and suction that stands grass up. Replace if sail is thinner than center of blade.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Wear from dirt/debris sucked into deck, unavoidable. Dull edge beats up grass rather than cutting; sharpen if blade is in good overall condition, replace if bad.
- fact: Blade bends on hitting a hard object (sometimes a jolt and grinding sound, sometimes unnoticed). Check for bent blade whenever cut is bad.
- confirm: Blade off: access underside (lawn tractor may require deck removal), set the blade on top of a new blade; gaps = replace. Blade on: park on flat surface, measure ground to blade tip, rotate 180 degrees, measure other side; more than 1/8" difference = replace.
- safety: Never straighten a blade; weakens metal, may crack and fly off; endangers people, pets, structures.
- fact: Balance: blade must weigh the same on both ends; imbalance from uneven wear or from sharpening that removes more from one side; causes uneven cut, vibration, even engine damage. Use a blade balancer (inexpensive) or hang on a nail sticking out 3/4" to 1" from the wall; remove metal from the heavier/lower end until it sits level.
- fix: Install per the Kubota operating manual with the correct hardware.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Table says blade incorrectly installed: remove and install with the blade sail point upward.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: blade wobbles, significant vibration at high speed, bad cut.
- confirm: grab both ends of blade and rock up/down for play; listen for knocking.
- fix: remove spindle housing and check spindle and bearing for wear/damage; replace.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Every deck plugs; reduced space for airflow and suction gives bad cut; extra strain on engine, possibly shutting it down. Scrape periodically. Wet grass clumps and clogs faster and tire tracks show in wet grass; avoid wet mowing, or scrape after. Silicone or Teflon spray can minimize (not prevent) sticking.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: Higher on one side gives uneven cut. Follow the Kubota operator's manual for side-to-side measuring/adjustment and the rake (front-to-rear pitch; on many decks front sits slightly lower than rear). Specs vary by deck model.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: cracking, shredding, shiny glazed look; belt sitting deep in pulley grooves. Replace with a new Kubota belt. Slows blade revolution.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: a good pulley is secure, flat and parallel to the deck; a bad bearing lets it shift side to side when engaged; spin by hand, feel resistance, listen for noise.
- fix: replace bearing; sometimes only the bearings, sometimes the whole pulley assembly depending on model.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Blade tip speed affects cut; run full throttle when deck engaged.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Heavier load in thick, tall, wet grass; slow down; too fast pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Wide spacing to save time leaves uncut strips; new mower takes a few mowings to judge overlap.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Hitting a post, tree or building tweaks the shell; repair or replace. Get a price for full deck replacement too (labor to swap components); weigh against mower age.

## page: procedures/measure-a-mower-blade
- step 1: Park on flat surface; measure ground to blade tip, record.
- step 2: Rotate 180 degrees; measure other side; difference over 1/8" = replace.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- fact: See manual for model; side-to-side and rake (front-to-rear).
