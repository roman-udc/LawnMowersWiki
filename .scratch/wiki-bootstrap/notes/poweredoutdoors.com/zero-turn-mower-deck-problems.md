## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "zero turn bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, unlevel-deck, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap
- tip: check tire pressure first - a low tire makes the deck sit unlevel; then belt, deck parts, plugged deck, mowing conditions
- safety: park flat and level, remove ignition key and spark plug wires, wait for parts to stop
- fact: dull/worn blades - sharpen; replace blades extremely worn where the sails are thinner than the blade center or ends are worn and rounding
- fact: bent blade - replace
- fact: blade installed wrong - correct hardware; blade sails must point upward toward the deck
- fact: bent spindle - replace; inspect housing
- fact: bad deck belt/pulleys - replace belt that is stretched, worn, cracked or glazed; rotate pulleys by hand, replace if restriction, bearing noise or wobble
- fact: deck plugged - scrape
- fact: uneven tire pressure - make left and right even
- fact: unlevel deck - level side to side; deck sits slightly lower in front than rear; consult manual for specs
- fact: engine speed too low - highest throttle when engaging blades
- fact: ground speed too fast - slow down in thick, wet or tall grass; too fast can push grass over instead of cutting
- fact: operator error - overlap paths so no strips left; use a small deck like a push mower for ditches and steep grade changes

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: level side-to-side; front slightly lower than rear; see manual

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fix: overlap passes properly; small deck for ditches and steep changes in grade

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: sails point upward toward the deck; correct hardware

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fix: correct tire pressures, even left to right

## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, oil-or-rust-on-belt
- fact: worn/loose belt - cracks or shiny glaze - replace
- fact: belt keeper/guide damage - bend back to original position; replace if irreparable
- fact: shredded belt - replace; find and fix what shredded it
- fact: bad spindle housing bearing - replace spindle housing assembly
- fact: bad pulley bearing - some pulleys allow bearing replacement; otherwise replace whole pulley assembly
- fact: worn idler tensioner bracket or weak spring - replace
- fact: debris interfering with tension - remove leaves, mud, wood chips, stones under the tensioner
- fact: debris in pulley grooves - clean
- fact: oil or rust on belt - replace the deck belt

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: some vibration is normal; uneven ground adds; check deck belt, pulleys, spindle housings, blade shaft, blades when worse than usual
- fact: loose/missing engine mounting bolts - secure, replace missing
- fact: bad clutch - replace
- fact: unbalanced/damaged blades - replace and balance
- fact: material wrapped around blade spindle - remove; check spindle damage
- fact: bad spindle bearings - replace bearing or whole housing
- fact: worn pulleys/belt - replace
- fact: debris lodged - check deck and mower, remove

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fix: remove all debris/items wrapped around the blade shaft; check for spindle damage (also causes squealing)

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: most zero-turn mowers use an electric clutch; clutch solenoid requires power to turn blades
- fact: worn deck belt - replace if worn, cracked or glazed
- fact: belt falls off pulleys - replace worn deck parts: idler arm, spring, pulleys; replace stretched belt
- fact: bad PTO switch - replace
- fact: bad clutch - replace; on manual engagement clutch, replace worn clutch cable, lever, springs, linkages
- fact: weak battery - test, charge if below 12.7 volts for a 12-volt battery; replace if it won't hold a charge
- fact: bad safety switch - replace
- fact: blown fuse - same amperage; if it keeps blowing have a mechanic find the root cause

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fix: on a manual engagement clutch replace worn clutch cable, lever, springs and linkages

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- fact: grass buildup under the deck reduces airflow and makes the engine work harder; deck spray (DuPont mentioned) reduces but doesn't stop sticking; routinely scrape the deck
- fact: causes of buildup: mowing wet grass (mow only dry); low engine speed (mow at high throttle for air movement under deck); long grass (mow more often; double or triple cut if long)

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: see cleaning-the-mower buildup causes and fixes

## page: problems/mower-is-noisy
- alias: "zero turn deck squealing"
- causes-named: belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: squealing or odd noise from the deck; check belt first, then pulley and spindle bearings (possible bearing failure)
- fact: belt slipped and stuck between belt guides - reinstall correctly around pulleys; replace if worn
- fact: broken tensioner spring - replace
- fact: material wrapped around blade shaft - remove
- fact: bad spindle housing bearing - replace assembly; some zero-turns allow bearing replacement
- fact: bad pulley bearing - replace pulley assembly; some allow bearing replacement

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fix: replace broken tensioner spring / worn idler bracket
