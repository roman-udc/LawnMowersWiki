## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact: under ideal conditions a mower drive belt lasts three to four years; sooner with heavy use or poor care; longer than four years if mower kept clean and inspected every year. Replace within four years of installing a new one absent other signs. If bought used, inspect.
- fact: belts drive: self-propelled wheels; garden tractor wheels; commercial mower pumps; the deck blades; grass collection system.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm (visual): shiny belt with no ridges or bumps (slipping in pulley grooves); cracks or torn edges (right before it snaps); lots of slack between pulleys; more than 1/2" deflection (belt riding too far into pulley, pulley may be worn too).
- symptom: squeaking/squealing at start (turn off and inspect); mower stops or stutters; lots of vibration; not running at normal pace; won't move until belt warms; won't go uphill well; burning belt smell.
- tip: check tension springs (can break) and pulleys with dirt/mud/debris.
- symptom (self-propelled): squealing or loud sounds; suddenly need more force to push; much slower than normal.
- symptom (riding): harder to drive than before; stuttering feel (belt slipping).
- fact: belts that break often point to something else wrong, e.g. too many clippings in deck or an oil leak.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fact: see symptoms above for drive belts.

## page: procedures/replace-the-deck-belt
- fact: instructions for a standard mower where the deck doesn't need removal (your model may require it); photograph belt routing and spring first (many callers ask about routing).
- tools: a few wrenches
- step 1: set deck height as low as possible; take off deck covers.
- step 2: release tension and pull old belt out of left spindle pulley.
- step 3: find idler arm; remove hardware above it to reach the hook; remove the spring; raise deck to highest position.
- step 4: loosen idler hardware to remove idler; hold bolt head under deck to remove nut (caution reaching under deck).
- step 5: lower deck to lowest; remove hardware on the retaining belt keeper; remove belt off clutch pulley under the engine.
- step 6: install new: belt against rearmost idler pulley; reinstall belt keeper; belt on clutch pulley at back; return idler to position and raise deck to reinstall idler assembly, finger tighten only; hold bolt head under deck and tighten nut securely; lower deck fully.
- step 7: reinstall belt keeper into idler arm and spring hook; check tension and alignment in all pulleys; reinstall top parts.
