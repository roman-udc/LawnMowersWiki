## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: Add fresh fuel; check for tank leaks (fuel used faster than normal) and repair.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- brand: Ariens gas mowers: unleaded, min 87 octane, max 10 percent ethanol; avoid higher.
- fact: Moisture attracted by ethanol leaves gummy deposits as it evaporates, clogging fuel components; moisture and ethanol separate from gasoline and sink to the tank bottom, run hot through the engine and can damage it.
- fix: Drain and flush the tank; add fresh fuel with stabilizer and cleaner like Sea Foam Motor Treatment.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Clogged vent makes the tank act like a vacuum.
- fix: Clean the cap and open the vent; a thin piece of wire may unclog it; replace if it will not vent.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Fouled when covered with carbon or oil; misfire; sluggish or no start. Loose wire, wrong gap, broken porcelain or burnt electrode also stop starting.
- fix: Replace and gap per engine manufacturer; cleaning is possible but replacement recommended; plugs are the least expensive maintenance item and should be replaced annually.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Dirt in the engine can damage seals and score the cylinder; always run an air filter. Plugged filter: runs rough or won't start.
- tip: Start each season with a new filter; clean several times per season; more often in dry dusty conditions.
- fix: Paper: remove; wipe housing without letting dirt into the intake; tap against a solid surface (never compressed air, damages the paper); hold to light; reuse if light shines through, else replace.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Needed when the carburetor sits above the tank; builds pressure using crankcase vacuum.
- confirm: Look for a small housing crack or fuel leaking at seams. Otherwise verify flow at the inlet-port line first, then stop flow (shut-off valve, fuel clamps or hose pinch pliers), remove the line from the carburetor into a container, restore flow, start the mower, expect steady or pulsating flow.
- fix: Replace if flow is insufficient.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: Replace plugged or cracked filter; arrow on the side points toward the carburetor and away from the tank.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: step 1: shut off fuel (shut-off valve at the bottom of the tank or pinch pliers). step 2: pick a section. step 3: remove the end farthest from the tank into a container. step 4: turn fuel on and check flow. step 5: good flow: shut off and reattach; no flow: repair.
- fix: Remove the line, spray carburetor cleaner, blow with compressed air; replace if you cannot clear it.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: Old fuel gums up and clogs the carburetor.
- fix: Disassemble and clean with carburetor cleaner including the float bowl and needle; replace if too dirty to clean.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter reading about 12.7 V; charge if less; replace if it will not hold a charge.
- fix: Clean corroded terminals with baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda) and a wire brush.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: Confirm terminals not corroded and attached securely; clean as above.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- brand: Ariens uses several safety switches: prevent the deck from running without the operator present, and prevent starting when the parking brake is not engaged.
- confirm: Test with a multimeter or temporarily bypass to identify.
- safety: Never operate without the safety switch or with it bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fix: Test with a multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- brand: Some Ariens push mowers start by recoil; bad pulley, loose or missing spring, broken clips.
- fix: Replace spring and restring; replace the whole assembly if clips or pulley are damaged.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fix: After verifying the plug, check coil continuity with a multimeter; replace if broken.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Not the main cause but can leave a weak battery. Bad stator or alternator and other electrical parts.
- pro: Take to the local Ariens dealer; most lawn mower centers do not accept returns on electrical parts, so guessing is costly.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fix: Refer to the Ariens operating manual so as not to set off safety features that shut off the mower.
