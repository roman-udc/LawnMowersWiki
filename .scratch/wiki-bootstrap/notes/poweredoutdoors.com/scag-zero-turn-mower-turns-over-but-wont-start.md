## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "SCAG zero turn turns over but won't start"
- causes-named: wrong-choke-setting, clogged-air-filter, fouled-spark-plug, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Lack of air, fuel or spark.
- safety: Wait for the engine to cool, remove the ignition key and spark plug wires before repairs.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: Choke restricts air; cold engine needs more gas and less air. Once warm move it off; a warm engine must start with choke off.
- fix: Choke on/closed for a cold engine, off/open for warm. If correct and still airflow trouble, check the choke plate is not stuck and the cable moves freely; use carburetor cleaner to free a stuck plate and linkages; replace a stretched or worn choke cable.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- tip: Start each season with a new air filter and clean it several times during the season; check before operating.
- fix: Paper filter step 1: remove from housing without letting dirt fall into the intake.
- fix: step 2: wipe housing with a clean dry rag.
- fix: step 3: tap on a solid surface.
- fix: step 4: hold to light; reuse if light shows; replace if no light, extremely dirty or damaged.
- fix: step 5: install and reattach cover (if a foam pre-filter is used, clean and install it first).
- fix: Foam pre-filter (extra filter with a paper filter; not a primary foam filter; some engines use another type): inspect, replace if torn or brittle; wash with water and mild dish soap; rinse until clear; lay flat to dry (outdoors in the sun speeds it); install with the paper primary filter.
- mistake: NEVER add oil to a pre-filter, it damages the paper filter.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step: See paper and foam pre-filter steps above; consult the manual for other filter types.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: Remove with a 3/4" or 5/8" socket wrench, by engine model. Bad plug signs: dark tip, burnt electrode, broken porcelain.
- fix: Clean a slightly dirty plug with a small wire brush; ensure electrode gap correct; attach wire securely. Replace if damaged. Author recommends new plugs each season.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: Gas breaks down as soon as 30 days after purchase; leaves varnish and sticky deposits.
- brand: Scag: gasoline min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol. 4-cycle engines use gasoline; 2-cycle engines use a gas and oil mixture.
- fix: Drain with a fuel siphon pump; fill fresh fuel plus stabilizer such as Sea Foam; if you can start it, run it so the additive works through.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: Inline filter between fuel lines.
- fix: Replace an old or dirty filter.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- confirm: Follow the line from tank to carburetor for kinks; shut off fuel (valve or pinch-off pliers); check each section by removing the end farthest from the tank into a container and turning fuel on.
- fix: Remove the line, spray carburetor cleaner, blow compressed air; repeat; replace if not cleared or aged and cracking.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: Used when the carburetor sits higher than the tank. Most mowers use a vacuum fuel pump (crankcase vacuum).
- confirm: Verify fuel reaches the pump inlet first (else check line/filter). Then remove the line from the carburetor into a container, start the mower; steady or pulsating flow is good.
- fix: Replace the pump if no flow.
- brand: If a Scag uses an electronic fuel injection pump, measure with a fuel pressure gauge against the operator's manual spec; replace the pump if pressure is below the engine manufacturer's spec.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: Varnish from old gas plugs the fuel jet or sticks internal parts.
- confirm: Confirm good fuel flow to the carburetor, remove the air filter, spray carburetor cleaner into the intake and start; if it starts then runs sluggish and shuts off when the cleaner burns off, the carburetor is dirty.
- mistake: Do not use starter fluid.
- fix: Clean it, replace faulty parts, or replace the carburetor.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- fact: On Scag the tank vent is in the gas cap; plugging makes a vacuum that keeps gas from the carburetor.
- confirm: Loosen the cap and start; if it starts, keep it running while tightening the cap; if it sputters and shuts down and will not restart until the cap is loosened, the cap is the problem.
- fix: Replace the cap.
