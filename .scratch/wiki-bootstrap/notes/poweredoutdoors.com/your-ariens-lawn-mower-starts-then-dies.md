## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Ariens starts then dies", "stops running while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, dirty-cooling-fins, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, faulty-charging-system
- fact: Summary: fuel restriction (dirty carb, plugged filter, clogged lines, bad cap); plugged air filter; insufficient oil; bad ignition coil. Follow the Ariens owner's manual safety guidelines.
- pro: unsure = dealer/repair shop.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Ethanol-based gasoline can start to break down 30 days after purchase. Ethanol and moisture separate and sink to the tank bottom; burns hot and damages the engine; evaporating leaves gummy substance clogging lines, filter, carburetor.
- fix: drain with a fuel siphon; refill fresh; add additive (author: Sea Foam Motor Treatment) to stabilize, reduce moisture, clean.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Always run with a filter; restricted air makes the mower fail to start or start then die; can also cause overheating. Replace annually; clean regularly.
- step 1: Remove filter from housing without letting debris into the intake.
- step 2: Wipe housing with a dry cloth.
- step 3: Tap on a solid surface.
- step 4: Hold to light; reuse if light shows, otherwise replace.
- step 5: Install filter and cap.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Replace annually; arrow points in the direction of fuel flow (toward carburetor, away from tank).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: shut off flow with the shut-off valve at the bottom of the tank or pinch pliers; remove the line from the fuel pump into a container placed lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill without a pump; a push mower may not use a pump); start flow.
- fix: isolate the blocked section; shut off flow; remove; spray carb cleaner; blow compressed air; repeat; replace if not cleared.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: confirm fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; dies after running = disassemble and clean.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: Fins dissipate heat around block and cylinder head; plugged or broken fins let the engine overheat and die.
- fix: careful (hot engine); remove debris from fins; replace broken fins; clean around engine and shroud; ensure heat shield secure.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: Too much oil builds crankcase pressure; overheats and shuts down.
- fix: remove oil via drain plug or filter, or with an oil evacuator or turkey baster through the fill; check dipstick, adjust to the full line.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: Low oil from a leak or burning/using oil; friction heat can melt internal parts; shutdown from no oil likely means significant damage.
- pro: experienced small engine mechanic to tear down and test.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: dirty, damaged, mis-gapped plug or loose wires cause sluggish running and dying. Clean tip; replace if very dark, burnt electrode, damaged; gap per engine manufacturer; secure wires. Replace annually (inexpensive).

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: windings separate and short when hot; dies after running a while.
- confirm: ohmmeter, break in continuity; replace.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke reduces air for cold starts; must be moved to the correct position once warm or the mower dies.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: plugged vent creates vacuum in tank.
- confirm: run with and without the cap; runs without cap but dies eventually after refit = cap.
- fix: clean or replace.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: packed deck overheats and shuts down engine; scrape regularly; avoid wet grass; deck spray only minimizes.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Run at high engine speed, don't idle long (engine power charges the battery). If battery and wiring are good yet battery keeps needing charge, suspect the charging system; electrical parts expensive/non-returnable.
- pro: experienced mechanic for charging system diagnosis; replace battery if tests show bad.
