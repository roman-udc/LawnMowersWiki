## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "riding mower won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-wiring-or-ground, blown-fuse, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: troubleshooting order: dead battery; loose wires/corrosion/bad ground; blown fuse; safety switch; ignition switch; starter solenoid; starter motor test/replace. Article intro also mentions engine problems.
- safety: caution with electrical system; follow operator's manual
- pro: if unable to fix or complex, dealer or repair shop

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter; fully charged 12 volt reads about 12.7 volts; charge if lower
- fix: replace if won't hold charge; 12v batteries at hardware, automotive store or dealer; bring old battery; core fee averages $20

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: access the battery (under hood or under seat); screwdriver may be needed; don't remove from casing
- step 2: positive (red/plus) cable first onto positive terminal
- step 3: negative (black) onto negative terminal
- step 4: touch only rubber-coated things
- step 5: 12 volts; 2 amps up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge best
- safety: protective gear for skin and eyes

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: bouncing and vibration loosen cables and connections; moisture corrodes
- fix: baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); secure; replace damaged or severely corroded wires, connections, terminals

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- fact: loose wires, corrosion or bad ground (listed in troubleshooting steps); bad ground can fail the solenoid

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probes on each prong; near 0 good, infinity bad
- fix: replace with same amperage, never different; repeated blowing - repair shop

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: B (battery) and S (starter solenoid) prongs; key in start; probes on B and S, resistance; near 0 ohms good; infinite bad - replace

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: several safety switches on a riding mower prevent starting unless conditions met e.g. engaging brake
- confirm: multimeter test; temporary bypass for diagnosis only
- safety: never operate without or with bypassed safety switch

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch; fails when internal spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can cause failure
- confirm: need fully charged battery before testing

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid; remove and test
- pro: starter/alternator specialist shop test and rebuild before buying a new starter (author recommendation)
