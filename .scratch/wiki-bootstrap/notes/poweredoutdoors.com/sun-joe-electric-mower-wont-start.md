- pro: Authorized Sun Joe service center for damaged components (e.g., motor).

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Use a compatible battery; wrong size may cause no-start and worse electrical damage.
- fact: Sun Joe has external removable batteries charged on a charger, and internal batteries charged in the mower with a cord. Charge fully, then unplug: charger does not automatically shut off when full.
- fact: Charger LEDs (many Sun Joe chargers): red = charging; green = charged (check your charger manual).
- symptom (going bad): doesn't hold charge as long; gets very hot; charges slowly. Causes: age, extreme temperatures, mechanical failure.

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- fix: Check battery is securely in port with good contact; wipe battery compartment lid before opening so debris doesn't fall in. External battery slides into guide rails and snaps in; lid must close securely.

## page: causes/bad-charger
- fact: Use only charger/cord designed for that battery. Check outlet is fully plugged, standard 120V household outlet with sufficient power; plug style must match outlet (three-prong to three-prong); don't modify plug. Replace damaged charger or cord.

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- fact: Hot battery makes mower shut down and not start. Clean mower vents; let battery cool; keep charger away from extreme heat or cold.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Safety key prevents children accidentally pressing the safety handle; skipping procedure prevents start.
- step 1 (push/self-propelled): ensure battery securely in place.
- step 2: insert safety key into battery compartment, close cover.
- step 3: press safety lock button and pull switch lever toward handle, or squeeze one of the on/off switches on the handle (model-dependent) to start motor.
- step 4: release the safety lock button.
- step 5: mower shuts off when you release lever or switches.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: Test each with multimeter or temporarily bypass with jumper wire. Replace bad switch.
- safety: Never operate without all safety switches working.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- fact: Many wires run through mower and handle; loose or pinched wires short. Common pinch area near foldable handle hinge; fold carefully.
- confirm: Wiggle wires; multimeter.
- fix: Repair pinched wire with a wire nut after removing battery and safety key.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Grass lodged under deck can stop blade rotating and mower won't start. Overload (thick, tall, wet grass) can stop mower and prevent starting; raise cutting height and slow down; avoid wet grass.

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: Damaged motor or other parts; service center tests.

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- fact: Don't operate in rain: water can short out the mower.
