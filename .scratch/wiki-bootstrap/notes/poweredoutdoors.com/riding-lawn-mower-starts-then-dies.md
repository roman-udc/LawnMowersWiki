## page: problems/engine-only-runs-with-choke-on
- fact: reverse case: leaving choke on after start makes engine die (see wrong-choke-setting).

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas begins to break down as soon as 30 days after purchase; ethanol and the moisture it attracts gum up the fuel system.
- fix: drain tank (fuel siphon, approved gas container).

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- brand: riding mower needs unleaded, min octane 87, max ethanol 10%; higher ethanol can cause significant engine damage and shutdown after running a while; lower ethanol is better. Source links a separate "right fuel" article.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: remove air filter, spray carburetor cleaner into intake, try to start; if it starts and doesn't keep running, take the carb apart to clean.
- fix: clean (doable if mechanical and fine with small parts); or take to a small engine repair shop to clean or rebuild.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fact: residue from old fuel sticks in lines.
- confirm/fix: use the shut-off valve or crimp the lines to start and stop flow while checking sections; when found, remove the line, spray carb cleaner to loosen, blow out with compressed air; replace the line if it can't be cleared.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: listed together with fuel lines in the heading (no separate fix text).

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: fins circulate air around block and cylinder head; plugged with dirt and clippings, engine overheats and shuts down after running a while.
- fix: clean around the fins; replace broken fins; remove debris around engine; careful around a hot engine.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: extra oil builds pressure; crankshaft and rod must push through extra oil; overheating and dying; oil pushed through valve train into cylinder burns off creating smoke that clogs the air filter so the mower quits for lack of air.
- fix: drain a little oil: engine oil evacuator through the oil fill; or remove drain plug over an oil pan and quickly replace so too much doesn't drain; or remove the oil filter and let a little oil drain, then replace; or turkey baster through the oil fill (author's favorite; don't reuse for cooking).
- fix: check and replace air filter if plugged with smoke. If still not fixed, a small engine mechanic should check for damage from running with too much oil.

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step: choose one of the four methods above (evacuator, drain plug, oil filter, turkey baster).

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: plugged filter makes mower die; also sluggish running and costly internal damage.
- fix: remove filter, don't let dirt fall into intake, wipe housing, tap against something hard, hold to light; light through paper: reuse; otherwise replace.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: carbon buildup fouls plug, intermittent running problems; improperly gapped plug or loose plug wires can make it die after running.
- fix: try cleaning; very dark or damaged: replace. Replace plugs annually during maintenance; gap per engine manufacturer.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: dies once hot; windings separate and short out; plugs can't get voltage.
- confirm: ohm meter check for break in continuity; replace the coil if broken.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke restricts airflow to give more fuel before the engine warms; it helps cold starts. If left on after the engine starts and heats up, it keeps getting more fuel and less air and shuts down.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap vents to let air in so tank keeps correct pressure; blocked vent makes mower die after running.
- confirm: loosen or remove the cap and start; if it starts and keeps running, replace the cap; keep running a short time to see whether it dies again; if so, likely the cap.
- fix: may try clearing the clog, but a new cap may be needed.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: deck plugged with grass overworks and overheats the engine, so it shuts down while mowing; dull blades make it worse.
- fix: regularly scrape the deck and sharpen blades; don't mow wet (clumps); silicone or Teflon spray on the underside of the deck minimizes sticking but won't prevent all.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: dull blades worsen a plugged deck.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- tip: regularly check oil level and air filter, scrape deck, sharpen blades; annual maintenance prevents most causes above.
