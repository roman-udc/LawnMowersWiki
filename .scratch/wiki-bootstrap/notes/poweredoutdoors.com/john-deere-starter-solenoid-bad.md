## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "John Deere just hums or clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-motor
- symptom: Engine won't turn over; only a humming or clicking sound.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: Solenoid is an on/off electromagnetic switch that engages the starter motor. Usually mounted on the starter but not required to be; some are nearer the battery. Every John Deere electric-start engine has one.
- fact: Identification: round or square; 3 or 4 posts; some sit on top of the starter. Positive (+) battery wire attaches to one side; follow the positive cable to find it. Wire schematics vary by mower (may include wiring for lights or 12 V ports); basic three- and four-post diagrams shown in the source as images.
- symptom: Clicking or humming when key turned and mower doesn't start. The wire gets hot and smokes or melts.
- symptom: Inside are a spring and copper plate; fails if spring weakens or copper plate corrodes. Can also fail as a result of a weak starter, bad battery, or bad ground.
- confirm: Likely bad when, with the solenoid bypassed, the starter motor turns the engine over.
- mistake: Before replacing, check for loose or corroded wires or bad ground that keep the solenoid from getting power.

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- tools: volt-ohms meter, screwdriver (long), continuity light, wrenches for loose wires, needle nose pliers (if screwdriver doesn't work), battery charger (optional)
- step 1: Confirm battery has at least 12.3 V on a voltmeter.
- step 2: Set parking brake, make sure mower is in neutral, turn key to on.
- step 3: Bypass: lay a long screwdriver across the solenoid touching both the cable from the battery and the cable to the starter. A spark is common. If the engine turns over with the solenoid bypassed, solenoid is probably bad. Needle nose pliers can be used instead.
- step 4 (bench test, solenoid mounted on starter): remove the starter from the engine; attach battery charger negative (-) clamp to the starter case and touch the positive (+) clamp to the big post and the exciter wire on the solenoid.
- safety: Follow operator's manual precautions; always wear safety gear; sparks when bypassing are normal.
