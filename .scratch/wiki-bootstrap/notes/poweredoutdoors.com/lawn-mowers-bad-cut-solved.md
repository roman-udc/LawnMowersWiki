## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "lawn mower cuts uneven"; "bad cut"; "leaves piles of grass"; "grass looks shredded"; "brown tips after mowing"; "step cut"; "line of uncut grass"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, clogged-deck-chute, bad-tire, wrong-cutting-height, bent-crankshaft, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell, wrong-engine-speed
- safety: Always remove the spark plug before working under the deck.
- fact (FAQ): piles of grass = mowing wet/damp grass clumps under deck and on lawn; scrape deck, avoid mowing wet. Shredded look = worn blade tearing and bruising; visible a day or two after. Dull blade symptom: brown, ragged lawn a day or two after.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: worn/dull: strips of grass left behind, brown tips a couple days later (beaten, not cut). Bent: may leave a line of cut grass; some bends are slight.
- confirm: push mower: remove blade and lay on a new blade; gap = bent.
- fix: Replace worn blades (sometimes sharpenable). Bent: install a new blade.
- mistake: Don't try to straighten a bent blade: weakens the metal, safety hazard if a piece breaks off and is thrown at high speed.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail/fin (high end of blade) must point up toward the deck. Upside down can hit the ground, damaging grass and mower. Blade must be balanced (equal weight each side) or it vibrates deck and may damage spindle; install securely, not loose or wobbly.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Deck depth plus spinning blade creates suction that stands grass up; clippings plugging the deck remove the lift. Clogged deck also strains the engine.
- tip: Silicone spray designed for mower decks helps (doesn't fully solve). Don't mow wet grass; mow regularly so grass doesn't clump.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: Loose or damaged wheel: mower doesn't sit level, uneven or step cut. Tighten loose hardware; replace damaged wheel with the same size.

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- fact: If height settings differ left and right, one side sits lower and the deck is unleveled, giving taller cut on one side. Check both sides; fix damaged components affecting height.

## page: causes/bent-crankshaft
- confirm: Careful of the sharp blade. Remove spark plug, blade, and blade adapter. Turn the crankshaft and measure that it stays centered, using a dial indicator or a stationary object. If it shifts to one side, it's bent.
- fact: Replacing the crankshaft costs more than a new mower; author says replace the mower. Bending it back is a significant safety risk.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Tall or thick grass: slow ground speed; going too fast pushes grass over instead of cutting, may clog deck and overwork engine. Slow walking/self-propelled speed.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Shell tweaked by hitting trees, rocks, hard surfaces; the deck shape creates suction; damaged shell compromises cut; may try to restore original form.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Engine must run at full throttle for good cut; throttle lever fast setting (rabbit icon). Many push mowers run full throttle automatically. If engine feels weak, small engine mechanic; engine replacement costs more than a new push mower.
