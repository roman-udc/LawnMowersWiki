Overview: 10 Toro problems as tables (cause / reason / solution): doesn't start, dies while mowing, smoking, weak hydrostatic transmission, won't drive straight or steer, won't move, vibrating, uneven cut, leaking gas, blades won't engage.
- safety (all): remove ignition key, disconnect spark plug(s), secure mower so it doesn't move; follow owner's manual.

## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Toro doesn't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter
- fact: Often air or fuel restrictions (old fuel clogging, plugged air filter); also electrical (battery, charging system, switches) and spark plugs.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-recoil-starter
- fact: Dead battery, loose cables, corroded terminals cause starting problems; bad safety switch may stop start; bad ignition switch or coil = no start; pull-start mower may have broken recoil.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: fill with fresh fuel.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Fuel breaks down over time, less efficient and prone to clogging the system and engine damage.
- fix: drain tank, fill with fresh fuel; use fuel additive like Sea Foam to stabilize fuel and help clean the fuel system; use right fuel (link to Toro mower gas article).

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: vent in cap clogs, tank forms vacuum, restricting fuel flow.
- fix: use a thin piece of wire to clear the clogged vent; replace cap if needed.

## page: causes/bad-gas-cap-seal
- symptom: seal fails, gas splashes out around the cap (leaking gas). Fix: replace gas cap.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: excessively dirty or broken plug, bad connection, not gapped right; dirty/damaged plug fouls out causing intermittent running problems.
- fix: replace (or clean) plug, secure connections/wires, gap to manufacturer spec.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: dirt and grass plug filter; fix: remove and clean, replace if in bad condition. Also a plugged air filter alone can make a Toro smoke.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace the fuel filter (dirty filter restricts flow). Old fuel filter plugged can also contribute to gas leaking per source.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: failed or leaking pump no longer makes the pressure to pump fuel; pump can wear and leak at the seams. Fix: replace pump.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fact: bad fuel deposits and dirt build up. Fix: carb cleaner and compressed air to clear the clog; replace line if necessary.
- tools: carb cleaner, compressed air.

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-line
- fact: rubber fuel lines dry and crack, gas leaks. Fix: replace fuel lines.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: dirty and clogged due to bad fuel and ethanol deposits. Fix: clean all components, replace if needed. In leaking gas context: collects old-gas residue causing blockages/part failures; clean and replace damaged parts.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fix: charge; replace if won't hold charge; make sure cables tight, clean corrosion from terminals. Battery not charging (bad charging system) = Toro dies after running: test battery and charging system and replace bad electrical components.
- causes-named related: faulty-charging-system, bad-battery

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: safety switches prevent starting in certain situations; test and replace faulty one. Also may block blade engagement.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fix: test and replace bad switch (ignition switch or coil bad = won't start).

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fact: pull-start mower may have damaged or broken recoil; check if it can be restrung or if broken parts like the pulley can be replaced; replace recoil assembly if needed.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Toro dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, faulty-charging-system, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Same causes as no-start plus overheating (plugged cooling fins; clogged deck straining engine).

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: fins packed with dirt, oil, grass can't cool the engine block. Fix: replace broken fins and clean them.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: too much oil raises crankcase pressure, causing overheating and dying. Fix: remove a little oil to correct level. In smoking: excessive oil builds pressure, letting oil into cylinder through the valve train; drain a little oil to manufacturer's recommended level.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: windings can separate and short; check continuity with ohm meter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke is for cold starts; if it continues to restrict airflow after engine warms engine shuts off. Make sure choke in correct position.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: clogged mower deck strains engine so it works hard and may shut down. Fix: periodically scrape deck to keep clean.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-head-gasket
- fact: Plugged air filter can cause smoking; addressed early avoids significant damage. Other items costlier if not caught.
- fact: oil level too low increases friction, extreme heat; take to small engine repair shop to inspect engine damage; adding oil often too late.
- fact: internal problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket): bring to local small engine repair shop.
- pro: internal engine problems go to small engine shop.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: low oil -> friction/heat; smoking. Low or wrong oil also overheating (see Cub Cadet source).

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- fact: internal problems including piston ring, valve train or engine gasket make smoke; shop job.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Toro won't move"; "Toro has a weak hydrostatic transmission"
- causes-named: bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper (drive release lever in wrong position), worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: Toro zero-turn uses hydraulic system with pumps. Check missing/worn/damaged drive components. Drive release levers: mower cannot be pushed without hydros released; levers must be in operating position or mower won't move. Drive release lever lets hydrostatic mowers roll freely.
- fix table: drive pump belt worn or fallen off -> replace worn/cracked belt, ensure securely on pulleys; bad tensioner pulley (bearing fails) -> replace tensioner pulley (weak-transmission version: replace pulley and grease tensioner arm); missing idler arm spring (can't tension belt) -> replace; low/old hydraulic oil -> change at Toro's recommended intervals, check level periodically; hot hydraulic fluid (sign of hydrostatic damage) -> let cool; if runs fine cold but weak when hot, have Toro dealer check; air in hydraulic system -> must be bled after changing fluid, follow Toro procedures in owner's manual.
- brand: some transmission systems are sealed and can't be serviced; see dealer.
- pro: hot-fluid weakness -> dealer.

## page: causes/bad-hydrostatic-transmission
- fact: hot hydraulic fluid can be a sign of damage to hydrostatic system; dealer check.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fact: bad drive belt makes pump inefficient; replace frayed, cracked or worn belt.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: low or old oil fails to lubricate, system seems weak. Some sealed systems can't be serviced.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: air must be bled after fluid change or mower can't move at normal speeds.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- (see above)

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Toro won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, loose-steering-linkage
- fact: unequal tire pressure pulls to one side; keep to manufacturer spec, equal both sides. Bad dampers on zero-turn make mower jerk to one side moving forward: replace damper. Tracking adjustment needed on zero turn: when one tire moves faster, adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed per owner's manual. Worn or loose steering components (bushings, washers, gears) on mowers with a steering mechanism: inspect and replace failed parts.

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change rotation speed of the tire; refer to owner's manual.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: unequal pressure pulls to one side, also causes uneven cut.

## page: problems/loose-steering
- causes-named: loose-steering-linkage (worn bushings, washers, gears)

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact table: loose/missing engine mounting bolts (reduce vibration): secure, replace missing. Clutch bad (bearing failure and falls out; manual-engagement clutch can have bushing and linkages fail): replace clutch. Unbalanced/damaged blades: replace damaged blades, balance before installing. Material (string, long weeds) wrapped on blade spindle: remove, check spindle damage. Bad spindle housing bearings: replace bearing or whole spindle housing assembly. Worn pulleys/deck belt: replace damaged/worn. Debris lodged: check deck and mower for small items; "don't overlook the small stuff". Loose or missing hardware can also cause vibration.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Toro bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: list in 3x4 grid: worn blade; uneven tire pressures; engine speed too low; bent blade; unleveled deck; ground speed too fast; blade installed wrong; bent blade spindle or spindle bearing; damaged deck shell; clogged deck; worn deck pulleys or belt; operator error. No detail given.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: evaporating gas makes the leak hard to find; often no large wet spot.
- fact: bad fuel tank: metal tank can rust through, poly tank leaks at seams; replace the tank; a rust hole can sometimes be repaired if replacement tank unavailable. Metal and plastic shut-off valves prone to leaking: replace. Old fuel filter can plug -> replace.

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-tank
- see above. ## page: causes/bad-shutoff-valve - see above (replace valve).

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: Engage PTO and nothing happens. Table: worn deck belt won't grip pulleys -> replace. Worn tensioner spring (holds idler pulleys in line; can wear or fall off) -> replace worn/missing spring and worn tensioner bracket/arm. Bad pulley bearing (seize or wobble, belt rolls off) -> replace bearing or pulley assembly. Faulty PTO switch (fails to pass battery voltage to clutch) -> replace. Bad clutch (no longer transfers engine power to blades) -> replace clutch. Worn clutch cable (manual clutch): cable, lever, linkages wear -> replace. Weak battery (clutch solenoid needs power) -> charge; replace if won't hold charge. Bad safety switch -> test and replace. Blown fuse -> replace with same capacity fuse; consult mechanic for root cause if repeatedly blows.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: replace with same-capacity fuse; consult mechanic to find root cause if keeps blowing.
