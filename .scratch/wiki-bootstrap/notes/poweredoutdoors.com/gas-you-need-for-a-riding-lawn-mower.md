## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: riding mowers use unleaded gasoline, minimum octane 87, maximum ethanol 10% (sold as regular or E10).
- fact: some riding mowers need other fuels such as diesel; check the manufacturer's guide. For any major-brand riding mower with a 4-cycle gas engine, traditional unleaded is the best choice.
- fact: best fuel is ethanol-free, sold at select stations as recreation fuel (REC-90) or in canisters at hardware stores; Pure-gas.org lists ethanol-free stations in the US and Canada. More expensive but runs cleaner and causes fewer starting/running problems.
- fact: ethanol is plant-based, corrosive to the mower fuel system, leaves gummy deposits that restrict fuel; ethanol gas attracts moisture and as it breaks down the water/ethanol mix sinks to the bottom of the tank; running it can make the engine run extremely hot and overheat.
- fact: avoid gas above 10% ethanol: E15, E30, E85 contain up to 15%, 30%, 85% ethanol.
- tip: buy fresh gas before mowing and use it within 30 days; buy from a larger, well-known (busy) station for fresher, higher-quality fuel.
- mistake: filling a plastic gas can and letting it sit outside for long periods; gas left in the elements can become less potent or ineffective.
- tip: buy the best gas that is affordable; more expensive is not always better.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: stabilizer lets fuel last a little longer; add when you can't use the gas within 30 days. Author adds it to every tank. Add it when fuel is fresh; it cannot reverse degradation.
- brand: author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment (stabilizes, reduces moisture, cleans the fuel system).
- step 1 (storage tips): use a gas can designed for unleaded gasoline (other containers can break down).
- step 2: add stabilizer.
- step 3: rotate stored gas, oldest first.
- step 4: keep in a dry location away from combustibles; store fuel indoors away from moisture (summary says so).

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: gas above 10% ethanol (E15/E30/E85) is wrong for the mower.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: use within 30 days or stabilize.
