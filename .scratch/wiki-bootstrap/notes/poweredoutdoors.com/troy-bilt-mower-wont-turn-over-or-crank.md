## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Troy-Bilt mower won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-wiring-or-ground, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- symptom: items keeping the starter motor from getting power
- safety: take precautions; consult a mechanic if uncomfortable with the electrical system

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- tip: keep fully charged, especially in extended storage, to extend battery life
- fix: replace if it won't hold a charge; bring old battery to avoid core fee

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed); don't remove from casing
- step 2: positive (red/plus) cable first
- step 3: negative (black) cable
- step 4: touch only rubber-coated things
- step 5: 12 volts; start at 2 amps, no more than 10 amps; slow charge best
- safety: protective gear

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: vibration loosens connections; moisture corrodes terminals
- fix: disconnect battery and remove components before cleaning; small wire brush with baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); replace terminals or components if corrosion severe

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probe on each prong; near 0 good; infinity bad
- fix: same amperage; repeated blowing - Troy-Bilt dealer or repair shop

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: prongs B (battery) and S (starter solenoid); key in start; resistance near 0 ohms good; infinite bad - replace

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system
- confirm: multimeter; temporary bypass for diagnosis only
- safety: never operate without or with bypassed safety switch

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch; weak spring or corroded copper plate; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause failure
- confirm: fully charged battery required before testing

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground and solenoid; remove and test
- pro: starter/alternator specialist shop test and rebuild first (author recommendation)
