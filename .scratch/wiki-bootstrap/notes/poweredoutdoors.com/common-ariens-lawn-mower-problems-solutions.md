## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Ariens mower won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil
- fact: mower needs air, fuel, spark. Easiest checks: plugged air filter, dirty carburetor, fuel restriction, dirty plug, old fuel. Then electrical: ignition coil, battery, terminals, charging system, safety switch.
- fix: empty tank -> fill with fresh fuel. Old fuel -> drain and refill; add Sea Foam to stabilize and help clean the fuel system.
- fix: clogged fuel cap vent -> use a thin piece of wire to clear the vent; replace cap if needed.
- fix: plug -> replace, secure connections, gap to manufacturer spec. Air filter -> clean or replace. Fuel filter -> replace. Fuel pump (failed/leaking, no pressure) -> replace. Fuel lines -> carb cleaner and compressed air; replace if necessary. Carburetor -> clean; replace if needed.
- fix: battery -> charge, replace if it won't hold a charge; tighten cables and clean corrosion. Safety switch / ignition switch or coil -> test and replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Ariens lawn mower dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, bad-gas-cap-seal, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, bad-cooling-shroud
- fact: same causes as won't-start plus overheating: broken/plugged cooling fins, dirty engine shroud, heat shield not securely in place (blocks air circulating around cylinder head and block); plugged air filter, low oil level, wrong oil grade; insufficient fuel, wrong choke, bad gas cap, clogged deck, faulty ignition coil.
- fix: cooling fins packed with dirt/oil/grass -> replace broken fins, clean fins, clean around shroud.
- fix: too much oil raises crankcase pressure, overheats and dies -> remove a little oil.
- fix: ignition coil windings separate and short; check continuity with ohmmeter, replace if bad.
- fix: choke left on restricts airflow after warm-up -> set choke correctly.
- fix: gas cap vents plugged -> replace cap that no longer vents.
- fix: clogged deck strains engine -> periodically scrape the deck clean.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-head-gasket, spilled-oil-on-engine
- fact: catch early: plugged filter, bad engine gasket, too much oil, spilled oil from a recent oil change. Low oil smoke = more serious, extensive engine damage may have occurred; see a small engine mechanic.
- fact: too much oil -> crankcase pressure lets oil get into the cylinder through the valve train; drain a little to the manufacturer's recommended level.
- fact: low oil -> friction and extreme heat; take to repair shop; adding oil is often too late.
- fact: internal problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) -> take to small engine repair shop.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- causes-named: bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system, bypass-valve-open
- fact: old/low hydro oil can make the mower move slowly or not at all. Check drive belt, tensioner pulley and spring (worn or missing).
- fact: drive release lever lets hydrostatic mowers roll freely; if recently pushed manually, make sure levers are back in operating position or it won't move.
- fix: worn/cracked drive pump belt -> replace; belt must be securely around pulleys. Tensioner pulley bearing failed -> replace. Idler arm spring broken/missing -> replace.
- fix: low/old hydro oil -> change at Ariens-recommended intervals; check level periodically.
- fact: hot hydraulic fluid may indicate hydrostatic transmission damage; let oil cool; if mower runs fine cold but weak when hot, have an Ariens dealer check it.
- fact: air must be bled from hydraulic system after changing the fluid or mower won't move at normal speed; follow Ariens procedure in the owner's manual.

## page: problems/engine-loses-power
- fact: weak hydrostatic transmission (feels weak) causes: bad drive belt (frayed/cracked/worn, pump not working efficiently), bad tensioner pulley (replace pulley and grease the tensioner arm), low or old hydraulic oil.
- brand: Ariens specifies hydraulic fluid change intervals; low level or aged oil develops transmission problems.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Ariens won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fact: unequal tire pressure pulls to one side; keep to manufacturer spec, equal on each side.
- fact: bad dampers on a zero turn make the mower jerk to one side going forward; replace damper.
- fix: tracking adjustment on zero-turn or stand-on: when one tire runs faster, adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change that tire's rotation speed; see owner's manual.

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: adjust the speed adjustment bolt on zero-turn/stand-on mowers to change the wheel rotation speed when one tire moves faster than the other; refer to owner's manual.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: low tire can throw off deck level and give an uneven cut; unequal pressures pull mower to one side. Keep to manufacturer spec, equal on both sides.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: loose/missing engine mounting bolts -> secure and replace missing bolts (bolts reduce vibration).
- fact: bad clutch: bearing failure/bearing falling out of clutch causes vibration; a manual-engagement clutch can have a failed bushing and linkages. Replace the clutch.
- fact: damaged or unbalanced blades -> replace; blades must be balanced before installing.
- fact: string or long weeds wrapped around blade shaft -> remove debris, check spindle for damage.
- fact: bad bearing in spindle housing -> replace bearing or whole housing assembly. Worn deck belt/pulley bearings -> replace.
- tip: small debris stuck anywhere on deck/mower can cause shaking; "don't overlook the small stuff."

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: check tire pressure first; a low tire throws off deck level.
- fact: list: worn blade, bent blade, blade installed wrong, clogged deck; uneven tire pressure, unleveled deck, bent spindle or spindle bearing, worn pulleys or belt; engine speed too low, ground speed too fast, damaged deck shell, operator error.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal, old-fuel
- symptom: burnt spots in the lawn or a strong fuel odor.
- fact: old fuel softens plastic components, making seams susceptible to leaking.
- fix: carburetor buildup from old gas -> clean and replace damaged parts. Old (plugged) fuel filter -> replace. Fuel pump leaking at seams -> replace. Metal tank rust hole or poly tank leaking at seams -> replace tank; a rust hole can sometimes be repaired if no replacement is available. Metal/plastic shutoff valves prone to leaking -> replace. Rubber fuel lines dry and cracked -> replace. Gas cap seal failure lets gas splash out around cap -> replace cap.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: too much oil raises crankcase pressure, can overheat and kill the engine, and push oil into the cylinder via valve train (smoke). Remove a little oil to correct level.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- fact: hot fluid can signal hydrostatic damage; if mower is fine cold and weak when hot, dealer check.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: bleed air after changing hydraulic fluid per Ariens owner's manual or the mower won't move at normal speed.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: low or old hydro oil fails to lubricate system and makes it seem weak; check level periodically; change at Ariens intervals.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: broken/missing idler arm spring can't tension the drive belt; replace.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- fact: drive release lever (release hydros for manual pushing) must be in operating position or mower won't move.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Ariens routine maintenance suggested: replace/clean filters, clean mower, check for loose or missing parts, change engine and hydraulic oil and filters, lubricate components.
