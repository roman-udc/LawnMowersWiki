## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: no set replacement interval; depends on how often the mower is used and how well maintained.
- symptom: visible wear/damage; squealing from the deck; vibration; uneven cut; mower moves slowly.
- confirm: glazed shiny belt must be replaced; cracks mean replace (can break while mowing); check sidewall for wear or fraying from rubbing a bracket or other deck component.
- fact: Cub Cadet belts wear faster with after-market belts not of OEM quality; failures in pulleys, tensioner bracket, and spring also wear belts sooner. Debris in the deck stresses the belt and can make it snap.
- fact: worn belt slips on blade pulleys, cannot spin blades fast enough; blade speed matters because blades create air movement and suction under deck that lifts grass.
- fact: a belt that powers the transmission or hydraulic pumps, when worn, slips on the pulley and the mower moves slowly.
- fact: vibration can come from slack from improper tension or a stretched worn belt; if belt looks fine, check pulleys and look for stuck debris.
- tip: replacing a Cub Cadet riding mower deck belt is something most homeowners can do with arm strength, bending and a few hand tools.

## page: problems/mower-is-noisy
- alias: "squealing noise from my Cub Cadet deck"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: high-pitch squeal when belt runs too loose or too tight (wrong tension), when wrong type of belt is used, or when a pulley has a worn bearing.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: worn belt slips; wrong tire pressure can throw the deck off level.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: mower moves slowly if drive belt slipping; also low or old hydraulic fluid.

## page: causes/wrong-belt
- fact: wrong type of belt can cause squeal; after-market belt of lesser quality wears faster.

## page: procedures/replace-the-deck-belt
- fact: considered doable by homeowners; links to a separate Cub Cadet belt guide.
