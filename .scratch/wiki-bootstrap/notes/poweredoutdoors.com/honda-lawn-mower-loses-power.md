## page: problems/engine-loses-power
- alias: "Honda mower bogs down, runs sluggish, isn't giving the power it once did"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, fouled-spark-plug, wrong-engine-speed, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, mowing-too-fast, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- safety: remove spark plug wire; follow operator's manual.
- pro: if none of these fix it, small engine mechanic - internal problems hard to diagnose without tools.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: restricted air -> sluggish and possibly quits.
- step paper: remove; wipe housing with dry cloth without dropping dirt in intake; tap on solid surface; hold to light; reuse if light seen, replace if not.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: fuel unstable from 30 days; ethanol attracts moisture, gums and degrades system.
- tip: buy from busy station; unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol (lower is better); additive to stabilize/reduce moisture; store leftover cool and dry away from elements.
- fix: siphon old fuel, fill fresh.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: new filter with arrow on housing toward carburetor.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: stop/start flow with shut-off valve or crimp line; check sections; remove clogged section; carb cleaner then compressed air; repeat; replace if can't clear.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: old fuel clogs components; clean (gumming, hard crusty deposits); check components; shop option; replace assembly if still failing.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: intermittent spark -> power loss. Inspect tip for carbon, dirt, oil.
- fix: replace if dirty or damaged (recommended); if only dirty and not very dark, wire-brush clean and reuse.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: deck needs full engine power; throttle must be at "fast" when deck engaged; check lever.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: low oil -> friction, heat, power loss; check before each mowing; correct to dipstick lines; if caught early adding oil and finding why low may fix; otherwise serious damage, experienced mechanic.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: overfilling -> smoke; pressure pushes oil into cylinder via valve train; bluish-white smoke from burning; thick smoke can plug air filter -> check air filter and spark plug.
- fact: continued running with too much oil can cause seal damage, hydrolock, bent piston rod (as stated).
- fix: remove a little oil with oil evacuator, drain plug, or turkey baster.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: extra load; engine works harder in thick grass; adjust speed to conditions.

## page: causes/cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fact: strains engine, poor cut; mow dry for less clippings under deck.
- tip: tall grass: set highest cutting height, first cut, then lower and second cut; extremely tall: walk-behind brush cutter (rental center).

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: engine loses power turning blades through packed deck; scrape regularly; under-deck air movement from blades creates suction to stand grass up; clogged deck also gives bad cut.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: dull blades magnify power loss with packed deck.
- tip: sharpen at least every 25 hours of use; more often in sandy or gravel conditions.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- fact: too much oil -> bluish-white smoke (see overfilled-oil).
