## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Snapper lawn mower shaking"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade (unbalanced), debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: also loose or missing hardware, worn/damaged deck brackets.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) under engine absorb vibration; replace missing/worn/broken.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: manual engagement clutch (belts/pulleys) bushing and linkage wear; electric clutch ball bearing fails (article says "electric mower" but means electric clutch). No clutch on Snapper push mower: check engagement linkages and bushings.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: unbalanced blade wobbles, vibrates, can damage spindle housings. Imbalance from sand wearing unevenly, or sharpening removing more metal from one edge.
- confirm: blade balancer or nail on wall with nail head 3/4" to 1" away from wall; center hole on nail; shave a little metal off the side that hangs lower until level.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: string, wire, long weeds wrap around spindle; remove and check spindle/housing for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: key out, spark plug wires disconnected; blades installed, gloved hands on each end, rock up/down; movement or knock. Disassemble housing, check bearing wear or spindle shaft damage; replace.
- fact: Snapper push mower: also inspect for bent crankshaft that unbalances blade.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: stretched deck belt slack vibrates; replace if glazed, cracked or stretched.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: pulley may sit higher on one side; confirm by rotating slowly, feel restriction or bearing noise, or insecure seating. Some pulleys have replaceable bearings; others sealed, replace pulley/bearing assembly.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: idler bracket on Snapper zero turn and riding decks, pulley one side and spring on other; bracket hole wears larger, spring weakens/stretches; replace worn parts; check engagement linkage for wear.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: small debris under pulleys, tensioner and elsewhere causes big vibration.
