## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- alias: "Cub Cadet deck belt keeps coming off"
- alias: "belt keeps breaking / shredding"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, oil-or-rust-on-belt
- brand: Cub Cadet riding mowers - deck must be removed to change the deck belt (time consuming)
- symptom: belt comes off or breaks repeatedly; isolate the main cause first
- tip: prevention: clean the deck after each mowing; check for rubbing on deck components; keep oil and rust off the belt; periodically check pulleys and spindle housings for bearing failure; fix or replace damaged brackets or belt keepers
- safety: debris (dry clippings, leaves) near the hot belt can catch fire; keeping the deck clean prevents mower fires
- pro: article offers a separate guide for replacing a Cub Cadet deck belt (slide deck out from under mower; remove some nuts and screws)

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: belt looks loose, glazed and shiny, or cracking
- fix: replace the worn belt

## page: causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing
- fact: some Cub Cadet decks use belt guide keepers - small rod-shaped posts beside the pulleys; they sit about 1/8" to 1/4" from the pulley so belt shouldn't rub
- symptom: keepers move out of place or bend, often when slack belt hits them; belt may then come off
- fix: bend back into place with a hammer or other tool; replace if that doesn't work
- symptom: shredded belt - belt rubbing on a keeper or a deck bracket, or routed incorrectly around the deck components
- confirm: confirm belt installed correctly; look for a shiny piece of metal on the deck where the belt rubs
- fix: adjust so there is no rubbing; if it's a bracket with a bushing, replace the bushing or the whole bracket assembly

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: check each pulley runs flat and parallel to the deck; should not sit higher on one side
- symptom: pulley not running flat lets belt jump off; usually a bad bearing in an idler pulley
- fix: idler pulleys have a sealed bearing so replace the whole pulley

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: bad spindle housing assembly can make the mower vibrate and knock the belt off a pulley
- confirm: wear work gloves; remove ignition key and spark plug boots; grab each side of the blade and rock it up and down; listen for knocking and feel for movement
- fix: if knocking or movement, take spindle housing apart and check bearing; some spindle housings are sealed (replace whole assembly), others can be rebuilt

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: deck has a tensioner bracket and spring holding the idler pulley in line; bracket typically has a pulley on one side and spring on the other
- symptom: worn hole in the bracket where the spring attaches, or worn spring, creates vibration that makes the belt come off

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: debris buildup stops the tension pulley moving as designed so belt loosens and comes off
- symptom: stones, wood chips or packed mud in pulley grooves or under the pulley can make the belt come off or break (belts break when stretched around extra material, old or new)
- fix: clean the deck after every mowing

## page: causes/oil-or-rust-on-belt
- symptom: oil leaking onto pulleys and belt makes belt come off, slip and build heat
- fix: clean pulleys and replace the belt
- symptom: rust dries out and cracks the belt; replace the belt; inspect and clean pulleys for rust; replace pulleys that can't be cleaned sufficiently

## page: procedures/replace-the-deck-belt
- fact: Cub Cadet riding mower deck must be removed to change deck belt; article links its own guide (not in this source)

## page: procedures/replace-the-idler-pulley
- fact: idler pulley sealed bearing - replace the whole pulley

## page: procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly
- fact: sealed spindle housings are replaced whole; others rebuildable
