- fact: Source lists 7 reasons.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: Hustler won't move after being pushed/free-rolled (e.g. onto trailer or back to garage).
- fact: Bypass (drive release) rods must be re-engaged; hydrostatic neutral rods are at the rear of the mower above the transmissions. Two wheel motors, left and right.
- confirm: Check levers on the wheel motors are engaged and pointed to the right (as seen sitting on the seat).
- fix: Push levers to the right to drive forward and reverse.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fix: Check pump drive belt hasn't fallen off and is seated on pulleys; replace if any wear.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Source describes the tensioner pulley: most are hard plastic, wear or break; bearing can fail. Replace worn pulley or one with bad bearing; keep tensioner arm greased so it doesn't seize.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: Idler spring tensions the pump drive belt. Replace if broken or fell out.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: mower won't move or seems very weak while running.
- confirm: Check level before use with oil cool; wipe around caps before removing to avoid contamination (dirt is often a factor in failed hydraulics); check for hydraulic fluid leaks.
- fix: Add fluid to the full mark on dipsticks or overflow tanks (depends on model).
- brand/part: Hustler transmission fluid: SAE 20W-50 motor oil (source wording: "Use SAE 20W-50 motor oil in the Hustler transmission. Do not mix these two types of oil" - text appears garbled, one oil named).
- brand: Hustler change interval: oil and filters after initial 75 hours or 1 year (whichever first), then every 200 hours or 2 years (whichever first).
- pro: Hydraulic leaks: Hustler dealership.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- fact: Low or bad fluid lubricates poorly; friction overheats it; hot fluid can indicate more extensive damage.
- pro: Dealership if leaking from hydraulic pump, or mower runs fine cold but stops when hot.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- symptom: moves slowly or not at all after fluid change.
- step 1: Raise rear drive tires off the ground.
- step 2: With bypass valve open and mower running, move drive levers forward and reverse so tires spin both directions; about 6 times.
- step 3: Repeat with bypass valve closed about 6 times, or until no excessive noise and tires turn at normal speed.
- step 4: Check hydraulic fluid level, add as needed.
- step 5: Refer to operator's manual for model-specific procedure.

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- see steps under causes/air-in-hydraulic-system (about 6 cycles bypass open, then about 6 bypass closed).

## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Hustler: SAE 20W-50 motor oil; first change 75 hours or 1 year, then 200 hours or 2 years; replace filters too; bleed air after.
