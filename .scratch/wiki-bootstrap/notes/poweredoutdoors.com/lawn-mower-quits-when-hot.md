## page: problems/engine-dies-when-hot
- alias: "lawn mower quits when hot / shuts down in the middle of mowing"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, old-fuel, dirty-carburetor, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- safety: follow owner's manual; disconnect spark plug wire(s) before draining oil.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: plugged filter restricts air, engine gets hot and quits. Start season with clean filter; check and clean several times a season; more often when dusty.
- step paper: tap on solid surface; NO compressed air (damages); light test; reuse if light shows; replace if not; reinstall and cover.
- step foam: replace if dry, brittle, torn, brown spots; wash with mild dish soap and water; rinse until clear and soap-free; lay flat to dry (sun faster); coat with filter oil, squeeze excess so not dripping; reinstall.
- step common: remove from housing without knocking dirt into intake; wipe housing with dry clean cloth.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- see above.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol and moisture leave gummy sticky solution and separate and sink; running it makes engine run hot and maybe quit. Unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; less is better.
- fix: siphon tank into approved container; fresh fuel + additive. Author recommends Sea Foam Motor Treatment (says petroleum-based, won't hurt engine, has cleaning agents).

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: make sure fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in intake; runs a bit then shuts down = carb needs cleaning.
- fix: DIY if mechanical, or repair shop.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: best oil: air-cooled engine oil with high zinc concentration (cooling agent); small engines air-cooled unlike cars. Most makers recommend SAE30 or 10W-30; higher ambient temps may need 20W-50. Kawasaki Motors chart referenced. Wrong oil can overheat and shut down. Fix: drain and refill with correct oil.
- fact low: check before each use; friction heat burns oil and parts; extensive damage; may need engine replacement. Adding oil rarely fixes; mechanic tests.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: pressure; crankshaft and rod push through excess oil, internal parts under load, engine hot, quits; oil pushed into cylinder via valve train -> thick smoke -> plugs filter -> starves engine.
- fix: disconnect plug wire(s); drain little via drain plug, oil filter, oil fill hole, oil evacuator or turkey baster; check dipstick; check air filter for smoke plugging; reconnect wires; if still problems, mechanic.

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step: as above.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: clean annually; fins push air to keep block and cylinder cool.
- fix: remove debris, replace broken fins, clear around shroud, heat shield firmly in place.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: carbon buildup, broken porcelain or burnt electrode; may spark enough to start but not keep running.
- fix: clean if needed; replace if extremely dark or damaged; wires securely attached.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: stops when hot; windings separate and short; ohmmeter continuity test; replace.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: run with and without cap; shuts off with cap, fine without = cap.
- fix: may try cleaning cap vent; author replaces with new cap.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: packed deck + dull blades overheat and shut down engine. Scrape periodically, sharpen blades, don't mow wet.
