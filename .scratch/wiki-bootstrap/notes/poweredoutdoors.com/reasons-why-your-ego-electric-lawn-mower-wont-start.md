## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "EGO electric mower won't start"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, battery-not-seated, bad-charger, overheated-battery, incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, bad-motor, wet-electrical-components
- safety: Take precautions with electric current and sharp blades. If still under EGO's warranty, consult EGO before repairs to avoid voiding it. Always remove the battery before working on the deck.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Affects both push mowers and EGO zero turns. Some EGO mowers have a display showing charge; otherwise the charger may use LEDs.
- symptom: Going bad: no longer holds charge as long; becomes very hot; charges slowly. Replace for these signs; fails from age, extreme temperatures, mechanical failure.

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- confirm: Battery must be securely in place; on many EGO mowers you hear a click and the battery release button springs up when seated.

## page: causes/bad-charger
- confirm: Some EGO units charge in the mower via a charging port, others use a battery charger. Check outlet and cord connections fully plugged in; standard 120 V household outlet; plug must match outlet, don't modify. Extension cord must be undamaged and rated to handle the load; wrong cord risks overheating both components, damage and fire hazard.

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- symptom: EGO mower shuts down when battery excessively hot; let it cool before restart.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: EGO's safety button/key prevents children accidentally starting the mower.
- step (push mower): with battery in place press the safety button; while depressed, pull the safety bar to the handle; release the safety button. Mower shuts off when the safety bar is released.
- step (zero turn): insert the safety key and press start/stop button for 3 seconds; press the driving mode button; release the parking brake and pull the levers toward the centre.
- fact: Push mower handle: foldable handle must be unfolded and secured in operating position; side rails extended to maximum length and clamps locked; rails can move out of position causing no start - release clamps, check extended position, relock.
- fact: EGO zero turn safety interlock shuts mower off if you leave the seat with blades engaged.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: Test each switch by temporarily bypassing with a jumper wire; replace bad one.
- safety: Never operate without all safety switches working.
- fact: EGO push mower switches: foldable handle switch; side rails fully extended switch; safety bar switch (bar must be pulled in to run; release shuts off). EGO zero turn: parking brake/pedal switch; seat switch; charging-port switch. Check manual for others.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- symptom: Loose plug-ins: check around the battery with mower off and battery removed; unplug and reconnect to ensure good connections (zero turn has many).
- symptom: Loose or pinched wires short; EGO push mowers known to pinch wires where the handle folds for storage.
- confirm: Wiggle wires or multimeter.
- fix: Wire nut repair with battery removed; check warranty first (DIY repair can void it).

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Grass lodged under deck inhibits blade rotation; safety features prevent the mower turning on. Overload (thick, tall, wet grass) also stops mowing/starting.
- fix: Remove battery, clear deck; raise cutting height and slow down.

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: Start switch and motor damage: take to professional repair shop (pricey parts, need electrical/mechanical knowledge). Common replacement items: power cords, batteries, blades, plugs and safety switches. Judge whether repair is worth it.

## page: causes/wet-electrical-components
- symptom: Water can short the mower so it won't start (operating in rain).

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- fact: Read user manual for safety features on a new mower; see source link for dangers of electric mowers; rain can short out an electric mower.
