## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "stair step cuts"
- alias: "scalping the lawn"
- alias: "Sun Joe cuts uneven"
- causes-named: bad-tire, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, wrong-cutting-height, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, mowing-too-fast
- fact: 9 causes: damaged wheel; worn/dull blade; bent blade; blade installed incorrectly; blade not tight; cutting height; deck plugged; ground speed too fast; uneven lawn.
- safety: shut off mower, remove battery, remove safety key, wait for all parts to stop before troubleshooting.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: check wheels first: loose or damaged wheel makes mower sit unlevel. Tighten loose hardware, replace damaged wheels.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: worn blade tears grass tips; lawn turns brown a day or two after mowing; tips with little tears.
- fix: remove and sharpen with a file if in good condition; replace if extremely worn, gouged, rounded blade ends, or sail thinner than center of blade.
- fact: balance: equal weight both ends. Unbalanced by uneven sand wear or uneven sharpening. Use blade balancer (relatively inexpensive; unbalanced blade can cause significant damage) or nail on wall with head sticking out 3/4" to 1"; remove metal from end hanging lower until level.
- fact (bent): from rock, stump, toy impact; uneven cut and vibration; best check: park on flat-level surface, remove battery pack(s), wait for parts to stop, remove blade and lay on a new Sun Joe blade; even a slight bend causes bad cut.
- mistake: never straighten a bent blade; weakened area can break off and fly at high speed.
- tip: walk lawn before mowing.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: replace blade if sail thinner than center.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: install with sail up (high sides create air movement to toss grass). Sail toward ground: damage to blade motor; beats up and tears grass; can hit high spots and shock blade/motor. Also wrong blade damages mower.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- fact: blade not securely attached fails to rotate/cut; follow manual torque specs for blade bolt.

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- fact: deck set too low can't handle the grass volume; raise deck height for thick tall grass so the motor isn't overworked.
- tip: double- or triple-cut tall/thick grass: highest setting for first cut, lower for each additional pass.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: plugged deck leaves no space for blade to create airflow/suction; also makes motor work harder.
- fix: regularly clean with deck scraper or wire brush; avoid wet grass (clumps and sticks).

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: too fast pushes grass over; heavy load in thick/tall/wet grass; motor can overload and shut off. Slow down.

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- safety: remove battery pack(s) before working under deck.
