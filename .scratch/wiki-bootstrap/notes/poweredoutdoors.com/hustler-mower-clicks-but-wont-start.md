## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Hustler clicks but won't start or turn over"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor, faulty-charging-system
- fact: Only a click because the solenoid isn't providing power to engage the starter motor.
- safety: Always disconnect the negative (black) battery cable before electrical repairs; follow Hustler manual; consult a mechanic if uncomfortable.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: Constant vibration loosens cables. Check cables battery-to-solenoid and solenoid-to-starter are secure; check terminals for corrosion interfering with continuity.
- fix: Replace cables/wiring that keep coming loose or are broken. Disconnect battery; clean corroded parts with wire brush and baking soda mix (2 cups water + 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); repair/replace corroded wiring; dielectric grease on terminals and wiring; replace damaged terminals.
- tip: Make sure mower is dry before storing in a dry location to reduce corrosion.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter red/black prongs to same-color terminals; most Hustler batteries 12 V; reading 11.5 V near dead, 12.7 V fully charged.
- fix: Charge on charger; replace if it won't hold charge. If it charges but keeps dying, check the charging system.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Safety gear. step 2: access battery (screwdriver to open body). step 3: leave battery in casing with cables attached. step 4: connect red (positive) clamp first to positive terminal, black to negative. step 5: touch only rubber coating. step 6: set 12 V; start with 2 amps up to no more than 10 amps. step 7: with a gauge, leave connected until fully charged.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- confirm: Black ground cable from battery to Hustler frame: good contact, no corrosion; check solenoid ground (3-post solenoid is self-grounded). Replace damaged ground cable; remove corrosion.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Click or hum and no start, after battery, cables, connections confirmed good. Electromagnet switch engaging starter; usually on starter but not necessarily; follow positive wire from battery. Fails from weak spring, corroded copper plate, bad ground, weak starter, bad battery.
- confirm: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches; if mower starts bypassing the solenoid, replace.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: Only after the rest is good; starter can be removed and tested.
- tip: Pricey; have the dealership confirm, or a repair shop specializing in starters/alternators test it.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Bad charging system doesn't keep battery charged. Possible bad stator/alternator, regulator, other electrical. Hard to troubleshoot; have a mechanic familiar with it test (electrical parts can't be returned).
