## page: problems/engine-only-runs-with-choke-on
- alias: "Cub Cadet only runs with the choke on (or half on)"
- fact: running with choke on signals an underlying problem to fix before it becomes major. Engine getting too much air or not enough fuel.
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: grouping in Source: lack of fuel (old gas, dirty carb, clogged line, bad pump, plugged filter, bad cap); too much air (bad carb gasket, puncture in fuel line).
- safety: remove ignition key and spark plug boot before repairs; follow operator's manual.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas degrades from 30 days after purchase; ethanol attracts moisture, leaves varnish/deposits and is corrosive.
- brand: Cub Cadet requires unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; never use more than 10% ethanol.
- fix: drain old gas, add fresh gas with additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment).

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-line
- symptom: puncture or crack sucks air into line -> engine gets too much air -> needs choke (problems/engine-only-runs-with-choke-on).
- confirm: follow line from tank to carb looking for punctures or cracks.
- fix: replace with new section of same diameter.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: shut off flow at shut-off valve, remove end of a section into a container, turn flow back on, look for good flow.
- fix: shut off, remove line, spray carb cleaner, compressed air, repeat; if can't clear replace with same diameter line.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: small part between fuel lines; replace annually; plugged = reduced fuel, choke needed to correct ratio.
- fix: insert new inline filter, arrow in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Cub Cadet uses a pump if carb is higher than tank; most are vacuum pumps using engine vacuum.
- confirm: confirm fuel at inlet port; remove line from carburetor into container, test flow.
- fix: replace if insufficient flow, cracks or leaks.

## page: causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat
- symptom: only runs with choke (problems/engine-only-runs-with-choke-on).
- fact: gasket behind carb deteriorates, stops sealing, lets extra air in -> runs lean (more air, less fuel); choke compensates.
- fix: remove linkages and bolts, remove carb and gasket, install new gasket, reattach carb, bolts, linkages; good time to clean carb.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: usually due to old gas; passageways and internal parts clog.
- fix: clean (DIY if mechanical) or rebuild/replace if still bad; or small engine dealer.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Cub Cadet tank vents through gas cap; plugged -> vacuum, fuel doesn't flow.
- confirm: loosen cap; if you can take choke off and engine keeps running, cap may be bad; tighten cap with engine running - if it runs sluggish and needs choke again, but fine when loose, cap is the problem.
- fix: replace cap.

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Cub Cadet: unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol.
