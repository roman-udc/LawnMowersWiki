## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Husqvarna zero turn won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: needs air, fuel, spark; electrical failures (loose wires, weak battery, bad starter solenoid) can also stop it turning over.
- safety: park on flat level surface, remove ignition key and spark plug wires before repairs; follow operator's manual.
- fix: empty tank -> fresh fuel; old fuel -> empty tank, fill with fresh fuel plus a cleaning additive like Sea Foam; plug dirty/damaged -> replace; air filter dirty -> clean, very dirty/damaged -> replace; fuel filter -> replace; clogged line -> remove and clean, replace if needed; pump -> replace; carburetor -> remove and clean, rebuild or replace if damaged; battery -> charge, replace if won't hold, secure cables, remove corrosion; safety switch, ignition switch -> replace; solenoid -> test and replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Husqvarna zero turn starts, stalls and dies"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, clogged-deck-chute, bad-ignition-coil, fouled-spark-plug
- fact: besides the no-start causes, check plugged deck, bad ignition coil, low or high engine oil level.
- fix: old fuel -> drain, mix fresh fuel with additive to stabilize, reduce moisture and clean the fuel system. Cooling fins -> clean around, replace damaged. Carburetor -> remove/clean; rebuild/replace if it can't be cleaned or internal parts damaged. Fuel cap/tank vent no longer vents -> replace cap.
- fix: wrong oil level -> add or remove oil to engine manufacturer's level; consult a mechanic if that doesn't fix it (possible extensive engine damage).
- fix: plugged deck -> scrape deck, sharpen blades. Ignition coil with break in continuity -> replace. Plug dark/damaged/worn -> new plugs; slightly dirty and good -> clean with wire brush.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Husqvarna won't move forward or reverse"
- causes-named: bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: hydrostatic transmission pumps fluid to move wheels; pressure is so great you can't free-roll the zero turn without bypassing the transmission.
- fix: transmission bypass levers (one near each drive wheel) must be in operating position. Worn drive pump belt -> replace and confirm seated on pulleys. Bad tensioner pulley -> replace and grease the tensioner arm. Broken/missing idler arm spring -> replace. Low/old hydro oil -> change at Husqvarna intervals, keep full.
- brand: some transmissions are unserviceable (no hydraulic oil service); consult the Husqvarna dealer.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: vibration worsens shaking/bouncing; check engine mounting bolts, clutch, linkages and debris; deck parts can fail so pulleys and blades wobble and the deck belt loosens.
- fix: loose/missing engine mount bolts -> secure/replace. Failing electric clutch -> replace; manual-engagement clutch -> replace worn cable, lever or bushings. Unbalanced/damaged blades -> replace, balance. Foreign material on blade shaft -> remove, inspect spindle and housing. Spindle bearings worn -> replace. Worn/loose belt -> replace. Pulleys with restriction when turned by hand or wobble -> replace. Debris lodged -> clean all crevices.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fact: engine oil helps keep the engine cool; use an air-cooled engine oil of right viscosity that is high in zinc; keep oil full. Check cooling fins, engine shroud and engine guard for blocked airflow; inspect air filter, deck, blade condition.
- fix: wrong oil -> drain and refill; use SAE30 or 10W-30 in most conditions (very warm conditions may need a different viscosity). Missing/loose engine guard -> replace/attach securely. Dirty air filter -> replace. Plugged deck -> scrape. Dull blades -> sharpen or replace. Overworking engine -> slow ground speed to conditions, avoid wet grass, double or triple cut an overgrown lawn.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- fact: smokes when air filter is plugged, oil incorrect, or a bad engine gasket lets oil seep onto a hot surface like the muffler. If cause not found: internal issue such as worn piston ring or burnt valve; have a small engine technician test.
- fix: plugged filter -> replace. Too much oil -> remove a little from crankcase; check filter condition. Low oil -> technician inspect; chances are the engine is damaged. Bad engine gasket -> locate and repair.

## page: problems/engine-loses-power
- alias: "Husqvarna zero turn weak hydrostatic transmission"
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: weak hydro when oil is old or tank low, or when drive belt or tensioner pulley worn or broken.
- fix: replace worn pump drive belt; replace bad tensioner pulley and grease tensioner arm; change hydraulic oil at regular intervals and keep it full.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Husqvarna zero turn won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, brake-hung-up, air-in-hydraulic-system, bad-hydrostatic-transmission, unbalanced-load
- fact: check tire pressures and dampers; if good, track the mower so each tire rotates at the same speed.
- fix: tire pressure to manufacturer spec, equal both sides. Bad dampers -> replace.
- confirm: brake hung up: disengage hydros, raise drive wheels off ground, spin each wheel by hand and feel for resistance from worn brake parts; repair as needed.
- fact: zero-turn has two independent transmissions; air trapped in one makes that wheel move slowly; bleed the hydraulic system.
- fix: tracking -> adjust speed adjustment bolt to change the rotation speed of the tire so both move at the same speed (owner's manual).
- fact: transmission may fail on one side; consult Husqvarna dealer.
- fact: a trailer or other attachment can throw the mower off balance and make it pull to one side; remove it or rebalance so weight sits in the center of the load.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Husqvarna running rough and sputters, bogs down"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: look for restricted fuel or airflow, intermittent spark, and extra load (fast ground speed, plugged deck, dull blades). Throttle not at full speed also makes engine sluggish with blades engaged.
- fix: old fuel -> drain, refill with gas/stabilizer mix. Clogged line -> carburetor cleaner and compressed air; replace if can't clear. Fuel filter -> replace. Carburetor -> remove and clean, rebuild or replace. Gas cap no longer venting -> replace. Plug very dark/worn/damaged -> new; dirty -> wire brush. Fast ground speed -> slow down, especially tall, thick or wet grass. Low engine speed -> throttle to highest position when engaging blades. Plugged deck -> scrape. Dull blades -> sharpen.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fix: belt worn/cracked/glazed -> replace. Belt falls off -> replace worn deck parts: idler arm, spring, pulleys; replace stretched belt. Bad PTO switch -> replace. Bad clutch -> replace; manual engagement clutch: replace worn cable, lever, springs, linkages. Weak battery -> test; charge if under 12.7 V on a 12 V battery; replace if it won't hold. Safety switch -> replace. Blown fuse -> replace with same amperage fuse.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: hard to find if gas evaporates leaving no wet spots. Common spots: carburetor and dry cracked fuel lines; plastic degrading, seals drying.
- fix: carburetor bowl gasket between bowl and carburetor -> replace. Stuck float or float needle -> clean carburetor and replace damaged parts. Cracked line -> replace. Tank leaking at seams -> replace. Shutoff valve -> replace. Weak fuel filter -> new. Pump -> new. Gas cap seal -> replace cap; some caps allow buying only the rubber seal.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Husqvarna zero turn clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: just clicking usually points to the starter solenoid; also check what affects power to it: battery, cables, terminals, ground; then starter motor.
- fix: replace damaged cables, remove corrosion from terminals, secure loose cables. Battery below 12.7 V on a 12 V battery -> charge; replace if it won't hold. Ground: remove corrosion; ground cable from battery and from starter solenoid must make good contact; 3-post solenoid is self-grounded. Solenoid -> test and replace if faulty. Starter motor bad -> replace.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Husqvarna zero turn bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: check tire pressure first (low tire unlevels deck), then deck top and underside components.
- fix: dull blades -> sharpen; replace if blade sails thinner than center or blade ends worn and rounding. Bent blade -> replace. Blade installed wrong: correct hardware, sails pointed upward toward the deck. Bent spindle -> replace; inspect housing. Belt stretched/worn/cracked/glazed -> replace; rotate pulleys slowly by hand; replace when restriction, bearing noise or wobble. Plugged deck -> scrape. Tire pressure even left and right.
- fix: unlevel deck -> level side to side; deck sits slightly lower in front than rear; see manual for specifications.
- fix: engine speed too low -> throttle at highest when engaging blades. Ground speed too fast -> slow down in thick, wet or tall grass; running too fast can push grass over instead of cutting.
- tip: operator error: overlap paths properly so strips aren't left; use a small format deck like a push mower for ditches and steep grade changes.
