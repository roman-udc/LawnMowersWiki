## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Husqvarna mower won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter
- fact: Won't start because engine can't get air or fuel (restriction); bad battery, loose terminals, failed safety switch, faulty charging system also prevent starting.
- fix (per cause): empty tank -> fill with fresh fuel. Old fuel -> drain, refill fresh, stabilizer like Sea Foam. Clogged cap vent -> clear vent with thin wire; replace cap if needed. Bad plug (dirty/broken/bad connection/wrong gap) -> replace, secure connections, gap to manufacturer spec. Plugged air filter -> clean or replace. Plugged fuel filter -> replace. Bad/leaking fuel pump (no longer makes pressure) -> replace. Blocked fuel lines -> carb cleaner and compressed air; replace if necessary. Dirty carb (bad fuel, ethanol deposits) -> clean components; replace if needed. Bad battery/loose or corroded terminals -> charge, replace if it won't hold charge, tighten cables, clean corrosion. Bad safety switch -> test and replace. Bad ignition switch or coil -> test and replace. Broken recoil (pull start) -> see if it can be restrung or broken parts like pulley replaced; replace assembly if needed.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Husqvarna mower clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Clicking without turning over: bad battery, ground, starter solenoid or starter motor; also check cables/wiring securely attached, have good continuity, not corroded.
- fix: Remove corrosion, replace damaged components, secure wiring. Weak battery -> test, trickle charger if needed, replace if won't hold charge. Bad ground: ground cable from battery and ground cable from starter solenoid must make good contact and be free of corrosion; a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded. Bad solenoid -> test and replace. Bad starter motor -> replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Husqvarna loses power and dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, clogged-deck-chute, bad-ignition-coil, fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Dies while mowing when fuel or air filtration clogged (plugged filter, dirty carb, bad fuel); also check what keeps the engine cool (dirt/grass packed around engine, broken cooling fins).
- fix: Bad fuel -> drain, fresh fuel, additive like Sea Foam. Cooling fins packed with dirt/oil/grass -> clean, replace broken fins. Fuel pump moves gas when carb sits higher than tank -> replace bad pump. Wrong oil level: too little causes friction and heat in crankcase, too much builds crankcase pressure -> correct level; see engine mechanic if that doesn't fix it (possible engine damage). Plugged deck puts extra load -> scrape deck regularly, avoid cutting wet grass. Ignition coil can separate and short when hot -> replace one with break in continuity. Dirty plug gives intermittent spark -> clean or replace when extremely dirty or worn.

## page: problems/engine-dies-when-hot
- alias: "Husqvarna quits when hot"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, old-fuel, dirty-carburetor, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-ignition-coil, clogged-deck-chute, low-compression
- fact: Often fuel-related issues, wrong oil type, wrong oil amount, plugged cooling fins, bad ignition coil, or plugged deck.
- fix: Old fuel leaves varnish and sticky deposits -> drain, fresh gas with system cleaner and stabilizers like Sea Foam or STA-BIL. Dirty carb (residue clogs fuel jet or internals) -> remove, clean with carb cleaner, rebuild/replace if necessary. Wrong oil type/viscosity runs hot -> use air-cooled engine oil like the oils provided by Kawasaki; see oil viscosity chart in the overheating article. Too much oil -> drain a little to manufacturer level. Too little -> take to small engine shop or Husqvarna dealership; often too late. Cooling fins: they push air around the engine block; clean, replace broken fins, clean around engine block and under the engine shroud. Coil fails when hot as windings separate -> test with ohmmeter, replace at break in continuity. Plugged deck keeps blades from turning easily and loads engine -> scrape regularly, avoid wet grass. Internal problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) -> small engine shop.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Husqvarna mower smokes"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-head-gasket, rich-fuel-mixture
- fact: Smoke when engine runs too rich (burning more fuel than air), or engine oil burns off due to oil leak, insufficient level, or internal engine problem.
- fix: Plugged air filter -> clean/replace. Too much oil: excess pressure pushes oil into the cylinder through the valve train -> drain a little to manufacturer level. Low oil: friction, extreme heat -> small engine repair shop to inspect damage; adding oil often too late. Internal (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) -> shop.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Husqvarna running rough or sputtering"; "bogs down"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Due to lack of air, fuel, or spark; or overworking engine: fast ground speed, low engine speed, plugged deck, dull blades.
- fix: Choke wrong gives too much or too little air; must be open once warm; check choke lever placement, cable and plate. Old fuel varnish -> drain, fresh fuel with additive like Sea Foam. Fuel line -> carb cleaner and compressed air; replace if cracked. Varnish in carb passageways -> remove and clean, rebuild or replace. Bad cap -> new cap. Plug -> replace, gap correctly, wire attached. Fast ground speed in thick/tall/wet grass -> slow down. Low engine speed (engaging blades takes a lot of power) -> throttle lever in highest position. Plugged deck -> scrape. Dull blades -> remove, sharpen, balance before reinstalling.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- alias: "Husqvarna engine overheats"
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- safety: When the engine smells hot, stop running it right away to avoid extensive engine damage.
- fix: Wrong oil for ambient temperature or not designed for air-cooled engines -> drain, fill with fresh air-cooled engine oil with viscosity best for outdoor temperatures. Low oil -> change oil, add fresh; may have engine damage, diagnose with small engine mechanic. Plugged or damaged fins can't move air to cool cylinder heads and block -> clean, replace damaged. Engine guard missing or out of place (shields engine to keep air circulating) -> securely attach. Very dirty air filter starves engine -> replace. Deck full of debris -> scrape. Dull blades magnify plugged deck -> sharpen and balance. Overworking (long, wet, thick grass, more than mower can handle) -> slow down, avoid wet grass, double or triple-cut an overgrown lawn.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Husqvarna won't move forward or reverse"
- causes-named: bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, missing-axle-key, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: Bad drive belt, broken tensioner spring, worn tensioner pulley, low hydraulic oil. Some mowers have sealed hydraulic systems you can't service; take to Husqvarna dealership. Hydrostatic transmissions in riding and zero-turn mowers have different service procedures; see owner's manual.
- fix: Drive release lever (lets hydrostatic mowers roll freely) must be in operating position. Missing key in the axle -> replace key. Worn/fallen drive pump belt -> replace worn or cracked belt, make sure it's securely around pulleys. Tensioner pulley bearing fails -> replace. Idler arm spring broken/missing can't tension belt -> replace. Low or old hydraulic oil -> change at Husqvarna's recommended intervals, check level periodically. Hot hydraulic fluid can signal transmission damage -> let it cool; if mower runs fine cold but weak when hot, have dealership check. Air must be bled after changing hydraulic fluid or mower can't reach normal speeds -> follow Husqvarna procedures in owner's manual.

## page: causes/bad-hydrostatic-transmission
- fact: Weak hydrostatic transmission: hydraulic oil low or old; bad hydro belt or pulley can make it seem weak. Some transmissions are non-serviceable (sealed; oil can't be changed). Take to repair shop or Husqvarna dealership with hydraulic problems.
- fix table: bad drive belt (pump not efficient) -> replace frayed, cracked or worn belt. Bad tensioner pulley -> replace pulley and grease the tensioner arm. Low or old oil -> change at recommended intervals, check level.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Husqvarna won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, loose-steering-linkage
- fix: Unequal tire pressure pulls to one side -> fill to manufacturer's spec; tires on each side need equal pressure. Bad dampers on zero turn jerk mower to one side going forward -> replace damper. Tracking: when one tire moves faster than the other -> adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed (see owner's manual). Worn/loose steering components on riding mower (bushings, washers, gears) -> inspect and replace failed parts.

## page: problems/loose-steering
- fact: Worn bushings, washers, and gears can make the mower not steer one way or the other (riding mower).

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: Zero-turn tracking: adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change the rotation speed of the tire that's faster; refer to the owner's manual (no steps given).

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Husqvarna mower vibrating"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Missing/broken parts, debris under pulleys or wrapped around spindles. Look closely; littlest items cause big vibration.
- fix: Loose/missing engine mounting bolts (reduce vibration) -> secure, replace missing. Bad clutch: bearing failure/falling out of clutch; manual-engagement clutch bushing and linkages can fail -> replace clutch. Unbalanced/damaged blades; blades must be balanced before install -> replace damaged. String or long weeds wrapped on blade shaft -> remove, check spindle damage. Bad spindle housing bearing -> replace bearing or whole housing assembly. Worn pulleys/deck belt (bearing failure) -> replace. Debris lodged in deck -> remove.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Husqvarna blades won't engage"
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, worn-or-stretched-belt, missing-idler-arm-spring, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: Deck belt worn or fell off; PTO switch bad; clutch failed; manual clutch linkages/cable worn; battery fails to power electric clutch solenoid.
- fix: Worn belt can't grip -> replace belt that is worn, cracked, or shiny. Belt coming off: worn idler arm and spring fail to tension, belt stretched, pulleys not parallel to deck -> replace worn/damaged deck parts and stretched belt; route correctly. Bad PTO switch (doesn't let battery energize clutch) -> replace. Bad clutch -> replace. Worn clutch cable, lever, spring, linkages -> replace. Weak battery: test, charge if 12-volt battery reads less than 12.7 volts. Bad safety switch: seat switch doesn't sense operator, won't allow blade engagement (source's solution cell says "Replace a bad battery", apparent error). Blown fuse -> replace with same amperage; if it keeps blowing, mechanic to find root cause.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Husqvarna cuts uneven"
- causes-named: worn-blade-sail, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Not always the deck; a low tire can make mower not sit level. Check tire pressure first, then deck. Listed: worn blade, bent blade, blade installed wrong, clogged deck, uneven tire pressures, unleveled deck, bent blade spindle or bearing, worn deck pulleys or belt, engine speed too low, ground speed too fast, damaged deck shell, operator error.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "Husqvarna leaking gas"
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-primer-bulb, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: Hard to find leaks since fuel evaporates; only clue may be strong odor in garage. Follow fuel lines from the tank, check every fuel system component.
- fix: Dirty/failed carb (buildup from old gas blocks or fails parts) -> clean, replace damaged parts. Old filter, plastic seams fail -> replace. Pump wears, leaks at seams -> replace. Metal tank can develop rust hole; poly tank leaks at seams -> replace tank (rust hole sometimes repairable if no replacement available). Metal and plastic shut-off valves prone to leaking -> replace. Rubber lines dry and crack -> replace. Primer bulb rubber cracks or isn't attached well to hose -> replace. Gas cap seal fails, gas splashes out around cap -> replace cap.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Dull blades turning through plugged deck load engine; sharpen and balance before reinstall.
