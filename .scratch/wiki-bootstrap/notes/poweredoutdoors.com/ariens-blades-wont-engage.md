## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Ariens blades won't engage"
- alias: "Ariens mower blades won't turn on"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- safety: Never reach under the deck until all precautions in the Ariens operator's manual are followed: remove ignition key, remove spark plug wire(s), wait for all parts to stop moving.
- pro: If fuses keep blowing, take mower to an Ariens service dealership or repair shop to find the root cause of the electrical failure.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: blades-wont-engage. Worn deck belt: cracks, wear, fraying, shiny glazed appearance; may sit deep in pulley grooves.
- fix: Replace a worn belt even if it isn't the main reason blades won't engage; replace once it shows wear signs.
- fact: Belt grip on pulleys affects blade speed; fast blade speed is required to create suction under the deck to lift grass for an even cut.

## page: causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing
- symptom: Belt has come off the pulleys so it no longer turns them. Look for stretched belt, bad pulley, worn tensioner arm, or missing spring (article's "belt came off pulleys" section; it does not name a belt guard/keeper specifically).
- fact: Article links to a separate Ariens "belt keeps coming off" article.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: Tensioner arm and spring hold the idler pulleys inline; on most decks the pulley is on one side of the bracket and the spring on the other. The bracket hole where the spring attaches can wear, or the spring itself can wear; belt becomes loose and vibrates off the pulleys.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Each pulley has a bearing; a worn bearing lets the pulley wobble and not sit parallel to the deck; one side may sit higher than the other. Extra movement can make the deck belt roll off the pulley.
- fix: Replace a pulley found to have a bad bearing.

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: PTO switch is usually a knob on the mower that allows battery voltage to power the clutch; when it fails blades won't turn on.
- confirm: Check for continuity in the switch.
- fix: Replace a bad switch.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: PTO (Power Take Off) clutch transfers engine power to the blades by engaging the drive belt.
- fix: A worn or faulty clutch must be replaced when it fails to power the blades.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- symptom: Electric clutch uses battery power; weak battery can't give the clutch solenoid enough power to engage blades.
- confirm: Check voltage with a multimeter; fully charged 12 V battery reads about 12.7 V. Charge when below that.
- fix: If battery won't hold a charge, replace with a new 12 V mower battery (hardware/automotive store or mower dealer). Bring old battery; core fee averages $20 unless you return the old one.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- safety: Wear protective gear for skin (electric shock) and eyes. Do not touch anything without a rubber coating while charging.
- step 1: Access battery and terminals (may need a screwdriver to uncover; battery is under hood or under seat). Do not remove battery from its casing.
- step 2: Connect charger positive cable first (red / plus sign) to the positive terminal.
- step 3: Attach negative cable (black) to the negative terminal.
- step 4: Set charger voltage and amperage. Average lawn mower battery is 12 V. More amperage charges faster; start with 2 amps and work up to no more than 10 amps. A slow charge is best.
- tools: battery charger, screwdriver

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- symptom: Ariens has a seat switch under the seat (operator presence system); blades won't turn on without the operator in the seat. A failed seat switch may not sense the operator and prevents blade engagement.
- confirm: Test with a multimeter, or temporarily bypass the switch to identify a bad one.
- safety: Do not operate a mower without the safety switch installed; always have safety switches installed and working.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- symptom: No power from battery to the clutch.
- fix: Replace blown fuse with the same capacity fuse. Repeated blowing: take to Ariens dealer/repair shop.
