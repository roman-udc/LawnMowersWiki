## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- fact: click or hum when turning the key indicates checking the solenoid; wire on solenoid hot/smoking/melting also indicates bad solenoid
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: fill with fresh gasoline octane 87 or higher, ethanol no greater than 10%

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas starts to break down as soon as 30 days after purchase; ethanol attracts moisture, gummy deposits, corrosion; ethanol/water separates over time; less ethanol is better
- brand: MTD requires unleaded gasoline min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol
- fact: 4-cycle ("4-stage" in Source): separate fill ports for oil and gas, straight unleaded; 2-cycle: one fill port for gas/oil mix; MTD 2-cycle gas-to-oil mix 40:1 for mowers made after 2002; 32:1 before; never straight gas in a 2-cycle; 2-cycle oil differs from engine oil; unsure: check operator's manual
- fix: remove old fuel with fuel siphon, add fresh with Sea Foam Motor Treatment (stabilize, clean, reduce moisture)

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: plugged vent creates a vacuum in tank
- confirm: run with and without cap; if it starts and runs without cap but dies after running with cap on, cap problem; don't let dirt enter tank
- fix: replace cap (online or MTD dealer)

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: dirty with carbon, damaged or burnt electrode: replace; loose wire or wrong gap also
- fix: inspect; may clean with wire brush; author prefers to replace the inexpensive part; gap per engine maker spec; secure wire; bad plug misfires and runs sluggish

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace annually, clean several times per season

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- paper: step 1 remove housing cover; step 2 remove filter without letting dirt into intake; step 3 wipe housing; step 4 tap plastic/rubber part of filter on hard surface; step 5 hold to light: see light = reuse, no light or oil-covered = replace; step 6 install; step 7 reattach cover
- foam: remove cover and filter, wipe housing; reuse only if not brittle, no dark spots, no tears; wash with mild dish soap and water, rinse until clear; lay flat to dry; coat with foam filter oil once completely dry; reinstall; reattach cover

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: pump present when tank sits below carburetor; vacuum pump uses crankcase vacuum; old fuel deteriorates it
- confirm: inspect vacuum pump for cracks; fuel outside pump or cracks mean it can't make pressure; use shut-off valve or pinch pliers to start/stop flow; verify fuel reaches pump, then check for steady or pulsating flow out

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace if no flow; arrow on new filter points in direction of fuel flow

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fix: remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air until free of dirt and gummy residue, or replace line

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: crusty buildup and gummy deposits from old ethanol fuel; runs rough or won't start
- fix: clean if somewhat mechanical, otherwise repair shop; replace if in very bad condition

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: test with multimeter, want about 12.7 volts; charge if lower; replace if won't hold charge
- fact: loose cables or corroded terminals also cause starting trouble

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence system switches kill the engine when operator leaves seat; faulty switch may not sense operator and block starting
- confirm: temporarily bypass to identify a bad switch
- safety: never operate without safety switches installed and working

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: key turned, nothing happens; test with multimeter; replace if bad

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: supplies voltage to plug
- confirm: after confirming plug is good, check continuity with ohm meter; replace if break

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic on-off switch that actuates starter; click/hum on key turn; wire on solenoid getting hot, smoking or melting
- fix: test; replace if bad

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: not main reason for no-start but causes weak battery; bad stator or alternator among electrical parts
- pro: let small engine mechanic identify the part; guessing is expensive

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fact: push mowers without electric start; bad pulley, loose/missing spring or broken clips keep recoil from working
- fix: try replacing spring and restringing; if clips or pulley damaged, replace the recoil assembly

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: safety features prevent starting unless starting procedure is followed, including setting parking brake; choke must be correct (restricts air for cold start, must be adjusted off once warm or engine shuts down); follow MTD operating manual
