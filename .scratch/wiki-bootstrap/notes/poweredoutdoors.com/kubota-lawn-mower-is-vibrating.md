## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Kubota lawn mower vibrating / bumpy uncomfortable ride"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: see operator's manual; make sure mower can't start or move; work gloves around blade.
- pro: unsure -> Kubota dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: engine mounting bolts under the engine absorb most vibration; can loosen, wear or break. Inspect; replace worn, damaged or missing.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: Kubota uses manual engagement (belts and pulleys: check bushings and linkages, replace worn parts) or electric clutch (bearing can fail and cause vibration: replace clutch assembly).

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- fact: incorrect or unbalanced blades wobble and spin unevenly.
- tip: author prefers OEM blades; aftermarket may be good quality and cheaper but check center hole size and blade length; lay aftermarket blade on OEM blade to compare.
- fact unbalanced: heavier on one end; at speed severe vibration, may damage spindle housing bearing. Caused by uneven wear (dirt under deck) or uneven metal removal during sharpening.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: string, wire, long sturdy weeds wrap spindle shaft (blade shaft - round rod where blade attaches). Inspect; remove; then check spindle and bearing for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fact: spindle bends / bearing damaged by blade hitting hard object; bearing can also fail with age.
- confirm: work gloves; grab each end of blade and rock up and down; feel movement, maybe knocking sound. If movement: remove blade and spindle housing, inspect.
- fix: replace damaged parts. On most Kubota decks bearing replaceable in spindle housing; on some models must replace whole spindle housing assembly.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: worn/slack belt sits loosely or comes off; replace if stretched, cracked, or glazed. If good: check routing, tension (remove slack). Pulleys not parallel to deck also unseat belt.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: each pulley securely attached, no wobble (one side higher off deck than other = bad bearing); rotate slowly by hand, feel restriction, listen for bearing noise.
- fact: some pulleys use sealed bearings; replace pulley and bearing assembly.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: idler bracket hole wears larger, spring stretches -> vibration. Replace worn bracket or spring; check bearing and bushing in engagement linkage.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: small debris (even a wood chip) can cause extreme vibration; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat/parallel to deck. Remove spindle covers; clean deck regularly.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly
- fact: bearing replaceable in housing on most Kubota decks; some models need full housing assembly.
