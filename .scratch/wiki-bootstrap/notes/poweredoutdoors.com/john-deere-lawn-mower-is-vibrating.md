## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "my John Deere shakes so bad I feel like I'll shake out of the seat"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: Before work remove the ignition key, disconnect the spark plug boots, set the parking brake; follow the owner's manual for more.
- tip: Pre-mow checks, routine maintenance and cleaning after each use catch problems early; check for loose or missing hardware, blade and spindle damage, and deck wear all season.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) absorb engine vibration; worn, broken or missing bolts increase vibration.
- fix: Replace missing or worn mounting bolts.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Electric clutch: vibration when its ball bearing fails. Manual clutch with belts and pulleys: check bushings and linkages for wear.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (unbalanced blades)
- fact: Blades must be balanced (equal weight on both sides) before installing; unbalanced blades do not spin evenly, vibrate at high speed, and can damage spindle bearings. Cause: dirt in the deck wearing the blade unevenly, or removing more metal from one side while sharpening.
- confirm: Use a blade balancer, or a nail in the wall: nail head 3/4" to 1" from the wall; hang the blade by its center hole on the nail.
- fix: Shave a little metal off the side that hangs lower until the blade sits level on the nail.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: String, wire or a long weed can wrap around the spindle.
- fix: Inspect spindles, remove foreign objects, check the spindle housing assembly for damage.
- tip: Walk the yard before mowing to remove rocks, toys, branches.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- confirm: With heavy work gloves, grab each side of the blade and rock it up and down; movement or a knocking sound means remove the spindle housing assembly to check wear; likely a bad spindle bearing.
- fix: Depending on deck model you may replace just the bearing or the whole spindle housing assembly.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- confirm: Worn belt signs: cracks, glazed shiny appearance, loose belt, sitting deep in pulley grooves.
- fix: Replace a worn deck belt.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- confirm: Pulleys should sit flat, parallel to the deck, and not wobble. Turn each slowly by hand; feel for restriction and listen for bearing noise.
- fix: Replace the pulley.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Spring weakens and stretches; the bracket hole it attaches to wears larger.
- fix: Replace bracket and spring; also check the bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Wood chips, stones, acorns stuck under pulleys, tensioner and elsewhere cause vibration.
