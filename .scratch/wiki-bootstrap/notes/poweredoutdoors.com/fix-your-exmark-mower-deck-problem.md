Note: Source says steps apply to Exmark zero turns, walk-behinds and stand-on mowers.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "tips of grass look brown a couple of days after mowing"
- alias: "uncut strip of grass left"
- alias: "uneven lawn cut after mowing"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Diagnose in order, simple to complicated: blades, tire pressure, deck level, spindle bearing, belts, pulleys/idler bracket, grass stuck under deck.
- fact: Early warning signs of deck problems: brown grass tips a couple of days after mowing; uneven cut; uncut strip. Owners often wait until noise or burnt-rubber smell, which means costly repair.
- safety: engine off, key removed; disconnect spark plug boot(s) (some engines have one plug, some two); heavy-duty leather work gloves; wait until all moving parts stop.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Exmark deck vibration: bad bearings in cutter housing, unbalanced blades, debris wrapped around blade shaft or lodged in deck components (links to Exmark vibration article).

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: day after mowing grass looks brown/burnt; pull a blade of grass: tips brown, little cuts, shredded = bruised grass (beaten not cut).
- fix: bruised grass -> blades must be replaced; if only dull and otherwise in good condition, remove and sharpen.
- confirm (bent): park on flat surface; measure ground to one blade tip, spin blade 180 degrees, measure opposite tip; variance over 1/8" -> replace blade. Repeat for other blades under the deck.
- mistake: never attempt to straighten an Exmark blade.

## page: procedures/measure-a-mower-blade
- step 1: park mower on flat surface.
- step 2: measure from ground to one blade tip.
- step 3: spin blade 180 degrees; measure the opposite tip.
- step 4: over 1/8" variance = replace with new blade. Repeat for each blade.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- symptom: blades fine but uneven cuts; check drive tires (large rear tires). Soft tire keeps deck from sitting level, one side higher than other.
- fix: set to manufacturer's recommended pressure.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- confirm: mower parked on flat surface; measure deck height at all four corners; should be same.
- fix: adjust per owner's manual. Leveling can be tricky; can take a trained dealer up to two hours; dealer can do it.
- pro: Exmark dealer if hard to adjust.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- step 1: flat surface, tires to spec. step 2: measure deck height at four corners. step 3: adjust per owner's manual until equal.
- time: up to two hours for a trained dealer.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: plug boots off; leather gloves; grab one blade at each end, rock up and down; feel motion and listen for a knock. Movement or noise = bad bearing in spindle housing (a.k.a. quill assembly).
- fix: decide whether to rebuild or replace cutter housing assembly (typically includes bearing). Exmark dealer typically carries only complete cutter housing assembly and won't recommend rebuilding for longevity reasons.
- tip: replacement steps in separate article (replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts).

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: belts with shiny/glazed edges must be replaced.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: with deck belt off, spin pulleys; should move smoothly and silent; noise or restriction = replace pulley. Check idler pulley bracket for play where it mounts to the deck; any movement = new bracket.
- fact: idler bracket must move and spring back so that when blades are turned on the instant load doesn't snap the belt; if it can't move, belt slips and blades slip in heavy/deep grass, giving bad cut next day.
- fact: bracket has bushing (plastic or brass) or bearings; most Exmark mowers use brass bushings or bearings. Usually has a grease zerk; if not, disassemble, grease, reassemble.
- tip: keep the idler bracket greased during routine maintenance checks.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: grass stuck under deck disrupts cut: deck depth gives room to stand grass up for blades; plugged space loses lift.
- tip: reduce buildup (can't prevent altogether): always run at full throttle; avoid cutting wet grass; mow regularly so grass doesn't get too long and clump; coat underside of deck with Teflon or silicone spray designed for mower decks.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Deck maintenance at least twice per mowing season for average homeowner; more often if yard heavily watered or fertilized (moisture and fertilizer erode deck). Scrape and clean undersides more than once a year; while under deck check all parts.
- tip: links to Exmark hydraulic oil/filter change and winterizing articles.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- tip: scrape deck underside regularly.
