## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- alias: "Spartan zero turn deck belt keeps coming off the pulleys"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-belt, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, oil-or-rust-on-belt
- safety: remove ignition key and spark plug boots before working on the deck.
- tip: clean the deck after each mowing and before storage; clippings and dirt hold moisture and speed corrosion; dry the mower completely (sunny day outside, or leaf blower).

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: cracks, stretched belt, glazed shiny look, or sits deep in the pulleys.
- fix: replace; worn belts slip and no longer turn blades at high speed.
- tip (opinion): author highly advises against hardware-store lookalike belts (type, size, quality may differ); prefers an OEM belt.

## page: causes/wrong-belt
- fact: shredding along edges: first confirm the right belt and correct routing; a decal with the belt layout may be on the mower; otherwise owner's manual or parts diagram.
- fact: shredding commonly from incorrect installation or rubbing on a bracket that has moved out of place; shiny wear spot on a bracket shows rubbing; adjust bracket; bushing may need replacing; replace an unadjustable bracket; then fit a new belt.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fact: failed spindle bearing makes the blade wobble; at speed the vibration can knock the belt off.
- confirm: with work gloves, grab each end of the blade and rock up and down; vertical movement or knocking while blade is secure to housing means remove blade and disassemble the spindle (cutter) housing; likely a bad bearing; inspect the spindle.
- fix: replace parts; complete spindle assembly can be bought, some models allow rebuild parts.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: bad bearing makes pulley wobble and sit angled to the deck so belt slips off.
- confirm: spin each pulley by hand; resistance, wobble or bearing noise = replace pulley.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Spartan has a tensioner bracket and spring; too much tension damages deck components; too little makes belt vibrate, slip and come off.
- confirm: check tensioner, bushing and spring condition; clear debris restricting the tensioner.
- safety: dry debris on top of deck can catch fire from belt heat.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: dirt, stones, grass packed in pulley grooves stretch the belt so it rolls off and can snap.
- fix: remove debris from grooves and under pulleys.

## page: causes/oil-or-rust-on-belt
- fact: spilled/leaking oil lubricates the belt so it slips off; prolonged oil makes belt swell. Fix the leak, clean oil, replace belt.
- fact: rust transferring to belt dries it out, cracks and breaks it; remove rust, replace pulleys/parts that can't be cleaned, install new belt.
