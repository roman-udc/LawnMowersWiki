## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Hustler blowing blue, black or white smoke"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- safety: On smoke, stop mowing and shut off the engine. Remove ignition key and spark plug wire before repairs; wait for engine to cool and moving parts to stop; follow Hustler operator's manual.
- fact: Blue or white smoke = burning excess oil: damaged piston rings or valve train, engine gasket oil leak, oil pushed into cylinder by a plugged air filter. Black smoke = excess fuel burning: plugged air filter gives higher fuel concentration (runs rich); if not the filter, look for another air restriction.
- pro: Internal damage needs a small engine mechanic (teardown, leak-down and compression checks). Low-oil smoking: Hustler dealership or trustworthy small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Never run without an air filter, not even briefly while waiting for a replacement (dirt gets in; damages cylinder and engine seals). Start each season with a new filter and clean it several times. Replace when extremely dirty, covered in oil, or damaged. Plugged filter can restrict all air, engine draws air from the crankcase and pulls oil that burns in the cylinder. Smoke can in turn plug the filter.
- step (paper): remove filter; wipe housing with dry clean cloth, no dirt into intake; tap against a solid surface; hold to light, reuse if light shows through; reinstall with cover.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: Manufacturer's oil amount is not a suggestion; keep at "full" on dipstick. Low oil: friction, heat, oil burns, internal parts can melt, causes smoke. Changing/adding oil likely won't solve it as engine already damaged. Check oil level before each mowing.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: Too much oil raises crankcase pressure, pushes oil into cylinder and into air intake via valve train; burns and smokes.
- fix: Remove a little oil via drain plug or oil filter, or with an oil evacuator or turkey baster from the fill area; recheck dipstick, add/remove until "full". Replace the air filter if oil-covered or smoke-plugged.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- fact: Bad gasket leaks oil onto hot surface such as the muffler, burns and smokes. Replace; difficulty varies by location.

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- confirm: Remove spark plug(s); oil on the plug indicates piston ring or valve train problem (can't tell which). Piston ring/cylinder wall scoring lets oil into the combustion chamber; requires engine teardown.

## page: causes/bad-valve-train
- fact: An overheated Hustler can develop a burnt valve; only determined by removing cylinder head and leak-down test (mechanic); mechanic must cut seat and valve correctly.
