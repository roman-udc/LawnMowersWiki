## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Gravely zero turn won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Look for what keeps power from reaching the starter motor.
- safety: Remove the black negative battery cable before repairing electrical system; follow Gravely operator's manual.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- tip: Keep fully charged, especially in cold storage; batteries can freeze when not fully charged.
- fix: Replace if won't hold charge. New battery from dealer, hardware or auto store; bring old battery to avoid core fee.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Wear protective gear (eyes and skin). Access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed to uncover); do not remove battery from casing.
- step 2: Connect positive cable first (red or plus sign) to the positive terminal.
- step 3: Connect negative cable (black or minus) to negative terminal.
- step 4: Don't touch anything without a rubber coating.
- step 5: Set charger voltage and amperage; mower batteries usually 12 V. Start with 2 amps, work up to no more than 10 amps. Slow charge is best.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: Vibration loosens cables and connections; moisture causes corrosion that breaks continuity.
- fix: Disconnect battery and remove components; clean with small wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda). Replace terminals/components if corrosion is severe.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: Multimeter probe on each prong of the fuse measuring resistance: near 0 = good; infinity = bad.
- fix: Replace with same amperage. If it keeps blowing, take to a dealer/repair shop for root cause.

## page: procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse
- step 1: Multimeter (resistance) probes on each fuse prong. step 2: near 0 good, infinite bad. step 3: replace with same amperage.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: Turn key and nothing happens.
- confirm: Multimeter on resistance; find prongs marked B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid); key in start position; probes on B and S. Good: near 0 ohms. Bad: infinite resistance, replace.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Gravely has an operator presence control system. Test with multimeter; may temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: Never operate a mower without the safety switch or with a bypassed switch.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: Electromagnetic switch that starts the starter motor. Fails when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause failure.
- confirm: Needs a fully charged battery before testing.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: Check only after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid. Starter can be removed and tested.
- pro: Author recommends a repair shop specializing in starters and alternators to test and rebuild before buying a pricey new starter.
