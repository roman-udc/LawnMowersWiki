## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Ariens mower bad or uneven cut"
- alias: "stair-step cut", "strip of grass left between passes"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, unlevel-deck, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- safety: follow Ariens owner's manual; remove ignition key, disconnect spark plug wires, engage parking brake before working on mower and deck.
- tip: check tire pressures before anything else.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: a low tire throws off deck level (sits lower on one side).
- confirm: tire pressure gauge; reading should match the pressure on the tire sidewall; add air to a low tire.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: worn tips leave a strip of grass between the tips; wear is unavoidable from sand drawn in by blade suction.
- fix: rounded tips or sail thinner than blade center: replace with new Ariens blades; good but dull: remove and sharpen.
- confirm (bent, blade on): mower on flat level surface; measure from the ground to a blade end and record; rotate blade 180 degrees and measure from same ground spot; more than 1/8 in difference means bent, do not reuse.
- confirm (bent, blade off): compare with a good blade, place used blade on top; gaps or a bend, or doesn't sit flat: do not reuse.
- mistake: never straighten a bent blade; metal weakens and cracks, risk of breaking off at high speed.
- fact: bent from hitting a solid object; check the spindle and bearing before removing the blade.
- fact: unbalanced blade: one side heavier from uneven wear or from removing more metal on one side when sharpening; causes wobble, spindle bearing damage at high speed and significant vibration.
- confirm: blade balancer (inexpensive) before installing, or hang on a nail in the wall; shave metal off the heavy side until it stays level on the nail.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: worn sail can't create the airflow and suction needed for a good cut.

## page: procedures/measure-a-mower-blade
- step 1: mower on flat level surface; measure from ground to a blade end, record.
- step 2: rotate blade 180 degrees; measure from the same ground spot; record.
- step 3: difference over 1/8 in means bent.

## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- fact: balance check with balancer or nail; replace when tips rounded or sail thinner than center.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: blade movement while rotating, significant vibration, uneven cut.
- confirm: blade installed, grab each end, rock up and down for movement or knocking; if so remove blade and spindle housing to check bearing/spindle; replace damaged parts.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Ariens uses blade air movement with deck shell and baffles to make suction; plugged deck reduces airflow area and makes the engine work harder.
- fix: scrape deck regularly; avoid wet mowing (clumps).

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- symptom: one side lower gives a stair-step cut.
- fix: leveling procedures vary by model; follow the operator's manual; level side to side and front to rear; on most decks the front sits slightly lower than the rear; check manual for exact specs.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- fact: see above; no numeric spec given.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: worn belt fails to grip pulleys and turn blades at the speed needed; shiny glazed appearance, no longer sits tight to the pulleys, sits deep in the pulleys, cracks.
- fix: replace.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: bad bearing means pulley doesn't sit flat; vertical movement, one side higher off the deck.
- confirm: slowly spin each pulley by hand feeling for restriction and listening for bearing noise; wobble. fix: replace pulley.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: engage the deck at full throttle; engine speed at its highest gives enough power to spin blades.
- pro: if the engine no longer gives the power needed, have a small engine mechanic test the engine.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: fast ground speed overloads the engine; mower doesn't cut all the grass or pushes it over.
- fix: slow down for tall, wet or thick grass and on inclines.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: overlap passes so no strips are left between; takes practice with a new mower; space paths to avoid mowing the same section twice.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: hitting a fence, tree or pole can damage the shell; if it can't be fixed replace it.
- tip: get a dealer quote for a replacement shell plus labor to swap components, compare with a full deck replacement; the price difference may be small.
