## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Husqvarna keeps shutting off after starting"
- symptom: lack of air, fuel or spark
- causes-named: old-fuel, water-in-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- safety: follow owner's manual
- pro: contact Husqvarna dealer or repair shop if not comfortable; only do repairs you can do safely

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down as soon as 30 days after purchase; ethanol attracts moisture which corrodes fuel systems and engine parts
- fix: drain with a fuel siphon; fresh fuel plus a fuel additive to absorb moisture

## page: causes/water-in-fuel
- symptom: sluggish running, possible shutdown; rain on a mower left outdoors can get water into the tank around the cap
- fix: drain with siphon, refill fresh fuel with additive

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fact: evaporated ethanol/moisture leaves gummy residue clogging fuel lines, filter and pump (restriction)
- fix: check all fuel components and remove clogs

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: dirty carb plugs/sticks small parts; runs sluggish or won't stay running
- confirm: confirm fuel flows to carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake and start; if it runs fine then dies, clean and inspect the carb

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace annually and clean several times per season
- step (paper) 1: remove filter; step 2: wipe housing without dirt falling into intake; step 3: tap on solid surface; step 4: hold to light, reuse if light shows through; step 5: reinstall and cover
- step (foam) 1: remove; 2: wipe housing; 3: inspect, dark spots/tears/dry brittle means replace; 4: wash with water and mild dish soap, rinse; 5: lay flat to dry (sun speeds); 6: when dry coat with foam air filter oil, fully covered but not dripping, wring out or blot excess; 7: reinstall and cover

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke restricts air for cold starts for richer mixture; if not readjusted after warm-up the engine stops from lack of airflow

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: fouled plug causes starting problems, rough running, quitting; wrong gap or loose wire too
- fix: remove wire then plug with a spark plug wrench; clean or replace if covered in oil and carbon; replace if burnt electrode or cracked porcelain

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: windings can separate and short out when mower gets hot, so plug gets no voltage (dies when hot)
- confirm: check for break in continuity with an ohmmeter

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: plugged vent makes vacuum in tank; mower stops
- confirm: after it stops, remove fuel cap and start; if it runs fine, put cap on and keep running; if it dies again but restarts when cap removed, cap is the problem
- fix: try cleaning/unclogging vent (doesn't always work); else new cap
