## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "DeWalt electric mower won't start"
- causes-named: bad-battery, battery-not-seated, bad-charger, overheated-battery, weak-or-dead-battery, handle-out-of-position (unmapped), incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass, bad-motor
- fact: Won't start due to dead battery, bad battery connection, bad safety switch, loose or pinched wire, or debris under the deck.
- safety: If under DeWalt warranty, consult DeWalt before repairs so as not to void it (1-800-4-DEWALT, 1-800-433-9258, or website request form).
- fact: Insufficient power: battery, connection or charger can be the problem.

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- symptom: (problems/battery-mower-wont-start) Battery not latched. Open battery compartment lid, slide battery in until you hear a click (battery release button springing into place).
- fact: Some DeWalt mowers use removable batteries charged on an external charger; always use the charger/cord designed for that battery.

## page: causes/bad-charger
- confirm: Check outlet: charger fully plugged in; standard 120V household outlet with sufficient power. Plug style must match outlet; do not modify the plug (3-prong needs 3-prong outlet). Replace a damaged charger or charging cord. (Source mentions "Sun Joe cord" in this DeWalt article, apparently a copy slip.)

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- symptom: A hot battery makes the mower shut down and not start. Clean mower vents; let battery cool before restarting; keep battery and charger out of extreme heat or cold.

## page: causes/bad-battery
- symptom: Failing when old, exposed to extreme temperatures, or mechanically failed. Signs: doesn't hold charge as long, gets very hot, charges slowly (a cold battery charges slower), no longer charges on the charger (charge light doesn't illuminate or shows an error blink pattern; the charger may be faulty).
- confirm: Some DeWalt batteries show charge level on the battery; others must be put on charger. Charger LED (check charger manual for model): blinking red = charging; solid red = charged; blinking red plus solid yellow = battery too hot or too cold, yellow goes off at proper temperature, charges slowly meanwhile.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- symptom: DeWalt push mower: safety button prevents children from starting. Procedure: with battery securely in place press the safety button; while depressed pull the safety bar to meet the handle; release the safety button; mower shuts off when the safety bar is released.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- brand: DeWalt push mower switches: foldable handle switch; side rails fully extended switch; safety bar switch (bar must be pulled into handle, release shuts mower off); safety button switch.
- confirm: Test each switch by temporarily bypassing with a jumper wire. Replace bad switch.
- safety: Never operate the mower without all safety switches working.
- symptom: Handle out of position: foldable handle must be unfolded and secured in operating position; side rails extended to maximum length with clamps locked; rails can slip out of position. Release clamps, check position, secure clamps.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- symptom: Loose or pinched wires short. DeWalt push mowers known for pinched wires, commonly where the handle folds into the machine for storage.
- confirm: Wiggle wires; multimeter to find a short.
- fix: Repair loose/pinched connections with a wire nut; remove the battery first. Self-repair can void warranty.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Grass/debris lodged under deck restricts blade rotation; the machine's safety features then prevent starting. Remove battery before working on deck.

## page: causes/cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- symptom: Mower stops and won't restart when overloaded (thick, tall, wet grass). Raise cutting height and slow down.

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: Damaged start switch or motor: usually pricey and best handled by professional repair shop with electrical knowledge; judge whether repair is worth more than the mower. Commonly replaced electric mower parts: power cords, batteries, blades, plugs and safety switches.
- pro: Start switch and motor damage go to a professional repair shop.
