## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Spartan zero turn won't turn over or crank"
- symptom: starter motor not getting enough power to spin the engine
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: prevent electrocution; remove the black negative battery cable before electrical repairs

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- tip: keep fully charged, especially in cold-weather storage since batteries can freeze when not fully charged
- fix: replace if won't hold a charge (Spartan dealer, hardware or auto store; bring old battery to avoid core fee)

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- safety: eye and skin protection
- step 1: access battery/terminals (screwdriver may be needed); leave battery in casing
- step 2: red (plus) cable to positive terminal first
- step 3: black (negative) cable to negative terminal
- step 4: touch nothing without rubber coating
- step 5: 12 volts; start at 2 amps, no more than 10 amps; slow charge is best

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: constant vibration loosens wires/components; corrosion breaks continuity
- fix: remove battery cables first, then corroded components; wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons); replace terminals if too corroded

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- symptom: blown by a surge in power or a short
- confirm: multimeter on each prong; near 0 good, infinity bad
- fix: same amperage; keeps blowing: dealer/shop for root cause

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: multimeter resistance; B (Battery) to S (Starter Solenoid) prongs with key at start; near 0 ohms good, infinite bad

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system with several safety switches
- confirm: multimeter, or temporary bypass for troubleshooting only
- safety: never operate without safety switch or with it bypassed

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch that starts the starter motor; fails when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause it
- confirm: fully charged battery first; then author's solenoid test article

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: after ruling out the above, remove and test the starter
- pro: starter/alternator repair shop to test and rebuild before buying new
