## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "lawn tractor is vibrating"
- alias: "tractor shakes so badly I feel like I'll vibrate out of the seat"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade (wrong/unbalanced), debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- tip: keep tractor clean and check hardware regularly to prevent vibration
- safety: follow owner's manual

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: bolts (motor mounts) under engine absorb vibration; wear or break over time; tighten loose, replace worn, broken or missing

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: electric clutch vibrates when ball bearing fails; manual engagement clutch (belt and pulleys type): check bushings and linkages for failure

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: wrong blade or unbalanced blade vibrates at high speed; unbalanced blade can damage spindle housing bearings
- tip: author prefers OEM; good aftermarket brands he has worked with: Stens and Oregon (author's opinion); must match center hole size and length; still not all fit, physically match against OEM blade
- fact: unbalance from uneven wear (dirt swirling under deck) or too much metal removed from one side during sharpening

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: string, wire, long sturdy weeds wrap around shaft (spindle); remove; check spindle and housing; walk lawn first

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: gloves; grab each end of blade and rock up and down; movement or knocking; remove blade and spindle housing, inspect bearing/spindle, replace damaged items

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: worn or slack belt doesn't sit securely, may come off; replace if stretched, cracked or glazed; else check routing and tension

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: pulleys not parallel to deck; check securely attached/no wobble; spin by hand, feel/hear; replace bearing; some pulleys sealed so replace pulley and bearing assembly

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: idler bracket hole wears larger, spring stretches; replace; check bearing and bushing in engagement linkage

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: wood chip can cause extreme vibration; clean deck regularly, remove spindle covers; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat
