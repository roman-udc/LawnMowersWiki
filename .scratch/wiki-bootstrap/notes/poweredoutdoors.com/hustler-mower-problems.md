## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Prevent problems by replacing filters, spark plugs, hydraulic oil and engine oil, cleaning the mower and inspecting for failures/leaks. Several times a season scrape the deck and lubricate spindles and castor wheels.

## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Hustler won't start" (zero-turn or walk-behind)
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting
- fact: Needs spark, fuel and air. Table rows: no gas - fill; old fuel - drain, fresh fuel plus Sea Foam; clogged cap vent - replace cap; plug dirty/broken/bad connection/wrong gap - replace and gap to spec; plugged air filter - clean/replace; plugged fuel filter - replace; bad or leaking fuel pump no longer creates pressure - replace; blocked fuel lines - carb cleaner and compressed air, replace if needed; dirty carb from bad fuel and ethanol - clean components, replace if needed; battery/cables/corrosion - charge, replace if won't hold, tighten, clean; safety switch - test/replace; ignition switch or coil - test/replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Hustler starts then dies / dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Table: cooling fins packed with dirt, oil, grass can't cool block - clean, replace broken fins; too much oil raises crankcase pressure and overheats, too little doesn't lubricate - correct level, see a small engine mechanic if that doesn't fix it; dirty/damaged plug - clean/replace, secure wires, gap correctly; coil windings separate and short - check continuity with ohm meter, replace; choke restricts air after warm-up - correct position; plugged fuel tank vent - replace; clogged deck strains engine - scrape periodically.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Hustler blowing white, black or blue smoke"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-head-gasket
- safety: Stop running the mower first; let it cool before working on it.
- fact: Look for plugged air filter, too much oil in the crankcase, leaking engine gasket; more serious: low oil or internal problems (piston ring, valve train, gasket) needing a small engine mechanic or Hustler dealer. Too much oil: drain to manufacturer's level. Low oil: adding oil often too late.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Hustler zero turn won't move or go forward"
- alias: "weak hydrostatic transmission"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: First check the drive bypass/release valves at the rear (they disengage the hydros so you can push the mower; mower free-rolls but won't drive); must be in operating position. Then belt, tensioner spring, tensioner pulley.
- fact: Weak hydro feel: bad drive belt (replace frayed/cracked/worn), bad tensioner pulley (replace and grease tensioner arm), low or old hydraulic oil (change at Hustler's recommended intervals, keep full).
- fact: Hot fluid can signal hydrostatic damage; if fine cold but weak when hot, have it checked at a Hustler dealership.
- fact: Air must be bled after changing hydraulic fluid per the Hustler operator's manual.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Hustler won't drive straight / steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fact: Unequal tire pressure pulls to one side; tires on each side need equal pressure, filled to manufacturer spec. Bad dampers make mower jerk to one side going forward; replace. Tracking: when one tire moves faster than the other adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed (see owner's manual).

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: Adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change rotation speed of the tire that's faster/slower; refer to owner's manual (no further steps).

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Hustler shakes and vibrates"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Causes: loose hardware, debris, ball bearing in the clutch failed, worn pulley bearings. Clutch: when a bearing falls out of the clutch it vibrates; replace clutch. String or long weeds wrapped around blade shaft: remove, check for spindle damage. Bad spindle housing bearing: replace bearing or the whole spindle housing. Blades must be balanced before installing.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Hustler bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, worn-blade-sail, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, bent-deck-or-deck-shell, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast
- fact: Check tire pressures first (a low tire stops the deck sitting level), then deck: blades, deck belt, spindle, spindle/pulley bearings, deck shell. Table lists also "operator error".

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "Hustler leaking gas / smell of gas"
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: Small puddle, or only a strong gas smell because it evaporated. Carb can leak from failed gasket or stuck float. Fuel filter softens/degrades from old fuel; fuel pump wears and leaks at seams; poly tank leaks at seams; shut-off valves prone to leak with time; fuel lines dry/crack or get punctured by a foreign object or the line clamps; gas cap seal fails and gas splashes out.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Hustler clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Click or hum suggests bad solenoid; also check power to the solenoid (battery, cables, terminals, ground); else starter motor.
- fact: Battery: place on charger if under 12.7 V for a 12 V battery; replace if it won't hold a charge. Ground: remove corrosion; ensure battery ground cable and solenoid ground cable make good contact (a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded). Loose wires give poor continuity; replace damaged cables.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Hustler blades won't engage"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: Worn belt (worn, cracked, shiny glazed) - replace; belt off pulleys - replace worn idler arm, spring, bad pulleys, stretched belt; PTO switch failed - replace; clutch - replace; manual-engagement clutch: replace worn clutch cable, lever, springs, linkages; weak battery under 12.7 V charge; seat safety switch may not detect operator - replace; blown fuse - replace with the same amperage, mechanic if it keeps blowing.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Hustler running rough, sputters, bogs down"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Look for fuel/air restriction, intermittent spark, extra engine load. Fixes: drain old fuel and refill with gas plus stabilizer; clean fuel line with carb cleaner and compressed air else replace; replace fuel filter; remove carb to clean, rebuild or replace; replace gas cap that doesn't vent; wire-brush a dirty plug, replace if very dark, worn or damaged; slow ground speed in tall, thick or wet grass; throttle at highest position when engaging blades; scrape deck; sharpen dull blades.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- alias: "Hustler engine overheats"
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast
- fact: Use an air-cooled engine oil of right viscosity that is high in zinc; keep level full. Wrong oil: SAE30 or 10W-30 in most conditions; different viscosity may be needed in very warm conditions. Check cooling fins, engine shroud, engine guard; missing engine guard stops air circulating around engine - replace/secure. Plugged air filter - replace; plugged deck - scrape; dull blades - sharpen/replace; overworking: slow ground speed, avoid wet grass, double or triple-cut an overgrown lawn.
