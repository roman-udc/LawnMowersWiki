## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Bad Boy mower feels like a jackhammer"
- alias: "my Bad Boy mower shakes / vibrates"
- alias: "deck shakes when blades engage"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Source summary list of vibration causes: loose hardware, bushings and linkages; loose or worn engine mounting bolts; failed clutch ball bearing; unbalanced blades; failed bearings in pulleys or spindle housing; debris lodged in the mower. Not fixing it can cause additional damage.
- safety: Before any work follow the owner's manual: set the parking brake, remove the ignition key, disconnect the spark plug wires.
- safety: Wear good work gloves when handling the blade (sharp edges).
- pro: Source advises a professional if you lack skills/knowledge/condition to repair safely; local Bad Boy dealership or repair shop.
- fact: Source links a Bad Boy troubleshooting hub "Common Bad Boy Lawn Mower Problems & Solutions".

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Engine sits on mounting bolts (motor mounts) that absorb engine vibration.
- confirm: Inspect the bolts for looseness.
- fix: Tighten loose bolts; replace worn or broken ones.
- brand: Bad Boy push mower: check the engine bolts if excessive vibration appears once the blade is engaged.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (blades wobble when engaged; vibration through deck; possible knocking sound)
- confirm: With gloves on, grab each end of the blade and rock it up and down; look for movement, listen for knocking.
- fix: If there is movement, remove the blade and spindle housing, inspect for bearing or spindle damage and repair.
- fact: Bad bearings or a bent spindle in the spindle housing cause blade wobble. Unbalanced blades can damage spindle housing bearings.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (wrong or unbalanced blade)
- fact: Wrong or unbalanced blade spinning at very high speed causes vibration and damage. An unbalanced blade has one end heavier than the other, from uneven wear or removing more metal from one side when sharpening.
- fact: Blade needs the correct center hole size and the correct length.
- tip: Author uses OEM blades to be sure of fit. Aftermarket blades with matching length and center hole may still not fit; physically match an aftermarket blade to an OEM blade. Author bought blades with correct measurements from two suppliers and one supplier's did not fit.
- fix: Balance blades with a blade balancer before installing.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: String, wire or even a long narrow weed can wrap around the blade shaft and make the deck shake.
- fix: Inspect the shaft and remove wrapped material, then check spindle and spindle housing for damage.
- tip: Walk the lawn before mowing to remove rocks, toys, sticks.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (belt slack, not seated on pulleys)
- fix: Replace the deck belt if stretched, cracked or glazed. If it looks good, check routing and tension to remove slack.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (pulley wobbles; one side sits higher off the deck)
- confirm: Check each pulley is securely attached and does not wobble. Rotate each pulley slowly by hand; feel for restriction and listen for bearing noise.
- fix: Replace the bearing. Some pulleys use sealed bearings; then replace the whole pulley and bearing assembly.
- fact: Pulleys not parallel to the deck can keep the belt from seating.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (loose idler bracket and spring)
- fact: Idler bracket and spring wear: the spring hole in the bracket wears larger and the spring stretches.
- fix: Replace a worn idler bracket or spring. Check the bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage and replace if worn.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Even a wood chip can cause extreme vibration. Debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat and parallel to the deck.
- fix: Remove spindle covers, clean the deck regularly, inspect all parts for lodged items.
- tip: Clean the mower after each mowing to prevent vibration and corrosion from moisture in debris.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- tip: Clean after each mowing to keep debris off (prevents vibration and moisture corrosion). Source links "How to Clean Your Mower for Best Performance".
