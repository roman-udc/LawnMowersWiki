## page: procedures/start-a-zero-turn-mower
- fact: a safety interlock won't allow starting unless PTO switch is off, brake is on, and mower is in neutral
- confirm (pre-start checks): fuel shut-off valve(s) open; engine oil at full on dipstick; hydraulic oil level in overflow tanks; sufficient fuel; clean debris and dirt from engine, exhaust system and cutting deck; air filter clean and undamaged; safety interlock system working; seat belt and ROPS (rollover protection) in good, working condition
- step 1: move motion control (steering) levers to the widest/outward position = neutral
- step 2: engage parking brake (lever or foot pedal; if none, brake may engage when levers are placed in neutral)
- step 3: push PTO switch down (off); it turns the blades on, must be off to start
- step 4: throttle lever halfway between slow (turtle) and fast (rabbit) if there is a separate choke lever or knob; with no separate choke, put throttle at full to engage the choke for a cold start
- step 5 (cold engine): choke ON: needs rich mixture (more fuel, less air), the choke plate closes restricting air; once started, move choke to off; lever: to choke-open position; knob: pull up (as the Source words it)
- step 6 (warm engine): choke OFF so plate stays open; lever to choke-closed position; knob: push down (as the Source words it; note Source's lever/knob wording is as written)
- step 7: turn key to start; release when engine starts; don't hold start more than 10 seconds; wait 1 minute before retrying
- fact (stop procedure): 1 bring to a stop; 2 steering levers to neutral (outward); 3 set parking brake; 4 throttle halfway between slow and fast; 5 let engine run at least 30 seconds; 6 key to off
- fact: if it still won't start suspect electrical, fuel or air intake: old fuel, weak battery, bad starter solenoid, loose wiring, dirty carburetor

## page: guides/zero-turn-safety
- fact: safety interlock requires PTO off, brake on, neutral to start; pre-start checks include seat belt and ROPS in good working condition and interlock system working

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: interlock conditions above; never hold key in start more than 10 s
