## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Cub Cadet running rough"
- alias: "mower sputtering / bogging down"
- symptom: engine not getting enough air, fuel or spark; or overworked by fast ground speed, low engine speed, plugged deck, dull blades
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- safety: wait for engine to cool and moving parts to stop; remove spark plug wire before repairs; follow Cub Cadet operator's manual

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: plugged filter starves engine of air, rough running, and can overheat the engine
- fact: filter keeps dirt out of the intake; always run with a filter; check it in pre-mow routine
- tip: replace once a year and clean several times per season; more often in very dusty conditions
- fix: clean if slightly dirty; replace if extremely dirty, damaged, or no longer sealing over the intake

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step (paper) 1: remove filter from housing
- step (paper) 2: wipe dirt out of housing without letting any fall into the intake
- step (paper) 3: tap filter on a solid surface to knock dirt out
- step (paper) 4: hold to a light; if light shines through the paper reuse, otherwise buy new
- step (paper) 5: reinstall and attach cover
- step (paper) 6: if a foam pre-filter is also used, wash with mild detergent and water, rinse, air dry; DO NOT apply oil to the pre-filter
- step (foam) 1: remove filter; wipe housing without letting dirt fall into the intake
- step (foam) 2: inspect; dark spots, tears, or dry/brittle foam means replace
- step (foam) 3: wash in water with mild detergent, rinse out soap
- step (foam) 4: lay flat to dry (sun speeds it up)
- step (foam) 5: when completely dry coat with foam air filter oil, fully covered but not dripping; wring out or blot with paper towel if too much
- step (foam) 6: reinstall and attach cover
- mistake: oil goes only on a primary foam filter, never on a foam pre-filter used with a paper filter

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke restricts air mixed with gas to start a cold engine; once warm it must go to the off position for extra air
- symptom: choke in wrong position or stuck open/closed gives rough running and bogging
- confirm: choke lever closed for cold start, open to start/run warm; check the choke plate actually opens and closes when moving the lever
- fix: carburetor cleaner to free a stuck choke; replace a worn choke cable

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas begins to break down as soon as 30 days after purchase
- fact: most gas contains ethanol (plant-based), attracts moisture; water/ethanol mix leaves varnish and sticky deposits when it evaporates, causing restrictions and component failures
- fix: replace old fuel; add fresh gas plus fuel system cleaner/stabilizer such as Sea Foam Motor Treatment
- fact: use gas with minimum 87 octane and maximum 10% ethanol; ethanol-free is best

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: use the fuel shut-off valve or hose crimp pliers to stop/start flow and check flow out of the line into a container placed lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill without a pump)
- fix: shut off fuel, remove the line, spray carburetor cleaner in it, blow compressed air through; repeat until clear
- fix: replace with line of same length and diameter if clog won't clear or line is cracking

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: most Cub Cadets use an inline filter between fuel lines; annual replacement item
- symptom: plugged with dirt, carb starves, engine bogs and runs rough
- fix: replace; install with the arrow on the filter pointing in direction of fuel flow

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: first make sure fuel reaches the carburetor (check line, filter, and fuel pump if fitted); then remove air filter, spray carburetor cleaner into the air intake and let engine run; if it runs well and then goes sluggish again, the carburetor needs cleaning
- fix: clean carburetor (article links How to Clean Your Cub Cadet Lawn Mower Carburetor)

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- brand: on Cub Cadet the tank vent is built into the gas cap
- confirm: when running rough loosen the fuel cap; if it runs better the cap isn't venting; retighten, wait for it to go sluggish again, loosen and listen; if rough running stops the cap is likely the problem
- fix: buy and install a new cap

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: dirty plug gives intermittent spark and rough running; wrong gap or loose plug wire do the same
- fix: remove and inspect; very dark, burnt electrode or damaged: replace; gap to engine manufacturer spec; attach wire securely

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: thick, tall or wet grass needs slower speed than dry short grass; too fast strains engine, bogs it, gives poor cut
- fix: slow down when engine bogs
- tip: for tall grass make multiple cuts: highest cutting height first, then lower for later passes

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: run engine at highest speed when engaging blades or it runs rough
- fix: set throttle lever to highest speed (rabbit icon on many mowers)

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- symptom: deck packed with clippings makes engine work harder, sluggish
- fix: scrape with a deck scraper or wire brush; avoid cutting wet/damp grass (clumps)
- tip: deck spray helps some but is not a miracle solution

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: dull blades tear grass leaving brown tips a couple of days later; magnify plugged deck problem since more power is needed to turn dull blades through packed grass
- fix: remove, sharpen and balance blades (see Cub Cadet blade article)

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- step 1: remove old dirty inline filter
- step 2: install new inline filter with arrow pointing in direction of fuel flow
- fact: replace annually

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-line
- fact: replace with same length and diameter line when clog can't be removed or line is cracking
