## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: also check for a faulty fuel gauge or a fuel leak. Fill with fresh fuel; repair leak or gauge.

## page: causes/fuel-valve-off
- fact: valve may have been shut when working on fuel system or storing; must be in on/open position.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down as soon as 30 days after purchase; gummy deposits restrict fuel.
- fix: drain with a fuel siphon pump; add fresh fuel with ethanol no greater than 10% plus an additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment stabilizes, reduces moisture, cleans the fuel system).

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: clogged vent -> vacuum restricts fuel; run with and without the cap; starts without but shuts down after refitting -> replace cap.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- brand: Honda push mowers have a blade control lever safety feature that stops the mower when the operator is absent; must be pulled back and held against the handlebar to start and run.
- fix: hold the blade lever against the handlebar while turning the key (electric start) or pulling the recoil (manual start). Releasing it stops the engine and the blades.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace; arrow on housing points in direction of fuel flow: toward carburetor, away from tank.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- step 1: stop fuel flow with shutoff valve (or clamp the line if no valve).
- step 2: remove the end of a hose section furthest from the tank into a container.
- step 3: restore flow; container must be lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill).
- step 4: no good flow -> stop flow, remove line, spray carb cleaner, blow compressed air.
- step 5: can't clear -> replace line.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: verify fuel reaches the carburetor; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; if it starts, runs and shuts down, disassemble to clean.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace annually; check and clean regularly; replace when very dirty or damaged.
- paper: remove; wipe housing and cover (no dirt into intake); tap to loosen dirt; light test; replace if no good light, very dirty, damaged, or it no longer seals the intake; reinstall.
- foam primary: inspect for damage; if good, clean in soapy water, rinse until water runs clear; squeeze and dry; lightly saturate in clean engine oil, squeeze out excess; reinstall. Do not add oil to a pre-filter used with a paper filter (damages the paper filter). Check operator's manual for which fluid to use.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: dirty tip, cracked porcelain, burnt electrode; clean deposits; replace if very dark or damaged. Gap per Honda operator's manual; wrong gap or loose plug wires cause starting problems.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: plug good first; ohmmeter continuity; break -> replace.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fix: restring if rope unstrung; replace broken pulley, springs, clips; price a whole recoil replacement first.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: electric start: try the manual recoil; if it starts, the electric start has the problem.
- brand: Honda battery charger is optional on most Honda mowers. Charge by plugging the charger into the port connector on the handle closest to the battery, then into a 120-volt outlet.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- brand: Honda electric start has 2 fuses: the larger amp fuse bad -> electric starter won't work; the lower amp fuse bad -> battery won't charge. If fuses fine, replace battery; if still no charge, experienced mechanic.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fix: test with multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic on-off switch actuating the starter motor; test if you hear a click or hum when turning the key or solenoid wires get hot and smoke; replace if bad.
