## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Scag starts then dies"
- alias: "mower quits running"
- alias: "sputters and shuts off"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, dirty-cooling-fins, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Summary: plugged air filter; restricted fuel flow; dirty plug; oil too high or low; increased load; overheating.
- pro: internal engine problem -> small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas starts breaking down as quickly as 30 days after purchase. Ethanol (corn or other high-starch plants) attracts moisture, corrodes fuel components, leaves gummy substance; ethanol/water separates and sinks to tank bottom; burns very hot through engine and can damage it.
- fact: choose min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; avoid E15, E30, E85. Use within 30 days; stabilize if more (add Sea Foam Motor Treatment while fuel is fresh).
- fix: drain with a fuel siphon pump; refill with fresh fuel + additive (stabilizes, cleans fuel system, reduces moisture).

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: starves engine of air, can quit running. Start each season with new filter; check and clean several times. Never run without filter - dirt can cause significant engine damage.
- step (paper): remove cover; remove filter without letting dirt in intake; clean housing and cover; tap on solid surface; hold to light - see light, reuse; else new; reinstall and cap.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: deposits from old/dirty fuel clog; runs badly and may die. Replace annually, more often with dirty fuel.
- fix: remove and install new; keep a rag for fuel spill; arrow on housing points in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: use fuel shut-off valve or pinch-off pliers; stop flow; isolate section; remove end furthest from tank into container; container must be lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill without pump); start flow and watch; good flow -> shut off, reattach, check next section.
- fix: shut off flow, remove line; spray carb cleaner to loosen, blow compressed air through; repeat; replace line if cannot clear.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: varnish/deposits from old gas; sluggish and stall.
- confirm: first confirm fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; starts, runs, then dies = disassemble and clean.
- tip: avoid using starter fluid (link in Source).

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: cooling fins push air to keep block and cylinder cool; plugged or broken fins let engine overheat and shut down.
- fix: caution, engine hot; remove debris from fins, replace broken fins; clear debris around engine and shroud; ensure heat shield attached securely.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: excess oil raises crankcase pressure; crankshaft and rod push through extra oil; engine overheats and dies.
- fix: remove a little oil through drain plug or oil filter, or with an oil evacuator or turkey baster through the fill area; check dipstick; add/remove until at full line.
- tools: oil evacuator or turkey baster.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: low oil from leak or engine burning oil; friction builds heat; engine overheats and dies; internal parts can melt.
- pro: if shut down from lack of oil, probably significant damage; take to experienced small engine mechanic to tear down and test. Possible damage: blown gasket, burnt valve, damaged internals.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: fouled plug fires intermittently -> sluggish or dies; also bad gap, damage, loose wire.
- fix: clean tip; replace if very dark, burnt electrode or damaged; gap to engine manufacturer spec; secure wires. Replace plugs annually.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: windings separate and short when hot; no voltage to plug; mower dies after running a while.
- confirm: ohmmeter, break in continuity -> replace.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke reduces air for cold start; must be moved to off after warm or mower sputters and dies.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: remove or loosen cap and run; if it runs well, reinstall cap and let run; sputters and shuts off after a while = clogged vent. Determine whether vent is on tank or in cap.
- fix: replace plugged tank vent.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: plugged deck makes engine work harder, overheats, shuts down.
- fix: regularly scrape deck; avoid wet mowing; deck spray minimizes (not a miracle product).

## page: problems/battery-keeps-dying
- fact: run Scag at high engine speed; don't idle long periods (engine power needed to charge battery). If battery keeps needing charging after confirming battery and wiring good and tight, suspect charging system; link to testing article.
- causes-named: faulty-charging-system, bad-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground
- pro: replace bad battery; charging system faults - experienced mechanic (many components; electrical parts usually not returnable).
