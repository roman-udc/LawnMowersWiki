## page: problems/engine-overheats
- symptom: smell of mower running hot, or visible smoking; stop running it and find the cause.
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- safety: stop mower, remove ignition key, wait for engine to cool, remove spark plug boots before repairs; follow operator manual.
- pro: if trouble continues after an oil change, low oil may have caused extensive damage; Kubota servicing dealer or engine dealer.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- brand: small engines on Kubota riding/zero-turn mowers are Kohler, Briggs & Stratton and Kawasaki; use air-cooled engine oil with a high concentration of zinc (cooling agent). Car oil has little zinc.
- fact: SAE30 or 10W-30 typically recommended; 20W-50 in higher ambient temperatures (this article does not mention 5W-30). Kawasaki oil viscosity/temperature chart; follow operator manual.
- fix: drain wrong oil, add correct air-cooled oil.
- symptom (low): friction heat; oil thickens if run low; check oil before each use.
- fact: consistently low oil: oil leak (replace gaskets; check oil filter seal), burning oil (plugged air filter), using oil (overheating, valve or ring problem: repair center).
- fix: correct slightly low level; full oil change if it is the cause of hot smell.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: fan on top of engine pulls cool air in and blows it down through hot parts and out the bottom; fins push air over cylinder head and block.
- fix: remove engine cover, clean fins, replace damaged, clean dirt from cover and around engine; annual maintenance.
- mistake: washing can push dirt and water into the engine area; don't spray water into the engine.

## page: causes/bad-cooling-shroud
- fact: engine guard keeps cool air circulating; without it cool air exits. Fix: correct position, tighten/replace mounting screws, replace missing/damaged guard.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- confirm: shine a flashlight through the paper element; good light and good condition: tap out dirt on a solid surface and reinstall; dull/no light, damaged, very dirty/dark: replace.
- brand: some Kubota engines use an inner air filter (narrow filter inside a canister-style paper filter) or a foam pre-cleaner wrapping a paper filter; they add protection.
- tip: replace annually; clean several times a season; more often in dusty conditions or heavy use. Don't stretch filter life (author opinion).

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fix: scrape with deck scraper/wire brush/putty knife; check frequently; deck coatings help but don't eliminate buildup; avoid wet grass; full throttle.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fix: sharpen about twice a season or about every 25 hours for the average homeowner; balance.

## page: causes/cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fix: slower ground speed; avoid wet grass; double/triple-cut (highest deck first then lower slightly); full throttle.

## page: guides/engine-cooling-times
- fact: let engine cool before storing in garage/storage; best outdoors; lift hood on riding mower; run under no load to start cooling.
