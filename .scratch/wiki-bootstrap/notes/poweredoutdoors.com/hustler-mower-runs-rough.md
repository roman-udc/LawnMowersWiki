## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Hustler runs rough / bogs down"
- symptom: not enough air, fuel or spark; or overworked engine
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- safety: let engine cool; remove spark plug wires before repairs; see operator's manual

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- tip: replace once a year, clean several times a season; more often in very dusty conditions or heavier-than-homeowner use
- fix: clean if a little dirty; replace if extremely dirty, damaged or no longer sealing
- step (paper) 1: remove from housing; 2: wipe housing without dirt into intake; 3: tap on solid surface; 4: hold to light, reuse if light shows and filter isn't damaged or covered in oil; 5: reinstall and cover; 6: if foam pre-filter, wash with mild detergent and water, rinse, air dry
- symptom: plugged filter makes engine sluggish and possibly overheat

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: closed for cold start, open to start/run warm; check the choke plate opens/closes with the lever; carb cleaner frees stuck choke; replace worn choke cable

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol and water it attracts separate from gas over time and sink to the bottom of the tank; leaves sticky deposits and is highly corrosive to fuel components
- fix: replace fuel; fresh gas plus cleaner/stabilizer like Sea Foam Motor Treatment; gas min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol, ethanol-free best

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: shut-off valve or hose crimp pliers to start/stop flow; container lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill without a pump)
- fix: shut off fuel, remove line, carb cleaner then compressed air, repeat; replace with same length and diameter if can't clear or cracking

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: inline filter, maintenance part; change annually, sooner if plugged/damaged
- fix: install new with arrow pointing in the direction of fuel flow

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: ensure fuel reaches carb (else check fuel line, filter, fuel pump); remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in intake and run; runs well then sluggish again means clean carb

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- brand: Hustler tank vent is built into the gas cap
- confirm: loosen cap while running rough; if it improves, retighten and wait for it to bog again, loosen and listen
- fix: new Hustler gas cap

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: replace if very dark, burnt electrode or damaged; gap to engine manufacturer spec; reattach wire securely after repairs

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- tip: slow down when engine bogs; for tall grass multiple cuts: highest cutting height first, then lower

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fix: throttle to highest engine speed when engaging blades (rabbit icon)

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fix: scrape deck with scraper or wire brush; avoid wet/damp grass; deck spray helps but isn't a miracle solution

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: tears grass, brown tips days later; magnifies plugged deck load on engine
- fix: remove, sharpen and balance (author links zero turn blade maintenance article)
