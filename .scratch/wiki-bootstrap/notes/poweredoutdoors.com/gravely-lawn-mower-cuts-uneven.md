## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "bad or uneven cut, scalping, leaving ridges"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys (plugged deck), bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell, uneven-lawn (unmapped)
- safety: Remove ignition key and spark plug wires before working on the deck.
- tip: Check tire pressure before anything else; skipping it can lead to deck adjustments that must be reversed.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- symptom: Low tire pressure makes the deck sit unlevel. Make pressure equal to the value on the tire sidewall.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Grass tips turn brown a couple days after mowing = dull blades. Extremely worn, rounded blade ends leave strips uncut. Wear rate depends on conditions: thin grass exposes deck to more dirt and wears blades faster.
- symptom: Bent blade from hitting a root or rock. Chips/gouges: small nicks sharpen out, large need new blade.
- confirm (blade on): park on flat ground, remove key and spark plug boot; measure from one blade tip to ground, rotate blade 180 degrees, measure other tip; more than 1/8 inch variance = replace; repeat for each blade.
- confirm (blade off): lay old blade on a new one; any gap = install new blade.
- mistake: Never straighten a mower blade; it compromises the metal and can crack. A bent blade means checking spindle bearing for impact damage.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- symptom: Sail is the high side; if thinner than center of blade, bad cut. Sail creates air movement and suction under the deck that stands grass up before the blade passes.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail/fin must point up. Upside down can hit the ground, tear up grass, and damage spindle and bearing. Balance blades before installing (unbalanced = uneven cut and vibration).

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Plugged deck can't make suction (deck, baffles and blade airflow). Scrape deck periodically, more often in wet grass (sticks and clumps). Some owners use silicone deck spray; helps but doesn't prevent all sticking.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Key and spark plug boot(s) off; with heavy gloves grab each blade end and rock up and down; movement or a knock = bad spindle housing bearing or bent spindle. Repair or replace spindle housing.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Turn each deck pulley slowly; resistance or noise = replace. Belt must be free of cracks/wear and run securely around pulleys.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Run full throttle whenever the deck is engaged.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Slow ground speed in thick, tall or wet grass; mowing too fast breaks suction and pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Overlap each pass when striping to avoid leaving strips.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Deck shell shaped to create suction; hitting trees/rocks tweaks it and compromises the cut.
