## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Kubota mower isn't getting fuel"
- symptom: engine runs sluggish, quits, or won't start
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- safety: follow Kubota owner's manual
- pro: dealer or repair shop if unsure

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- symptom: fuel starvation also shows as sluggish running or quitting (same causes)

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol attracts moisture, leaves gummy deposits, causes corrosion and premature failure of fuel parts
- fact: diesel Kubota: moisture and sediment corrode and clog fuel components
- fix: siphon tank into a container designed for gasoline (plastics degrade and leak); take old fuel to an authorized recycling center; refill with fuel plus stabilizer
- tip: Sea Foam Motor Treatment stabilizes fuel for up to two years (author's claim) and cleans fuel system
- brand: Kubota gas mowers need unleaded, minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- tip: replace annually at full service
- fix: replace; arrow on plastic filter points in direction of fuel flow

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: start/stop flow with shut-off valve at tank bottom or hose pinch-off pliers; check each section to isolate
- fix: remove clogged section, carb cleaner, compressed air, repeat; replace if can't clear or dry and cracked
- fact: article title/intro also mentions kinked lines

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: pump needed when carb sits higher than tank; most are vacuum pumps (pressure from crankcase)
- confirm 1: first confirm fuel reaches the pump: stop flow, remove line from pump inlet, put in container lower than tank, restart flow; no flow means blockage in lines/filter
- confirm 2: reattach inlet line; remove carb line into container; start flow and mower; expect steady or pulsating flow; if not, replace pump
- brand: injection fuel pump: read pressure from the pressure hose with a fuel pressure gauge and compare to Kubota spec in operator's manual; replace if lower

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: ensure fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake; if it starts and won't continue running, remove and clean carb
- fact: clogged small parts incl. the fuel jet

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: run with and without cap; fine without but eventually sluggish/off with cap means plugged
- fix: clean the vent; else new Kubota cap
