## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "Kobalt electric battery mower won't start"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery (wrong size battery, not installed correctly, dead/won't hold charge), bad-charger, overheated-battery, incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, bad-motor
- safety: always remove the battery before any repair or before working on the deck.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: use the recommended battery size and capacity; wrong battery may not power the motor and may damage the mower.
- fact: battery should slide into grooves of the compartment; on most Kobalt mowers you hear a click when latches snap in.
- confirm: some models show charge via LED lights on the battery, on the mower's indicator panel, or only when placed on the charger.
- symptom: fully charged battery no longer holds charge as long, charges very slowly, or gets extremely hot: replace. Failure from age, extreme temperatures, mechanical failure.

## page: causes/bad-charger
- fact: charger uses standard 120V outlet; plug style must match outlet; do not modify plug. Insert battery so contacts engage the charger; lights indicate charge level.
- confirm: check charger-to-outlet connection.

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- fact: mower has a protection system that shuts the mower off when the battery gets too hot (especially in hot conditions). Let the battery cool before restarting.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- brand: Kobalt push mower starting: with battery in place, push the safety button; pull the safety bar (bail switch) to the handlebar to start the motor; release the safety button; mower shuts off when you release the safety bar. The safety button prevents children starting it.
- fact: on some Kobalt mowers the side rails must be fully extended with locking clamps in place and handle locked.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: test each switch by temporarily bypassing with a jumper wire; replace bad switch (including bail switch).
- safety: never operate without all safety switches working.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- confirm: wiggle wires to check for a short; use multimeter.
- fix: repair loose/pinched connections with a wire nut; remove battery first.
- fact: repairing a damaged wire yourself can void the warranty; check with Kobalt.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: grass under deck can inhibit blade rotation and prevent starting; scrape, remove battery first. Avoid wet grass, raise cutting height in thick/tall grass, slow down.

## page: causes/bad-motor
- pro: damaged start switch or motor: take to a professional repair shop; Kobalt customer service 1-888-356-2258 for authorized repair locations. Components pricey; repair may cost more than the mower is worth.
- fact: commonly replaced electric mower parts: batteries, blades, safety switches.

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- fact: article mentions dangers of electric mowers; remove battery before repairs; warranty can be voided by self-repair.
