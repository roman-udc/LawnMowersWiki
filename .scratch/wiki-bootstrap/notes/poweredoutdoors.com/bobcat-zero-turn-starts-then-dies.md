## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Bobcat zero turn starts then dies / keeps shutting down"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: remove spark plug wires before repairs; follow operator's manual.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol gas attracts moisture, corrosive to fuel system; evaporation leaves varnish; mix separates and sinks to tank bottom.
- tip: buy and use fresh fuel within 30 days; unleaded 87 octane minimum with minimum 10% ethanol content (Bobcat tip as stated); avoid high-ethanol E15 and E85; store fuel dry, away from combustibles; mix with additive to keep stable.
- fix: drain old gas into approved container (fuel siphon pump); mix fresh gas with additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment); fill, run engine so treated fuel works through system.

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Bobcat per Source: unleaded min 87 octane, min 10% ethanol; avoid E15 and E85.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: consume fuel within 30 days; store in dry location away from combustibles; use stabilizer additive.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: dirt plugged filter -> engine dies from lack of fuel.
- fix: replace; if filter has an arrow it must point in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fact: gummy substance from old fuel blocks line; dies after running a while.
- confirm: use shut-off valve to start/stop flow while checking each section for flow.
- fix: turn off supply, remove line, spray carb cleaner, blow compressed air; if can't clear install new line.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: most zero-turn pumps are vacuum pumps, build pressure off engine block and pump fuel to carb. Fail mechanically or from degradation by old gas.
- confirm: verify flow into pump; turn off flow, remove line from carburetor, put in container, turn on flow, start mower; steady or pulsating flow = pump OK.
- fix: replace if no good flow.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: fuel reaches carb; remove air filter; spray carb cleaner in intake; start; if starts, runs fine, then shuts down, carb is suspect.
- fix: remove, disassemble, clean (links to zero-turn carb cleaning article) or take to Bobcat dealer / small engine shop.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace once a year, check several times per season; clean more often in dusty conditions.
- step paper: remove element; wipe housing with clean dry cloth without letting dirt into intake; tap against hard surface; do NOT use compressed air (damages paper); hold to light - replace if light not shining through, damaged, or oily; reinstall and cover.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step 1-5 as above; mistake: compressed air on paper element.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fact: overheating when cooling system not clean.
- fix: remove dirt on fins, replace broken fins; clean around block and shroud; heat shield correctly installed.

## page: procedures/clean-the-cooling-fins
- step: remove dirt on fins; replace broken fins; clear dirt around engine block and shroud; check heat shield installed correctly.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: check oil before each use (dipstick); low oil -> friction, heat, burning oil and parts, overheating shutdown.
- pro: adding oil usually does not fix it after shutdown from lubrication loss; significant engine damage likely; experienced small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: too much oil builds crankcase pressure, mower shuts down.
- fix: remove oil via drain plug, oil filter, or evacuator/turkey baster at fill; repeat until correct.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: dirty or wrong gap, loose wires -> sluggish or dies. Clean; replace if very dark or damaged; gap per engine maker spec; wires secure.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: windings separate and short when hot; plug lacks voltage; dies after running a while. Ohm meter for continuity break; replace if broken.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: leaving choke on after warm-up kills engine.
- fix: check lever position; if still airflow trouble with correct lever and clean filter, check for stuck choke plate (must open/close with lever); carb cleaner loosens stuck choke.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap vent plugged -> vacuum in tank, gas can't flow; starts but dies after running a while. (Source's SOLUTION is blank.)

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: plugged deck strains engine, overheats, shuts down; dull blades magnify.
- fix: scrape deck regularly, sharpen blades; also gives nicer cut.
