## page: guides/winterizing-and-storage
- fact: Purpose: prevent moisture collecting in fuel system, engine oil and electrical components, which causes runability issues and corrosion.
- step 1: Gather supplies: engine oil (1-3 quarts; check manual for type/quantity) and filter if used, filter wrench, socket or spanner wrenches for drain plug, drain pan, air filter, fuel filter, spark plug(s), wire brush, deck scraper or putty knife, rubber and heavy work gloves, safety glasses, rags, fuel stabilizer, gas container if draining tank, cardboard/newspaper for work surface.
- step 2: Change engine oil (see procedure below).
- step 3: Replace air filter.
- step 4: Replace fuel filter (arrow in direction of fuel flow); check fuel system for leaks: follow fuel line from tank, check for cracks/leaks in hose and clamp areas, replace cracked lines; check fuel pump and carburetor for leaks, so storage area isn't filled with fumes.
- step 5: Replace spark plug (best once a year; dirty plug can foul, misfire or no start).
- step 6: Remove debris around engine and cooling fins; if hydrostatic transmission, clean around fans and transmission.
- step 7: Clean top of deck; check belt and pulleys. Some mowers need deck removal (see manual). Remove belt covers, clear debris under pulleys and brackets. Replace belt if glazed, stretched or shredded. Pulleys flat and parallel to deck; turn by hand; replace if restriction or bearing noise.
- step 8: Scrape deck underside and sharpen blades. Check spindle bearings: gloves, rock blade up/down; movement or knocking = remove spindle housing and inspect; some mowers allow bearing replacement, others whole spindle assembly. Sharpen or replace blades (author replaces yearly, keeps good old set as spare). Scrape deck with scraper, putty knife or wire brush; remove all debris because it holds moisture and rusts the deck.
- step 9: Add fuel stabilizer or drain tank. Drain: siphon out as much as possible, run engine until it shuts off and won't restart. Author doesn't drain; uses stabilizer (STA-BIL or Sea Foam) per bottle directions in a full tank, then runs engine a few minutes to circulate. Stabilizers keep fuel up to 2 years. Without stabilizer, spring starting problems from gummed fuel system.
- step 10: Fully charge or remove battery. A 100% charged battery won't freeze. To remove: gloves and eye protection; mower off; remove hold-down brackets; adjustable wrench, turn black negative terminal counterclockwise to loosen and remove cable, then red positive; inspect terminals/cables, wire brush corrosion; store in cool dry place away from combustibles.
- step 11: Tire pressure: inflate to manual's pressure to minimize flat spots from sitting; can add a couple of PSI higher since pressure drops in cold. Flat spots usually work out in use; sometimes tire must be replaced. Low pressure in storage worsens flat spotting.
- step 12: Check for missing/unsecured parts; secure hardware; order missing parts.
- step 13: Grease zerks (wheels, front axle, idler arm pivots) and lubricate linkages, brake pedals, deck spindles, pivot points; see manual for lubrication points.
- step 14: Wash with garden hose; dry completely before storage (moisture corrodes electrical components). Dry in sun/breeze; leaf blower or air compressor with blower tip helps clear water from electrical components.
- step 15: Store in cool dry place away from combustibles; tarp or drop cloth indoors; waterproof cover outdoors.
- step 16: Rodent prevention: wires coated with soy products attract rodents; mothballs around mower or author's Grandpa Gus's Mouse Repellent (natural; packets and spray on wires); choose products safe for pets.
- tip: Storage locations: garage/pole barn (tarp); basement/house: never with fuel, away from combustibles; under deck: rain gets through boards, use heavy-duty marine-grade cover; shed: measure mower to fit the door.

## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- step 1: Warm oil: run engine a couple of minutes, shut off (warm oil drains faster).
- step 2: Disconnect spark plug lead(s) and remove ignition key; don't reconnect until all maintenance done.
- step 3: Clean around oil filter, fill area and drain plug area.
- step 4: Drain pan under drain plug or hose; remove plug, drain.
- step 5: Replace plug once oil stops.
- step 6: If filter used, remove old one; thin film of fresh oil on new filter seal; install.
- step 7: Fill with oil per manufacturer's directions.

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- fact: Replace once a year.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: Replace for next season or clean and reuse.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter

## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- fact: Do at winterization; check spindle bearings by rocking blade.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- fact: Full charge before storage (a fully charged battery will not freeze) or remove to cool dry storage.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Stabilizer (STA-BIL, Sea Foam) keeps fuel up to 2 years; add to full tank, run a few minutes.

- fact: Wash with hose then dry thoroughly.
