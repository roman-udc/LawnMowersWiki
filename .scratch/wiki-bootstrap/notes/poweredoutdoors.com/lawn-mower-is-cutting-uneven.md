## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "lawn mower cuts uneven"
- alias: "lawn turns brown a day or two after cutting"
- causes-named: bad-tire (damaged/loose wheel), wrong-cutting-height, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, bent-crankshaft, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- safety: remove spark plug wire and wear heavy gloves when working at the deck.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- fact: loose or damaged wheel makes the mower wobble. Fix: tighten loose hardware; replace damaged wheel with same size.

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- fact: settings must match left and right, else one side sits lower, deck unlevel, taller cut on one side.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: bent from impact with stump/rock; blade not level/parallel cuts unevenly. Check blade hardware: loose blade wobbles.
- confirm: remove spark plug wire, remove blade and lay on a new blade; gaps/noticeable bends mean replace.
- mistake: never straighten a bent blade; weakens metal, piece can break off and fly out.
- fix: replace blade extremely worn or with gouges in cutting edge; otherwise remove, sharpen, balance, reinstall. Sharpen every 25 hours, more in sandy conditions.
- fact: dull edge tears grass; lawn browns a day or two later.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: sail (high sides) helps create air movement to stand grass tall; replace blade when sail shows significant wear.

## page: causes/bent-crankshaft
- symptom: uneven cut on a push mower.
- confirm: spark plug removed, clamp holding the safety lever to the handle to disable flywheel brake, carefully spin blade and watch the center bolt; if bolt moves all over, crankshaft most likely bent. Or remove blade and blade adapter, turn crankshaft, and measure that shaft stays centered (dial indicator or stationary object); shifts to one side = bent.
- fix: replace the mower; replacing crankshaft costs more than a new mower.
- mistake: bending crankshaft back is a significant safety risk.
- safety: protect hands from blade.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: clogged deck restricts air movement/suction; scrape periodically, avoid wet grass.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: throttle in high position; if power lost, small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: match speed to conditions; too fast pushes grass over, worse on bumpy ground.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: deck designed to maximize blade airflow; repair damaged deck to original shape with no holes or punctures.
