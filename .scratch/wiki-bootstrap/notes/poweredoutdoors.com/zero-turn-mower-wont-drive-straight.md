## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "zero turn won't drive straight"
- alias: "zero-turn pulls to the left/right"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, (tracking adjustment), brake-hung-up, air-in-hydraulic-system, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, bad-hydrostatic-transmission, unbalanced-load
- tip: compensating with steering levers isn't ideal; fix the cause, which could be a bigger issue

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: low tire makes it pull; also check left and right drive tires are the same size (different sizes also pull)
- fix: confirm same sizes, fill to manufacturer's spec, equal pressure each side

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: linkages run from steering levers to a plate on the hydraulic motors; adjustments vary by manufacturer/model; consult operator's manual
- step 1: adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change rotation speed of the tire so both move at the same speed (per manual)

## page: causes/brake-hung-up
- fact: brake can fail on one side so the tire drags
- confirm: step 1 flat surface, set parking brake; step 2 chock front wheels; step 3 raise rear drive tires with jack stands; step 4 disengage hydros by releasing transmission bypass levers; step 5 release parking brake; step 6 spin each drive wheel for resistance; step 7 if resistance, remove wheel and inspect braking system (varies by model; use parts diagram) and repair

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: air slows mower and can make it pull; two independent hydraulic pumps and wheel motors, one per side; trapped air on one side slows that wheel

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1: flat surface; chock front wheels
- step 2: raise rear drive tires with jack stands
- step 3: release bypass levers to OPEN
- step 4: start engine and release parking brake
- step 5: move speed levers forward and backward about 6 times
- step 6: stop engine, engage parking brake
- step 7: close bypass levers to CLOSED
- step 8: move speed levers forward and backward about 6 times
- step 9: repeat as needed until no excessive noise and wheels move at normal speeds
- fact: oil level drops when air is removed; check and top up to full
- tip: see operator's manual for model-specific steps

## page: causes/bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- symptom: jerky movement when operating; steering dampers (shocks) loose or bad
- fact: dampers absorb negative feedback from hydro pumps; one each side attached to bottom of the steering arm; wear over time
- fix: replace bad damper(s)

## page: causes/bad-hydrostatic-transmission
- fact: two independent transmissions; if one side weakens/fails the mower pulls to that side
- pro: dealer diagnoses pump and wheel motor failure and replaces

## page: causes/unbalanced-load
- fact: towed trailer/attachment load not balanced at the rear can pull mower to one side; balance the load
- safety: don't exceed zero-turn's towing capacity; transmissions not designed for heavy loads; costly damage
