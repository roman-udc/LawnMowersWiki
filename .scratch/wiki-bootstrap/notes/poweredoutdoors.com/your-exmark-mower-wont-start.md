## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Exmark zero turn won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, wrong-fuel, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, bad-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, faulty-charging-system, incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Gas-powered Exmark guide (diesel has a separate guide). Exmark offers gas, diesel, and propane models.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- tip: Author notes this is overlooked in the heat of the moment; fix: add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Exmark (non-diesel, non-propane) needs unleaded gasoline octane 87 or greater and ethanol 10% or less. Wrong fuel/high ethanol can stop starting and cause significant engine damage. Read pump labels.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Gas shelf life about 30 days; ethanol attracts moisture; ethanol and water cause sluggish running and gummy buildup restricting fuel flow.
- fix: Drain and flush the tank; add fresh gas with an additive (Sea Foam) to stabilize and clean.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Cap must vent; otherwise tank vacuum restricts fuel flow.
- confirm: Remove cap and run the mower; if runs fine, replace cap and see if it starves; shuts off after running a while -> cap likely bad.
- fix: Try cleaning; else new Exmark cap (author typically replaces).

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Check wires secure; tips for carbon; burnt electrode or damaged porcelain; gap correctly. Fix: replace plug(s), gap, wires secure.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Plugged with grass and dirt, engine can overheat and shut down; running dirty or no filter risks serious engine damage. Replace more often in dry dusty conditions.
- step: Exmark air filter cover attached by clips or thumbscrews; remove cap and filter without knocking dirt into intake; wipe housing with dry cloth; tap on a solid surface; hold to light; reuse if light shows through paper, else replace.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Old gas degrades plastic and corrodes internals; can't build pressure; may leak at seams.
- confirm: Verify flow to the pump with shut-off valve or clamps; disconnect fuel hose from carburetor into a container; start mower; watch for constant or pulsating flow. Replace if not.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- tip: Author replaces fuel filter at least yearly (least expensive filter on the mower).
- fix: Replace; install with flow following the arrow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: Start/stop flow with shut-off valve under tank (or clamp); remove one end of hose into container; check flow. When checking the hose between tank and pump, container must be lower than the tank so gravity works.
- fix: Remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air; replace if can't clear. Replace dry, cracking lines even if no clog.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fix: Mechanical owners can disassemble and clean with carb cleaner; or small engine mechanic; or replace if too dirty.

## page: causes/bad-battery
- fix: Test with multimeter; need about 12.7 volts; if less, charge; replace if won't hold charge.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: Clean corroded terminals with baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda) and wire brush; confirm terminals attached securely.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Exmark safety switches: prevent starting unless parking brake engaged; disengage deck and shut off the mower if operator not sensed; possibly others. Failed switch = no start.
- confirm: Test with multimeter; can temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: Never operate a mower without the safety switch; never run with a switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: Key turned and nothing, not even turnover; test switch with multimeter; replace if bad. Also check ignition coil continuity with multimeter after confirming plug works.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; wire on solenoid hot, smoking or melting. Replace if bad.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Not main cause but can leave a weak battery; bad stator or alternator among electrical parts; test charging system with an ohmmeter (linked).
- pro: Dealer or repair shop should check the charging system; guessing replacing electrical parts is expensive; parts stores usually won't take electrical returns.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Exmark safety precautions require certain steps to start and operate; follow the operating manual so safety features don't shut it off.
