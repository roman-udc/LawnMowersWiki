- safety: Park on flat level surface, parking brake, remove key, unplug spark plug wires, protect hands and eyes.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Check tires first; low tire makes deck sit lower on that side.
- confirm: Gauge; match pressure on tire sidewall; add air.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Normal wear from sand/dirt drawn in by suction. Tips become rounded or sail thinner than center = replace. Source says if edges merely dull on otherwise good blades: "remove your blades and replace them" (sharpening not mentioned here).
- confirm (on): park level; measure ground to blade end, rotate 180 degrees, measure again; more than 1/8" difference = bent.
- confirm (off): lay on a known good blade; gaps or bend = don't reuse.
- mistake: Straightening a bent blade; metal weakens, cracks; blade may break off.
- fact: Unbalanced blade (uneven wear or uneven metal removal during sharpening) wobbles, damages spindle bearing at high speed, sends vibration through mower. Use blade balancer; or nail in wall; shave heavy side until level.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: Rounded blade tips leave a strip of grass between them; worn sail can't create air movement/suction needed.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Blade installed, grab each end, rock up/down; movement or knock = remove blade and spindle housing and inspect; replace damaged parts. Check before removing blade.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Plugged deck reduces airflow/suction and loads engine. Scrape regularly; avoid mowing wet (clumps and sticks).

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: Low side gives stair-step cut. Level side-to-side and front-to-rear; on some Snapper deck models the front sits slightly lower than the rear; follow owner's manual.
- brand: Snapper leveling procedures vary by model; refer to operator's manual.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- fact: Per operator's manual; side-to-side and front-to-rear; some decks front slightly lower.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: shiny glazed appearance; no longer tight to pulleys; sits deep in pulleys; cracks. Replace.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Slowly spin each pulley by hand; feel restriction, listen for noise, check wobble; replace pulley. Bad bearing lets pulley sit unevenly (vertical movement).

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Engage deck at full throttle. If engine doesn't give needed power, have small engine mechanic test.
- pro: small engine mechanic for lack of engine power.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Fast speed overloads engine and leaves uncut grass or pushes it over; slow down in tall, wet, thick grass and on inclines.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Overlap paths to avoid strips; takes practice on a new mower.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Hitting fence, tree, pole damages deck shell. If it can't be fixed, replace shell.
- tip: Get quotes from Snapper dealer for shell replacement (with labor to swap components) vs complete deck; price difference may be small (author's suggestion).
