## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "zero turn won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-wiring-or-ground, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: wear safety gear and follow the operator's manual.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fix: charge it and check it holds a charge; replace if it fails. Buy at a servicing dealer or hardware/automotive store; bring the old battery (core charge otherwise).

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: protective gear for eyes and skin; access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed); don't remove battery from casing.
- step 2: connect charger positive (red, plus) cable first to the positive terminal.
- step 3: attach negative (black, minus) to the negative terminal.
- step 4: don't touch anything without a rubber coating.
- step 5: set to 12 volts; start at 2 amps and work up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge best.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fact: constant vibration loosens connections; water collects in connections and corrodes them.
- fix: clean corrosion with a wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); replace severely corroded connections and terminals.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- fix: replace or fix wires between the starting components that have breaks in continuity or damage.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probe on each fuse prong, resistance: near 0 good; infinity bad.
- fix: same amperage replacement; if it keeps blowing, dealership or repair shop for root cause.

## page: procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse
- step 1: multimeter resistance across fuse prongs; near 0 good, infinity bad. step 2: replace with same amperage.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: insert and turn key and nothing happens.
- confirm: multimeter continuity; prongs marked B (battery) and S (starter solenoid); key in start; one probe on B, one on S; good near 0 ohms; bad infinite, replace.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: zero turn has an operator presence control system; a defective switch prevents cranking.
- confirm: multimeter, or temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: never operate without the safety switch; never run with a switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch that engages the starter motor; bad when spring weak or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can make it fail; needs fully charged battery before testing (see "How to Tell Your Lawn Mower Solenoid is Bad").

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid, remove and test the starter.
- tip: have a starter/alternator repair shop test and rebuild before buying a new starter.
