## page: procedures/replace-the-deck-belt
- brand: MTD brand family (guide applies to any with these stamps): Troy-Bilt (Americas), Rover (Pacific), WOLF-Garten (Europe), Remington, Yard Machines, Columbia, Craftsman, Cub Cadet, Boelens. Steps may vary by model.
- tip: Use an OEM belt for quality and fit. Match the belt: check the product number on the old belt and buy a duplicate; if worn off, look up parts diagrams at MTD Parts.
- tip: Belts sold at local MTD dealer or online.
- difficulty: do-it-yourself; basic tools.
- time: push mower about 30-45 minutes; riding mower about an hour.
- safety: Mower must be cool before working: let it cool 10-15 minutes. Hot gas mowers can exude fumes and the muffler can ignite vapors.
- safety: Wear gloves and goggles throughout.
- step 1 (push, drive belt): Tools: screwdriver or nut driver set; ratchet/socket set; 2 sandwich bags; block of wood; gloves; goggles; replacement drive belt.
- step 2 (push): Let the mower cool 10-15 minutes.
- step 3 (push): Choose a sheltered, stable, flat, spacious work area; ideally a workbench/platform where the mower can be strapped.
- step 4 (push): Unplug the spark plug. Unscrew the fuel cap, lay a sandwich bag flat over the opening, screw the cap back on so the bag is a barrier (stops fuel seeping when tipped). Battery-powered mower: unplug the battery.
- step 5 (push): Tilt the mower up; if on a table strap down the handle (or weigh it with a bag of sand). Wedge the wood block between blade and deck so the blade can't spin.
- step 6 (push): With a gloved hand on the blade, loosen the blade bolt (socket wrench/ratchet); remove blade, blade support and blade bolt from the crankshaft. Grip firmly; damaging the blade reduces efficiency.
- step 7 (push): Remove bottom drive cover mounting screws and debris shield mounting bolts (store in the second sandwich bag, label if you can't tell them apart); twist and maneuver off the debris shield, clear of the crankshaft pulley end.
- step 8 (push): Roll the drive belt off the crankshaft pulley and remove the crankshaft pulley entirely, leaving the crankshaft end. Leave the belt beside the crankshaft end rather than around it. If the belt isn't on the pulley when you get here, it was knocked off or too loose and needs replacing.
- step 9 (push): Release the handle/stand upright; remove the top drive mounting screws with a screwdriver. If there is a height adjustment lever near the top drive mount, release it before pulling the drive cover off.
- step 10 (push): Unhook the belt keeper that secures the drive belt to the top of the transmission; pull the old belt out; slip the new belt over the transmission pulley and through the deck opening.
- step 11 (push): Reverse: reattach the belt keeper on top of the transmission drive pulley; replace the drive cover, reconnect height adjuster lever, tighten screws. Tip the mower back and secure the handle. Wrap the belt around the crankshaft end and refit the crankshaft pulley, aligning pulley indentions or it won't sit properly; roll the belt snugly into the pulley groove. Refit debris shield, its bolts, bottom drive cover screws, blade, blade support, blade bolts. Tighten the blade bolt by hand first, wedge with the wood block, then tighten with a torque wrench. Remove block, handle straps and the bag from the fuel tank, reconnect spark plug.
- step 12 (riding, ground drive/deck belt) tools: socket wrench set, pliers, torque wrench, gloves, goggles, new belt.
- step 13 (riding): Ignition off, remove the key. Lift the hood and disconnect the spark plug wire. Lower the deck to its lowest position with the lever. Turn the lever near the steering wheel that disengages the cutting blades.
- step 14 (riding): Store all nuts, bolts, washers carefully; keep the manual handy for diagrams. Under the mower, remove the ground drive belt from the engine pulley and release it from the belt keepers.
- step 15 (riding): Below the engine pulley remove the front lift link support retaining pin and washer; detach the support (silver rod) from the deck. At the left suspension arm remove the clip and detach the arm from the frame. Remove the left rear deck bracket retaining clip and washer and release the bracket from the deck. Repeat on the other side so both suspension arms and deck brackets are disconnected.
- step 16 (riding): Slide the deck out from under the mower but not completely yet. Release the locking tabs with pliers and pull the blade cable out of the deck bracket. Disconnect the blade cable spring from the idler arm and set it aside. Then move the deck fully out for working room.
- step 17 (riding): Before removing the old belt, note how the new one should be routed: photograph the undercarriage or use the manual diagram. Remove the old belt from the idler pulleys and belt guides.
- step 18 (riding): Route the new belt around the pulleys per photo/diagram. Slide the belt under the deck, reconnect blade spring and cable, arms and supports; confirm all belt guides, clips and pins are reconnected. With the deck reconnected, roll the new belt onto the engine pulley by pulling the deck belt from the right side of the mower to the pulley; turn the pulley slowly while rolling the belt on. The narrow side of the V-belt goes into the pulley grooves. Belt should feel tight; pull it around the pulleys to check it moves securely. Reconnect the spark plug wire and lower the hood.
- tools: screwdriver/nut driver set, ratchet/socket set, 2 sandwich bags, block of wood, gloves, goggles, pliers, torque wrench.

## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- fact: The Source intro says it will answer why the belt might slip off and how to prevent it, but the article body contains only the replacement steps (no cause list).
- fact: If the belt is off the crankshaft pulley when you open a push mower, it was knocked off or is too loose and needs replacing.
