## page: procedures/replace-the-deck-belt
- brand: Cub Cadet riding mower; need to remove the deck.
- fact: overview: remove belt from PTO pulley, then lift arms, rod and parts attached to the deck; slide deck out, replace belt, slide deck back, reverse steps.
- step 1: parking brake on, PTO off, lower the deck to its lowest position.
- step 2 (manual PTO only): loosen (do not remove) hardware on the right and left idler pulleys; remove the hex screw holding the rod over the engine pulley and remove the rod; remove the PTO cable and unhook the spring by removing the pin attaching the cable to the deck bracket.
- step 3: move the deck drive pulley: put a ratchet wrench in the square hole of the idler bracket and turn until the pulley is in the forward position.
- step 4: remove the belt from around the PTO pulley.
- step 5: remove the deck lift arms: cotter pin at the end of each lift arm near the rear of the mower (left and right); remove pins, slide the deck pin out of the lift arms.
- step 6: remove the front lift rod: another cotter pin attaches it to the deck; remove pin, slide rod out of the bracket; raise the deck lift lever to the highest position to get the rod out of the way.
- step 7: confirm nothing else attaches the mower to the deck; slide the deck out from underneath.
- step 8: remove belt covers over the idler pulleys (some decks) by removing the screws.
- step 9: take a photo of the belt routing before removing the belt.
- step 10: remove belt from the pulleys.
- step 11: install the new belt around spindle pulleys and idler pulleys using the photo; retighten left and right idler pulleys if loosened; refit belt covers.
- step 12: slide deck back under; reattach front lift rod (secure with cotter pin; lower the lift lever if raised) and left/right lift arms (pins through frame and arm, cotter pin).
- step 13 (manual PTO only): replace rod over the engine pulley with hex screw; reattach spring and PTO cable.
- step 14: install belt around PTO pulley: pull the belt from the right side of the deck onto the PTO pulley, narrow side of the belt in the pulley groove; hold the belt at the pulley and rotate the pulley to the left until it rolls on; use the drive ratchet on the idler bracket to move the deck drive pulley to help routing.
- step 15: rotate the belt by hand to confirm it rides securely around all pulleys with no slack.
- tools: ratchet wrench (drive ratchet), pliers for cotter pins (implied; Source says remove cotter pins), phone camera.
- mistake: not photographing routing; wrong routing found only after reinstalling the deck is a lot of rework.
- fact: homeowner deck belt can last up to four years with proper maintenance; damaged deck, debris and amount of use shorten it.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: bad Cub Cadet deck belt: belt squeals, bad lawn cut, blades not turning, or belt falling off; worn belt no longer turns blades fast enough for a good cut.

- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
