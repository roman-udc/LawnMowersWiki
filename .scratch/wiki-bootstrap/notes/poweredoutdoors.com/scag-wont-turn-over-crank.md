## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "SCAG won't turn over", "won't crank", "clicking sound or a hum"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Often the starter motor isn't getting the power it needs.
- safety: Always remove the black negative battery cable before electrical repairs.
- pro: repeated blown fuses: dealer/repair shop.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Keep battery charged, especially during storage, to extend life.
- fix: if it won't hold a charge, replace (dealer, hardware or automotive store); bring the old battery to avoid a core fee.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- safety: protective gear for eyes and skin.
- step 1: Access battery (screwdriver to uncover); don't remove from casing.
- step 2: Positive (red/plus) charger cable on positive terminal first.
- step 3: Negative (black) on negative terminal.
- step 4: Touch only rubber-coated parts.
- step 5: 12 V; start with 2 amps up to no more than 10; slow charge is best.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fact: Vibration can shake wiring/components loose; moisture corrosion hurts continuity.
- fix: remove battery cables, clean with wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water : 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); replace terminals/components that won't clean.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probe on each prong of the fuse measuring resistance; near 0 = good; infinity = bad.
- fix: replace with the same amperage.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: turn the key and nothing happens.
- confirm: multimeter on resistance; prongs marked B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid); key in the start position; probes on B and S; near 0 ohms = good; infinite = bad, replace.

## page: procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse
- fact: Resistance across fuse prongs near 0 good, infinity blown; same amperage replacement.

## page: procedures/test-the-battery
- fact: SCAG article refers to a separate battery-drain article for testing (no numbers here).

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: SCAG has an operator presence control system; a defective switch can stop cranking.
- confirm: multimeter; may temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: never operate a mower without the safety switch; never run with a switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: Electromagnetic switch that initiates the starter. Fails when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause failure. Needs a fully charged battery before testing.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: After ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid, remove and test the starter.
- pro: Have a starter/alternator specialty shop test and rebuild if possible before buying a pricey new starter.
