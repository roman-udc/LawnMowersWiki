- fact: Source summary: belt comes off when worn or shredded; belt keeper bent; spindle or pulley bearings bad; tension not correct; debris lodged in deck; belt covered in rust or oil. 8 reasons listed (worn belt, damaged keeper, shredded belt, bad spindle bearing, bad pulley bearing, insufficient tension, debris, oil/rust).
- safety: Remove the ignition key and the spark plug boot before working on the deck, plus the operator's manual procedures.
- pro: Consult professional if unsure; local Scag dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: belt slips, fails to turn blades at high speed (affects cut); worn belt loosens and rolls off pulleys (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- confirm: cracks, stretched, glazed shiny appearance, or belt sits deep in pulley grooves = worn, replace.
- mistake: buying a comparable belt at the local hardware store; author highly advises against - may not be right type, size or quality.
- tip: author prefers OEM belt: right material designed for the mower; deck belt affects cut quality.
- fact: Shredded belt (weak, likely to break): most common reasons are incorrect installation or rubbing on a bracket that has moved out of place.
- step 1: if edges shredding, check you have the right belt and correct routing around deck pulleys (decal on mower may show belt layout; else owner's manual or parts diagram).
- step 2: with deck engaged, check for brackets the belt rubs; look for shiny wear spot on bracket.
- step 3: adjust brackets so they no longer rub; if the bracket has a bushing it may need replacement; replace a bracket that cannot be adjusted.
- step 4: replace the belt once no more rub points.

## page: causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing
- symptom: some Scag mowers use a rod 1/8" to 1/4" away from the pulley (belt keeper / belt guide); belt does not actually touch it, it is an extra precaution (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- fact: when belt has slack it can hit the keeper and bend the rod, increasing chance belt comes off.
- confirm: check belt keeper and bracket.
- fix: bend back into shape (may need a hammer or other tools); if cannot reshape rod or bracket to sit correctly, replace with new.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: failed bearing in spindle housing (cutter housing) makes blade wobble; at high speed excessive vibration can knock belt off (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- confirm: safely access under deck, wear work gloves, grab each end of blade and rock up and down feeling movement; knocking noise. If blade is secure to cutter housing but vertical movement, remove blade and disassemble spindle housing assembly.
- fix: likely a bad bearing; inspect spindle for damage; replace bad parts; can buy complete spindle assembly or replace bearing in housing.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: bad deck pulley bearing makes pulley wobble, no longer secure, sits angled to deck (should be flat and parallel), belt slips off from vibration (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- confirm: slowly spin each pulley by hand; resistance, wobble or bearing noise = bad bearing.
- fix: the pulley must be replaced.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: Scag has a tensioner bracket and spring on the deck for proper belt tension. Too little tension: belt vibrates, slips, comes off. Too much tension damages deck components (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- confirm: check area around tensioner bracket and spring; make sure tensioner, bushing and spring in good condition; debris on top of deck can interfere with the tensioner pulley movement.
- fix: remove debris restricting tensioner.
- safety: dry debris collected on top of the deck can catch fire from belt heat.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: mud and debris packed in pulley grooves stretch the belt as it runs around the pulley; belt rolls off or snaps (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- fix: inspect pulleys, remove dirt, stones, grass and debris from grooves and under pulleys.
- tip: clean top of deck for performance and safety; link to author's deck-problems guide (how-to-find-prevent-substantial-lawn-mower-deck-problems).

## page: causes/oil-or-rust-on-belt
- symptom: oil spilled/leaked on deck lubricates the belt so it slips off pulleys; prolonged oil makes belt swell (problems/belt-keeps-coming-off).
- fix (oil): find and repair oil leak; clean oil from deck and components; replace deck belt.
- symptom (rust): rust forms from moisture; transfers to belt, belt dries out, cracks, breaks.
- fix (rust): remove rust from deck and components; replace pulleys/components where rust cannot be fully removed; install new belt once deck is clean of rust.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- tip: clean mower after each mowing and before storage: remove grass clippings and dirt (they hold moisture and accelerate corrosion).
- tip: ensure mower completely dry before storage; leave outside on a sunny day; can speed up with a leaf blower.

## page: guides/belt-types
- tip: use OEM belt; hardware-store belts may differ in type, size, quality.
