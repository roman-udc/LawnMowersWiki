## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Lawn Boy requires unleaded gasoline, minimum octane 87, maximum ethanol 10%. Lawn Boy 2-cycle mowers use a gasoline and oil mix; 4-cycle use straight gasoline. Wrong gas can seriously damage the engine.
- fact: Use fresh gasoline from a reputable source with low ethanol; the lower the ethanol the better.
- fact: Ethanol attracts moisture from the air (corrosive to the fuel system). Fuel and water separate over time; the ethanol/water mix leaves gummy deposits and is very harmful when it separates and runs hot through the engine.
- fact: Never use E15, E30, E85 (up to 15%, 30%, 85% ethanol).
- fact: Gasoline can begin breaking down 30 days after purchase; buy what you can use in 30 days; if not, add a stabilizer.
- fact: Ethanol-free fuel is always best but more expensive; sold at some stations as recreation fuel or REC-90, and in canisters for shelf storage.
- tip: Author adds Sea Foam Motor Treatment to every tank (stabilizes, cleans the fuel system, reduces moisture) - opinion.
- fact: Identify 2-cycle vs 4-cycle: Fuel cap - original 2-cycle cap lists the gas-to-oil ratio (16:1 or 32:1) or shows a fuel and oil can. Fill ports - 2-cycle has one fill port (gas/oil mix); 4-cycle has two (one for oil, one for gas). Operator's manual states which.
- fact: 4-cycle Lawn Boy: straight unleaded, min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; never mixed gas.
- fact: 2-cycle Lawn Boy: ratio 32:1 (32 parts gas to 1 part oil); mowers older than 1971 need 16:1. Check manual or fuel cap if unsure of age.
- fact: 2-cycle oil: premium 2-cycle oil certified ISO-L-EGD and JASO M345 FD. Source mentions Lawn Boy oil and a 16 oz Briggs & Stratton bottle with a measuring feature.
- mistake: Putting straight gas in a 2-cycle Lawn Boy: it will most likely seize and die, because a 2-cycle burns an oil/fuel mix and gets no lubrication without oil; ruins the engine.
- fact: Mix chart (oil per gas): 32:1 = 4.0 oz per 1 gal, 8.0 oz per 2 gal, 10.0 oz per 2.5 gal. 16:1 = 8.0 oz per 1 gal, 16.0 oz per 2 gal, 20.0 oz per 2.5 gal.
- step 1: Use the chart to decide how much mix to prepare.
- step 2: Remove the cap from an approved gas can; add unleaded fuel (Source's step says min 89 octane and max 10% ethanol; elsewhere in the same Source min 87 - inconsistency).
- step 3: Add the 2-cycle oil per the chart.
- step 4: Replace the cap.
- step 5: Gently shake to mix.
- step 6: Add to the Lawn Boy fuel tank.
- tip: Store gasoline in a dry, cool location away from combustibles. Add stabilizer when it can't be used within 30 days.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Stabilizer extends life past 30 days; Sea Foam stabilizes, cleans, reduces moisture (see choosing-the-right-gas). Store gas dry, cool, away from combustibles.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Wrong or old gas in a Lawn Boy causes starting and running problems; straight gas in a 2-cycle seizes the engine.
- brand: Lawn Boy 2-cycle (gas/oil mix) vs 4-cycle (straight gas).
