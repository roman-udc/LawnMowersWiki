- alias: "Weed Eater lawn mower won't start"

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: also check for a broken fuel gauge or a fuel leak (running out sooner than usual). Fill with fresh gas, octane 87+, ethanol max 10%.
- brand: if 2-cycle engine, mix 2-cycle oil with gas before adding.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas sitting over winter or more than 30 days breaks down, leaves varnish and sticky deposits. Ethanol attracts moisture, can cause corrosion; mixture separates and sinks to tank bottom; engine runs extremely hot on it.
- brand: Weed Eater requires unleaded, min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol ("regular" or "E10"). 4-cycle (4-stage): straight gas, separate fill ports for oil and gas. 2-cycle: one fill port for gas/oil mix; gas-to-oil 40:1 (confirm in operator's manual, varies by engine model); never run straight gas in a 2-cycle (ruins engine); 2-cycle oil differs from engine oil.
- fact: avoid E15 and E85 (up to 15% and 85% ethanol); ethanol-free best but costlier; puregas.org lists stations; canisters at hardware stores.
- fix: siphon old fuel out, add fresh; additive Sea Foam Motor Treatment or STA-BIL stabilizes, cleans, reduces moisture; stabilize fuel bought beyond 30 days of use.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap vents; plugged vent makes vacuum, fuel can't flow.
- confirm: loosen or remove cap (don't let debris in tank); if starts, suspect cap. Reinstall and run; if it dies and won't restart until loosened, replace cap.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- confirm: remove and check for dirty insulator tip, broken porcelain, burnt electrode: replace. Mildly dirty: wire brush. Gap to engine spec; attach boot securely. Bad plug causes misfire and sluggish running.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace annually, clean several times per season.
- step: paper: remove cover; remove filter without dropping dirt in intake; wipe housing; tap plastic/rubber part against hard surface; hold to light; reuse if light seen; replace if no light or oily; reinstall and reattach cover.
- step: foam: same removal; reuse only if not brittle, no dark spots, no tears; wash in mild dish soap and water, rinse until clear; lay flat to dry; once dry coat with clean engine oil, reinstall. (Note: foam filter is oiled here; contrast with Country Clipper note on foam pre-filter over paper.)

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace if no flow; arrow in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: check each section while starting and stopping fuel flow.
- fix: remove line, spray carb cleaner, compressed air; or replace.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: not all Weed Eater mowers have one; present when tank sits below carburetor. Wear and old fuel deteriorate components.
- confirm: inspect vacuum pump for cracks/fuel outside = replace. Ensure fuel reaches pump. Shut fuel valve or pinch pliers, remove carb line into container, open fuel and start mower; steady or pulsating flow = pump probably fine; poor flow = replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: crusty buildup and gummy deposits from old ethanol fuel; engine runs rough or won't start.
- fix: clean (if mechanical) or shop; replace if very bad.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter about 12.7 V; charge if lower; replace if won't hold charge.
- fact: loose cables or corroded terminals also cause starting problems.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system kills engine when operator leaves seat; faulty switch may not detect operator.
- confirm: temporarily bypass to identify; replace bad switch; never operate without switch.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: after plug verified, check continuity with ohm meter; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; wire on solenoid hot, smoking or melting.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: riding models with battery; bad stator or alternator and other parts; if battery keeps dying and needs charger suspect it.
- pro: small engine mechanic; guessing is expensive.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- symptom: push mower with no electric start won't start by pull; bad pulley, loose/missing spring, broken clips.
- fix: replace spring and restring; if other parts damaged, replace recoil assembly.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: safety features block starting unless starting procedure followed (e.g. parking brake set). Choke restricts airflow for cold engine; must be adjusted off when warm or engine shuts down.
