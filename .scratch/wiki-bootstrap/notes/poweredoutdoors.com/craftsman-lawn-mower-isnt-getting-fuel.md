## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Craftsman mower isn't getting fuel / won't start"
- alias: "runs sluggish almost like it's going to die"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- safety: follow manufacturer safety guidelines.
- pro: if unsure how to diagnose safely, use dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas attracts moisture, leaves gummy residue clogging fuel system, degrading components, crusty carb buildup. Can break down as quickly as 30 days after purchase.
- tip: use within 30 days or stabilize with additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment).
- fix: siphon tank into approved container, refill with fresh fuel plus additive.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: if not changed regularly the plastic softens from sitting fuel and filter can fail and leak at seams; cracked or seam-leaking filter must be replaced.
- fix: replace with arrow on filter side pointing toward carburetor, away from tank.

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-filter
- fact: soft plastic from old fuel -> leaks at seams or cracked; replace.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm/fix: work one section at a time; stop flow with shut-off valve (bottom of tank; not every mower has one) or hose pinch pliers; remove end farthest from tank into a container LOWER than the tank; restart flow; poor flow = restriction. Stop flow, remove line, spray carb cleaner, blow with compressed air, repeat; reattach. If cannot clear, replace line. Replace also if dry and cracking before it leaks.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: plastic or metal pump present when tank sits lower than carb; vacuum pump builds pressure off the crankcase to push fuel up to carb. Replace if cracked, damaged or leaking.
- confirm: (1) stop flow; remove line from pump inlet into container lower than tank; start flow; fuel into container = flow to pump OK (if not, check filter/lines and container position); reattach. (2) remove line from carburetor into container, start flow, start mower; steady or pulsating stream = pump OK.
- fix: replace pump if damaged or not pumping stream.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: carb mounted top or side of engine block, usually below or behind air filter; fuel jet can clog or stick.
- confirm: remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; if starts and won't keep running, remove and clean carb.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap vent equalizes pressure; clogged = vacuum, fuel can't leave tank.
- confirm: run with and without cap; runs without, dies after cap reinstalled and running a while -> bad cap.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- step: arrow toward carburetor.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-line
- tip: replace a dry, cracking line before it leaks; replace when can't be cleared.
