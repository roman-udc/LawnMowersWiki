## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "SCAG shaking so bad I feel like I'll shake out of the seat"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: Follow operator's manual; disconnect spark plug boot; wait for all moving parts to stop before diagnostics or repair.
- fact: 8 reasons: engine mounting bolts, clutch, mower blades, material around blade spindle, spindle components, deck belt and pulleys, idler bracket and spring, debris lodged.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: Engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) under engine absorb vibration; loosen, wear or break over time. Replace worn, damaged or missing bolts.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: SCAG most likely has an electric clutch; its bearing can fail and send vibration through mower; replace the clutch.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Wrong or unbalanced blades wobble and vibrate. Author uses OEM blades. Aftermarket blades with same length and center hole size as OEM may still not fit (slight variations) and cause spindle damage and vibration; physically match a candidate blade against an OEM blade; use correct hardware.
- fact: Unbalanced = heavier on one side; from uneven wear (dirt sucked under deck) or uneven metal removal when sharpening; can damage spindle housing bearings.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- symptom: String, wire, long sturdy weeds wrapped around the spindle (blade) shaft, the round rod where the blade attaches.
- fix: Remove, then check spindle/bearing for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: Hitting something solid under the deck bends spindle/bearing; bearings also fail over time; extra movement with each blade turn causes severe deck vibration.
- confirm: Heavy gloves; grab blade and rock up and down; feel extra movement, hear knocking.
- fix: Remove blade, spindle and housing to inspect; replace damaged spindle or bearing.
- tip: Some manufacturers sell only complete spindle housing assemblies, but on most SCAG decks the bearing alone can be replaced in the housing (cheaper).

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: Look for cracks, stretch, glazed shiny look; check routing and tension. Worn belt gives slack, vibration, may fall off.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Pulleys must be securely attached and parallel to the deck; slanted pulley = bad bearing. Rotate by hand: restriction or bearing noise = replace.
- tip: Some pulleys (e.g., idler pulleys) have sealed bearings; replace pulley and bearing assembly as a unit rather than fight to replace the bearing.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Idler bracket hole wears larger and spring stretches, causing vibration; replace worn bracket or spring; check bearing and bushing in engagement linkage and replace if worn.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Small debris (even a wood chip) lodged in the mower causes extreme vibration; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat and parallel. Remove spindle covers and clean deck regularly; check the whole mower, not just deck.
