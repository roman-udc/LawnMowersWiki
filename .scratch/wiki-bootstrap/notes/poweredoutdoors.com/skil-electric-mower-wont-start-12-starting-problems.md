## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "SKIL electric mower won't start"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, battery-not-seated, bad-charger, overheated-battery, incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass, bad-motor
- fact: won't start when motor overworked, power supply insufficient, bad electrical connection, or incorrect starting procedure; covers 20 V and 40 V mowers
- safety: remove the battery before repairs; take care with electric current and sharp blades
- fact: warranty: consult SKIL service center before repairs that may void warranty
- pro: damaged components/motor: SKIL customer service 1-877-754-5999; call before dropping off at a service center

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: use a battery compatible with the mower; wrong battery can fail to start and cause more severe electrical damage
- fact: most SKIL batteries are removed and charged in an external charging station specific to that battery
- fact: charger LEDs (refer to charger manual): blinking green = charging; solid green = charged; blinking red = battery may be defective; solid red = battery too cold or too hot, move to a location without extreme temperatures
- symptom: failing battery: fully charged but doesn't hold charge as long; charges very slowly; gets very hot; replace
- fact: batteries fail from age, extreme temperatures or mechanical failure

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- fact: battery must slide into guides and snap in; click when battery-release button springs back
- step 1: wipe around the lid so dirt doesn't fall in the compartment
- step 2: press battery-release button, remove battery
- step 3: check contacts are clean; remove dirt
- step 4: slide battery into guides until click
- step 5: close compartment lid securely

## page: causes/bad-charger
- fact: check outlet and cord connections fully plugged in; standard 120 V household outlet with sufficient power; plug style must match outlet (do not modify plug; 3-prong into 3-prong); clean charger vents; keep charger out of extreme heat or cold

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- fact: hot battery shuts down mower and won't start; clean mower vents; let battery cool

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: SKIL safety button/key prevents children accidentally starting
- step 1: ensure battery securely in place
- step 2: press and hold the safety button
- step 3: pull bail switch (safety bar) toward handle to start motor
- step 4: release the safety button
- fact: mower shuts off when bail switch released
- fact: self-propelled: with mower started and bail held, pull self-propelled lever to handle; adjust speed with knob
- fact: foldable handle must be locked: turn handle adjustment knobs (one each side at bottom of handle) to unlock, pull handle up, turn to lock (engages lock pin); or it won't start
- fact: side rails must be fully extended and locked

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: test with multimeter or temporarily bypass with jumper wire; replace any bad switch
- safety: never operate without all safety switches working
- brand: SKIL push/self-propelled switches: safety bar/bail lever switch (bar must be pulled into handle; released = shuts off); momentary switch for on/off button; check manual for others

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- fact: wires run through mower and along handle; loose or pinched wires short; common spot near the handle fold; fold/unfold carefully with wires on outside of the handle
- confirm: wiggle wire; multimeter
- fix: wire nut repair; remove battery and safety button first
- safety: wiring repairs/modifications outside an authorized SKIL repair center can void all warranties

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: grass in deck may inhibit blade rotation and prevent starting; remove battery before deck work

## page: causes/cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fact: overload from thick, tall, wet grass; raise cutting height, slow down, avoid wet grass

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: motor damage or other components; trained technician tests
