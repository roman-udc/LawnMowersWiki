## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol attracts moisture; the mix leaves gummy deposits, blockages, corrosion and premature failure of fuel parts. Diesel Ferris: moisture and sediment also corrode and clog.
- fix: siphon pump to drain tank into a gasoline-rated container (plastics degrade quickly and leak); take old fuel to an authorized recycling center; refill with fuel plus stabilizer. Author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment (stabilizes fuel up to two years, cleans system).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- tip: replace the fuel filter annually during a full service (author's recommendation).
- fix: arrow on the plastic filter must point in the direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: start/stop flow with the fuel shut-off valve at the bottom of the tank or hose pinch-off pliers; check each section.
- fix: remove clogged section, spray carb cleaner, blow compressed air, repeat; replace if it won't clear or the line is dry and cracked.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: pump needed when the carburetor is higher than the tank (works against gravity). Most mowers use a vacuum pump building pressure off the crankcase.
- step 1: stop fuel flow; remove the line from the pump inlet port; put it in a container lower than the tank; restart flow; fuel out = flow OK, else find blockage in lines/filter.
- step 2: reattach inlet line; remove the line from the carburetor into a container; start flow and start mower; steady or pulsating flow expected, else replace pump.
- brand: injection fuel pump: read pressure from the pressure hose with a fuel pressure gauge; compare with operator's manual spec; replace pump if below spec.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: clogged small parts incl. fuel jet.
- confirm: verify fuel reaches carb; remove air filter; spray carb cleaner in intake; if it starts but won't keep running, remove and disassemble carb to clean.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: run the mower with and without the cap; fine without, but sluggish/shuts off with cap = plugged cap.
- fix: try cleaning the vent; otherwise buy a new Ferris cap.
