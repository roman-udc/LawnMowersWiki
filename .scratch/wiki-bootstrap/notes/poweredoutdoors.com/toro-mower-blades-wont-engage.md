## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Toro blades won't engage or turn on"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- safety: Working around deck is dangerous: remove ignition key and spark plug wires; wait for parts to stop.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: cracks, wear, fraying, shiny glazed look, sits deep in pulley grooves; belt slips so blades don't turn, or turn at reduced speed (affects cut because fast blade speed creates suction).
- fix: replace.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Tensioner arm and spring hold idler pulleys inline; typically pulley on one side of bracket and spring on other. Wear makes belt vibrate off pulleys; spring can break or stretch; bracket hole can wear.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Bad bearing makes pulley wobble and not sit parallel to deck; belt rolls off.
- confirm: Rotate by hand, feel resistance/listen for noise; grab each side and check for rocking play. Good pulley sits securely.
- fix: Replace pulley assembly.

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: PTO switch is usually a knob that lets battery voltage power the electric clutch; failure = blades won't turn.
- confirm: Continuity check; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: PTO clutch transfers engine power to blades by engaging drive belt; replace worn/faulty clutch.

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: Manual engagement clutch has lever and cable; check lever, cable, spring, bushings, linkages; replace worn parts.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Electric clutch draws battery power; weak battery can't energize clutch solenoid.
- confirm: Multimeter; fully charged 12 V battery reads about 12.7 V; charge if lower.
- fix: Charge; replace if it won't hold charge. Core fee averages $20 unless you bring the old battery. Batteries at hardware, automotive store or dealer.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Protective gear (skin, eyes). Access battery (under hood or under seat; may need screwdriver); don't remove it from casing.
- step 2: Positive (red, plus) cable first to positive terminal.
- step 3: Negative (black) cable to negative terminal.
- step 4: Touch only rubber-coated parts.
- step 5: 12 V; start at 2 amps, up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge is best.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- brand: Toro safety system: deck won't engage with operator out of seat; lifting out of seat with deck engaged turns deck off and stops blades. Failed seat switch fails to sense operator, preventing engagement.
- confirm: Multimeter, or temporarily bypass for identification.
- safety: Do not operate without safety switch installed.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fact: No power battery-to-clutch can be a blown fuse. Replace with same size; repeated blowing = Toro service dealer or repair shop for root cause.
