## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Toro zero turn won't start"
- fact: won't start without enough fuel, air or spark.
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, wrong-fuel, old-fuel, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, faulty-charging-system, incorrect-starting-procedure
- safety: remove ignition key and unplug spark plug wires before repairs.
- tip: routine maintenance and fresh gas minimize problems.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- fact: battery, cables, connections: check when mower clicks, hums or makes no noise at start.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Toro zero-turns: unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol. Avoid E15 and E85 (15% and 85% ethanol) - significant engine damage.
- fix: if wrong fuel used drain and flush tank (manual siphon pump); add fresh gas with additive (Sea Foam).

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down from 30 days; buy from busy station; use additive if more than 30-day supply; stabilizers only work on fresh gas and cannot reverse old gas effects. Ethanol/moisture evaporation leaves deposits that damage fuel components and cause restrictions.

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- brand: Toro zero-turn: unleaded, min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; avoid E15, E85.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: dirty plug -> intermittent spark. Replace if very dark, broken porcelain or burnt electrode; if slightly dirty, clean with small wire brush; gap per engine manufacturer (Toro operator's manual); secure wires.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- step paper: remove cover; remove filter carefully; don't drop dirt into intake; wipe housing with dry cloth; tap on solid surface; light test; reuse if light seen, replace if no light, extremely dirty or damaged.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- see above.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: works against gravity to move fuel up to carburetor; most zero-turns have vacuum pump using engine vacuum. Replace if leaking.
- confirm: shut off fuel at valve under tank or pinch line; remove hose from pump inlet into container lower than tank; start flow - flow means fuel reaches pump, none means restriction before pump; shut off; reattach inlet hose; remove hose from carburetor into container; start flow and engine; look for steady or pulsating flow; shut off mower; reattach hose to carb.
- fix: replace if no constant or pulsating flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: installed between fuel lines to strain fuel; replace annually (author recommendation); arrow in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: isolate by starting/stopping flow and checking sections.
- fix: remove line; carb cleaner then compressed air; if can't clear, replace with right diameter. Replace dry cracking lines even without clog.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: can't regulate air-fuel; old fuel leaves gummy crusty deposits sticking small parts. Clean, replace stuck/damaged parts, or shop clean, or new carb assembly.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter; <12.7 V -> charger; replace if won't hold charge.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: remove battery cables first; clean corrosion with baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda) and wire brush; make sure cables secure.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system. Multimeter test; temporary bypass for troubleshooting only. Never operate without safety switch or with it bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: insert key, turn, nothing happens. Multimeter; replace.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; wire on solenoid hot, smoking or melting.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap also vents; failure -> vacuum, no fuel flow.
- confirm: loosen cap and start; if runs then sluggish after running with tightened cap, suspect cap. Replace.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: bad stator or alternator among possible causes; can cause weak battery.
- pro: bring to Toro dealer; many components, electrical parts expensive and typically non-returnable.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: zero-turn safety features shut engine off if steps not followed; set parking brake and place speed levers (control levers) in neutral before starting; see operator's manual.

## page: procedures/start-a-zero-turn-mower
- fact: set parking brake and speed levers in neutral before starting Toro engine.
