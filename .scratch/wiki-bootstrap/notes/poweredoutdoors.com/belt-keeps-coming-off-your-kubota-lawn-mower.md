## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- alias: "Kubota mower belt keeps coming off"
- alias: "belt keeps falling off the deck"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, oil-or-rust-on-belt
- symptom: belt comes off repeatedly; some Kubota models require removing the mower deck each time to reinstall
- safety: prevent starting/rolling: remove ignition key and spark plug wires, set parking brake, use wheel chocks; extra care working under the deck; see Kubota operator's manual
- fact: belt comes off when loose or worn, a bracket is worn/damaged, a pulley or spindle bearing has failed, or debris collects on the deck

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: correctly installed belt doesn't sit securely against pulleys (stretched); worn belt shows cracking, fraying or shiny glazed look; belt sits deep in pulley grooves
- fix: replace the belt
- tip: author prefers OEM belts from the Kubota dealer; an automotive store may not match exactly; in his experience non-OEM belts perform worse and last less
- safety: an incompatible belt can pose safety risks
- fact: a mower deck belt can cost up to $200

## page: causes/wrong-belt
- fact: non-OEM belts matched at an automotive dealership likely won't be an exact match, perform worse and wear faster (author's experience); can pose safety risks

## page: causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing
- fact (weak fit): a belt shredded along its edges is from incorrect routing or rubbing on a metal bracket; the Source does not mention a missing guard/keeper
- fix: find the bracket (look for a shiny smooth spot from rubbing); bend it back into place or replace it; if the bracket has a bushing, replace bushing or bracket assembly
- tip: route belt per owner's manual; Kubota parts diagrams often don't show belt routing; dealer should help if manual is lost
- fact: shredded belt is weakened and can break

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: idler tensioner bracket has a pulley on one side and a spring on the other; spring can weaken or fall off so belt keeps coming off
- fact: the hole in the bracket can wear larger, causing excessive vibration
- fix: replace a bad/worn tensioner bracket or spring

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: debris on deck and under tensioner can stop the tensioner moving freely, so belt more likely to fall off
- fact: mud packs pulley grooves; stones and acorns can get stuck; the belt must stretch around the extra material and can slip off or break
- fix: clean debris from grooves and deck
- tip: clean deck also reduces chance of a small fire from hot belt friction against dry debris

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: bad pulley bearing lets the pulley sit not flat/parallel to the deck, higher on one side; belt slips off
- confirm: check each pulley for extra movement/up-and-down play; spin each by hand slowly; feel for restriction or bearing noise
- fix: replace the pulley; some pulleys allow bearing replacement, others have sealed bearings so the pulley and bearing assembly is replaced

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: bad spindle housing bearing lets blade wobble; with deck engaged the wobble becomes serious vibration that can knock off the belt
- confirm: gloves on; get under deck; grip each side of a blade and rock it up and down; good bearing has no movement or knocking
- fix: if movement/noise, remove spindle housings and check the bearing; most Kubota housings allow bearing replacement, some models need whole spindle housing assembly
- tip: have the deck model and serial number (not the mower's) for parts; use Kubota online parts diagram or dealer

## page: causes/oil-or-rust-on-belt
- symptom: oil leak dripping on deck/belt makes belt slip off and can make it swell if it stays on long
- fix: find and repair the leak, clean oil off deck and pulleys, replace the belt
- fact: rust dries the belt prematurely, causing cracks and possible break; clean rust off deck components; if very rusty replace components; replace belt once components no longer have rust
