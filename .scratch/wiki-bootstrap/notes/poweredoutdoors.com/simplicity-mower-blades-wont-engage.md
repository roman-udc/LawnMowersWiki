## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Simplicity mower blades won't spin / turn on"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, bad-pto-switch, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, bad-pto-clutch, worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Electric clutch: check battery, safety switch, PTO switch, fuse for power to the clutch. Manual clutch: clutch cable, lever, linkages, bushings.
- safety: Remove spark plug boots and ignition key; wait for parts to stop.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter: fully charged 12 V battery about 12.7 V; charge below that.
- fix: Weak battery can't power clutch solenoid. Charge: battery under hood or seat, leave in casing; connect positive (red) cable first, then negative (black); touch only rubber; 12 V; start with two amps up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge best. Replace if it won't hold a charge; take old battery for core fee about $20 otherwise.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- fact: see weak-or-dead-battery steps (positive first, 12 V, 2 to 10 amps, slow charge best).

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: Usually a knob that lets battery voltage power the clutch. Check continuity; replace if a break.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Seat switch under the seat is part of the operator presence system; blades won't start without operator in seat and shut off if leaving the seat with blades engaged. Test with multimeter or temporarily bypass; do not operate without it.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: Same amperage; if it keeps blowing, take to Simplicity dealer or shop.

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fix: Manual clutch: check lever, cable, spring, bushings, linkages; replace worn parts.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: PTO clutch transfers power from engine to blades by engaging the drive belt; replace if worn/faulty.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Cracks, wear, fraying, shiny glazed look; may sit deep in pulley grooves. Replace even if not the main cause. Belt grip affects blade speed; fast blade speed makes suction for even cut.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: Tensioner arm and spring hold idler pulleys in line (pulley one side of bracket, spring on other); spring hole in bracket or spring wears, belt loosens, vibrates, comes off.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Worn bearing lets pulley wobble, one side higher off the deck; belt can roll off. Replace pulley.
