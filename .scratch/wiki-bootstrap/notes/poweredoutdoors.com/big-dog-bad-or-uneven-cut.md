## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Big Dog zero turn has a bad or uneven cut"
- alias: "grass tips turn brown a couple of days after mowing"
- alias: "strips of grass left uncut between blade ends"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- tip: check tire pressure before any other step; skipping it may lead to unnecessary deck adjustments that must be reversed.
- safety: before working on deck remove ignition key and spark plug wires (per Big Dog manual).
- mistake: never attempt to straighten a bent blade; it compromises the metal and makes it prone to crack; cracked blade is hazardous at speed.
- fact: uneven/bumpy lawn lets deck scalp; rolling the lawn helps (lawn-care, minor).

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- symptom: low pressure makes deck sit unlevel, uneven cut (problems/uneven-cut).
- confirm: pressures correct and the same on right and left sides.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: grass tips turn brown a couple days after mowing (dull); extremely worn, rounded blade ends leave strips of uncut grass; chips/gouges; bent blade after striking root/rock.
- fact: blade wear speed depends on conditions; thin-grass lawns expose deck to more dirt and wear blades faster than thick lawns.
- confirm: without removing blade: park on flat ground, remove key and spark plug boot, measure from blade tip to ground, rotate blade 180 degrees and measure other tip; more than 1/8" variance means replace blade. Repeat for each blade.
- confirm: by removing blade: flat ground, remove key/boot, remove blade, lay old blade on a new one; any gaps means replace.
- fix: small nicks removed by sharpening; replace if gouges too large. If bent, also check spindle bearing for damage (can fail on significant impact).
- mistake: don't straighten blades.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- symptom: sail (high side of blade) thinner than center of blade gives bad cut.
- fact: sail creates air movement under the deck at high speed; with deck area this makes suction that stands grass tall, then blade cuts evenly.
- confirm: when inspecting edges, also check sail for wear.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: high side (sail/fin) must point up. Upside down can hit the ground and tear up grass and damage blade spindle and spindle bearing.
- tip: make sure blades are balanced before install; unbalanced blades cause uneven cut and vibration.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: deck uses area under deck, baffles and blade air movement to make suction; plugged deck cannot. Check and scrape periodically; more often in wet grass (clumps).
- tip: some owners use silicone spray made for mower decks to reduce sticking; helps but doesn't prevent all.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: remove key and spark plug boot; with heavy gloves grab each end of blade and rock up and down; movement or a knock means bad bearing in spindle housing or bent spindle.
- fix: repair or replace damaged parts.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: turn each deck pulley slowly; resistance or noise from the bearing means replace. Pulleys not rotating smoothly must be replaced.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: belt in good condition without cracks and wear, running securely around pulleys.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: run engine at full throttle whenever deck engaged.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: adjust ground speed in thick, tall or wet grass; slower ground speed reduces engine load; mowing too fast prevents good suction and pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: when mowing stripes, overlap each pass to avoid leaving strips.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: shell can get tweaked by hitting trees, rocks, hard surfaces; shell shape creates suction so damage compromises cut.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- symptom: uneven cut (low tire pressure makes the deck sit unlevel).
