## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: solenoid is an electromagnet on/off switch actuated to engage the starter motor; usually mounted on the starter motor but need not be; some are mounted closer to the battery than the starter.
- fact: every Ariens electric-start engine has one.
- fact: inside are a spring and a copper plate; goes bad if the spring gets weak or the copper plate corrodes; can also fail as a result of a weak starter, bad battery, or bad ground. "Electrical items can fail at any time."
- symptom: (riding or zero-turn Ariens) click or hum when key turned and mower doesn't start (problems/engine-wont-crank).
- symptom: a wire gets hot and begins to smoke or melt.
- confirm: bypass the solenoid; if the starter turns the engine over with the solenoid bypassed, solenoid is likely bad.
- confirm: check for loose or corroded wires or a bad ground BEFORE replacing the solenoid; these keep the solenoid from getting the power it needs.
- fact: identification: may be round or square, 3 or 4 posts; some sit on top of the starter; the battery positive (+) cable attaches to one side, so follow the positive cable from the battery to find it. Wiring schematics differ by mower; some include wiring for lights and 12-volt ports.
- brand: Ariens; "not all wire schematics are the same for every lawn mower."

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver (long), continuity light, wrenches (to check loose wires), needle nose pliers (if screwdriver doesn't work), battery charger (optional)
- step 1: make sure the battery has full charge; use voltmeter, at least 12.3 volts.
- step 2: get mower ready to start: set parking brake, make sure in neutral, turn key to on position.
- step 3: bypass the solenoid: lay a long screwdriver across the solenoid so it touches the two cables, the cable from the battery and the cable to the starter. May throw a spark; this is common. If the engine turns over while bypassed, good chance solenoid is bad. If screwdriver does not work well, use needle nose pliers to jump it.
- step 4: bench test (solenoids mounted on the starter): remove the starter from the engine; attach battery charger negative (-) clamp to the case of the starter, touch positive (+) clamp to the big post and the exciter wire on the solenoid; watch whether the starter works with the solenoid.
- safety: follow all precautions in the Ariens operator's manual; bypass may spark (normal); always wear safety gear.
- pro: if unsure how to diagnose/repair safely, have a professional (local Ariens dealer or repair shop) do it.
