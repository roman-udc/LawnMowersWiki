## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "smoke pouring out of my Husqvarna mower"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train, bad-head-gasket (engine gasket)
- confirm: Smoke color clue. Black smoke = engine running rich (more fuel than air): check/clean/replace air filter first, then other airflow restriction. Blue or white smoke = burning excess oil: check oil level, then piston rings, valve train, engine gasket.
- tip: Checking oil level and air filter before each mowing could prevent costly repairs.
- confirm (internal damage): remove spark plug (3/4 inch or 5/8 inch socket depending on mower); oil buildup on the tip indicates internal problem (piston ring or valve train); pinpointing needs engine teardown.
- pro: Low-oil damage, piston ring, valve train: take to a Husqvarna dealer or small engine shop to see if the engine can be rebuilt or replaced.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: Packed filter restricts air; rich mixture gives black smoke. Starved engine may overheat and draw air from the crankcase, pulling oil into the cylinder where it burns (smoke).
- fact: Always run with an air filter; check at least a couple times a year, more in dry dusty conditions.
- fix (paper filter): remove cover (one or two thumbscrews/knobs); lift filter out without knocking dirt into the intake; wipe housing with dry clean cloth; tap filter on hard surface to knock out dirt; never use compressed air (packs dirt into paper, damages filter); hold to light, if no light shows through, replace.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- symptom: Oil level on dipstick is a requirement; low oil causes friction, heat, components can melt, oil burns off. Adding oil to salvage rarely helps once extensive heat has built up.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: Too much oil raises crankcase pressure, pushes oil into cylinder or up to air intake via valve train; burns off when hot and smokes.
- fix: Drain a little: loosen drain plug and quickly retighten; take oil from the oil filter; turkey baster in fill area (don't reuse for cooking); oil evacuator.

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- symptom: Piston ring problem: scoring in the cylinder wall lets oil enter combustion chamber and burn.
- symptom: Valve train problem: burnt valve from overheating; muffler glowing red hot can indicate burnt valve or timing problem.
- confirm: Valve train verified only by removing the cylinder head and doing a leak-down test (small engine mechanic). Fix: valve and seat cut at certain angles so it seats and contacts the block.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- symptom: Failing engine gasket lets oil leak onto the hot muffler and burn off (smoke). Sometimes easy to find and replace, harder depending on location.
