- pro: charging-system problems to local Scag dealer; carburetor cleaning to Scag dealer or small engine shop.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: Easy to forget; a fuel leak can empty tank faster than usual. Fix leaks; add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Scag gas models: unleaded, 87 octane or higher, ethanol 10% or less.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Sticky substance clogs fuel system; ethanol moisture corrodes fuel components; breaks down as quickly as 30 days.
- fix: Drain and flush tank with a siphon; add fresh fuel with additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment: stabilize, clean, reduce moisture).

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: Run mower with and without cap; starts without and dies with cap after running a while = plugged cap.
- fix: Clean cap vent, otherwise replace.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Carbon-fouled plug misfires. Clean with wire brush unless very dark (replace). Replace if cracked porcelain or burnt electrode. Maintenance item: replace at least once a year.
- fix: Gap per engine manufacturer; secure wires.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Dirt in engine damages seals and scores cylinder. Check and clean several times per season; start season with new filter.
- step (paper): remove carefully, no dirt into intake; dry clean cloth in housing; tap on solid surface; don't use compressed air; hold to light; reuse if light shows; replace otherwise; reinstall and reattach cover.
- mistake: Never run a Scag without an air filter, even briefly.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Clogs if not changed annually or filtering very dirty fuel. Replace, arrow pointing in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: Stop fuel flow, remove hose end from line section furthest from tank, put in container, start flow; insufficient flow = restriction.
- fix: Stop flow, remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air until free of debris/gummy residue; repeat; replace if not cleared.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Most Scag mowers use a vacuum fuel pump (crankcase vacuum); replace if it can't build pressure, cracked, or leaking.
- confirm step 1: confirm fuel reaches pump inlet port; if not, check line restriction or filter.
- confirm step 2: shut off fuel, remove line from carburetor into container.
- confirm step 3: turn fuel on, start mower.
- confirm step 4: watch for steady or pulsating fuel flow; insufficient = replace pump.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fix: Disassemble, clean with carburetor cleaner including float bowl and needle; replace if too dirty; or have Scag dealer/small engine shop do it.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter; about 12.7 V expected; below that, charge. Replace if it won't hold a charge.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: Clean with baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda) and wire brush; check tight.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Scag uses several switches: deck won't run without operator present; won't start with parking brake not engaged.
- confirm: Multimeter, or temporarily bypass to identify.
- safety: Don't operate without safety switch or with it bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fact: Key turned, mower doesn't start or turn over. Test with multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: After verifying good plug, check coil continuity with multimeter; replace if break.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Not main cause but weak battery results. Bad stator or alternator among possible parts.
- pro: Scag dealer; electrical parts usually non-returnable.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Scag requires: operator in seat, parking brake set, levers in open/outward position; choke setting may be wrong. See operator's manual.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: Choke setting may prevent starting.

## page: procedures/start-a-zero-turn-mower
- fact: Scag start requirements: in seat, parking brake set, control levers open/outward, choke correct.
