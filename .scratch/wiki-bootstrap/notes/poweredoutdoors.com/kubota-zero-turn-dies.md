## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Kubota zero turn sputters and dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, clogged-deck (unmapped)
- safety: Remove ignition key and spark plug wires before repairs; follow Kubota operator's manual.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: Gas breaks down as soon as a month after purchase. Ethanol attracts moisture; ethanol-water mix separates and sinks to tank bottom, runs extremely hot, is corrosive, and leaves varnish and gummy deposits restricting flow.
- fix: If fuel has been in the tank more than 30 days, drain it. Kubota zero-turns require unleaded gasoline minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol. Mix gas with an additive like Sea Foam Motor Treatment before adding to the tank (stabilize, reduce moisture, clean system); run about 15 minutes to work through the system.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Plastic inline filter; replace when plugged; install with the arrow on the housing pointing in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fix: Use fuel shut-off valve to start/stop flow while checking each section; if blocked, turn off fuel, remove line, carb cleaner then compressed air; else install new line of same length and diameter; turn fuel on.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Most Kubota zero-turns use a vacuum pump (engine vacuum pulls fuel from tank to carb); can fail or degrade from old fuel.
- confirm: Confirm fuel flows to the pump; then turn off fuel, remove the line from the carburetor into a container, turn fuel on and start; steady or pulsating flow = working; poor flow = replace pump.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: Varnish plugs passages and sticks small internal parts.
- confirm: Verify fuel flow to carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; if it runs fine then bogs down and shuts off, carburetor is suspect.
- fix: Remove, disassemble and clean; or a small engine repair shop.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Replace yearly and clean several times a season; more often under heavy use or dusty conditions. Starved engine may overheat and shut down.
- fix (paper): remove element without letting dirt fall into intake; wipe housing with clean dry cloth; tap on hard surface; no compressed air; hold to light, replace if no light, damaged or oil-covered; reinstall and reattach cover.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fix: Remove dirt from fins and around engine block and shroud; replace broken fins; make sure the heat shield is correctly installed to help air circulation.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- symptom: Check oil before each use. Low oil: friction builds heat in crankcase, burns oil and parts, overheats and shuts down. Adding oil rarely fixes it afterwards.
- pro: Have a mechanic assess damage at a Kubota service center or the engine dealer (Kawasaki, Kohler or other engine maker).

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: Pressure builds in crankcase. Fix: remove a little via drain plug, oil filter, or oil evacuator / turkey baster at the fill; repeat until level is correct.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: Clean if dirty; replace if very dark or damaged; correct electrode gap per manufacturer; secure wires. Loose plug wires make it sluggish or die.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: Winding separates and shorts when hot; plug lacks voltage; dies after running a while. Check continuity with an ohmmeter; replace if break.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: Choke must be on/closed to start a cold engine, then off/open once warm; left on it dies because it needs more air.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: Cap vent not working makes tank vacuum; starts then shuts down after running.
- confirm: Loosen cap to let air in; if it runs better, retighten and see if the problem recurs; replace bad cap.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: See clogged-air-filter steps.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Kubota zero-turn battery not charging: run at high engine speed, don't idle long (engine power charges battery). If battery and wiring are good and it keeps going dead, suspect the charging system; have an experienced mechanic diagnose (parts thrown at it; electrical parts usually not returnable).
