## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "my Ariens mower is vibrating"
- alias: "feel like I'm going to shake out of my seat"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Source lists 9 reasons: engine mounting bolts, clutch, mower blades (wrong size / unbalanced), material wrapped around blade shaft, bad spindle housing components, deck belt, pulleys, idler bracket & spring, debris lodged in mower.
- fact: Fixing vibration prevents additional damage to the mower.
- safety: Follow operator's manual safety guidelines. Never work on the mower deck without first disconnecting the spark plug wires.
- pro: Consult a professional if you lack skills/knowledge/condition to do the repair safely; local Ariens dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: significant vibration coming from the engine (problems/mower-is-vibrating). A running engine vibrates; engine mounting bolts (also called motor mounts) under the engine absorb some of it.
- confirm: inspect the bolts for worn, broken or missing.
- fix: replace worn/broken/missing engine mounting bolts; tighten loose ones.
- brand: Ariens push mower has engine mounting bolts under the engine; only check them if you feel excessive vibration once the blade is engaged.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- symptom: Ariens mower shakes; ball bearing may fail on an electric clutch (problems/mower-is-vibrating).
- fix: the clutch needs to be replaced.
- brand: push mowers have no clutch; they have a blade-engage lever. Check the lever for loose or worn components.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: wrong or incorrectly balanced blade rotates unevenly (problems/mower-is-vibrating); blade of wrong length may hit the deck shell.
- fact: Wrong-size blades: author prefers OEM blades for quality and correct fit. Aftermarket can be good but be careful: need correct center hole size and blade length; matching those does not guarantee fit. Check by laying an aftermarket blade on top of an OEM blade. Wrong blades can cause additional damage to the mower.
- fact: Unbalanced blade = weighs more on one side. Causes: uneven wear in normal use (sand under the deck wears one side), or removing more metal from one side when sharpening.
- fact: Unbalanced blade rotates unevenly, can damage bearings in the spindle housing, creating blade movement and significant vibration at high speed.
- fix: keep blades balanced using a blade balancer to confirm equal weight at each end.
- tools: blade balancer.
- tip: link in Source to Ariens blade sharpening/balancing article (poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-sharpen-ariens-mower-blades/).

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- symptom: mower shakes (problems/mower-is-vibrating). String, wire and sturdy weeds can wrap around the blade spindle.
- fix: remove debris wrapped around the shaft, then check spindle and housing for damage.
- tip: walk the lawn and remove items before mowing, especially string, wire, sturdy weeds.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: blades wobble when engaged, causing vibration in the deck (problems/mower-is-vibrating).
- confirm: put on work gloves; grab each end of the mower blade and rock it up and down, looking for movement and listening for knocking.
- fix: if movement or knocking, remove the blade and spindle housing; inspect for bearing or spindle damage and repair.
- safety: wear work gloves (sharp blade edges).

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: stretched, worn deck belt has slack and does not sit securely in the pulleys (problems/mower-is-vibrating).
- confirm: replace when cracking, stretching or glazed shiny appearance.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: belt not seated securely because pulleys are not parallel to the deck, or pulley wobbles/one side sits higher than other (bad pulley bearing); also loose idler bracket and spring (problems/mower-is-vibrating).
- confirm: check belt routing and tension (remove excess slack) first. Check each pulley is secure with no wobble. Slowly rotate each pulley by hand, feel for restriction and listen for bearing noise.
- fix: replace the bearing. Some pulleys use only sealed bearings: replace the pulley and bearing assembly.
- fact: Idler bracket and spring wear over time: the bracket hole where the spring attaches can wear larger, spring stretches; causes vibration. Replace worn idler bracket or spring. Check bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage; replace if worn.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: something as small as a wood chip can cause extreme vibration (problems/mower-is-vibrating).
- fact: Debris collects under pulleys so they don't sit flat/parallel to deck; vibration arises as the deck moves around the pulley.
- fix: check small crevices and components; remove spindle covers and clean the deck regularly.

## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- tip: balance blade after sharpening using a blade balancer; remove equal metal from both sides.
