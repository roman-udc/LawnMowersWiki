## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Hustler mower has a bad cut"
- alias: "strips of uncut grass" / "cuts uneven"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, worn-blade-sail, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: also unlevel yard/high spots (not mower fault).
- safety: before troubleshooting cut problems remove the ignition key and spark plug wires, wait for everything to stop moving; follow operator's manual.
- fix: table: worn blade -> sharpen/replace; low tire pressure -> set to correct; bent blade -> replace; blade incorrectly installed -> reinstall with blade sail point upward; clogged deck -> scrape; deck not level -> level side to side and front to back; damaged spindle/housing -> replace; bad belt/pulleys -> replace; low engine speed -> full throttle; fast ground speed -> correct speed for conditions; wrong paths -> overlap; damaged deck shell -> repair/replace; unlevel yard -> smaller deck / roll lawn.
- tip: check tire pressures first; a low tire throws off deck level, and leveling the deck before checking tires means redoing steps.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: a low tire can throw off the level of the deck and cause uneven cut; check before adjusting the deck.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: blade sail = the high side of the blade that creates air movement under the deck; the airflow/suction stands grass tall for an even cut. Replace the blade when the sail is thinner than the center of the blade.
- fact: worn blade ends can leave strips between blades; replace a blade with rounded ends.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: dull edge beats and tears grass; tips turn brown a day or two after mowing. Sharpen if otherwise good; replace if in bad condition or edge gouges can't be sharpened out. Wear is normal (dirt/debris sucked in wears metal).
- confirm (blade removed): gain access under deck (lawn tractor may need deck removed), remove the blade and lay it on a new blade; gaps = replace.
- confirm (blade on): park on a flat surface; measure ground to blade tip on one side, rotate blade 180 degrees, measure the other side; if the difference is greater than 1/8 inch replace the blade.
- mistake: never straighten a bent blade; the metal is weakened, may crack and throw pieces at high speed, endangering people, pets and structures.
- fact: blades bend when hitting hard objects; sometimes a jolt/grinding noise, sometimes subtle.
- fact: blade balance: before installing, a blade must weigh the same on each end. Wear and sharpening (more metal shaved off one side) unbalance it. Use a blade balancer (inexpensive); remove metal from the heavy end. Alternate: hang the blade on a nail sticking out of a wall 3/4 to 1 inch; remove metal from the end hanging lower until it sits level. Unbalanced blades cause uneven cut, vibration and engine damage. Install per manual with correct hardware.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fix: remove and install with the blade sail point upward.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: blade wobbles when turning, vibration at high speed, bad cut.
- confirm: grab both blade ends and rock up/down for extra play; listen for knocking; if so, remove spindle housing and check spindle and bearing for wear or damage.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: clippings reduce the airspace needed for airflow/suction so grass isn't lifted; also strains the engine. Scrape regularly. Wet grass clumps and clogs faster and tire tracks show; avoid mowing wet grass, scrape after if you must.
- tip: silicone or Teflon spray under the deck can minimize sticking (nothing prevents it entirely).

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: higher on one side gives uneven cut. Follow the Hustler operator's manual to measure and level side to side, and to set the rake (front-to-rear pitch; on many decks the front sits slightly lower than the rear). Measurements vary by deck model.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: signs: cracking, shredding, shiny glazed look; belt sitting deep in the pulley grooves. Slows blade rpm.
- fix: replace with a new Hustler belt.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: good pulley is secure, flat and parallel to deck; bad bearing lets the pulley shift side to side when engaged; rotate by hand, feel resistance, listen for bearing noise; replace bearing or full pulley assembly depending on model.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: blade tip speed affects cut; always run full throttle with the deck engaged. If full throttle but power is down, take to a Hustler dealer or small engine shop.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: thick, tall or wet grass loads the engine; slow down. Too fast pushes grass over without time to stand it up.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: spacing paths too wide leaves uncut strips; a new mower may take a few mowings to judge overlap.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: hitting a post, tree or building can tweak the shell; repair or replace. Tip: get a price on a full deck replacement too; difference may be small once labor to swap components is included; weigh against the mower's age.

## unmapped
- lawn conditions: uneven lawn/high spots cause scalping; lawn roller; wide decks scalp dips and ditches; use small push mower there. (lawn-care advice, no page; suggest skip or note under uneven-cut.)
