## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "electric mower leaves uneven cuts or scalps"
- causes-named: bad-tire (damaged wheel), dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, wrong-cutting-height, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, mowing-too-fast, uneven-lawn (unmapped)
- safety: Shut off, remove battery, remove safety button/key, wait for parts to stop.
- tip: Check wheels first, then the blade.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- symptom: Damaged or loose wheel makes the mower sit unlevel. Tighten loose hardware; replace damaged wheels.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Worn blade tears grass ends; tips turn brown a day or two after mowing. Check: handful of grass, torn tips = sharpen/replace.
- fix: Sharpen with a file if blade still good; replace if extremely worn, gouged, rounded ends, or sail thinner than center.
- fact: Balance: blade becomes unbalanced from uneven wear by sand or from removing more metal from one end when sharpening. Use a blade balancer, or hang on a nail mounted on the wall with head sticking out 3/4 to 1 inch; remove metal from the end that hangs lower until level. Unbalanced blade can damage mower.
- symptom (bent): Hits rock, stump or toy; also causes vibration. Walk lawn first. Check: park on flat level surface, remove battery pack(s), wait for parts to stop; remove blade and lay on a new Toro blade; even a slight bend causes bad cut.
- mistake: Never straighten and reuse a bent blade; weakened metal can break off and fly.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail (high side) must face up; down tears grass, can hit high spots and damage the blade motor.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- symptom: Blade not tight won't rotate/cut. Torque per operator's manual.

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- fix: Too low overworks motor: raise deck in thick tall grass; double or triple cut: highest setting first, lower each pass.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Plugged deck lacks airflow room for suction; also strains motor. Clean with deck scraper or wire brush; avoid wet grass.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- symptom: Pushes grass over instead of cutting; thick/tall/wet grass is heavy load; motor can overload and shut off.
