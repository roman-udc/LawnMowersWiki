## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "zero turn won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: Take caution with electrical system (injury/electrocution); consult experienced mechanic if unsure.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Fully charged 12 V battery reads about 12.7 V; lower = charge.
- fix: Charge; replace if it won't hold a charge; bring old battery to avoid core fee.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Protective gear for eyes and skin; access battery and terminals (screwdriver); don't remove from casing.
- step 2: Positive (red, plus) cable first to positive terminal.
- step 3: Negative (black) to negative terminal.
- step 4: Don't touch anything without rubber coating.
- step 5: 12 V; start with 2 amps, up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge best.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fact: Zero-turn bounce and vibration loosens wires and connections; break in continuity.
- fix: Check tight; remove corrosion with baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 heaping tablespoons); secure; replace damaged or severely corroded components.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fact: Protects electrical system; blows from short or overload.
- confirm: Multimeter probe on each prong measuring resistance; near 0 good; infinity bad.
- fix: Replace with same amperage; repeated blowing = dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: Prongs B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid); key at start; resistance probes B and S; near 0 ohms good; infinite = replace.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- brand: Zero-turns use OPC (operator presence control) with switches that prevent start unless conditions met, e.g. brake engaged.
- confirm: Multimeter, or temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: Never operate without or with bypassed safety switch.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: Electromagnetic switch that engages starter motor; fails when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery, bad ground also.
- confirm: Fully charged battery required before testing.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: After ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid: remove starter and test.
- tip: Have a starter/alternator specialty shop test and rebuild before buying new (opinion).
