## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "zero turn is sputtering", "bogging down and running sluggish"
- causes-named: old-fuel, water-in-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-carburetor, fouled-spark-plug, wrong-engine-speed, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: Follow owner's manual; remove ignition key and spark plug wires before working under deck; never reach under until all moving parts stop; protect hands from blades.
- pro: If these aren't the cause or you're not comfortable, take to small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Gas needs unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol. Fuel can break down as quickly as 30 days after purchase. Ethanol attracts moisture, corrosive to fuel system; ethanol plus moisture leaves gummy residue that clogs fuel system.

## page: causes/water-in-fuel
- fact: Water separates and sinks to bottom of tank; can enter when mower is left outside in hard rain (cap seal may not keep it out).
- fix: Drain tank, fill fresh fuel, add additive like Sea Foam to stabilize and reduce moisture (author uses it every tank).

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Mowing stirs dirt that plugs filter; engine sluggish, sputters; can damage engine. New filter annually, clean several times per season.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: Carburetor regulates fuel-air mix; wrong mix (too much air or fuel) runs rough; old fuel gums internals.
- confirm: Confirm fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carburetor cleaner in intake, start; if engine runs fine then shuts off, carb likely dirty.
- fix: Clean; replace damaged parts or the carburetor if still malfunctioning.

## page: procedures/clean-the-carburetor
- fact: Source links its own steps; only the test above is given.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- confirm: Remove plug wire by hand, then plug with spark plug wrench.
- fix: Clean or replace plug with oil or carbon buildup; brake cleaner or rubbing alcohol plus wire brush removes buildup; very dark tip = new plug. Gap to manufacturer spec; wires secure.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Always run full throttle with deck engaged; otherwise bogs and sputters. If at full throttle but power lost, mechanic.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Packed deck makes engine work harder; wet grass plugs faster; dull blades magnify. Scrape regularly; check/sharpen blades every 25 hours of use.
