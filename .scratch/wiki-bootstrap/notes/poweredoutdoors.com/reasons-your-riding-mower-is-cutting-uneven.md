## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "riding mower cutting unevenly, leaving strips, stair-step cut, scalping"
- causes-named: worn-blade-sail, wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: 13 causes table: worn blade; low tire pressure; bent blade; blade installed incorrectly (sail up); clogged deck; deck not level (side to side and front to back); damaged spindle/housing; bad deck belt or pulleys; engine speed too low (full throttle); ground speed too fast; incorrect mowing paths (overlap); damaged deck shell; uneven yard/high spots (smaller deck or roll lawn).
- tip: Check tire pressure first, before touching the deck, or you may make unnecessary deck adjustments.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: Blade and deck create air movement/suction to lift grass; it also draws dirt that wears the blade metal; extreme wear leaves strips. Can't be prevented; check, sharpen, replace.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Low tire makes the deck not sit level. Use pressures on the tire sidewall.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Bent by tree root, rock, toy; impact may not be noticed; check for bent blade whenever cut gets bad. Also check spindle bearing damage.
- safety: Remove ignition key and unplug spark plug wires; thick work gloves.
- confirm: Remove blade (remove deck if needed) and lay on a new blade; gaps = bent. Or on flat surface measure ground to blade tip, rotate 180 degrees, measure again; more than 1/8" difference = replace.
- mistake: Straightening a bent blade; compromises metal, may crack and fly, risk to people, pets, structures.
- tip: Walk the yard before mowing.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail (high side) must face upward toward the deck. Upside down tears and bruises grass and sail can dig into ground causing spindle damage. Some blades are stamped "BOTTOM" or "THIS SIDE DOWN"; otherwise remember "Sail Up!"
- fact: Use correct hardware in correct order (owner's manual diagram or online parts diagram) or blade can move.
- fact: Balance: equal weight on each side. Unbalanced causes movement, spindle bearing damage, uneven cut. Becomes unbalanced from uneven wear or removing more metal from one side when sharpening. Use blade balancer, or nail in wall with head 3/4" to 1" out; remove metal from the end hanging lower until level.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Plugged deck reduces airflow/suction and loads engine. Scrape periodically.
- tip: Avoid cutting wet grass (clumps and sticks). Spray clean, dry deck with silicone or Teflon spray to reduce buildup (not a miracle; reduces scraping frequency).

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- confirm: Measure deck side to side for equal readings; also check rake (front to rear): some models need equal, others front slightly lower than rear.
- fact: Procedures vary by model; see owner's manual.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Grab each end of blade and rock up and down; listen for knocking or extra play; then disassemble spindle housing and check spindle/bearing.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Worn, stretched, cracked, shredded or glazed; fails to grip and spin pulleys at high speed.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Turn each pulley slowly by hand; resistance or noise = bad bearing; pulley then not level/parallel to deck.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Blade tip speed affects cut quality; run full throttle with deck engaged. If power seems off, small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Slow down in thick, tall or wet grass to avoid overworking engine; too fast pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Paths too wide without overlap miss strips; practice finding spacing.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- symptom: Hit fence post, tree, building; tweaked shell.
- fix: Repair or replace; get price of replacement shell and complete deck assembly from dealer plus labor to swap components.

## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact: Uneven yard: wide decks (48, 52, 60") scalp dips and ditches; use a push mower with smaller deck; use a weighted lawn roller for high spots.
- note: lawn-care content (roller) borderline; kept as uneven-cut cause "unlevel yard".
