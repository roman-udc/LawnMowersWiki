## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Cub Cadet bad or uneven cut"
- alias: "grass tips brown a couple of days after mowing"
- alias: "uncut strips of grass"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Blades create airflow/suction under the deck that lifts grass for a good cut. First check tire pressure (a low tire throws deck level), then blades and deck components.
- fact: Table fixes: dull/worn blade: sharpen; replace if sail is thinner than blade center, end rounded or gouged. Bent blade: replace, never straighten. Installed wrong: correct hardware, blade sails point upward toward the deck. Plugged deck: scrape. Low tire: correct, make left and right pressures equal. Deck unlevel: level per manual. Bent spindle/bearing damage: replace. Worn belt: replace stretched, worn, cracked, shredded belt. Bad pulley bearing: replace. Engine speed too low: full throttle when engaging blades. Ground speed too fast: slow for thick, tall, wet grass and inclines. Overlap/tall grass: overlap paths; double or triple-cut. Deck shell damaged: straighten if possible, else replace shell or complete deck. Uneven lawn: lawn roller; small format mower for ditches.
- tip: Dull-blade tell: day after mowing grass looks brown/burnt; pulled grass tips are brown, have little cuts or are shredded (bruised, beaten not cut). Replace blades if bruised grass seen; sharpen if just dull and in good condition.
- step: Bent blade check in place: park on a flat surface, measure ground to one blade tip, rotate blade 180 degrees, measure opposite tip; over 1/8 in variance means replace. Never straighten.
- step: 7-step deck checklist: (1) remove spark plug boot(s) and key, wear heavy leather gloves, wait for parts to stop; (2) blade condition; (3) tire pressure on the rear drive tires; (4) deck level: park on flat surface, measure deck height at all four corners (all equal); front of a zero-turn deck may need to sit slightly lower than the rear, see manual; leveling can take a trained dealer up to two hours; (5) spindle housing bearing: grab a blade at each end and rock up/down, feel and listen for a knock; movement or noise means bad bearing in the spindle housing (quill assembly); decide whether to rebuild or replace the cutter housing assembly (typically includes the bearing); some engines have one plug, others two; (6) belts: replace if edges shiny/glazed; (7) pulleys and idler brackets: with belt off spin pulleys and feel/listen; noise or restriction means replace pulley; check idler bracket for play where it mounts to the deck (replace bracket if movement).
- tip: Idler bracket must move and spring back to keep belt tension, so the instant load of engaging blades doesn't snap the belt. If not greased it may bind, the belt slips and blades slip in heavy/deep grass, leaving a bad cut the next day. Brackets use plastic or brass bushings, or bearings; may have a grease zerk; if not, disassemble, grease, reassemble. Grease during routine maintenance checks.
- tip: Reduce grass buildup under deck: full throttle, avoid wet grass, cut regularly, coat underside with Teflon or silicone spray made for mower decks. Buildup removes the under-deck space that stands grass up.
- fact: Deck maintenance: at least twice per mowing season for the average homeowner; more often if yard is heavily watered or fertilized (moisture and fertilizer erode the deck).

## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- alias: "Cub Cadet deck belt keeps falling off or breaking"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, oil-or-rust-on-belt
- fact: Table: stretched/worn belt: replace. Belt keeper damaged: bend back or replace. Shredded: replace, find and fix what it rubs against, check routing around pulleys. Bad pulley bearing: replace bearing or pulley assembly. Bad spindle housing bearing: replace bearing or housing assembly. Worn idler bracket/spring: replace. Debris interfering with tension: clean around and under tensioner, ensure it moves. Debris in pulley grooves: remove. Oil on belt: fix leak, clean deck, new belt. Rust on belt: remove rust, replace very rusty components and belt.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Cub Cadet mower deck vibrating"
- causes-named: bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-clutch, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Common reason: unbalanced blades (one side heavier from wear or sharpening).
- fact: Table: worn clutch: replace an electric clutch with ball bearing failure; on a manual clutch check belt, pulleys, bushings. Blades installed wrong: correct hardware, sails point up toward deck, tighten nuts to the manual's spec. Unbalanced: balance to equal weight each end, remove metal from the heavier end. Foreign material wrapped around blade shaft: remove, inspect spindle housing. Bad spindle bearing: replace bearing or housing. Loose/stretched belt: replace. Bad pulley: replace. Debris in deck: remove.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Cub Cadet blades won't engage or turn on"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: Blades won't keep rotating when belt is worn and no longer grips. Also check what fails to engage the clutch solenoid on an electric clutch, or the worn clutch cable, linkages, bushings on a manual clutch.
- fact: Table: worn belt: replace. Belt off pulleys: look for worn tensioner arm or spring, loose belt, worn pulley bearings. Faulty PTO switch: replace. Bad electric clutch: replace. Manual clutch: replace worn cable, bushings, linkages. Weak battery: charge or replace. Bad safety switch: replace. Blown fuse: replace with same capacity; mechanic for root cause if it keeps blowing.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Low tire = deck not level; check first; left and right pressures must be equal; drive tires are the large rear tires.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Install with correct hardware; blade sails must point upward toward the deck.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: See 7-step checklist: measure all four corners; front of zero-turn deck may sit slightly lower; dealer may need up to two hours.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- fact: Park flat, measure deck height at all four corners, adjust per owner's manual; zero-turn front may sit slightly lower than rear; up to two hours for a trained dealer; have dealer do it if difficult.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Rock blade ends up/down, listen for knock; replace bearing/spindle/housing.

## page: causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing
- fix: Bend keeper back to original position or replace if damaged.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fix: Remove material wrapped around the blade shaft; inspect spindle housing for damage.

## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact: Belt, pulley bearings, spindle bearings, idler bracket bushings, clutches wear.
