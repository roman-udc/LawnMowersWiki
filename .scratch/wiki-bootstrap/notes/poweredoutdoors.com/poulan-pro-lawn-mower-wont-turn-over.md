## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Poulan Pro won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-wiring-or-ground, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: before electrical repairs remove the black negative cable from the battery.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: keep battery fully charged, especially in storage; a not fully charged battery in cold can freeze and be damaged.
- fix: if it fails to hold charge, replace (dealer, hardware or auto store); bring old battery, core fee otherwise.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: wear eye and skin protection; access battery and terminals (screwdriver to uncover); do not remove battery from casing.
- step 2: connect positive (red, plus) charger cable first to positive terminal.
- step 3: connect negative (black) cable to negative terminal.
- step 4: don't touch anything not rubber-coated (electrocution).
- step 5: set charger to 12 V (typical); more amperage charges faster; start with 2 amps and work up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge is best.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fact: wiring shakes loose from vibration; moisture corrosion affects continuity.
- fix: remove battery cables, clean with wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); replace terminals/components if can't be cleaned.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fact: fuse protects electrical system from surges or shorts.
- confirm: multimeter probe on each prong of fuse measuring resistance: near 0 good; infinity bad.
- fix: replace with same amperage; if keeps blowing, dealer/repair shop for root cause.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: turn key and nothing happens.
- confirm: multimeter on resistance; prongs marked B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid); key in START position; probes on B and S; good = near 0 ohms; bad = infinite resistance, replace.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system; brake switch or seat switch can fail.
- confirm: multimeter, or temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: never operate without safety switch; never run with a switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch that initiates starter motor. Goes bad when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause failure.
- confirm: fully charged battery needed before testing (see procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid).

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid.
- confirm: starter can be removed and tested.
- pro: have a starter/alternator repair shop test it before buying a new starter.
