## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "WORX electric mower won't start"
- symptom: insufficient power supply, bad electrical connection, overworked motor, or incorrect operating procedure
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, battery-not-seated, bad-charger, overheated-battery, incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, clogged-deck-chute, bad-motor
- safety: remove the safety key and battery before repairs; handle electric currents and sharp blades with care
- fact: if under warranty consult WORX first so repairs don't void it
- pro: authorized WORX service center for damaged motor/parts; WORX customer service 1-866-354-WORX

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: use a compatible battery; wrong size can cause no start and worse electrical problems
- fact: models with two batteries need both fully charged; one dead battery can stop the mower
- fact: most use an external charging station (must be the one for that battery); some use a charging cord
- fact: LED system on mower or charger shows charge; more green lights = more charge; with two batteries the LEDs show the lowest-charged one
- symptom: battery going bad: fully charged battery not holding charge as long; gets very hot; charges very slowly
- fact: batteries fail from age, extreme temperatures or mechanical failure
- fix: buy a replacement battery

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- confirm: battery secure and making good contact in port; wipe battery compartment lid before opening so debris doesn't fall in
- fix: remove and reinstall; battery slides into guide rails and snaps in; lid must close securely

## page: causes/bad-charger
- fact: check outlet and cord connections so charger is fully plugged in; standard 120V household outlet with sufficient power
- fact: WORX plug style must match outlet (3-prong to 3-prong); don't modify the plug
- tip: clean charger vents; keep charger away from extreme heat or cold

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- symptom: hot battery makes mower shut down and not start
- fix: clean mower vents; let battery cool before restarting

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: check operator's manual for new/used mowers; WORX safety key prevents children accidentally depressing the safety handle; skipping it prevents starting
- step 1 (push and self-propelled): battery securely in place and safety key inserted
- step 2: pull the bail (safety bar) toward the handle and press the safety lock-out button to start the motor
- step 3: release the release safety button
- fact: mower shuts off when the safety bar is released

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: test each with multimeter, or temporarily bypass with a jumper wire
- fix: replace bad switch
- safety: never operate without all safety switches working
- fact: push/self-propelled switches: bar/bail lever switch (bar must be pulled to handle; releasing shuts off) and momentary switch for on/off button; check manual for others

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- symptom: loose or pinched wire shorts; wiggling a wire may reveal it; multimeter can find a short; common pinch spot near the handle fold
- tip: fold/unfold carefully so wires stay on the outside of the handle
- fix: repair pinched wire with a wire nut; remove battery and safety key first
- fact: repairs/modifications outside an authorized WORX repair centre can void all WORX warranties

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- symptom: grass/debris under deck inhibits blade rotation and can prevent starting; mower also stops/won't start when overloaded by thick, tall or wet grass
- fix: remove battery and safety key before deck work; raise cutting height, slow down, avoid wet grass

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: damaged motor or other parts need service-center testing

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- safety: don't use an electric mower in the rain; water can short it out and prevent starting
- fact: author links a dangers-of-electric-mowers guide
