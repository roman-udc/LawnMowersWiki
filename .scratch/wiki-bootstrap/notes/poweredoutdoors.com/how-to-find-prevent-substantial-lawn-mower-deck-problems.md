## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "grass tips brown a couple days after mowing"
- alias: "uncut strips of grass"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Blades create air movement/suction under deck that lifts grass. Check tire pressure first (low tire = deck not level), then blades and deck components.
- fix: dull blade: sharpen; replace if sail thinner than center, tip rounded, or gouges. Bent: replace, never straighten. Wrong install: correct hardware, blade sails point upward toward deck. Plugged deck: scrape. Tires: correct, left and right equal. Unlevel deck: per operator's manual. Bent spindle: replace; check spindle bearing. Worn belt (stretched, worn, cracked, shredded): replace. Pulley bearing restricting rotation or wobbly: replace. Engine speed: full throttle when engaging blades. Ground speed: slow down in thick, tall or wet grass or on inclines. Path overlap/tall grass: overlap properly; double or triple-cut very tall grass. Damaged deck shell: straighten if possible, else replace shell or complete deck. Uneven lawn: roll with lawn roller to avoid scalping; small mower for ditches (lawn-care, minor).
- tip: Check for bent blade on flat surface: measure ground to blade tip, rotate to opposite end, measure again; over 1/8" variance = replace. Repeat for each blade.
- tip: Level deck: measure at all four corners from ground to bottom of deck; variance, follow owner's manual (varies by model).

## page: problems/belt-keeps-coming-off
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, oil-or-rust-on-belt
- fact: Occurs when deck components worn, debris stuck, or belt worn so proper tension can't be kept.
- fix: stretched/worn belt: replace. Damaged keeper: bend back or replace. Shredded: replace, find item belt rubs, check routing. Pulley bearing: replace pulley bearing/assembly. Spindle housing bearing: replace bearing or housing assembly. Worn idler tensioner bracket/spring: replace. Debris around tensioner: remove, make sure tensioner moves. Debris in pulley grooves: remove. Oil: fix leak, clean oil from deck and components, new belt. Rust: remove rust, replace very rusty components, new belt.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, blade-installed-upside-down, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Common reason is unbalanced blades (one side heavier) from wear or from sharpening.
- fix: Worn clutch: replace electric clutch with ball bearing failure; on manual-engagement clutch check belt, pulley, bushings. Blades installed wrong: correct hardware, sails point upward to deck, tighten nuts to operator's manual spec. Unbalanced: remove a little metal from heavy end until equal. Material wrapped around blade shaft: remove, inspect spindle housing. Bad spindle bearing: replace bearing or housing assembly. Loose/stretched belt: replace. Bad pulley: replace. Debris lodged in deck: remove.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- causes-named: worn-drive-belt, worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: Blades stop turning when belt is worn and no longer grips pulleys. Also check things that fail to engage the clutch solenoid on an electric clutch, or worn clutch cable, linkages, bushings on a manual clutch.
- fix: worn belt: replace. Belt off pulleys: look for worn tensioner arm or spring, loose belt, worn pulley bearings. Faulty PTO switch: replace. Bad electric clutch: replace. Manual clutch cable/bushings/linkage worn: replace. Weak battery: charge, replace if won't hold charge. Bad safety switch: replace. Blown fuse: replace with same capacity; mechanic finds root cause if it keeps blowing.

## page: problems/mower-is-noisy
- alias: "mower deck squealing"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Check belt first; belt slipped and stuck between belt guides: reinstall correctly, replace if worn. Then pulley and spindle bearings.
- fix: broken tensioner spring: replace. Material wrapped on blade shaft: remove. Bad spindle housing bearing: replace assembly (some zero-turns allow bearing only). Bad pulley bearing: replace assembly (some zero-turn pulleys allow bearing replacement).

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Grass buildup under deck reduces airflow and makes engine work harder. Causes: mowing wet grass (only mow dry), low engine speed (mow at high throttle), long grass (mow more often; double or triple-cut).
- tip: Silicone deck spray reduces sticking (won't stop it all); scrape deck routinely. Cutting wet grass avoided; cut regularly; full throttle with deck engaged.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Worn blades beat the grass, ends turn brown in a couple of days. Sharpen or replace dull blades. Bent blade: 1/8" variance test; never straighten (weakened, spins at high speed).

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: gloved, grab each end of blade and rock up/down; movement or noise = bad bearing in spindle housing. Some manufacturers allow bearing replacement, others only the whole assembly.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: With deck belt removed, spin pulleys; must be smooth and quiet; noise or restriction = replace. Check idler bracket for play where it mounts to deck.
- tip: Idler bracket must be kept greased so it doesn't seize; it is designed to move and spring back to keep belt tension. If it can't move, belt slips and blades slip in heavy/deep grass.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- confirm: Belt must have no cracking, wear or glazed appearance.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Install with correct hardware, blade sails pointing upward toward deck; tighten nuts to operator's manual spec.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- confirm: Measure from ground to bottom of deck at all four corners.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- step 1: Check tire pressures first.
- step 2: Measure all four deck corners ground to deck bottom on level surface.
- step 3: If variance, follow operator's manual (varies by manufacturer/model).

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Deck maintenance at least twice per mowing season for average homeowner; more often if yard heavily watered or fertilized (moisture and fertilizer erode deck). Scrape/clean underside more than once a year; inspect all deck parts then.
- safety: Remove spark plug boot and ignition key before deck work; wait for parts to stop; heavy gloves; author pockets the key.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- fact: Scrape deck routinely; silicone deck spray; avoid wet grass.
