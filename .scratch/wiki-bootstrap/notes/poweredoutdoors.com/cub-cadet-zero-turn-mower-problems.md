## page: guides/zero-turn-safety
- safety: follow the operator's manual. Always park on a flat level surface, remove the ignition key, and remove the spark plug wires before repairs.
- tip: keep the mower clean and stored dry to prevent corrosion; routine maintenance and fresh gasoline reduce problems and extend life.

## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn turns over and won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: engine needs air, fuel and spark to form the explosion; battery, starter solenoid and switches can also fail.
- fix per cause: empty tank - fill with fresh fuel; old fuel - empty tank, refill with fresh fuel plus a fuel-system-cleaning additive like Sea Foam; bad plug - replace dirty/damaged plug; air filter - clean or replace if very dirty/damaged; fuel filter - replace; clogged fuel line - remove and clean, replace if needed; bad pump - replace; dirty carb - remove and clean, rebuild or replace if damaged; battery/cables/terminals - charge weak battery, replace if it will not hold a charge, secure cables and remove corrosion; safety switch / ignition switch - replace; starter solenoid - test and replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn starts then dies / stalls"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, clogged-deck-chute, bad-ignition-coil, fouled-spark-plug
- fact: same items as no-start plus plugged mower deck, bad ignition coil, low or high engine oil level.
- fix: old fuel - drain tank, mix fresh fuel with stabilizer additive (stabilize, reduce moisture, clean the fuel system); cooling fins - clean around them and replace damaged fins; carb - clean, rebuild or replace if it can't be cleaned or internal parts are damaged; fuel cap that no longer vents - replace; engine oil - correct the level by adding or removing a little oil to engine manufacturer's recommendation, consult a mechanic if that doesn't fix it (possible extensive engine damage); plugged deck - scrape the deck and sharpen blades; ignition coil with a break in continuity - replace; plug dark in colour, damaged or worn - replace, slightly dirty good plug - clean with a wire brush.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Cub Cadet won't move forward or reverse"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: the hydrostatic transmission pumps fluid to move the wheels; pressure is so great you cannot free-roll the zero turn without bypassing the transmission.
- fact: transmission bypass levers - there is one near each drive wheel; must be in the operating position.
- fact: worn pump drive belt - replace and confirm it sits securely on the pulleys. Bad tensioner pulley - replace and grease the tensioner arm. Missing/broken idler arm spring - replace. Low/old hydraulic oil - change at Cub Cadet's recommended intervals and keep full. (Note in Source: some transmissions are unserviceable; consult the Cub Cadet dealer.)
- brand: Cub Cadet zero-turn; some hydro transmissions are unserviceable.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- fact: Cub Cadet "transmission bypass levers", one near each drive wheel, must be in operating position.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn vibrates"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: a vibration can make shaking and bouncing worse; pulleys and blades wobbling can loosen the deck belt or make it fall off.
- fix: engine mount bolts - secure, replace worn/missing. Failing clutch - replace a bad electric clutch; on a manual-engagement clutch replace worn clutch cable, lever or bushings. Blades - replace damaged, ensure balanced. Material wrapped around blade shaft - remove it, inspect spindle and housing. Spindle bearings - replace when worn. Deck belt - replace worn or loose. Pulleys - replace damaged pulleys or ones that feel restricted when turned by hand or wobble from a bad bearing. Debris - clean mower checking all crevices.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- alias: "Cub Cadet engine overheats"
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast
- fact: engine oil plays an important part in keeping the engine cool. Use an air-cooled engine oil of the right viscosity that is high in zinc; keep oil level full.
- fact: check items blocking airflow: cooling fins, engine shroud, engine guard. Inspect air filter, mower deck and blade condition.
- fix: wrong oil - drain and fill with fresh oil; use SAE30 or 10W-30 in most conditions, a different viscosity may be needed in very warm conditions. Missing/loose engine guard - replace/secure. Plugged air filter - replace. Plugged deck - scrape off clippings and debris. Dull blades - sharpen, replace worn. Overworking the engine - slow ground speed to match conditions, avoid cutting wet grass, double or triple-cut an overgrown lawn.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn is smoking"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, spilled-oil-on-engine, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- fact: smokes when the air filter is plugged, engine oil is not correct, or a bad engine gasket lets oil seep onto a hot surface like the muffler; otherwise internal problems (worn piston ring or burnt valve).
- fix: plugged filter - replace. Too much oil - remove a little oil from the crankcase until correct, check air filter. Low oil - small engine technician inspection; correcting the level is unlikely to save an engine that began smoking from low oil. Bad engine gasket - locate and repair. Internal ring/valve train problems - technician testing.
- pro: small engine technician for continued smoking and internal damage.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- (see above) weak hydrostatic transmission: causes are bad pump drive belt (replace), bad tensioner pulley (replace, grease tensioner arm), low or old hydraulic oil (change at regular intervals, keep full). Hydro oil old or tank low makes the transmission weak; drive belt or tensioner worn/broken can also feel weak.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn doesn't drive straight / pulls to one side"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fix: tire pressure to manufacturer spec, equal on each side; bad dampers - replace with new; tracking adjustment - adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed so each tire moves at the same speed (owner's manual).

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- step 1: check tire pressures and dampers first.
- step 2: adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change the rotation speed of the tire so both tires turn at the same speed; refer to the owner's manual.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Cub Cadet running rough and sputters / bogs down"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: look for items that restrict fuel or airflow or cause an intermittent spark, and items that load the engine (fast ground speed, plugged deck, dull blades). Throttle not at fastest speed makes the engine sluggish when blades are engaged.
- fix: old fuel - drain and refill with gas and stabilizer; clogged fuel line - clear with carburetor cleaner and compressed air, new line if it can't be cleared; filter - replace; carb - clean, rebuild or replace; gas cap that no longer vents - replace; dirty plug - wire brush, new plug if very dark, worn or damaged; fast ground speed - slow down for tall, thick or wet grass; low engine speed - throttle at highest position when engaging blades; plugged deck - scrape; dull blades - sharpen.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn blades won't engage"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: blades won't engage when PTO switch is faulty, battery weak, safety switch failed or clutch bad; won't turn when belt is worn or off the pulleys from a stretched belt, bad pulley bearing or worn tensioner parts.
- fix: deck belt worn, cracked or shiny/glazed - replace. Belt falls off pulleys - replace worn or damaged deck parts incl. worn idler arm, worn spring, bad pulleys; replace a stretched belt. Bad PTO switch - replace. Bad clutch - replace; on a manual-engagement clutch replace worn clutch cable, lever, springs and linkages. Weak battery - test, charge if under 12.7 V for a 12 V battery, replace if it won't hold a charge. Safety switch - replace. Blown fuse - replace with same amperage.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn leaking gas"
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-gas-cap-seal
- confirm: leak can be hard to find if gas has evaporated; check the carburetor and dry cracked fuel lines. Plastic components degrade and seals dry out.
- fix: carb bowl gasket (between bowl and carburetor) - replace; stuck float or float needle - clean carb and replace damaged parts; cracked fuel line - replace; tank leaking at seams - replace; shut-off valve - replace; weak fuel filter - new; fuel pump - new; gas cap seal - replace cap (on some caps you can buy only the rubber seal).

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- fact: just clicking on start points to a bad starter solenoid; also check what affects power to the solenoid: battery, cables, terminals, ground; if none, check the starter motor.
- fix: damaged cables - replace; remove corrosion from terminals and components; secure loose cables. Battery - test, charge if below 12.7 V (12 V battery), replace if it won't hold a charge. Ground - remove corrosion, ensure the ground cable from the battery and the ground cable from the starter solenoid make good contact (a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded). Solenoid - test and replace. Starter motor - replace.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- fact: Cub Cadet: a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Cub Cadet zero turn bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: first check tire pressure (a low tire makes the deck sit unlevel), then components above and under the deck.
- fix: dull/worn blades - sharpen; replace blades extremely worn where the sails are thinner than the center of the blade or the ends are worn and rounding. Bent blade - replace. Blade installed wrong - correct hardware; blade sails must point upward toward the deck. Bent spindle - replace, inspect spindle housing. Deck belt stretched, worn, cracked or glazed - replace; rotate pulleys slowly by hand and replace if you feel restriction, hear bearing noise or see wobble. Plugged deck - scrape. Uneven tire pressure - correct, even left and right. Unlevel deck - level side to side; deck sits slightly lower in front than rear; see operator's manual for exact spec. Engine speed too low - throttle at highest when engaging blades. Ground speed too fast - slow down in thick, wet or tall grass; too fast can push grass over instead of cutting. Operator error - overlap paths so no strips are left between passes; use a small format deck like a push mower for ditches and steep grade changes.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: replace blades where sails are thinner than the blade center or the ends are worn and rounding.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: sails must point up toward the deck.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: deck sits slightly lower in front than rear; level side to side; low tire can cause unlevel deck.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fix: overlap paths properly.

## page: procedures/test-the-battery
- fact: below 12.7 V on a 12 V battery - place it on a charger; replace if it won't hold a charge.
