## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Husqvarna won't start"
- alias: "Husqvarna turns over but won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, wrong-fuel, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-coil, incorrect-starting-procedure, wrong-choke-setting, fuel-valve-off
- fact: Engine needs air, fuel and spark; a mower that cranks (turns over) shows battery, starter solenoid and starter motor work; look at air filter, bad fuel, carburetor, spark plug.
- confirm: Fuel-system test: check fuel shut-off valve is open (often closed for storage/transport). Remove air filter cover and filter, spray carburetor cleaner into the intake (author prefers carb cleaner over starter fluid), try to start (recoil or key). If it tries to start or starts: fuel system problem (most common: dirty carburetor, clogged fuel filter, clogged fuel lines; fuel pump if equipped). If it doesn't attempt to start: most likely a spark plug problem.
- confirm: Airflow test: remove air filter, inspect it (clean or replace if dirty/damaged). Check choke plate: manual choke - lever on: plate closed; lever off (on some models fast throttle position): plate open. Auto-choke (newer Husqvarna push mowers, thermostat-controlled): closed when cold, open when warm. If not opening/closing correctly check choke linkage and cable; loosen a stuck choke by lubricating linkages and choke shaft with carb cleaner; replace bad choke cable.
- confirm: Spark test: remove plug boot, attach spark plug tester to the plug wire and to the plug (plug still installed), crank; glow in transparent section = spark. No glow: bad plug or ignition coil; on electric start also check battery, wiring, solenoid.
- tools: spark plug tester, multimeter, carburetor cleaner, compressed air

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: Add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Unleaded, octane 87 or higher, ethanol 10% or less; higher ethanol can cause significant engine damage; less ethanol is better. Husqvarna 4-cycle engines: minimum 87 octane, max 10% ethanol.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: Shelf life about 30 days before it breaks down and loses volatility. Ethanol attracts moisture; ethanol/water mixture separates and sinks; as moisture evaporates a gummy substance clogs fuel components.
- fix: Drain and flush the tank; refill fresh fuel with an additive such as Sea Foam.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: Plugged vent makes a vacuum, fuel can't flow.
- confirm: Remove cap and start; if it runs, replace cap and keep running; if it shuts off, cap is the problem.
- fix: Clean the cap; a thin piece of wire may unclog the vent; else replace.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: Carbon buildup or oil; cracked porcelain; wrong gap; loose connection; misfire.
- fix: Inspect, gap correctly, replace; attach wires securely. Cleaning a dirty plug with a wire brush is fine; replace if very dirty, broken porcelain, or damaged electrode.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: Mower may not start, or runs rough and overheats.
- step (paper): remove cover and filter; wipe dirt from housing and cover with clean dry cloth (don't let dirt fall into intake); tap element on solid surface; hold to light - good light through means reuse, otherwise replace; reinstall.
- step (foam): remove; wipe housing; inspect (very dark, brittle, torn = replace); wash in water and mild detergent; rinse till clear; squeeze (don't wring - tears); dry; apply clean engine oil to completely cover, squeeze out excess; reinstall.
- mistake: NEVER apply oil to a foam pre-filter (wraps around a paper element); it can damage the paper filter.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- symptom: Riding mower, lawn tractor, zero turn: moves gas to the carburetor when tank sits lower than carb; draws using engine vacuum. Housing may have a small crack or leak at seams.
- confirm: Verify fuel flow to the pump (use shut-off valve or fuel clamps to start/stop flow; some mowers have no valve), then confirm steady or pulsating flow out of the pump.
- fix: Replace bad pump.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: Cracked or plugged filter: replace; arrow on inline filter points in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fix: Remove line, spray carb cleaner, blow compressed air until free of debris and gummy residue; replace if you can't clear.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: Confirm gas reaches the carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; if it starts then stops once the cleaner burns off, carb likely clogged.
- fix: Remove and take apart, clean with carb cleaner including float bowl and needle; replace if too dirty; may need rebuilding.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fix: Test with multimeter: need about 12.7 V; charge if lower; replace if it doesn't hold a charge.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: Clean corroded terminals with baking soda solution: 2 cups water with 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda; scrub with wire brush; make sure attached securely.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- symptom: Switches prevent deck running without operator present, and prevent start when the parking brake isn't engaged.
- confirm: Multimeter test or temporarily bypass.
- safety: Do not operate a mower without the safety switch; never run with it bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: Key turn does nothing, mower doesn't turn over.
- confirm: Multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Electromagnetic on-off switch that actuates the starter motor. A click or hum when turning the key; a wire on the solenoid that gets hot, smokes or melts.
- fix: Test and replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- symptom: Push mowers: bad pulley, loose/missing spring, broken clips.
- fix: Try replacing spring and restringing; if clips/pulley damaged replace the whole recoil assembly.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fix: After confirming plug is good, check coil continuity with a multimeter; replace if broken.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- symptom: Riding/tractor/zero turn: not main reason for no-start but causes a weak battery; bad stator or alternator.
- pro: Take to dealer; guessing at parts is expensive and most centers don't accept electrical part returns.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fix: Follow operator's manual; safety features shut off the mower otherwise.
