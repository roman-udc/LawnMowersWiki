## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Kubota zero turn won't move forward or reverse"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- safety: Don't tow the mower (can damage the transmission). Wait for the transmission to cool; careful with hot hydraulic fluid. Remove the ignition key and spark plug wires before repairs.
- fact: Source's intro text says "drive release levers are in the operating position" but the intended meaning is they are NOT in the operating position (bypass); the section heading says not in the operating position.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: Pushed by hand earlier; transmission bypass levers left in "bypass" so hydros aren't engaged.
- brand: Rods on Z100, Z200 and Z400 series; levers on Z700 series, left and right sides.
- fix: Put them in the operating position so the drive levers move the mower.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- fix: Check that the pump drive belt hasn't fallen off and is secure around the pulleys; replace if any wear shows.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Tensioner pulley (usually hard plastic) or its bearing breaks or wears.
- fix: Replace; keep the tensioner arm greased.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- fact: Spring tensions the pump drive belt; replace if broken or fallen out.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: Old or low fluid: won't move or seems very weak.
- confirm: Check the level before use while the oil is cool. Wipe around the caps before removing. Dirt is often a factor in hydraulic failure; don't contaminate the system.
- fix: Add fluid to FULL on the dipsticks or overflow tanks (model-dependent). Check for leaks when low; contact a Kubota turf dealer for leaks.
- brand: Kubota transmission oil and capacity table:
  - Z121SKH, Z122EBR, Z122RKW, Z125SKH: 5.1 Qts, SAE20W-50
  - Z231BR, Z231KH, Z231KW, K251BR, K251KH: 5.1 Qts, SAE20W-50
  - Z411KW, Z421KW, Z421KT: 5.1 Qts, SAE20W-50
  - Z723KH, Z724KH, Z725KH: 6.3 Qts, Parker HT-1000
  - Z724XKW, Z726XKW, Z751KWi, Z781: 7 Qts, Parker HT-1000

## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Change at regular intervals (no hours stated in this Source); oil/capacity table above.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- symptom: Bad or low fluid can't lubricate; friction and overheating. Can also result from more extensive damage.
- pro: Take to a dealership if the hydraulic pump leaks, or the mower runs fine cold but stops running when hot.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- symptom: Mower slow or won't move; noisy when moving; oil gets hot and expands more than normal. Bleed after changing fluid or any time the transmission is opened.

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1: Raise the rear drive tires off the ground if possible; otherwise do it in an open area free of obstructions. Make sure there is sufficient oil in the transmission.
- step 2: Open the bypass valves, release the parking brake if on, start the engine.
- step 3: Move the drive levers slowly forward then reverse about 6 times.
- step 4: Close the bypass valves. Throttle high; move the drive levers forward and reverse about 6 times.
- step 5: Repeat until there is no excessive noise and the tires move at normal speeds.
- step 6: Shut off the engine, engage the parking brake, check the expansion tanks are at the FULL COLD line; add oil to that line if not.
