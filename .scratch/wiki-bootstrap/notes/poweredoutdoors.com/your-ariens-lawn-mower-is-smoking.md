## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Ariens lawn mower is smoking"
- alias: "cloud of smoke from mower"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket (engine gasket), worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train, spilled-oil-on-engine, rich-fuel-mixture
- fact: also oil spilled on engine/muffler, or excessive fuel burned in cylinder.
- fact: blue or white smoke = burning excess oil (damaged piston rings, valve train, engine gasket leak, or oil pushed into cylinder from plugged air filter). Black smoke = excess fuel burning (plugged air filter makes mixture rich); if not the filter, look for another air restriction.
- safety: follow owner's manual guidelines.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- mistake: never run the mower without an air filter, not even briefly; dirt gets to engine, damaging cylinder and seals.
- fact: replace annually, clean several times per season; clean mower often in dusty conditions.
- fact: why smoke: restricted air makes engine run rich (black smoke); a very plugged filter makes engine draw air from the crankcase, pulling oil that burns in cylinder; smoke itself can plug the filter, worsening it.
- step: paper element: remove from housing; wipe housing with dry cloth, no dirt into intake; tap on solid surface; hold to light, reuse if light seen else replace; reinstall and attach cover.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: engine oil amount is a requirement not a recommendation. Low oil: friction, heat, oil burns, parts melt, smoke; adding oil likely won't fix, probably engine damage; take to mechanic. Check oil before each mowing.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: too much oil raises crankcase pressure and pushes oil into cylinder and air intake through valve train; burns and smokes.
- fix: remove a little via drain plug or oil filter, or evacuator/turkey baster at fill area; recheck dipstick, repeat; check air filter for oil/plugging and replace if so.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- fact: bad gasket leaks oil onto hot surface like muffler, burns and smokes.

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- confirm: remove spark plug(s); oil on plug signals piston ring or valve train problem (doesn't say which). Teardown plus leak-down and compression checks needed to identify.
- fact: piston ring or cylinder wall scoring lets oil into combustion chamber.
- pro: small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/bad-valve-train
- fact: overheated mower can develop a burnt valve; found only by removing cylinder head and leak-down test; mechanic cuts valve and seat correctly.

## page: causes/rich-fuel-mixture
- fact: black smoke from higher fuel concentration due to restricted air.

## page: causes/spilled-oil-on-engine
- fact: oil spilled on engine or muffler smokes as it burns off.
