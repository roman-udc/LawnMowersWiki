## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Ferris zero turn won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting
- fact: needs spark, fuel and air; air restriction from stuck choke or plugged filter; fuel restriction from component failure, clogged line or filter.
- fact: table rows: no gas, bad/old fuel (drain, fresh fuel, Sea Foam), clogged cap vent (replace cap), bad plug (dirty/broken/connection/gap), plugged air filter, plugged fuel filter, bad pump, blocked lines (carb cleaner + compressed air), dirty carb, bad battery/loose/dirty terminals, bad safety switch, bad ignition switch or coil.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: most no-start causes can also be the reasons a mower quits running; also ignition coil, wrong oil level, dirty/broken cooling fins, clogged deck.
- fact: too much oil raises crankcase pressure causing overheat and dying; too little oil does not lubricate, causing overheat and shutdown. Correct oil; see a small engine mechanic if that does not fix it (possible significant engine issue).
- fact: coil windings can separate and short; check continuity with ohm meter.
- fact: choke left on after warm-up keeps restricting airflow and can shut engine off.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "white, black or blue smoke"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, bad-valve-train, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- safety: stop running the mower and let it cool before working on it.
- fact: catching a plugged filter, too much oil or leaking engine gasket early avoids significant engine problems; low oil or internal problems may need a small engine mechanic to run tests.
- fact: too much oil: drain a little to manufacturer level; low oil: shop inspection, adding oil is often too late; internal (piston ring, valve train, gasket): small engine repair shop or Ferris dealer.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: unequal tire pressure pulls to one side; both sides need equal pressure to manufacturer spec. Bad dampers make mower jerk to one side moving forward; replace. Tracking: adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed, see manual.
- fact: weak hydro feel: bad hydraulic (drive) belt or pulley (replace frayed/cracked/worn belt; failed tensioner pulley: replace and grease tensioner arm); old or low hydraulic oil (change at Ferris-recommended intervals, check level).

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Ferris won't move or go forward"
- causes-named: brake-hung-up, worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: check drive bypass valves first (rear of mower; they disengage the hydros so you can push the mower); when bypassed the mower free-rolls but won't drive. Must be in operating position. (Source calls them "bypass valves" and "drive-release arms/levers".)
- fact: then belt, tensioner spring, tensioner pulley; broken/missing idler arm spring can't tension belt; hot fluid: let cool, dealer check if fine cold but weak hot; air must be bled after fluid change per operator's manual.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: loose hardware, lodged debris, failed clutch ball bearing, worn pulley bearings. Engine bolts reduce vibration; clutch bearing failure; blades must be balanced; string/long weeds wrapped on blade shaft; bad spindle housing bearing (replace bearing or housing); worn belt/pulleys; small debris.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: check tire pressure first (low tire = deck not level), then blades, deck belt, spindle, spindle/pulley bearings, deck shell. Table lists same causes as Bad Boy article.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- fact: puddle, or only a strong gas odor because leaked gas evaporated.
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, cracked-fuel-filter, bad-shutoff-valve, bad-gas-cap-seal, cracked-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank
- fact: carb leaks from failed gasket or stuck float; filter becomes soft and degrades from old fuel; pump leaks at seams; poly tank leaks at seams (replace tank); shut-off valves leak with time; rubber fuel lines dry/crack and can be punctured by a foreign object or by the clamps; gas cap seal failure lets gas splash out.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Ferris clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-motor
- fact: click or hum suggests bad solenoid; check power to solenoid (battery, cables, terminals, ground), then starter motor. Test battery; charge if under 12.7 V on a 12 V battery; replace if it won't hold charge. Ground: remove corrosion; battery ground and solenoid ground must contact well; 3-post solenoid is self-grounded.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: faulty PTO switch, weak battery, failed safety switch, bad clutch; blades won't turn if belt worn or off pulleys (stretched belt, bad pulley bearing, worn tensioner parts). Replace belts that are worn, cracked or glazed shiny; worn idler arm, spring or pulleys; manual clutch: replace cable, lever, springs, linkages; blown fuse: same amperage, if repeats get a mechanic.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: fuel/air restriction, intermittent spark, extra load (fast ground speed, plugged deck, dull blades); throttle must be at highest when blades engaged. Dirty plug: wire brush or replace if very dark, worn or damaged.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fact: Ferris requires air-cooled engine oil high in zinc; SAE30 or 10W-30 in most conditions; different viscosity may be needed in very warm conditions. Check cooling fins, engine shroud, engine guard (missing guard lets air escape; replace/secure). Avoid cutting wet grass; double or triple cut overgrown lawn.
- mistake: Source table has copy errors (low-oil row says "drain the fuel tank" and the fins row text is cut off); do not cite those cells.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: before each use check air filter and engine oil level and look for fuel and oil leaks; clean the mower after each use; scrape the deck and lubricate spindles and castor wheels several times a season; do annual full maintenance; more often for commercial use.

## page: guides/engine-oil-guide
- fact: Ferris: air-cooled engine oil with high zinc; SAE30 or 10W-30 in most conditions.
