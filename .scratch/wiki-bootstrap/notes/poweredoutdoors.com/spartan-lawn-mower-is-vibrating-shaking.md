## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: some vibration is normal (engine; uneven lawn bounce); extreme vibration is a fault.
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade (wrong/unbalanced), debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: remove spark plug wire and ignition key before repair; extreme caution with the deck.
- step 1: check engine bolts for wear. step 2: clutch ball bearing. step 3: material on blade shaft. step 4: spindle housings. step 5: blade balance. step 6: pulley bearings. step 7: worn or stretched belt. step 8: idler bracket and spring. step 9: debris lodged.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: motor mounts absorb much engine vibration; look at bolts under the engine; replace worn, broken, missing.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- symptom: ball bearing of an electric clutch can fail and shake the mower; replace the clutch.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: author always uses OEM blades. Wrong blades cause vibration and damage.
- tip: aftermarket blade: correct length and center hole size is necessary but not sufficient; lay on an OEM blade to verify exact match; a blade that's a little off may not sit securely on the spindle, hit the deck shell, vibrate, and cut poorly.
- symptom (unbalanced): heavier at one end; wobbles; significant vibration at high speed. Causes: normal use (dirt wears blades unevenly); sharpening removing more metal from one end.
- fix: balance with a blade balancer before installing.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: wire or string in grass can wrap the spindle and shake the mower; remove, then check spindle and housing for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fact: impact can bend spindle; bearings can wear.
- confirm: remove spark plug wire, work gloves, grab each end of the blade and rock up/down; blade should feel secure; movement and maybe a knocking sound means remove blade and spindle housing, inspect bearing/spindle, repair.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: stretched belt with slack doesn't sit securely around pulleys; slack makes the deck vibrate.
- fix: replace if stretched, cracked, glazed; if good, check routing and adjust tension to remove slack.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: failing pulley bearings let pulley not sit flat/parallel; belt runs along unstable pulleys causing vibration.
- confirm: check each pulley is securely attached with no wobble (one side higher than the other); rotate slowly by hand, feel restriction, listen for bearing noise; replace.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: loose or worn idler bracket and spring: hole in bracket enlarges, spring stretches; vibration.
- fix: replace worn bracket or spring; check the bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage and replace if worn.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: small debris (even a wood chip) in crevices causes extreme vibration; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat/parallel; debris buildup affects the tensioner so it can't keep tension, causing slack and shaking.
- tip: remove spindle covers and clean the deck regularly; clean after each mowing to avoid vibration and corrosion from moisture in debris.
