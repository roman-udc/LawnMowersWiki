## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "white / black / blue smoke from mower"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train, bad-head-gasket
- fact: black smoke: engine running very rich (more fuel than air); check/clean/replace air filter first, then other airflow restrictions.
- fact: blue or white smoke: burning excess oil; check oil level first, then piston rings, valve train, engine gasket.
- tip: checking oil level and air filter before each mowing could prevent costly repairs.
- pro: internal problems need a small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: plugged filter starves engine of air; runs rich -> black smoke; engine pulls air out of the crankcase (only air supply it finds), which pulls oil into the cylinder; smoke as the oil burns off.
- safety: never operate without a filter even though it will run; engine compromised, life shortened.
- tip: check at least a couple times per season; more in dry dusty conditions.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step 1: remove filter cover from housing, gently pull the filter out without knocking dirt into intake.
- step 2: wipe remaining dirt in housing with clean dry rag.
- step 3: tap filter on a solid surface. Never use an air compressor on a paper filter.
- step 4: hold to light; light visible: reuse; none: replace.
- fact: some Troy-Bilt mowers use a foam filter (separate instructions).

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: most Troy-Bilt engines have a dipstick; must read full level.
- symptom (low): lack of lubrication, friction, engine very hot, can melt parts, oil burns off giving smoke; often extensive damage by then (mechanic or replacement). Adding oil rarely fixes it by then.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: high oil raises crankcase pressure; oil gets into cylinder and up to the air intake through the valve train; burns off giving smoke.
- fix: drain a little: loosen the drain plug and quickly tighten; remove oil filter and take some oil; turkey baster at the oil fill area (don't reuse for cooking); oil evacuator.

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step 1: loosen drain plug then quickly tighten to allow a little to drain.
- step 2: or remove oil filter to let some oil out.
- step 3: or turkey baster at fill area; or oil evacuator.

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- confirm: easy check: remove spark plug (3/4" or 5/8" socket by mower); oil on the plug indicates internal failure (piston ring or valve train); identifying which requires engine teardown. Scoring in the cylinder wall may let oil into the combustion chamber.
- pro: mechanic.

## page: causes/bad-valve-train
- symptom: burnt valve (overheated); red-hot muffler may indicate burnt valve or timing problem.
- confirm: only by removing the cylinder head and a leak-down test (mechanic).
- fix: mechanic cuts valve and seat at certain angles so it seats correctly and makes good contact; valve seat installed correctly to complete the combustion chamber.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- symptom: bad engine gasket lets oil leak onto the hot muffler; burns off as smoke; location can make replacement harder.
