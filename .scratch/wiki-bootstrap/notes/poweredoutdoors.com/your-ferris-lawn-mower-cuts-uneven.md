## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- symptom: Low tire makes mower and deck sit unlevel. Check pressure to value in operator's manual; left and right equal. Check first or deck adjustments may need reversing.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Wear from dirt sucked under deck; sandy lawns wear faster than thick grass. Dull: beats up grass, tips brown couple of days later. Rounded ends leave strip uncut.
- fix: Sharpen dull; replace when sail thinner than middle or ends rounded. Small nicks sharpen out; large gouges replace.
- confirm (blade on): flat ground, key and plug boots off; measure tip to ground, rotate 180, measure other tip; over 1/8 inch variance = replace; repeat each blade. Blade off: lay on new blade; gaps = replace.
- mistake: Never straighten a blade. Check spindle bearing after bend/impact.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: Sail is the high side that creates air movement; with deck shell and baffles creates suction lifting grass.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail faces up toward deck. Upside down tears grass, can damage spindles. Balance blades before installing (unbalanced = uneven cut and vibration).

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Plugged deck reduces needed space/suction. Scrape regularly, more with wet grass. Silicone deck spray helps but not fully.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Key and plug boots off; gloves; grab blade ends, rock up/down; movement or knock = bad bearing or bent spindle; repair/replace spindle housing.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Turn each pulley slowly; resistance or noise = replace. Belt without cracks/wear, running securely on pulleys.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Full throttle with deck engaged; too slow gives insufficient blade tip speed.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Slow ground speed in thick/tall/wet grass; too fast pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Overlap each pass; author left strips trying to save passes and it took longer to fix.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Shell gets tweaked by hitting trees/rocks; may be repairable; else replace deck shell or complete deck. Tip: ask Ferris dealer for two quotes: deck shell plus labor to swap components vs. complete deck; consider age of mower.
