## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "push mower keeps shutting down, won't stay running"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, engine-overheats (clogged deck, wrong oil, plugged filter)
- safety: Disconnect spark plug wire and wait for all parts to stop moving before repairs.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Gas can degrade as soon as 30 days after purchase; ethanol attracts moisture, creating varnish and gummy deposits that clog and cause component failure.
- tip: Buy only what can be used within 30 days; min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; two-cycle push mowers: mix gas with high-quality 2-cycle oil; extra fuel: stabilize with Sea Foam Motor Treatment or STA-BIL while still fresh (it cannot reverse old fuel).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: When looking for restriction check fuel filter, fuel line, fuel pump; if flow is fine, move to carburetor. Restrictions come from old gas or dirt/debris.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: Carburetors eventually wear out; old gas varnish makes internals stick or clogs the fuel jet.
- confirm: 1) confirm fuel flow to carb (else check filter and lines); 2) remove air filter without letting dirt into intake; 3) spray carburetor cleaner into the intake and let mower run (don't use starter fluid); runs strong then sluggish/shuts off = dirty carb; 4) disassemble and clean.
- fix: If still bad after cleaning, rebuild or replace.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Replace annually; clean several times a season; more often in dust. Plugged filter can cause overheating.
- fix (paper): tap on solid surface; hold to light; reuse if light shows; replace if no light, extremely dirty or oil-covered; reattach cover.
- fix (foam primary filter, not a pre-filter on a paper one): wash with water and mild dish soap; rinse until soap is gone; lay flat to dry (sun speeds it); lightly coat with filter oil; replace if brittle, brown-spotted, or torn.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: Choke restricts air for a cold start; once warm it must be open/run. Automatic chokes: no lever doesn't mean no choke problem. Choke and linkages can stick (manual or automatic): lubricate choke shaft and linkage with carburetor cleaner.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: Very dark, broken porcelain, or burnt electrode: replace. Lightly dirty: clean with wire brush. Gap per owner's manual; plug and lead secure. Then check ignition coil if still bad.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: Windings separate and short when mower gets hot; plug can't get voltage. Check continuity with ohmmeter.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Vent equalizes pressure; otherwise vacuum stops fuel flow.
- confirm: After it stops, remove cap and start; if it runs, put cap back on while running to see if it shuts off again. Replace cap.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- fact: Overheated push mower engine shuts down: low oil, wrong oil, clogged deck, plugged air filter.
