## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Toro clicks but won't start", "clicking or humming sound"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor, faulty-charging-system
- safety: wear protective gear; consult a professional if uncomfortable with electrical; ALWAYS disconnect the negative (black) battery cable before electrical repairs.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- confirm: cables from battery to solenoid and solenoid to starter are secure; look for corrosion on terminals/components.
- fix: replace cables/wiring that keep coming loose or are broken; disconnect battery; clean corroded parts with wire brush and baking soda mix (2 cups water + 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); repair/replace corroded wiring; add dielectric grease; replace damaged terminals.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: not fully charged = may not turn the engine over. Charge when low; replace if it won't hold a charge; if it charges but keeps dying, check charging system.
- confirm: multimeter, red/black probes on same-color terminals; 12 V: reads 11.5 to 12.7; near 11.5 V almost dead, 12.7 V fully charged.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Safety gear (acid, shock).
- step 2: Access battery (screwdriver may be needed).
- step 3: Leave battery in casing with terminal cables attached (as stated).
- step 4: Charger red (positive) cable first; red clamp on positive, black clamp on negative terminal.
- step 5: Touch only rubber-coated parts.
- step 6: 12 V; start at 2 amps, up to no more than 10.
- step 7: If the charger has a gauge, stay connected until fully charged.

## page: procedures/test-the-battery
- fact: 11.5-12.7 V on 12 V battery.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- confirm: black ground cable battery-to-frame: good contact, no corrosion; check solenoid ground; a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded.
- fix: replace damaged ground cable; remove corrosion at battery and solenoid grounds.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: keeps clicking.
- fact: Electromagnet switch engaging the starter motor; usually mounted on the starter but needn't be; follow the positive wire from the battery. Failure: weak internal spring, corroded copper plate; bad ground, weak starter or bad battery can also result.
- confirm: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches; if it starts when bypassing the solenoid, replace it.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: after ruling out the rest, remove and test the starter.
- pro: pricey; have the dealer confirm, or a starter/alternator repair shop test/rebuild.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: On a zero turn, lawn tractor or riding mower, a bad charging system won't keep the battery charged. Check with a volt-ohm meter.
- pro: mechanic familiar with the charging system; possible bad stator/alternator, regulator; electrical parts not returnable.

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- fact: tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches; bypass test.
