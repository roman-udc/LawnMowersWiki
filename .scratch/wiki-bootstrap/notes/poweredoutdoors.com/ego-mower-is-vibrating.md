## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "EGO electric mower shaking / vibrating"
- causes-named: bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, dull-or-bent-blade, bad-motor
- fact: EGO vibrates when blade is installed incorrectly, bent, or unbalanced; if blade hit a foreign object the motor shaft may be damaged.
- safety: At first sign of shaking: shut off, remove safety key, remove battery before inspecting blade and motor shaft. Wear heavy work gloves. Follow operator's manual.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- symptom: Blade loose or installed incorrectly.
- fix: Install parts in order: (1) flange first. Push/self-propelled: inner flange mounted to the fan, fan and inner flange mount to motor shaft, fan blades pointing toward the deck. Zero-turn: flange mounts to motor shaft. (2) Blade on flange with side printed "THIS SIDE FACING GRASS" facing outward; if unprinted, blade sails (high sides) point toward the deck. (3) Washer, blade bolt into shaft, finger tight. (4) Align blade holes with flange holes; hold with a screwdriver in a blade hole and another screwdriver in the fixing hole to stop spinning. (5) Torque with torque wrench per owner's manual.
- brand/part: EGO push or self-propelled blade bolt torque 36-43 ft-lb.
- brand/part: EGO zero-turn blade bolt torque 36-41 ft-lb.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Bent blade (after hitting rock or stump) gives bad/uneven cut and vibration; sometimes not obvious.
- confirm: Park on flat level surface, remove battery pack(s), wait for moving parts to stop. Remove blade and lay on a new EGO blade; even a very slight bend causes bad cut.
- fix: Replace. NEVER straighten a bent blade: weakens metal, piece can break off and fly at high speed.
- tip: Walk the lawn first for objects.
- fact: Unbalanced blade: equal weight each end needed. Becomes unbalanced when dirt wears it unevenly or more metal removed from one side when sharpening. Failing to balance can vibrate and damage motor shaft.
- confirm: Blade balancer; or nail in wall with head 3/4" to 1" from wall, blade center on nail; or screwdriver secured horizontally in a vise. Blade should sit level; if not shave metal off the side hanging lower.

## page: causes/bad-motor
- symptom: Bent motor shaft makes blade wobble and mower shake.
- confirm: Grab both blade ends and check for extra movement.
- pro: If blade installed correctly and shaft suspected, have an EGO service center diagnose and repair.

## page: procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades
- fact: Balance blade before reinstalling; method above.
