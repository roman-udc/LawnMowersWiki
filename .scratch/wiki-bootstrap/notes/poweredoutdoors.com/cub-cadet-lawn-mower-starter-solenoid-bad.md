## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Click or hum when turning the ignition key and engine won't turn over (problems/engine-wont-crank). Also a wire getting hot, smoking or melting.
- fact: Solenoid is an electromagnet on/off switch that engages the starter motor. Usually mounted on the starter motor but need not be; some are mounted closer to the battery. Every Cub Cadet electric-start engine has one. Round or square, 3 or 4 posts. Find it by following the positive (+) battery cable.
- fact: Inside is a spring and copper plate; solenoid goes bad if spring weakens or copper plate corrodes. Can also fail as a result of weak starter, bad battery or bad ground.
- confirm: Solenoid likely bad when bypassed and starter then turns the engine over.
- tip: Before replacing, check for loose or corroded wires or bad ground, which can keep the solenoid from getting power.
- fact: Water in the electrical system can also prevent starting (links to related article on mower left in rain).

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches (check loose wires), needle nose pliers (if screwdriver doesn't work), battery charger (optional)
- step 1: Verify battery has at least 12.3 volts (voltmeter).
- step 2: Set parking brake, mower in neutral, key to ON.
- step 3: Bypass: lay a long screwdriver across the solenoid touching the two cables (cable from battery and cable to starter). A spark is common. If the engine turns over, solenoid is probably bad. Needle nose pliers can jump it if the screwdriver doesn't work.
- step 4: Bench test for solenoid mounted on starter: remove starter from engine; attach battery charger negative (-) clamp to the starter case and touch the positive (+) clamp to the big post and the exciter wire on the solenoid; watch whether the starter works with the solenoid.
- safety: Bypass can spark; always wear safety gear; follow Cub Cadet operator's manual.
