## page: procedures/jump-start-the-battery
- fact: yes, a mower battery can be jump-started from a car battery: car batteries are typically 12 V and most mower batteries are 12 V.
- safety: do not jump if the two batteries are not rated the same voltage (battery explosion, injury). Double-check, not every car/mower is 12 V.
- safety: the car must be turned off. The amperage the car's alternator generates is too much for the mower and could explode the battery. (Source also says you may need to turn the car key enough to initiate electrical power but not turn the engine over.)
- safety: never open a battery casing (hazardous material inside).
- step 1: inspect jumper cables: no frays, clamps firmly attached, no breaks in the cable.
- step 2: confirm both batteries are rated at the same voltage.
- step 3: inspect both batteries for leaking acid or severe corrosion on terminals; if seen, replace the battery rather than jump it. Check cables are tight, not frayed or damaged, and the battery is mounted securely. Wipe dust away carefully near the terminals (live voltage).
- step 4: position car and mower so cables reach with little tension; neither vehicle may touch the other (can complete a circuit chassis to chassis).
- step 5: identify positive and negative terminals on both batteries. Red (positive) cable to the positive terminal of the dead battery, other red end to the car's positive terminal. Clamps secure and not touching other exposed metal.
- step 6: black (negative) cable to the negative terminal of the live (car) battery first; other end to an exposed, unpainted metal object on the dead vehicle (a bolt or chassis part), away from the battery if possible, not touching exposed metal on the car. This reduces explosion chance.
- step 7: turn the key on the mower (do not start the car; do not crank the car engine). The mower's charging system will charge the battery; you only supply the juice to start.
- step 8: once the mower runs, disconnect in reverse order: negative from the mower, negative from the car battery, then positive from the car first, then positive from the mower. Care: still live voltage.
- step 9: let the mower run for a while so its alternator charges its battery.
- confirm: if the mower will not run, recheck the setup and connections; there may be another problem or the battery may be too dead and need replacement.
- safety: electrical - a 10-milliamp shock can potentially kill; most mowers have 12-amp engines and cold-cranking amps can be much higher. Remove the key from the ignition so the mower is definitely off while hooking up; communicate with any helper. Work one-handed when attaching/removing battery cables, other hand not on chassis or exposed metal; if using both hands use rubber-insulated gloves. Remove metal jewelry, rings, low-hanging necklaces.
- safety: mechanical - limit people around, keep small children away, helpers stand clear when jump-starting; disengage the blade if the mower can (most can) so it cannot start moving; wear tight clothing or short sleeves; wear safety glasses in case the battery explodes.
- tip: alternatives to a car jump: new battery, portable jump starter (a battery with cables already attached; charge it per manufacturer before use; follow its instructions; recharge after), trickle charger (charges slowly like a phone; adjustable voltage and amperage to match 12 V lawn tractor battery or 24 V truck battery; not a quick fix), combination jump starter + trickle charger (some include an air compressor; under $100 in stores/online). Newer devices often have built-in indicators warning if cables are hooked up wrong.
- tip: a leaking or severely corroded battery is a safety hazard; replace it instead of jumping it.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: mower battery life is not infinite; they die at inopportune times.
- fix: jump-start (procedures/jump-start-the-battery), trickle-charge, or replace if leaking/corroded or too dead.

## page: causes/bad-battery
- symptom: leaking battery acid or severe corrosion on terminals means replace, do not jump.

- fact: the mower's own alternator/charging system recharges the battery once running; let it run after a jump.
- fact: trickle charger / portable jump starter / combo unit options as listed above.
