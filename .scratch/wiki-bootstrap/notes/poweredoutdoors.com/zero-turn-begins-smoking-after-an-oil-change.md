## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "zero turn starts smoking after an oil change"
- causes-named: spilled-oil-on-engine, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: other reasons: engine gasket failure leaking oil onto hot component; piston ring or valve train issue (already developing).
- safety: operator's manual precautions; careful around hot engine.
- pro: experienced mechanic or dealer if unsure.

## page: causes/spilled-oil-on-engine
- fact: oil dripped on engine or muffler during change doesn't hurt mower; burns off when hot and smokes.
- tip: wipe oil drips after refilling.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: pressure pushes oil into cylinder through valve train; burning gives bluish-white smoke; thick smoke can plug air filter -> sluggish; replace filter plugged with smoke and oil; check plugs, replace if oil-covered.
- confirm: remove dipstick, wipe with clean rag, reinsert, remove and read; good = between the full lines.
- fix: remove a little oil: drain plug (quickly remove and replace); remove air filter and let a little oil drain out of it (as stated); oil evacuator from fill; turkey baster from fill (don't reuse for cooking). Recheck dipstick until within full lines.

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step: four methods above; recheck level between full lines.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact low: friction, extreme heat, oil burns off, components melt; damage likely; add oil to try, but mechanic diagnosis recommended.
- fact wrong: wrong viscosity burns and smokes; use engine-manufacturer's recommended viscosity; air-cooled engine oil with high zinc (cooling agent); car oil has little zinc since cars liquid-cooled. Most makers recommend SAE30 or 10W-30; 20W-50 for higher temps; Kawasaki Engine Oil Viscosity & Temperature chart referenced.

## page: guides/engine-oil-guide
- fact: air-cooled oil high in zinc; SAE30 or 10W-30 typical, 20W-50 in higher ambient temps; follow engine manufacturer.

## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- tip: wipe any oil drips after refilling; check level on dipstick after.
