## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "push mower sputters"
- alias: "push mower runs rough"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: look at items limiting air, fuel, spark, then extra strain on the engine
- safety: wait for engine to cool and parts to stop; remove spark plug boot(s)

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- tip: replace once a year, clean several times a season; more often in dusty conditions or heavy use
- safety: NEVER run a push mower without an air filter; dirt can cause permanent engine damage
- fix: clean if a little dirty; replace if extremely dirty, damaged or not sealing

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: PAPER filter: step 1 remove from housing; step 2 wipe dirt from housing without dropping any in the intake; step 3 tap on a solid surface; step 4 hold to light - light through the element and undamaged = reuse, else replace; step 5 reinstall and attach cover; step 6 if foam pre-filter, wash with mild detergent and water, rinse, air dry, DO NOT APPLY OIL
- fact: FOAM filter: step 1 remove from housing; step 2 wipe housing without dropping dirt in the intake; step 3 wash with mild detergent and water; step 4 rinse until soap is gone, squeeze out water, lay flat to dry; step 5 when dry, lubricate with filter oil or engine oil, lightly saturated not dripping, squeeze out excess; step 6 replace if excessively dirty with dark spots or tears; step 7 reinstall

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke closed for cold start, open for warm; wrong or stuck choke makes engine run rough and bog
- fix: verify lever position; check the choke plate moves; carburetor cleaner for stuck choke; replace worn choke cable
- brand: some push mowers have an automatic choke - contact the dealer if problems

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas good only about 30 days; ethanol attracts water, leaves varnish, corrosive
- fix: replace old fuel; add fresh with cleaner/stabilizer like Sea Foam Motor Treatment
- fact: 2-cycle engines use gas and oil mix: unleaded 87 octane minimum, 10% ethanol maximum, with air-cooled 2-cycle oil (article links mix rates)
- fact: 4-cycle engines use straight gas, 87 octane minimum, 10% ethanol maximum; separate fill ports for gas and oil; do not mix

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: 2-cycle push mowers need gas/oil mix; 4-cycle straight gas (87 octane min, max 10% ethanol)

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: use shut-off valve or hose crimp pliers to stop/start flow; remove line from carburetor, collect in a container lower than the tank; restart flow and watch
- fix: no good flow - shut off, remove line, carburetor cleaner, compressed air, repeat; replace with same length and diameter if cracked or can't clear

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: inline filter; replace annually or sooner if plugged/damaged; arrow in direction of fuel flow

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: ensure fuel reaches carburetor (check fuel line and filter); remove air filter, spray carburetor cleaner into the intake, start; runs well then sluggish after the cleaner burns off = clean the carburetor

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: pressure gauge for tank vacuum, or loosen cap while rough: immediate improvement suggests cap; retighten, wait for sluggishness, loosen again
- fix: new cap

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: replace if very dark, burnt electrode, damaged; otherwise clean buildup with wire brush; gap to manufacturer spec; attach wire securely

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fix: slow down; tall grass - multiple cuts highest height first

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fix: throttle lever to the highest speed when engaging the blade; speed setting right for conditions
- brand: some push mowers have automatic throttle - no adjustment; contact dealer if problems

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fix: scrape deck with scraper or wire brush; avoid wet grass; deck spray helps but not miracle

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: tear grass, brown tips a couple of days later; magnify plugged deck
- fix: remove blade, sharpen and balance
