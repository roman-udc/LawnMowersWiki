## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Husqvarna mower vibrating / shaking"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Vibrates when engine mounting bolts aren't secure, clutch going bad, debris caught in the mower, blades incorrectly installed; worn/damaged deck components also contribute.
- safety: Before working on the deck remove the ignition key and unplug the spark plug wire(s).
- tip: Clean the mower after each mowing.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: Engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) absorb vibration. Inspect, tighten loose bolts, replace worn or broken ones. On a push mower check engine bolts if excessive vibration appears once the blade engages.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: Husqvarna uses an electric or manual clutch. Electric clutch: ball bearing may fail and shake. Manual (belt and pulley) clutch: bushing and linkages can fail. Push mowers have no clutch; they use a lever to engage the blade; check it for loose or worn parts.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Wrong, incorrectly installed or unbalanced blades give excessive movement. Wrong size: need correct center hole and length; even with right measurements aftermarket blades may not fit; physically compare with an OEM blade (author bought aftermarket blades by correct measurements from two suppliers and one set didn't fit).
- fact: Unbalanced blade (one end heavier from uneven wear or from more metal removed on one side when sharpening) spins unevenly, can damage spindle bearings, and vibrates significantly at high speed. Use a blade balancer before installing.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- fact: Use the right hardware in the right order (check owner's manual or parts diagram); missing parts or wrong order leaves the blade not securely attached, causing vibration.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: Wire, string or long weeds can wrap the blade shaft (spindle). Inspect, remove, and check spindles/housings for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Wearing good work gloves, grab each end of the blade and rock it up and down to feel play.
- fix: Sometimes only the bearing in the housing can be replaced, sometimes the whole spindle housing, varies by Husqvarna model.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: Worn belt with slack vibrates; replace if glazed, shredded or stretched.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Turn each pulley slowly by hand; it should turn smoothly; replace the pulley if restriction or bearing noise. A bad bearing leaves the pulley not parallel to the deck so the belt doesn't sit securely; it may vibrate off the pulleys.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: A loose idler bracket and spring wear over time and shake; replace. Also check bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage and replace if needed.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Debris collects under pulleys if belt covers aren't removed and the deck top isn't cleaned often; pulleys no longer sit flat and parallel to the deck and vibrate. Even small items cause shaking; check every part.
