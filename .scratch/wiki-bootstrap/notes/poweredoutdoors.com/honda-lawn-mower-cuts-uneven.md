## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Honda lawn mower has a bad or uneven cut"
- alias: "strips of uncut grass or scalping"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure (damaged/mismatched wheel), unlevel-deck (cutting height set differently), dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap
- tip: before measuring a blade, confirm wheels are equal size and undamaged and cutting-height settings are all the same.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: damaged wheel wobbles or doesn't move, causing uneven cut; replace with a wheel of same size as opposite side; unequal wheels make deck sit unlevel. (wheel damage, not pressure, in this Source.)

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: cutting height adjusters must all be set to the same height or the deck won't sit level.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: bent blade not parallel to deck/ground makes cut look terrible (hit stump, rock, hard object). Dull blade beats up grass, tears blades that turn brown a day or two after mowing.
- confirm: compare removed blade to a new one, or measure on mower: park on flat even surface, measure ground to blade tip, rotate 180 degrees and measure same spot; difference over 1/8" means replace blade.
- fact: sharpen blades after every 25 hours of use, more often in sandy conditions.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: worn sail (high sides) gives less air movement under the deck; sail thinner than blade center means replace blade.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: packed deck restricts air movement/suction. Scrape deck periodically; avoid mowing wet grass (clumps, sticks to deck).

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: throttle should be set to high; deck needs engine power, otherwise blade speed slows. If engine lost power, take to an experienced small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: slow down in thick, wet or tall grass; too fast pushes grass over; worse on bouncy uneven ground.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: overlap paths or strips of grass remain.
