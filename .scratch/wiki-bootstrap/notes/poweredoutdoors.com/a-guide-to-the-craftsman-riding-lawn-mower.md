## page: procedures/change-the-engine-oil
- brand: Craftsman riding mower. Change engine oil and filter after 5 hours on a new mower, then every 50 hours.
- tools: drain pan or approved container holding about 2 1/2 quarts; cloth for filter oil; drain hose (supplied with mower) or drain sleeve
- step 1: Locate the drain. On a Craftsman the oil drain valve is on the side near the base of the engine. Open the hood by pulling up near the steering wheel; look for the oil valve or drain port.
- step 2: Warm the engine first (warm oil flows more freely), then turn it off; avoid burns from the hot engine.
- step 3: Remove the oil fill cap and dipstick to vent the engine so oil drains faster.
- step 4: Determine whether the mower has a drain valve or drain plug.
- step 5 (drain hose): uncap the drain valve, attach the supplied hose with the other end in the drain pan, push in the drain valve and turn counter-clockwise to open; when done remove hose and reattach valve cap.
- step 5 (drain plug): place the drain sleeve around the drain plug with the free end in the drain pan, remove the plug, let oil drain, replace plug.
- step 6: Replace the oil filter if the engine has one: have a cloth ready for spilled oil, turn filter counterclockwise to remove, let oil drain into pan, wipe a little fresh oil on the new filter's gasket before spinning it on.
- step 7: Refill with SAE30 or 10W-30 air-cooled engine oil to the fill level on the site gauge or dipstick.
- mistake: Overfilling with oil can cause engine issues.

## page: guides/engine-oil-guide
- fact: Craftsman riding mower: SAE30 or 10W-30 air-cooled engine oil; 10W-30 can replace SAE30 and works well in colder temperatures. Do not overfill. Capacity roughly 2 1/2 quarts (size of drain container named).

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Craftsman riding-mower service guideline (author says refer to owner's manual for the specific model).
- fact: Before each use: keep mower clear of debris; check/clean cooling fins, around the engine, under the hood, mower deck, and around the transmission.
- fact: Every 10 hours: lubricate pedal pivot points; clean battery terminals; clean hood/dash air vents.
- fact: Every 25 hours: lubricate axles and rims; clean air filters; check blades.
- fact: Every 50 hours: change engine oil and filter; change fuel filter; change air filter; check belts and pulleys.
- fact: Every 100 hours: check hardware; check spark plug.
- fact: New mower: first oil and filter change at 5 hours.

- fact: Engage blades with throttle at fast (rabbit) and PTO to "PTO ON"; always mow with throttle fast. Cut height: move deck lift lever to the notch for the desired height.
