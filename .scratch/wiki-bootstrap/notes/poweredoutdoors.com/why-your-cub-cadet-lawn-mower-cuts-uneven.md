## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Cub Cadet cuts uneven"
- alias: "cut looks terrible"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: cut-quality types: Scalping (close cut; low tire, uneven lawn, unlevel deck, deck height too low); Step cut (ridges; low tire, unlevel deck, bent blade, spindle damage, wrong blade install); Streaking (strips; worn/dull blades, clogged deck, incorrect paths, too fast, low engine speed); Stringers (patches; worn/dull blades, clogged deck, too fast, low engine speed).
- tip: an uneven/bumpy lawn can scalp; rolling the lawn smooths it. Remove toys and rocks before mowing; take care around obstacles.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: very worn blades leave strips; dull blades bruise grass and it browns a couple of days later. Sharpen if in good enough condition; replace if not.
- confirm: bent blade (removed): park flat and level; remove ignition key and plug wires; wait for parts to stop; access underside; set blade on a new blade; gaps = replace.
- confirm: bent blade (in place): measure ground to tip one side, rotate 180 degrees, measure other; more than 1/8 inch difference = replace.
- safety: never straighten a blade; weakened metal can crack and break off at high speed, endangering people, pets and structures.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: high side of blade (sail/fin) must point up; upside down hits the ground, tears grass, can damage spindle. Blades must be balanced before install.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: deck design and blade airflow create suction under deck that lifts grass; plugged deck can't; wet grass sticks and leaves clumps.
- tip: scrape periodically, more often in wet grass; some owners use silicone spray made for mower decks (helps but won't prevent all sticking).

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: a low tire can leave the deck unlevel; commonly overlooked.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- confirm: tires at spec first; on a level surface measure distance from ground to the base of the deck at all four corners; if not same, follow owner's manual leveling instructions.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: remove spark plug boot and key; heavy gloves; grab each end of blade and rock up/down; movement or knock = bad spindle housing bearing or bent spindle; repair or replace spindle housing.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: worn/stretched belt fails to move around pulleys fast, slowing blades.
- confirm: cracking, shredding, shiny glazed look, sits deep in pulley grooves; replace with new Cub Cadet belt.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: good pulley sits secure, flat, parallel to deck; bad bearing lets it move and shift side to side with deck engaged.
- confirm: rotate by hand for resistance or bearing noise; grab both sides and rock up/down; excessive play = replace bearing or pulley assembly (varies by model).

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fix: full throttle whenever deck is engaged.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: slow ground speed in thick, tall, wet grass; make multiple cuts: first at higher deck setting then lower; too fast can't create suction and pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: overlap each pass to avoid strips.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: shell formed to create suction; hitting trees, rocks, etc. can tweak it and compromise the cut.
