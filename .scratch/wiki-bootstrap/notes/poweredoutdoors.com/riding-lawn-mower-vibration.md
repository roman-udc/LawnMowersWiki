## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "riding mower vibrating so badly I feel like I'll shake out of the seat"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: park on flat level surface, set brake, wood chocks; remove ignition key and disconnect spark plug boots; thick work gloves and safety glasses; if lifting mower to reach deck, lift securely (jacks if possible) or remove deck; check owner's manual.
- tip: it can be as small as a wood chip lodged in the mower.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: engine bolts (motor mounts) sit under engine and absorb vibration; broken, worn or missing bolts must be replaced.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: manual engagement clutch (belt and pulleys): bushings and linkages can fail and vibrate. Electric clutch: failing ball bearing vibrates.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: wrong, wrongly installed or unbalanced blades wobble each rotation.
- fact: author prefers OEM blades; for aftermarket, match blade length and center hole size, compare to an OEM blade; some aftermarket blades with stated length/hole size didn't fit; try another aftermarket maker.
- fact: use correct hardware installed in right order (see owner's manual diagram/parts diagram).
- fact: unbalanced blade (one side heavier) vibrates and damages spindle bearing; imbalance from uneven wear from dirt or from removing more metal from one side when sharpening. Balance with a blade balancer after sharpening, before install.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: thin wire, string, long weed can wrap around the blade shaft; check shaft, then check spindle housings for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: grab both ends of blade (wear gloves), rock up/down, listen for knock, feel play.
- fix: remove housing, inspect, replace bearing; on some decks bearing not replaceable so replace spindle assembly.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: slack in stretched belt vibrates; replace when cracked, shredded or shiny glazed.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: pulley with bad bearing may not sit level/parallel to deck; extra movement felt when belt rotates.
- confirm: rotate each pulley slowly by hand; replace if restriction, bearing noise, or not secure.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: loose idler bracket and spring wear and make mower shake; replace; also check bearing and bushing in engagement linkage.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: bark or stone caught under a pulley or in mower can cause shake. Clean mower after every mowing (author uses leaf blower).
