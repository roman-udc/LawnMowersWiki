## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "my Cub Cadet is shaking badly"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Source summary: missing or loose engine bolts, failing clutch, unbalanced blades, worn deck parts, debris lodged in the mower.
- safety: Before checking: turn off the mower, remove the key, remove the spark plug boot. Cub Cadet may use two spark plugs.
- safety: Wear good work gloves when rocking the blade.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) reduce engine vibration felt through the mower.
- fix: Tighten; replace any missing, broken or worn bolts.
- brand: On a push mower, check engine bolts if vibration starts once the blade is engaged.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (wrong size, unbalanced or incorrectly installed blade)
- fact: Not every blade fits every mower: a blade for a 52" deck might fit some 52" decks but not all. Need correct center hole and blade length.
- tip: Even with correct center hole and length an aftermarket blade may not work; author found this over years matching aftermarket to OEM blades.
- fact: Unbalanced blade shakes the deck enough to feel in the operator's seat; from more wear on one side (sand and debris), or from removing more metal from one end when sharpening.
- fact: Blade needs correct washers and bolts to mount to the spindle housing, installed in the correct order (check manual or parts diagram); missing or misordered pieces cause wobble and vibration.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- confirm: Look at the top of the blade for wire or string wrapped on the blade shaft (spindle).
- fix: Remove it and check for further damage to the shaft.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating (blade wobbles when engaged)
- confirm: Gloved, grab each end of the blade and rock it up and down; any movement or a knocking sound means a bad bearing.
- fix: Replace the bearing; may be possible to replace only the bearing or the whole spindle housing assembly depending on deck model.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fix: Check the deck belt for wear or shredding; shiny glazed appearance means replace. A stretched belt or lots of slack causes vibration. Source links a Cub Cadet deck belt replacement article.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- confirm: Slowly turn each pulley; feel for resistance and listen for bearing noise.
- fix: Replace the pulley if bad.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fix: Check idler bracket and tensioner spring for wear, plus the bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage.
- fact: Many things that make the belt come off also cause vibration.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Wood chips, stones and acorns can lodge; items under pulleys make the belt vibrate as it runs around the pulley.
- tip: Clean the Cub Cadet after each mowing.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- symptom: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- fact: Cub Cadet mowers use an electric or manual clutch; an electric clutch can have ball bearing failure; a manual engagement clutch (belt and pulleys) can have bushing and linkage failure. A push mower has no clutch, but has a blade engagement lever to check.
