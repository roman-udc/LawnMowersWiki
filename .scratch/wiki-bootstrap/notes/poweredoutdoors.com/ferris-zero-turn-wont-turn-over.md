## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Ferris zero turn won't turn over or crank"
- symptom: nothing happens when key turned; not enough power reaching starter motor
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: avoid electrocution; disconnect the black negative battery cable before electrical repairs

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: keep fully charged, especially in extended storage
- fix: test (author links his battery article); charge; replace if it won't hold a charge; buy at Ferris dealer, hardware or auto store; bring old battery, core fee typically $15-$25 otherwise

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- safety: wear eye and skin protection
- step 1: access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed to uncover); do not remove battery from its casing
- step 2: connect positive (red, plus sign) charger cable to positive terminal first
- step 3: connect negative (black) cable to negative terminal
- step 4: touch nothing without rubber coating
- step 5: set charger to 12 volts (typical for mower batteries) and amperage; start with 2 amps, work up to no more than 10 amps; more amps charges faster but slow charge is best

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: vibration loosens cables/wiring/connections; moisture corrodes terminals breaking continuity
- fix: secure connections; clean corrosion with small wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); disconnect battery and remove components before cleaning; replace terminals/components if corrosion severe

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probes on each prong of the fuse measuring resistance; near 0 good, infinity bad
- fix: replace with same amperage
- pro: repeated blown fuses: dealer or repair shop to find root cause

## page: procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse
- step 1: probe each fuse prong with multimeter on resistance
- step 2: near 0 = good; infinity = bad; replace with same amperage

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: key turned and nothing happens, no crank
- confirm: multimeter on resistance; find prongs marked B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid); key to start position; probe B and S; near 0 ohms good, infinite means replace

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Ferris has operator presence control system; defective switch stops cranking
- confirm: multimeter test; may temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only
- safety: never operate a mower without the safety switch; never run with a switch bypassed

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch that triggers starter motor
- symptom: fails when spring weakens or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause failure
- confirm: battery must be fully charged before testing; author links steps to diagnose a bad solenoid

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid, remove starter and test it
- pro: have a shop specializing in starter/alternator repair test and rebuild if possible before buying a pricey new starter
