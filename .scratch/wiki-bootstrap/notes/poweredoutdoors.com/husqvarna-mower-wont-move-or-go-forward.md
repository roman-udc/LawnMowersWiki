## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Husqvarna won't move forward or reverse"
- alias: "mower just quit moving"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, missing-axle-key, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: Source summary: worn drive belt; hydraulic fluid low, old or hot; air trapped in the hydraulic system; missing axle key (lawn tractor); broken tensioner pulley and spring (zero turn).
- fact: Non-transmission items can also stop movement when engine sputters/shuts off: battery and charging system, plugged fuel filter and lines, plugged air filter, dirty carburetor.
- safety: Shut off the mower and remove the spark plug wire before working; caution around a hot transmission.
- pro: If hydraulic fluid is hot and leaking, take it to the Husqvarna dealership; if it runs fine cold but stops when hot, have an experienced mechanic check the transmission.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- fact: Husqvarna hydrostatic mowers pump transmission fluid to turn the wheels. A bypass lever lets you push the mower by hand; it must be disengaged afterwards or the mower will not move.
- brand: Zero turn: two levers at the rear, one per transmission (left and right). Lawn tractor: one bypass lever at the rear, near the drawbar or under the seat.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- brand: Zero turn pump drive belt: check it sits on the pulleys. A worn belt slips and the mower moves slowly; a belt that fell off means no movement. Replace worn; reinstall fallen or broken belt.
- brand: Lawn tractor motion drive belt: not worn, correctly routed, seated around pulleys and inside belt guards. If a belt fell off, check pulleys are good and belt guards not bent or out of place.

## page: causes/missing-axle-key
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- fact: After a recent tire change on a riding lawn tractor a small narrow-bar key may have fallen out of the axle; it must be installed for the wheel to move.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- brand: Zero turn tensioner pulleys are often hard plastic with a bearing in the center; pulley can break or bearing can fail. Replace; ensure belt is around pulleys and inside belt keepers.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- fact: Idler spring tensions the drive belt. Replace if broken, missing or stretched.
- tip: Keep the tensioner arm greased so it moves and does not seize.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- fact: Some Husqvarna transmissions are sealed and unserviceable (oil cannot be changed); check the operator's manual. Serviceable ones need oil changes at Husqvarna's interval; the first change comes sooner than later ones.
- fact: Old or low oil can make the mower not move or seem very weak.
- fix: Add hydro oil until the full level when cool; check for leaks.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move
- fact: Bad or low fluid cannot lubricate efficiently, raising friction and fluid temperature; may indicate more extensive damage.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- symptom: problems/mower-wont-move (moves slowly)
- fact: After changing hydraulic fluid on serviceable transmissions, all air must be bled out.

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- fix: Zero turn step 1: flat surface; chock the front wheels so the mower cannot roll forward.
- fix: Zero turn step 2: raise rear drive tires off the ground on jack stands; let them spin forward and backward until no excessive noise and wheels move at normal speeds.
- fix: Zero turn step 3: open the bypass valve using the drive release levers at the rear.
- fix: Zero turn step 4: start, fast idle throttle, release the parking brake.
- fix: Zero turn step 5: move drive levers forward then reverse 6 times.
- fix: Zero turn step 6: shut off, set the parking brake, close the bypass valve, repeat the process.
- fix: Zero turn step 7: start, release brake, cycle levers forward and reverse until no excessive noise and normal wheel speed both directions.
- fix: Lawn tractor step 1: flat open area; engine off, parking brake set; disengage the transmission with the rear lever so the tractor freerolls.
- fix: Lawn tractor step 2: start; throttle slow; release the brake; depress drive pedal forward about 5 seconds, then reverse 5 seconds; repeat several times.
- fix: Lawn tractor step 3: shut off, set brake, engage the transmission with the rear lever.
- fix: Lawn tractor step 4: with the transmission engaged repeat; then with 10 feet of clear area in front and behind, start, throttle about halfway, drive forward 10 feet and backward 10 feet, repeat several times.
- tip: Check the operator's manual for model-specific bleeding steps.
