## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "pull up the PTO switch and nothing happens, Spartan blades won't engage"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- safety: Before reaching under the deck: remove ignition key and spark plug wires, wait for parts to stop.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Cracks, fraying, shiny glazed look; sits deep in the pulley grooves. Slipping reduces blade speed; fast blade speed is needed for suction/even cut.
- symptom: Belt came off pulleys: stretched belt, bad pulley, worn tensioner arm, missing spring.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: Tensioner arm and spring hold idler pulleys inline (pulley one side of bracket, spring other); bracket hole or spring wears; belt loosens and vibrates off.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Bearing wear lets pulley wobble, one side higher; belt rolls off; replace pulley.

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: Usually a yellow knob on the Spartan that allows battery voltage to the clutch. Check continuity; replace if a break.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: PTO clutch transfers engine power to blades by engaging drive belt; replace if worn/faulty.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Fully charged 12 V battery about 12.7 V; charge below.
- fix: Charge: gear on; battery under hood or seat, leave in casing; positive (red) cable first then negative (black); touch only rubber; 12 V; start 2 amps, up to no more than 10; slow charge best. Replace if won't hold; core fee averages $20 without old battery.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Seat switch under seat is part of operator presence system; blades won't turn on without operator. Test with multimeter or temporarily bypass; don't operate without it.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: Same capacity fuse; protects electrical system from surge or short; if continues, Spartan dealer or shop.
