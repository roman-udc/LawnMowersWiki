## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Poulan Pro lawn mower won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, wrong-fuel, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-recoil-starter, bad-ignition-coil, faulty-charging-system, incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: starting fails from fuel restriction (bad fuel, bad gas cap, plugged fuel filter, clogged lines, dirty carb, bad fuel pump), air restriction, or electrical problem (plugged air filter, bad spark plug, faulty switch, bad battery).
- tip: guessing parts is expensive; most lawn mower centers don't accept returns on electrical parts.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-safety-switch
- symptom: key turned and the mower doesn't start or even turn over (ignition switch); click or hum (solenoid).

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: may have forgotten to refuel, fuel gauge stopped working, or developed a leak. fix: repair leak or gauge; add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- brand: Poulan Pro 4-cycle: unleaded, octane 87 or higher, ethanol 10% or less (regular or E10); 2-cycle needs gas and oil mix.
- fact: ethanol attracts moisture, leaves gummy deposits; the water/ethanol mix separates and sinks to the tank bottom and runs hot in the engine, damaging it.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas breaks down as soon as 30 days after purchase; buy only what can be used in 30 days; buy from a reputable busy station; stabilizer if more than can be used in 30 days; store in an approved container, dry, away from moisture and heat.
- fix: drain tank with a fuel siphon pump; add fresh fuel with Sea Foam (cleans fuel system, reduces moisture, stabilizes).

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: clogged cap vent makes a vacuum so fuel can't leave the tank.
- confirm: remove the cap when it won't start and try starting; if it starts and runs fine, reinstall cap and keep running; if it sputters and shuts off after a short time, bad cap.
- fix: replace the cap (cleaning may not work and the problem returns).

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: dirt, carbon, oil buildup; burnt electrode; cracked porcelain; misfire or no fire.
- fix: remove and inspect; dirty: clean with a wire brush; very dark or damaged: replace; verify correct gap and securely attached plug wire (loose wire also causes no-start or rough running).

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: clippings and dust clog; engine can overheat and not get enough air to keep running.
- tip: replace annually, clean several times a season. mistake: never run without an air filter, even briefly; a little dirt can damage the engine.
- brand: Poulan Pro models use paper or foam filters.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step (paper) 1: remove housing cover. 2: remove filter without letting dirt fall into intake. 3: wipe dirt from housing. 4: tap against solid surface. 5: hold to light; light through paper: reuse; no light, very dirty, damaged or oily: replace. 6: install and reattach cover.
- step (foam): same removal; inspect: replace if damaged, torn, dry, brittle or oil-saturated; otherwise wash with water and mild dish detergent, rinse until water runs clear and soap gone, lay flat to air dry (sun speeds it), lightly saturate with air filter oil (fully coated, not dripping), install and reattach cover.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace a plugged or cracked filter; arrow on the side points in direction of fuel flow, toward carburetor, away from tank.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- fact: arrow toward carburetor away from tank.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom: clogged or kinked lines block fuel.
- fix: remove fuel line, spray carb cleaner in, blow compressed air through until free of debris and gummy residue; replace the line if clog won't clear.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: used when the carburetor sits higher than the tank; a vacuum fuel pump builds pressure off the engine that moves a diaphragm forcing gas to the carb; replace if leaking at the seams; bad pump may not be visible without checking flow.
- confirm: first verify fuel reaches the pump inlet (if not, check fuel line or filter restriction). Then stop fuel flow with shut-off valve or pinch pliers; remove the fuel line from the carburetor into a container; restore flow, start the mower; watch for consistent or pulsating flow; no good flow means replace the pump.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: carb regulates fuel mixed with air; old gas leaves deposits.
- fix: if mechanical, take it apart, clean with carb cleaner including float bowl and needle; replace if too dirty to clean.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter about 12.7 volts; below that, charge; replace if won't hold charge.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: check terminals not corroded and attached securely; clean in baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda) and wire brush.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: switches prevent the deck from running without the operator present; on a riding mower another prevents starting when the parking brake isn't engaged.
- confirm: test with multimeter or temporarily bypass to identify a bad switch.
- safety: do not operate without the safety switch; never run with a switch bypassed; check manual for all switches on the model.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: test with multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; wire attached to solenoid gets hot, smokes or melts.
- fix: test per separate article; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fact: some Poulan Pro push mowers use recoil start; fails from bad pulley, loose or missing spring, broken clips.
- fix: try replacing spring and restringing; if clips or pulley are damaged, replace the recoil assembly.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: provides voltage so the plug fires.
- confirm: after confirming plug is good, check coil continuity with multimeter; a break in continuity means replace.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: riding mower: not main cause of no-start but contributes to a weak battery; clue: battery keeps dying and needs repeated charging; stator or alternator can be bad. Source mentions an ohmmeter test (separate article).
- pro: take to Poulan Pro dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Poulan Pro safety precautions require certain steps to start and operate; check the manual so you don't trip a safety feature that shuts the mower off.
