## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Ego mower cuts uneven"
- alias: "strips of uncut grass"
- alias: "grass tips turn brown after mowing"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, wrong-cutting-height, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- safety: before work remove safety key, battery pack(s) and any other power source; wait for moving parts to stop; wear heavy gloves and eye protection
- tip: check tires/wheels first, before the deck, since a low tire or damaged wheel makes the deck sit unlevel; confirming pressures first avoids wrong adjustments you then must reverse
- brand: Ego

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: push mower: wheels can come loose or get damaged so mower doesn't sit level; tighten hardware, replace damaged wheels
- fact: zero turn: gauge rear and front tires to Ego's recommended pressures (operator's manual); left and right rear equal, left and right front equal; add/remove air to match

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: worn blades rounded at tips leave strips of uncut grass; grass tips brown a couple days after mowing; handful of grass shows bruised, torn, brown ends
- fact: blades wear from dirt circulating under the deck
- fix: remove; sharpen if in good condition; replace if very worn or gouged
- symptom: bent blade (from rock, stump etc.) gives uneven cut and vibration, even if slightly bent
- confirm: park on flat level surface, remove safety key and battery pack(s)
- confirm (removed): lay blade over a new Ego blade; or take to Ego service center
- confirm (installed): measure from ground to blade tip on one side, rotate 180 degrees, measure other side; more than 1/8 inch difference means likely bent; remove to confirm
- mistake: never straighten a bent blade; weakened metal can break off and fly out at high speed injuring people, pets, objects
- tip: walk yard and remove objects before mowing

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- symptom: sail (high side) facing the ground tears grass, may hit ground, can damage blade motors and lawn
- fix: install with sail pointing up toward the deck; some blades marked "THIS SIDE DOWN" or "THIS SIDE FACING GRASS"; "Sail Up!"
- fact: balance resharpened blades (equal weight each end); new blades need no balancing
- tip: blade balancer inexpensive and worth it; or hang on a nail sticking out of wall 3/4 to 1 inch and remove metal from the end hanging lower until level

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- symptom: loose blade fails to rotate so mower doesn't cut
- fix: tighten blade bolt to torque spec in operator's manual

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- symptom: deck set too low for the amount of grass compromises cut
- fix: raise deck height

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- symptom: grass buildup under deck spoils cut; air movement and suction under deck make the cut; clogged deck also slows blade speed and strains motor/engine
- tip: don't cut wet grass (clumps under deck and in yard)

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: zero turn deck must be level side to side per spec; then adjust front-to-rear; front should sit a little lower than rear; see operator's manual for the model spec

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: some Ego models have a blade speed setting (slower to save battery); tall or thick grass needs a faster blade tip speed
- pro: if cutting worse than before, grass isn't thick and blade is good, have Ego service center check blade motor or power supply

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- symptom: too fast pushes grass over instead of cutting; thick, tall, wet grass puts heavy load so slow down

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: deck shell moves air with blade to create suction that stands grass up for an even cut; damaged shell hurts cutting
- fix: try to restore shape; otherwise replace the deck shell

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- safety: remove safety key and battery before blade/deck work
