## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Spartan zero turn won't move forward or reverse"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- safety: Remove spark plug wire and ignition key before repairs; wait for the transmission to cool; caution with hot hydraulic fluid; follow Spartan operator's manual.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: After pushing the mower (hydro levers disengaged to free-roll), levers not returned to "operating" position; mower won't drive.
- confirm: Hydrostatic neutral levers are on the Parker wheel motors near the rear wheels (one per side, left and right). Levers must be pointed to the right as seen from the seat.
- fix: Push levers to the right.

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- confirm: Check belt hasn't fallen off and is correctly on pulleys; replace if any wear.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Tensioner pulley or bearing broken; most tensioner pulleys are hard plastic and can wear or break.
- fix: Replace worn pulley or bad bearing; keep the tensioner arm greased so it doesn't seize.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- symptom: Idler spring tensions the pump drive belt; broken or fallen out. Replace.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: Old or low fluid: no movement or weak.
- confirm: Check level with the oil cool; wipe around caps before removing (dirt is often a factor in hydraulic failures).
- fix: Add until full level on the dipsticks or overflow tanks (model-dependent). Check for leaks when low; dealer for leaks.
- fact: Use Parker HT-1000 transmission oil (source misspells "Parket"); change oil and filters after the first 250 hours; later changes every 1000 hours.

## page: procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Parker HT-1000 transmission oil; first change oil and filters at 250 hours; then every 1000 hours.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- symptom: Bad/low fluid overheats; can reflect more extensive damage.
- pro: Spartan dealer if pump leaks or mower runs fine cold but stops running when hot.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- symptom: Slow or no movement; must bleed after changing fluid.

## page: procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system
- step 1: Raise rear drive tires off the ground.
- step 2: With the mower running, move drive levers forward then reverse so tires rotate both ways.
- step 3: Repeat until no excessive noise and tires move at normal speed. Follow operator's manual for the model.
