## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: routine upkeep for a Spartan: replace filters and spark plugs, scrape the deck, sharpen blades, clean the mower, deck and around the engine.

## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Spartan mower won't start"
- fact: needs air, fuel, spark: check electrical components, spark plug, battery; air restriction (plugged filter, stuck choke, wrong choke setting); fuel restriction from old fuel (filter, lines, pump, carb).
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, bad-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting
- fix: table identical in substance to the Gravely article: empty tank -> fill; bad fuel -> drain, fresh fuel, Sea Foam; clogged cap vent -> thin piece of wire to clear vent, replace if needed; bad plug -> replace, secure, gap to spec; plugged air filter -> clean/replace; plugged fuel filter -> replace; bad/leaking pump -> replace; blocked lines -> carb cleaner and compressed air, replace if needed; dirty carb -> clean components, replace if needed; battery/terminals -> charge, replace if no hold, tighten, clean corrosion; safety switch -> test, replace; ignition switch or coil -> test, replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Spartan starts then dies" / "dies while mowing"
- fact: a plugged-vent fuel cap can show up after mowing a while (vacuum in tank); overheating - inspect cooling fins for broken or plugged.
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fix: bad fuel -> drain, fresh, Sea Foam; plugged filter -> clean/replace; dirty/broken fins (packed with dirt, oil, grass) -> replace and clean; clogged lines -> carb cleaner + compressed air; dirty carb -> clean, replace if needed; bad ignition coil (plug can't get voltage) -> replace; oil level too low overheats and shuts down, too much builds crankcase pressure -> correct to full level per manufacturer; plugged deck overworks engine -> scrape, sharpen blades.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- fact: caused by plugged air filter, wrong oil level, or internal problems; wrong air-to-fuel ratio or hot oil burning off.
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train, bad-head-gasket
- fix: plugged filter -> clean/replace; too much oil (pressure, oil into cylinder via valve train) -> drain to manufacturer's level; low oil (friction, heat) -> small engine shop; adding oil often too late; internal (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) -> Spartan dealer or small engine repair shop.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Spartan won't move or go forward" / "weak hydrostatic transmission"
- fact: old hydraulic oil or low level; check drive belt, tensioner pulley and spring; check the hydraulic release (drive release) lever, may have been moved when pushing the mower manually; must be in operating position.
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fix: belt worn/off -> replace, seat on pulleys; tensioner pulley bearing fails -> replace; idler arm spring broken/missing -> replace; low/old oil -> change at Spartan-recommended intervals, check level; hot fluid (sign of hydro damage) -> let cool; if fine cold but weak hot, Spartan dealer; air -> bleed per Spartan owner's manual after fluid change.
- fact: weak hydro: bad drive belt (frayed, cracked, worn), bad tensioner pulley (replace pulley and grease tensioner arm), low or old oil.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Spartan won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, (tracking adjustment)
- fix: unequal tire pressure -> set to spec, both sides equal; bad dampers (mower jerks to one side going forward) -> replace; tracking (one tire faster; applies to zero-turn or stand-on) -> adjust the speed adjustment bolt per owner's manual.

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: adjust speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed (zero-turn or stand-on mower); see manual.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Spartan vibrating and shaking"
- fact: some engine vibration is normal; severe vibration needs addressing.
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fix: loose/missing engine bolts -> secure/replace; clutch bearing failure or manual-engagement clutch bushing/linkage -> replace clutch; unbalanced/damaged blades -> replace, balance before install; string/weeds on blade shaft -> remove, check spindle; spindle housing bearings -> replace bearing or housing assembly; worn pulleys/belt -> replace; debris -> remove ("don't overlook the small stuff").

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Spartan bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: worn-blade-sail, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: 3-column list only: worn blade, bent blade, blade installed wrong, clogged deck / uneven tire pressures, unleveled deck, bent spindle or bearing, worn deck pulleys or belt / engine speed too low, ground speed too fast, damaged deck shell, operator error.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "Spartan leaking gas"
- fact: strong odor or burnt grass spots; follow fuel lines out of the tank; fuel evaporates leaving only fumes. Common leak area on Spartan: carburetor: bowl gasket dries and loses seal, or float sticks. Old gas degrades plastics in filter, pump, tank seams.
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fix: dirty/failed carb -> clean, replace damaged parts; old filter -> replace; pump leaking at seams -> replace; tank: metal can rust through, poly leaks at seams -> replace (a rust hole can sometimes be repaired if no replacement tank is available); shut-off valve metal/plastic leaks -> replace; cracked rubber fuel lines -> replace; gas cap seal -> replace cap.
