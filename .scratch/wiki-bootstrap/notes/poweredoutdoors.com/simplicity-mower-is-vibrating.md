## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Simplicity mower vibrating and shaking"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring (idler bracket/spring), debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- safety: Remove ignition key and spark plug boots before working on deck.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: Engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) on the bottom of the engine absorb vibration. Tighten loose; replace worn, broken, missing. Push mower: check engine bolts when vibration appears once blade is engaged.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: Tractor/riding/zero-turn: ball bearing in an electric clutch can fail and shake the mower. Manual engagement clutch (belts and pulleys): bushing and linkages can fail. Push mower has no clutch; uses a lever to engage the blade; check lever for loose/worn parts.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Wrong blades or unbalanced blades vibrate. Author prefers OEM blades; if aftermarket, lay on OEM blade to match first (correct center hole and length alone don't guarantee fit). Imbalance from normal use (dirt wears unevenly) or sharpening (more metal removed one side). Balance with balancer or nail on wall; remove metal from the side hanging lower.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: Wire, string, long weeds wrap spindle and are hard to see; remove, then check spindle and housing.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Gloves; grab each blade end and rock up/down; movement or knocking = remove blade and spindle housing, inspect, repair.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: Slack lets belt not sit securely and deck vibrate. Replace if stretched, cracked, glazed. If belt looks good, check routing and tension to remove slack.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Failed bearings make pulleys wobble, not parallel to deck, one side higher. Confirm by turning each slowly by hand; feel restriction, hear bearing noise; replace pulley and bearing assembly.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Loose/worn idler bracket and spring shake the mower; hole in bracket where spring attaches wears larger and spring stretches; replace worn bracket or spring.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Even a wood chip can cause extreme vibration. Debris collects under pulleys (not flat/parallel) and affects tensioner, adding slack. Remove spindle covers, clean deck regularly.
- tip: Clean after each mowing, also prevents corrosion from moisture in debris.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- fact: Clean after each mowing; debris causes vibration and corrosion.
