## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "SCAG won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, faulty-charging-system
- fact: Engine needs spark, fuel and air to form combustion. Check air restriction (plugged air filter, wrong choke setting). Old gas corrodes fuel components or leaves deposits restricting fuel lines and carburetor. Other causes: bad spark plug, bad battery, corroded wiring, bad switch, faulty charging system.
- fix (table): no gas: fill fresh fuel. Bad/old fuel: drain and refill, add additive like Sea Foam to stabilize/clean. Clogged fuel cap/tank vent: replace. Bad spark plug (dirty/broken, bad connection, wrong gap): replace, secure connections, gap to manufacturer spec. Plugged air filter: clean, replace if bad. Plugged fuel filter: replace. Bad fuel pump: replace. Blocked fuel lines: carb cleaner and compressed air, replace if needed. Dirty carburetor (bad fuel and ethanol deposits): clean components, replace if needed. Bad battery/loose or dirty terminals: charge, replace if won't hold charge, tighten cables, clean corrosion. Bad safety switch: test and replace. Bad ignition switch or coil: test and replace.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "SCAG starts then dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Many no-start causes also cause starts-then-dies; also check faulty ignition coil, wrong oil level, dirty/broken cooling fins, clogged deck.
- confirm: Ignition coil: check continuity with an ohmmeter for a break; replace if bad (windings can separate and short out).
- fact: Too much oil raises crankcase pressure so engine overheats and dies; too little oil doesn't lubricate so it overheats and shuts down. Correct the level; see a small engine mechanic if that doesn't fix it (possible significant engine problem).
- fact: Choke is for cold starts; if it keeps restricting airflow after warm-up the engine can shut off. Put choke in correct position.
- fact: Cooling fins packed with dirt, oil, grass prevent cooling the engine block; replace broken fins, clean fins.
- fact: Clogged deck strains the engine; scrape the deck periodically.
- fact: Spark plug: dirty/damaged plug fouls and causes intermittent running; clean or replace, ensure wires secure and plug gapped correctly.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "SCAG blowing white, black or blue smoke"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-head-gasket
- fact: Stop running the mower once smoke is seen and find the cause. Minor: plugged air filter, too much crankcase oil, bad engine gasket. More serious: low engine oil or internal engine problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) need a small engine mechanic.
- fact: Excess oil builds crankcase pressure and gets into the cylinder through the valve train; drain a little oil to the manufacturer's recommended level. Low oil raises friction and extreme heat; often too late to fix by adding oil.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "SCAG won't move / weak hydrostatic transmission"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, missing-idler-arm-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: First check the drive bypass valves (drive release arms at the rear that disengage the hydros so the mower can be pushed). When bypassed the mower free-rolls but won't drive; set to operating position.
- fact: Then check worn/fallen drive pump belt (replace worn or cracked belt, ensure seated around pulleys), failed tensioner pulley bearing (replace), broken or missing idler arm spring (replace), low or old hydraulic oil (change at SCAG recommended intervals; check level periodically), hot hydraulic fluid (let cool; if runs fine cold but weak when hot, have SCAG dealer check; may signal hydrostatic damage), air in system (bleed after fluid change per owner's manual).
- fact: Weak hydrostatic: bad hydraulic/drive belt or pulley, old or low hydraulic oil. Frayed/cracked/worn belt replace; failed tensioner pulley replace and grease the tensioner arm.

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: Mower free-rolls but won't drive; drive release/bypass valves are at the rear of the mower.
- fix: Put drive release arms in operating position.

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- symptom: If the idler arm spring breaks or goes missing it can't put tension on the drive belt.
- fix: Replace spring.

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- confirm: Mower fine when cold, weak when hydraulic system gets hot.
- pro: SCAG dealer check.

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: Must bleed air after changing hydraulic fluid or mower can't move at normal speeds; follow SCAG procedure in the owner's manual.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Low or old oil fails to lubricate and system seems weak; change at SCAG recommended intervals and check level.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "SCAG won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fact: Unequal tire pressure pulls to one side; keep to manufacturer spec, equal on both sides. Bad dampers make mower jerk to one side going forward; replace damper. Tracking: when one tire moves faster than the other mower pulls; adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed per owner's manual.

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: Adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change the rotation speed of one tire; refer to owner's manual.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "SCAG vibrating and shaking"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Causes: loose or missing engine mounting bolts (secure/replace); clutch bearing failure falling out of the clutch (replace clutch); damaged/unbalanced blades (replace, balance before installing); string or long weeds wrapped around blade shaft (remove, check spindle for damage); bad spindle housing bearing (replace bearing or whole spindle housing assembly); worn pulleys or deck belt; small debris lodged in deck.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "SCAG bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Check tire pressures first; a low tire makes the deck not level. Then the deck: blades, deck belt, spindle, spindle/pulley bearings, deck shell. Table lists: worn blade, bent blade, blade installed wrong, clogged deck, uneven tire pressures, unlevel deck, bent spindle or spindle bearing, worn deck pulleys or belt, engine speed too low, ground speed too fast, damaged deck shell, operator error.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "SCAG leaking gas"
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal, clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Sometimes only a gas smell (evaporated). Carburetor leak from failed gasket or stuck float; also fuel filter, shut-off valve, gas cap, lines, pump.
- fact: Carburetor holds old-gas buildup causing blockages or part failure: clean and replace damaged parts. Fuel filter softens and degrades from old fuel: replace. Fuel pump wears and leaks at seams: replace. Poly tank can leak at seams: replace tank. Shut-off valves leak with time: replace. Rubber lines dry and crack, or are punctured by a foreign object or clamps: replace. Gas cap seal fails and gas splashes out around the cap: replace cap.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "SCAG runs rough and sputters"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Look for items that restrict air, fuel or spark, or make the engine work harder (too fast, plugged deck, dull blades).
- fix: Old fuel: drain, refill with gas and stabilizer. Clogged line: clear with carb cleaner and compressed air, else new line. Plugged filter: replace. Dirty carb: remove to clean, rebuild or replace. Bad gas cap not venting: replace. Dirty plug: clean with wire brush; new one if very dark, worn, damaged. Slow ground speed for tall, thick, wet grass. Throttle lever to highest position when engaging blades. Scrape deck. Sharpen blades.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "SCAG blades won't engage or turn on"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: Won't engage when PTO switch faulty, weak battery, failed safety switch, or bad clutch; blades won't turn when belt worn or off pulleys from stretched belt, bad pulley bearing, worn tensioner parts.
- fix: Belt worn, cracked or shiny/glazed: replace. Belt falls off: replace worn idler arm, spring, bad pulleys, stretched belt. Bad PTO switch: replace. Bad clutch: replace; on manual-engagement clutch replace worn clutch cable, lever, springs, linkages. Weak battery: test, charge if under 12.7 volts for a 12 V battery, replace if it won't hold charge. Safety switch: replace. Blown fuse: replace with same amperage.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Charge if reading below 12.7 V on a 12 V battery; replace if won't hold a charge.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fix: Replace the clutch; manual engagement: replace worn cable, lever, springs, linkages. Clutch bearing failure causes vibration.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: Replace with a fuse of the same amperage.

## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: To keep a SCAG running at its best: regularly clean the mower, check engine oil and hydraulic oil, clean the air filter, sharpen blades, scrape the deck; perform routine oil changes as required.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fix: Place the throttle lever in its highest position when engaging the blades; low engine speed also cited for uneven cut.
