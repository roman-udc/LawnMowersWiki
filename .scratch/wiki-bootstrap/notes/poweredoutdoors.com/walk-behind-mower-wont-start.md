## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "walk-behind mower won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter, weak-or-dead-battery, blown-fuse, bad-starter-solenoid, incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Walk-behinds range from 18" to 60" decks; may use manual recoil or more complex ignition. Commercial walk-behinds add ignition switches, 12 V batteries and charging systems that can also cause no-start.
- safety: Follow operator's manual.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: Add fresh fuel (people skip the obvious when frustrated).

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Gas breaks down as soon as 30 days; loses combustibility; ethanol attracts corrosive moisture, gummy deposits restrict flow. Choose max 10% ethanol, min 87 octane; least or no ethanol best for small engines.
- fact: Most walk-behinds are 4-cycle (unleaded gasoline); some 2-cycle still used, where gasoline and oil are mixed in the tank.
- tip: If can't use within 30 days add stabilizer (Sea Foam stabilizes, reduces moisture, cleans).
- fix: Siphon tank; refill fresh with additive.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Cap vent equalizes pressure; plugged = vacuum, no fuel.
- confirm: Remove cap, start and run; replace cap and keep running; sputters and shuts down = bad cap.
- fix: Replace; cleaning doesn't always work or only temporarily.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: Carbon buildup and oil; broken porcelain; burnt electrode; wrong gap; loose wire cause misfire or intermittent starting.
- fix: Inspect; replace for carbon buildup, cracked porcelain, burnt electrode or very dark tip; gap properly; secure wires. Light dirt: wire brush and reuse.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Filter plugs with clippings and dirt. Never run without a filter; a small amount of dirt can score the cylinder, blow gaskets and necessitate engine replacement.
- step 1 (foam): Remove cover (knobs, clips or wing nuts); wipe cover. Remove filter without letting dirt fall into the intake. Wipe housing.
- step 2 (foam): Replace if very dark, torn or brittle; reuse if good.
- step 3 (foam): Wash with mild dish soap and water; rinse until water runs clear; lay flat to dry (sun speeds it).
- step 4 (foam): Lightly coat with filter oil, fully coated but not dripping; blot excess with paper towel (new foam filter also gets oil). Reinstall, secure cover.
- step 1 (paper): Remove cover and filter as above; wipe housing.
- step 2 (paper): Tap against hard surface using the plastic or rubber part, not the paper element; no compressed air (damages it).
- step 3 (paper): Hold to light; light through paper = reuse; no light or oil-covered = replace. Install, secure cover.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- fact: Foam and paper steps as above.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom: Lines are black rubber so clogs aren't visible; check one section at a time with shut-off valve under tank or pinch-off pliers.
- fix: Remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air; repeat; replace if stuck (hardware store or online). Replace dry or cracked lines even without a clog.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Many walk-behinds need no pump; only when carburetor sits higher than the tank. Old fuel degrades pump; vacuum from crankcase builds pressure. Leaking pump must be replaced.
- confirm: Good supply to inlet; shut off fuel, disconnect hose from carburetor into container, turn on and start; consistent steady or pulsating flow = OK; else replace.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: Replace annually or more if fuel is dirty; arrow toward carburetor, away from tank.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fix: Clean by taking apart, carb cleaner on carb including float bowl and needle; replace failed components or whole carb if too dirty; small engine mechanic option.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Number and type varies by model (e.g., prevent deck running without operator); see manual. Test with multimeter or temporarily bypass.
- safety: Never operate without or with bypassed safety switch.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: Check spark plug is good first; ohmmeter continuity; break = replace.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- symptom: Rope no longer retracts. Sometimes restring and replace spring; if clips or pulley damaged replace the recoil assembly.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Electric-start: try recoil; starts with recoil = electric start problem.
- fix: Charge per manual; check for blown fuse; replace battery that won't hold; mechanic for electric start system.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Click or hum when turning key; wire on solenoid hot, smoking or melting. Replace if bad.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Safety features require certain steps to start/operate; varies by model; read operator's manual so you don't trip features that shut the mower off or block starting.
