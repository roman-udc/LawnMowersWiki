## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Husqvarna mower won't stay running"
- fact: if it starts but won't run, engine likely isn't getting enough gas or air.
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, engine-overheats (low oil, wrong oil, clogged deck, plugged air filter)
- pro: if not comfortable, Husqvarna dealer or repair shop.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down from 30 days after purchase; ethanol attracts moisture, corrosion, gummy deposits; separates and sinks.
- fix: empty tank (fuel siphon pump); mix fresh gas min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol with additive (Sea Foam Motor Treatment or STA-BIL); fill; run mower a while to work through system.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: fuel jet clogs or small parts stick.
- confirm: confirm fuel reaching carb (pump moving fuel; if not check filter, lines, pump); remove air filter carefully; spray carb cleaner in intake; if it runs strong then sluggish/shuts off, carb dirty; disassemble and clean; rebuild or replace if problems continue.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: starves engine; sluggish, may stop; also engine damage. Replace annually, clean several times per season.
- step paper: remove; wipe housing; tap; light test; reinstall and cover.
- step foam: remove; wipe housing; inspect (dark spots, tears, dry brittle = replace); wash with water and mild detergent; rinse; lay flat, sun speeds drying; coat completely with foam air filter oil but not dripping (wring out or paper towel excess); reinstall.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- see above paper and foam steps.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke closed to start cold; once warm must be open for correct mix; otherwise sluggish and won't stay running.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: carbon/dirt/oil coating = no spark. Replace if very dark, broken porcelain, or burnt electrodes. If lightly dirty and good condition, clean with wire brush. Gap to manufacturer spec (owner's manual); loose attachment also causes running problems. If still trouble, check ignition coil.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: windings separate and short when mower gets hot; no voltage to plug; check continuity break with ohmmeter.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap vent equalizes pressure; blocked = vacuum, no fuel flow, shutdown.
- confirm: after it stops, remove cap and start; if runs fine suspect vent; put cap back while running and see if it shuts off later. Replace cap.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- fact: overheating shuts engine down: low oil, wrong oil type, clogged deck, plugged air filter.
