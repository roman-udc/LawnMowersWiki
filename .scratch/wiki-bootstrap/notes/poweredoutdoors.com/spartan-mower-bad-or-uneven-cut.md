## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Spartan mower has a bad or uneven cut"; "brown tips a couple of days after mowing"; "strips of uncut grass"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- safety: Remove spark plug wire (boot) and ignition key before troubleshooting/repairs.
- fact: Check order: tire pressure first (otherwise you may make unnecessary deck adjustments that must be reversed).

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: Check both drive tires to operator's manual pressure; left and right must be equal; lower tire makes deck sit unlevel = bad cut. Check air-filled caster wheels too.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Blades are wear items; wear faster in sandy lawns / thin grass because deck suction pulls in dirt. Dull blades beat up grass, brown tips a couple of days later. Worn blades with rounded ends may leave a strip of grass between blades.
- fix: Sharpen dull edges. Replace if the sail is thinner than the middle of the blade or the ends are rounded. Chips/gouges: small ones sharpened out; too large -> replace. Bends from hitting tree roots or rocks.
- confirm (without removing): park on flat ground, remove key and spark plug boots; measure from one blade tip to the ground; rotate 180 degrees and measure the other end; over 1/8" variance = replace; repeat for each blade.
- confirm (removing): remove blade, lay old over new; any gaps = replace.
- fact: After finding a bend, check the spindle bearing (can fail from significant impact).
- mistake: NEVER straighten a blade; compromises metal, prone to crack; a cracked blade spinning fast is hazardous.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: Sail is the high side; creates air movement; with deck shell and baffles creates suction lifting grass.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail faces up toward the deck; upside down tears grass and may damage blade spindles. Balance blades before install; unbalanced gives uneven cut and vibration.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Plugged deck reduces needed under-deck area, can't create suction. Scrape regularly, more often in wet grass (sticks, clumps). Deck spray helps but doesn't prevent all sticking.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Remove spark plug boot(s) and key; with heavy gloves hold each end of the blade and rock up and down; movement or knock = bad spindle housing bearing or bent spindle; repair or replace spindle housing.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Turn each deck pulley slowly; resistance or noise = replace.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: Belt must be without cracks/wear and run securely around pulleys.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Full throttle when the deck is engaged; slow engine speed doesn't give blade tip speed for a quality cut.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Slow ground speed in thick, tall, wet grass; too fast loads engine, weakens suction, pushes grass over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Overlap each pass when mowing stripes or strips of grass are left; author's anecdote of mowing with fewest passes.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Shell tweaked by hitting trees, rocks, hard surfaces; try to repair; else replace deck shell or whole deck. Get two quotes from the Spartan dealer: shell plus labor to swap components vs complete deck replacement; consider mower age.

## unmapped
- Uneven lawn / scalping: rolling the lawn (lawn-care advice; skip).
