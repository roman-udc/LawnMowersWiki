## page: problems/engine-dies-when-hot
- alias: "zero turn gets hot and quits"
- symptom: shuts down after getting hot; air or fuel restriction, low oil, bad plug or coil, plugged deck, wrong oil, old fuel
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, old-fuel, dirty-carburetor, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- safety: prevent mower starting/moving while working, remove spark plug boots; beware hot and sharp parts
- pro: experienced mechanic at dealer or repair shop

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: replace annually, clean several times a season; plugged filter restricts air, engine gets hot and shuts down
- step (paper) 1: remove filter; wipe housing with clean dry cloth, no dirt into intake
- step (paper) 2: tap on solid surface; never use compressed air (damages the filter)
- step (paper) 3: hold to light; no light means replace, do not reuse
- step (paper) 4: reinstall and cover

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: ethanol moisture leaves gummy residue; ethanol corrosive; water/ethanol separates and sinks to the tank bottom as fuel ages, then runs through and makes engine run hot and quit
- fact: diesel zero turn: diesel sitting a long time forms solids and condensation in the tank, clogs fuel system; old diesel looks dark
- fix: siphon into approved fuel storage container; fresh fuel plus additive (author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment, petroleum-based)

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: first confirm fuel reaches carb; remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in intake; starts, runs, dies means carb
- fix: disassemble and clean, or shop, or replace carb

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: use air-cooled engine oil (author: high zinc concentration used as cooling agent); mowers are air-cooled unlike liquid-cooled cars
- fact: most small engine makers recommend SAE30 or 10W-30; may need other viscosity like 20W-50 in higher ambient temperatures
- tip: use Kawasaki Motors oil viscosity chart with operator's manual (link to Kawasaki 4-cycle engine oil page)
- symptom: wrong oil overheats and shuts off
- fix: replace with correct viscosity for mower and ambient temperature; see mechanic if problems continue
- symptom (low level): check oil before each mowing; low oil from leak or engine burning oil; friction/heat; overheats, smokes, burns oil, melts parts
- fix: top up to dipstick mark; if very hot from low oil, likely damage needing small engine mechanic

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: crankshaft and rod move through excess oil and can't move freely; crankcase pressure builds, engine loaded, gets hot and quits; smoke can plug filter causing overheating
- fix: drain a little via drain plug or oil filter, or oil evacuator or turkey baster; check dipstick; adjust until between fill lines

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- symptom: plugged or damaged fins don't dissipate heat; engine overheats and quits
- fix: remove dirt/debris; repair damaged fins; clean around engine and shroud; make sure the heat shield is securely in place

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: check for tip buildup; clean or replace if dark; inspect for broken porcelain or burnt electrode; gap per spec; wires secure

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: when hot the coil stops working; windings separate and short; not enough voltage to plug
- confirm: ohm meter for a break in continuity
- fix: replace the coil (Source says "replace the mower", evidently a typo for coil)

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: run with and without cap; fine without but shuts down after a while with cap on means cap
- fix: replace cap

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- symptom: packed deck strains engine, gets hot and quits; dull blades magnify it
- fix: scrape deck; sharpen blades; avoid wet grass; silicone or Teflon spray reduces sticking

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: dull blades on a packed deck magnify strain; sharpen

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- fact: most of the above causes produce overheating shutdown
