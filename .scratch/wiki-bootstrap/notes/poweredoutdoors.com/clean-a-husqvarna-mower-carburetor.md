## page: procedures/clean-the-carburetor
- symptom: mower runs sluggish or won't start; dirty carburetor gumming up from old fuel.
- confirm: with fuel confirmed reaching the carburetor, spray carburetor cleaner into the air intake and start; if it starts, runs and then dies, disassemble and clean the carburetor.
- tools: pliers, screwdriver, socket/ratchet set, carburetor cleaner, thick wire, needle nose pliers; flashlight; rag
- step 1: take multiple photos of the carburetor before and during tear-down (many small parts).
- step 2: shut off fuel supply with the fuel shut-off valve on the bottom of the fuel tank; if no valve, crimp the fuel line.
- step 3: detach throttle and choke cables from carburetor.
- step 4: remove hardware attaching carburetor to the air filter housing.
- step 5: slowly remove springs from carburetor; do not stretch them or they must be replaced; may need to twist carb. Don't rip the gasket between engine block and carburetor or replace it.
- step 6: remove the bowl: rag for remaining fuel; remove the screw at the bottom of the bowl and lower the bowl. Rubber-band-like gasket around bowl: keep carb cleaner/other substances off it (ruins it); replace gasket before reattaching if contaminated.
- step 7: check the stem hanging down in the center of the carburetor; holes can clog with old fuel gum; use flashlight and a thick wire to unclear.
- step 8: remove gummy deposits and white crusty buildup from components and carb using carburetor cleaner; almost impossible to remove all crusty material.
- step 9: ensure components including float needle and float are in good working order; reassemble in reverse order using photos.
- tip: if cleaning fails, small parts may need replacement or fuel damage may need carb replacement (procedures/rebuild-the-carburetor, procedures/replace-the-carburetor).
- brand: Husqvarna uses other manufacturers' small engines; have engine model and spec available to order correct carburetor or rebuild kit (online, Husqvarna dealer, or authorized engine dealer).

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: carb no longer regulates fuel-air mixture. Symptoms listed: won't start; backfires from running lean; engine sputters and runs rough; engine surges; consumes too much fuel.
- fix: clean (see procedure); run fresh gas, don't let it sit long.
