## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: may be undetected fuel leak, forgotten refuel, or faulty fuel gauge. Fix: check tank, check for leak or faulty gauge, add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas breaks down as quickly as 30 days; ethanol attracts moisture, premature corrosion, gummy deposits clog lines and carb components.
- fact: Wright requires unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol; no E15/E85 (up to 15%/85%). Ethanol-free is best but pricey.
- tip: buy only 30 days' worth; stabilize with Sea Foam Motor Treatment otherwise.
- fix: drain with fuel siphon pump; mix fresh gas with Sea Foam or STA-BIL to stabilize and reduce moisture.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: cap vent equalizes pressure; plugged -> vacuum, no fuel flow, no start.
- confirm: loosen or remove cap and run; if starts and runs fine, cap suspect; continue running with cap on; shuts off after a while and restarts only with cap removed = bad cap.
- fix: replace the cap.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: fouled plug, wrong gap or loose wire = intermittent fire.
- confirm: remove with socket wrench; look for cracked porcelain, burnt electrode, dirty; dark/damaged = replace; slightly dirty = wire brush (author usually just replaces; inexpensive).
- fix: gap per engine manufacturer; secure wire.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: zero-turn stirs up lots of dust; plugged filter starves engine and prevents start; very dirty filter can overheat engine. Never run without a filter, even briefly.
- tip: new filter each season; check/clean several times a season; dustier conditions -> more often.
- step (paper): remove from housing; wipe housing with dry clean rag (no dirt in intake); tap on solid surface; don't use compressed air; hold to light; light visible -> reuse, else replace; reinstall and cover.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: replace annually; sooner with dirty fuel; arrow on filter side points in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: can't see clog; use fuel shut-off valve or pinch pliers; with flow stopped remove end furthest from tank into container lower than tank (fuel can't flow uphill); start flow and check.
- fix: stop flow, remove line, spray carb cleaner, blow compressed air, repeat; replace if can't clear. Fuel line at hardware store or online.
- tip: replace fuel lines that are dry and starting to crack even if not clogged.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: old gas degrades pump; pump uses engine vacuum to move fuel to carburetor. Check for leaks; verify fuel to pump; disconnect fuel hose from carburetor into container, start, watch for constant or pulsating flow; else replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: old fuel deposits clog the fuel jet and gum small components; must be disassembled and cleaned of gummy and crusty buildup.
- fix: clean with carb cleaner including float bowl and needle; if too dirty replace: have engine model and spec number and contact local Wright dealer; or small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: clean corroded terminals with baking soda solution: 2 cups water + 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda, wire brush; check tight.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter about 12.7 volts; less -> charger; won't hold charge -> replace.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Wright switches prevent start without parking brake engaged and levers in the open/outward position; another switch disengages deck and shuts off mower if operator not sensed. Possibly more.
- confirm: multimeter; temporary bypass for troubleshooting only.
- safety: never operate without safety switch or with one bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: contributes to weak battery; bad stator or alternator; if battery keeps dying and needs constant charging suspect it.
- pro: Wright dealer or small engine shop; otherwise you replace electrical parts until one works; stores usually won't take electrical returns; ensure mechanic has charging system experience.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: follow starting procedure per Wright manual; also choke: reduces air to cylinder for cold start; set to off once warm or mower sputters and dies.
