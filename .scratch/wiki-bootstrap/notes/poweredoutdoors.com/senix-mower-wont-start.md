## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "SENIX mower won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-air-filter, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter
- fact: needs air, fuel, spark.
- safety: follow operator's manual; remove spark plug wire before repairs.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: SENIX tank is small; easy to run out; may also be from a fuel leak or forgetting last fill. Check tank first.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas breaks down and loses combustibility soon after purchase; ethanol leaves varnish and gummy deposits.
- tip: buy fresh fuel min 87 octane; never over 10% ethanol, ethanol-free best; consume within 30 days; stabilizer if not; store in approved container away from moisture and combustibles.
- mistake: stabilizer must be added to fresh fuel; it will not reverse the effects of old fuel.
- fix: drain old gas; mix fresh gas with additive (author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment in every tank).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: shut off fuel (valve) or clamp line; remove end at carb into container placed lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill without a pump); turn on fuel and watch. Good flow = line is not the problem.
- fix: with fuel off remove line (if no shut-off valve you may have to drain the tank); carb cleaner and compressed air repeatedly; replace if clog persists or line is damaged/cracking.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: SENIX may have a fuel strainer attached at the bottom of the tank (screen) to keep dirt out of the fuel line.
- fix: with tank drained, remove strainer, inspect for clogging, clean screen or replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: after verifying fuel reaches carb, remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in air intake, start; if it starts, runs, then dies once the cleaner burns off, clean the carb.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: SENIX tank vents through the fuel cap.
- confirm: loosen/remove cap and try to start; if it starts but stops again after the cap is replaced and run a while, bad cap; replace.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- tip: replace annually; check and clean regularly.
- fix paper: remove cover and filter; wipe housing and cover with clean dry cloth; tap on solid surface; hold to light; good light = reuse, else replace.
- fix foam: wipe housing; replace if very dark, brittle or torn; wash in water and mild detergent; rinse until clear; squeeze (don't wring, can tear); dry; apply clean engine oil to fully cover, squeeze out excess.
- mistake: never oil a foam pre-filter (the one wrapped around a paper element); it can damage the paper filter.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: faulty when tip dirty, porcelain cracked or electrode burnt. Clean deposits with wire brush; replace if very dark or damaged; gap to manufacturer spec in the operator's manual; loose plug wires also cause no-start.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: ensure plug is good first; check coil continuity with ohmmeter; break = replace.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fact: rope can become unstrung; broken pulley, springs or clips; sometimes restringing is enough; price a full recoil replacement before replacing parts; replacing assembly may be cheaper.
