## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Worx electric mower bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: bad-tire, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade, wrong-cutting-height, clogged-deck-chute, mowing-too-fast
- fact: 9 causes: damaged wheel; worn/dull blade; bent blade; blade installed incorrectly; blade not tight; cutting height too low; plugged deck; ground speed too fast; uneven lawn.
- safety: Shut off mower, remove battery, wait for parts to stop before troubleshooting.

## page: causes/bad-tire
- symptom: Damaged or loose wheel makes mower sit unlevel; deck closer to ground on one side. Inspect wheels, tighten loose hardware, replace damaged wheels.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Dull blade tears grass; lawn tips turn brown a day or two after mowing; torn tips on a handful of grass.
- fix: Sharpen with a file if still good. Replace if extremely worn, gouged, rounded ends, or sail thinner than center. Sail = high side that creates airflow.
- fact: Balance: equal weight both ends; unbalanced by sand wear or uneven sharpening; blade balancer, or nail 3/4" to 1" out of wall, remove metal from end hanging lower.
- confirm (bent): park on flat level surface, remove battery pack(s), wait for parts to stop; remove blade and lay on a new Worx blade; even a slight bend gives a bad cut.
- mistake: Never straighten a bent blade; weakened metal may break off and fly.
- tip: Walk the lawn for rocks, stumps, toys first.

## page: causes/blade-installed-upside-down
- fact: Sail must face up. Upside-down blade tears grass, can hit high spots and damage the blade motor. Blade may be stamped with which side faces up.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- fact: Loose blade fails to rotate, no cutting. Torque blade bolt to operator's manual spec.

## page: causes/wrong-cutting-height
- fact: Deck too low can't handle thick tall grass; raise height. Double- or triple-cut: first cut at highest setting, lower each pass.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Plugged deck lacks area for airflow/suction; also makes motor work harder. Clean with deck scraper or wire brush; avoid wet grass (clumps).

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: Too fast pushes grass over; thick, tall, wet grass overloads and motor can shut off; slow down.

## page: guides/mower-parts-that-wear-out
- fact: Uneven lawn (ruts, bumps) isn't fixed by the mower; author rolls lawn every spring after snow melts (rodent tunnels); otherwise bad cut and mower bounces.
