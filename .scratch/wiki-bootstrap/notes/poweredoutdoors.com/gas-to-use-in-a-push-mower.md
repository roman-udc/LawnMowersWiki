## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Most push mowers today use 4-cycle (4-stage) engines but many 2-cycle (2-stage) remain; they need different fuel. Wrong fuel causes fuel system issues and engine damage.
- fact: A push mower requires unleaded gasoline, minimum octane 87, maximum ethanol 10%. 4-cycle: gasoline. 2-cycle: gasoline and oil mix. Straight gasoline in a 2-cycle engine will damage it.
- safety: Follow owner's manual precautions; handle fuel in a well-ventilated area; keep fuel away from combustibles and extreme heat.
- fact: Ethanol attracts moisture from the air, which is corrosive to the fuel system; fuel and water separate over time, leaving gummy deposits that restrict fuel; harmful if the separated mix runs through the engine. Lower ethanol is better.
- fact: Never use E15, E30 or E85 (up to 15%, 30%, 85% ethanol). (The Source writes E35 in the 4-cycle section and E30 earlier, an inconsistency.)
- fact: Gasoline can begin breaking down as soon as 30 days after purchase. Buy what you can use in 30 days; add stabilizer to fuel not used quickly.
- fact: Ethanol-free fuel is best but more expensive; sold at some stations as recreation fuel or REC-90, and in canisters (e.g. 2-cycle 50:1 premix, 2-cycle 40:1 premix, 4-cycle ethanol-free fuel).
- tip: Author adds Sea Foam Motor Treatment to every tank; it stabilizes, cleans the fuel system and reduces moisture.
- fact: Identify engine type: (1) Fuel cap: an original 2-cycle cap lists the gas-to-oil ratio (32:1, 40:1 or 50:1) or shows a fuel/oil can; (2) fill ports: 2-cycle has one fill port for the mix, 4-cycle has two (engine oil and gas); (3) operator's manual lists mixed or straight gas.
- mistake: Adding straight gas to a 2-cycle push mower: it will most likely shut off and seize, since it gets no lubrication.
- fact: 2-cycle mix ratios 32:1, 40:1, or 50:1 (50:1 = 50 parts gasoline to 1 part oil); varies by manufacturer; check manual or fuel cap.
- fact: For the mix, use unleaded gasoline min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol (the mixing steps in the Source say min 89 octane, an inconsistency), and a 2-cycle premium oil certified ISO-L-EGD and JASO M345 FD.
- step 1: Use the chart to decide how much mix to prepare.
- step 2: Remove cap from an approved gas can and add unleaded fuel.
- step 3: Add the 2-cycle oil per the chart.
- step 4: Replace cap.
- step 5: Gently shake until mixed.
- step 6: Add to the mower's tank.
- fact: Oil per gas (ounces): 50:1: 2.6 oz per 1 gal, 5.2 oz per 2 gal, 6.4 oz per 2.5 gal. 40:1: 3.2 / 6.4 / 8.0 oz. 32:1: 4.0 / 8.0 / 10.0 oz.
- tip: Manufacturer's 2-cycle oil can be used; Kawasaki 2-cycle mix comes in 5.2 oz and 6.4 oz bottles for 2 gal or 2.5 gal at 50:1; 6.4 oz bottle with 2 gal gives 40:1.
