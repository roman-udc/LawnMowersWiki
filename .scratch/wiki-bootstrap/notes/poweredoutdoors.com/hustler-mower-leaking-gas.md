## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "strong fuel odor", "puddle of gas under mower", "burnt grass trail"
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, clogged-fuel-line (cracked-fuel-line), clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, bad-gas-cap-seal
- confirm: Checklist: carburetor around bowl or near air intake; fuel lines and clamps; fuel filter; fuel pump; shut-off valve; tank seams; cap seal.
- safety: Work in well-ventilated area; gas fumes harmful; remove spark plug wire; follow operator's manual.

## page: causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat
- fact: Bowl gasket sits between carburetor and bowl (bottom), looks like a rubber band; dries out from temperature cycling near engine.
- step 1: Shut off fuel with shut-off valve or crimp the line.
- step 2: Wipe dirt from around carburetor.
- step 3: Remove screw at bottom, remove bowl (rag for remaining fuel).
- step 4: Remove old gasket.
- step 5: Fit new gasket; keep carburetor cleaner and other material off the gasket.
- step 6: Reinstall bowl, tighten screw; wipe down carburetor.
- fact: Stuck float (leak near air intake): float acts like a gatekeeper regulating fuel; old-fuel deposits stick it; fuel overflows. Carburetor must be taken apart; clean carb and float or replace float. Stuck float needle: replace; tapping side of carburetor with rubber mallet is temporary.
- tip: To order carb parts you need engine model and specs from the engine housing, not mower model/serial.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- symptom: soft or cracked filter leaking at seams (unchanged filter degraded by fuel).
- fix: Replace; don't break off soft ends when removing; install arrow in direction of fuel flow. Replace regularly.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Old fuel degrades pump plastic housing, softens, leaks at seams; replace leaking vacuum fuel pump.

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-tank
- fact: Hustler tank is high-density polyethylene; seams fail with time; replace tank.

## page: causes/bad-shutoff-valve
- fact: Prone to leaking; replace.

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-line
- fact: Lines dry with age, crack; clamps can loosen or puncture the line. Replace dry/cracked lines; convert pinch-style clamps to worm gear clamps (they don't pinch).

## page: causes/bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: Gas sloshes and leaks around cap if seal fails; leak may leave no wet spot as fuel evaporates.
- confirm: Gently rock mower back and forth to splash fuel to cap area; wet spot = replace cap.
- safety: Don't rock so far the mower flips over.

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- fact: arrow faces fuel flow.
