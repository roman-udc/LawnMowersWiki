## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Hustler mower vibrates and shakes"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade (wrong/unbalanced blades), debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: always some vibration; this is more extreme than normal
- safety: remove ignition key and spark plug wires; wait for parts to stop; follow operator's manual

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: engine mounting bolts (motor mounts) under the engine absorb much engine vibration; vibration worsens if they break, loosen or go missing
- fix: inspect; tighten loose; replace worn or missing

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: PTO clutch transfers engine power to blades; ball bearing can fail on an electric clutch and shake the mower; replace the PTO clutch

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: wrong size blades: Hustler offers deck-specific blades; aftermarket may not fit even with right length and center hole size; wrong blade can cause vibration and damage deck shell and spindle housings; author prefers OEM
- tip: if aftermarket, lay a new OEM blade on top to match center hole, length and width; manufacturer's fit claims aren't always right (author has received non-fitting aftermarket blades)
- fact: unbalanced blade (heavier on one end) wobbles and vibrates at high speed; causes: normal use (dirt wears blades unevenly), sharpening removing more metal from one end; balance with blade balancer before installing

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: wire, string, long weeds wrap around spindle and are hard to see; inspect and remove; then check spindle and housing for damage

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: gloves; spark plug wires off and key out; grab each end of blade and rock up and down; look/feel for movement, listen for knocking
- fix: if movement, remove blade and spindle housing, inspect bearing or spindle, repair or replace

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: stretched belt leaves slack, doesn't sit securely, deck vibrates; replace if stretched, cracked, or glazed; if good, check routing and tension to remove slack

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: failed bearings let pulleys sit not flat/parallel to deck; mower shakes as belt runs
- confirm: check each pulley is securely attached with no wobble; one side higher than other suggests bad bearing; rotate slowly by hand, feel for restriction, listen for bearing noise
- fix: replace bearing; some pulleys have sealed bearings so replace pulley and bearing assembly

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: idler bracket hole where spring attaches can wear larger; spring can stretch; replace worn idler bracket or spring; check bearing and bushing in engagement linkage and replace if worn

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: small debris in crevices (even a wood chip) can cause severe vibration; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat; buildup affects tensioner leaving slack, shaking, belt may come off
- tip: remove spindle covers and clean deck regularly; clean zero-turn after each mowing (also prevents corrosion from debris holding moisture)
