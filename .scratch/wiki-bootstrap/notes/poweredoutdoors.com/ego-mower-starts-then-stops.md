## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "EGO electric mower starts then stops"
- alias: "EGO mower just shuts off while mowing"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, overheated-battery, battery-not-seated, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-safety-switch, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass, bad-motor
- fact: shuts off when battery dead, battery or circuits hot, wiring loose/pinched/damaged, or safety switch bad; motor may be under extreme load from plugged deck, dull blades, fast ground speed, or wet/tall/thick grass
- safety: always remove safety key and battery before repairs; consult EGO manual
- fact: repairs/modifications may void any remaining warranty; contact EGO for warranty repairs
- pro: for bad motor or other failed components contact EGO customer service 1-855-346-5656 (mowers) or 1-877-346-9876 (zero-turns) to find a repair location

## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- fact: same causes apply (dead battery, hot battery, poor contact, safety switch)

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: check the battery first; EGO may have LED lights showing charge on the mower; on some models remove battery and put it on the external charger to read the level
- fact: a fully charged EGO battery unused for over 30 days may automatically discharge to a lower capacity for storage
- fact: battery's recoverable capacity shrinks over time, reducing mowing time; batteries go bad from age, extreme temperatures or mechanical failure
- symptom: signs of a failing battery: fully charged battery doesn't hold charge as before; charges very slowly; gets very hot
- fix: low charge: charge on external charger until full, reinstall; if won't hold charge, replace; recycle the old battery (Call2Recycle for drop-off/ship locations)

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- fact: when EGO battery or mower circuit gets excessively hot, mower shuts down as part of its safety system
- fix: let mower and battery cool before restarting; move out of direct sunlight

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- symptom: battery contacts not touching the compartment contacts, from incorrect install or dirt/debris in compartment
- fix: remove battery, inspect and brush out compartment; line battery up with guides and slide into position; on most EGO mowers you hear a click and the battery release button springs into place

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- symptom: wires loosened or pinched can short and stop the mower; common spots on EGO are where wires move and get pinched, e.g. in/near the handle where it folds for storage
- confirm: wiggle wires to find a short; multimeter also works
- fix: repair pinched connections with a wire nut; remove safety key and battery first
- safety: DIY electrical repair can void warranties; check with EGO if under warranty

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: test each switch by temporarily bypassing with a jumper wire; replace any bad switch
- safety: never operate mower without all safety switches working
- brand: EGO push mower switches: foldable handle switch; switch ensuring side rails fully extended; safety bar switch (bar must be pulled into handle to run; released = mower shuts off)
- brand: EGO zero turn switches: parking brake/pedal switch; seat switch; charging-port switch; read owner's manual for others

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: grass and debris packed under deck keeps blade from turning freely; mower shuts off to protect motor
- fix: with battery and safety key removed, check under deck and scrape off buildup; inspect shaft around blade so blade moves freely and nothing is wrapped around the shaft
- fact: regular deck scraping also protects the motor from overworking

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: dull blade alone likely won't strain the motor enough to shut it down, but combined with deck packed with clippings it magnifies the problem
- fix: remove, sharpen, reinstall; replace if gouged, extremely worn or cracked

## page: causes/cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fact: too thick, tall or wet grass puts extreme load on motor; moisture (rain, morning dew) causes buildup under deck
- fix: cut dry grass; double or triple-cut tall grass (highest setting first, then lower); slow ground speed in tall or thick grass; cut narrower sections

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: motor or other components may have failed and need diagnosis and repair; contact EGO customer service (numbers above)
