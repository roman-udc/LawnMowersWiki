## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Walker mower vibrating and shaking"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Some vibration is normal (engine, uneven lawn). Source's checklist order: engine bolts; clutch ball bearing; material on blade shaft; spindle housings; blade balance; pulley bearings; belt; idler bracket and spring; debris.
- safety: Remove spark plug wire and key before repairs; extreme caution at deck; operator's manual.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- fact: Engine mounting bolts ("motor mounts") absorb much engine vibration. Check bolts under engine are in good condition and secure; replace worn, broken, missing bolts.

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- fact: Ball bearing in an electric clutch can fail and shake the mower; replace clutch. (Also idler: check bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage; replace if worn.)

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Wrong or unbalanced blades vibrate. Author uses OEM blades. For aftermarket: blade length and center hole must match, and even then lay it on an OEM blade to confirm exact match; a blade slightly different may not seat on spindle, may hit deck shell, vibrates, poor cut.
- fact: Unbalanced = one end heavier; causes: uneven wear from dirt under deck, or too much metal removed from one end in sharpening. Balance with blade balancer before installing.

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fact: Unseen wire or string in grass can wrap the spindle. Inspect, remove, then check spindle and housing for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: Spark plug wire off, gloves; grab each blade end and rock up/down; blade should feel secure; movement or knocking = remove blade and spindle housing, inspect for bearing or spindle damage, repair.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fact: Stretched belt slack doesn't sit securely on pulleys and vibrates. Replace if stretched, cracked, glazed. If good, check routing and tension adjustment to remove slack.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: Failed bearing leaves pulley not flat/parallel to deck; belt runs on unstable pulley.
- confirm: Check each is secure with no wobble; rotate by hand; feel restriction, hear noise; replace bearing.

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: Hole in idler bracket where spring attaches wears larger; spring stretches; vibration. Replace worn bracket or spring.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Even a wood chip can cause extreme vibration. Remove spindle covers and clean deck regularly; debris under pulleys keeps them from sitting flat; debris on tensioner causes slack.
- tip: Clean after each mowing to avoid vibration and corrosion from moisture in debris.
