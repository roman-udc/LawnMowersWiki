## page: procedures/clean-the-carburetor
- symptom: bad Kawasaki carb: engine won't start; backfires from running lean; sputters and runs rough; surges; consumes too much fuel and smokes.
- confirm: with good fuel supply to carb confirmed, remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into intake, start; if it starts, runs, then dies, disassemble and clean.
- tools: pliers, screwdriver, socket/ratchet set, carburetor cleaner, thick wire, needle nose pliers.
- tip: take multiple photos before and during teardown for reassembly (many small parts).
- step 1: shut off fuel with shut-off valve at bottom of tank; if none, crimp fuel line.
- step 2: detach throttle and choke cables.
- step 3: remove hardware attaching carb to air filter housing.
- step 4: slowly remove springs from carb; do not stretch them (else replace); may need to twist carb. Don't rip gasket between engine block and carb (replace if torn).
- step 5: locate bowl on bottom; have rag for remaining fuel; remove screw at bottom of bowl and lower bowl off. Gasket around bowl looks like a rubber band.
- mistake: getting carb cleaner or other substance on the bowl gasket ruins it; must replace before reattaching.
- step 6: stem hangs down in center; holes in stem can clog with gum; use flashlight and thick wire to unclog.
- step 7: check other components and carb body for gumming and white crusty buildup; remove with carb cleaner. Note: almost impossible to remove all crusty material.
- step 8: ensure float needle and float in good order; reassemble in reverse using photos.
- step 9: use fresh fuel and let bowl fill.
- fact: fuel jet clog or stuck float are typical failures; old fuel main cause; use fresh fuel with low ethanol.
- brand: Kawasaki engine; need engine model and spec to order correct parts.

## page: procedures/rebuild-the-carburetor
- fact: if cleaning fails, small components may need replacing or old fuel damage too great; rebuild or replace. Have Kawasaki engine model and spec ready. Rebuild kit or replacement from Kawasaki small engine dealer, local equipment dealer, or online.
