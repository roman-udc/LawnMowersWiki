## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "click, click, click when I turn the key"; "SCAG won't turn over or crank"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, wet-electrical-components
- tip: Solenoid good but won't crank: check battery, cables, starter switch, safety switches. Water in the electrical system (after sitting in rain) is another reason it won't start.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Click or hum when the key is turned and the mower doesn't start; a wire gets hot and smokes or melts.
- fact: Electromagnet switch that engages the starter motor; usually mounted on the starter motor but may be closer to the battery. Every SCAG electric-start engine has one. Round or square, 3 or 4 posts; some attach at the top of the starter. Positive (+) battery wire attaches to one side; follow the positive cable from the battery to find it. Wiring schematics vary; some include lights and 12-volt ports.
- fact: Fails when the spring weakens or the copper plate corrodes; also from weak starter, bad battery or bad ground.
- confirm: Starter turns the engine when the solenoid is bypassed = solenoid likely bad.
- mistake: Replacing it before checking for loose or corroded wires or a bad ground.

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches (loose wires), needle nose pliers (if screwdriver doesn't work), battery charger (optional)
- step 1: Battery at least 12.3 volts on a voltmeter.
- step 2: Set the parking brake, mower in neutral, key to ON.
- step 3: Lay a long screwdriver across the solenoid touching the cable from the battery and the cable to the starter. A spark is common. Engine turns over = solenoid likely bad. Needle nose pliers can jump it instead.
- step 4: Before replacing, check for loose or corroded wires or bad ground.
- step 5: Bench test for a starter-mounted solenoid: remove the starter; charger negative (-) clamp to the starter case, positive (+) clamp touched to the big post and exciter wire on the solenoid.
- safety: Spark is normal; always wear safety gear. Follow the SCAG operator's manual.

## page: guides/mower-got-wet
- fact: Water in the electrical system can keep the mower from starting; Source links a "won't start after leaving in the rain" article.
