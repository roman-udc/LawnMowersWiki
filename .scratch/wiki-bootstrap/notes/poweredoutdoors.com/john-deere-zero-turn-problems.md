## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "John Deere zero turn won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: Needs air, fuel, spark; battery, starter solenoid and switches can also fail.
- fix: fresh fuel; drain old fuel and add cleaner additive (Sea Foam); replace dirty/damaged plug; clean/replace air filter; replace fuel filter; clean/replace fuel line; replace pump; clean/rebuild/replace carburetor; charge or replace battery, secure cables, remove corrosion; replace bad safety/ignition switch; test and replace solenoid.
- safety: Park on flat level surface, remove ignition key and spark plug wires before repairs; follow operator's manual.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "John Deere zero turn starts, stalls and dies"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, clogged-deck-chute, bad-ignition-coil, fouled-spark-plug
- fix: Wrong oil level: add or remove a little to the engine manufacturer's level; consult mechanic if that doesn't fix (possible extensive engine damage). Plugged deck: scrape, sharpen blades. Coil with a break in continuity: replace. Plug dark, damaged or worn: new; slightly dirty: wire brush. Fuel cap not venting: replace.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "John Deere zero turn won't move forward or reverse"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: Hydrostatic transmission pumps fluid to move wheels; pressure is so great you can't free-roll without bypassing the transmission. Check bypass levers are not bypassing; check belt, pulleys, tensioners; oil fresh and full.
- fix: Bypass levers to operating position (one lever near each drive wheel). Worn drive pump belt: replace, confirm seated on pulleys. Bad tensioner pulley: replace and grease tensioner arm. Missing/damaged idler arm spring: replace. Low/old hydraulic oil: change at John Deere intervals, keep full. Note: some transmissions are unserviceable; consult John Deere dealer.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "John Deere zero turn vibrates and shakes"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fix: Loose/missing engine mount bolts: secure/replace. Failing electric clutch: replace; manual clutch: replace worn cable, lever, bushings. Blades damaged/unbalanced: replace, balance. Foreign material on blade shaft: remove, inspect spindle and housing. Spindle bearings: replace when worn. Deck belt worn/loose: replace. Pulleys: replace if restriction by hand, or wobble from bad bearing. Debris: clean, check all crevices.
- fact: Seat suspension still lets you bounce; vibration worsens it.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- alias: "John Deere engine overheats"
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- fact: Engine oil helps keep engine cool; use air-cooled engine oil of right viscosity, high in zinc; keep oil full. Check airflow blockers: cooling fins, engine shroud, engine guard.
- fix: Wrong oil: drain and refill; use 10W-30 in most conditions, different viscosity may be needed in very warm conditions. Missing engine guard: replace; tighten loose guard. Dirty air filter: replace. Plugged deck: scrape. Dull blades: sharpen, replace worn. Overworking: slow ground speed, avoid wet grass, double or triple-cut overgrown lawn.
- mistake/note: In the Source the "Low engine oil level" and "Plugged engine cooling fins" table rows have solutions garbled (fuel tank/carburetor text, truncated); don't cite them.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "John Deere zero turn smoking"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, bad-head-gasket, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- fact: Smokes when air filter plugged, oil wrong, or bad engine gasket lets oil seep onto hot surface like the muffler. Unknown cause: worn piston ring or burnt valve.
- fix: Replace air filter; remove oil until correct; low oil means probably engine damage, technician; repair bad gasket; small engine technician for internal tests.

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: Weak hydrostatic transmission when hydraulic oil old or tank low, or drive belt/tensioner pulley worn or broken.
- fix: Replace worn pump drive belt; replace bad tensioner pulley and grease tensioner arm; change oil at regular intervals and keep full.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "John Deere zero turn won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper
- fix: Tires to manufacturer spec and equal left and right. Replace bad dampers. Tracking: adjust speed adjustment bolt so each tire rotates at same speed per owner's manual.

## page: procedures/adjust-the-tracking
- fact: Adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change tire rotation speed so both tires move at the same speed; owner's manual.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "John Deere running rough, sputters, bogs down"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, clogged-deck-chute, dull-or-bent-blade
- fix: Same list as SCAG: drain old fuel and refill with stabilizer; clear line with carb cleaner and compressed air or replace; replace filter; clean/rebuild/replace carb; replace non-venting cap; wire-brush plug, new if very dark/worn/damaged; slow down in tall, thick, wet grass; throttle at highest when engaging blades; scrape deck; sharpen blades.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "John Deere blades won't engage"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fix: Worn/cracked/glazed belt: replace. Belt falls off: replace worn idler arm, spring, bad pulleys, stretched belt. PTO switch: replace. Clutch: replace; manual clutch cable, lever, springs, linkages. Battery below 12.7 V on 12 V: charge; replace if won't hold. Safety switch: replace. Fuse: same capacity.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "John Deere zero turn leaking gas"
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: Gas may evaporate leaving no wet spots. Common places: carburetor, dry cracked lines; plastic degrades, seals dry out.
- fix: Replace gasket between carb bowl and carburetor; stuck float or float needle: clean carb, replace damaged parts; cracked line replace; tank leaking at seams replace; shut-off valve replace; weak filter replace; pump replace; gas cap seal replace (on some caps the rubber seal is sold separately).

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "John Deere clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Click suggests bad solenoid; check what affects power to solenoid: battery, cables, terminals, ground; then starter motor.
- fix: Replace damaged cables, remove terminal corrosion, secure loose ones; battery under 12.7 V charge, replace if no hold; clean ground (battery ground cable and solenoid ground cable must make good contact); a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded; test solenoid; replace bad starter motor.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "John Deere zero turn bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, dull-or-bent-blade, worn-blade-sail, blade-installed-upside-down, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: Check tire pressure first (low tire unlevels deck).
- fix: Dull blades: sharpen; replace when sails thinner than center or ends worn and rounded. Bent blade: replace. Blade installed wrong: correct hardware, sails point upward toward the deck. Bent spindle: replace, inspect housing. Belt stretched/worn/cracked/glazed: replace; pulleys: rotate by hand, replace if restriction, bearing noise or wobble. Scrape deck. Tires even left/right. Deck level side to side; sits slightly lower in front than rear; spec in operator's manual. Throttle highest when engaging blades. Slow down in thick, wet, tall grass; too fast pushes grass over instead of cutting. Operator error: overlap passes so strips aren't left; use a small push mower for ditches and steep grade changes.

## page: procedures/level-the-deck
- fact: Deck level side to side, slightly lower in front than rear; exact spec in operator's manual.
