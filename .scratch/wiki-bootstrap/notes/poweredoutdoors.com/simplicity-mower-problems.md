## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Keep mower well maintained: change filters, regular hydraulic and engine oil changes, keep clean, use fresh fuel. Problems still arise from wear on lawn tractor, riding mower, or zero-turn. Source is a cause/reason/solution table article (14 problems); no step-by-step procedures.

## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Simplicity won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-recoil-starter, incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Needs gas, air, spark. Starting steps: set parking brake and make sure PTO switch is off. Fixes: fill tank; drain old fuel, fresh plus Sea Foam; thin wire to clear cap vent, replace cap; replace plug, gap to spec; clean/replace air filter; replace fuel filter; replace failed or leaking pump; carb cleaner and compressed air on lines; clean carburetor components, rebuild or replace; charge/replace battery, tighten, clean terminals; test/replace safety and ignition switches; recoil: restring or replace parts, else replace assembly.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Simplicity loses power and dies"
- causes-named: wrong-choke-setting, old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, bad-ignition-coil, fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Choke must be moved to off once engine warms or it runs sluggish and dies (not enough air). Fuel pump moves gas to the carb when the carb sits higher than the tank. Too little oil: friction/heat; too much: crankcase pressure; correct level, mechanic if no help. Coil can separate and short out when hot; replace coil with a break in continuity.

## page: problems/engine-dies-when-hot
- alias: "Simplicity quits when hot"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, old-fuel, dirty-carburetor, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-ignition-coil, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- fact: Wrong oil type or viscosity makes engine run hot: use air-cooled engine oil like the oils provided by Kawasaki (viscosity chart in the author's overheating article). Old fuel: drain; fresh gas with fuel system cleaner and stabilizers like Sea Foam or STA-BIL. Carb residue clogs fuel jet or internal parts: remove, clean with carb cleaner, rebuild or replace. Cooling fins: clean, replace broken, clean around block and under shroud. Coil: ohmmeter, replace if break in continuity. Deck: scrape, avoid wet grass. Internal (piston ring, valve train, gasket): small engine shop.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Simplicity smokes"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-head-gasket
- fact: Smoke when oil burns off a hot surface, engine overheats, or internal damage. Too much oil: drain to manufacturer level. Low oil: shop; adding oil often too late. Internal problems: shop.

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "Simplicity won't move" / "weak hydrostatic transmission"
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, worn-drive-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system, bad-hydrostatic-transmission
- fact: Simplicity lawn tractors and zero-turns use hydrostatic transmissions. Drive release lever (zero turn) must be in operating position; riding mowers have a bypass rod that must be disengaged. Worn/fallen drive pump belt: replace, route around pulleys; bad tensioner pulley bearing: replace and grease tensioner arm; missing idler arm spring: replace; low/old hydraulic oil: change at Simplicity intervals, keep full; hot fluid can signal transmission damage - dealer if fine cold but weak hot; air: bleed after fluid change.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "Simplicity won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper, loose-steering-linkage
- fact: Unequal tire pressure; tires each side need equal pressure to manufacturer spec. Bad dampers (zero turn) jerk to one side - replace. Tracking: one tire faster; adjust speed adjustment bolt, see owner's manual. Riding mower: worn bushings, washers and gears can stop it steering one way; inspect and replace steering parts.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "Simplicity vibrating / shaking"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Clutch: bearing failure/falling out causes vibration; manual engagement clutch can have bushing and linkage failures; replace clutch. String/long weeds on blade shaft: remove, check spindle. Spindle housing bearings: replace bearing or whole housing. Debris: stones, acorns, woodchips lodged under pulleys etc. Balance blades before installing.

## page: problems/engine-runs-rough
- alias: "Simplicity running rough / sputtering / bogs down"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, mowing-too-fast, wrong-engine-speed, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade
- fact: Choke must be open once warm; check choke lever, cable and plate. Fast ground speed in thick/tall/wet grass: slow down. Low engine speed: throttle highest when engaging blades. Dull blades: remove, sharpen, balance before reinstalling. Fuel line: replace if cracks.

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "Simplicity blades won't engage"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, belt-guard-or-keeper-missing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse
- fact: Electric clutch models: battery and PTO switch; manual engagement clutch models: cable and linkages (source says "choke cable" - appears to be a slip for clutch cable). Belt worn, cracked or shiny: replace. Belt off: worn idler arm and spring, stretched belt, pulleys not parallel to deck; route correctly. Weak battery under 12.7 V on a 12 V: charge. Seat switch not sensing operator blocks blade engagement. Blown fuse: same amperage; mechanic if repeated (the source table says "Replace a bad battery" for the safety switch row, an apparent error; fix is replace the switch).

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- alias: "Simplicity leaking gas"
- causes-named: bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, dirty-carburetor, cracked-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-gas-cap-seal, cracked-primer-bulb
- tip: Follow the fuel line out of the tank and check each component; if nothing, check gas cap seal. Hard to find after residue evaporates.
- fact: Carb bowl gasket dries and fails to seal; stuck float or float needle from old gas overflows - clean carb, replace damaged parts; poly tanks degrade and leak at seams - replace (patch if none available); fuel filter plastic degrades or housing cracks; pump weak at seams; primer bulb cracked or line loose behind it.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Simplicity clicks and won't turn over"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-motor
- fact: Click/hum: mower not getting power to starter; likely solenoid but check what feeds it. Loose or corroded cables/terminals: clean and secure. Weak battery: trickle charger if needed; replace if it won't hold charge. Ground: battery ground cable and solenoid ground cable must be clean and make good contact (3-post solenoid is self-grounded). Replace bad starter motor.

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Simplicity bad or uneven cut"
- causes-named: worn-blade-sail, dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: Start with tire pressures, then deck. Mower may be working as designed; slow down for conditions. Table also lists operator error.

## page: problems/engine-overheats
- alias: "Simplicity overheats"
- causes-named: wrong-oil-or-low-oil, dirty-cooling-fins, bad-cooling-shroud, clogged-air-filter, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, dull-or-bent-blade, mowing-too-fast
- safety: If the engine smells hot, stop running it right away.
- fact: Wrong oil for ambient temperature or not designed for air-cooled engines: drain and refill air-cooled oil of suitable viscosity. Low oil: change/refill; mechanic for damage. Engine guard shields engine and keeps air circulating: securely attach it. Overworking: slow down, avoid wet grass, double or triple-cut an overgrown lawn. Sharpen and balance blades.
