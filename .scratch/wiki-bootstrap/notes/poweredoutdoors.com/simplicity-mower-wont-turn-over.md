## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Simplicity mower won't turn over or crank"
- symptom: starter motor isn't getting power to spin the engine
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: disconnect the negative (black) battery cable before electrical repairs; follow operator's manual

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- tip: keep fully charged, especially in storage; a not-fully-charged battery stored in cold weather can freeze and be damaged
- fix: replace if won't hold a charge (dealer, hardware or auto store; bring old battery to avoid a core fee)

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- safety: wear eye/skin protection
- step 1: access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed); don't remove battery from casing
- step 2: positive (red, plus) cable on positive terminal first
- step 3: negative (black) cable on negative terminal
- step 4: touch nothing without a rubber coating
- step 5: set charger to 12 volts; start at 2 amps, up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge is best

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: vibration loosens wires; corrosion breaks continuity
- fix: remove battery cables first then corroded components; clean with wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); replace terminals if too corroded

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- confirm: multimeter probes on each fuse prong, resistance: near 0 good, infinity bad
- fix: same amperage; repeated blowing: dealer/repair shop for root cause

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: nothing happens when key turned
- confirm: multimeter resistance, B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid) prongs, key at start; near 0 ohms good, infinite bad

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system
- confirm: multimeter, or temporary bypass for troubleshooting only
- safety: never operate without safety switch; never run with it bypassed

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: weak spring or corroded copper plate; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also make it fail
- confirm: battery fully charged first; then diagnostic steps in author's solenoid article

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid; remove and test
- pro: starter/alternator repair shop to test and rebuild before buying a pricey new starter
