- fact: Needs sufficient fuel, air, spark. 16 reasons. Safety: owner's manual.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: Easy to overlook; may also come from an unnoticed fuel leak. Fill with fresh gas, 87+ octane, ethanol not above 10%.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Less effective as quickly as 30 days. Ethanol-moisture separates and sinks, runs hot (engine damage), corrodes fuel system, evaporates leaving gummy residue and blockages.
- tip: Buy from a busy gas station and use within 30 days; else Sea Foam Motor Treatment to stabilize.
- fix: Siphon out old fuel; add fresh.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: Runs without cap, dies with cap after a while.
- fix: Replace cap (online or Simplicity dealer).

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Carbon buildup, burnt electrodes, cracked porcelain = misfire.
- fix: Wire-brush clean; replace if dark, broken porcelain, burnt electrodes; gap to spec; secure wires.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Starves engine; may also overheat if it does start.
- step (paper): remove carefully without dropping dirt in intake; wipe housing with clean dry cloth; tap on hard surface; do not use air compressor; hold to light; reuse if light shows; otherwise replace.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Needed when tank sits below carburetor; old fuel deteriorates it.
- confirm: Inspect vacuum pump for cracks or fuel outside pump; use shut-off valve or clamps to stop/start flow; verify fuel reaches pump, then steady or pulsating flow out of pump.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- confirm: Fuel running in must run out; if not, replace. Arrow on housing points in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fix: Remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air until clear of dirt and gummy residue; or replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: Crusty/gummy deposits from old ethanol fuel; runs rough or won't start.
- fix: Clean if mechanical, else repair shop; replace if very bad.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter ~12.7 V; charge if lower; replace if it won't hold charge. Loose cables, corroded terminals also cause starting problems.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Operator presence control: kills engine when operator leaves seat; faulty switch may not sense operator.
- confirm: Temporary bypass to identify; never operate without; replace.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fact: Key turned, nothing. Test with multimeter; replace if bad.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: After good plug verified, ohm meter continuity; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; hot, smoking or melting wire on solenoid.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Contributes to weak battery. Bad stator or alternator among possibilities.
- pro: Small engine mechanic to identify; avoid guessing parts.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- brand: Simplicity safety features require procedure including setting parking brake. Choke: restricts airflow for cold start; must be moved off once warm or engine shuts down.
- fix: Refer to Simplicity operating manual.
