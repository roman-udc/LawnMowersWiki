## page: problems/mower-is-noisy
- alias: "my lawn mower is really loud"
- alias: "rattling or grinding noise from the mower"
- alias: "clunky noise from under the deck"
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, clogged-muffler-or-spark-arrestor, engine-mount-loose, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: mowers are loud by design: low to the ground, hit every bump, blades spin fast. How well the mower is maintained is one of the biggest factors in how loud it gets.
- tip: parts to check in a tune-up when extremely loud: blades, deck spindles, belts, pulleys, rollers, air filter, fuel caps, pull cords.
- tip: lubricate moving parts with WD-40 or similar to cut squeaking/squealing; lubricating bearings especially reduces noise and bearing replacement.
- fact: some low-cost mowers have poorly built engines that are loud regardless (opinion; "buy a better engine" is buying advice, skip).
- fact: article says many mowers last 7-10 years (hedged, not true for all makers); if extremely loud after everything tried, replacement may be due.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: damaged, bent or loose blades are louder: rattling of loose blades or metal-on-metal grinding (problems/mower-is-noisy).
- fix: replace the blades for a quieter cut. Does not help rocks/debris hitting blade and deck.

## page: causes/clogged-muffler-or-spark-arrestor
- symptom: (damaged muffler, not clogged) metal muffler cracks/breaks from weathering or corrosion; noise breaks through the steel and the muffler is loud (problems/mower-is-noisy).
- symptom: bafflers inside the muffler can break off and bounce around, causing a metal rattling sound.
- fix: repair or replace the muffler.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: terrain bounce works parts loose so they rattle (problems/mower-is-noisy). Article says loose body parts generally, not the engine mount specifically.
- fix: quick tune-up; make sure all pieces and parts are in the proper places.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- symptom: overdue oil change is one of the most common reasons for a loud engine; when oil gets too thin the engine gets loud (problems/mower-is-noisy).
- fact: engine fan and valve lash are also large culprits; many mechanics recommend regular valve lash adjustment to keep the mower quieter.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: too much grass and debris caught under belts and pulleys causes very loud clunky noises (problems/mower-is-noisy).
- tip: clean under the deck frequently, especially with moist or unusually long grass.
