## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Honda lawn mowers (4-cycle) use unleaded gasoline, octane 87 or higher, ethanol no greater than 10 percent; often sold as regular or E10.
- fact: pump handle color differs between stations; read the decals on the pump.
- fact: article covers 4-cycle engines; some Honda mowers have 2-cycle engines requiring gas and oil mix (see separate article).
- fact: never use fuel with more than 10% ethanol. E15, E30, E85 are 15%, 30%, 85% ethanol.
- fact: ethanol is corn-based, attracts moisture from air, is corrosive to the fuel system and leaves gummy residue; as gasoline breaks down the water/fuel mixture separates and sinks to the bottom of the tank; running it can overheat the engine and cause extensive damage.
- fact: ethanol-free options: recreation fuel (REC-90) at select stations, up to $3.00/gallon more than regular (puregas.org lists stations in US and Canada); canisters of 4-cycle fuel at hardware stores (most expensive, convenient for small mowers/handhelds).
- fact: old fuel causes fuel restrictions and leaking due to degrading fuel components.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: fuel can begin to break down as soon as 30 days after purchase.
- tip: buy at busy well-known stations; buy only what you can use within 30 days; add stabilizer to fuel not used within 30 days; store in dry cool place away from outdoor elements.
- fact: author adds Sea Foam Motor Treatment to all gas; says it stabilizes fuel up to two years, minimizes moisture, cleans fuel system; petroleum-based, safe in Honda mower (author's opinion/product claim).
