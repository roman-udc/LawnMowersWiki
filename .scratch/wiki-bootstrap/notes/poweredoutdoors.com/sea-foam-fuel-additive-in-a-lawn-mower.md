## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Stale gasoline is a big culprit for mowers not starting after sitting; gasoline needs to be used within 30 days unless a stabilizer is added.
- fact: Sea Foam is usable in any gas or diesel engine and fuel system; petroleum-based without harsh chemicals; on the market over 70 years.
- fact: Claimed benefits: reduces moisture in fuel; stabilizes fuel up to two years; cleans carburetor (liquifying deposits and residues on needles, seats, float bowls); improves performance; cleans intake valves; extends engine life.
- fact: Reasons to use an additive: (1) valve seat erosion from built-up particulates, (2) ethanol's corrosive effect on metals (iron, steel), leaving salt deposits that can clog the fuel pump, (3) moisture in the fuel system.
- fact: Gasoline leaves a gummy corrosive residue causing blockages; worse with long sitting such as winter.
- fact: Dosage: a 16-ounce can treats up to 16 gallons. Fill a 1-gallon can with 1 gallon of gas, then add 2 oz per gallon when cleaning the fuel tank (more than 2 oz is okay; more cleans better) or 1 oz per gallon for regular fuel maintenance. Author uses it in every tank.
- fact: Fuel system cleaners remove dirt and debris that accumulate; easiest alternative to disassembling the engine or a pro cleaning.
- mistake: Do not use additives targeting diesel engines (Diesel System Cleaner, Diesel Emissions Reducer) in a gas mower.
- fact: Alternatives: Archoil AR 6200 (mainly for diesel, usable with gas; stabilizer and detergent); Lucas Safeguard Ethanol Fuel Conditioner (older product; lubricant and detergent for ethanol deposits); STA-BIL Original (older; stabilizer up to two years, helps prevent corrosion and remove water, but effectiveness has waned against ethanol fuels compared to Sea Foam, author's opinion).

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Ethanol in modern gasoline is corrosive and leaves salt deposits that can clog the fuel pump.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Stale gas after sitting is a leading cause of no-start; stabilizer extends life.
