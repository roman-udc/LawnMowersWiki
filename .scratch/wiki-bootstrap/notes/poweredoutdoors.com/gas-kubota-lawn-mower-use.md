## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- brand: Gas Kubota mowers (riding, zero-turn, stand-on; all four-stroke) use unleaded gasoline, octane 87 or higher, ethanol no more than 10%. Read the labels on the pump. Kubota Tractor Corporation also offers diesel mowers; this article covers gas only (check the owner's manual).
- fact: Gas begins to break down as soon as 30 days after purchase; stabilize fuel stored longer than 30 days; store unused gas in a dry location away from combustibles. Gas left in a plastic container for long periods becomes unstable.
- fact: Ethanol (corn-based) is corrosive to the fuel system and degrades plastics/components. Old fuel leaves sticky gummy residue blocking filter, fuel lines and carburetor, so the engine runs sluggish or not at all.
- fact: Ethanol fuels attract moisture; when fuel breaks down the ethanol-water mix separates and sinks to the bottom of the tank; running it can make the engine run very hot and overheat, causing engine damage.
- fact: Ethanol-free fuel (recreation fuel, REC-90) is better than 10% ethanol fuel; more costly, especially in canisters; fuel stations are cheaper; up to $3.00 more per gallon (as stated).
- opinion: Author uses high-quality 10% ethanol gas plus Sea Foam Motor Treatment in each tank (prevents gumming, reduces moisture, stabilizes fuel for up to two years, cleans fuel system); use in the tank, for stored excess fuel and for off-season storage with fuel in the mower.
- tip: Buy fresh fuel, use a stabilizer, avoid high ethanol percentages.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: 30 day fuel life; Sea Foam stabilizes up to two years per the author; store in dry place.
