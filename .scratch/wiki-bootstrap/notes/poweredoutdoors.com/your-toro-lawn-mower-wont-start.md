## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Toro lawn mower won't start"
- alias: "Toro won't start after running out of gas"
- fact: lack of fuel, air or spark. Remove spark plug wires before repairs.
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, wrong-choke-setting, incorrect-starting-procedure, old-fuel, wrong-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, bad-battery, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-recoil-starter, low-compression, faulty-charging-system
- confirm: FUEL test: check fuel shut-off valve is open (often closed for storage/transport); remove air filter cover and filter; spray carburetor cleaner into air intake (author prefers over starter fluid); try to start. If it tries to start or starts: fuel system problem (most common: dirty carburetor, clogged fuel filter, clogged fuel lines; fuel pump if used). If it doesn't try: most likely spark plug problem.
- confirm: AIR test: remove air filter cover and filter; inspect filter (clogged restricts air; clean or replace). Inspect choke plate with filter removed. Manual choke: lever ON = plate closed; lever OFF = plate open (on some models off means fast throttle position). Auto-choke (newer Toro push mowers, thermostat-controlled): plate closed when cold, opens as engine warms. If not operating correctly check choke linkage and cable; loosen stuck choke by lubricating linkages and choke shaft with carburetor cleaner; replace bad choke cable.
- confirm: SPARK test: remove plug boot; attach spark plug tester to the ignition wire; other end to the plug (plug installed); crank with pull cord or electric start; glow in transparent section = spark. No glow: bad plug or bad ignition coil; on electric start models also battery, wiring, starter solenoid.
- fact: cold engine must run rich: more fuel, less air.
- fact: FAQ: first things to check: spark plug, air filter, carburetor, old gas residue. After running out of gas, mower won't start when air trapped in fuel lines or carburetor clogged from sediment from tank bottom. Old gas leaves residue, carburetor failure, clogged lines and filter. Bad solenoid clicks/hums on key turn and wiring may get hot and smoke.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fact: also check for fuel leak or faulty gauge. Fix: add fresh gas.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Toro has safety features requiring steps (safety switches, choke position).
- brand: Toro push mowers with choke lever: full choke for cold start, squeeze bail lever against handle and pull rope; once warm adjust to closed (off) position. Auto choke: squeeze bail and pull rope.
- brand: Toro riding/zero-turn: sit on seat; PTO switch off; parking brake on; neutral; choke lever full choke (closed) for cold engine; turn key; move choke to open once warm.
- fix: see operating manual; procedures vary slightly by model.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: choke restricts airflow so cold engine runs rich; set to off once warm or the engine stops.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Toro uses regular unleaded with no more than 10% ethanol (less is better), octane 87 or greater; 87 usually sold as regular. Check pump label. Wrong gas can prevent start and cause significant damage.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas lasts about 30 days before breaking down; ethanol collects moisture, evaporates leaving gummy substance; ethanol can degrade fuel system parts.
- fix: drain and flush the fuel tank; add fresh fuel with stabilizer/cleaner like Sea Foam or STA-BIL.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: small hole in cap lets tank vent; clogged vent forms vacuum that starves fuel.
- confirm: remove cap, start mower and let it run (don't let debris in tank); replace cap and if it sputters out/shuts off, cap is likely bad.
- fix: replace cap (cleaning may be tried).

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: excessive carbon and oil buildup, wrong gap, poor connection cause misfire. Fix: inspect for carbon or cracked porcelain, replace, gap correctly.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: plugged filter can also cause overheating and internal damage.
- step: paper: remove cover and filter; wipe housing and cover with clean dry cloth without letting dirt into intake; tap filter on solid surface; hold to light, good light = reuse, else replace; reinstall, reattach cover.
- step: foam: same removal; replace if very dark, brittle or torn; wash in water and mild detergent; rinse until clear; squeeze (don't wring); let dry; coat completely with clean engine oil and squeeze out excess; install.
- mistake: NEVER apply oil to a foam pre-filter (wraps around a paper element); damages paper filter.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: gas sitting degrades plastic pump components so it no longer holds pressure; may leak at seams; usually must test.
- confirm: start/stop flow with valve or clamps; check flow at hose to pump inlet; then disconnect hose from carburetor into container, start mower, watch for constant or pulsating flow. Replace if not.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: remove hose end into container, start/stop flow with valve or clamps per section.
- fix: remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air; replace if can't clear or dry/cracked (online or hardware store).

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fix: take apart and clean with carb cleaner including float bowl and needle; replace if too dirty.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter about 12.7 V; charge if lower; replace if no hold.
- fix: clean corroded terminals with 2 cups water and 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda, wire brush.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: safety switches prevent deck running without operator, and starting without parking brake engaged.
- confirm: multimeter or temporary bypass; never run bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: multimeter; replace.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: after plug is OK, check continuity with multimeter; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click/hum when key turned; wire hot, smoke, melt.

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- symptom: Toro push mower pull rope won't start it; bad pulley, loose/missing spring, broken clips.
- fix: replace spring and restring; replace recoil assembly if other parts damaged.

## page: causes/low-compression
- fact: listed as item 16 with no content in the Source.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: contributes to weak battery; bad stator or alternator, other electrical parts.
- pro: Toro dealership; otherwise replacing parts guesswork; parts stores usually don't accept electrical part returns.
