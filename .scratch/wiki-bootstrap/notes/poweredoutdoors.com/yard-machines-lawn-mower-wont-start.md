- safety: Follow owner's manual.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: Fill with fresh gas, 87+ octane, 10% or less ethanol. 2-cycle engines need 2-cycle premium oil added.

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- brand: Yard Machines requires unleaded min 87 octane, max 10% ethanol. 4-cycle engine: separate fill ports for oil and gasoline; straight unleaded in fuel tank. 2-cycle engine: one fill port for gas/oil mix. Gas-to-oil mix 40:1 for mowers manufactured after 2002; 32:1 before that. Never run straight gas in a 2-cycle (likely ruins engine); 2-cycle oil differs from engine oil. If unsure of engine type, consult manual.
- fact: Ethanol-water mixture separates and burns hot, can damage engine.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Breaks down causing corrosion and restrictions; consume within 30 days or add stabilizer. Ethanol: gummy deposits, corrosive.
- fix: Siphon old fuel out; add fresh with additive such as Sea Foam Motor Treatment (stabilize, clean, reduce moisture).

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: Dirty, damaged or wet plug: no-start or sluggish. Also wrong gap or loose wire.
- fix: Inspect for carbon, dark tip, burnt electrode, broken porcelain; replace if damaged or very dirty; if only slightly dirty, wire brush. Author prefers replacing with new (inexpensive). Gap to spec; secure wire.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Plugged filter causes start/run issues and overheating. Replace annually; clean several times a season.
- step (paper): remove housing cover; remove filter without dropping dirt in intake; wipe housing; tap plastic or rubber part on hard surface; hold to light; reuse if light visible; replace if no light or oil-covered; install; reattach cover.
- step (foam): remove cover and filter; wipe housing; reuse only if not brittle, no dark spots, no tears; wash in mild dish soap and water, rinse until clear; lay flat to dry; coat with foam filter oil when fully dry; reinstall and cover.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Inline between fuel lines. Replace if no flow; arrow points in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: Shut off fuel at shut-off valve (or hose pinch pliers); remove line end furthest from tank into container placed lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill); start flow; weak flow = clog.
- fix: Shut off fuel, remove hose section; spray carburetor cleaner; blow compressed air; repeat; reinstall. If not cleared, replace with fuel line of same diameter and length.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Needed when tank sits lower than carburetor; not all mowers have one. Old fuel degrades components.
- confirm: Inspect vacuum fuel pump for cracks or fuel outside pump (replace). Verify fuel reaches pump using shut-off valve or pinch pliers; check steady or pulsating flow out of pump.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: Crusty/gummy deposits from old ethanol fuel; engine runs rough or won't start.
- fix: Clean if mechanical, else repair shop; replace if very bad.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: Runs and starts without cap but dies after running with cap on = vent problem.
- fix: Replace with a new Yard Machines fuel cap.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: Riding mowers use battery; push/self-propelled electric-start models use a small battery. Loose cables, corroded terminals.
- confirm: Multimeter ~12.7 V; charge if lower; replace if it won't hold charge.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: Operator presence control: kills engine when operator leaves seat. Faulty switch may not sense operator in seat (riding) or the safety bar depressed (walk-behind).
- confirm: Temporarily bypass to identify. Don't operate without it. Replace bad switch.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fact: Some models have key switch; key turned and nothing. Test with multimeter; replace.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: After good plug verified, ohm meter continuity of coil; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum on key turn; wire on solenoid hot, smoking or melting.
- fact: Electromagnetic switch actuating starter.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Not main cause but contributes to weak battery; riding mowers. Bad stator or alternator among possible parts; test with ohmmeter.
- pro: Small engine mechanic to identify the failing part (avoid parts guessing).

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- fact: Push mower without electric start: bad pulley, loose/missing spring, broken clips.
- fix: Replace spring and restring; if clips or pulley damaged, replace the recoil assembly.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: Safety features prevent starting unless procedure followed (set parking brake on riding models).

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: Choke restricts airflow, more fuel into cylinder for cold start; must be adjusted off when warm or engine shuts down.
