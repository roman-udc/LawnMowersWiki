## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "Spartan mower smoking white, blue or black smoke"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- safety: Shut off mower immediately and don't keep running it. Remove ignition key and spark plug wire; wait for engine to cool and moving parts to stop.
- fact: Blue/white smoke = excess oil burning (damaged piston rings, valve train, bad gasket, or oil pushed into cylinder by plugged air filter). Black smoke = excess fuel; plugged air filter makes rich mixture; if not filter, look for other air restriction.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: Never run without a filter, even briefly (dirt damages cylinder and engine seals). Start each season with new one, clean several times a season; check before running. Replace if extremely dirty, covered in oil or damaged.
- fact: Why it smokes: rich running -> black smoke; if fully restricted, engine draws air from crankcase and pulls oil that burns in cylinder. A smoking mower can plug the filter further.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- step 1: Remove filter from housing.
- step 2: Wipe housing with dry clean cloth; keep dirt out of the intake.
- step 3: Tap filter on a solid surface.
- step 4: Hold to light; reuse if light shows through; otherwise replace (also if oil-covered, very dirty, damaged).
- step 5: Install and attach cover.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: Oil level must be at "full" line on dipstick. Low oil: friction, heat, oil burns, internal parts can melt, smoke. Changing oil and refilling probably won't fix since engine was likely damaged.
- pro: Spartan dealership or trustworthy small engine mechanic.
- tip: Check engine oil before each mowing.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: Raises crankcase pressure, pushes oil into cylinder and into air intake through valve train; burns, smoke.
- fix: Remove a little oil via drain plug or oil filter, or with an oil evacuator or turkey baster from fill area; recheck dipstick; repeat until "full". Check air filter for oil/plugging; replace if so.

## page: procedures/drain-excess-oil
- step 1: Remove a little oil: drain plug, oil filter, or oil evacuator / turkey baster through fill opening.
- step 2: Recheck dipstick; add or remove until at "full".
- step 3: Check air filter for oil or plugging; replace if so.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- fact: Bad gasket leaks oil onto hot surface like muffler; burns and smokes. Replace; difficulty varies by location.

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- confirm: Remove spark plug(s) and look for oil; indicates piston ring or valve train problem but not which. Exact diagnosis needs engine teardown with leak-down and compression checks.
- fact: Piston ring or scored cylinder wall lets oil into combustion chamber.
- pro: Small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/bad-valve-train
- fact: Overheated mower can develop a burnt valve; only found by removing cylinder head and leak-down test; mechanic cuts seat and valve to complete the combustion chamber.
