## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- note: Source section "Won't Start" (mixes crank/no-crank causes)
- symptom: fuel or air restriction from plugged air filter, clogged fuel system, failing fuel components; electrical: dead battery, faulty charging system, dirty spark plug, bad switches, bad connections
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: fill with fresh fuel

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: fuel breaks down over time, less efficient and prone to clog the fuel system (and cause engine damage)
- fix: drain tank and fill with fresh fuel; use a stabilizer/additive like Sea Foam to stabilize and help clean the fuel system

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: clogged vent makes a vacuum in the tank restricting fuel flow
- fix: clear the vent with a thin piece of wire; replace cap if needed
- fix: replace a cap that no longer vents (starts-then-dies section)

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: excessively dirty or broken plug, bad connection, not gapped correctly; causes starting trouble and, when dirty, intermittent running (starts-then-dies)
- fix: replace plug, secure connections, gap to manufacturer spec; or clean/replace, make sure plug wire securely attached and gap correct

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: dirt and grass plug filter, blocking airflow; also cited for no-start, starts-then-dies and smoking
- fix: remove and clean; replace if in bad condition

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: replace the fuel filter (dirty filter restricts fuel flow)

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- symptom: failed or leaking pump no longer creates pressure to pump fuel; also leaks at seams
- fix: replace pump

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom: bad fuel deposits and dirt build up and block lines
- fix: carb cleaner and compressed air to clear the clog; replace line if necessary

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: carb dirty/clogged from bad fuel and ethanol deposits, preventing fuel flow
- fix: clean all carb components; replace if needed (cites separate article how-to-clean-your-riding-mower-carburetor)
- symptom (leaking gas section): carb collects buildup from old gas causing blockages or part failures; clean and replace damaged parts

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fix: charge battery; replace if it won't hold a charge (also weak battery won't give clutch solenoid the power to engage blades)

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: make sure cables are tight; clean corrosion from terminals

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- symptom: safety switches prevent starting in certain situations; failed switch means no start; a bad safety switch may also stop blades engaging
- fix: test and replace faulty switch

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fix: test and replace bad switch (no start when ignition switch or coil is bad)

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: coil windings can separate and short out
- confirm: check for a break in continuity with an ohm meter
- fix: replace if found bad

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- symptom: pull-start mower with damaged/broken recoil can't start the engine
- fix: see if recoil can be restrung or broken parts (e.g. pulley) replaced; replace assembly if needed

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "riding mower dies while mowing"
- symptom: mower suddenly dies mid-mow; old fuel clogs components; overheating (plugged air filter, wrong oil, wrong level, plugged deck); faulty charging system not keeping battery charged; dirty plug; bad coil
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, bad-gas-cap-seal, clogged-deck-chute, faulty-charging-system, wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: engine must be kept cool or it will die

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- symptom: fins packed with dirt, oil, grass so air can't cool the engine block
- fix: replace broken fins, clean fins

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: too much oil increases crankcase pressure, engine overheats and dies; also (smoking) pressure forces oil into the cylinder through the valve train
- fix: remove a little oil until at manufacturer recommended level

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- symptom: choke is for cold starts; if it continues to restrict air after warm-up the engine shuts off
- fix: make sure choke is in correct position

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- symptom: clogged mower deck strains the engine, may shut it down
- fix: periodically scrape the deck clean

## page: causes/bad-gas-cap-seal
- symptom: seal fails so gas splashes out around the cap (leaking gas section)
- fix: replace gas cap

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- symptom: smoke may be fixed by new air filter, correcting oil level or replacing a gasket causing an oil leak; otherwise internal problem needing tests by a small engine mechanic
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train, bad-head-gasket
- fix: low oil: friction creates extreme heat; take to a small engine shop to check damage; adding oil is often too late
- pro: internal problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) need diagnosis by small engine repair shop

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "riding mower won't go forward"
- symptom: drive levers on hydrostatic mowers not in correct position; bad tensioner, missing idler arm spring, worn belt; old hydraulic fluid or air in system makes it slow or not move
- causes-named: bypass-valve-open, missing-axle-key, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, worn-drive-belt, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: article links "9 Reasons Your Mower Won't Move or Go Forward"

## page: causes/bypass-valve-open
- symptom: drive release lever (lets hydrostatic mowers roll freely) not in operating position
- fix: put lever in the operating position

## page: causes/missing-axle-key
- symptom: small key in axle may have fallen out of place
- fix: replace the key

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: bearing in tensioner pulley fails; tensioner pulley fails to keep tension on drive belt; bad pulley bearing can seize or wobble so belt rolls off
- fix: replace tensioner pulley (and grease the tensioner arm); replace bad bearing or pulley assembly

## page: causes/missing-idler-arm-spring
- symptom: broken/missing spring can no longer put tension on the drive belt
- fix: replace spring

## page: causes/worn-drive-belt
- symptom: worn belt slips on pulley, mower sluggish; belt can break or fall off so mower won't move; weak hydrostatic transmission feel
- fix: replace frayed, cracked or worn belt

## page: causes/low-hydraulic-oil
- symptom: low/old hydraulic oil fails to lubricate, system seems weak
- fix: change hydraulic oil at manufacturer intervals; check level periodically
- brand: some transmissions are sealed and can't be serviced; see dealer

## page: causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid
- symptom: mower runs fine cold but becomes weak when hydraulic system heats up; hot fluid can indicate hydrostatic transmission damage
- fix: let fluid cool; have the dealer check it
- pro: manufacturer dealership

## page: causes/air-in-hydraulic-system
- symptom: air must be bled after changing fluid or mower can't move at normal speed
- fix: follow owner's manual procedure for changing fluid and bleeding air

## page: causes/bad-hydrostatic-transmission
- symptom: weak transmission can come from bad drive belt, bad tensioner pulley, low/old hydraulic oil (section "Hydrostatic Transmission is Weak")

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "riding mower won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, loose-steering-linkage
- fact: unequal tire pressure pulls mower to one side; keep to manufacturer spec with equal pressure each side
- fact: worn bushings, washers, gears stop the mower steering one way or the other; inspect and replace failed steering parts

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fix: check tire pressure, fill to manufacturer's spec, equal on each side

## page: causes/loose-steering-linkage
- symptom: worn bushings, washers, gears in steering
- fix: inspect, replace failed parts

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- tip: check for extra debris; author found surprising little things stuck causing big vibration
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: engine bolts reduce vibration; loose or missing ones cause vibration
- fix: secure and replace missing bolts

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- symptom: bearing failure/bearing falling out of clutch causes vibration; manual-engagement clutch can have failing bushing and linkages
- fix: replace the clutch
- symptom: bad clutch no longer transfers power from engine to blades (blades won't engage)

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: damaged/unbalanced blades send vibration through the mower; worn or bent blade also in uneven-cut list
- fix: replace damaged blades; blades must be balanced before installing

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- symptom: string or long weeds wrapped around blade shaft cause vibration
- fix: remove all debris; check spindle for damage

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- fix: replace bearing in spindle housing or replace whole spindle housing assembly

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- note: listed as a table of causes only
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: also lists "blade installed wrong", "operator error"
- fact: article links "Reasons Your Riding Mower is Cutting Uneven"

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- fact: fuel evaporates so leak is hard to find unless seen dripping or wet spot
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fix: old fuel filter plugged: replace; pump wears and leaks at seams: replace; metal tank can rust through, poly tank leaks at seams: replace tank (a rust hole can sometimes be repaired if no replacement tank is available); metal and plastic shut-off valves prone to leaking: replace; rubber fuel lines dry and crack: replace

## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "engage the PTO and nothing happens"
- symptom: worn deck components, bad PTO or safety switch, weak battery, bad clutch
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-pto-switch, bad-pto-clutch, bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-safety-switch, blown-fuse

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- symptom: spring holds idler pulleys in line; can wear or fall off
- fix: replace worn/missing spring; replace worn tensioner bracket/arm

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- symptom: switch fails to let battery voltage power the clutch
- fix: replace

## page: causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage
- symptom: manual clutch: worn cable, clutch lever or linkages fail to engage clutch
- fix: replace worn items

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: replace with same capacity fuse; if it keeps blowing consult a mechanic to find root cause
- pro: repeated blown fuses

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: worn deck belt won't grip pulleys to turn blades
- fix: replace
