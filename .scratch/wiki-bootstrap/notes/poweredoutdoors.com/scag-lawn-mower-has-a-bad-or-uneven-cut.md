## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Scag bad cut"
- alias: "Scag uneven cut, strips of grass left"
- alias: "grass brown a couple of days after mowing"

- safety: Remove the spark plug boot and wait for all parts to stop moving; follow operator's manual.
- tip: Check tire pressure first so you don't make unneeded adjustments then have to reverse them.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- symptom: Drive tire pressures must be equal; if one is lower the deck doesn't sit level; grass on one side of the deck is cut longer.
- fix: Use gauge; set to the SCAG spec in the operator's manual.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- symptom: Dirt wears blade so ends become rounded and sail thin. Rounded ends leave uncut strips; worn sail reduces the airflow/suction that lifts grass.
- fix: Replace blades when tips are rounded or sail is thinner than the centre.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom (dull): Blade edges naturally dull; dull blades bruise and tear grass leaving a brownish look a couple of days later.
- confirm: Grab a handful of grass; torn brown ends mean dull blade.
- tip: Author starts each season with new blades and sharpens a couple of times per season; more often for heavy use or dusty conditions.
- symptom (bent): Loud clunk or grinding after hitting something; even a slight bend gives a bad cut; impact can also damage spindle and bearing. Even if you don't remember hitting something.
- confirm: On mower, flat level surface: measure from ground to blade end, rotate 180 degrees, measure from same spot; more than 1/8" difference = bent, don't reuse. Off mower: lay on a good blade; gaps or no flat fit = don't reuse. Check spindle/bearing before removing.
- mistake: NEVER straighten and reuse a bent blade; weakens metal, likely to crack; can break off and injure people.
- tip: Walk the lawn before mowing for tree roots, rocks, toys; remove what you can and mow around tree roots.
- symptom (unbalanced): One side heavier from uneven wear or uneven metal shaved during sharpening; wobble, significant vibration and spindle bearing damage at high speed.
- confirm: Blade balancer (inexpensive); or nail in wall head about 1 inch from wall, centre of blade on nail; should sit level and parallel to ground; if one side is lower shave metal off that end.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: Blade wobbles; vibration; uneven cut.
- confirm: With blades on and heavy gloves, grab each blade end and rock up and down; good spindle shows no movement; movement or knocking: remove blade and spindle housing, check spindle and bearing; replace damaged parts.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Deck packed with clippings reduces airflow space and suction; also strains the engine.
- fix: Scrape regularly; avoid wet mowing (clumps stick); deck spray only minimizes, doesn't prevent.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- symptom: One side lower gives a stair-step cut.
- fix: Procedures vary by model; see operator's manual. Level side-to-side, then check rake (front to rear) against model spec; the front of the deck sits slightly lower than rear.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Worn belt fails to grip pulleys, lowering blade tip speed and cut quality. Signs: glazed shiny look, cracks, stretched belt not tight to pulleys, or sitting deep in the pulleys.
- fix: Replace.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- symptom: Pulley doesn't sit flat; vertical movement; one side higher off the deck.
- confirm: Slowly spin each pulley by hand; feel for restriction, listen for bearing noise.
- fix: Replace the pulley.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: Engage the deck at full throttle; need highest engine speed to send enough power to blades.
- pro: If engine no longer gives needed power, have a small engine mechanic test it.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- symptom: Overloaded engine; mower doesn't cut all the grass or pushes it over instead of cutting.
- fix: Slower for tall, wet, thick grass and on hills.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fix: Overlap passes to avoid strips between passes; takes practice with a new mower; space paths to avoid mowing the same section twice.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- symptom: Running into fence, tree, pole can damage the deck shell.
- fix: Repair or replace shell; get a quote from the Scag dealer for replacement deck shell plus labor to swap components, and compare with complete deck replacement (author says the price difference may be small).
