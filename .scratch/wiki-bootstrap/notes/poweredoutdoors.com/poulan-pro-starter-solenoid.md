## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "turn the key and only hear clicking or humming"
- causes-named: bad-starter-solenoid, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-motor

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: clicking or humming on key turn and no start; the wire gets hot and smokes or melts.
- fact: Solenoid is an on/off electromagnet switch that engages the starter motor.
- fact: Internal spring and copper plate; fails if the spring weakens or the copper plate corrodes; can also fail due to a weak starter, bad battery or bad ground.
- fact: Likely bad when the starter motor turns over while the solenoid is bypassed.
- fact: Location: may be mounted on the starter but needn't be; some are on the frame closer to the battery. Follow the red positive cable from the battery. Round or square, 3 or 4 posts; some sit on top of the starter; every electric start engine has one. Positive wire from the battery attaches to one side. Wiring diagrams for 3- and 4-post solenoids vary by mower; may include lights and 12 V ports.
- mistake: Before replacing, check for loose or corroded wires or a bad ground that stop the solenoid getting power.

## page: procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid
- tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches (loose wires), needle nose pliers (if screwdriver fails), battery charger (optional)
- safety: Remove the black cable first, then the red, from the battery before electrical repairs. Bypass may spark (normal); wear safety gear.
- step 1: Confirm the battery has at least 12.3 V on a voltmeter.
- step 2: Set the parking brake, put in neutral, turn key to on.
- step 3: Bypass: lay a long screwdriver across the solenoid touching the cable from the battery and the cable to the starter. If the engine turns over, the solenoid is probably bad. Needle nose pliers can be used to jump it if the screwdriver doesn't work.
- step 4: Bench test (solenoid mounted on the starter): remove the starter from the engine; with a battery charger attach the negative (-) clamp to the starter case and touch the positive (+) clamp to the big post and the exciter wire on the solenoid.

## page: procedures/replace-the-starter-solenoid
- fact: replacement not described beyond "replace if bypass starts the engine"; check wiring and ground first.
