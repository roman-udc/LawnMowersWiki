## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "lawn mower won't start"
- causes-named: fuel-valve-off, empty-fuel-tank, weak-or-dead-battery, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, old-fuel
- fact: First check spark plug and air filter, then confirm fuel supply. Engine needs air, fuel and spark.
- tip: New mower or first use since storage: check the fuel shut-off valve is open; may have been closed for transport or storage.
- confirm: Fuel test: remove air filter housing cover; remove air filter without knocking dirt into intake; wipe out housing; spray carburetor cleaner into the air intake; start. If it starts and runs briefly, fuel isn't reaching the engine: check fuel line, filter, pump, carburetor.
- safety: Never use starter fluid (author links an article on why).

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- symptom: Forgot to fill, or a fuel leak depleted the tank.
- fix: Fill with fresh fuel; inspect fuel filter, lines, pump, carburetor for leaks.

## page: causes/fuel-valve-off
- fact: New or post-storage mower: confirm valve open.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Volt meter; fully charged 12 V battery reads 12.7 V. Charge if lower; replace if won't hold charge.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: Plug covered in dirt, carbon, oil gives intermittent spark; broken porcelain or burnt electrode prevents start.
- confirm: Remove with 3/4" or 5/8" socket wrench (depends on engine model). Lightly dirty: clean with wire brush and reuse. Dark color, burnt electrode or broken porcelain: replace.
- fix: Gap to engine manufacturer spec; secure plug wire. Loose wire or wrong gap also causes no-start.
- tip: Author replaces plug annually; replaces if suspect (inexpensive).

## page: procedures/replace-the-spark-plug
- fact: Socket 3/4" or 5/8"; verify gap per engine manufacturer; attach wire securely.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: Plugged filter can prevent starting and cause overheating, smoke, engine damage.
- tip: Start each season with a new air filter; check and clean a few times per season.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: Sticky deposits and varnish restrict the fuel system, so not enough fuel to start.
