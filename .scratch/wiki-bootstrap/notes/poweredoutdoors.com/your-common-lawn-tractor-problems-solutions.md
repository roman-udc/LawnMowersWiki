## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "lawn tractor won't start"
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, fouled-spark-plug, clogged-air-filter, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil
- fact: needs fuel, air, spark; fix list: fill tank; drain old fuel and refill with stabilizer (Sea Foam); clean/replace cap vent; replace plug, secure, gap to spec; clean/replace air filter; replace fuel filter or pump; carb cleaner + compressed air on lines; clean carb; charge/replace battery, tighten cables, clean terminals; test/replace safety switch, ignition switch or coil.

## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "lawn tractor dies while mowing"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, clogged-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, faulty-charging-system, weak-or-dead-battery, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: can also overheat (plugged filter, cooling fin buildup, low oil, plugged deck); faulty ignition coil dies when hot and shorts; charging system not working; plugged cap vent.
- fact: battery that doesn't hold charge or bad charging system can make the tractor die after running; test battery and charging system and replace bad parts.

## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, overfilled-oil, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, bad-head-gasket, bad-valve-train, worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- fact: caught early some are fixable; internal damage is costly. Too much oil: drain to recommended level; low oil: shop inspection, often too late; internal: small engine mechanic.

## page: problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side
- alias: "lawn tractor won't drive straight or steer correctly"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, loose-steering-linkage, worn-drive-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, low-hydraulic-oil
- fact: unequal tire pressure pulls to one side; equal pressure at manufacturer spec.
- fact: worn bushings, washers and gears in steering can stop steering one way or the other; inspect and replace failed parts.
- fact: weak hydrostatic: bad drive belt (frayed/cracked/worn), failed tensioner pulley (replace, grease the arm), low or old hydraulic oil (change at manufacturer intervals; some systems are sealed and non-serviceable, see dealer).

## page: problems/loose-steering
- causes-named: loose-steering-linkage
- fact: worn bushings, washers and gears (see above).

## page: problems/mower-wont-move
- alias: "lawn tractor won't move or go forward"
- causes-named: brake-hung-up, missing-axle-key, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, missing-idler-arm-spring, worn-drive-belt, low-hydraulic-oil, hot-hydraulic-fluid, air-in-hydraulic-system
- fact: hydrostatic drive release lever must be in operating position (release lets it roll freely).
- fact: a small key in the axle may have fallen out of place; replace the key. (See causes/missing-axle-key.)
- fact: tensioner pulley bearing failure: replace. Missing/broken idler arm spring: replace. Worn drive belt slips (sluggish), can break or fall off (no movement): replace.
- fact: low/old hydraulic oil; hot fluid (let cool; if fine cold but weak hot see dealer); air must be bled after fluid change per manual.

## page: causes/missing-axle-key
- fact: small key in the axle can fall out of place; replace it; mower then doesn't move.

## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, dull-or-bent-blade, debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: engine mounting bolts secure/replace; clutch bearing failure (manual-engagement clutch bushing and linkages); balanced blades; string/weeds on blade shaft; spindle housing bearing; worn belts and pulleys; small debris (author finds surprising little things stuck).

## page: problems/uneven-cut
- causes-named: dull-or-bent-blade, blade-installed-upside-down, clogged-deck-chute, wrong-tire-pressure, unlevel-deck, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: table lists: worn/bent blade, blade installed wrong, clogged deck, uneven tire pressures, unlevel deck, bent spindle/bearing, worn pulleys/belt, low engine speed, fast ground speed, damaged deck shell, operator error.

## page: problems/mower-is-leaking-gas
- causes-named: dirty-carburetor, cracked-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, cracked-fuel-tank, bad-shutoff-valve, cracked-fuel-line, bad-gas-cap-seal
- fact: fuel evaporates making leaks hard to find; old/dry/degraded fuel components; metal tank can rust through; poly tank leaks at seams; replace tank, or sometimes repair a rust hole if no replacement tank is available; metal and plastic shutoff valves leak; rubber lines dry and crack; cap seal fails.
