## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: Redex is a brand of fuel additives with a range of products; Source says it can be used in any combustion engine including lawn mowers (petrol, diesel or hybrid) if appropriate amounts are used.
- fact: Redex products named: Octane Booster (power), Fuel System Cleaner / Advanced Fuel System Cleaner (MPG, emissions), Petrol Emissions Reducer. Source says tested and improved since 1922; for a mower stick to Redex Fuel System Cleaner at the proper amount.
- fact: reasons for an additive: (1) valve seat erosion from built-up particulates/debris, which the cleaner helps remove; (2) ethanol in modern gasoline is corrosive to metals such as iron and steel and leaves salt deposits that can clog the fuel pump.
- fact: gasoline leaves gummy corrosive residue that blocks and drains engine performance; worse when left sitting long (e.g. over winter), where a fuel system cleaner helps.
- step 1 (using a fuel system cleaner): fill a 1-gallon gas can with 1 gallon of gas.
- step 2: add the cleaner; typically two teaspoons per gallon if the cleaner is designed for lawn mowers.
- fact: if using an automobile-designed cleaner such as Redex in a mower, use it at half strength. Full strength will not cause problems initially, but too much cleaner can strip the fuel tank lining because mower tanks are smaller than car tanks.
- mistake: do not use additives targeting diesel engines (Diesel System Cleaner, Diesel Emissions Reducer) in a gas/petrol mower.
- mistake: Redex Petrol Power Booster raises octane by 1.5; Source says it will not hurt but is unnecessary for a small mower engine and is wasted money (opinion).
- fact: alternatives named: Archoil AR 6200 (mainly diesel but usable in gas; stabilizer and detergent); Sea Foam (multipurpose; stabilizer extending fuel life up to two years; lubricant, system cleaner, moisture reducer); Lucas Safeguard Ethanol Fuel Conditioner (older product; lubricant and detergent against ethanol deposits); STA-BIL Original (older; fuel stabilizer extending fuel life up to two years; helps prevent corrosion and remove water build-up; Source says effectiveness has waned against ethanol fuels compared to Sea Foam).
- fact: a fuel system cleaner is the easiest way to clean dirt/debris that enters via the air intake, versus disassembling the engine or paying a shop (Source's view).

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gasoline's gummy corrosive residue worsens with long sitting, e.g. winter; fuel system cleaner/stabilizer helps (see guide notes).
