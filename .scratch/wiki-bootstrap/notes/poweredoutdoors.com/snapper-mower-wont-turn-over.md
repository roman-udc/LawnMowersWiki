## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Snapper mower won't turn over or crank"
- fact: look for what keeps the starter motor from getting power.
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor
- safety: before electrical repairs remove black negative cable from battery; see operator's manual.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: keep fully charged, esp. in storage; not-fully-charged battery stored in cold can freeze and be damaged.
- step charge (12 V): wear protective gear; access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed to uncover; don't remove battery from casing); connect positive (red, plus) first; then negative (black); don't touch anything without rubber coating; set charger to 12 V; start at 2 amps, work up to no more than 10 amps; slow charge is best.
- fix: if won't hold charge replace; bring old battery (core fee otherwise).

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step: as above.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fact: wiring and components shake loose from vibration; corrosion from moisture affects continuity.
- fix: remove battery cables first; wire brush and baking soda solution (2 cups water to 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda); replace terminals/components if can't clean.

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fact: fuse protects against surge or short.
- confirm: multimeter probe on each prong measuring resistance: near 0 = good; infinity = bad.
- fix: replace with same amperage; if keeps blowing, dealer/repair shop for root cause.

## page: procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse
- step: as above.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: nothing happens on key turn. Multimeter set to resistance; terminals marked B (Battery) and S (Starter Solenoid); key in start position; probes on B and S; near 0 ohms good, infinite = bad, replace.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system. Test with multimeter; may temporarily bypass for troubleshooting only. Never operate without safety switch or with it bypassed.
- safety: never run mower with a safety switch bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch; goes bad when spring weak or copper plate corrodes; weak starter, bad battery or bad ground can also cause failure. Need fully charged battery before testing.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- fact: after ruling out battery, cables, wiring, ground, solenoid, starter can be removed and tested.
- tip: have a starter/alternator repair shop test and rebuild if possible before buying a pricey new starter.
