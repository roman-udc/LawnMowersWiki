## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- alias: "Ferris zero turn clicks but won't turn over or start"
- causes-named: loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-wiring-or-ground, bad-starter-solenoid, bad-starter-motor, faulty-charging-system
- safety: Remove spark plug wire before repairs; always disconnect the negative (black) battery cable before electrical repairs; see a mechanic if uncomfortable with electrical system.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- symptom: Loose, damaged, or corroded cables keep the starter from getting power.
- fix: Replace cables/wiring that keep coming loose or are broken (bad cables can contribute to a bad battery). Disconnect battery; clean corroded components with a wire brush and baking soda mix (2 cups water plus 3 heaping tablespoons baking soda). Repair/replace corroded wiring. Add dielectric grease to protect terminals and wiring; replace damaged terminals.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: Multimeter red and black prongs to matching terminals; most riding/zero-turn batteries are 12 V. Reading 11.5 V = almost dead; 12.7 V = fully charged.
- fix: Charge on a battery charger; replace if it won't hold a charge. If it charges but keeps dying, check charging system.

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: Put on safety gear (eyes and skin; acid/shock). step 2: access battery and terminals (screwdriver may be needed to uncover). step 3: leave battery in casing with cables attached. step 4: connect red (positive) clamp first to positive terminal, then black to negative. step 5: skin should only touch rubber coating of cables and clamps. step 6: set charger to 12 V; more amps charges faster, start with 2 amps and work up to no more than 10 amps. step 7: if charger has a gauge, keep connected until fully charged.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- confirm: Check black ground cable from battery to Ferris frame for good contact and no corrosion; also check solenoid ground (a 3-post solenoid is self-grounded).
- fix: Replace damaged ground cable; remove corrosion at battery and solenoid grounds.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: Keeps clicking. Solenoid is an electromagnet switch engaging the starter motor; usually mounted on starter but doesn't have to be; follow positive wire from battery. Fails via weak internal spring, corroded copper plate, bad ground, weak starter, bad battery.
- confirm: Tools: volt-ohm meter, screwdriver, continuity light, wrenches. If mower starts when the solenoid is bypassed, replace it.

## page: causes/bad-starter-motor
- confirm: Only after battery, cables, wiring, ground and solenoid are good; starter can be removed and tested.
- tip: Starters are pricey; have the dealership confirm, or take the starter to a repair shop that specializes in starters/alternators, which can test and often rebuild it.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: Ferris zero-turn has an internal alternator under the flywheel called a stator; flywheel passing over it makes electricity to keep the battery charged. A bad charging system leaves a weak battery. Possible: bad stator/alternator, regulator, other electrical problem.
- pro: Troubleshooting the charging system is difficult; have a mechanic familiar with it test, otherwise you end up throwing parts at it (electrical parts can't be returned).
