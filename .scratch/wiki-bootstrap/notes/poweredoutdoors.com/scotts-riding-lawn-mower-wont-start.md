## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Scotts riding mower won't start"
- fact: won't start without air, spark and fuel.
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, old-fuel, wrong-choke-setting, clogged-air-filter, bad-fuel-pump, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, weak-or-dead-battery, loose-or-corroded-battery-cables, bad-safety-switch, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-starter-solenoid, faulty-charging-system, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- safety: remove spark plug wire before repairs.

## page: problems/engine-wont-crank
- fact: if mower won't even turn over, check battery, cables, terminals (battery must read 12.7 V or greater); also safety switch, ignition switch, solenoid.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: fresh gas 87 octane or higher; ethanol no greater than 10%. Easy to forget when frustrated.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down after about 30 days; ethanol moisture leaves gummy deposits in lines, filters, carb.
- fix: remove old fuel (siphon pump); add additive (Sea Foam) to fresh gas; run mixture through.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: cold engine needs choke closed (restricts air); warm engine needs choke off/open.
- step throttle-lever-choke models: move lever to choke position; once running and warming, move to full throttle.
- step separate choke knob models: pull knob out to close choke; throttle to full; start; once warm push knob in.
- fact: if choke position right and still airflow trouble, check plugged air filter, stuck choke, or worn choke cable.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: plugged filter -> engine runs hot, hunts for air including crankcase air; can cause overheating, smoke, engine damage.
- step paper: remove without dropping dirt in intake; wipe housing; tap on hand or solid surface; light test through every area - replace if cannot see light, damaged, torn, or no longer seals.
- step foam pre-filter: never oil a pre-filter (damages paper primary filter); replace if dark spots, brittle, torn; wash in water and mild detergent; rinse; squeeze dry, don't wring (tears); lay flat till dry.
- mistake: wringing out foam; oiling a pre-filter.

## page: procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter
- see above.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: pump present when tank lower than carb. Three ports: inlet, outlet, and port connected to line from crankcase that pressurizes pump. Replace if small cracks or fuel leaking.
- confirm inlet: turn off fuel valve or pinch pliers (not every mower has valve); disconnect line from inlet into container lower than tank; open flow; fuel should flow; if not check lines/filter.
- confirm outlet: reconnect inlet line; remove line from carburetor into container; start mower; steady or pulsating flow expected; replace pump if it can't consistently pump.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: old fuel gums when evaporating plus dirty fuel plug filter; replace.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- fix: shut off fuel, remove line, carb cleaner (loosens) then compressed air (dislodges), repeat; replace if can't clear.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: wrong mix -> runs rough or won't start. Most times cleaning works; use carb rebuild kit for damaged parts; replace carb if cleaning fails.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: battery must be 12.7 V or more; test with multimeter; <12.7 V charge; if won't hold charge replace.

## page: causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables
- fix: clean corroded terminals with baking soda solution (2 cups water, 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda) and wire brush.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: several safety switches; fault prevents start. Test with multimeter or temporarily bypass to identify. Do not operate mower without the safety switch installed.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: inspect for carbon buildup, cracked porcelain insulator, burnt electrode -> replace; gap per manufacturer; secure wire; loose wire or wrong gap -> starting or intermittent running problem.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- symptom: key turned and mower doesn't start or turn over. Test with multimeter; replace.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- fact: after plug verified, check continuity with multimeter; replace if break.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic switch actuating starter. Signs: click or hum when turning key; wire attached to solenoid gets hot, smokes or melts. Replace if bad.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: not main cause of no-start but can cause weak battery; bad stator or alternator among causes. Test charging system with ohmmeter (linked article).
- pro: have small engine mechanic find the cause; guessing is expensive.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: tank vent is in gas cap; plugged = vacuum; replace broken cap.
