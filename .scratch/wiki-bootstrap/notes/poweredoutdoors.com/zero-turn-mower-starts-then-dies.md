## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "zero turn starts, stalls and dies / dies in the middle of the lawn"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, bad-fuel-pump, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, dirty-cooling-fins, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, fouled-spark-plug, bad-ignition-coil, wrong-choke-setting, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Engine dies when not getting air, fuel or spark; overheating from oil level, plugged fins, plugged deck.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Ethanol (corn-based) attracts corrosive moisture; evaporates leaving gummy deposits; separates and sinks to tank bottom; runs hot through engine, potential damage. Use fresh unleaded, no more than 10% ethanol.
- fix: Siphon into approved container, set aside for recycling; refill and add stabilizer. Author uses Sea Foam Motor Treatment (stabilizes, reduces moisture, cleans; petroleum-based).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: Replace; arrow in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom: Gummy deposit blockage causes dying after running a while.
- confirm: Shut-off valve to start/stop flow and check each section. Remove line, carb cleaner then compressed air; else new line.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Most zero-turn pumps are vacuum pumps building pressure off the engine block. Check inflow, then outflow from carburetor line into container with engine started: steady or pulsating = OK, else replace.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: fuel flow to carb; remove air filter; spray carb cleaner in intake; start; starts, runs fine, then shuts down = carburetor.
- fix: Remove, disassemble, clean; or small engine shop.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- tip: Replace once a year; check several times a season; clean more often in dusty conditions.
- step 1: Remove paper element; wipe housing with clean dry cloth, no dirt into intake.
- step 2: Tap against hard surface; no compressed air (damages paper).
- step 3: Hold to light; replace if no light shows, damaged or covered in oil; else reuse.
- step 4: Install and reattach cover.

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- fix: Remove dirt from fins, engine block and shroud; replace broken fins; heat shield correctly installed for circulation.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: Check oil before each use (dipstick). Low oil: friction, crankcase heat, burning oil and parts, overheat and shutdown; adding oil rarely fixes; likely significant damage; small engine mechanic.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: Excess oil builds crankcase pressure; remove a little via drain plug, oil filter, or fill area with evacuator or turkey baster; repeat to level.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fix: Clean if dirty; new if very dark or damaged; gap to engine manufacturer spec; wires secure.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: Windings separate and short out when hot; dies after running a while.
- confirm: Ohmmeter continuity; break = replace.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: Choke for cold starts; left on after warm-up, mower dies.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: Starts but shuts down after running a while as vacuum forms.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: Plugged deck strains engine, overheats, shuts down; dull blades worsen; scrape deck and sharpen blades.

## page: problems/battery-keeps-dying
- fact: Run at high engine speed; don't idle long; engine power charges the battery. If constantly recharging with good battery and wiring, suspect charging system; experienced mechanic recommended (parts-throwing is costly; electrical parts usually non-returnable).

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- pro: Mechanic recommended.
