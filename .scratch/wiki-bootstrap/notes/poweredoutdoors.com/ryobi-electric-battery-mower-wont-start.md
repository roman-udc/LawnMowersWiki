## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "RYOBI electric mower won't start"
- causes-named: battery-not-seated, bad-charger, overheated-battery, weak-or-dead-battery, bad-battery, incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, cutting-wet-or-tall-grass
- safety: Remove safety key and battery before repairs; under warranty consult RYOBI first (repairs/modifications outside an authorized RYOBI repair center can void all warranties). Support 1-800-525-2579, www.ryobitools.com.
- fact: Insufficient power (bad connection, dead or bad battery) or bad electrical connection (safety switches, loose/damaged wires), incorrect operating procedure, or debris under deck.
- fact: Water can short out the mower; see rain article (mower got wet).
- pro: If nothing found, authorized RYOBI service center.

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- fix: Check battery is securely in the port and making good contact.

## page: causes/bad-charger
- confirm: Some RYOBI units (zero-turn) charge in the mower via a charging port; most push and self-propelled use an external charger. Check outlet and cords fully plugged in; standard 120V; plug style must match outlet, don't modify plug.

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- symptom: Hot battery makes mower shut down and not start; let it cool.

## page: causes/bad-battery
- fact: Charger LED (see charger manual); zero-turn has charging port LEDs and possibly a charge display; lights can indicate charging, full, failed to charge, defective.
- symptom: Failing: doesn't hold a charge as long, charges slowly, gets very hot. Fails when old, exposed to extreme temps, or mechanical failure.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- step (push/self-propelled): battery in, safety key inserted; pull bail (safety bar) to handle and press start button; release start button; mower shuts off when safety bar is released.
- step (zero-turn): battery charged; with drive levers open sit on mower; insert key to on; release parking brake; pull levers in to center neutral; raise the blade engage knob to activate blades. Interlock shuts blades off if you leave the seat with blades engaged.
- fact: Handle must be unfolded and secured in operating position; if the starter cable runs outside the handle, ensure it isn't pinched.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- brand: Push/self-propelled switches: foldable handle switch; safety bar/bail lever switch (release shuts off); momentary on/off button switch. Zero-turn: parking brake/pedal switch, seat switch, key switch.
- confirm: Test each or temporarily bypass with jumper wire.
- safety: Never operate without all safety switches working.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- symptom: Loose/pinched wires; push and self-propelled mowers known to pinch a wire where the handle folds for storage.
- confirm: wiggle wires; multimeter.
- fix: wire nut repair, battery removed first.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Grass jammed under deck prevents blade rotation; safety features then block starting. Remove battery and key first. Overload (thick, tall, wet grass): raise cut height and slow down.
