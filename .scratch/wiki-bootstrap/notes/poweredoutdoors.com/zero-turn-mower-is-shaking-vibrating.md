## page: problems/mower-is-vibrating
- alias: "zero turn shaking excessively, vibrating"
- causes-named: engine-mount-loose, bad-pto-clutch, bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade (wrong or incorrectly installed blades), dull-or-bent-blade (unbalanced), debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, worn-or-stretched-belt, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, loose-hardware (unmapped), debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- fact: Caused by worn or loose hardware, failing clutch, loose linkages, unbalanced blades, bad spindle/pulley bearings. Author found a wood chip under a pulley.
- safety: Remove key; disconnect spark plug boots; parking brake and wheel chocks; jack stands to lift and access under deck; work gloves and safety glasses; manual.
- tip (prevention): clean dirt/debris after each mowing; check/sharpen/replace blades and check spindle and pulley bearings when changing blades; install correct, balanced blades; check periodically for loose/missing hardware.
- pro: If still unsolved, experienced mechanic at brand dealer or small engine repair shop.

## page: causes/engine-mount-loose
- symptom: Special engine mounting bolts (motor mount) under engine absorb vibration; any worn, broken or missing must be replaced.

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- symptom: Electric clutch: ball bearing can fail. Manual engagement clutch: bushing and linkage failure.

## page: causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade
- symptom: Wrong blades: styles (high-lift, low-lift, gator, mulching) and sizes differ; e.g. many 52 inch blades do not fit every 52 inch deck. Need blade length and center hole size; matching listed measurements can still be wrong.
- tip: Author buys OEM; good aftermarket includes Stens and Oregon; match aftermarket to an OEM blade.
- symptom: Incorrect installation: use manufacturer's hardware in correct order (check manual or parts diagram); a slight movement becomes big vibration at speed; concave washers cupped side toward the blade.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- symptom: Unbalanced blade: heavier on one end, wobbles, can feel like shaking out of the seat; from unequal wear (dirt) or removing more metal from one side when sharpening. Can damage spindle housing bearings and possibly the engine. Use a blade balancer (inexpensive).

## page: causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle
- fix: Remove wire, string, long weeds from spindle/blade shaft; check spindle housings for damage.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- symptom: Bearings fail from wear, unbalanced blades, impact.
- confirm: Thick gloves; grab blade ends and rock up/down; listen for knock, feel movement; disassemble housing and check; check other spindles.
- fix: Replace bearing, or the whole spindle housing assembly on models with sealed bearings.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: Turn each pulley slowly by hand for resistance or noise; replace if so or if it doesn't sit flat/parallel, extra movement.

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: Worn/stretched belt with slack vibrates: glazed, cracked, shredded; replace. Worn idler bracket (enlarged hole) and tensioner spring (stretched, broken, missing) add movement.
