## page: problems/engine-only-runs-with-choke-on
- alias: "zero turn only runs with choke on"
- alias: "dies when choke is turned off; runs with choke on or halfway"
- causes-named: old-fuel, clogged-fuel-line, bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, clogged-fuel-filter, dirty-carburetor, bad-fuel-pump, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: Engine getting too much air or not enough fuel. Lack of fuel: old gas, dirty carburetor, clogged fuel line, bad fuel pump, plugged fuel filter, bad fuel cap. Too much air: bad carburetor gasket, puncture in fuel line.
- safety: Shut off, remove ignition key and spark plug wires, let engine cool.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Gas can break down as soon as 30 days; buy what you can use in that time. Unleaded, minimum 87 octane, max 10% ethanol. Ethanol (plant-made) attracts moisture; water/ethanol leaves varnish that restricts flow and corrodes; separates and sinks.
- fix: Drain with a fuel siphon pump; add fresh gas with an additive (author: Sea Foam Motor Treatment).

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- symptom (puncture): A puncture or crack lets air be sucked in, so the engine gets too much air. A clog restricts fuel. Either makes it run only with choke on.
- confirm: Follow line from tank to carburetor checking for punctures/cracks. For a clog: shut off fuel at shut-off valve, remove the end of a section into a container, turn fuel on, watch for good flow.
- fix: If poor flow, shut off, remove line, carb cleaner then compressed air, repeat; replace with same-diameter new line if you can't clear it or find a puncture.

## page: causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat
- symptom: Gasket behind the carburetor deteriorates, no longer seals, lets extra air in so engine runs lean (higher air concentration, less fuel than needed); choke compensates.
- fix: Access carb, carefully remove linkages and bolts, remove carb and gasket; install new gasket; reattach carb, bolts and linkages. Good time to check if carb needs cleaning.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: Small part between fuel lines; replace annually. Plugged filter reduces fuel, so choke is needed to correct the ratio.
- fix: Insert a new inline filter; arrow on the housing points in the direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: Often old gas clogging passageways and sticking internal components.
- fix: Clean (if a little mechanical); rebuild or replace if cleaning doesn't work; or take to a small engine dealer.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: Used when carburetor sits higher than the tank; most riding mowers have a vacuum pump using engine vacuum.
- confirm: Confirm fuel reaches the inlet port; then remove line from carburetor into a container and check flow.
- fix: Replace if not enough flow, cracks or leaks.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: Cap broken/plugged: tank forms vacuum, restricts fuel.
- confirm: Loosen cap to let air in; if you can move the choke off and the engine keeps running, cap may be bad. Replicate: tighten the cap while running; if it runs sluggish and needs choke but is fine with choke off when cap is loosened, the cap is most likely the problem.
- fix: Replace the cap.

## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Mowers need unleaded, 87+ octane, max 10% ethanol.
