## page: problems/uneven-cut
- alias: "Simplicity mower bad or uneven cut"
- alias: "stair-step cut" / "strip of grass left between blades"
- causes-named: wrong-tire-pressure, worn-blade-sail, dull-or-bent-blade, bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing, clogged-deck-chute, unlevel-deck, worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, wrong-engine-speed, mowing-too-fast, incorrect-path-overlap, bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- safety: follow owner's manual; secure the mower: remove ignition key, disconnect spark plug boots, engage parking brake.
- tip: check tire pressure first; if a low tire is found after deck adjustments those may need to be undone.

## page: causes/wrong-tire-pressure
- fact: a low tire makes the deck sit lower on one side. Use a tire pressure gauge; left side pressures must equal right side.

## page: causes/worn-blade-sail
- fact: blade tips can wear so a strip of grass is left between them; the sail (high side of blade) wears and can't create suction. Normal wear from dirt drawn under the deck.
- fix: replace with new Simplicity blades when tips are rounded or the sail is thinner than the blade center; sharpen if good but dull.

## page: causes/dull-or-bent-blade
- confirm (blade on): park on a flat level surface; measure from the ground to the blade end, rotate 180 degrees, measure same spot; more than 1/8 inch difference = bent; don't reuse.
- confirm (blade off): lay on a known good blade; gaps or bend = don't reuse.
- mistake: never straighten a bent blade (weakens metal, may crack and break off at high speed; danger to people, pets, structures).
- tip: check the spindle and bearing before removing a bent blade (impact may have damaged them).
- fact: unbalanced blade (one side heavier from uneven wear or sharpening) wobbles, can damage spindle bearings at high speed and send significant vibration. Use a blade balancer (inexpensive); or hang on a nail and shave the heavy side until it stays level.

## page: causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing
- confirm: with blade installed, grab each end, rock up and down; movement or knocking = bearing most likely bad; remove blade and spindle housing and check; replace damaged parts.

## page: causes/clogged-deck-chute
- fact: blades, deck shell and baffles create suction to lift grass; packed deck reduces airspace, causes uneven cut and engine strain. Scrape regularly; avoid wet conditions.

## page: causes/unlevel-deck
- fact: one side lower gives stair-step cut; level side to side and front to rear; on most decks the front sits slightly lower than the rear; procedures and specs vary by model (see manual).

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- fix: replace if stretched and no longer tight to pulleys, sits deep in pulleys, has cracks or a shiny glazed look.

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- fact: bad pulley bearings: pulley not flat/secure, vertical movement (one side higher off the deck), may seize.
- confirm: spin each pulley slowly by hand; feel restriction, listen for bearing noise, look for wobble; replace pulley.

## page: causes/wrong-engine-speed
- fact: deck engaged requires full throttle; if power is down at full throttle have a small engine mechanic test the engine.

## page: causes/mowing-too-fast
- fact: slow down for tall, wet or thick grass and on inclines; too fast leaves grass uncut or pushed over.

## page: causes/incorrect-path-overlap
- fact: overlap passes to avoid strips; takes practice with a new mower.

## page: causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell
- fact: hitting a fence, tree, pole damages the shell; repair or replace. Get a Simplicity dealer quote for a replacement shell plus labor to swap components and compare to full deck replacement; price difference may be small.
