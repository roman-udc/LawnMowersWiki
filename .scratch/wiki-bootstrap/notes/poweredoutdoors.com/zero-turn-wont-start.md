## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- brand: zero-turns require unleaded, min octane 87, max ethanol 10%. E15 and E85 (15% and 85% ethanol) appearing at more stations can cause significant engine damage.
- fix: wrong fuel in tank: drain and flush; manual siphon pump works well; refill fresh gas with stabilizer/cleaner like Sea Foam.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas breaks down as soon as 30 days; buy from a busy station; ethanol attracts moisture, running issues, gums fuel system; if you have more than 30-day supply add stabilizer.

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: add fresh fuel.

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: vent clogged makes tank vacuum, carburetor starved.
- confirm: run with and without cap: starts and runs without cap, dies after a short time with cap on = bad cap.
- fix: try to clean the clog; replace cap if unsuccessful.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- fact: loose plug wires, wrong gap, or dirty plugs cause intermittent running/starting.
- fix: inspect for carbon or cracked porcelain; replace if damaged; clean tips if dirty; correct gap; secure wires.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- step: remove filter; wipe housing with dry cloth, no dirt into intake; tap on solid surface; hold to light; reuse if light seen, else replace.

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: pump works against gravity to lift fuel to carburetor; most zero-turns use vacuum-style pump; fuel degrades it over time; leaking = replace.
- confirm: shut fuel with valve under tank or clamps; remove hose from pump inlet into container lower than tank; open flow; flow confirms fuel reaches pump, no flow = restriction before pump. Re-fit inlet hose. Remove hose from carburetor into container, open flow, start engine, watch for steady or pulsating flow; afterwards shut off mower and reattach hose.
- fix: replace pump if no constant or pulsating flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- tip: replace once a year during maintenance; arrow in direction of fuel flow.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: start/stop flow and check sections.
- fix: remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air; if can't clear, replace with correct diameter; replace dry/cracking lines even without clog.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- fact: old fuel forms gummy crusty deposits that make small parts stick.
- fix: clean and replace stuck/damaged parts; repair shop or new carburetor assembly if not comfortable.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- confirm: multimeter; under 12.7 V charge; replace if won't hold charge; clean corroded terminals with 2 cups water and 3 rounded tablespoons baking soda, wire brush.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: operator presence control system; test with multimeter or temporary bypass for troubleshooting only; never operate bypassed.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- confirm: multimeter; replace.

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- symptom: click or hum at key; wire hot, smoking or melting.

## page: causes/faulty-charging-system
- fact: bad stator or alternator and others; contributes to weak battery.
- pro: zero-turn dealership; electrical parts expensive and usually non-returnable.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- fact: safety procedures need steps followed; see operating manual.
