## page: problems/mower-is-smoking
- alias: "SCAG mower blowing white, blue or black smoke"
- causes-named: clogged-air-filter, wrong-choke-setting, wrong-oil-or-low-oil, overfilled-oil, bad-head-gasket (engine gasket), worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides, bad-valve-train
- fact: black smoke = engine running rich (more fuel, less air); check plugged air filter, wrong choke. White or blue smoke = burning oil: oil level wrong, worn piston rings, valve train, bad engine gasket, wrong oil viscosity.
- safety: do not keep running while smoking; smoke not always great damage but can become significant.

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- fact: plugged filter makes engine run rich (black smoke); engine may also pull oil from crankcase into cylinder, burning (black smoke).
- fix: replace annually, check/clean several times per season; more often with heavy or dusty use; commercial use check before each use.
- step: paper filter: remove from housing; wipe housing with dry cloth without letting dirt into intake; tap on solid surface; do not use compressed air (damages filter); hold to light; reuse if light shows; replace if no light, extremely dirty, damaged, or oily; reattach cover.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fact: incorrect choke setting causes black smoke; sticking manual choke: lubricate choke cable so it moves freely.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- fact: low from short fill after oil change, wrong viscosity, or leak. Friction heat burns oil and melts parts.
- fix: adding oil probably won't help since damage likely done; small engine mechanic diagnosis and estimate to decide rebuild vs replace engine.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- fact: excess oil pushed into cylinder or up to the air intake through the valve train; burns and smokes.
- fix: replace air filter if oiled; set level at dipstick mark; drain a little via drain plug, oil filter, or evacuator/turkey baster at fill port.

## page: causes/bad-head-gasket
- fact: failing engine gasket leaks oil that smokes when burning off hot engine or muffler; locate and replace; difficulty depends on location.

## page: causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides
- confirm: oil on the spark plug indicates piston ring or valve train problem; scoring in cylinder wall or worn rings lets oil into combustion chamber.
- pro: experienced small engine mechanic; engine teardown needed; replace engine vs new mower depending on age.

## page: causes/bad-valve-train
- confirm: only by removing cylinder head and leak-down test by mechanic/dealer. Burnt valve (overheated, valve edge falls apart); muffler glowing red indicates burnt valve or timing problem. Valve and seat cut at certain angles to seat correctly.
