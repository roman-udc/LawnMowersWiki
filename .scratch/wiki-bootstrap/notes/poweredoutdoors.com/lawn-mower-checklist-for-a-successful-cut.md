## page: guides/routine-service-and-maintenance
- fact: Pre-use checklist takes about five minutes (longer if problems found); manufacturers publish daily checklists; consult owner's manual for model-specific items.
- fact 1 Fuel: check level in a well-ventilated area, fill if needed.
- fact 2 Oil: park on level surface, engine off, key removed; check level; do not run with too little or overfilled oil (can damage engine). Most engines use SAE30 or 10W-30 air-cooled engine oil; refer to manual.
- fact 3 Air filter: dirty filter restricts air; engine runs rich (more fuel than air) and smokes; lack of air also overheats and damages engine. Remove carefully (no dirt into intake), wipe housing with dry cloth.
  - paper: tap on hard surface; hold up to light; light visible = reuse, else replace.
  - foam primary element (not foam pre-filters): brown spots, tears, brittle = replace; else wash in water and mild dish soap, rinse, lay flat to air dry (sun is faster), lightly saturate with foam filter oil per bottle (clean engine oil also usable), reinstall.
- fact 4 Blades: engine off, spark plug removed; never reach under deck while anything moving; lift discharge chute to look at blade sharpness and grass buildup; if dull or buildup, get under deck to clean and sharpen/replace.
- fact 5 Tire pressure (air-filled tires): usually about 12-15 psi depending on tire size.
- fact 6 Belts: look for visible cracks.
- fact 7 Linkages: on riding/zero-turn move throttle and choke linkage to check they move freely; move deck engagement lever or PTO switch.
- fact 8 Safety system: if mower fails any, do not operate until repaired. ROPS: check mounting hardware is secure; folding ROPS upright and securely held (knobs or pins). Seatbelt: check wear/cuts, latch works; replace if latch fails or damaged. Operator presence: mower should shut down and PTO not engage when operator leaves seat on riding mower; newer push mowers have an extra lever/bar held down to keep blades engaged.
- tip: author's anecdote - sons ran mower out of fuel mid-lawn skipping checks.

## page: guides/cleaning-the-mower
- tip: after each mowing remove debris so clipping moisture doesn't sit on the mower or collect in pulley/belt system or under the deck; leaf blower keeps it clean and dry; store dry; periodic deeper cleanings in season.

## page: guides/zero-turn-safety
- fact: ROPS / seatbelt / operator presence checks (see item 8).

## page: guides/engine-oil-guide
- fact: SAE30 or 10W-30 air-cooled engine oil on most engines; check manual.
