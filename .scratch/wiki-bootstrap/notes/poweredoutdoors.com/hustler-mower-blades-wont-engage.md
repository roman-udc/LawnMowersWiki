## page: problems/blades-wont-engage
- alias: "pull up the PTO knob and nothing happens"
- alias: "blades spin too slow"
- causes-named: worn-or-stretched-belt, worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing, bad-belt-tensioner-spring, bad-pto-switch, bad-safety-switch, bad-pto-clutch, weak-or-dead-battery, blown-fuse
- safety: working with the deck is very dangerous; remove ignition key and spark plug wires, wait for all parts to stop, wear safety gear

## page: causes/worn-or-stretched-belt
- symptom: signs: cracking, worn side walls, fraying, shiny glazed look, sits deep in pulley grooves
- fact: worn belt slips; blades may still turn at reduced speed hurting cut; fast blade speed creates suction under the deck
- fix: replace even if not the main cause

## page: causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring
- fact: tensioner arm and spring hold idler pulleys inline; spring can break or stretch; hole in bracket can wear; belt vibrates off

## page: causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing
- confirm: slowly rotate the pulley by hand, feel resistance or listen for bearing noise; grab each side and check for rocking play; good pulley sits securely
- fix: replace the pulley assembly with the bad bearing

## page: causes/bad-pto-switch
- fact: usually a knob powering the clutch from battery voltage
- confirm: check continuity; replace if there is a break

## page: causes/bad-pto-clutch
- fact: transfers engine power to the blades by engaging the drive belt; replace if worn or faulty

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: electric clutch uses battery power; fully charged 12 volt reads about 12.7 volts; charge if lower

## page: procedures/charge-the-battery
- step 1: access battery (under hood or seat); don't remove from casing
- step 2: red/positive cable first to positive terminal
- step 3: black/negative to negative terminal
- step 4: touch only rubber-coated things
- step 5: 12 volts, start 2 amps up to max 10 amps; slow charge best
- fact: replace if it won't hold charge; bring old battery; core fees average $20

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- fact: deck will not engage when operator is not in the seat; lifting out of the seat with deck engaged turns it off
- confirm: test seat switch with multimeter or temporarily bypass to diagnose
- safety: do not operate without safety switch

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fix: replace with same capacity fuse
- pro: if repeated, Hustler dealer or repair shop
