## page: problems/engine-only-runs-with-choke-on
- alias: "mower starts but won't stay running unless choke is on"
- causes-named: old-fuel, dirty-carburetor, clogged-fuel-line, cracked-fuel-line, clogged-fuel-filter, bad-fuel-pump, bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat, plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: engine is getting too much air or not enough fuel; choke compensates by restricting air to meet the air-to-fuel ratio
- fact: lack of fuel: old gas, dirty carburetor, clogged fuel line, bad fuel pump, plugged fuel filter, bad fuel cap. Too much air: bad carburetor gasket, puncture in fuel line
- safety: remove ignition key and spark plug boot before repairs

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: gas breaks down as soon as 30 days; ethanol attracts moisture, leaves varnish, is corrosive
- brand: Kubota requires unleaded with minimum 87 octane and maximum 10% ethanol; never more than 10%
- fix: drain old gas; add fresh gas with an additive like Sea Foam Motor Treatment or STA-BIL; run the engine so treated fuel works through; it may then run without choke

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: fuel passageways and internal parts clog, usually from old gas
- fix: clean (DIY if mechanical), else rebuild or replace; or have a small engine mechanic do it

## page: causes/cracked-fuel-line
- symptom: puncture or crack lets air be sucked in so the engine gets too much air
- confirm: inspect lines from the tank to the carburetor for punctures or cracks
- fix: replace with new line of same diameter

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: shut off fuel at the shut-off valve, remove end of a line section into a container, reopen flow and watch for good flow
- fix: no good flow - shut off, remove line, carburetor cleaner then compressed air, repeat; replace with same diameter if can't clear

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fact: inline filter between fuel lines; replace annually
- fix: new inline filter with arrow on the housing pointing in the direction of fuel flow

## page: causes/bad-fuel-pump
- fact: moves fuel uphill to carburetor; most Kubota mowers use a vacuum pump using engine vacuum
- confirm: confirm fuel reaches the inlet port, then remove the line from the carburetor into a container and test the flow
- fix: replace if insufficient flow, cracks or leaks

## page: causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat
- symptom: gasket behind carburetor wears and no longer seals; extra air makes engine run lean (higher concentration of air, less fuel); choke compensates
- confirm: inspect carburetor, check bolts tight and gasket condition
- fix: access carburetor, carefully remove linkages and bolts, remove carburetor and gasket, install new gasket, reattach carburetor, bolts and linkages; good time to clean the carburetor

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- fact: tank vents through the gas cap; plugged vent causes vacuum that stops fuel reaching the carburetor
- confirm: pressure gauge for vacuum; or: loosen cap, put choke off; if engine continues with choke off suspect cap; tighten cap and keep running choke off; if it sputters after a while and won't run well until cap loosened, bad cap
- fix: replace cap

## page: procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter
- step 1: remove plugged filter
- step 2: insert new inline filter between lines with arrow in flow direction
