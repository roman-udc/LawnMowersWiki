## page: problems/battery-mower-wont-start
- alias: "Greenworks mower won't start"
- causes-named: weak-or-dead-battery, battery-not-seated, bad-battery, bad-charger, overheated-battery, incorrect-starting-procedure, bad-safety-switch, bad-wiring-or-ground, debris-on-deck-or-pulleys, bad-motor
- fact: Summary: insufficient battery power (wrong size, incorrect install, dead/won't hold charge), loose or pinched wires, bad safety switch, debris under deck.
- safety: always remove the battery before electrical repairs and before working on the deck.

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- fact: use correct battery size for the model. Some models show charge via LEDs: on battery, on mower indicator panel, or by putting battery on charger.
- fact: going bad = fully charged but doesn't hold charge as long, charges very slowly, or gets very hot -> replace. Fails from age, extreme temperatures, mechanical failure.

## page: causes/battery-not-seated
- fix: battery should slide into grooves of the compartment; on most Greenworks you hear a click when seated.

## page: causes/bad-charger
- confirm: check charger-to-outlet connection; standard 120V outlet; plug style must match outlet; do not modify plug. Insert battery so pack contacts engage charger securely; battery lights show charge level.

## page: causes/overheated-battery
- fix: let it cool before restarting; mower shuts down when battery excessively hot.

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- brand: Greenworks uses a safety key to prevent children accidentally starting by depressing bail switch bar/handles.
- step 1: with battery securely in place, insert safety key.
- step 2: press and hold start button.
- step 3: pull the safety bar (bail switch) to meet handlebar, or the start handles to meet handlebar (varies by model).
- step 4: release the safety button.
- step 5: mower shuts off when you release the safety bar.

## page: causes/bad-safety-switch
- confirm: test each switch (incl. bail switch) by temporarily bypassing with jumper wire; replace bad switch.
- safety: never operate without all safety switches working; link to dangers of electric mowers article.

## page: causes/bad-wiring-or-ground
- confirm: wiggle wires to check for short; multimeter.
- fix: wire nut for loose/pinched connections; remove battery first; check with Greenworks about warranty - DIY wire repair may void it.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: grass clippings, dirt stuck under deck inhibit blade rotation; overloading from plugged deck can stop mower and prevent start.
- fix: remove battery; scrape deck.
- tip: reduce load: avoid cutting wet grass, raise cutting height in thick/tall grass, slow down.

## page: causes/bad-motor
- fact: start switch and motor damage: take to professional repair shop (pricey, need mechanical/electrical knowledge). Common replacement parts: batteries, blades, safety switches. Weigh repair cost versus mower value.
