## page: guides/choosing-the-right-gas
- fact: Husqvarna 4-cycle ("4-stage" in Source) mowers use unleaded gasoline, minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol. Avoid E15 and E85. 2-cycle engines (not covered): consult manual; require gas/oil mix.
- fact: Ethanol-free is best; sold at select stations as REC-90 / recreation fuel, or pricier in canisters at hardware stores as 4-cycle fuel.
- fact: Ethanol = corn-based fuel. Attracts moisture from air, causing separation; ethanol-water layer fed through engine causes ignition problems; ethanol is corrosive and burns hot, can cause overheating and irreversible damage. E85 (up to 85% ethanol) damages small engines.
- tip: Use freshly purchased gas within 30 days; don't stock up; buy from a busy gas station (faster turnover); don't let fuel sit outside collecting moisture; store away from combustibles.
- tip: Buying more than a 30-day supply: use a plastic gas can designed for unleaded (some plastic containers break down), add fuel stabilizer; Sea Foam stabilizes up to two years and reduces moisture (author's claim). Empty the can in use before adding new gas; use stored gas first; don't add fresh to old.
- tip: Basics: fresh fuel, stabilizer if needed, avoid more than 10% ethanol. Add stabilizer at end of season for storage.

## page: guides/fuel-storage-and-stabilizers
- fact: as above (storage container, stabilizer, 30 days, rotate stock).

## page: causes/wrong-fuel
- fact: Ethanol above 10% (E15, E85) harms Husqvarna engines.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: Gas should be used within 30 days; breaks down in containers over time.

## page: guides/winterizing-and-storage
- fact: At season end add fuel stabilizer to reduce moisture and gumming in the fuel system.
