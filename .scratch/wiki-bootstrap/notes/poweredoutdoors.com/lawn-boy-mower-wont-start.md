## page: problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start
- alias: "Lawn Boy won't start"
- fact: needs air, fuel and spark
- causes-named: empty-fuel-tank, fuel-valve-off, old-fuel, plugged-fuel-cap-vent, incorrect-starting-procedure, clogged-fuel-filter, clogged-fuel-line, dirty-carburetor, clogged-air-filter, fouled-spark-plug, weak-or-dead-battery, blown-fuse, bad-ignition-switch, bad-ignition-coil, bad-recoil-starter, bad-starter-solenoid

## page: causes/empty-fuel-tank
- fix: refill with fresh fuel; also check for a leak
- brand: 2-cycle engine (older Lawn Boys) needs a gas/oil mix

## page: causes/fuel-valve-off
- fact: valves used to shut fuel off for transport/storage; must be open
- fix: turn valve on; not all mowers have a shut-off valve

## page: causes/old-fuel
- fact: breaks down from 30 days after purchase; ethanol attracts corrosive moisture and leaves gummy residue
- fix: drain with fuel siphon pump; fresh fuel, ethanol max 10%, min octane 87 for a 4-stroke; older Lawn Boys may be 2-cycle needing gas/oil mix; add Sea Foam Motor Treatment
- brand: Lawn Boy 4-stroke: 87 octane, max 10% ethanol

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- confirm: run mower with and without cap; starts without but eventually shuts down with cap on means cap can't vent
- fix: clean cap or replace with new Lawn Boy cap

## page: causes/incorrect-starting-procedure
- brand: Lawn Boy push mowers have a blade control bar safety; pull it back and hold against handlebar to start and run
- fact: hold bar while turning key (electric start) or pulling recoil (manual); releasing it stops engine and blades
- fix: use right starting procedure; check safety switch works

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- fix: install new with arrow pointing toward carburetor, away from tank
- fact: change regularly

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: stop flow with shut-off valve (or crimp with pinch pliers if no valve); remove hose end furthest from tank into a container lower than the tank; start flow and see fuel
- fix: remove line, carb cleaner, compressed air, repeat; replace if can't clear

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- confirm: after verifying fuel reaches carb, remove air filter, spray carb cleaner in intake and start; starts, runs, shuts down means dirty carb
- fix: take apart and clean (author links push mower carb cleaning article)

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- tip: replace annually, check/clean several times a season
- step (paper): tap on solid surface; hold to light, reuse if light shows; replace if no light or covered in oil; install and attach cover
- step (foam): replace if dry, brittle, torn or damaged; wash in mild dish detergent and water; rinse until soap gone and water clear; lay flat to dry; when completely dry coat with foam filter oil; install and attach cover

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: dirty tip, cracked porcelain, burnt electrode; wrong gap or loose wires
- fix: new plug gapped per Lawn Boy operator's manual, wire secure; to clean use wire brush on carbon; very dark or damaged must be replaced

## page: causes/weak-or-dead-battery
- brand: electric-start Lawn Boy; try manual recoil; if it starts with recoil the electric start is the problem
- fix: charge with charger plugged into the port connector on the handle nearest the battery, into a 120-volt outlet; if it won't charge check the fuse; if fuse fine replace battery; still not charging means have mechanic troubleshoot charging

## page: causes/blown-fuse
- fact: bad fuse means battery won't charge and electric start won't work

## page: causes/bad-ignition-switch
- fix: test and replace if bad

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- confirm: spark plug must be good first; check coil continuity with ohm meter
- fix: replace if break in continuity

## page: causes/bad-recoil-starter
- symptom: rope unstrung, broken pulley, springs or clips
- fix: restring or replace broken parts; compare price of full recoil assembly, may be better to replace whole assembly

## page: causes/bad-starter-solenoid
- fact: electromagnetic on-off switch that actuates the starter
- symptom: click or hum on turning key; wires at solenoid get hot and smoke
- confirm: author links solenoid test

## page: guides/electric-mower-care-and-safety
- fact: charger plugs into port connector on handle nearest battery (Lawn Boy electric start)
