## page: problems/engine-starts-then-dies
- alias: "Gravely starts then dies"
- alias: "zero-turn sputters and stops mid-mow"

- pro: Contact a professional mechanic if unsure of skills.

## page: causes/old-fuel
- symptom: Most gas contains ethanol, which attracts moisture; causes corrosion and restrictions, gummy deposits clogging fuel components. Over time the water/ethanol mixture separates and sinks to the tank bottom; damaging when run through the engine at extreme temperatures.
- fact: Gas breaks down quickly; buy fresh and use within 30 days; add stabilizer if it will last longer.
- fact: Use unleaded with minimum 87 octane and max 10% ethanol; never high ethanol; low or ethanol-free is best.
- fix: Drain tank with a fuel siphon pump; refill with fresh fuel plus a fuel additive such as Sea Foam Motor Treatment (stabilizes, cleans fuel system, reduces moisture).

## page: causes/clogged-air-filter
- symptom: Dirty conditions plug the filter; airflow restricted, mower quits.
- fact: Author replaces annually at routine service; clean several times per season. Never run without an air filter, even briefly; a little dirt can mean engine damage.
- step 1: Remove filter from housing without letting dirt fall into the intake; wipe housing and cover with a dry cloth.
- step 2 (paper element): Tap the filter on a solid surface to loosen dirt.
- step 3: Hold up to a light; if light shines through, reuse; if not, replace.
- step 4: Reinstall filter and housing cap.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-filter
- symptom: Filter strains fuel leaving tank; dirt and gummy deposits clog it; engine runs sluggish and dies.
- fact: Replace annually.
- fix: Stop fuel flow, remove filter, install new. The arrow on the filter must point in the direction of fuel flow, toward the carburetor and away from the tank.

## page: causes/clogged-fuel-line
- confirm: Shut off fuel at the shut-off valve (or pinch line with pinch pliers); isolate a section; remove the end furthest from the tank into a container placed lower than the tank (fuel can't run uphill without the pump); open fuel flow; good flow means that section is fine; reattach and check next section.
- fix: Remove the blocked section, spray carb cleaner into the line, blow compressed air through, repeat; if it won't clear, replace the line.

## page: causes/dirty-carburetor
- symptom: Old fuel left in carb forms gummy hard deposits; parts stick or clog.
- confirm: First confirm fuel is reaching the carburetor. Remove air filter, spray carb cleaner into the intake, start; if it starts then dies after the cleaner burns off, carb needs cleaning.
- fix: Disassemble and clean (see carburetor cleaning guide).

## page: causes/dirty-cooling-fins
- symptom: Starts then dies when engine gets hot; fins plugged with debris or broken (fins push air around block and cylinder head).
- fix: Take caution on hot engine; remove debris from fins; replace broken fins; clean around engine and shroud; ensure heat shield is attached securely.

## page: causes/overfilled-oil
- symptom: Too much oil builds crankcase pressure; can overheat and shut down.
- fix: Remove oil via drain plug or oil filter, or evacuate through the fill area with an oil evacuator or turkey baster; check dipstick; add or remove until at the full line.

## page: causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil
- symptom: Oil leak or engine burning oil; too little oil lets friction build heat, engine overheats and dies; heat can melt internal parts.
- pro: If shut down from lack of oil, significant damage is likely; take to an experienced small engine mechanic to tear down and test.

## page: causes/fouled-spark-plug
- symptom: Dirty/fouled, damaged, not properly gapped, or loose wire makes it sluggish and die.
- fix: Clean tip; replace if very dark, burnt electrode or damaged; gap to engine manufacturer spec; attach wires securely. Replace plugs annually.

## page: causes/bad-ignition-coil
- symptom: Winding separates and shorts when hot; plugs lack voltage; mower dies after running a while.
- confirm: Ohmmeter check for a break in continuity.
- fix: Replace if there's a break.

## page: causes/wrong-choke-setting
- fix: Choke lever must be off once engine is warm for added airflow, or the mower will die (choke reduces air for cold starts).

## page: causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent
- symptom: Plugged vent makes a vacuum in tank, no fuel flow.
- confirm: Run with and without the cap; if it runs with cap off and eventually shuts off with cap on, the cap is the problem.
- fix: Clean the cap or replace.

## page: causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys
- symptom: Packed deck makes engine work harder, overheat and shut down.
- fix: Scrape deck regularly; avoid wet-grass mowing; deck spray can minimise buildup (not a miracle product).

## page: causes/bad-alternator
- symptom: (Battery not charging section) run at high engine speed, don't idle long; engine power needed to charge battery.
- confirm: If battery and wiring are good and you still keep charging it, suspect the charging system.
- pro: Have an experienced mechanic diagnose the charging system; many components; electrical parts usually can't be returned.
