# Best Place to Buy a Lawn Mower in Grand Rapids MI

Source: https://poweredoutdoors.com/best-place-buy-a-mower-grand-rapids-mi/

I greatly support small businesses that offer support after the sale. If you’re looking for a push, self-propelled, or entry-level riding mower, a big box store like Home Depot or Lowes may be right for you.

**However, if you’re looking for a place that will provide you with detailed information and provide support after the sale, I believe the best place to buy a lawn mower is at a dedicated lawn mower and outdoor equipment dealership**.

Don’t know where to buy a lawn mower in Grand Rapids? I’m here to help!

I have listed some full-service mower dealerships servicing Grand Rapids and the surrounding areas including **Ada, Allendale, Byron Center, Cascade, Grand Haven, Grandville, Holland, Jenison, Kentwood, Lowell, Muskegon, Sparta, Wyoming, and more**.

## Why Choose a Lawn Mower Dealership in Grand Rapids

- **Dealership and Manufacturer Support**: Most dealerships have a good working relationship with the manufacturer and are able to provide support when you run into a problem with your purchase.
- **Stocking Parts Dealer**: Dealers typically keep a good supply of parts on hand to keep your mower up and running. Always ask your dealer if they are able to get parts quickly that aren’t kept in stock.
- **Servicing Dealer**: Many top brands require dealers to have manufacturer-trained individuals on staff to provide repairs and warranty support.
- **Trained Sales Team**: Available to answer your questions and identify the best mower for your yard size and terrain.
- **Equipment Set-Up and Delivery**: A trained individual sets up and inspects equipment. Many dealerships offer delivery as a service.
- **Test the Equipment**: Most dealers will allow you to operate a mower to see how it cuts and maneuvers.

## Things to Consider Before You Buy at a Big Box Store or Online

While buying at a big box store or online is convenient, there are a few things to consider before placing that order.

- **Closest Service Location**: Is there a close location for service? Will you have to drive far for service or have to ship your mower to a service location?
- **Turn Around Time for Repairs**: Will you be without a mower for long?
- **Parts Accessibility**: Is there a place to quickly source parts nearby or online?
- **Equipment Set-Up**: Will you have to assemble your lawn mower before using?
