# How do I fix a flat tire on my riding mower?

Source: https://www.searspartsdirect.com/diy/symptom/riding-mowers-tractor-repair/1234652/flat-tire/ltrtr142

Check your tractor tires regularly. When a tire goes flat, you can often [repair it with tire sealant](https://www.searspartsdirect.com/diy/article/how-to-repair-a-riding-lawn-mower-tire-video). You can also sometimes install an inner tube to repair leaks that you can't fix the leak with tire sealant. Replace the tire if you find too much damage to repair.

## Replace the riding mower front tire

Riding lawn mower tires are durable, but like all tires they wear out over time or can be punctured. Replace a damaged front lawn tractor tire with a new one that's the same size as the other front tire.

Repair guide: [How to replace a riding lawn mower front tire](https://www.searspartsdirect.com/diy/repair-guide/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-front-tire)

## Replace the riding mower rear tire

Rear tires on a riding lawn mower need deep tread for good traction to move the mower through the grass when mowing. Replace a worn or damaged rear tire with one that's the same size as the other rear tire.

Repair guide: [How to replace a riding lawn mower rear tire](https://www.searspartsdirect.com/diy/repair-guide/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-rear-tire)
