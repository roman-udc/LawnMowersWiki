# Belt types

## Summary

The kinds of V-belts used on mowers, the difference between a drive belt and a deck belt, and how to buy the right one. Use it when a customer asks which belt to buy, or alongside [Belt keeps coming off](../problems/belt-keeps-coming-off.md) and [Wrong belt](../causes/wrong-belt.md).

Sources: [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/v-belts-what-are-they-weve-got-the-answer.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/this-is-why-your-belt-keeps-falling-off-your-lawn-mower.md)

## Key facts

- **V-belts** carry power from the engine to the blades or wheels. They are trapezoid-shaped closed loops of strong synthetic cords encased in rubber.
- **Three main types in America**, chosen by engine horsepower:

| Type | Power range | Designation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fractional horsepower | Under 1 hp | 2L, 3L, 4L, 5L (number = top width in eighths of an inch) | Small, often electric motors (fans, washing machines). |
| Classic | A fraction of 1 hp up to 500 hp | Letters A to E plus inside length (A is 0.5 in wide, B 0.66 in, C 0.88 in) | Oldest style; used on lawn mowers; sturdy and tolerates dirt. |
| Narrow | 1 hp to 1,000 hp | Top-width number, "V", outside length (for example 5V500) | About three times the power of a classic belt. |

- **Riding mowers usually have two belts:** a drive belt (engine power to the wheels, forward and reverse; typically polyester cords) and a deck belt (turns the blades). They do different jobs, so buy them as two different belts even if both are classic or narrow.
- **The deck belt should be sturdier than the drive belt:** when blades slow in thick or tall grass the belt keeps pulling, creating high friction. The author of one Source suggests Kevlar cords rather than polyester for a deck belt (single expert-blog view).
- **Cost:** mower belts cost about $20 to $200; a good OEM deck belt about $80 to $250.

## Recommendations

1. Buy the OEM belt for the model. Hardware-store belts can differ in type, size and quality, and a wrong belt may not stay on. See [Wrong belt](../causes/wrong-belt.md).
2. Match the type and size of the old belt; drive and deck belts are not interchangeable.
3. For a transmission drive belt, one Source recommends a repair shop. See [Replace the deck belt](../procedures/replace-the-deck-belt.md) for deck belts.

## Tips & life hacks

- An end-of-season shop inspection can save time and money; some shops run winter specials and guarantee the work into spring.
- If a belt won't stay on, check the deck as well as the belt. See [Belt keeps coming off](../problems/belt-keeps-coming-off.md).

## Common mistakes

- Using a fractional-horsepower belt to replace a classic or narrow belt: it handles only a fraction of 1 hp and breaks under heavier loads.
- Buying a cheap generic belt instead of the OEM belt.
- Running a cracked or stretched belt: see [Worn or stretched belt](../causes/worn-or-stretched-belt.md) and [Worn drive belt](../causes/worn-drive-belt.md).
