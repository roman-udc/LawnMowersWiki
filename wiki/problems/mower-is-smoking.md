# Mower is smoking

## Summary

Smoke comes from the engine or muffler while the mower runs, sometimes as a big cloud at start-up or after an oil change. Two things make a gas mower smoke: the engine burns more fuel than air (it runs rich, giving black smoke), or engine oil gets hot and burns off, either inside the cylinder or on the outside of a hot engine or muffler (blue or white smoke). The cheap causes are a plugged air filter, too much or too little oil, and oil spilled during an oil change. The expensive ones are internal: piston rings, the valve train, or a leaking engine gasket. Smoke isn't always an expensive repair, but caught late it can cost an engine.

## Quick triage

1. **Stop the engine.** Shut it off as soon as you see smoke and don't keep running it; a small problem can become a big one. Let the engine cool, remove the key and the spark plug wire(s) before working on it. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-mower-is-smoking.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-blowing-smoke.md), [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-mower-is-smoking-white-blue-or-black-smoke.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-is-blowing-blue-black-or-white-smoke.md)
2. **Note the smoke colour.** Black or gray: the engine runs rich, so start with the air flow (step 3). Blue or white: oil is burning, so start with the oil (steps 4 to 6). Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-smoking.md), [S22](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-toro-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S23](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
3. **Air filter.** Clean or replace a plugged filter. If the filter is fine, look for another air restriction such as a choke that is on or stuck. An oil-soaked filter on a push mower means the mower was tipped. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md)
4. **Spilled oil.** If the oil was just changed, look for oil on the engine or muffler and wipe it off. Sources: [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-starts-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md), [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-begins-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md)
5. **Oil level.** Too much: drain a little until the level is at the manufacturer's mark. Too little: adding oil is often too late, and the mower should be checked for engine damage by a shop. Sources: [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-gravely-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-john-deere-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-troy-bilt-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-snapper-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
6. **Oil leaks.** Look for oil dripping onto the muffler from a leaking gasket. Sources: [S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-honda-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
7. **Spark plug.** Remove the plug (a 3/4 in or 5/8 in socket, depending on the engine) and look for oil on it. Oil means an internal problem (piston rings or valve train): send it to a small engine mechanic. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Check the oil level and the air filter before each mowing; it could prevent costly repairs. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S38](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-mower-is-smoking.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S22](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-toro-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
- Start with the easiest checks and rule them out before the internal ones. Sources: [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-smoking.md), [S22](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-toro-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
- After the repair, run the mower for 10 to 15 minutes to burn off the oil left inside, until the smoking stops. Source: [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md)
- Overfilled oil can make the engine smoke and overheat; whitish-blue smoke after an overfill comes from oil burning in the cylinder. Sources: [S58](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/guide-to-draining-oil-from-your-lawn-mower.md), [S59](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/this-is-what-happens-if-you-put-too-much-oil-in-a-lawn-mower.md), [S50](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/too-much-oil-bad-boy.md), [S51](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/too-much-oil-cub-cadet-lawn-mower.md), [S60](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/too-much-oil-husqvarna-mower.md), [S52](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/too-much-oil-john-deere.md), [S53](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/too-much-oil-kubota-mower.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/too-much-oil-toro-mower.md), [S62](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-loses-power.md)
- Wrong fuel, or fuel mixed with another substance, can also make a mower smoke; use fresh, correct gas and a stabilizer if it won't be used within 30 days. Source: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas.md)
- Blue or white smoke can also come from a blocked breather tube or from tilting the mower so oil leaks out. Source: [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-replacement.md)
- A Toro article lists the same set (too much oil, too little oil, plugged air filter, piston ring failure, valve train problem, failed engine gasket) when the engine quits when hot. Source: [S64](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/solved-toro-lawn-mower-quits-when-hot.md)
- Air filter types and cleaning are covered in detail by one guide. Source: [S56](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/guide-to-lawn-mower-air-filters-differences-how-to-clean-them.md)

## Safety

- Stop the engine as soon as smoke appears. Wait for it to cool and for moving parts to stop, then remove the key and the spark plug wire(s) before any repair. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-blowing-smoke.md), [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-mower-is-smoking-white-blue-or-black-smoke.md), [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-problems-and-solutions.md)
- Take care around the hot engine, for example when removing the air filter or wiping up oil. Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-starts-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md), [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-begins-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md)
- Follow the operator's manual safety instructions, and get a professional if you aren't skilled or able to repair it safely. Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S54](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-ariens-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## When to send to a technician

- Oil on the spark plug, or smoke that remains after the air filter and oil level are right: piston ring or valve train problems need a small engine mechanic to tear the engine down and run leak-down and compression checks (see [Check compression](../procedures/check-compression.md)). Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-mower-is-smoking.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-blowing-smoke.md), [S33](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-riding-lawn-mower-problems.md)
- Smoking from low oil: there is likely significant engine damage; send it to the dealer or a small engine shop. Sources: [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-is-blowing-blue-black-or-white-smoke.md), [S40](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-scag-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-ariens-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
- Before paying for an engine repair, get a quote and compare it with a replacement engine or a new mower, depending on the damage and the mower's age. Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-is-blowing-blue-black-or-white-smoke.md)
