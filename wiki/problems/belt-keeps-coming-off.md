# Belt keeps coming off

## Summary

The deck belt (or sometimes the drive belt) keeps slipping off the pulleys, or keeps breaking or shredding, so you have to keep putting it back on. When the deck belt is off the blades stop; when the drive belt is off the mower won't move. A belt comes off when it is loose or worn, when the tensioner spring or bracket is worn, when a bearing in a pulley or spindle housing has failed, when a belt keeper or bracket is bent out of place, when debris on the deck stops the tensioner or packs the pulley grooves, or when oil or rust gets on the belt. Find the cause before fitting a new belt: deck belts are pricey, and a new one will come off too.

## Quick triage

1. **Make it safe.** Remove the ignition key and the spark plug boot(s), set the parking brake, and chock the wheels; wear gloves.
2. **Inspect the belt.** Replace a stretched, worn, cracked or glazed belt. If it's shredded, confirm it's the right belt and routed as the decal or manual shows, then find the bracket it rubs (a shiny spot on the metal).
3. **Check the tensioner.** Replace a worn bracket or spring, and make sure the tensioner moves freely.
4. **Spin the pulleys and check the spindles.** Replace a pulley bearing or pulley assembly, or a spindle bearing or housing assembly, that is rough or loose.
5. **Clean the deck.** Remove leaves, mud, wood chips and stones from under the tensioner and debris from the pulley grooves.
6. **Check the keepers.** Bend bent keepers back to 1/8" to 1/4" from the pulley, or replace them.
7. **Stop oil and rust.** Repair an oil leak, clean the deck, remove rust and replace very rusty pulleys.
8. **Fit a new belt** once the cause is fixed: [Replace the deck belt](../procedures/replace-the-deck-belt.md).

## Brand notes

- **Kubota:** on some models the deck has to come off every time to put the belt back on.
- **Cub Cadet riding mowers:** the deck must be removed to change the deck belt, which is time consuming.
- **Bad Boy:** most models need 70-75 lbs of belt tension (check the manual); too little lets the belt slip or come off, too much damages deck components.
- **Exmark:** new belts often snap because foreign material is caught in the pulleys, not because they are old.
- **MTD push mowers:** if the belt is off the crankshaft pulley when you open the mower, it was knocked off or is too loose and needs replacing.

## Tips & life hacks

- Extend belt life: clean the deck after each mowing, check for rubbing on deck components, keep oil and rust off the belt, periodically check the pulleys and spindle housings for bearing failure, and fix or replace damaged brackets and belt keepers.
- Isolate the main cause before replacing the belt.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** belt prices are given as $60 to $250 ([S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), expert-blog), $20 to $200 ([S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/this-is-why-your-belt-keeps-falling-off-your-lawn-mower.md), expert-blog) and $80 to $250 for OEM riding mower deck belts ([S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-belt-keeps-falling-off-your-riding-mower-fixed.md), expert-blog).

## Safety

- Before working on the deck, remove the ignition key and disconnect the spark plug wire(s) or boot(s); set the parking brake and chock the wheels so the mower can't start or roll.
- Raise the deck securely on jack stands (some zero-turns require removing the deck). Wear heavy work gloves: the blades and deck have sharp edges.
- Never work on the deck until all moving parts have stopped.
- Dry clippings and leaves near the hot belt can catch fire: keep the deck clean.
