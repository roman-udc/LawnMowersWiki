# Engine won't crank or turn over

## Summary

You turn the key and the engine doesn't spin: nothing happens at all, or you only hear a click or a hum. In most cases the starter motor isn't getting the power it needs to turn the engine. The usual suspects, in the order most Sources check them, are a weak or dead battery, loose or corroded battery cables and wiring, a bad ground, a blown fuse, a bad ignition switch, a bad safety switch, a bad starter solenoid and, last, the starter motor itself. Less often the engine can't turn because it is seized, or hydrolocked by too much oil. On a pull-start mower, a broken recoil starter stops you turning the engine over.

If the engine spins normally but never fires, see Related problems.

## Quick triage

1. **Starting conditions:** set the parking brake and disengage the blades (PTO); the mower won't start otherwise. On a riding mower or zero-turn, sit in the seat.
2. **Battery:** test it with a multimeter. A 12-volt battery should read 12.7 volts or more; charge it if it reads less, and replace it if it won't hold a charge.
3. **Cables and terminals:** tighten loose cables, clean corrosion off the terminals and replace damaged cables.
4. **Ground:** the battery ground cable and the starter solenoid ground cable must make good, corrosion-free contact. A 3-post solenoid is self-grounded.
5. **Fuse:** check for a blown fuse and replace it with one of the same amperage.
6. **Ignition switch:** test it with a multimeter.
7. **Safety switches:** test the seat, brake and PTO switches.
8. **Starter solenoid:** test it with the battery fully charged. A click means the solenoid coil is getting power from the battery through the ignition switch; no click means the solenoid failed or its coil isn't getting power.
9. **Engine and starter motor:** check that the engine isn't seized. If everything above checks good and the solenoid clicks but the starter doesn't run, suspect the starter motor.

## Tips & life hacks

- **Narrow it down before buying parts.** Throwing expensive parts at the problem gets costly; a volt-ohm meter, a battery charger and a continuity light cover most checks. Source: [S41](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-tell-your-lawn-mower-solenoid-is-bad.md)
- **Rule out the cheap things before the starter.** Check the battery, cables, wiring, ground and solenoid first. Starters are pricey: have a dealer confirm the starter is bad, or take it to a starter and alternator shop, which can test and often rebuild it. Sources: [S23](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-clicks-wont-start.md), [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-mower-wont-turn-over.md), [S66](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-clicks-but-wont-turn-over.md)
- **Charge the battery before testing the solenoid.** The solenoid test needs a fully charged battery. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/a-walker-mower-wont-turn-over.md)
- **Intermittent starting on a lawn tractor:** check the wiring connections on the seat and brake interlock switches. Side-to-side play in the brake pedal can keep the brake arm from touching the brake switch; tighten the brake arm to remove the play. Source: [S101](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md)
- **If the solenoid tests good** and the mower still won't turn over, go back to the battery, cables, starter switch and safety switches. Sources: [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-starter-solenoid-bad.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-husqvarna-starter-solenoid.md), [S69](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-bad-solenoid.md)

## Parts commonly needed

- **12-volt battery:** replace it if it won't hold a charge.
- **Battery cables and terminals:** replace damaged cables; clean and tighten the rest.
- **Fuse:** replace with one of the same amperage.
- **Ignition switch, safety switches (seat, brake, PTO), starter solenoid:** replace a part that fails its multimeter test.
- **Starter motor:** replace it with a manufacturer-approved part once everything else checks good.
