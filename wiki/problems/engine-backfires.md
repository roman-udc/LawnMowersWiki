# Engine backfires

## Summary

The engine pops or bangs. The Sources give two situations. While running, a dirty or bad carburetor can make the engine run lean (too much air, not enough fuel) and backfire. When you shut it off, a hot engine can backfire if too much fuel went into it before the ignition was turned off: the heat of the engine ignites the fuel left in the cylinder.

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/clean-your-cub-cadet-carburetor.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-riding-mower-carburetor.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md)

## Diagnostic questions

| Question | Answer A → Causes | Answer B → Causes |
|---|---|---|
| Does it backfire only when you shut off a hot engine? | Yes → shutdown backfire: idle at the lowest throttle for 1 minute before shutting off | No → next question |
| Does it backfire while running, perhaps with sputtering, surging or hard starting? | Yes → [Dirty carburetor](../causes/dirty-carburetor.md) (running lean) | No → check the carburetor anyway |

Sources: [S6](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/clean-your-cub-cadet-carburetor.md)

## Quick triage

1. **Backfire at shutdown:** move the throttle to the lowest position and let the engine idle for 1 minute before turning it off.
2. **Backfire while running:** clean the carburetor ([Clean the carburetor](../procedures/clean-the-carburetor.md)); if you aren't comfortable with small parts, a small engine repair shop can clean or rebuild it.
