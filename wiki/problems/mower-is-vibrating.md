# Mower is vibrating

## Summary

Every running mower vibrates a little, and an uneven lawn adds bounce. This Problem is vibration or shaking that is clearly worse than normal: the handle shakes your arms on a push mower, or the seat shakes so hard on a riding or zero-turn mower that the owner feels they'll be shaken off it. It usually comes from something loose, worn, out of balance or stuck: loose or missing engine mounting bolts and other hardware, a failing blade clutch bearing, an unbalanced, bent or wrong blade, string or weeds wrapped around the blade shaft, a bad spindle bearing, a worn deck belt or pulley bearing, or debris such as a stone, acorn or wood chip lodged in the deck. On a push mower a bent crankshaft is also possible. Fixing it early prevents further damage to the mower.

## Quick triage

1. **Make it safe.** Park on a flat, level surface, set the parking brake, remove the key and disconnect the spark plug wire(s) (some engines have two). On a battery mower remove the safety key and the battery. Wait for all parts to stop moving. Wear work gloves around the blades. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-lawn-mower-vibration.md), [S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md)
2. **Engine mounting bolts and other hardware.** The bolts that hold the engine to the frame absorb engine vibration. Tighten loose ones and replace missing, worn or broken ones; also look for other loose or missing nuts and bolts. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S44](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-exmark-mower-problems-and-solutions.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md)
3. **Blade clutch or engagement lever.** An electric clutch has a ball bearing that can fail and make the mower vibrate; a manual-engagement setup has bushings and linkages that wear. Push mowers have no electric clutch but have a blade engagement lever to check. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-craftsman-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S44](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-vibrating.md)
4. **Blades.** Look for a bent, damaged or unbalanced blade (worn more on one side by sand or uneven sharpening). If a blade was just replaced, confirm it is the right length and center-hole size and installed correctly. Balance blades before installing them. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S53](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S33](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-find-prevent-substantial-lawn-mower-deck-problems.md)
5. **Blade shaft.** Look at the top of each blade for string, wire or long weeds wrapped around the shaft; remove it and check the spindle for damage. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-cub-cadet-mower-problems.md)
6. **Spindle bearings.** Grab each blade at both ends and rock it up and down; any movement means a bad bearing, so replace the bearing or the whole spindle (cutter) housing. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-kubota-problems-and-solutions.md)
7. **Belt and pulleys.** Remove the deck covers, clean out the area, and check the belt for wear. Turn each pulley by hand: restriction or wobble from a bad bearing means replace it. Check the idler bracket and spring. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S36](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md), [S50](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-shaking.md)
8. **Debris.** Check every crevice of the deck and mower: a small stone, acorn or wood chip wedged in a pulley can rattle the whole deck each time the belt passes it. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S52](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-solved.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-craftsman-lawn-mower-problems.md)
9. **Push mower, still shaking.** If the blade and deck parts check out, the crankshaft may be bent from a blade strike. Sources: [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-honda-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S43](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mower-deck-is-vibrating.md)

## Brand notes

- **Honda push mowers:** named causes include worn engine mounting bolts, a loose engagement lever, a broken blade adapter, a bent or unbalanced blade, a bent crankshaft and a sheared flywheel key. An unbalanced blade can also damage the crankshaft. Sources: [S32](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/change-sharpen-your-honda-lawn-mower-blades.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-honda-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
- **EGO (battery):** the mower vibrates when the blade is installed incorrectly, bent or unbalanced; if the blade hit a foreign object the motor shaft may be damaged. Sources: [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-is-vibrating.md)
- **Cub Cadet:** some models use two spark plugs, so disconnect both before working on the deck. Sources: [S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-is-vibrating.md)
- **Kubota and Gravely zero-turns:** impact damage to a spindle bearing sends vibration through the mower when the deck is engaged, and an unbalanced blade after sharpening can damage the spindle bearing. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/change-and-sharpen-your-kubota-lawn-mower-blades.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/change-sharpen-your-gravely-mower-blades.md)
- **Riding mowers and tractors (Sears):** vibration on blade engagement points to the blade drive system: an unbalanced blade, a worn pulley or mandrel (spindle), a bad blade belt, debris caught inside a pulley, or loose nuts and bolts on the pulleys, mandrels or blades. Sources: [S62](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/mower-deck-vibrates.ltrtr140.md), [S64](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/won-t-cut-grass.ridtr01.md), [S63](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md)
- **Gravely and Simplicity:** the two brands' vibration articles are nearly identical. Sources: [S58](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-gravely-lawn-mower-vibration.md), [S47](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-is-vibrating.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Don't overlook the small stuff: authors repeatedly found a single wood chip, acorn or pebble wedged in a pulley causing the whole vibration. Check every option, however odd. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S52](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-solved.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-kubota-problems-and-solutions.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md)
- Clean dirt and debris off the mower after each mowing, and check for loose or missing hardware regularly. Sources: [S35](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-vibrating-problem.md), [S59](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-lawn-tractor-is-vibrating.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md), [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md)
- Each time you change the blades, also check the spindle and pulley bearings, and install the correct, balanced blades. Sources: [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md)
- A blade can be checked for balance with a blade balancer or by hanging it on a nail. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md)
- Vibration can make pulleys and blades wobble enough to loosen the deck belt or throw it off. Sources: [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-mower-problems.md), [S36](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)
