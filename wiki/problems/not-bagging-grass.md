# Mower doesn't bag grass

## Summary

The bagger leaves clippings (or leaves) in the yard, the bags won't fill, or the catcher keeps clogging. Every Source gives the same root cause: lost airflow. The system needs air to carry the clippings from the deck into the bags, and anything that restricts it stops the bagging: buildup under the deck, in the chute, the blower tubes, the inlet tube or the inlet screen; worn or wrongly fitted blades; a slow engine; bad belts; a worn fan or blower housing on a belt-driven system; wet grass; and bags that are wrong, worn or not fitted tightly. First identify the collection system: a flow system uses the blades' airflow alone (on a push mower the grass goes straight through the discharge chute into the bag), a belt-driven system adds a belt-driven fan (impeller) in a blower housing, and a pull-behind collector has its own engine and vacuum.

## Quick triage

The Source's order of checks:

1. Clogged blower tubes.
2. Clogged deck (raise the mower safely on jacks or jack stands to look underneath).
3. Worn blades.
4. Wrong or incorrectly installed blades.
5. Clogged chute.
6. Clogged inlet tube.
7. Plugged inlet screen.
8. Bad deck belt.
9. Bad blower belt.
10. Worn fan or housing.
11. Bad blower bearings.
12. Engine speed.
13. Wet grass.
14. Wrong or damaged bags.
15. Bagger not secured.

## Tips & life hacks

- Don't mow wet grass and expect good bagging.
- Mow at full throttle and a slower ground speed.
- Spray silicone on the underside of the deck, inside the blower tubes and on the inlet screen; scrape the deck regularly and run sharp blades.
- Seal any area of the bagger where air can escape.
- For bagging, use a high-lift blade (a taller sail).
- Leaves may need more than one pass.
- For general owner know-how, see the Guide: [Cleaning the mower](../guides/cleaning-the-mower.md).

## Safety

- Follow the operator's manual safety instructions before diagnosing or repairing.
- Raise the mower with jacks or jack stands that keep it in the air before looking under the deck.
- Remove the key and spark plug boots before scraping the deck.
