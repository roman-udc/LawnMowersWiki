# Engine starts then dies

## Summary

The engine starts, then stalls after a few seconds, a few minutes, or partway through the job. An engine needs air, fuel and spark to keep running, and most Sources say a mower that starts but won't stay running is short of one of them, usually fuel. Old gas leaves deposits that restrict the fuel filter, fuel lines, fuel pump and carburetor, and a plugged gas cap vent starves the engine as it runs. A clogged air filter or a choke left on chokes off the air. A fouled spark plug or an ignition coil that fails when hot cuts the spark. The second big group is overheating and overload: dirty cooling fins, too much or too little oil, a plugged deck or dull blades. On a riding mower, a bad seat switch can kill the engine when the brake is released. Battery mowers stop when the battery is flat or hot, a wire is loose, or the motor is overloaded.

If the engine spins but never fires, or doesn't spin at all, see Related problems.

## Quick triage

1. **Fuel level.** Check the tank isn't low or empty. Source: [S82](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md)
2. **Gas cap.** Loosen the cap (don't take it off) and start the engine. If it now keeps running, the cap vent is plugged: clean or replace the cap. Sources: [S82](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md), [S37](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-fuel.md), [S77](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-lawn-tractor-isnt-getting-fuel.md)
3. **Choke.** The choke is for cold starts. Left on after warm-up it keeps restricting the air, and the engine runs sluggishly and shuts off. Sources: [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-john-deere-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S64](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-problems.md)
4. **Oil level.** Too much oil raises crankcase pressure, and too little oil causes friction and heat; either can overheat the engine until it dies. Correct the level to the engine maker's mark. Sources: [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-scag-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-cub-cadet-mower-problems.md), [S41](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)
5. **Fuel age.** Old gas restricts the fuel filter, lines, pump and carburetor. Drain it and refill with fresh fuel; several Sources add a stabilizer or cleaning additive. Sources: [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-gravely-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-mower-problems.md), [S68](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md)
6. **Air filter.** Clean or replace a plugged filter. Sources: [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-gravely-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-snapper-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
7. **Fuel filter, lines and pump.** Look for a plugged filter, clogged lines or a pump that doesn't move fuel. Clear lines with carburetor cleaner and compressed air; replace a plugged filter or bad pump. Sources: [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-john-deere-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-husqvarna-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
8. **Carburetor.** If the engine starts on a spray of carburetor cleaner and then won't keep running, clean the carburetor; rebuild or replace it if cleaning doesn't work. Sources: [S75](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-john-deere-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S77](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-lawn-tractor-isnt-getting-fuel.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-mower-problems.md)
9. **Cooling fins and deck.** Clean fins packed with dirt, oil or grass and replace broken ones. Scrape a plugged deck. Sources: [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-ariens-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S20](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-troy-bilt-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-bad-boy-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
10. **Spark plug and ignition coil.** Clean or replace a dirty plug, secure the wires and gap it. An ignition coil whose windings separate and short when hot shows a break in continuity on an ohmmeter: replace it. Because the engine starts, it has spark, so one Source says the plug is not the first place to start. Sources: [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-gravely-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-honda-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S32](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/fix-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md)
11. **Battery and charging system.** A battery that doesn't hold a charge or a charging system that doesn't charge can make a riding mower die after running. Test both and replace bad parts. Sources: [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-gravely-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S73](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-common-lawn-tractor-problems-solutions.md)

## Brand notes

- **Craftsman zero-turns with some Kohler 7000 Series engines:** the engine can stall because air in the evaporator line between the fuel tank and the carburetor interrupts fuel flow. A service technician has to connect a vent line between the evaporator line and the air filter housing. Source: [S82](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md)
- **Bad Boy:** the fuel tank vents through the gas cap or rollover vents; plugged vents restrict fuel. Source: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-bad-boy-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)
- **Husqvarna, Simplicity:** a fuel pump moves gas to the carburetor on models where the carburetor sits higher than the tank. Sources: [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-husqvarna-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S64](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-problems.md)
- **Husqvarna zero-turns:** often built with a Kawasaki engine. For an engine problem, go to a Husqvarna service center or the engine maker's dealer (Kawasaki, Kohler and so on). Source: [S42](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-starts-then-dies.md)
- **EGO (battery):** the mower shuts off when the battery is dead, the battery or circuits are hot, wiring is loose, pinched or damaged, or the safety switch is bad, and when the motor is overloaded by a plugged deck, dull blades, a fast ground speed or wet, tall or thick grass. Remove the safety key and battery before any repair. Repairs may void the warranty; for a bad motor, EGO customer service (1-855-346-5656 for mowers, 1-877-346-9876 for zero-turns) finds a repair location. Source: [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-starts-then-stops.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Routine maintenance prevents most of these: start each season with a full service, and check the oil and air filter, scrape the deck and sharpen the blade every year. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/8-reasons-your-lawn-mower-wont-stay-running.md), [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S56](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-lawn-tractor-starts-then-dies.md)
- Check the oil each time you mow. A dropping level can reveal a leak or oil burning before it damages the engine. Source: [S65](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md)
- A plugged cap vent and a clog moving through the fuel system both show up only after mowing a while, so a mower that runs fine at first can still have a fuel problem. Sources: [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md), [S66](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-exmark-mower-problems-and-solutions.md)
- A dull blade makes a plugged deck worse; scrape the deck and sharpen the blade together. Sources: [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-mower-problems.md)
- Don't throw parts at it: work through the checks in order, and ask a mechanic if you aren't sure of the cause. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/8-reasons-your-lawn-mower-wont-stay-running.md)

## Safety

- Remove the ignition key and the spark plug wires (or boots) before any repair, so the engine can't start. On a battery mower, remove the safety key and the battery. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-starts-then-dies.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-starts-then-stops.md)
- Let the engine cool and wait for all parts to stop moving. Set the parking brake on a riding mower. Sources: [S31](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-or-zero-turn-starts-then-dies.md), [S65](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S55](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-wont-stay-running.md)
- Wear eye protection and protect your hands from sharp parts. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md)
- Never disable the seat switch: it is an essential safety device that kills the engine when the brake is released with nobody in the seat. Source: [S81](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-dies-when-you-release-the-brake-video.md)
- Follow the safety instructions in the operator's manual. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/8-reasons-your-lawn-mower-wont-stay-running.md), [S35](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-mower-starts-then-dies.md)

## When to send to a technician

- The owner isn't sure of the cause or can't do the repair safely: engine and electrical problems are often better taken to a mechanic, dealer or repair shop. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/8-reasons-your-lawn-mower-wont-stay-running.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md), [S65](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md)
- The oil level has been corrected and the problem continues: low-oil shutdowns often cause permanent engine damage. Sources: [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-dies-while-mowing.md), [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-scag-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S30](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-problems-and-solutions.md)
- The wrong fuel was used, or problems continue after fresh fuel. Sources: [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-dies-while-mowing.md), [S23](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-dies-while-mowing.md)
- The charging system needs diagnosing, or an internal engine problem is suspected. Sources: [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-dies-while-mowing.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md)
- Everything above has been checked and it still dies. Source: [S32](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/fix-lawn-mower-starts-then-dies.md)
- Kohler 7000 vent-line fix (see Brand notes), or a failed motor on a battery mower. Sources: [S82](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-starts-then-stops.md)
