# Loose steering

## Summary

The riding mower's steering wheel has too much play, the wheels don't turn with the steering wheel, steering slips when turning, the mower won't steer one way or the other, or the front wheels point outward, wobble or don't line up. The cause is worn or loose steering parts (tie rods, steering shaft, sector gear, bushings, washers, gears) or a worn front axle or bent wheel spindle.

## Quick triage

1. Check the tie rods, steering shaft and sector gear assembly, and the bushings, washers and gears; replace damaged parts.
2. Check the front wheels and front axle: a bent or worn wheel spindle or a worn front axle won't allow proper steering.

Sources: [S7](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/loose-steering.ridtr09.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-troy-bilt-lawn-mower-problems.md)
