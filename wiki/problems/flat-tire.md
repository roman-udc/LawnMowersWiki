# Flat tire

## Summary

A riding mower tire has gone flat. Most flats are punctures in the tread, often from a nail or screw, and can be repaired with tire sealant, which seals punctures up to 1/4 inch in diameter. An inner tube can fix some leaks that sealant can't. A tire with sidewall damage, or too damaged to repair, is replaced.

## Quick triage

1. Inflate the tire and spray or brush soapy water over it: bubbles show the leak. Look for a nail or screw.
2. Tread puncture: seal it with tire sealant ([Repair a flat tire](../procedures/repair-a-flat-tire.md)).
3. Leak sealant can't fix: install an inner tube.
4. Sidewall damage or a tire too damaged to repair: [Replace a tire](../procedures/replace-a-tire.md).
