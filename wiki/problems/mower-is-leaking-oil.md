# Mower is leaking oil

## Summary

Oil leaks from the engine. The Sources give two causes. A bad engine gasket (the Sources rarely say which; a head gasket or sump gasket) lets oil leak out, and oil dripping onto the hot muffler burns off and smokes. Too much oil in the crankcase builds pressure that can blow out seals and gaskets, so the engine leaks oil. Check the oil level first.

## Quick triage

1. Check the oil level. If it is overfilled, drain oil down to the full mark on the dipstick.
2. Look for the leak: a leaking head gasket or sump gasket, or oil dripping onto the muffler. Replace a damaged engine gasket.

## Brand notes

- **Exmark, John Deere:** how hard an engine gasket is to replace depends on where it is; some are easy to find and replace.
- **Twin-cylinder engines (Husqvarna and others):** gaskets blown by overfilling cost more to replace than on a push mower, because the engine has to be removed.

## Tips & life hacks

- Overfilling caught early may be fixed by draining to the full mark; left too long, it can need significant engine work.

## Parts commonly needed

- Engine oil to bring the level right.

## Safety

- Oil leaking onto the hot muffler burns off and smokes.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-mower-is-smoking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## When to send to a technician

- A gasket in an awkward place, or one that needs the engine removed (twin-cylinder engines).
- Overfilling that has gone on long enough to cause damage.
