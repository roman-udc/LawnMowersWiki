# Engine hard to start when cold

## Summary

The mower is hard to start, or won't start, on cold mornings or in the snow. Some causes are made worse by the cold: a choke that doesn't close fully (a cold engine needs the richer mixture the choke gives), oil too thick for the temperature, a battery that has lost charge or frozen, and on a diesel the glow plugs that warm the engine. The main Source also lists general no-start causes that may simply have shown up on a cold day (old fuel, a dirty spark plug, clogged filters or fuel line, a bad fuel pump, a dirty carburetor, a bad starter solenoid or safety switch, a faulty charging system). Those aren't cold-specific, so this page routes them: if the engine doesn't turn over, see [Engine won't crank or turn over](engine-wont-crank.md); if it turns over but won't fire, see [Engine cranks but won't start](engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md).

## Quick triage

1. **Choke.** Remove the air filter housing and move the choke lever: the choke valve should close when the lever is engaged and open when it isn't. Adjust the cable and lubricate the linkage if not.
2. **Oil.** Check the operator's manual for the viscosity for the outdoor temperature; drain and refill if it is wrong.
3. **Battery.** Check it with a multimeter (about 12.7 volts); charge it if lower, replace it if it won't hold a charge. Secure and clean the cables and terminals.
4. **Diesel:** wait for the glow plugs as part of starting; test them and replace any that don't work.
5. **Carbureted engine only:** a little carburetor cleaner can help start an engine in colder than usual temperatures. Not for diesel or 2-cycle engines.

## Tips & life hacks

- Most manufacturers recommend SAE30 or 10W-30 oil, but 5W-30 may be needed in colder temperatures, especially on riding mowers used for snow work.
- A battery left in the cold without a good charge can freeze and stop holding a charge: below 20 F, a battery with less than 20% charge may freeze. Keep it charged through the cold months.
- Use fresh gas: it can break down as quickly as 30 days after purchase.

## Safety

- Follow the precautions in the operator's manual.
- Never run the mower without the safety switches installed; a bypass is only a temporary test.
- Don't use carburetor cleaner on diesel or 2-cycle engines.
