# Mower cuts uneven

## Summary

The mower leaves a bad or uneven cut: strips or patches of uncut grass, ridges in a stair-step pattern, spots cut too close, or grass tips that look ragged and turn brown a day or two later. The blades have to spin fast and create airflow under the deck that stands the grass up so it can be cut cleanly. Most Sources group the causes in three areas: the deck doesn't sit level (a low tire, an unlevel deck, a damaged deck shell), the blades or deck parts are worn or damaged (dull, bent or upside-down blades, spindle, belt, pulleys, a plugged deck), and the way the mower is run (engine not at full throttle, ground speed too fast, passes that don't overlap). Brown tips and a slightly uneven cut are early warning signs; waiting until there's noise or a burnt-rubber smell usually means a costlier deck repair.

## Quick triage

1. **Make it safe.** Engine off, key out, spark plug wire(s) off (some engines have one plug, some two); on a battery mower take out the battery and safety key. Wait for everything to stop moving and wear heavy gloves. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S31](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/fix-your-exmark-mower-deck-problem.md), [S29](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-electric-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S60](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/sun-joe-electric-mower-bad-cut.md)
2. **Tires and wheels first.** A low tire stops the deck sitting level. Check the pressure in all tires and make left and right equal; on a push mower check that no wheel is damaged or loose. Do this before touching the deck, or you may make deck adjustments you later have to reverse. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-lawn-mower-problems-fixed.md), [S42](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S76](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-mower-and-tractor-common-questions.md), [S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-electric-mower-bad-cut.md)
3. **Throttle and ground speed.** Run the engine at full throttle when you engage the blades, and slow down in thick, tall or wet grass; going too fast pushes the grass over instead of cutting it. Sources: [S73](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S77](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-does-my-lawn-tractor-cut-unevenly.md), [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-troy-bilt-lawn-mower-problems.md)
4. **Blades.** Check each blade is sharp, straight, tight and installed with the sails pointing up toward the deck. To check for a bend, measure from the ground to one blade tip, turn the blade half a turn and measure the other tip: more than 1/8 inch difference means replace it. Never straighten a bent blade. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S36](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-find-prevent-substantial-lawn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/big-dog-bad-or-uneven-cut.md), [S60](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/sun-joe-electric-mower-bad-cut.md)
5. **Clean the deck.** Scrape off grass packed under the deck; buildup blocks the airflow that lifts the grass. Sources: [S77](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-does-my-lawn-tractor-cut-unevenly.md), [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-bad-uneven-cut.md)
6. **Deck level.** On a flat surface, measure the deck height at all four corners. Level it side to side and front to back to the owner's manual; on many zero-turns the front sits slightly lower than the rear. See [Level the deck](../procedures/level-the-deck.md). Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S73](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S74](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-level-a-riding-lawn-mower-deck-for-an-even-cut-video.md)
7. **Spindles, belt and pulleys.** Grab a blade at each end and rock it: a knock or movement means a bad spindle bearing. Replace a stretched, cracked or glazed belt. With the belt off, spin each pulley by hand; restriction, bearing noise or wobble means replace it, and check the idler bracket for play. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S73](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S75](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/not-cutting-evenly.ridtr02.md)
8. **Mowing pattern.** Overlap each pass; the blade reaches only to the deck edge, so overlapping by about an inch makes sure every blade of grass is cut. Sources: [S77](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-does-my-lawn-tractor-cut-unevenly.md), [S38](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)
9. **Engine.** If the engine doesn't run smoothly while mowing, give it a [tune-up](../procedures/tune-up.md); if it has lost power even at full throttle, have a small engine shop test it. Sources: [S75](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/not-cutting-evenly.ridtr02.md), [S72](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md)

## Brand notes

- **Exmark:** the brand is known for its cut quality and a patented baffle system that controls airflow under the deck. The author prefers to have deck leveling done at an Exmark dealer. Sources: [S69](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-bad-or-uneven-exmark-mower-cut.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-exmark-mower-problems-and-solutions.md)
- **John Deere:** the deck-leveling procedure varies by model; follow the owner's manual. Failed pulley and spindle housing bearings, an unlevel deck and worn blades are named for an uneven cut. Sources: [S42](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S41](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-belt-keeps-coming-off.md)
- **Kubota:** decks normally give a good cut, so an uneven one usually points to operator error (paths not overlapped, fast ground speed, low engine speed) or the causes above. Sources: [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-kubota-problems-and-solutions.md)
- **Cub Cadet:** leveling a deck can take a trained dealer up to two hours. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md)
- **Battery mowers (Ego, Greenworks, Kobalt, RYOBI, Sun Joe, Toro, Worx):** the causes are the push-mower list (damaged wheel, worn, bent, upside-down or loose blade, cutting height, plugged deck, ground speed). Electric zero-turn models can also have low tire pressure or an unlevel deck. Sources: [S29](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-electric-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S33](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/greenworks-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S44](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kobalt-electric-mower-bad-cut.md), [S53](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ryobi-electric-mower-bad-uneven-cut.md), [S60](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/sun-joe-electric-mower-bad-cut.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-electric-mower-bad-cut.md), [S67](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/worx-mower-has-bad-uneven-cut.md)
- **Honda push mowers:** before measuring a blade, confirm the wheels are the same size and undamaged and that every cutting-height setting matches. Sources: [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-honda-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Check tire pressure before anything else: a low tire found after leveling the deck means redoing the work. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S55](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-lawn-mower-bad-uneven-cut.md), [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-bad-uneven-cut.md)
- Pull a few grass blades after mowing: brown tips with little cuts or a shredded look mean the grass is being bruised, not cut. Replace bruising blades; just sharpen blades that are dull but in good condition. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S49](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mowers-bad-cut-solved.md)
- To keep grass from building up under the deck: mow at full throttle, avoid wet grass, cut regularly, and coat the underside with a Teflon or silicone spray made for mower decks. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md)
- Service the deck at least twice a mowing season, more often on heavily watered or fertilized lawns. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md)
- Grease the idler bracket during routine checks: a binding bracket lets the belt and blades slip in heavy grass, which shows up as a bad cut the next day. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md)
- Double- or triple-cut very tall grass. Sources: [S36](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-find-prevent-substantial-lawn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md)
- Inspect blades before every use, and replace any that are bent, cracked or worn. Sources: [S77](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-does-my-lawn-tractor-cut-unevenly.md). This Source also says to replace blades at the start of each season. A newer manufacturer Source says every 1–3 seasons and supersedes that rule; see the callout on [Dull or bent blade](../causes/dull-or-bent-blade.md).
- A loose or worn deck belt can keep the blades from spinning fast enough for a good cut. Sources: [S64](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-craftsman-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-belt-is-bad.md)

## Safety

- Before working on or under the deck, turn the engine off, remove the key and disconnect the spark plug wire(s); set the parking brake and park on a flat, level surface. On a battery mower remove the battery pack(s) and safety key. Sources: [S51](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-lawn-tractor-cuts-uneven.md), [S57](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S53](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ryobi-electric-mower-bad-uneven-cut.md), [S49](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mowers-bad-cut-solved.md)
- Never reach under the deck while the engine runs; wait for all moving parts to stop. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S50](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md)
- Wear heavy work gloves (and eye protection) around the sharp blades. Sources: [S31](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/fix-your-exmark-mower-deck-problem.md), [S29](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-electric-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-mower-cut-is-uneven.md)

- The owner is unsure how to diagnose or repair the deck safely, or can't find the cause. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S72](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md)

- Deck leveling on a riding or zero-turn mower can take a trained dealer up to two hours; some owners prefer the dealer to do it. Sources: [S63](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troubleshoot-cub-cadet-mower-deck-problems.md), [S69](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-bad-or-uneven-exmark-mower-cut.md)
