# Engine only runs with the choke on

## Summary

The engine starts and runs with the choke on (or half on), but sputters and dies when the choke is pushed off. The choke corrects the air-to-fuel ratio, so needing it means the engine is getting too much air or not enough fuel. Not enough fuel: old gas, a dirty carburetor, a clogged fuel line, a bad fuel pump, a plugged fuel filter or a bad fuel cap. Too much air: a bad carburetor gasket or a puncture in the fuel line. Running on the choke uses more fuel, and the mower may underperform or develop engine problems if the cause isn't fixed.

## Quick triage

1. **Make it safe.** Shut the engine off, remove the ignition key and the spark plug boot(s), and let the engine cool.
2. **Fresh fuel.** Drain old gas and refill with fresh gas plus a stabilizer such as Sea Foam Motor Treatment or STA-BIL.
3. **Gas cap.** Loosen the cap and put the choke off. If the engine keeps running, tighten the cap and let it run: if it sputters after a while and won't run well until the cap is loosened, replace the cap.
4. **Fuel flow.** Shut off the fuel, take the end of a line section into a container, turn the fuel back on and watch the flow, working down the line, the inline filter and (if fitted) the fuel pump. Clear or replace the part that restricts flow; fit a new filter with its arrow in the direction of fuel flow.
5. **Air leaks.** Follow the fuel line from the tank to the carburetor looking for punctures or cracks, and check that the carburetor bolts are tight and the gasket behind it is good.
6. **Carburetor.** Clean it; if it still isn't right, rebuild or replace it, or have a small engine mechanic do it.

## Brand notes

- **Toro and riding mowers:** use unleaded gas, minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol, never more. The gas tank vents through the gas cap.

## Tips & life hacks

- Gas can start to break down about a month after you buy it: use it within 30 days.
- Replace the inline fuel filter once a year.
- When the carburetor is off to replace the gasket, it's a good time to clean it.
