# Mower is leaking gas

## Summary

The owner smells gas around the mower, in the garage or in the shed, or finds a wet spot, a puddle, gasoline stains on the floor, or a trail of dead or burnt grass (gasoline kills grass). Often there is only the smell: gas evaporates, so a small leak leaves no wet spot by the time anyone looks. The leak can be anywhere fuel passes, from the gas cap and tank, through the shut-off valve, fuel lines, filter and fuel pump, to the carburetor and primer bulb. Old fuel is behind many leaks: it degrades and softens plastic parts until their seams leak, and rubber parts dry out and crack with age. A leak wastes fuel, kills the lawn and is a fire risk, so fix it before using the mower again.

## Quick triage

1. **Move the mower out and ventilate.** Roll it out of the garage into fresh air, or open the garage door, before working. Let the engine cool and remove the spark plug wire(s). Sources: [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S38](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S30](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-leaking-gas.md)
2. **Find the spot.** If you can't see a leak, park the mower on cardboard overnight and look for gas spots. Look under the mower for fresh spots. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/10-reasons-your-lawn-mower-could-be-leaking-gasoline.md), [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)
3. **Start at the tank.** Check the fuel tank, the gas cap and the shut-off valve first. Sources: [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-leaking-gas.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md)
4. **Follow the fuel line out of the tank** and check every part the fuel passes through for dampness, staining or cracks: fuel lines and clamps, fuel filter, fuel pump, carburetor (around the bowl and near the air intake), and the primer bulb if there is one. Tighten loose fittings and wipe up spills straight away. Sources: [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-leaking-gas.md), [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-lawn-mower-problems-fixed.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-husqvarna-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S30](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S41](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S50](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-craftsman-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)
5. **Nothing found? Check the gas cap seal.** Rock the mower to slosh gas up to the cap and watch for a wet spot around it. Sources: [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-leaking-gas.md), [S39](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-problems.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/10-reasons-your-lawn-mower-could-be-leaking-gasoline.md)
6. **Check the fill level.** Fill only to the bottom of the fill neck, not to the brim. Source: [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)
7. **Still smells after the fixes?** A deeper problem such as a faulty fuel shut-off valve may be the cause; if you can't find the leak, take the mower to a repair shop. Sources: [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md), [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Gas evaporates and leaves no stain, unlike oil, so the smell may be the only clue; by the time you smell it, the wet spot may be gone. Sources: [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-mower-gas.md), [S46](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-john-deere-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-toro-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S36](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S35](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S53](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-lawn-tractor-is-leaking-gas.md), [S54](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-leaking-fuel.md)
- A faint gas smell while mowing isn't unusual; a strong or lingering odor means a leak or another problem. Gas fumes rise about 7 inches before they dissipate, so the odor shouldn't be heavy in the garage. Sources: [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md), [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas.md)
- Gas has a shelf life of about 30 days, and older gas smells stronger. Buy fresh fuel from a busy station, and add a fuel stabilizer if it won't be used quickly. Non-ethanol (recreational) fuel reduces old-fuel problems. Sources: [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas.md), [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-leaking-gas.md)
- While you're working on the fuel system, replace parts that are likely to leak soon: plastic parts gone soft, dry lines beginning to crack, and clamps that may puncture the line. Source: [S44](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md)
- Check for leaks before the first cut of the year. Source: [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)
- Don't store a leaking mower in an unventilated area. Source: [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-snapper-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)

## Safety

- Gas fumes are harmful and can ignite: work in a well-ventilated area away from combustibles, keep ignition sources far away (a small spark can ignite fumes), and don't work in an enclosed area full of fumes. Sources: [S56](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S23](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ariens-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md), [S37](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md)
- Wait for the engine to cool, remove the spark plug wire(s) or boot(s), and wear gloves and safety glasses.
