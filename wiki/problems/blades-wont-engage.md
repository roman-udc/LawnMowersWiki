# Blades won't engage

## Summary

The engine runs, but when you pull up the PTO knob or push the blade lever, the blades don't turn on, or they turn too slowly to cut. The Sources split the causes in two. Either nothing makes the clutch engage (on an electric clutch: a bad PTO switch, a weak battery, a failed safety switch, a blown fuse or a bad clutch; on a manual clutch: a worn cable or linkage), or the blades don't turn because the deck belt is worn or has come off the pulleys (stretched belt, bad pulley bearing, worn tensioner arm or spring).

## Quick triage

1. **Make it safe first.** Remove the ignition key and the spark plug wire(s), and wait for all parts to stop moving before reaching under the deck.
2. **Check the deck belt.** Replace a belt that is worn, cracked, shiny (glazed) or broken. If it's off the pulleys, look for a worn idler (tensioner) arm or spring, a stretched belt, worn pulley bearings, or pulleys not parallel to the deck, and route the new belt correctly.
3. **Check the pulleys.** A bad pulley bearing lets the pulley seize or wobble so the belt rolls off. Failed mandrel pulleys can also stop the blades spinning properly.
4. **Electric clutch: check what powers it.** Test the battery and charge it if a 12 V battery reads less than 12.7 volts ([Charge the battery](../procedures/charge-the-battery.md)). Check the fuse ([Test and replace a fuse](../procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse.md)). Check the PTO switch for continuity. Make sure the seat switch senses you ([Replace the seat switch](../procedures/replace-the-seat-switch.md)).
5. **Electric clutch: test the clutch** ([Test the PTO clutch](../procedures/test-the-pto-clutch.md)). A bad clutch no longer passes engine power to the blades and must be replaced.
6. **Manual clutch: check the lever and cable.** Look for a broken lever or a worn or broken cable, and worn springs, bushings or linkages.

## Tips & life hacks

- If the engine runs but the blades don't cut, check the blade belt first. If the belt is OK, move on to the idler pulley, the cable and lever (manual clutch), or the PTO switch and electric clutch.
- A weak battery that won't hold a charge needs replacing, not just charging.

## Parts commonly needed

- Deck (blade) belt: replace if worn, cracked, shiny or broken.
- Idler (tensioner) arm, spring, pulleys or pulley bearings.
- PTO switch; electric PTO clutch.
- Manual clutch parts: cable, lever, springs, bushings, linkages.
- Seat (safety) switch; fuse of the same amperage; 12 V battery.

## Safety

- Never reach under the deck until you have followed the operator's manual precautions: remove the ignition key, remove the spark plug wire(s) or boot(s), and wait for all parts to stop moving.
- Let the engine cool before working on it. Wear safety gear; working around the deck is dangerous.
- If you lack the skills, knowledge or condition to do the repair safely, consult a professional.
