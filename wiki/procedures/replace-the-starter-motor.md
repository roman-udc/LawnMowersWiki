# Replace the starter motor

Fixes: [Bad starter motor](../causes/bad-starter-motor.md).

The starter motor spins the flywheel to start the engine when you turn the key. If you hear the starter solenoid click but the motor won't run, confirm the starter is the problem, then replace it with a manufacturer-approved part.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-motor-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Before you start

- Have you confirmed the starter is the problem? A click from the solenoid with no motor movement points to the starter; the solenoid test is in [Test the starter solenoid](test-the-starter-solenoid.md).
- This procedure is for a riding mower or lawn tractor with the starter under the engine blower housing.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-motor-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Safety

- Let the engine cool. Park on a level surface and set the parking brake.
- Turn the ignition off, remove the key and wear work gloves.
- Disconnect the negative battery cable before you start.
- Repairs can be hazardous; proceed only if you're confident, and leave some repairs to a qualified technician.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-motor-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Tools & parts

- Wrench set
- Nut driver set
- Work gloves
- Replacement starter motor (manufacturer-approved)

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-motor-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. **Disconnect the battery.** Lift the seat, remove the bolt holding the negative cable to the battery and tuck the cable away from the post.
2. **Remove the blower housing.** Lift the hood. Remove the air duct screws and pull off the duct. Turn each air filter cover knob counterclockwise and remove the cover and the air filter. Remove the air filter housing screw, then the front and rear blower housing bolts, and lift off the blower housing.
3. **Remove the old starter.** Remove the lower dash fastener and pull off the lower dash. Remove the nut holding the starter wire and pull the wire off. Remove the starter mounting bolts (a wire retainer comes off with the left bolt) and pull off the starter.
4. **Install the new starter.** Position it on the engine and attach it with the right mounting bolt. Fit the wire retainer and left bolt, route the wires through the retainer and tighten the left bolt. Attach the starter wire with its nut. Reinstall the lower dash and fastener.
5. **Reinstall the blower housing.** Position it on the engine, aligning the air diverter inside. Reinstall its bolts, the air filter housing screw, the filter and cover, and the air duct. Lower the hood.
6. **Reconnect the battery.** Reconnect the negative cable to the negative terminal and lower the seat.

## Tips & common mistakes

- The rear blower housing bolts are longer than the front ones; put them back in the right holes. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-motor-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Verify the fix

Turn the key to START: the starter should spin the engine.
