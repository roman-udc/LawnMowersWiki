# Clean under the deck

Fixes: [Debris on deck or pulleys](../causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys.md), [Oil or rust on belt](../causes/oil-or-rust-on-belt.md).

Scrape packed grass, dirt and debris from under the mower deck, and clear debris, oil and rust from the top of the deck around the belt, pulleys and tensioner. Buildup under the deck spoils the airflow the blades need for a good cut and makes the engine work harder.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-zero-turn-lawn-mower.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-mower-overheating.md)

## Before you start

- What kind of mower is it? A push mower is easy to prop up; a riding mower, lawn tractor or zero-turn needs to be lifted (jack stands, a lift, car ramps or a trailer tailgate) to reach the underside.
- Does the deck have belt covers? They come off to reach the top of the deck.
- If you are cleaning oil off the deck, find and fix the oil leak first.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-zero-turn-lawn-mower.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-husqvarna-riding-mower.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-lawn-mower-for-best-performance.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-zero-turn-mower.md)

## Safety

- Remove the ignition key and the spark plug wire(s) before working on the deck, so the mower can't start. Wait for all parts to stop moving and wear safety gear.
- Support a raised mower securely on jack stands or a lift.
- Dry debris on top of the deck can catch fire from the heat and friction of the belt.
- Don't spray cold water on a hot engine, and keep water away from the carburetor.

## Tools & parts

- Deck scraper; a metal putty knife or stiff wire brush also works
- Jack stands or a mower lift
- Leaf blower, compressor with a blow gun, or soft brush
- Garden hose (not a pressure washer around the spindles and pulleys)
- Rags, work gloves, safety glasses
- Replacement pulleys or components, if rust can't all be removed

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-zero-turn-lawn-mower.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-lawn-mower-for-best-performance.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-zero-turn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. Remove the key and spark plug wire(s) (see Safety).
2. Blow loose grass, dirt and leaves off the mower with a leaf blower.
3. Raise the mower to reach the underside of the deck: on a push mower, set the front end up on a jack stand or something firm; on a riding mower, tractor or zero-turn, use jack stands or a lift.
4. Scrape the underside of the deck with a deck scraper, metal putty knife or stiff wire brush to remove all packed dirt, grass and debris.
5. Clean the top of the deck: put the deck in its lowest position, remove the belt covers if it has them, and remove debris from under the pulleys and brackets, around the tensioner bracket and spring, and from the top of the deck. If you can't get to the top of the deck, remove the deck following the owner's manual.
6. If there is oil on the deck, clean it off the deck and components. If there is rust, clean it off the deck, pulleys and brackets, and replace any pulley or component too rusty to clean.
7. With the deck clean, check the pulleys and belt. Replace a worn belt (see [Replace the deck belt](replace-the-deck-belt.md)); a belt with oil or rust on it needs replacing. Sharpen the blades.

## Brand notes

- **Husqvarna riding mowers:** lower the deck to its lowest position and remove the belt covers to clean the top of the deck.
- **Ferris zero-turns:** debris on top of the deck can interfere with the tensioner pulley; clear it from around the tensioner bracket and spring.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-husqvarna-riding-mower.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-zero-turn-belt-keeps-coming-off.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Scrape the deck every time you service the mower.
- To reduce buildup, mow only dry grass, mow at full throttle, and mow often enough to avoid cutting long grass. A deck spray reduces how much grass sticks, but doesn't stop it.
- When washing, use a garden hose rather than a pressure washer around the spindle housings and pulleys: the bearings are sealed, but not against high pressure.
- Some decks have a washout port for a garden hose. The Source finds it is often plugged and very messy.
- Don't replace an oily or rusty belt without cleaning the deck first: the new belt will fail the same way.

## Verify the fix

The underside and top of the deck are free of packed grass, debris, oil and rust, the tensioner moves freely, and the mower cuts evenly without bogging down.
