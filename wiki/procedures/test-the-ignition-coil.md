# Test the ignition coil

Fixes: [Bad ignition coil](../causes/bad-ignition-coil.md); also tells a bad coil apart from a [fouled spark plug](../causes/fouled-spark-plug.md).

A spark test tells you whether the ignition system is making spark. No spark with a good plug means the ignition coil is likely bad.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-push-mower-wont-start.md)

## Before you start

- Is the plug itself good? Test with a good plug installed, so a fouled plug isn't mistaken for a bad coil.
- Is it an electric-start mower? On those, no spark can also come from the battery, wiring or starter solenoid.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-push-mower-wont-start.md)

## Safety

- Remove the spark plug wire while you work on the mower; reconnect it only through the tester for the test.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-push-mower-wont-start.md)

## Tools & parts

- Spark plug tester
- Spark plug wrench, if the plug has to be installed first

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md)

## Steps

1. Make sure the spark plug is installed.
2. Pull the spark plug boot off the plug and connect the tester's boot to the plug.
3. Connect the engine's plug wire to the other end of the tester.
4. Crank the engine with the pull cord or the key.
5. Watch the tester's transparent section: a glow means there's spark. No glow means a bad plug or a bad ignition coil; on an electric-start mower it can also be the battery, the wiring or the starter solenoid.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-push-mower-wont-start.md)

## Brand notes

- **Troy-Bilt push mowers:** the expert Source uses the same spark test, cranking with the pull cord or electric start. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-push-mower-wont-start.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Test for fuel as well as spark: the same Source sprays carburetor cleaner into the air intake to check the fuel side. If the engine fires with carburetor cleaner, the problem is fuel, not spark. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-push-mower-wont-start.md)

## Verify the fix

If the tester shows no spark with a good plug, replace the coil (see [Replace the ignition coil](replace-the-ignition-coil.md)) and repeat the test: it should now glow.
