# Change the hydraulic oil

Fixes: [Low or old hydraulic oil](../causes/low-hydraulic-oil.md), [Hot hydraulic fluid](../causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid.md).

Low or old hydraulic (transmission) oil makes the mower seem weak and can damage the hydrostatic transmission. On serviceable transmissions you replace the two hydraulic filters, drain the oil that comes out with them, refill through the expansion reservoirs and then purge the air. One author says that if you can change engine oil, you can probably change the hydraulic oil and filters. Change at the manufacturer's interval; don't change early unless the oil has been getting hot or you suspect contamination.

## Before you start

- Is the transmission serviceable? Some entry-level zero-turns and some riding mowers have sealed, maintenance-free transmissions whose oil can't be changed: contact the dealer for those.
- Is a change due? Check the hours against the manufacturer's interval; most have an earlier first change after a break-in period, some as soon as 75 hours. If a full change isn't due, just check the level and top off to the "full cold" line.
- Which oil does the mower or transmission manufacturer specify? Use only that oil (see Brand notes).
- Which procedure does your model use? Steps vary by make and model: check the operator's manual.

## Safety

- Park on a flat surface, remove the key, engage the parking brake and wait for all moving parts to stop.
- The engine and transmission can be hot: let them cool before you touch them.
- When you raise the rear, support it on jack stands or jacks.
- Take the used oil to a recycling center, in marked containers.

## Tools & parts

- Oil drain pan, rags, cardboard under the mower to catch spills.
- Socket for spin-on filters on some models; jacks or jack stands to get the rear wheels off the ground.
- New hydraulic oil filters (usually two, one per transmission).
- Fresh hydraulic oil of the type the manufacturer specifies.

Sources: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-exmark-zero-turn-hydraulic-oil-filter.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-zero-turn-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. Park on a flat surface with cardboard underneath, remove the key and set the parking brake.
2. Find the filters: one near each rear wheel, often behind a guard or skid plate. Remove the guard. Wipe the dirt from around the filters, oil fill caps and any drain plug before opening anything, so no dirt gets into the hydraulic system.
3. Put the drain pan under one filter, unscrew it and let the oil drain. Repeat for the other filter.
4. Put a thin layer of fresh hydraulic oil on each new filter's rubber seal. Screw it on clockwise; once it contacts the filter adapter, turn it about one more full turn.
5. Clean around the expansion reservoirs (small plastic tanks on top of the mower behind the seat, usually one for each transmission, left and right) and remove the caps. Fill with fresh oil to the "full cold" line.
6. If each transaxle has a vent plug: loosen the vent plugs, fill through the reservoir until oil comes out of the vent plug, tighten the plugs, then keep filling to the "full cold" line.
7. Purge the air from the system: see [Purge the hydraulic system](purge-the-hydraulic-system.md).
8. Let the hydraulic system cool, recheck the oil level and top up to "full cold" if needed.

- Exmark: Exmark recommends Exmark Premium Hydro Oil. Mobil 1 15W-50 can substitute (not on Yanmar diesel models), but then change the oil twice as often (a 500-hour interval becomes 250 hours). Lazer Z with a Yanmar diesel uses Ruby Tran Fluid; Z-Spray models use 5W-40 synthetic hydraulic oil. Quest E-Series and some S-Series (Quest S 42") are sealed: see the dealer.
- Exmark: you change only the oil that drains out with the filters; don't change the oil in the whole hydraulic system unless it's contaminated or has become very hot, because doing so can introduce contaminants.
- Exmark Radius: fill until oil comes out of the vent plug on each transaxle, then to "full cold" on the expansion reservoir.
- Exmark Lazer Z E/S/X: raise the rear with jacks, remove the pump drive belt, unscrew the filters with a socket, install the new filters and torque them to 14 ft-lb (per Source, verify against the manual); slide the seat forward, fill through the left and right hydraulic caps until the dipstick reads full, and reinstall the pump drive belt before purging.
- Husqvarna: some residential zero-turns have maintenance-free transmissions with no service or level check.
- Gravely: after contact, turn the new filter about another 3/4 to a full turn.
- Kubota and Walker oils and capacities are listed on [Low or old hydraulic oil](../causes/low-hydraulic-oil.md).

## Tips & common mistakes

- Dirt is the enemy: wipe around every cap, filter and plug before opening, use fresh oil, and don't leave oil bottle caps off.
- Use only the oil the mower or transmission manufacturer recommends; interval and fluid type depend on the transmission manufacturer, and a different oil can change the interval.
- Don't skip the purge: air left in the system keeps the mower from moving at normal speed.
- A dealer service can also check the starting system, safety switches, charging system, belts and pulleys: ask for a general check when you book it.

## Verify the fix

- With the system cool, the oil sits at the "full cold" line on each reservoir.
- After purging, the drive wheels turn at normal speed with normal noise, forward and reverse.
