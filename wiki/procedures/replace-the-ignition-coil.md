# Replace the ignition coil

Fixes: [Bad ignition coil](../causes/bad-ignition-coil.md).

The ignition coil sends voltage to the spark plug. Replace it when a spark tester shows no spark with a good plug, or when the plug wire is damaged.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/lawn-tractor-won-t-start.ridtr04.md)

## Before you start

- Confirm the coil is the problem first: see [Test the ignition coil](test-the-ignition-coil.md). No spark with a good plug points to the coil.
- The steps below are for a riding mower engine with a blower housing.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md)

## Safety

- Repairs to power equipment can be hazardous. Use the proper tools and safety gear, follow all instructions, don't go ahead unless you're confident, and leave some repairs to a qualified technician.
- Park on a level surface, set the parking brake, turn the ignition off and remove the key. Wear work gloves.
- Disconnect the battery's negative cable before you start.

## Tools & parts

- Wrench set
- Feeler gauges
- Work gloves
- New ignition coil (manufacturer-approved part)

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md)

## Steps

1. Disconnect the battery: park on a level surface, set the parking brake, turn the ignition off and remove the key. Lift the seat, remove the bolt on the negative cable and tuck the cable away from the post.
2. Remove the engine blower housing: lift the hood, remove the air duct screws and duct, turn the air filter cover knobs counterclockwise and remove the cover and filter, remove the air filter housing screw, then remove the front and rear blower housing bolts and lift the housing off.
3. Remove the old coil: disconnect the spark plug wire and pull it out of the engine bracket. Turn the flywheel so the magnet points away from the coil. Remove the coil mounting screws, lift the coil, disconnect the kill wire and remove the coil.
4. Install the new coil: move the spark plug boot from the old coil to the new one and connect the kill wire. Position the coil and loosely install the two mounting screws. Pull the coil away from the flywheel, tighten the screws, then turn the flywheel so the magnet lines up with the coil.
5. Set the air gap: insert a feeler gauge between the coil and the magnet (0.011" on most engines). Holding the gauge in place, loosen the mounting screws so the magnet pulls the coil against it, then tighten the screws. Turn the flywheel by hand and remove the gauge. Route the spark plug wire through the engine bracket and connect it to the plug.
6. Reinstall the blower housing (line up the air diverter), its bolts, the air filter housing screw, the filter and cover, and the air duct. Lower the hood.
7. Reconnect the negative battery cable and lower the seat.

## Brand notes

- **Briggs & Stratton engines** on Craftsman, Husqvarna, Toro, MTD, Troybilt, Murray, John Deere, Snapper, Ariens, Poulan and Weed Eater riding mowers and lawn tractors: the steps above apply. Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- The air gap matters: check your engine's spec; 0.011" is correct for most engines in the manufacturer guide. Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md)

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** one manufacturer guide calls this an easy repair ([S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/easy-diy-riding-lawn-mower-repairs.md), manufacturer) vs a difficulty of 3 on its own scale, with blower housing removal and an air gap to set ([S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-ignition-coil.md), manufacturer). This page rates it moderate.

## Verify the fix

Connect a spark plug tester and turn the key: you should see spark. The engine should then start.
