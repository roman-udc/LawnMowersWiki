# Replace the idler pulley

Fixes: [Worn pulley or idler bearing](../causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing.md).

The deck idler pulley tightens the blade belt against the blade pulleys when the blade engagement lever is engaged. Replace it if it is worn or broken. The steps below come from an Exmark zero-turn deck article; other decks are similar, but check your owner's manual.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/mower-deck-vibrates.ltrtr140.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md)

## Before you start

- Confirm the pulley is bad: turn it by hand; resistance or bearing noise means replace it. A seized pulley, or a bearing blown out of the center, needs no test.
- Idler pulleys have a sealed bearing (riveted on Exmark): buy the whole pulley with the bearing installed, not just a bearing.
- Have all four parts on hand: pulley, carriage bolt, spacer and lock nut. The old carriage bolt is often damaged.
- Find out how your deck belt is tensioned: most current Exmark decks use a self-tensioning spring; older models use spring-loaded tensioners that must be removed first.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md)

## Safety

- Turn the engine off and remove the spark plug boot so the engine can't start while you work under the deck. Keep the key in your pocket so no one tries to start the mower.
- If you raise the mower to reach under the deck, support it on jack stands.
- This is a high-speed pulley: make sure all the hardware is in place and tight before the belt goes back on.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md)

## Tools & parts

- Tools: 3/8 wrench, socket, jack stands (if needed).
- Parts: idler pulley (with bearing), carriage bolt, spacer, flanged lock nut.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md)

## Steps

1. Turn the engine off and remove the spark plug boot.
2. Remove the deck belt (remove a spring-loaded tensioner first if your deck has one).
3. Raise the deck to its highest position so you can reach under it. Use jack stands if you need more height.
4. Scrape the deck and clean all debris from where the pulley bolt goes through, so the bolt sits square.
5. From under the deck, slide the carriage bolt through the hole. On top, slide the spacer over the bolt, set the pulley on the spacer, and loosely tighten the nut with a 3/8 wrench to hold everything in place.
6. Make sure the square part near the head of the carriage bolt fits the square hole in the deck. Hold the bolt tight against the deck from underneath and tighten the lock nut from the top with a socket. Torque it to the spec in your owner's manual. The flange of the lock nut contacts the bearing so the pulley spins properly.
7. Put the deck belt back on (see [Replace the deck belt](replace-the-deck-belt.md)).

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md)

## Brand notes

- **Exmark:** the idler pulley assembly is a carriage bolt, lock nut, pulley and spacer. The spacer is sometimes mounted to the deck with an aluminum teardrop-shaped piece, or is a bushing (wider bottom, narrower top) that goes through the pulley. A seized bearing can take out that bushing; the bushing is not part of the bearing. Per Source, the carriage bolt is a grade 5, 3/8 in bolt about 3 1/2 in long (verify against catalog). Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md)
- **Cub Cadet:** idler pulleys have a sealed bearing; replace the whole pulley. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Don't skip cleaning around the bolt hole: debris keeps the bolt from sitting square.
- With the deck raised, it's a good time to grease the deck, the idler arm under the deck and the drive belt tensioner.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/replace-exmark-mower-deck-parts.md)

## Verify the fix

- The new pulley turns smoothly and silently by hand and sits flat on the deck.
- With the belt on, rotate it around the pulleys a couple of times and tug it to check the tensioner spring reacts. Before refitting the belt covers, start the engine and engage the deck two or three times to make sure the belt stays on.
- The deck no longer vibrates, and the blades spin when engaged.
