# Read a wiring schematic

Fixes: [Bad wiring or ground](../causes/bad-wiring-or-ground.md). Also used to trace the short behind a [Blown fuse](../causes/blown-fuse.md) that keeps blowing.

A wiring schematic shows how the electrical parts connect. Tracing a circuit on it with a multimeter lets you find the broken wire or failed part, so you buy the right part. The method here is general; the manufacturer videos it comes from use household-appliance examples.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/using-a-wiring-schematic-to-trace-a-current-video.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-read-a-wiring-schematic-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/wiring-schematic-symbols-explained-video.md)

## Before you start

- Do you have the schematic for your exact model? Look in the owner's manual or on a sticker on the machine; some online parts diagrams include it (search by model number), and it can be ordered from the replacement parts diagram page.
- Not all schematics are the same: some mowers add lights or 12-volt ports, and generic diagrams show only basic wiring.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-read-a-wiring-schematic-video.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/troubleshooting-a-riding-lawn-mower-blown-fuse-video.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-lawn-mower-starter-solenoid-bad.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/poulan-pro-starter-solenoid.md)

## Safety

- Disconnect power before testing parts for continuity.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/using-a-wiring-schematic-to-trace-a-current-video.md)

## Tools & parts

- Wiring diagram (and parts diagram, to find harness connections)
- Multimeter, an essential tool for electrical troubleshooting

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/using-a-wiring-schematic-to-trace-a-current-video.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/troubleshooting-a-riding-lawn-mower-blown-fuse-video.md)

## Steps

1. Learn the symbols. Lines are wires connecting the electrical components. Wire colours are labelled on the lines with abbreviations, and a colour chart is printed on the schematic itself: use it to check you're on the right wire.
2. Find the circuit for the part that isn't working. Current travels in a loop called a circuit; any break, a broken wire or a failed component, stops it flowing.
3. Follow the circuit from the power source through each switch, wire and component and back to the return.
4. With power disconnected, test each component in the circuit for continuity with the multimeter. A closed switch should read near 0 ohms; infinite resistance through it means replace it.
5. If the switches are good, test each wire in the circuit the same way, and repair or replace broken wires.
6. If the switches and wiring are all good, the remaining part at the end of the circuit is the fault, because everything else is ruled out.

## Tips & common mistakes

- Accurate diagnosis helps you buy the right part. More complicated circuits are traced with the same basic method. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/using-a-wiring-schematic-to-trace-a-current-video.md)
- Use your own mower's wiring diagram if the wiring differs from an example. Source: [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/troubleshooting-a-riding-lawn-mower-blown-fuse-video.md)

- For general owner know-how, see the Guide: [Reading wiring schematics](../guides/reading-wiring-schematics.md).

## Verify the fix

After repairing the wire or replacing the part, the circuit tests continuous end to end and the part works.
