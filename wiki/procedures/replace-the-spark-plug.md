# Replace the spark plug

Fixes: [Fouled spark plug](../causes/fouled-spark-plug.md).

## Before you start

- How many cylinders does the engine have? A two-cylinder engine has two plugs and two wires.
- What electrode gap does the owner's manual specify?
- What socket size does the plug take? It is 3/4" or 5/8", depending on the engine model.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)

## Safety

- Park on a level surface in a well-ventilated area, set the parking brake, turn the ignition off and remove the key.
- Let the engine cool before you touch the plug.
- Wear work gloves, and disconnect the spark plug wire before you start.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)

## Tools & parts

- Spark plug wrench, or a deep socket and ratchet
- Gap tool
- Work gloves
- New spark plug (one per cylinder)

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-tune-up-a-riding-lawn-mower-video.md)

## Steps

1. Park the mower, set the brake, remove the key and let the engine cool.
2. Lift the hood and disconnect the spark plug wire. Disconnect both wires on a two-cylinder engine.
3. Remove the old plug with the spark plug wrench, or with a deep socket and ratchet.
4. Use the gap tool to set the gap on the new plug to the owner's manual spec. On the engine in the manufacturer guide it is 0.03".
5. Thread the new plug in by hand, then tighten it firmly with the wrench. Don't overtighten it, so it comes out easily next time.
6. Reconnect the spark plug wire or wires firmly, and lower the hood.

## Tips & common mistakes

- Replace the plug once a year, or as often as the owner's manual says. Do it as part of a yearly tune-up. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-tune-up-a-riding-lawn-mower-video.md)
- If you find a wet plug, find out why fuel or oil is reaching it before you just swap the plug. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)
- Overtightening makes the plug hard to remove next time. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md)
- While the plug is out, check the plug wire for drying and cracks. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)

## Verify the fix

The engine should start and run smoothly. If it still cranks without firing, connect a spark tester between the plug and the wire and crank the engine. If there's no spark with a new plug, the ignition coil is the next suspect.
