# Replace the spark plug

Fixes: [Fouled spark plug](../causes/fouled-spark-plug.md).

A broken, fouled, dirty or corroded spark plug makes the engine hard to start, misfire or run rough, and can raise fuel use. Replace the plug once a year, or as often as the owner's manual says, and any time it's fouled or its ceramic insulator is cracked.

## Before you start

- How many cylinders does the engine have? A two-cylinder engine has two plugs and two wires. Some Cub Cadet mowers use more than one plug.
- What electrode gap does the engine manufacturer or owner's manual specify?
- What socket size does the plug take? It is 3/4" or 5/8", depending on the engine model.

## Safety

- Park on a level surface in a well-ventilated area, set the parking brake, turn the ignition off and remove the key.
- Let the engine cool before you touch the plug.
- Wear work gloves, and disconnect the spark plug wire before you start.
- If you're doing a full service, leave the plug wire off until the service is finished, so the engine can't start while you work.
- Repairs can be hazardous; some should be done by a qualified technician.

## Tools & parts

- Spark plug wrench, or a 3/4" or 5/8" deep socket and ratchet
- Gap tool or feeler gauge
- Wire brush (if you clean and reuse a plug)
- Work gloves
- New spark plug, one per cylinder (manufacturer-approved part)

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-tune-up-a-riding-lawn-mower-video.md), [S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-will-not-start.md), [S20](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/service-a-husqvarna-zero-turn-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. Park the mower, set the brake, remove the key and let the engine cool.
2. Lift the hood if there is one, and pull the spark plug wire (boot) off the plug. Disconnect both wires on a two-cylinder engine.
3. Wipe around the plug to remove loose dirt so it can't fall into the cylinder.
4. Remove the old plug with the spark plug wrench, or with a 3/4" or 5/8" deep socket and ratchet.
5. Inspect it: a dark carbon-coated tip, a burnt or broken electrode, cracked porcelain, corrosion or heavy buildup mean replace it. A lightly dirty plug can be cleaned with a wire brush and reused (Sources disagree on this; see the clean-or-replace callout on [Fouled spark plug](../causes/fouled-spark-plug.md)).
6. Use the gap tool or feeler gauge to set the gap on the new (or cleaned) plug to the engine manufacturer's spec. On the engine in the manufacturer guide it is 0.03".
7. Thread the plug in by hand, then tighten it firmly with the wrench. Don't overtighten it, so it comes out easily next time.
8. Reconnect the spark plug wire or wires firmly (last, if you're doing a full service), and lower the hood.

- Replace the plug once a year, as part of a yearly tune-up, service or winterizing. - Replace it at the start of the season if the mower was hard to start last year or the plug shows corrosion. Source: [S26](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-maintenance-checklist-by-season.md)
- For winter storage, the manufacturer guide puts 1 oz of oil in each cylinder, runs the engine 5 seconds, then installs new gapped plugs. Source: [S28](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/storing-your-lawn-tractor-for-winter.md)

- Overtightening makes the plug hard to remove next time. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-spark-plug.md)
- While the plug is out, check the plug wire for drying and cracks. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)

## Verify the fix

The engine should start and run smoothly. If it still cranks without firing, connect a spark tester between the plug and the wire and crank the engine (see [Test the ignition coil](test-the-ignition-coil.md)). If there's no spark with a good plug, the ignition coil is the next suspect.
