# Start a zero-turn mower

Fixes: [Incorrect starting procedure](../causes/incorrect-starting-procedure.md).

A zero-turn's safety interlock system won't let the engine start unless the operator is in the seat, the parking brake is on, the steering levers are in neutral and the PTO (blade) switch is off. Many "won't start" calls are just a missed step.

Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-zero-turn-mower-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kawasaki-engine-wont-start.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Before you start

Pre-start checks:

- Fuel shut-off valve(s) open and enough fuel in the tank.
- Engine oil at the full mark on the dipstick, and hydraulic oil at the right level in the overflow tanks.
- Debris and dirt cleaned off the engine, exhaust and cutting deck.
- Air filter clean and undamaged.
- Safety interlock system working, and the seat belt and rollover protection (ROPS) in good condition.
- The exact procedure varies by model: check the operator's manual.

Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-zero-turn-wont-start.md)

## Safety

- Start the mower only from the operator's seat, with the parking brake on and the blades (PTO) off.
- Keep the seat belt and rollover protection in working condition.

Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-zero-turn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Tools & parts

- The ignition key
- The operator's manual for your model

No parts are needed.

## Steps

1. Sit in the operator's seat.
2. Move the steering (motion control) levers to the widest, outward position: this is neutral.
3. Engage the parking brake (lever or foot pedal). On some models there's no separate brake, and moving the levers outward engages it.
4. Push the PTO (blade) switch down to off.
5. Set the throttle. With a separate choke, set it partway between slow (turtle) and fast (rabbit). With no separate choke, put the throttle at full to engage the choke for a cold start.
6. On a cold engine, set the choke to on (full choke). On a warm engine, leave it off.
7. Turn the key to start and release it as soon as the engine starts. Don't hold it in start for more than 10 seconds; wait 1 minute before trying again.
8. Move the choke to off, let the engine idle at half throttle for a few minutes, then go to full throttle to mow.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** most Sources set the throttle halfway between slow and fast for a cold start ([S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-zero-turn-mower-wont-start.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md), expert-blog) vs about 1/3 throttle on a Grasshopper ([S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/grasshopper-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), expert-blog). Follow the operator's manual.

## Brand notes

- **Cub Cadet, John Deere, Husqvarna:** pushing the steering levers outward engages the brake on some models; others have a separate brake lever. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-zero-turn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Grasshopper:** throttle about 1/3; to drive off, release the parking brake and move the steering levers together. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/grasshopper-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Scag:** in the seat, parking brake set, control levers open (outward), choke set correctly. Source: [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Dixon, Toro:** set the parking brake and put the speed levers in neutral before starting. Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dixon-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-zero-turn-wont-start.md)
- **Kawasaki engines:** in the seat, brake on, PTO off; choke for a cold start. Source: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kawasaki-engine-wont-start.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Let the engine warm up for a few minutes before mowing. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-wont-start.md)
- To stop: bring the mower to a stop, move the steering levers to neutral, set the parking brake, set the throttle halfway, let the engine run at least 30 seconds, then turn the key off. Source: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md)
- If it still won't start with every step done, suspect fuel, electrical or air intake: old fuel, a weak battery, a bad starter solenoid, loose wiring or a dirty carburetor. Source: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md)

## Verify the fix

The engine starts with the key and keeps running once the choke is off.

Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-zero-turn-mower.md)
