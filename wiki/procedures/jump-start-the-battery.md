# Jump-start the battery

Fixes: [Weak or dead battery](../causes/weak-or-dead-battery.md).

A riding mower with a 12-volt battery can be jump-started from a car's 12-volt battery in a pinch. It's a stopgap: charge the battery properly afterwards, because jumping it repeatedly can eventually damage the mower's charging system.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/can-a-lawn-mower-be-jumpstarted-by-a-car-battery.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/jump-starting-a-riding-lawn-mower-battery-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-wont-turn-over-or-click-video.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/dead-battery.ltrtr141.md)

## Before you start

- Are both batteries 12 volts? Check the owner's manual. Never jump if the two batteries aren't the same voltage: the battery can explode. A 6-volt mower battery must be charged with a charger, not jumped.
- Is the mower battery leaking acid or badly corroded at the terminals? Replace it instead of jumping it.
- Are the jumper cables sound: no frays or breaks, clamps firmly attached?
- Use a car, not another riding mower: jumping from a mower can damage its electrical system.

## Safety

- Batteries contain acid and give off explosive gases. Wear safety glasses and work gloves, don't lean over the battery while connecting cables, and keep sparks, flames and cigarettes away.
- Never jump from a running engine: shut the car off.
- Take the key out of the mower while you hook up. Work one-handed when attaching or removing cables, with the other hand off the chassis, or wear rubber-insulated gloves. Remove rings, metal jewelry and hanging necklaces.
- The two vehicles must not touch each other, and the free red clamp must not touch any metal.
- Keep children and bystanders away, disengage the blades, and wear close-fitting clothing.

## Tools & parts

- Jumper cables
- A car with a good 12-volt battery (or a portable jump starter)
- Safety glasses and work gloves

## Steps

1. Park the mower on a level surface, set the parking brake and shut off the blades as the owner's manual says for starting.
2. Park the car next to the mower so the cables reach without tension and the vehicles don't touch. Shut the car's engine off. Open the car hood and lift the mower seat.
3. Connect one red cable clamp to the mower battery's positive (+) terminal, then the other red clamp to the car battery's positive (+) terminal.
4. Connect one black cable clamp to the car battery's negative (-) terminal.
5. Connect the other black clamp to bare, unpainted metal on the mower frame, away from the battery and fuel tank.
6. Turn the mower key and start the mower. Don't start the car: its battery gives enough power.
7. Disconnect in reverse order: black from the mower frame, black from the car, red from the car, red from the mower. The clamps are still live while you do this.
8. Let the mower run a few minutes so its alternator charges the battery, then fully charge it with a charger as soon as you can. If it won't charge, replace it.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** one Source contradicts itself about the car's key. It says the car must be turned off, and also that you may need to turn the car key enough to initiate electrical power without turning the engine over ([S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/can-a-lawn-mower-be-jumpstarted-by-a-car-battery.md), expert-blog). Every other Source says shut the car off and don't start it (for example [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/jump-starting-a-riding-lawn-mower-battery-video.md), manufacturer).

## Tips & common mistakes

- Ideally use a battery charger; jump only occasionally (see [Charge the battery](charge-the-battery.md)). Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/jump-starting-a-riding-lawn-mower-battery-video.md)
- Alternatives to a car: a new battery, a portable jump starter (charge it first and follow its instructions), a trickle charger (slow, not a quick fix), or a combined jump starter and trickle charger. Newer units often warn you if the cables are hooked up wrong. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/can-a-lawn-mower-be-jumpstarted-by-a-car-battery.md)
- If the mower still won't run, recheck the connections; the battery may be too dead and need replacing, or there's another problem. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/can-a-lawn-mower-be-jumpstarted-by-a-car-battery.md)

## Verify the fix

The mower starts and keeps running with the cables removed. Afterwards, check the battery with a voltmeter to see that it's charging (see [Test the battery](test-the-battery.md)).
