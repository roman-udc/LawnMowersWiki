# Test the PTO clutch

Fixes: [Bad PTO clutch](../causes/bad-pto-clutch.md). Use this test on a riding mower or zero-turn with an electric PTO clutch when the blades won't engage, the deck runs too slow, or the mower vibrates and slows as the blades start.

## Before you start

- Flip the PTO switch and listen. If you don't hear the clutch click on, start with the power check (step 1).
- Find your mower's own clutch torque specification before you take anything apart. Each machine is different.

## Tools & parts

- Multimeter (volt/ohm meter).
- Electric PTO clutch, only if the tests show it has failed.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/a-look-into-how-lawn-mower-clutches-work.md)

## Steps

1. **Check the power to the clutch.** With the PTO switch on, measure the voltage on the power wire going into the clutch. Expect about 12 volts. If the clutch isn't getting enough power, the problem is elsewhere in the mower, not in the clutch.
2. **Check the coil resistance.** If the clutch has power, set the multimeter to ohms and measure across the electromagnet. Expect 1 to 3 ohms. A high reading means too much resistance; a low reading can mean a wiring problem.
3. **Inspect the clutch.** Remove it and look at the hub and brake pad for buildup, corrosion, obstructions and wear.
4. **Check the hardware.** Inspect the plates, bolts, springs and pulleys. When you put it back, tighten the clutch to about 50 to 60 ft-lbs, but use your machine's specification if it differs.

## Brand notes

The Source gives no brand-specific values. The 50 to 60 ft-lbs torque is a general figure; check the specification for your machine.

## Tips & common mistakes

- Keeping the clutch tight at the right torque helps it last longer.
- Don't replace the clutch before the power check: a clutch that gets no power is not the problem.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/a-look-into-how-lawn-mower-clutches-work.md)

## Verify the fix

The clutch clicks on when you flip the PTO switch, and the blades engage and come up to speed without the mower slowing or vibrating.
