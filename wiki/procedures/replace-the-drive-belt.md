# Replace the drive belt

Fixes: [Worn drive belt](../causes/worn-drive-belt.md).

The drive belt carries engine power to the wheels. Replace it when it is worn or broken; if it only slipped out of place and is in good shape, reinstall it instead. Only one Source gives step-by-step instructions: a guide for MTD-family mowers, which covers the self-propelled push mower drive belt in detail and the riding mower ground drive belt in less detail. No Source gives steps for the pump drive belt on a hydrostatic zero-turn. For the belt that turns the blades, see [Replace the deck belt](replace-the-deck-belt.md).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-belt-on-an-mtd-lawn-mower.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/won-t-move.ltr1818.md)

## Before you start

- Push mower or riding mower? The steps below are different for each.
- Get the matching belt: read the product number off the old belt and buy a duplicate, or look it up in the parts diagram for your model. The Source recommends an OEM belt for quality and fit.
- Keep the owner's manual handy: its diagrams show where the parts are and how the belt is routed over the pulleys and through the belt keepers.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-belt-on-an-mtd-lawn-mower.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/storing-your-lawn-tractor-for-winter.md)

## Safety

- Let the mower cool for 10-15 minutes after running: a hot muffler can ignite fuel vapors, and hot parts can burn you.
- Turn the ignition off, remove the key and disconnect the spark plug wire. On a battery mower, unplug the battery.
- Wear gloves and goggles for the whole job. Wedge a block of wood between the blade and the deck before loosening the blade bolt.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-belt-on-an-mtd-lawn-mower.md)

## Tools & parts

- Push mower: screwdriver or nut driver set, ratchet or socket wrench set, two sandwich bags, a block of wood, gloves, goggles, and the new drive belt.
- Riding mower: socket wrench set, pliers, torque wrench, gloves, goggles, and the new drive belt.
- Work on a stable, flat, sheltered surface; a workbench where the push mower can be strapped down is ideal.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-belt-on-an-mtd-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

**Self-propelled push mower:**

1. Unplug the spark plug. Seal the fuel tank: unscrew the cap, lay a sandwich bag flat over the opening and screw the cap back on, so fuel can't seep out when the mower is tipped.
2. Tilt the mower up on its handle and strap the handle down (or weigh it down). Wedge the block of wood between the blade and the deck.
3. Holding the blade with a gloved hand, loosen the blade bolt, then remove the bolt, blade and blade support.
4. Remove the bottom drive cover mounting screws and the debris shield mounting bolts (bag and label them), then twist the debris shield off the end of the crankshaft pulley.
5. Roll the drive belt off the crankshaft pulley and remove the pulley. Leave the belt beside the crankshaft end, not around it. If the belt was already off the pulley, it was knocked off or too loose and needs replacing.
6. Release the handle and stand it upright. Release the height adjustment lever if one sits next to the top drive cover, then remove the top drive cover screws and the cover.
7. Unhook the belt keeper that holds the belt on top of the transmission, pull the old belt out, and slip the new belt over the transmission pulley and through the deck opening.
8. Reattach the belt keeper over the new belt on the transmission drive pulley, then refit the drive cover, height adjustment lever and screws.
9. Tip the mower back and secure the handle. Wrap the new belt around the crankshaft end, refit the crankshaft pulley with its indentations aligned, and roll the belt into the pulley groove.
10. Refit the debris shield and its bolts, the bottom drive cover screws, and the blade support, blade and blade bolt. Tighten the bolt by hand, wedge the blade with the block, then tighten it with a torque wrench.
11. Remove the block, the handle strap and the bag from the fuel tank, and reconnect the spark plug.

**Riding mower (ground drive belt):**

1. Remove the key, disconnect the spark plug wire, lower the deck to its lowest position and disengage the blades.
2. Under the mower, take the belt off the engine pulley and release it from the belt keepers. Store every pin, clip and washer you remove.
3. On the MTD riders in the Source the deck comes out first: disconnect the front lift link support, the suspension arms, the rear deck brackets and the blade cable and spring, then slide the deck out. Those deck removal steps are covered in detail in [Replace the deck belt](replace-the-deck-belt.md).
4. Photograph the belt routing (or use the manual's diagram), then remove the old belt from the idler pulleys and belt guides.
5. Route the new belt around the pulleys and through the guides and keepers as in the photo or diagram. Reinstall the deck and reconnect every arm, bracket, guide, clip and pin.
6. Roll the belt onto the engine pulley, narrow side of the V into the groove, slowly turning the pulley as you go.
7. Reconnect the spark plug wire and lower the hood.

## Brand notes

- **MTD family (Troy-Bilt, Rover, WOLF-Garten, Remington, Yard Machines, Columbia, Craftsman, Cub Cadet, Boelens):** the step-by-step guide applies to mowers with these brand stamps, though some steps vary by model. The riding mower part of that guide switches between "ground drive belt" and "deck belt", so check the manual to be sure which belt you are changing.
- **Exmark zero-turns:** the pump drive belt is removed and reinstalled when changing the hydraulic filters, with the rear wheels raised off the ground, but the Source gives no steps for removing or installing it.
- **Sears (Craftsman):** sends readers to separate repair guides for replacing the ground drive belt on a riding mower and on a zero-turn; those guides are not among the Sources.

## Tips & common mistakes

- Photograph the routing before the old belt comes off.
- Keep the bolts in a bag and label them, especially the drive cover screws and debris shield bolts, which are easy to mix up.
- Align the crankshaft pulley's indentations when refitting it, or it won't sit properly.
- If the belt had fallen off, find out why before fitting the new one; see [Worn drive belt](../causes/worn-drive-belt.md) for the tensioner, spring and belt guard checks.

## Verify the fix

- The new belt should feel tight and secure. Pull it around the pulleys by hand to check it moves around each pulley securely and isn't loose.
- The mower should drive forward again without slowing down on hills.
