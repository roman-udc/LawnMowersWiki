# Clean or replace the air filter

Fixes: [Clogged air filter](../causes/clogged-air-filter.md). Also used for [Oil in air filter or tipped mower](../causes/oil-in-air-filter-or-tipped-mower.md) (replace the oil-soaked filter) and [Rich fuel mixture](../causes/rich-fuel-mixture.md) (black smoke from a restricted air supply).

The air filter traps dirt in the air going into the carburetor. A clogged filter starves the carburetor of air: the engine runs sluggish, loses power, and can overheat, because dirt also clogs the cooling fins. A paper filter is cleaned by tapping it, a foam filter by washing it; either is replaced when it's too dirty or damaged.

## Before you start

- Which kind of filter does the engine have? There are three setups:
  - a **paper element** on its own;
  - a **paper element with a foam pre-filter** (pre-cleaner) wrapped around or in front of it;
  - a **primary foam filter**, with no paper element.
  For other filter types, see the operator's manual.
- Where is it? Usually near the top of the engine above the carburetor, under a plastic or metal cover held by thumb screws, knobs, wing nuts or clips.
- Filters by engine size (expert Source): push mower engines up to 10 HP use a thin, flat paper or foam filter about 5" x 7", sometimes with a small foam pre-cleaner; 12 to 17 HP engines (common on V-twins) use an oval or round paper element 8" to 10" across with a foam pre-cleaner around it; 18 HP and larger and commercial engines use a canister paper filter (about 10" long and 4" across) with an inner filter.

## Safety

- Turn the engine off, remove the key and let the engine cool; it can be hot. Set the parking brake on a riding mower.
- Disconnect the spark plug wire (boot) before you remove the filter (both wires on a two-cylinder engine).
- Wear work gloves.
- Don't let dirt fall into the air intake while the filter is out.

## Steps

### Paper filter

1. Wipe loose dirt off the area around the filter cover, then remove the cover.
2. Wipe dirt off the rubber seal around the filter, then lift the filter out carefully so no dirt falls into the air intake. On a riding mower with an intake tube, turn the cover knobs counterclockwise, pull off the cover and release the filter from the intake tube.
3. Wipe the remaining dirt out of the housing and cover with a clean, dry rag.
4. Tap the filter (on its plastic or rubber rim) against a solid surface or your hand to knock out loose dirt. Don't blow it out with compressed air, and don't clean it with gasoline, oil, kerosene or any petroleum product.
5. Hold it up to a light. If light shines through the paper and it's undamaged, reuse it. If no light comes through, or it's oily, wet, very dirty, ripped or damaged, replace it.
6. Canister filters: pull the inner filter out of the outer one, check it the same way, then put it back inside the outer filter.
7. Install the filter (fitting it to the intake tube if there is one) and reattach the cover.

### Foam pre-filter (used with a paper filter)

1. Remove it with the paper filter.
2. Replace it if it has holes or tears, dark spots, or is dry and brittle.
3. Otherwise wash it in water with mild dish detergent and rinse until the water runs clear. Squeeze the water out gently.
4. Pat it dry with a paper towel or lay it flat until it's completely dry.
5. Do **not** oil it: oil on a pre-filter damages the paper filter it sits on.
6. Fit it back around or in front of the paper filter and install both. Replace the pre-filter whenever you replace the paper filter.

### Primary foam filter

1. Remove the cover and filter and wipe the housing without letting dirt fall into the intake.
2. Inspect it: replace it if it's torn, has dark or brown spots, is dry and brittle, or is soaked with oil.
3. Otherwise wash it in water with mild dish soap to remove dirt and oil, and rinse until the water runs clear and the soap is gone.
4. Squeeze out the water and lay it flat to dry completely; sun speeds this up.
5. When it's completely dry, coat it with foam air filter oil: fully covered but not dripping. Squeeze out or blot off the excess with a paper towel. A new foam filter also gets oil.
6. Install it and reattach the cover.

## Brand notes

- **Briggs & Stratton engines** on Craftsman, Husqvarna, John Deere, Ariens, Toro, MTD, Troybilt, Murray, Snapper and Cub Cadet riding mowers and lawn tractors: the filter and foam pre-filter slide together onto an intake tube under a cover with retaining knobs. - **Troy-Bilt:** some models use a foam filter instead of paper. Source: [S78](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

- **Husqvarna zero-turns:** install a new Husqvarna filter once a year and clean it several times a season. Source: [S73](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/service-a-husqvarna-zero-turn-lawn-mower.md)

## Verify the fix

With a clean or new filter installed and the cover secured, the engine should start and run with normal power, without sputtering, smoking or overheating.
