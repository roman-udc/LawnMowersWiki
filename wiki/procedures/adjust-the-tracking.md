# Adjust the tracking

Fixes: [Bad drive control linkage or damper](../causes/bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper.md) (tracking out of adjustment).

When a zero-turn or stand-on mower no longer moves forward in a straight line because one drive tire turns faster than the other, the tracking needs adjusting. Linkages run from the steering levers to a plate on the hydraulic motors; a speed adjustment bolt changes the rotation speed of one tire so both turn at the same speed. The location and steps vary by manufacturer and model, so the Sources all send you to the owner's manual for the details.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-wont-drive-straight.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-is-weak-on-one-side.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-ariens-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md)

## Before you start

- Check the tire pressures first: tires on each side need equal pressure, at the manufacturer's spec.
- Check the steering dampers: a bad or loose damper makes the mower jerk to one side and is replaced, not adjusted.
- Rule out a brake dragging on one side, air in one transmission and an unbalanced attachment. Several Sources list these before tracking.
- Find the tracking adjustment in your owner's manual.

## Steps

1. On level ground, drive the mower straight forward and note which way it veers, which tells you which tire is moving faster.
2. Find the linkages from the steering levers to the plate on the hydraulic motors, and the speed adjustment bolt, using the owner's manual.
3. Adjust the speed adjustment bolt to change the rotation speed of the faster (or slower) tire so both tires move at the same speed.
4. Drive straight again and repeat until the mower tracks straight.

## Tips & common mistakes

- Don't just compensate with the steering levers: a mower that won't drive straight could have a bigger issue behind it.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-wont-drive-straight.md)

## Verify the fix

The mower moves forward in a straight line with both tires turning at the same speed.
