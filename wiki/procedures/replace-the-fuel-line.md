# Replace the fuel line

Fixes: [Clogged fuel line](../causes/clogged-fuel-line.md), and the damage from [old fuel](../causes/old-fuel.md).

A fuel line that is clogged can often be cleared. Replace it when it can't be cleared, or when it is dry, cracked or leaking.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-and-stratton-engine-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-fuel.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-lawn-mower-running-rough-sputtering.md)

## Before you start

- Does the mower have a fuel shut-off valve? If not, you'll need pinch-off pliers or clamps to stop the fuel.
- Note the length and diameter of the old line: a new line must match both.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-and-stratton-engine-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-lawn-mower-running-rough-sputtering.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-push-lawn-mower.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-mower-loses-power.md)

## Safety

- Gasoline is flammable. Stop the fuel flow before you take a line off, catch fuel in a container, and work away from heat.
- Carburetor cleaner is flammable too.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-and-stratton-engine-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-push-lawn-mower.md)

## Tools & parts

- Fuel pinch-off pliers or clamps (if there's no shut-off valve)
- Carburetor cleaner
- Compressed air (a can works)
- A container for fuel
- New fuel line of the same length and diameter, if the old one can't be cleared or is cracked
- Worm gear clamps, to replace pinch-style clamps

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-and-stratton-engine-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-push-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. Stop the fuel flow with the shut-off valve or with pinch-off pliers.
2. Take off the end of the fuel line furthest from the tank and put it in a container set lower than the tank, so fuel can run downhill.
3. Restart the fuel flow. Good flow means the line is clear: reattach it.
4. If flow is poor, stop the fuel again and remove the line from the mower.
5. Spray carburetor cleaner into the line and blow it out with compressed air. Repeat until it's open.
6. Reattach the line. If it can't be cleared, or it's dry and cracking, replace it with new line of the same length and diameter.
7. When you replace a line, replace pinch-style clamps too; worm gear clamps are suggested.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-and-stratton-engine-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ariens-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-lawn-mower-running-rough-sputtering.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-push-lawn-mower.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-exmark-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md)

## Brand notes

- **Ariens, Ferris, Gravely, Exmark:** these Sources recommend swapping pinch clamps for worm gear clamps when you replace a cracked line. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ariens-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-exmark-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md)
- **Craftsman:** replace a dry, cracking line before it starts to leak. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-fuel.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Replacement fuel hose is sold at hardware stores. Source: [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-service-a-push-lawn-mower.md)
- Don't wait for a cracked line to leak; replace it when you see it drying out. Source: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-fuel.md)

## Verify the fix

With the line reattached and fuel turned on, fuel should flow freely to the end of the line, with no leaks at the clamps, and the engine should start.
