# Replace the starter solenoid

Fixes: [Bad starter solenoid](../causes/bad-starter-solenoid.md).

When you turn the key to start, the starter solenoid sends current to the starter. If it has failed, replace it with a manufacturer-approved part.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-solenoid-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Before you start

- Have you confirmed the solenoid is bad (see [Test the starter solenoid](test-the-starter-solenoid.md))? Replace it only after checking the battery charge, loose or corroded wires and the ground.
- This procedure is for a frame-mounted solenoid under the battery box on a riding mower or lawn tractor.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-starter-solenoid.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-tell-your-lawn-mower-solenoid-is-bad.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/poulan-pro-starter-solenoid.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-solenoid-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Safety

- Turn the ignition off and remove the key. Wear work gloves.
- Disconnect the negative battery cable first.
- Repair of power equipment can be hazardous. Don't proceed unless you understand all the steps; some repairs should be done only by a qualified technician.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-solenoid-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Tools & parts

- Wrench set
- Work gloves
- Replacement starter solenoid (manufacturer-approved)

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-solenoid-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. **Remove the battery.** Lift the seat. Remove the bolt holding the negative cable to the battery. Lift the insulating cover and remove the bolt holding the positive cable. Lift the battery out of the battery box.
2. **Remove the battery box.** Release the seat switch wire harness clip from the seat bracket for slack. Release the retaining tabs, lift the box out of the mower body and set it on the fender.
3. **Remove the solenoid.** Note where each wire connects (take a photo). Remove the mounting nuts from the terminal posts and pull the wires off. Pull the wires off the coil spades. Remove the solenoid mounting bolt from the frame, rotate the solenoid forward and pull it out.
4. **Install the new solenoid.** Move the mounting clip from the old solenoid to the new one. Position it on the frame and install the mounting bolt. Connect the wires to the coil spades, then put the wires on the terminal posts with the mounting nuts.
5. **Reinstall the battery box.** Position it in the opening, align the wire harness and battery cables, lower it in and engage the retaining tabs. Reconnect the seat switch harness clip to the seat bracket.
6. **Reinstall the battery.** Lower it into the box. With the insulating cover on the cable, reconnect the positive cable, then the negative cable. Lower the seat.

## Brand notes

- **Craftsman, MTD, Troy-Bilt, Toro, Husqvarna, Murray, Cub Cadet, Ariens:** the manufacturer procedure covers common riding mowers and lawn tractors from these brands. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-solenoid-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)
- **Craftsman, Poulan Pro:** check battery charge, wiring and ground before replacing. Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-starter-solenoid.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/poulan-pro-starter-solenoid.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Take a photo of the wire connections before you remove them so you can reconnect them correctly. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-starter-solenoid-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)
- Replace the solenoid only if the engine cranks when the solenoid is bypassed; otherwise look at the wiring and ground. Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/poulan-pro-starter-solenoid.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-tell-your-lawn-mower-solenoid-is-bad.md)

## Verify the fix

Turn the key to START: you should hear the solenoid click and the engine should crank.
