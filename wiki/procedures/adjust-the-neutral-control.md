# Adjust the neutral control

Fixes: [Bad drive control linkage or damper](../causes/bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper.md) (neutral position mis-adjusted).

A zero-turn shouldn't move when both drive levers are in neutral, and a lawn tractor with an automatic transmission shouldn't move when the motion control lever is in neutral. If the mower creeps forward or backward in neutral, the neutral position needs adjusting. The Source gives short steps for both kinds of mower.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/creeps-in-neutral.ltr44448.md)

## Before you start

- Zero-turn (two drive levers) or lawn tractor with an automatic transmission (motion control lever)? The steps differ.
- If these steps don't match your tractor's drive system, use the owner's manual for adjusting neutral.
- Only one Source covers this job; check the owner's manual for your model before relying on it.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/creeps-in-neutral.ltr44448.md)

## Safety

A mower that creeps in neutral is dangerous: fix it before using the mower again.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/creeps-in-neutral.ltr44448.md)

## Tools & parts

- The owner's manual for your drive system. The Source names no other tools or parts.

## Steps

**Zero-turn:**

1. Pull off the back wheels.
2. Adjust the neutral control to eliminate movement.
3. Check for movement in neutral after each adjustment, and keep adjusting until the mower stays still in neutral.

**Lawn tractor with an automatic transmission:**

1. Loosen the lever adjustment bolt.
2. Move the motion control lever until the tractor stays still.
3. Tighten the adjustment bolt firmly.

## Tips & common mistakes

- Check for movement after each adjustment rather than making one large change.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/creeps-in-neutral.ltr44448.md)

## Verify the fix

With the controls in neutral, the mower stays still and doesn't creep forward or backward.
