# Repair a flat tire

Fixes: [Bad tire or wheel](../causes/bad-tire.md), [Wrong tire pressure](../causes/wrong-tire-pressure.md) (a tire that won't hold air).

## Before you start

- Where is the leak? Tire sealant can fix a hole in the tread, not in the sidewall. A sidewall hole or a tire with too much damage needs a new tire: [Replace a tire](replace-a-tire.md).
- Is a nail, screw or other object stuck in the tire? Leave it in until the sealant is in.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/flat-tire.ltrtr142.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-repair-a-riding-lawn-mower-tire-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-rear-tire.md)

## Safety

Wear work gloves and eye protection when handling the sealant.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-repair-a-riding-lawn-mower-tire-video.md)

## Tools & parts

- Pliers, work gloves, safety glasses.
- Tire sealant (the bottle comes with a hose); an inner tube for leaks sealant can't fix.
- Soapy water to find the leak; air to refill the tire.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/flat-tire.ltrtr142.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-repair-a-riding-lawn-mower-tire-video.md)

## Steps

1. Find the leak. If an object is stuck in the tire, don't pull it out yet: the sealant may not seal the hole. If you can't see the leak, put air in the tire and spray it with soapy water; leaking air makes bubbles.
2. Confirm the leak is in the tread. If it's in the sidewall, replace the tire instead.
3. Put on gloves and eye protection. Check the chart on the sealant bottle for the amount to use for your tire size.
4. Turn the wheel so the valve stem is at the 5 o'clock position.
5. Remove the valve cap and the valve stem core.
6. Open the sealant bottle, connect the supplied hose to the bottle tip, and connect the other end to the valve stem.
7. Squeeze the bottle to inject the right amount.
8. Disconnect the hose, wipe off any residue and reinstall the valve stem core.
9. Fill the tire to the recommended pressure and reinstall the valve cap.
10. Now pull out the stuck object, if there is one.
11. Drive the mower for 3 to 5 minutes to spread the sealant evenly inside the tire.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-repair-a-riding-lawn-mower-tire-video.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Pulling the object out before adding sealant can leave a hole the sealant won't seal.
- Sealant first; on some tires an inner tube can be installed instead of replacing the tire when sealant doesn't stop the leak.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/flat-tire.ltrtr142.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-repair-a-riding-lawn-mower-tire-video.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/what-are-the-riding-mower-parts-that-require-replacement-most-often.md)

## Verify the fix

The tire holds the recommended pressure after the sealant has been spread. If it still leaks, or the hole is in the sidewall, replace the tire.
