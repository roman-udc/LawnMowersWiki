# Check compression

Fixes (diagnoses): [Low compression](../causes/low-compression.md), [Worn piston rings or valve guides](../causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides.md), [Bad valve train](../causes/bad-valve-train.md), [Bad head gasket](../causes/bad-head-gasket.md). This is a test, not a repair: it tells you whether the engine has a compression problem before anyone tears it down.

## Before you start

- Check the oil level first, and drain any overfill.
- Know your engine model: real numbers need the right gauge and the engine's own specifications.
- Engines with an automatic compression release (ACR) read low on a plain compression test. Use a leak-down test on them unless the manufacturer gives ACR compression specs.

## Safety

Remove the spark plug wire before turning the blade by hand.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bolens-mower-wont-start.md)

## Tools & parts

- Compression gauge that fits the spark plug hole.
- Socket to remove the spark plug.
- A leak-down tester gives a better answer on ACR engines; the smoking articles leave leak-down tests to a mechanic.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bolens-mower-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-smoking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Steps

1. **Quick check (push mower).** With the spark plug wire off, turn the blade backward slowly to the compression stroke and feel for resistance. This only tells you roughly whether there is compression.
2. Check the oil level; drain excess oil if the engine is overfilled.
3. Remove the spark plug.
4. Thread the compression gauge into the spark plug hole and zero the gauge.
5. Turn the key briefly to start, so the starter spins the engine.
6. Read the gauge. Over 40 psi is expected, and many engines read over 80 psi. Under 40 psi means a compression problem.

## Brand notes

- Riding mowers (Sears): if compression is low, adjust the valve lash. The Source gives no numbers. Source: [S6](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/runs-rough-or-misfires.ridtr03.md)
- Kubota: a compression test can help identify a piston ring problem; the Source leaves it to a mechanic. Source: [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Don't take an ACR engine's low reading as a failed engine without a leak-down test or the manufacturer's ACR specs.
- The blade-turning check is only a quick check; use a gauge for real numbers.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bolens-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)

## Verify the fix

After a repair, the gauge reads above 40 psi (many engines over 80 psi).
