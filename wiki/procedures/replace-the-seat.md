# Replace the seat

Fixes: [Bad safety switch](../causes/bad-safety-switch.md), when the seat itself is the problem.

A riding mower's seat holds and presses the seat switch, which lets the engine run only when it senses someone in the seat. If the seat is cracked or broken, or its foam cushioning is missing or broken down, the seat may not press the switch, and the engine shuts off when you release the brake. The fix is a new seat. The seat also keeps you stable on the mower, so replace it when it's damaged or worn.

## Before you start

- Does the engine shut off when you release the brake while you're seated? Check the seat for cracks, breaks and flattened or missing foam.
- If the seat looks sound, test the seat switch itself first: see [Replace the seat switch](replace-the-seat-switch.md).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/easy-diy-riding-lawn-mower-repairs.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-seat-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Safety

- Park on a level surface, set the parking brake, turn the ignition off and remove the key. Wear work gloves.
- Disconnect the negative battery cable before you start.
- Repairs can be hazardous. Use the proper tools and safety gear, don't proceed unless you're confident, and leave some repairs to a qualified technician.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-seat-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Tools & parts

- Wrench set, slot screwdriver, work gloves.
- A new seat: use a manufacturer-approved part.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-seat-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Steps

1. **Disconnect the battery.** Lift the seat, remove the bolt holding the negative cable to the battery, and tuck the cable away so it can't touch the battery post.
2. **Remove the old seat.** Remove the retaining bolt and washer from the seat bottom. Press the seat adjustment lever and slide the seat fully forward. Release the locking tab and pull the seat off the bracket, supporting the adjustment lever as you go, then remove the lever.
3. **Move the seat switch.** Disconnect the wire harness from the seat switch, turn the switch counterclockwise to release it, and pull it off the old seat.
4. **Install the new seat.** Push the seat switch into the new seat's bottom and turn it clockwise to lock it. Connect the wire harness to the switch. Install the adjustment lever and support it while you snap the new seat into the bracket. Reinstall the retaining bolt and washer.
5. **Reconnect the battery.** Reconnect the negative cable to the negative terminal and lower the seat.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-seat-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Brand notes

- These steps apply to common Craftsman, Husqvarna, MTD, Troy-Bilt, Toro, Murray, Snapper, Cub Cadet and Ariens riding mowers and lawn tractors.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-seat-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Tips & common mistakes

- Don't forget to move the seat switch to the new seat and plug its harness back in.
- Replacing the seat won't help if the switch itself has failed. Test the switch if the seat is in good shape.

## Verify the fix

Sit on the mower, start it and release the brake. The engine should keep running.
