# Missing idler arm spring

## Summary

The idler arm spring pulls the idler (tensioner) arm so that its pulley keeps tension on a belt. On the drive side it tensions the pump drive belt that runs the hydrostatic transmission, or the drive belt on a lawn tractor. If the spring breaks, stretches or falls out, the belt goes slack and slips, and the mower moves weakly or not at all. On the deck, a worn idler bracket or stretched spring lets the idler move about and can make the mower vibrate.

The fix is easy: replace the spring (and a worn bracket).

## Before you start

- **Safety:** turn the mower off, remove the key and the spark plug wire(s), and let the transmission cool before you reach in.
- Which belt is affected? A mower that won't move points to the drive (pump) belt spring; vibration from the deck points to the deck idler bracket and spring.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower won't move](../problems/mower-wont-move.md): the drive belt has no tension, so the mower won't move forward or reverse, or moves slowly.
- [Mower is vibrating](../problems/mower-is-vibrating.md): the hole in the idler bracket where the spring hooks on wears larger, or the spring stretches, and the deck vibrates.

## How to confirm

1. Find the idler (tensioner) arm on the belt that drives the transmission pump or the wheels.
2. Check that the spring is there, hooked on at both ends, not broken and not stretched.
3. On the deck, check whether the spring hole in the idler bracket has worn larger.

## Fix

- Replace a spring that is broken, missing or stretched. One Source adds: adjust the spring tension if needed.
- If the drive belt fell off, check the spring and the tensioner pulley before refitting the belt.
- For deck vibration, replace the worn bracket or spring, and check the bearing and bushing in the engagement linkage; replace them if worn.

There is no separate procedure page for the spring.

## Parts & fitment

- Idler arm spring: match the spring for your model's drive (or deck) idler arm.
- Idler bracket: replace if the spring hole has worn larger.
