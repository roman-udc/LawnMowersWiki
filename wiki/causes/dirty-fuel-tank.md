# Dirty fuel tank

## Summary

A gas tank that has sat for months can collect debris, rust and old gas. A rusted metal tank can block the fuel line, upset the fuel supply and put rust inside the engine. Cleaning the tank is only needed when the mower has sat for a long time; left alone, a dirty tank can cause misfires, poor performance and lower fuel efficiency. Draining and cleaning the tank is an easy job; a tank with rust holes or cracks has to be replaced. Only one Source covers this Cause.

Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-metal-lawn-mower-gas-tank-in-9-easy-steps.md)

## Before you start

- Has the mower sat for a few months, for example over winter?
- Is the gas in the tank more than three months old?
- Is the tank metal? Rust is a metal-tank problem.

Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-metal-lawn-mower-gas-tank-in-9-easy-steps.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): after a long time sitting, a tank full of debris, rust or old gas may keep the mower from starting.
- Engine misfires or performs poorly, with lower fuel efficiency.

Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-metal-lawn-mower-gas-tank-in-9-easy-steps.md)

## How to confirm

Drain the tank, then shine a flashlight inside and look for debris, rust, cracks and holes. Debris in the tank, gas more than three months old, or an engine that isn't performing after a long sit are the signs the tank needs cleaning.

Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-metal-lawn-mower-gas-tank-in-9-easy-steps.md)

## Fix

Drain, rinse and clean the tank, check the fuel filter and lines, and refill with fresh gas. For rust, fill the cleaned tank with vinegar and leave it overnight to soften the rust, then finish cleaning in the morning and let the tank dry fully. Rust holes or cracks mean a new tank.

## Parts & fitment

- Gas tank: replace it if it has rust holes or cracks.
- Fuel filter: the Source says replace it every year.
- Fuel line: most lines are 1/4" or 5/16" rubber hose; match the old line. The author prefers worm-gear clamps to pinch-style clamps to cut down on leaks.

Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-clean-your-metal-lawn-mower-gas-tank-in-9-easy-steps.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- To keep rust from forming, swirl a baking soda and water mix in the tank, dispose of it, flush the tank with soapy water, then put a small amount of diesel in to coat the tank with a light oil. The Source says this doesn't harm the gasoline.
- Dispose of old fuel properly; don't use it in other tools or vehicles.
