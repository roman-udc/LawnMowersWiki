# Bad blade adapter or loose blade

## Summary

The blade has to be held tight and true on the spindle, motor shaft or crankshaft. Four things go wrong here:

- **Loose blade.** A blade bolt that isn't tightened to spec lets the blade slip, so it may not turn with the shaft and the mower doesn't cut. A loose blade also knocks or thuds.
- **Wrong or missing hardware, or hardware in the wrong order.** The blade isn't held securely, and a slight movement becomes a big vibration at blade speed.
- **Wrong-size or non-fitting blade.** Blades differ in length, center hole and style (high-lift, low-lift, gator, mulching), and many 52 inch blades don't fit every 52 inch deck. A blade that doesn't fit vibrates and can damage the deck shell and spindle housings. A blade that is slightly off may not sit securely on the spindle, may hit the deck shell, and cuts poorly.
- **Broken or worn blade adapter** (push mowers that use one). A hard hit on a stationary object, or many smaller hits, breaks the adapter. It also wears from a damaged crankshaft, a wrong blade or an unbalanced blade.

Tightening a blade is an easy fix. Replacing an adapter is a blade-job-level repair, but a hit hard enough to break it may also have damaged the crankshaft.

## Before you start

- Were the blades just changed? Wrong-blade and hardware problems usually follow a recent blade replacement.
- Is the new blade OEM or aftermarket? Which blade length and center hole size does the deck need?
- Push mower with a blade adapter: did it hit a rock, stump or root before the problem started?

## How to confirm

- Check that each blade is tightly secured.
- Check the blade hardware against the owner's manual or parts diagram: every piece present and in the order shown. A concave washer goes with its cupped side toward the blade.
- After a blade replacement, check the blade's length and center hole size, then lay the blade on a new OEM blade and compare center hole, length and width. Matching the listed measurements is not proof: several Sources received aftermarket blades sold as fitting that didn't fit.
- Push mowers: look for a broken or worn blade adapter. On a mower with a blade engagement lever, check the lever for loose or worn parts.

## Fix

- **Loose blade:** tighten the blade bolt to the torque spec in the operator's manual. The Sources give no general value; see Brand notes.
- **Wrong hardware or order:** reinstall with the manufacturer's hardware in the order on the parts diagram.
- **Wrong blade:** fit a blade that matches the deck. Several Sources prefer OEM blades for fit; match any aftermarket blade against an OEM blade first. See [Measure a mower blade](../procedures/measure-a-mower-blade.md) (the [Customer intake](../guides/customer-intake.md) Guide also covers taking a blade's measurements).
- **Broken or worn adapter:** replace the adapter and install new blades at the same time. Check the crankshaft for damage first.

Blade removal and installation steps are in [Sharpen and replace the blades](../procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades.md).

## Brand notes

- **EGO:** install order is flange first (push and self-propelled: inner flange mounted to the fan, fan blades toward the deck; zero-turn: flange on the motor shaft), then the blade with the side printed "THIS SIDE FACING GRASS" outward (unprinted: sails toward the deck), then washer and bolt finger tight. Align the blade holes with the flange holes, hold it with screwdrivers, and torque with a torque wrench: 36–43 ft-lb on push and self-propelled, 36–41 ft-lb on zero-turn (per Source, verify against the owner's manual). Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-is-vibrating.md)
- **Exmark:** a splined bushing sits in the blade's center hole. Source: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-and-sharpen-your-exmark-mower-blades.md)
- **Ferris, Hustler:** sell blades designed for their decks; aftermarket blades may not fit even with the right length and center hole. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-zero-turn-mower-deck-shakes-vibrates.md), [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-vibrates.md)
- **Honda push mowers:** a lever engages the blade; check it for loose or worn parts. Replace a broken adapter together with a new Honda blade. Source: [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-mower-is-vibrating.md)

## Parts & fitment

- **Mower blade:** match length, center hole size and width to the OEM blade for that deck, and the blade style. Two Sources' authors name Stens and Oregon as good aftermarket makers.
- **Blade hardware:** use the manufacturer's bolt, washers and any bushing, per the parts diagram.
- **Blade adapter** (push mowers): replace together with the blade.
