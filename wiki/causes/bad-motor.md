# Bad motor

## Summary

On an electric mower, a damaged motor or start switch stops the mower starting, makes it stutter or shut down, or (with a bent motor shaft) makes the blade wobble. Frequent malfunctions despite proper maintenance may mean the motor is failing from wear and tear or has reached its factory limits. The parts are pricey and the work needs electrical and mechanical knowledge, so this is a pro job; weigh the repair cost against the mower's value.

## Before you start

- Rule out the cheap causes first: battery charge and seating, the charger, the safety key and switches, wiring and debris under the deck.
- Is the mower still under warranty? Consult the manufacturer before repairs: work outside an authorized repair center can void the warranty.

## How to confirm

- Bent shaft: grab both ends of the blade and check for extra movement (with the blade installed correctly).
- Motor or switch damage needs testing by a trained technician or service center.

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/skil-electric-mower-wont-start-12-starting-problems.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/sun-joe-electric-mower-wont-start.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/worx-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Fix

Take it to a professional repair shop or the brand's authorized service center. Ask the dealer whether repair or replacement makes more sense given the mower's age and condition, the part cost and the labor: a repair may cost more than the mower is worth.

## Brand notes

- **EGO:** have an EGO service center diagnose a suspected bent shaft; contact EGO customer service for a failed motor.
- **Kobalt, SKIL, Sun Joe, WORX:** call the brand's customer service for authorized repair locations before dropping the mower off.
