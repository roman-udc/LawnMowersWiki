# Overfilled fuel tank

## Summary

Filling the tank to the brim leaves no room for the fuel to expand. As it warms up, fuel spills out or vents as vapor. It is common when the tank is filled in the morning and the mower is then left in the sun. The fix is easy: fill less.

> **Safety:** spilled gasoline and its vapor are flammable. Fill the tank with the engine off, and wipe up any spill before starting the mower.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)

## Before you start

- Was the tank filled right up to the top of the fill neck?
- Was the mower filled in the cool of the morning and then parked in the sun?
- What does the service manual give as the fuel capacity and fill level?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower is leaking gas](../problems/mower-is-leaking-gas.md): fuel spills or vapor vents from a tank filled to the brim, usually as the fuel warms up in the heat.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)

## How to confirm

The leak or gas smell follows a fill to the brim, and the fuel level sits above the bottom of the fill neck. If the tank is not overfilled, look for another leak, such as a [cracked fuel tank](cracked-fuel-tank.md) or a [bad gas cap seal](bad-gas-cap-seal.md).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)

## Fix

Stop filling at the bottom of the fill neck, not above it. Check the service manual for the fuel capacity and the right fill level. No part needs replacing.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-smells-like-gas-and-how-to-fix-it.md)
