# Oil or rust on belt

## Summary

Oil from an engine leak, or spilled oil, drips onto the deck, pulleys and belt. An oily belt can't grip the pulleys: it slips, builds heat and comes off, and oil left on it for a long time makes the belt swell. Rust from old deck parts and pulleys (moisture makes it form) transfers to the belt, dries it out early, and makes it crack and break.

The fix is moderate: stop the oil leak or remove the rust first, replace pulleys too rusty to clean, then fit a new belt.

## Before you start

- Is the engine leaking oil? Check the engine oil level before each use; oil that keeps going low may mean a leak.
- Are the pulleys and deck parts rusty?

## How to confirm

Look for oil on the belt, pulleys and deck, and rust on the pulleys, brackets and deck parts. A belt left in oil may look swollen; a rust-dried belt is cracked.

Sources: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/this-is-why-your-belt-keeps-falling-off-your-lawn-mower.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-mower-belt-keeps-breaking.md)

## Fix

1. Find and repair the oil leak, and clean the oil off the deck, pulleys and other components.
2. Clean the rust off the deck, pulleys and brackets. Replace any pulley or component so rusty that the rust can't all be removed.
3. Once the deck is clean of oil and rust, install a new belt: [Replace the deck belt](../procedures/replace-the-deck-belt.md).

Don't replace the belt without fixing the cause: belts are expensive, and the new one will fail the same way.

## Parts & fitment

- **Deck belt:** a new belt once the cause is fixed.
- **Pulley:** replace pulleys too rusty to clean fully.

Sources: [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-zero-turn-mower.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-kubota-lawn-mower.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Keep oil and rust off the belt and keep the pulleys clean.
- Clean the deck after each mowing and before storage: clippings and dirt hold moisture and speed up corrosion. Dry the mower completely (outside on a sunny day, or with a leaf blower).
- A cracked belt should be replaced as soon as possible.
