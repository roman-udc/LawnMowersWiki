# Water in fuel

## Summary

Water gets into the gas when a mower is left outside in hard rain (it can get in around the vented fuel cap, and a worn cap seal may not keep it out), or when ethanol in the gas attracts moisture during long storage and separates. Water is heavier than gas and sinks to the bottom of the tank, and it doesn't burn: the engine sputters, runs rough or sluggishly as if it's running out of gas, may shut down, or won't start at all. The fix is easy: drain the water-contaminated fuel and refill with fresh gas.

## Before you start

- Was the mower left outside in the rain, or stored a long time (for example over winter) with gas in the tank?
- Is it a gas or a diesel mower? Diesel tanks collect water the same way.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine runs rough](../problems/engine-runs-rough.md): the engine sputters and hesitates like it's running out of gas, even though the tank has fuel; the engine has trouble compressing the water.
- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): after rain or winter storage, the diluted fuel won't fire the engine. On a diesel mower, water at the bottom of the tank causes starting problems.
- [Engine starts then dies](../problems/engine-starts-then-dies.md): the mower runs sluggishly and may shut down.
- [Mower is smoking](../problems/mower-is-smoking.md): water in the fuel system is named as one reason a mower smokes; run it until it no longer sputters or smokes from the water.

## How to confirm

Shine a flashlight into the fuel tank and look for gas and water separating: the water (or the ethanol and water mix) sits at the bottom of the tank.

Sources: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-sputtering-solved.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-acts-like-its-running-out-of-gas.md)

## Fix

Drain the contaminated fuel, also drain the fuel lines and carburetor bowl, replace the fuel filter, and refill with fresh gas mixed with a moisture-reducing additive (such as Sea Foam or STA-BIL), then run the mower until it no longer sputters or smokes from the water: [Drain and refill the fuel tank](../procedures/drain-and-refill-the-fuel-tank.md). See also [Replace the fuel filter](../procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter.md).

On a diesel mower: drain the tank and refill with fresh diesel. Running the mower about a minute removes air and water from the system; Sea Foam can help clean the fuel system and reduce moisture.

## Brand notes

- **Husqvarna:** rain on a mower left outdoors can get water into the tank around the cap; drain it with a siphon and refill with fresh fuel and an additive.
- **Sea Foam:** one Source mentions it only as a link to its rain-damage article.

## Parts & fitment

- Fuel filter: replace it after draining water-contaminated fuel.
- Fuel stabilizer or fuel additive for the fresh gas.

Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/when-your-riding-mower-gets-wet.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-mower-wont-start-after-leaving-in-the-rain.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-sputtering-solved.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Add a fuel stabilizer to the gas; one author adds Sea Foam to every tank.
- Store the mower inside or covered, and keep all fuel in a dry place.
- Keep the fuel cap on securely, with its seal in good condition.
- For general owner know-how, see the Guides: [Choosing the right gas](../guides/choosing-the-right-gas.md), [Mower got wet](../guides/mower-got-wet.md).
