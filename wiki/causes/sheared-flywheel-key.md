# Sheared flywheel key

## Summary

The flywheel key is a small metal rectangle that keeps the flywheel lined up on the crankshaft. That alignment makes the flywheel magnet pass the ignition coil at the right moment. The key is designed to shear if the blade hits a rock or stump hard enough to make the flywheel slip. This protects more expensive parts. A sheared key throws off the spark timing. Replacing it is a moderate job that needs a flywheel puller.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Before you start

- Did the blade hit a rock, stump or root just before the engine stopped suddenly?
- Does a spark tester show spark? If not, check the spark plug and ignition coil first.
- Do you have a flywheel puller and a torque wrench?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): the engine stopped suddenly after a blade strike. Now it spins but won't fire, even though the spark tester sparks. The spark is there, but at the wrong time. A damaged key can also make the engine run rough after a strike.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/runs-rough-or-misfires.ridtr03.md)

## How to confirm

1. Check for spark with a spark plug tester. If the plug sparks, the coil is good, but the timing may be off.
2. Remove the blower housing and the flywheel, then inspect the key. If the key is damaged or sheared, that confirms the cause.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Fix

Replace the key. The Sears PartsDirect guide rates it difficulty 4 and about 60 minutes.

1. Disconnect the battery and remove the blower housing.
2. Feed nylon rope into the cylinder through the spark plug hole to jam the piston.
3. Remove the flywheel bolt and fan, then break the flywheel loose with a puller.
4. Remove what's left of the old key.
5. Line up the notch in the flywheel with the slot in the crankshaft, and push the new key in flush.
6. Reassemble and torque the flywheel bolt.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)

## Parts & fitment

- **Flywheel key:** use a manufacturer-approved key for your engine.
- **Tools:** wrench set, socket set, flywheel puller, slot screwdriver, hammer, torque wrench, nylon rope, work gloves.

## Tips & life hacks

- With an impact wrench, you can remove the flywheel bolt without jamming rope into the cylinder. Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)
- Tighten the two puller bolts a little at a time, alternating, so the flywheel lifts evenly. If it won't break free, tap the top of the flywheel with a mallet. Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)
- Leave some rope outside the cylinder so it's easy to pull out, and remember to remove it before starting. Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-the-flywheel-key-on-a-riding-lawn-mower.md)
