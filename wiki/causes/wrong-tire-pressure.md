# Wrong tire pressure

## Summary

A low tire, or unequal pressure between the left and right tires, makes the mower and its deck sit lower on one side, so one side cuts shorter. It is one of the most common and most overlooked reasons for an uneven cut, and the Sources say to check it first, before adjusting the deck, or you may have to undo those adjustments later. Unequal pressure also makes the mower pull to one side. The fix is easy: inflate the tires to the recommended pressure, equal on both sides.

## Before you start

- Riding mower, zero-turn or push mower? On zero-turns the drive tires are the large rear tires; some have air-filled caster wheels too. On push mowers the Sources point to loose or damaged wheels instead: see [Bad tire or wheel](bad-tire.md).
- Do you know the recommended pressure? It is on the tire sidewall or in the operator's manual. The Sources give no pressure values.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Uneven cut](../problems/uneven-cut.md): the deck sits lower on the side with the low tire; grass on one side is cut longer, or a stair-step cut. Over- or underinflation also causes uneven travel. A damaged or unequal wheel does the same (see [Bad tire or wheel](bad-tire.md)).
- [Mower pulls to one side](../problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side.md), or a zero-turn seems weak on one side: the low side drags or sinks into soft ground. Even a few pounds of difference can affect steering.
- Steering issues from a low tire.

## How to confirm

1. Check pressure with the tires cold.
2. Remove the valve cap and put a tire pressure gauge on the stem.
3. Compare with the recommended pressure (tire sidewall or operator's manual). Left and right drive tires must match; on a zero-turn, check the front pair to each other too.
4. Check all four tires on a riding mower, or both on a two-wheeled walk-behind with inflatable tires.

Check this before leveling the deck: unequal pressure mimics an unlevel deck.

## Fix

Add air with a compressor (or let some out) to the recommended pressure, equal on both sides, and replace the valve cap. Inspect a tire that keeps going low for slow leaks or damage, and [repair the flat tire](../procedures/repair-a-flat-tire.md). If the cut is still uneven with correct pressures, [level the deck](../procedures/level-the-deck.md).

## Brand notes

Every brand article gives the same advice: equal pressures, to the brand's spec in the operator's manual or the tire sidewall. Points of detail:

- Ego and Greenworks zero-turns: match left and right rear, and left and right front, to the operator's manual.
- Spartan: check the air-filled caster wheels too.
- RYOBI zero-turn: check drive-wheel pressures and inspect the caster wheels.
- Husqvarna zero-turn service: lug nuts must be tight as well.
- Gravely service: a low tire can cause steering issues and an unlevel deck; set to Gravely's psi (no number given).

## Tips & life hacks

- Check tires before any deck adjustment, so you don't have to reverse them later.
- A low tire is commonly overlooked.
