# Bad fuel shut-off valve

## Summary

Many mowers have a fuel shut-off valve that starts and stops the flow of gas from the tank. It usually sits at the bottom of the tank, sometimes inline in the fuel line. Plastic and metal valves are both prone to leaking as they age. The fix is easy: replace the valve. A valve that is simply closed is a different fault: see [Fuel valve off](fuel-valve-off.md).

## Before you start

- Does the mower have a shut-off valve at all? Not every mower has one (some push mowers, some Bad Boy and Craftsman mowers do, others don't).
- A zero-turn with two fuel tanks most likely has two valves, one by each tank.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-craftsman-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S22](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md)

## Safety

- A leaking valve drips gas from under the tank. Gasoline is flammable: catch leaking fuel in a container, wipe up spills, and keep sparks and flames away while you work.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/10-reasons-your-lawn-mower-could-be-leaking-gasoline.md), [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-leaking-gas.md)

## How to confirm

- Find the valve (at the bottom of the tank, or inline in the fuel line) and look for gas coming out of it.

Sources: [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-lawn-mower-leaking-gas.md), [S23](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md)

## Fix

Replace the leaking shut-off valve. There is no separate procedure page.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ariens-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-exmark-mower-problems-and-solutions.md), [S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-exmark-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-john-deere-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md)

## Brand notes

- Ferris: a zero-turn most likely has two valves, by the left and right fuel tanks.
- SCAG: the valve is often behind the seat.
- Gravely, Kubota, John Deere: the valve is at the bottom of the tank.
- Toro, Troy-Bilt: if fitted, the valve is under the fuel tank.
