# Mowing too fast

## Summary

A ground speed (or walking speed) too fast for the grass overloads the engine or motor. In thick, tall or wet grass, and on hills and inclines, the engine has to work harder. Going too fast pushes the grass over instead of cutting it, weakens the deck's suction so the grass isn't stood up, leaves grass uncut and can clog the deck. The overloaded engine bogs, sputters, loses power, runs hot or stalls, and a battery motor can overload and shut off. The fix is easy: slow down to suit the conditions.

## Before you start

- What was the grass like: thick, tall or wet, or short and dry?
- Was the mower going uphill or on an incline?
- How fast was the mower moving? On self-propelled mowers check the speed setting; on push mowers it's your walking speed.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Uneven cut](../problems/uneven-cut.md): grass pushed over instead of cut, grass left uncut, alternating taller and shorter stripes; worse on bumpy, uneven ground where the mower bounces.
- [Engine starts then dies](../problems/engine-starts-then-dies.md): running too fast for the conditions overworks the engine and makes it stop; a battery motor can overload and shut off.
- [Engine loses power](../problems/engine-loses-power.md) or bogs down, especially when the blades are engaged.
- Engine runs rough or sputters.
- Engine overheats.
- Mower doesn't bag grass: the catcher has no chance to pick it up.

## How to confirm

Slow down and see whether the engine stops bogging and the cut improves. If the engine bogs or gets hot, slowing down (and raising the deck) is the first step.

## Fix

- Slow the ground speed in thick, tall or wet grass and on hills and inclines; mow light, dry grass faster than heavy, wet grass.
- Run the engine at full throttle while you slow the ground speed.
- For tall grass, make several cuts: the highest cutting height first, then lower for each later pass.
- Avoid mowing wet grass, which clumps and drags the blades, and mow more often so the grass doesn't overgrow.
- If the engine bogs or gets hot, slow down, raise the deck and take breaks.

There is no repair Procedure for this Cause. See also [Wrong engine speed](wrong-engine-speed.md).

## Brand notes

- Battery push mowers (Kobalt, RYOBI, Sun Joe, Toro, Worx): mowing too fast in heavy grass can overload the motor so it shuts off.
- Greenworks: some models have a speed setting; on push mowers without self-propel, adjust your walking speed.

The other brand articles (Bad Boy, Big Dog, Bobcat, Craftsman, Cub Cadet, Ego, Ferris, Gravely, Honda, Husqvarna, Hustler, John Deere, Kubota, SCAG, Simplicity, Snapper, Spartan, Toro, Troy-Bilt, Ariens, Exmark) give the same slow-down advice.

## Tips & life hacks

- Very tall grass is better handled with a brush cutter or rotary mower.
- Double- or triple-cut long grass: highest deck setting for the first cut, then a little lower for the next.
