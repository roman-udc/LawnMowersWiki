# Incorrect starting procedure

## Summary

Mowers have safety features that block starting, or shut the mower off, unless the operator follows the starting steps: the bail lever held, the brake set, the PTO (blade) switch off, the operator in the seat, the safety key in. On gas engines the choke also has to be set right: on for a cold start, then off once the engine warms. The steps vary by brand and model, so the operator's manual is the reference. The fix is easy: start the mower the right way.

## Before you start

- What kind of mower is it: push, self-propelled, riding, zero-turn, battery or diesel? The steps differ for each.
- Is the operator's manual at hand? Procedures vary by model, even within one brand.
- Is the engine cold or warm (choke setting)?
- Is it a new or second-hand mower whose owner hasn't learned its starting steps yet?

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): the choke is in the wrong position, or a safety feature is not satisfied while the engine turns over. A blade control lever that isn't held against the handlebar stops the engine.
- [Engine won't crank](../problems/engine-wont-crank.md) (nothing happens, no click, when you turn the key): on riding mowers and zero-turns the brake isn't set, the PTO is engaged or the operator isn't seated.
- [Battery mower won't start](../problems/battery-mower-wont-start.md): the safety key or button wasn't used, or the handle isn't unfolded and locked.
- [Engine starts then dies](../problems/engine-starts-then-dies.md): the choke left on once the engine is warm makes it run sluggish, sputter and die; leaving the seat with the blades engaged, or getting off a zero-turn without setting the parking brake, shuts the mower off.

## How to confirm

Go through the safety conditions one by one before replacing any switch:

- **Push and self-propelled (gas):** the bail lever or blade control bar is pulled back and held against the handlebar while you pull the recoil or turn the key. Releasing it stops the engine.
- **Riding and zero-turn:** the operator sits centered on the seat (not too far forward) so the seat switch engages; the brake is engaged (foot pedal, a lever under the steering wheel, or zero-turn steering levers in the outward position); the PTO is off (electric PTO knob pushed down, manual PTO lever pulled back); the speed levers are in neutral.
- **Battery mowers:** the battery is securely in place, the safety key is inserted (not all models have one) and the safety button is used; the foldable handle is unfolded and locked, and the side rails fully extended with the clamps locked. Rails can slip out of position.
- **Choke:** on (full choke) for a cold engine, off once it warms up. If the choke lever doesn't work properly, check the choke linkage.
- **Diesel:** brake set, neutral, and wait for the glow plugs to warm the engine.

If every condition is met and it still won't start, suspect a faulty safety switch instead.

## Fix

Follow the starting steps in the operator's manual for your model. For a zero-turn, see [Start a zero-turn mower](../procedures/start-a-zero-turn-mower.md).

**Gas riding mower or zero-turn, cold engine (separate choke and throttle levers):**

1. Sit in the operator's seat.
2. Set the parking brake, or move the steering levers to the outward position. Put the speed levers in neutral.
3. Turn the PTO (blade) switch off.
4. Choke lever to full choke; throttle about halfway (one Source says about 1/3).
5. Turn the key to start and release it when the engine starts. Never hold the key in start longer than 10 seconds.
6. Move the choke to off, and idle at half throttle a few minutes before going to full throttle.
7. To drive, release the parking brake and move the steering levers in toward the center.

**Gas push or self-propelled:** set the choke for a cold engine (auto-choke models skip this), hold the bail lever against the handle, and pull the rope or turn the key. Move the choke off once warm.

**Battery push or self-propelled:** with the battery in place and the safety key inserted, press the safety button and pull the safety bar (bail switch) to the handle, then release the button. The mower stops when you release the bar. See the conflicting advice below on the order of these steps.

**Battery zero-turn:** insert the safety key, press start (EGO: hold the start/stop button 3 seconds and press the driving mode button), release the parking brake and pull the levers to the center.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** press the safety button first, then pull the safety bar to the handle ([S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/greenworks-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kobalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md), [S35](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-why-your-ego-electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S40](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/skil-electric-mower-wont-start-12-starting-problems.md), [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/sun-joe-electric-mower-wont-start.md), all expert-blog) vs pull the safety bar to the handle and press the button ([S37](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ryobi-electric-battery-mower-wont-start.md), [S46](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-electric-mower-wont-start.md), [S54](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/worx-electric-mower-wont-start.md), all expert-blog).
