# Disconnected fuel solenoid

## Summary

Many riding mower carburetors have a fuel solenoid valve. It shuts off fuel to the carburetor when you kill the engine, to prevent backfire. If the valve's wire harness is disconnected, the solenoid won't open, no fuel reaches the carburetor, and the engine cranks but won't start. Fix difficulty: easy (reconnect the wire).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md)

## Before you start

- Is it a riding mower or lawn tractor? The Sources describe this valve on riding mower carburetors.
- Has the carburetor recently been removed, rebuilt or replaced? The solenoid wire is disconnected during that work and must be reconnected afterwards.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-rebuild-a-riding-lawn-mower-carburetor.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-replace-a-riding-lawn-mower-carburetor.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): the engine fires briefly on a short burst of starter fluid (so the fault is fuel supply), and the wire harness is off the fuel solenoid valve on the carburetor.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md)

## How to confirm

1. Do the starter-fluid test. In a well-ventilated area, take off the air filter, spray a short burst of starter fluid into the air intake and try to start the engine. If it fires, the problem is fuel supply.
2. Find the fuel solenoid valve on the carburetor. On common Briggs & Stratton riding mower carburetors it threads into the float bowl.
3. Check that its wire harness is connected.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-rebuild-a-riding-lawn-mower-carburetor.md)

## Fix

- Reconnect the fuel solenoid valve's wire harness.
- If the carburetor was just off the engine, check that the fuel solenoid wire was reconnected during reassembly.
- If the wire is connected and the engine still isn't getting fuel, move on to the other fuel-supply checks (air filter, fuel filter, fuel line, fuel pump), and then the carburetor itself.

## Brand notes

- **Briggs & Stratton engines** on Craftsman, Troy-Bilt, Husqvarna, MTD, Poulan, Toro, Murray, Snapper, Yard-Man, Weed Eater, Ariens and John Deere riding mowers and lawn tractors: the fuel solenoid wire is disconnected before the carburetor comes off and reconnected after it goes back on. ## Parts & fitment

- **Fuel solenoid valve:** on the carburetors these Sources cover, it threads into the float bowl and is refitted with the bowl on a rebuild. The Sources only describe reconnecting its wire, not testing or replacing the valve.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/how-to-rebuild-a-riding-lawn-mower-carburetor.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Check the solenoid wire before deciding to rebuild or replace the carburetor. It costs nothing and can save you buying a part you don't need. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-spins-but-wont-start-video.md)
- Take a photo of the carburetor linkage connections before removing the carburetor, so everything goes back where it belongs.
