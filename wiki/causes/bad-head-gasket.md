# Bad head gasket

## Summary

A gasket on the engine fails and lets engine oil leak out. The oil drips onto a hot surface such as the muffler or the engine, burns off and makes smoke. Most smoking articles call this a "bad engine gasket" and don't say which gasket it is, so the leak may not be at the head gasket. The head gasket seals the cylinder head to the top of the engine: when it is damaged it leaks oil, and air leaking past it lowers compression so the engine loses power. Finding and replacing the gasket can be easy or hard depending on where it sits (moderate to pro).

## Before you start

- Ask what colour the smoke is. Blue or white smoke means oil is burning; a leaking gasket is one of the checks after the oil level, the piston rings and the valve train.
- Ask whether the oil level keeps dropping even though it is topped off, and whether there are oil spots under the mower.
- Rule out oil spilled during a recent oil change first: see [Spilled oil on engine](spilled-oil-on-engine.md).

## How to confirm

- Inspect the engine for an oil leak and follow it to the leaking gasket. Depending on where the gasket is, the leak can be easy or hard to find. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
- Look for oil spots under the mower and an oil level that keeps dropping. Source: [S11](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-riding-lawn-mower-overheats-and-how-to-cool-it-down.md)
- A damaged head gasket lets air leak out of the cylinder and lowers compression, which a compression check shows (see [Check compression](../procedures/check-compression.md)). Source: [S8](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/leaking-oil-or-gas.ridtr05.md)

## Fix

Find the bad gasket and replace it. How hard that is depends on the gasket's location: some are easy to reach, others are not. A leaking head gasket is replaced. Internal engine problems, including a failed engine gasket, are often sent to a small engine repair shop or dealer.
