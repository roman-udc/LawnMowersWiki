# Bad PTO clutch

## Summary

The PTO (power take-off) clutch passes engine power to the blades by engaging the deck drive belt. On riding mowers and zero-turns it is either an electric clutch mounted on the engine crankshaft, or a manual-engagement clutch worked by a lever, cable, belts and pulleys.

It fails in two ways:

- **It stops passing power,** so the blades won't engage or spin. A worn or faulty clutch that won't power the blades must be replaced.
- **Its bearing fails,** so the mower shakes. The ball bearing inside an electric clutch can wear out (and even fall out of the clutch), sending vibration through the whole mower. On a manual-engagement clutch, the bushings and linkages wear and fail.

Push mowers have no clutch: they use a blade-engagement lever instead.

The fix is to replace an electric clutch, or the worn cable, lever, springs and linkages of a manual one. The Sources give no difficulty rating.

## Before you start

- Is it an electric clutch (PTO switch) or a manual one (lever and cable)? The checks differ.
- Is it a push mower? Then there is no clutch: check the blade-engagement lever for loose or worn parts instead.
- Are the blades failing to turn, or turning but shaking? No power points to the clutch or its wiring; shaking points to the clutch bearing or the manual linkages.

## How to confirm

For an electric clutch that won't engage, [test the PTO clutch](../procedures/test-the-pto-clutch.md): about 12 volts at the clutch's power wire, 1 to 3 ohms across the coil, then remove it and inspect the hub and brake pad for buildup, corrosion and wear. If no power reaches the clutch, the fault is upstream, not the clutch.

For vibration, check the clutch bearing (electric) or the bushings and linkages (manual) for wear.

## Fix

- **Electric clutch:** replace the clutch (clutch assembly) when its bearing has failed or it won't engage the blades when switched on. The manufacturer Source links an outside repair guide for replacing the electric clutch; the wiki has no replacement steps yet.
- **Manual-engagement clutch:** replace the worn clutch cable, lever, springs, bushings or linkages.
- Check an electric clutch first with [Test the PTO clutch](../procedures/test-the-pto-clutch.md).

## Brand notes

- **SCAG** zero-turns most likely have an electric clutch.
- **Kubota** uses either manual engagement (belts and pulleys) or an electric clutch; replace the clutch assembly when its bearing fails.
- **Cub Cadet, Husqvarna, Craftsman, Gravely, Simplicity, Toro, Exmark, Snapper, Ariens:** riding and zero-turn models use an electric or manual clutch; their push mowers have no clutch, only a blade-engagement lever to check for loose or worn parts. The Snapper article says "electric mower" where it means an electric clutch.

## Parts & fitment

- **Electric PTO clutch:** mounts on the engine crankshaft. Tighten to your machine's torque specification (about 50 to 60 ft-lbs in general).
- **Manual clutch parts:** clutch cable, engagement lever, springs, bushings and linkages.
