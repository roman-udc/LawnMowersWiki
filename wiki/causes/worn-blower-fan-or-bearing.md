# Worn blower fan or bearing

## Summary

A belt-driven grass collection system has an extra belt driving a fan, called the impeller, inside a blower housing on the discharge side of the deck; it gives more air speed through the tube to the bags. Worn or damaged fan blades or housing, or too wide a gap between the fan and the housing, lose the airflow that carries clippings. The bearings on the fan shaft can also wear out; string or long grass wrapped around the shaft ruins them. Fix difficulty: moderate (finalizer judgement: the housing usually has to be taken apart).

## Before you start

- Is it a belt-driven collection system (a blower housing and an extra belt)? Flow systems move grass with the blade airflow alone.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower doesn't bag grass](../problems/not-bagging-grass.md): a worn fan or housing, a wide gap, or bad bearings lose the airflow to the bags.

## How to confirm

On most units the blower housing must be taken apart to check the fan. Check the fan blades and housing for wear or damage, the gap between fan and housing, and the shaft bearings, and look for string or grass wrapped around the shaft.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/15-reasons-why-your-lawn-mower-is-not-picking-up-grass.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mower-is-not-bagging-grass-solved.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/pick-up-leaves-with-a-mower.md)

## Fix

Replace a worn fan. Replace worn fan-shaft bearings; most blower housings must be disassembled to do it.
