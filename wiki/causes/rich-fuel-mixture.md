# Rich fuel mixture

## Summary

The engine burns more fuel than the air it gets, so it "runs rich" and blows black smoke. Usually the air supply is restricted, most often by a plugged air filter (see [Clogged air filter](clogged-air-filter.md)) or a choke that is on or stuck (see [Wrong choke setting](wrong-choke-setting.md)). A carburetor that runs too rich also gives black smoke and high fuel use. Most fixes are easy.

## Before you start

- Ask what colour the smoke is. Black (or gray) smoke points here; blue or white smoke means oil is burning and points to other Causes.
- Ask whether the choke was left on.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-is-blowing-blue-black-or-white-smoke.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower is smoking](../problems/mower-is-smoking.md): black smoke from the muffler; with a carburetor running too rich, high fuel consumption too. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-husqvarna-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-is-blowing-blue-black-or-white-smoke.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-ariens-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-toro-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/clean-your-cub-cadet-carburetor.md)

## How to confirm

Check the air filter first. If it is fine, look for another restriction to the air flow, such as a stuck choke. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/gravely-is-blowing-blue-black-or-white-smoke.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Fix

Clean or replace a plugged air filter and clear any other air restriction. A carburetor that runs too rich is cleaned.

- [Clean or replace the air filter](../procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter.md)
- [Clean the carburetor](../procedures/clean-the-carburetor.md)
