# Worn piston rings or valve guides

## Summary

A bad or worn piston ring, or scoring on the cylinder wall, lets oil get up into the combustion chamber, where it burns and the mower smokes while in use. The Sources describe piston ring and cylinder wall damage; none of them describes valve guides, so valve-side wear is covered on [Bad valve train](bad-valve-train.md). Finding it means tearing down the engine, and the engine may need replacing, so this is a pro fix. Caught late, internal problems like this are costly.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-smoking.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-lawn-mower-problems-fixed.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md)

## Before you start

- Rule out the simple causes first: the air filter and the oil level. Then check the spark plug for oil.
- Ask how old the engine is and how it has been cared for. A teardown can end in a choice between replacing the engine and buying a new mower.

## How to confirm

- Remove the spark plug (a 3/4 in or 5/8 in socket, depending on the engine) and look for excessive oil on it. Oil on the plug points to a piston ring or valve train problem but can't tell which. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-is-smoking.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-mower-is-smoking.md), [S37](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S40](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md), [S45](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-toro-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
- Pinpointing it needs an engine teardown with leak-down and compression checks by a mechanic; a compression test can help identify a ring problem (see [Check compression](../procedures/check-compression.md)). Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S37](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S42](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/snapper-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S44](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-mower-is-smoking-white-blue-or-black-smoke.md), [S46](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-ariens-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Fix

Either a ring problem or a scored cylinder means taking the engine apart; often the engine has to be replaced. Take it to a small engine repair shop or dealer if you are not mechanically inclined. Depending on the age of the mower, a new engine or a new mower may be the better choice (author opinion).

- [Check compression](../procedures/check-compression.md) (diagnosis)

## Parts & fitment

- Piston rings, or a whole replacement engine when the cylinder is scored. The Sources give no part numbers. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md), [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Internal problems (piston ring, valve train, engine gasket) do the most harm when they are not caught early; get the mower to a shop soon.
