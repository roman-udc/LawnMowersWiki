# Wrong belt

## Summary

A belt that isn't the right one for the mower, such as an aftermarket belt matched at an automotive or hardware store, is rarely an exact match. It performs worse, wears faster, can shred along its edges or squeal, and can pose safety risks. The Sources' authors recommend the OEM belt from the mower's manufacturer. The fix is easy: fit the correct belt.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-kubota-lawn-mower.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-craftsman-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-belt-is-bad.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-bad-boy-lawn-mower.md)

## Before you start

- Read the part number off the old belt, or look it up by model number in the parts diagram.
- If the belt is shredded along its edges, first confirm it is the right belt and routed correctly.

Sources: [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-craftsman-mower-deck-belt.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-belt-on-an-mtd-lawn-mower.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-zero-turn-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-belt-keeps-coming-off.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Belt keeps coming off](../problems/belt-keeps-coming-off.md): a belt that isn't an exact match wears faster and shreds or comes off; a wrong type of belt can squeal.

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-bad-boy-lawn-mower.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-kubota-lawn-mower.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-zero-turn-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-craftsman-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-belt-is-bad.md)

## How to confirm

Compare the part number on the belt with the one in the parts diagram or owner's manual for your model. Check the routing against the decal on the mower, the owner's manual or a parts diagram.

Sources: [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-zero-turn-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-craftsman-mower-deck-belt.md)

## Fix

Replace it with the correct belt for your model (the authors prefer OEM), routed as the decal or manual shows: [Replace the deck belt](../procedures/replace-the-deck-belt.md).

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-bad-boy-lawn-mower.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-craftsman-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-a-craftsman-mower-deck-belt.md)

## Brand notes

- **Cub Cadet:** a wrong type of belt can squeal; an aftermarket belt of lesser quality wears faster.
- **Kubota, Craftsman:** non-OEM belts matched at an automotive dealership or hardware store likely won't be an exact match.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-belt-is-bad.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/belt-keeps-coming-off-your-kubota-lawn-mower.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-craftsman-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md)

## Parts & fitment

- **Deck belt:** match the part number on the old belt; if it's worn off, look it up in the parts diagram by model number. A riding mower has two different belts (drive and deck), so buy each as its own part.

- Cheaper belts are tempting, but the authors report that OEM belts last longer and handle the mower's stress better.
