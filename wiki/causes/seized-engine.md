# Seized engine

## Summary

A seized (locked-up) engine can't turn, so the starter can't spin it. This is a job for a service technician (pro).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/lawn-tractor-won-t-start.ridtr04.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-clicks-but-doesnt-turn-over-video.md)

## Before you start

Rule out the cheaper causes of a click with no crank first: check that the starter cable is good and the starter is getting power.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-clicks-but-doesnt-turn-over-video.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine won't crank](../problems/engine-wont-crank.md): you hear a click when you turn the key, but the engine doesn't turn over.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/lawn-tractor-won-t-start.ridtr04.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-clicks-but-doesnt-turn-over-video.md)

## How to confirm

1. Confirm the starter cable is good, so the starter is getting power.
2. Remove the plug from the screen above the flywheel to reach the flywheel bolt.
3. Turn the flywheel bolt clockwise with a socket wrench to try to spin the engine. If it won't turn, the engine is locked up.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-clicks-but-doesnt-turn-over-video.md)

## Fix

A locked-up engine needs a service technician to diagnose and repair it.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/riding-lawn-mower-engine-clicks-but-doesnt-turn-over-video.md)
