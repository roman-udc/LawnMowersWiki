# Bad governor

## Summary

The engine governor adjusts the throttle automatically to hold the engine speed (RPM) steady. If the governor is misadjusted or not working properly, the engine can't react quickly enough to the extra load when the blades engage, so it bogs down. The one Source that names it gives no repair steps and no difficulty rating.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-mower-loses-power-when-the-blades-are-engaged.md)

## Before you start

- Does the engine run fine until the blades engage, then bog down?
- Have the basics been checked first: air filter, fuel age, fuel filter, blade drive belt and the deck underside? The Source says to rule these out before suspecting the engine.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-mower-loses-power-when-the-blades-are-engaged.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine loses power](../problems/engine-loses-power.md): the engine runs fine right up until the blades kick in, then bogs down because it can't keep up with the extra load.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-mower-loses-power-when-the-blades-are-engaged.md)

## How to confirm

The Source gives no test for the governor. It groups the governor with a dirty carburetor as the reasons an engine bogs only when the blades engage, after fuel, air, belt and deck causes are ruled out.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-mower-loses-power-when-the-blades-are-engaged.md)

## Fix

The Source says only that a misadjusted or faulty governor is a cause; it gives no adjustment or replacement steps. If the basics check out and the engine still bogs or stalls when the blades engage, the Source recommends calling a professional for a proper diagnosis.
