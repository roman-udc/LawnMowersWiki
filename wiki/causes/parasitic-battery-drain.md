# Parasitic battery drain

## Summary

Something keeps drawing power from the battery while the mower sits. The Sources name one case: the ignition key left in the ON position after mowing. On its own that doesn't affect the battery much, unless the mower has an oil light on the dash, or a 12-volt accessory cord is left plugged into an accessory port installed on the mower. Then the battery goes flat and is too weak to turn the engine over. The fix is easy: charge the battery, and turn the key off and unplug accessories after mowing.

## Before you start

- Was the key left on after the last mow?
- Does the mower have an oil light on the dash, or a 12-volt accessory port with something plugged in?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-things-that-are-draining-the-life-of-your-lawn-mower-battery.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-battery-keeps-dying.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Battery keeps dying](../problems/battery-keeps-dying.md): the battery is dead the next time you go to mow, after the key was left on with an oil light or a plugged-in accessory.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-things-that-are-draining-the-life-of-your-lawn-mower-battery.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-battery-keeps-dying.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-lawn-mower-battery-keeps-dying.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-battery-keeps-dying.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-battery-keeps-dying.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-battery-keeps-dying.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-battery-charging-times-explained.md)

## How to confirm

Check the key position and the accessory port. If the key was left on and the dash oil light or an accessory was drawing power, that explains a flat battery.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-things-that-are-draining-the-life-of-your-lawn-mower-battery.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-battery-keeps-dying.md)

## Fix

Charge the battery ([Charge the battery](../procedures/charge-the-battery.md)). Turn the key off and unplug 12-volt accessories when you finish mowing. If the battery still keeps dying, check the cables, the charging system and the battery itself.
