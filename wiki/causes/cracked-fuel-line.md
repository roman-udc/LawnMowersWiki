# Cracked fuel line

## Summary

Rubber fuel lines dry out with age and weather, turn brittle and crack, and gas leaks out of the cracks. Lines also leak where they are held by hose clamps: a clamp can loosen, or pinch and puncture the hose. A punctured or cracked line can also suck air in, so the engine gets too much air and only runs with the choke on. The fix is easy: replace the line, and swap pinch-style clamps for worm gear clamps while you're at it. A line that is blocked rather than leaking is a different fault (clogged fuel line).

## Before you start

- Note the diameter of the old line: the replacement must match it.
- Does the mower use pinch-style clamps? Plan to replace them with worm gear clamps.
- Does the mower have a fuel shut-off valve? Use it to stop the fuel while you replace the line.

## Safety

- Gasoline is flammable, and even a small split releases fumes and fuel. Stop the fuel with the shut-off valve before taking a line off, wipe up spills, and keep sparks and flames away.

## How to confirm

1. Follow the fuel line from the tank to the carburetor, checking each section for wet spots, cracks, dry rot, brittleness and tears.
2. Pay close attention to the sections held by hose clamps: look for loose connections, punctures or tears from the clamp, and residue or debris built up around the clamp.

## Fix

Replace the cracked, dry-rotted or punctured line with a new line of the same diameter, and install new clamps. Replacing dry, cracking lines before they leak is good practice.

## Brand notes

- Ferris zero-turns: follow the lines from both tanks; check hoses, connectors and clamps.
- Craftsman: bad fuel as well as weather dries and cracks the lines.
- Cub Cadet: many manufacturers use pinch-style clamps, which damage hoses faster than worm gear clamps.

## Parts & fitment

- Fuel line: match the diameter of the old line.
- Fuel line clamp: worm gear clamps are less likely to pinch or puncture the line than pinch-style clamps.

## Tips & life hacks

- When you replace a line, convert the factory pinch-style clamps to worm gear clamps.
