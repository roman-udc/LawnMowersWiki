# Bent deck or deck shell

## Summary

The deck shell is shaped so that the air moved by the spinning blades creates suction under the deck, which stands the grass up for an even cut. Hitting a tree, fence post, pole, rock, building or other hard surface can tweak or dent the shell, and severe rust can corrode holes in it. A damaged shell loses that airflow and suction and gives a poor or uneven cut. The fix is to restore the shell to its original shape or replace it; replacing a shell means moving every component over, so it is usually a dealer or pro job.

## Before you start

- Has the mower hit a tree, post, rock or building recently? Did the cut get worse after that?
- How old is the mower, and how much life does it have left? That decides whether a shell, a full deck or neither is worth buying.
- Get the deck model from the owner's manual or deck tag before asking the dealer for prices.

## How to confirm

- Look over the deck shell for dents, bends or a tweaked edge where it may have struck an obstruction.
- Look for heavy rust or holes and punctures in the shell; a deck that is badly rusted through should be replaced.

Sources: [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/not-cutting-evenly.ridtr02.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-your-riding-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-lawn-mower-has-a-bad-or-uneven-cut.md)

## Fix

1. Try to repair the shell back to its original shape, with no holes or punctures.
2. If it can't be repaired, replace the deck shell and move all components from the old deck to the new shell, or replace the complete deck assembly.
3. Before deciding, get two prices from the dealer: a replacement shell plus the labor to swap the components, and a complete deck. The difference may be small once labor is counted. Weigh both against the mower's age.

There is no Procedure page for this repair; a shell or deck replacement is usually done by the dealer.

## Parts & fitment

- Deck shell: must match the deck model; all components (the deck houses the blades, mandrel pulleys and blade belts) move over from the old deck.
- Mower deck assembly: a complete deck avoids the labor of swapping components.
