# Bad ignition switch

## Summary

The ignition switch (a key switch, or a toggle or push switch on some models) supplies power to the engine's electrical components. When it fails, turning the key does nothing and the mower won't turn over, or, less often, the engine won't stop when the key is turned off. Testing it takes a multimeter; replacing it is an easy job on most mowers.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine won't crank](../problems/engine-wont-crank.md): you insert and turn the key (or press the switch) and nothing happens; the mower doesn't start or even turn over. A riding mower that won't stop when the key is turned off also points to the switch. A switch with failed internal contacts can also short in the run position and blow the fuse (see blown fuse).
- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): general "won't start" articles list it for electric-start mowers: the key is turned but the mower won't start.

## How to confirm

Test the switch for continuity with a multimeter:

1. Unplug the wire harness from the back of the switch. On many riding mowers you open the hood, release the locking tabs and push the switch out of the dash.
2. Find the prongs marked **B** (Battery) and **S** (Starter Solenoid).
3. Turn the key (or toggle) to START and set the meter to resistance.
4. Touch one probe to B and the other to S. Near 0 ohms means the switch is good. Infinite resistance means it's bad and must be replaced.

In the START position the switch should connect the wire on B (red on the Craftsman example) to the wire on S (white).

If the mower keeps blowing fuses, check the switch in the RUN position too: B to A1 should read near 0 ohms and B to every other spade should read infinite. Resistance between B and any spade except A1 means the internal contacts have failed; replace the switch.

## Fix

Replace a switch that fails the test, using a manufacturer-approved part. On a keyless Smart Switch, reset the code first.

- [Replace the ignition switch](../procedures/replace-the-ignition-switch.md)
- [Reset the ignition code](../procedures/reset-the-ignition-code.md)
- [Test and replace a fuse](../procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse.md): if the fuse keeps blowing, the short can be in the ignition switch; this Procedure traces it.

Most brand articles simply say "test with a multimeter and replace if bad".
