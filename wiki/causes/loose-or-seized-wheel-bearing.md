# Loose or seized wheel bearing

## Summary

Over time, wheel bearings can wear out or seize and axles can begin to bind, especially after the mower has been used on rough terrain. A dragging or sticking wheel throws off the mower's balance and makes it favor one side. The fix is to replace the wheel, which the Source calls a straightforward fix. Only one Source covers this Cause.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-lawn-mower-pulls-to-one-side-while-cutting.md)

## Before you start

- The Source describes this on walk-behind mowers. Has the mower been used on rough terrain?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-lawn-mower-pulls-to-one-side-while-cutting.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower pulls to one side](../problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side.md): a dragging or sticking wheel makes the mower favor one side.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-lawn-mower-pulls-to-one-side-while-cutting.md)

## How to confirm

Spin each wheel by hand and check for resistance when turning, wobbling, cracks or bent rims.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/why-your-lawn-mower-pulls-to-one-side-while-cutting.md)

## Fix

If a wheel looks worse for wear, replace the wheel entirely.
