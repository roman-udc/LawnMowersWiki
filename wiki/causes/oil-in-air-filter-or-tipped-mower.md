# Oil in air filter or tipped mower

## Summary

On a push mower, tipping the mower the wrong way or mowing on a hillside lets engine oil run into the air filter and the carburetor. The oil burns and the mower smokes. The fix is easy: wipe out the oil, replace the oil-soaked filter and clean the carburetor.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Before you start

- Ask whether the mower was just tipped on its side (to clean or work under the deck) or used on a slope, and which side was up.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower is smoking](../problems/mower-is-smoking.md): smoke after the push mower was tipped or used on a hillside; the air filter is covered in oil. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md)

## How to confirm

Take off the air filter cover and look for oil on the filter and in the housing.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md)

## Fix

Wipe the oil out of the filter housing, replace the oil-covered filter, and clean the carburetor.

- [Clean or replace the air filter](../procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter.md)
- [Clean the carburetor](../procedures/clean-the-carburetor.md)

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-smoking.md), [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md)
