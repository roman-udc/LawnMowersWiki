# Empty fuel tank

## Summary

No gas, no start. The tank is empty or too low because the owner forgot the last fill-up, the fuel gauge stopped working, or a fuel leak emptied the tank sooner than expected. It is the most obvious cause and the one most often skipped: people troubleshoot filters and switches before looking in the tank. The fix is easy: add fresh fuel, and find and repair any leak.

## Before you start

- When was the tank last filled? Is the mower using gas faster than usual (a sign of a leak)?
- Does the mower have a fuel gauge, and does it work?
- Is it a 4-cycle engine (straight gasoline), an older 2-cycle engine (gas and oil mix), or a diesel?
- Is the tank a small one (many push mowers) that needs refilling often?

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): the engine turns over but doesn't fire because no fuel reaches it.
- Engine starts then dies, or the mower stops running or stalls while mowing: it runs out of gas mid-job. A low tank can also cut the fuel supply when mowing on a hillside or uneven ground, even when the tank isn't empty.

## How to confirm

- Look in the tank, or check the fuel gauge (a visual gauge or on the display).
- If the tank emptied sooner than expected, look for a fuel leak at the tank, fuel lines, fuel filter, carburetor and fuel pump (if fitted), and check that the fuel gauge works.

## Fix

1. Add fresh fuel. For 4-cycle gas engines: unleaded, 87 octane or higher (regular), no more than 10% ethanol.
2. For an older 2-cycle engine, mix 2-cycle oil into the gas before adding it. A 2-cycle engine has only one fill port, for the gas and oil mix.
3. For a diesel mower, fill with diesel.
4. If the mower uses fuel faster than normal, find the leak and repair or replace the leaking part. Repair or replace a faulty fuel gauge.

## Brand notes

- **Bolens, Briggs & Stratton, Toro, Lawn Boy, White Outdoor:** current mowers use 4-cycle engines and straight gasoline; very old push mowers may be 2-cycle, which need a gas and oil mix and have a single fill port. Sources: [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bolens-mower-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-stratton-mower-wont-start.md), [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-boy-mower-wont-start.md), [S54](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-push-mower-wont-start.md), [S61](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/white-outdoor-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Weed Eater, Yard Machines:** on a 2-cycle engine, mix 2-cycle (premium) oil with the gas before adding it. Sources: [S60](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/weed-eater-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S64](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/yard-machines-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Big Dog, Cub Cadet, John Deere:** minimum 87 octane, maximum 10% ethanol. One Cub Cadet buying tip says "minimum of 10% ethanol", a typo for the maximum. Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/big-dog-zero-turn-wont-start.md), [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-wont-start.md), [S30](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Bolens, PowerSmart, SENIX, Troy-Bilt push mowers:** small fuel tanks that run dry quickly. ## Parts & fitment

- **Gasoline:** fresh, unleaded, 87 octane or higher, no more than 10% ethanol, for 4-cycle engines. 2-cycle engines need a gas and 2-cycle oil mix. Diesel mowers take diesel.

## Tips & life hacks

- Check the tank first. The author of several articles has seen customers check filters and switches before the fuel. Sources: [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/country-clipper-zero-turn-mower-wont-start.md), [S30](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S48](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scotts-riding-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S58](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/walk-behind-mower-wont-start.md), [S67](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-exmark-mower-wont-start.md), [S70](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-troy-bilt-lawn-mower-dies.md)
- Diesel: let a hot engine cool a few minutes before filling. If it ran out of fuel, let it run about a minute to bleed air from the fuel system. Source: [S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
- **Safety:** if the mower stalls on slopes, add fuel, and avoid steep inclines. Source: [S35](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-keeps-stalling.md)
