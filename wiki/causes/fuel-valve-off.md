# Fuel valve off

## Summary

Many mowers have a fuel shut-off valve, usually near the bottom of the fuel tank. It is often closed for storage or transport, or while someone works on the fuel system. When it is closed, no fuel reaches the engine, so the engine turns over but won't start. Opening the valve is an easy fix.

## Before you start

- Does this mower have a fuel shut-off valve? Not all mowers do.
- Is the mower new, just out of winter storage, just transported, or just back from a fuel-system repair?

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/first-check-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-boy-mower-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start-after-winter.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/self-propelled-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): typically the first start of the season, or the first start after storage, transport or fuel-system work. The tank has gas but none reaches the engine.
- Mower isn't getting gas: at the start of the season the engine gets no fuel because the valve was closed for off-season storage.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/black-max-mower-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start-after-winter.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md)

## How to confirm

Find the valve (usually near the bottom of the fuel tank) and check its position. Make sure it is open before you diagnose anything else in the fuel system.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/craftsman-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/first-check-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/self-propelled-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Fix

Turn the fuel shut-off valve to the open (on) position, then try starting again.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/black-max-mower-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-boy-mower-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start-after-winter.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/solved-your-honda-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
