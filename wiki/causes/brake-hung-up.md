# Brake hung up

## Summary

A brake can hang up, or fail on one side, so it drags and doesn't let the wheel move freely. On a zero-turn the dragging tire slows that side, so the mower pulls or feels weak on one side. At the simple end, the parking brake has just been left on and the mower won't move.

Repair varies: releasing a parking brake is trivial; a seized or worn brake means removing the wheel and repairing the braking system, which differs by model.

## Before you start

- Is the parking brake fully released?
- Find your model's parts diagram: the braking system varies by model.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower pulls to one side](../problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side.md): one drive tire drags, so a zero-turn pulls or is weak on that side.
- [Mower won't move](../problems/mower-wont-move.md): the brake has been left on.

## How to confirm

1. Park on a flat surface and set the parking brake.
2. Chock the front wheels.
3. Raise the rear drive tires securely on jack stands.
4. Disengage the hydros by releasing the transmission bypass levers, so the wheels can move freely.
5. Release the parking brake.
6. Spin each drive wheel by hand and feel for resistance.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-wont-drive-straight.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-is-weak-on-one-side.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)

## Fix

If a wheel resists, remove the wheel and inspect the braking system (it varies by model; use the parts diagram). Repair or replace worn or seized parts. If the mower won't move at all, first check that the brake isn't simply left on.
