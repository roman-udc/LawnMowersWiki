# Bad gas cap seal

## Summary

The gas cap has a rubber seal (gasket) inside it. With age the seal dries out, shrinks or tears and no longer seals, so gas that sloshes up to the cap while mowing seeps out around it. The leak is easy to miss: the gas often evaporates before you see a wet spot, and all you notice is a gas smell, sometimes strong in a garage or shed. The fix is easy: replace the cap (or just the gasket, where one Source says that's enough).

## Before you start

- Is there a gas smell but no wet spot anywhere? Several Sources say to check the cap last, after the fuel lines, shut-off valve, tank and carburetor are ruled out.
- Does the mower lose power or stall after running a while, and run again once the cap is loosened? That points to the cap's vent, not its seal: see [Plugged fuel cap vent](plugged-fuel-cap-vent.md).

## Safety

- Gasoline and its fumes are flammable. Wipe up spilled gas, keep sparks and flames away, and don't store a leaking mower in a closed space.
- When you rock the mower to test the cap, rock it gently. Don't tip it far enough to flip it over.

## How to confirm

1. Look at the outside of the tank around the cap for a wet spot or gas stains.
2. No wet spot doesn't rule the cap out. With the cap on, rock the mower back and forth so fuel splashes up around the cap, and watch for a wet area forming outside the tank around the cap. If one forms, the seal is bad.
3. Take the cap off and inspect it: look for cracks in the cap, and check the gasket inside for being dry, brittle, wrinkled, torn or shrunk. A missing gasket also leaks.
4. Some smell is normal because the cap is vented, but not enough to fill a garage.

## Fix

Replace the gas cap. There's no separate procedure page: the new cap screws on in place of the old one. One Source says a cracked cap must be replaced but a worn gasket can be replaced on its own after wiping the cap and gasket clean.

## Brand notes

- Ferris, Gravely, SCAG, Husqvarna: the seal dries out; check the cap last, after everything else.
- Honda: replace with a new Honda cap.
- Craftsman: a replacement cap is available online or from a Craftsman service center.
- Lawn tractors bounce while mowing, which splashes fuel up to the cap.
