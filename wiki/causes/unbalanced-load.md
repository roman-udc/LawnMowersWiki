# Unbalanced load

## Summary

A grass catcher, a towed trailer or another attachment can throw the mower off balance and pull it to one side, which can also make a zero-turn seem weaker on one side. The fix is easy: center the load's weight, balance the towed load, or remove the attachment.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-is-weak-on-one-side.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-wont-drive-straight.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)

## Before you start

- Is a grass catcher, trailer or other attachment fitted or being towed?

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-is-weak-on-one-side.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower pulls to one side](../problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side.md): the mower pulls, or a zero-turn feels weak on one side, with an attachment or towed load fitted.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-is-weak-on-one-side.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-wont-drive-straight.md)

## How to confirm

Remove the attachment and drive the mower again. If it now drives straight, the load was the cause.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md)

## Fix

Rebalance the load so the weight sits in the center, or remove the attachment.

> **Safety:** don't exceed the zero-turn's towing capacity. Zero-turn transmissions are not designed for heavy loads, and overloading can cause costly damage.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-zero-turn-problems.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-is-weak-on-one-side.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-wont-drive-straight.md)
