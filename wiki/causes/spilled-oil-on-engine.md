# Spilled oil on engine

## Summary

Oil that drips onto the engine or muffler during an oil change, or leaks onto them, burns off when the engine gets hot and smoke rolls off the mower. Spilled oil doesn't hurt the mower. It is the easiest smoking cause to fix: wipe the oil off. Oil that keeps reaching the muffler from a leak points to a bad gasket instead (see [Bad head gasket](bad-head-gasket.md)).

## Before you start

- Ask whether the smoke started soon after an oil change or after oil was added. If so, rule out a spill before anything else.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-starts-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md)

## How to confirm

Look over the engine and muffler for oil drips or wet spots. Oil that keeps coming back after it has been wiped off is a leak, often from a bad engine gasket. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-starts-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-mower-is-smoking.md), [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-mower-is-smoking.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-your-lawn-mower-may-be-smoking.md)

## Fix

Find and wipe off the excess oil. Any oil left burns off when the engine is hot. If the oil is leaking from a failing gasket, find and replace the gasket (see [Bad head gasket](bad-head-gasket.md)); how hard that is depends on the gasket's location.

## Brand notes

- Bad Boy and Ariens: oil spilled on the engine or muffler smokes as it burns off. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md), [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-ariens-lawn-mower-is-smoking.md)
- Exmark and Ferris: a bad gasket leaks oil onto a hot surface like the muffler, where it burns and smokes. Sources: [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-mower-is-smoking.md), [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ferris-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Parts & fitment

No parts are needed for a spill. A leak needs the failed gasket replaced (see [Bad head gasket](bad-head-gasket.md)).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-starts-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Wipe up oil drips right after refilling the oil. Source: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-begins-smoking-after-an-oil-change.md)
- Check for a spill first: it is the cheapest cause to rule out. Source: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-smoking.md)
