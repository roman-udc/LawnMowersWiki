# Debris wrapped around blade spindle

## Summary

String, wire, or a long, sturdy weed hidden in the grass can wind itself around the blade shaft (the spindle shaft, the round rod the blade bolts to). The wrapped material throws the blade off balance, so the deck and the whole mower shake. It is hard to see while you mow, because it sits on top of the blade, up against the deck.

The fix is easy: pull the material off the shaft. The real question is whether it has already damaged the spindle or its housing, which turns an easy job into a moderate one.

## Before you start

- **Safety:** make sure the mower cannot start before you reach under the deck: remove the key and disconnect the spark plug wire(s). Wear work gloves, because the blade edges are sharp.
- Did the vibration start right after mowing over an area with string, wire, rope or long tough weeds? That points straight at this cause.
- On a riding mower or zero-turn, which spindle is it? Check every spindle on the deck, not just one.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-craftsman-lawn-mower-vibration-solved.md), [S29](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower is vibrating](../problems/mower-is-vibrating.md): the deck or the whole mower shakes while the blades turn; string, wire or weeds are wound around the blade shaft above the blade. A spindle that was damaged by the wrapped material can also squeal.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ariens-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-solved.md), [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-lawn-mower-problems-fixed.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-riding-lawn-mower-problems.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-zero-turn-problems-how-to-fix-them.md)

- [Battery mower won't start](../problems/battery-mower-wont-start.md): debris restricting the blade's rotation stops a battery mower turning on.

Sources for Battery mower won't start: [S30](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## How to confirm

1. With the mower made safe, look at the top of each blade where it meets the blade shaft (spindle shaft).
2. Look for string, wire, or long weeds wound around the shaft. Thin wire is easy to miss, so look closely.

Sources: [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/exmark-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-solved.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-stop-your-lawn-mower-from-shaking-vibrating.md), [S21](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-solved.md), [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-lawn-mower-vibration.md)

## Fix

1. Remove everything wrapped around the blade shaft.
2. Check the shaft, the spindle, its bearing and the spindle housing for damage. If the spindle or housing is damaged, replace it.

- [Replace the spindle assembly](../procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly.md)

## Parts & fitment

No part is needed if the spindle is undamaged. If it is damaged, the parts are the spindle (blade shaft and bearing) or the whole spindle housing; match them to your deck model.

Sources: [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kubota-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S17](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-lawn-mower-is-vibrating-and-shaking.md), [S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-craftsman-lawn-mower-vibration-solved.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Walk the lawn before you mow and pick up string, wire, sturdy weeds, rocks, toys and sticks.
- When you change or sharpen the blades, check the spindles too.
