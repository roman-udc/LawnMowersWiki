# Blown fuse

## Summary

The fuse protects the mower's electrical system from a power surge or a short. It blows most often because of a short circuit: a damaged wire or failed component lets current flow to the metal frame through an unintended path, and the fuse cuts it off to prevent wiring damage and fires. Bad wiring, an overload or failing components can also blow it. A blown fuse cuts power to the ignition circuit (nothing happens at the key), to the blade clutch, or to battery charging. Replacing the fuse is easy; finding the short behind a fuse that keeps blowing is harder.

## Before you start

- Where is the fuse? It's on all Craftsman and Toro mowers except some manual pull-start models. Location varies: start around the battery (under or next to it), then look behind the dash or in the wire harness. - Blades won't engage: no battery power reaches the blade clutch.
- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): general "won't start" articles list a blown fuse in the electrical system among the electric-start checks.

If the fuse keeps blowing, trace the short. With the key off and the fuse removed, measure DC voltage across the two sides of the fuse holder. Voltage means a short in the wire from the fuse holder to the ignition switch, or in the switch itself: unplug the switch and measure again to tell which. No voltage: fit a new fuse, turn the key to RUN, then off, and check the fuse. If it blew, the short is in the ignition switch, the wire it powers in RUN, or a harness on that wire; test the switch (see bad ignition switch), then unplug the harnesses and reconnect them one at a time (dash, then engine, then chassis on the Sears example) until the fuse blows. If it didn't blow, the short is intermittent: wiggle each harness and repeat. Keep a couple of spare fuses and the mower's wiring diagram handy. ## Fix

Replace the fuse with one of the **same amperage**; never use a different rating. If fuses keep blowing, find and repair the short (damaged wire, failed harness or ignition switch), or take the mower to a dealer or repair shop to find the root cause.

- [Test and replace a fuse](../procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse.md)
- [Read a wiring schematic](../procedures/read-a-wiring-schematic.md)
- [Replace the ignition switch](../procedures/replace-the-ignition-switch.md), if the switch's internal contacts caused the short.
