# Bent crankshaft

## Summary

On a push mower the blade bolts straight to the bottom of the engine's crankshaft. When the spinning blade hits something solid (a rock, root or stump), the crankshaft can bend. The blade then wobbles off-center, which shakes the mower and gives a bad, uneven cut. Changing a bent blade does not cure it.

This is a pro repair: the engine has to be removed and torn down to replace the crankshaft, and the Sources agree the job usually costs more than a new mower (or a new engine). Most of them recommend replacing the mower.

## Before you start

- Is it a push or self-propelled mower, where the blade mounts on the crankshaft?
- Did the blade hit a rock, root or other solid object before the shaking started?
- Has the blade already been checked or replaced? If a new, balanced blade still vibrates, suspect the crankshaft (see [Dull or bent blade](../causes/dull-or-bent-blade.md)).

## How to confirm

1. Remove the spark plug (so the engine can't start) and protect your hands from the sharp blade.
2. Quick check: clamp the safety (bail) lever to the handle so the flywheel brake is released. Carefully turn the blade by hand and watch the center blade bolt. If the bolt moves all over the place, the crankshaft is most likely bent.
3. If you're unsure: remove the blade and blade adapter, turn the crankshaft and measure whether the shaft stays centered, using a dial indicator or a fixed object as a gauge. If it shifts to one side, it is bent.

## Fix

There is no do-it-yourself Procedure for this.

- Most Sources: replace the mower, because a new crankshaft usually costs more than a new mower.
- A small engine shop can replace the crankshaft, but the engine must be removed and torn down. Get a quote first; it often costs more than a new engine.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** some Sources say a small engine mechanic replaces the crankshaft ([S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-honda-lawn-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-push-mower-problems-solutions.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mower-deck-is-vibrating.md), expert-blog), while others recommend skipping the repair and buying a new mower ([S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mowers-bad-cut-solved.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-vibrating.md), expert-blog). All agree the repair often costs more than the mower is worth, so get a shop quote before deciding.

**Safety:** never try to bend the crankshaft back straight; that is a significant safety risk. Running a mower with a bent crankshaft can be extremely dangerous.
