# Wrong engine speed

## Summary

The engine isn't run at full throttle while the deck is engaged. The blades need full engine power: at a lower engine speed the blade tip speed drops and the cut is poor or uneven, and the engine can run sluggish, sputter or bog down. On some battery mowers a slow blade-speed setting does the same. The fix is easy: put the throttle at its highest setting (the rabbit icon on many mowers). If the engine is at full throttle and still short of power, it needs a small engine mechanic.

## Before you start

- Where is the throttle lever when the blades are engaged?
- Does the mower have a throttle lever at all? Many push mowers don't: they run at full speed automatically as soon as the bail lever is engaged.
- Battery mower: does it have a blade-speed setting, and is it on the slow (battery-saving) setting?
- Has the engine lost power compared with how it used to run?

## Symptoms it produces

- [Uneven cut](../problems/uneven-cut.md): slow blade tip speed gives a bad or uneven cut.

- Bagger doesn't pick up grass: the bagger needs the engine at maximum horsepower.

## How to confirm

Check that the throttle is at the highest position with the deck engaged. Listen to the engine: if it doesn't sound like it's giving its usual power at full throttle, the engine itself is the problem. Most engines should run about 3200 to 3650 rpm for maximum power, per three bagging articles from the same blog.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/15-reasons-why-your-lawn-mower-is-not-picking-up-grass.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S16](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-loses-power.md), [S27](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mower-is-not-bagging-grass-solved.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/pick-up-leaves-with-a-mower.md)

## Fix

- Set the throttle lever to its highest position (fast, the rabbit icon on many mowers) whenever the deck is engaged, and engage the blades at full throttle.
- Battery mowers with a blade-speed setting: use the faster setting for tall or thick grass.
- Full throttle but still weak: have a small engine mechanic (dealer or repair shop) test the engine. On a battery mower that still cuts badly at the fast setting, with the right height and a good blade, the brand's service center checks the blade motor or power supply. This is a pro job. One Source notes that a new engine for a push mower costs more than a new push mower.

There is no repair Procedure for the throttle setting itself.

## Brand notes

- Ego and Greenworks: some models have a blade-speed setting to save battery; the slow setting gives a bad cut in thick grass. If cutting is still poor, contact the brand's service center.
- Toro, Troy-Bilt, Hustler, John Deere: at full throttle with low power, take it to the brand's dealer or a small engine shop.
- Some push mowers have an automatic throttle with no adjustment; contact the dealer if there are problems.

The other brand articles (Big Dog, Craftsman, Cub Cadet, Exmark, Ferris, Gravely, Honda, Husqvarna, Kubota) give the same full-throttle advice.

## Parts & fitment

No parts are replaced for a throttle setting. An engine that may be too small for a bagging system is a question for a mechanic.
