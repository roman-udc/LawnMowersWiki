# Bad tire or wheel

## Summary

A wheel that is loose, damaged or the wrong size, or a tire that is punctured, worn thin or flat-spotted, makes the mower sit unlevel or wobble. One side of the deck ends up closer to the ground and the cut is uneven. Tightening loose wheel hardware is easy; a punctured tread can often be sealed; a badly damaged tire or wheel is replaced.

## Before you start

- Push mower or riding mower? Push-mower wheels are usually tightened or replaced; riding-mower tires can be sealed or replaced.
- Is the tire losing air, or is the wheel loose, cracked or bent?
- Has the mower been stored a long time with underinflated tires?

## Symptoms it produces

- [Uneven cut](../problems/uneven-cut.md): the mower sits unlevel with the deck closer to the ground on one side, giving an uneven or step cut; a loose wheel makes the mower wobble.
- [Mower pulls to one side](../problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side.md): a damaged wheel (wobble, cracks, bent rim) can make the mower pull.
- Vibration: flat spots from long storage on underinflated tires cause vibration.

## How to confirm

Inspect each wheel: look for loose hardware, damage, cracks, bent rims, a wobble, or wheels of unequal size. On riding mowers, check for punctures, thin tread and flat spots. Several Sources say to check the wheels first, before other uneven-cut causes.

## Fix

- Tighten loose wheel hardware; replace a damaged wheel with one the same size.
- A puncture in the tread can usually be sealed with tire sealant; an inner tube can fix some leaks sealant can't: [Repair a flat tire](../procedures/repair-a-flat-tire.md).
- A tire with sidewall damage, thin tread or too much damage, or flat spots that won't go away, is replaced: [Replace a tire](../procedures/replace-a-tire.md).

## Parts & fitment

- Wheel: replace with the same size as the wheel on the opposite side.
- Riding-mower front tire: same size as the other front tire. Rear tire: same size as the other rear tire, with deep tread for traction.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mowers-bad-cut-solved.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/flat-tire.ltrtr142.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Keep tires properly inflated in storage: flat spots from long storage on underinflated tires sometimes can't be corrected.
