# Worn blade sail

## Summary

The sail is the high side of the mower blade. Spinning fast, it creates the air movement under the deck which, with the deck shell and baffles, makes the suction that stands the grass up before the blade cuts it, and carries clippings into the bagger tubes. That same suction draws in sand and dirt, which wear the sail thin. This wear is normal and can't be prevented. A worn sail can't move enough air: the cut is bad or uneven and the bagger doesn't fill. Replace the blade when the sail is thinner than the center of the blade. Fix difficulty: moderate (the same job as replacing the blades).

## Before you start

- When were the blades last replaced, and does the mower cut sandy or dusty ground?
- Make the mower safe before handling the blades; see [Sharpen and replace the blades](../procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades.md).

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower cuts uneven](../problems/uneven-cut.md): without enough suction the grass isn't stood up, so the cut is bad; worn, rounded blade ends also leave strips of uncut grass between blades.
- [Mower doesn't bag grass](../problems/not-bagging-grass.md): worn sails move less air, so grass and leaves aren't pushed into the tubes.
- [Mower is vibrating](../problems/mower-is-vibrating.md): dirt wears the blade unevenly, which can unbalance it.

## How to confirm

Remove and inspect the blade. Check the sail as well as the cutting edges: the sail should be the same thickness as the center of the blade. Worn sails look thinner at the ends than toward the center. Also check the tips: rounded, worn ends mean a new blade.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/big-dog-bad-or-uneven-cut.md), [S40](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-lawn-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/change-and-sharpen-your-husqvarna-lawn-mower-blades.md), [S22](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/how-to-change-sharpen-your-troy-bilt-mower-blades.md), [S12](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-mower-problems.md)

## Fix

Replace the blade when the sail is thinner than the blade center or the tips are rounded; a blade that is good but dull can be sharpened. After sharpening, check the balance: hang the blade by its center hole on a blade balancer or a nail in the wall, and file a little metal off the low side until it sits level. See [Sharpen and replace the blades](../procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades.md).

## Brand notes

- **Simplicity:** replace with new Simplicity blades when the tips are rounded or the sail is thinner than the center.
- **Bagging (riding and zero-turn):** use a high-lift blade, which has a taller sail (fin) for the lift and air movement bagging needs.
- The blade-change articles for Bad Boy, Craftsman, Exmark, Gravely, Honda, Husqvarna, John Deere, Kubota and Troy-Bilt and the cut articles for many brands give the same thinner-than-center rule.

- Mower blade of the right type for the deck; a high-lift blade for bagging.

## Tips & life hacks

- Sail wear is normal from the dirt drawn under the deck; check the sails every time you check or sharpen the blades.
- The bigger bagging picture: keep the blades sharp and in good condition so the system has the air movement it needs.
