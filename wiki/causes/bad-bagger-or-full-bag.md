# Bad bagger or full bag

## Summary

The grass collection system needs air to move through it. Today's bags are a loosely knit mesh of nylon or polyester with vents, so air escapes while the clippings stay in; a bag that doesn't pass air keeps expanding with grass and air until it is damaged or bursts at the seams. A bagger that isn't assembled and secured properly lets air escape, so there is too little air movement and grass builds up. Bags wear out in about two to three years. On some electric mowers a full bag also stops the mower turning on. Fix difficulty: easy.

## How to confirm

Check that every part of the bagger is secured in place with no gaps where air escapes. Look at the bags for damage and check they are the right ones for the system. On an electric mower that won't start, take off the bag, empty it, refit it and try again.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mower-is-not-bagging-grass-solved.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/pick-up-leaves-with-a-mower.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Fix

Reassemble and secure the bagger, and seal any area where air can escape. Replace worn or wrong bags with the correct ones; older cloth bags can sometimes be repaired, but mesh bags usually can't. Empty a full bag.
