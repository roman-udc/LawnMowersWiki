# Bad clutch cable or linkage

## Summary

Mowers with a manual blade-engagement clutch use a lever and a cable instead of a PTO switch and electric clutch. Shifting the lever to engaged pulls the blade clutch cable, which tightens the blade belt so the blades spin. Over time the lever, cable, spring, bushings and linkages wear, and a worn or broken cable or a broken lever stops the clutch from engaging, so the blades won't spin. The fix is to replace the worn parts; the Sources don't rate how hard that is.

## Before you start

- Does the mower have a blade-engagement lever and cable (manual clutch) or a PTO switch (electric clutch)? Most zero-turn mowers use an electric clutch; this Cause applies to the manual kind. For an electric clutch see [Bad PTO switch](bad-pto-switch.md) and [Bad PTO clutch](bad-pto-clutch.md). [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-deck-problems.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/won-t-cut-grass.ridtr01.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-blades-wont-engage.md)
- Is the blade belt OK? Check the belt first; if it's fine, move on to the idler pulley, the blade clutch cable and the clutch lever assembly. [S15](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/won-t-cut-grass.ridtr01.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower is vibrating](../problems/mower-is-vibrating.md): on a push mower, a loose or worn blade engagement lever can make the mower vibrate; on riding and zero-turn mowers, a manual clutch's worn bushings and linkages are named with vibration. ## How to confirm

Examine the blade engagement lever and the blade clutch cable. Check the lever, cable, spring, bushings and linkages for wear. A broken lever, or a cable that is worn or broken, confirms this Cause.

## Fix

Replace the worn parts: the clutch cable, lever, springs, bushings or linkages. Replace a broken engagement lever, and a blade clutch cable that is worn or broken. On a push mower, tighten a loose engagement lever and replace worn parts. No Source gives step-by-step instructions, so there is no Procedure page for it yet.
