# Engine mount loose

## Summary

A running engine always vibrates a bit. The engine mounting bolts under the engine (often called motor mounts) hold it to the frame or deck and soak up much of that vibration. Over time they work loose, wear, break or fall out, and then the engine's shaking passes straight through the mower. Other loose hardware (nuts, bolts, bushings and linkages on the deck and frame) shakes and rattles the same way.

The fix is easy: tighten the loose bolts and replace any that are worn, broken or missing.

## Before you start

- **Safety:** set the parking brake, remove the key and disconnect the spark plug wire(s) before you work on the mower. Wear gloves near the blade.
- Push mower: when does the vibration start? If it gets much worse once the blade is engaged, check the engine bolts.
- Some vibration is normal on a running engine. It gets worse as parts wear.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower is vibrating](../problems/mower-is-vibrating.md): strong vibration that comes from the engine; it can be extreme when the bolts are broken, loose or missing. On a push mower it often shows up once the blade is engaged.

Loose parts also rattle: one Source says bouncing over rough ground works parts loose so they rattle and make the mower louder. That article talks about loose body parts in general, not the engine mount.

- [Mower is noisy](../problems/mower-is-noisy.md): parts worked loose rattle.

Sources for Mower is noisy: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/5-reasons-why-lawn-mowers-are-louder-than-cars.md)

## How to confirm

1. Look at the mounting bolts under the engine. Check that each one is there, tight, and not worn or broken.
2. Check the rest of the mower and the deck for loose or missing hardware. Around the deck pulleys, look for loose hardware as well as debris under a pulley.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ariens-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/bad-boy-lawn-mower-is-vibrating.md), [S23](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/walker-mower-vibrating.md), [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md), [S34](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mower-deck-is-vibrating.md), [S33](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-vibrating.md)

## Fix

1. Tighten loose engine mounting bolts.
2. Replace any bolt that is worn, broken, damaged or missing.
3. Tighten or replace other loose hardware you find.

There is no separate procedure page for this; the steps above are the whole job.

## Parts & fitment

Engine mounting bolts. One Source calls them "special" engine mounting bolts, so replace a missing or worn one with the correct mounting bolt for your mower; match it to the old bolt or your parts list.

Sources: [S28](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-shaking-vibrating.md), [S24](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/wright-mower-vibrates-shakes.md)

## Tips & life hacks

- Check the mower now and then for loose or missing hardware, and tighten it before it shakes something else loose.
- Don't ignore the vibration: leaving it can cause more damage.
