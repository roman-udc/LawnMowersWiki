# Belt guard or keeper missing

## Summary

Belt keepers (belt guides) are small rods or posts beside the pulleys, sitting about 1/8" to 1/4" from the pulley. The belt doesn't touch them; they're an extra precaution that stops it jumping off. When the belt has slack, or snaps, it can hit a keeper and bend it out of place toward the pulleys, and then the belt slips off. A keeper or deck bracket that has moved can also rub the belt: a belt that rubs a bracket, or is routed wrong, shreds along its edges, weakens and can break.

The fix is easy: bend the keeper or bracket back into place, or replace it.

## Before you start

- Does your mower use belt keepers? Some decks do, some don't.
- Is the belt shredded along its edges? Then first confirm it is the right belt and routed correctly (decal on the mower, owner's manual or parts diagram), then look for a bracket it rubs.

## How to confirm

- Check each keeper and bracket: it should sit 1/8" to 1/4" from the pulley and never touch the belt.
- For a shredded belt, look for a shiny, smooth spot on the metal of the deck or a bracket where the belt has been rubbing.

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/this-is-why-your-belt-keeps-falling-off-your-lawn-mower.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-mower-belt-keeps-breaking.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-belt-keeps-coming-off-or-breaking.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/spartan-belt-keeps-coming-off.md), [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-belt-keeps-falling-off-your-riding-mower-fixed.md)

## Fix

- Bend a bent keeper back with a small hammer or vise grips to 1/8" to 1/4" from the pulley. If it can't be returned to its original position, replace it.
- For a rubbing bracket: adjust it so nothing rubs. If the bracket has a bushing, replace the bushing or the whole bracket assembly; replace a bracket that can't be adjusted.
- Then fit a new belt, routed through every keeper and over every pulley: [Replace the deck belt](../procedures/replace-the-deck-belt.md).

## Brand notes

- **John Deere:** some brackets bend back into place; others have a bushing that may only be sold as a bracket assembly; check with the dealer for available parts.
- **Kubota:** parts diagrams often don't show the belt routing; route the belt per the owner's manual, and ask the dealer if the manual is lost.
- **Toro:** on some mowers the guides travel alongside the belt.
- Gravely, SCAG, Snapper, Ferris, Cub Cadet, Husqvarna, Troy-Bilt and lawn tractor articles give the same 1/8" to 1/4" keeper rod and bend-back fix.

## Parts & fitment

- **Belt keeper (belt guide):** a rod or post on the deck; replace one that can't be bent back.
- **Deck bracket or bracket bushing:** some are sold only as a bracket assembly.

## Tips & life hacks

- When you refit a belt, route it through every belt keeper and over every pulley.
- Fix or replace damaged brackets and keepers as part of routine deck care, and check for rubbing on deck components.
