# Bad PTO switch

## Summary

The PTO switch is the control, usually a knob, that turns the blades on. On a mower with an electric PTO clutch, it lets battery voltage through to power the clutch. When the switch fails, no power reaches the clutch and the blades won't turn on. The fix is to replace the switch; the Sources don't rate how hard that is.

## Before you start

- Does the mower have a PTO switch and an electric clutch, or a manual blade-engagement lever and cable? Only mowers that use a PTO switch to activate the blades have this fault; a lever-and-cable mower points to [Bad clutch cable or linkage](bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage.md) instead. [S24](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/won-t-cut-grass.ridtr01.md), [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-blades-wont-engage.md)
- Is the battery charged and the fuse good? The switch only passes battery voltage on, so a weak battery or blown fuse gives the same symptom. [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-blades-wont-engage.md)

Sources: [S24](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/won-t-cut-grass.ridtr01.md), [S18](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/simplicity-mower-blades-wont-engage.md)

## How to confirm

Check the switch for continuity with a multimeter. A break in continuity means the switch is bad.
