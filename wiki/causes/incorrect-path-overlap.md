# Incorrect path overlap

## Summary

Mowing passes spaced too wide, usually to finish with fewer passes, leave thin strips of uncut grass between them. It is an operator habit, not a fault in the mower, and the fix is easy: overlap each pass. Several authors say they made this mistake themselves, and that fixing the strips took longer than mowing right the first time.

## Before you start

- Are the uncut strips between passes, rather than between the blades of one pass?
- Is the mower new to the operator? On a new mower it takes a few mowings to judge how much overlap is needed.

## How to confirm

Strips of uncut grass left between passes after spacing the paths wide (to save time or passes) point to this Cause.

Sources: [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-cuts-uneven.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/hustler-mower-bad-uneven-cut.md), [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-your-riding-mower-is-cutting-uneven.md)

## Fix

Overlap each pass so no strips are left between passes. The manufacturer Source says overlap by about an inch. Don't overdo it either: space the paths so you don't mow the same section twice. There is no repair Procedure for this Cause.

## Tips & life hacks

- On a new mower, experiment with the overlap for a few mowings.
- For ditches and steep changes in grade, a mower with a small deck works better.
