# Safety key missing

## Summary

Most battery mowers need a safety key inserted, or a safety button pressed, before they will start; it stops children starting the mower. A key that is missing, not pushed fully in, or not recognized keeps the mower from starting. The same safety system also blocks starting when a push mower's foldable handle isn't unfolded and locked, or its side rails aren't fully extended with the clamps locked. The fix is easy: seat the key and set the handle; if it still won't start, the switch that reads the key may be bad.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Before you start

- Does your model have a safety key? Not all do; some use only a safety button.
- Is the handle unfolded and locked in the operating position, with any side rails at full length?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Battery mower won't start](../problems/battery-mower-wont-start.md): nothing happens when you press the button and pull the safety bar.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## How to confirm

Check the key is in and fully engaged. Check the handle and side rails: the rails can slip out of position.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Fix

- Push the key in further, or remove and reinsert it.
- Release the side rail clamps, set the handle and rails in the operating position, and lock the clamps.
- If it still won't start, the switch may not be recognizing the safety device: see [Bad safety switch](bad-safety-switch.md). The full starting steps are on [Incorrect starting procedure](incorrect-starting-procedure.md).

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Brand notes

- **DeWalt push mowers:** have switches for the foldable handle, the fully extended side rails, the safety bar and the safety button; the side rails must be at maximum length with the clamps locked.

Sources: [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Parts & fitment

- **Safety key:** model-specific; keep it with the mower and away from children.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
