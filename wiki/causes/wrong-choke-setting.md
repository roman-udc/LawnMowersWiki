# Wrong choke setting

## Summary

The choke is a plate in the carburetor throat that restricts airflow, so a cold engine gets the rich mixture (more fuel, less air) it needs to start. A cold engine won't start with the choke off, and a warm engine won't start with the choke on. A choke plate that is stuck, or a choke cable that is stretched, worn or out of adjustment, gives the same result even when the lever is set right. Fix difficulty: easy.

## Before you start

- Is the engine cold or warm?
- Where is the choke lever or knob set, and does the choke plate actually follow it?
- Does the choke cable move freely, or does it feel stretched or worn?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start-when-cold.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): the choke is off/open on a cold engine or on/closed on a warm one. Or the lever is set right, but the choke plate is stuck or the cable binds.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-cranks-but-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-zero-turn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)

## How to confirm

1. Check the lever: it should be on/closed for a cold engine and off/open for a warm engine.
2. Remove the air filter housing and find the choke plate (valve).
3. Work the choke lever and watch the plate. It should close fully when the choke is engaged and open fully when it isn't.
4. Check that the choke cable moves freely and isn't stretched or worn.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start-when-cold.md)

## Fix

- Set the choke correctly: on/closed to start a cold engine, then off/open once the engine warms up. Leaving it on after warm-up can make the mower shut down.
- Free a stuck choke plate and linkages with carburetor cleaner. A penetrating lubricant also frees up the linkages.
- Adjust the cable so the choke is closed when the lever is engaged and open when it isn't. Replace a stretched, worn or damaged choke cable.

## Brand notes

- **Husqvarna, John Deere, SCAG:** same rule (on/closed for a cold engine, off/open for a warm one). Replace a stretched or worn cable with a new choke cable for the model. Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-zero-turn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)
- **Cub Cadet zero-turn:** use a lubricant on the choke linkages if the cable doesn't move freely. Source: [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-cranks-but-wont-start.md)

## Parts & fitment

- **Choke cable:** replace it when it is stretched, worn or damaged and can't be adjusted to fully open and close the choke. Match the mower model.

## Tips & life hacks

- If the engine only keeps running with the choke on, the choke itself isn't the fault. The engine is getting too much air or too little fuel. Look at the carburetor, fuel filter, fuel line, fuel pump, gas cap and carburetor gasket instead. Source: [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-only-runs-choke-on.md)
- Choke trouble shows up most on cool mornings and in cold weather, when the engine depends on the choke to start. Source: [S7](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-wont-start-when-cold.md)
