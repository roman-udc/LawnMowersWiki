# Air in the fuel system

## Summary

On a diesel mower, air trapped in the fuel system hurts starting and running. Fix difficulty: easy. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Before you start

- Is it a diesel mower? The Source describes this on diesel mowers.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): a diesel mower that is hard to start or runs poorly with air trapped in the fuel system.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## How to confirm

The Source gives no separate test. Bleeding the air out (see Fix) is quick and harmless, so try it.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

## Fix

Start the mower and let it run for a minute to remove the air from the fuel system. No Procedure page covers this yet.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)

No brand-specific information in the Sources yet.

No parts are needed.

## Tips & life hacks

- After clearing or replacing a fuel line on a diesel, run the engine up to a minute to bleed the system. Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/diesel-lawn-mower-wont-start.md)
