# Plugged fuel cap vent

## Summary

The fuel tank has to vent so the pressure inside matches the outside air. On a lawn mower the vent is in the gas cap. If the vent plugs, or the cap breaks, air can't get into the tank as fuel leaves it, and a vacuum forms. The vacuum holds gas in the tank, so the engine turns over but won't start, or starts and then dies once the cap is tight. The fix is easy: clean or replace the cap.

## Before you start

- Does the engine start with the gas cap loosened or removed?
- Is the cap visibly broken or damaged?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S8](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/start-a-lawn-mower-with-old-gas.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): the engine starts once the cap is loosened, then sputters and dies after the cap is tightened.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-zero-turn-cranks-but-wont-start.md)

## How to confirm

1. Loosen (or remove) the gas cap and try to start the engine. If it starts, the cap may be the cause.
2. Let it run and tighten the cap. If it begins to sputter, shuts down, and won't start again until you loosen the cap, the vent is plugged.

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S4](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S6](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/scag-zero-turn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md), [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md)

## Fix

Replace a bad or plugged gas cap with a new one.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** most Sources go straight to replacing the cap (S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, S8). One Source says you can first try cleaning the cap, but that this doesn't always work and you may still need a new one (S9).

## Tips & life hacks

- The cap test is free and quick. Do it before pulling fuel lines or the carburetor. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)
- Let the engine run a while with the cap back on. A plugged vent may take some time to build enough vacuum to stall it. Source: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md)
