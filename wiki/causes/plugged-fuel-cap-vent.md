# Plugged fuel cap vent

## Summary

The fuel tank has to vent so the pressure inside matches the outside air. On most mowers the vent is a small hole in the gas cap; some tanks vent through rollover vents on the tank instead. If the vent plugs, or the cap breaks, air can't get into the tank as fuel leaves it, and a vacuum forms. Think of a travel mug lid with a clogged air hole: the vacuum holds gas in the tank and starves the carburetor. The engine may turn over but not start, or start fine and then sputter and shut down after running a while, and not restart until the cap is loosened. The fix is easy: clean the vent or replace the cap.

## Before you start

- Does the engine start with the gas cap loosened or removed?
- Did the mower run for a while, shut down, and now won't restart? That pattern points to the cap more often than a mower that won't start from cold.
- Is the cap visibly broken or damaged?
- Does your tank vent through the gas cap or through rollover vents on the tank? You need to know which part to replace.

## Tips & life hacks

- The cap test is free and quick. Do it before pulling fuel lines or the carburetor. Source: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-turns-over-but-wont-start.md)
- Let the engine run a while with the cap back on. A plugged vent may take some time to build enough vacuum to stall it. Sources: [S9](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-isnt-getting-gas.md), [S10](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/carburetor-is-not-getting-fuel.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/briggs-and-stratton-engine-isnt-getting-gas.md)
- **Safety:** keep dirt and debris out of the tank while the cap is off. Run the test without the deck engaged so dust and grass stay out. Don't mow with the cap off or leave it off: debris and water get into the tank. Run without it only for the test. Sources: [S41](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/cub-cadet-dies-while-mowing.md), [S65](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-mower-dies-while-mowing.md), [S74](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/john-deere-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S88](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-is-running-rough.md), [S92](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-tractor-wont-start-solved.md), [S94](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/mtd-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S95](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/murray-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S104](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-your-mower-stopped-while-mowing.md), [S105](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/reasons-your-toro-lawn-mower-dies-solved.md), [S110](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-wont-start.md), [S136](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/weed-eater-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S138](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/why-your-craftsman-lawn-mower-dies-how-to-fix.md), [S145](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-exmark-lawn-mower-dies-while-running-solved.md), [S151](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-toro-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S152](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/your-troy-bilt-lawn-mower-dies.md)
