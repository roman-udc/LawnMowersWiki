# Worn drive belt

## Summary

The drive belt carries engine power to the wheels. On a hydrostatic zero-turn it is the pump drive belt: a V-belt off the engine that turns the hydraulic pumps. On a lawn tractor it is the ground (motion) drive belt from the crankshaft to the transaxle pulley. On a self-propelled push mower a belt drives the wheels. As the belt wears, cracks, frays, glazes or stretches, it slips on the pulleys: the mower moves slowly, feels weak, or won't climb hills. If it breaks or falls off, the mower won't move at all.

This page is about the belt that drives the wheels. The deck (blade) belt is covered by [Worn or stretched belt](worn-or-stretched-belt.md).

Replacing the belt is a moderate job.

## Before you start

- **Safety:** turn the mower off, remove the key and the spark plug wire(s), and let the transmission cool before working near the belt.
- Zero-turn or lawn tractor? The zero-turn pump drive belt and the lawn tractor motion drive belt are different belts in different places.
- Did the belt come off rather than wear out? Then check the tensioner pulley, the idler arm spring and the belt guards too, or the new belt will come off again.

## Symptoms it produces

- [Mower won't move](../problems/mower-wont-move.md): a belt that has broken or fallen off gives no movement at all. A worn belt slips, so the mower moves slowly or sluggishly, the hydrostatic drive feels weak (the pump works inefficiently), it slows down or won't drive going uphill, or it won't move until the belt warms up. A self-propelled mower suddenly needs more force to push and is much slower than normal.
- A slipping, loose or misaligned drive belt gives uneven power, so a self-propelled mower can veer to one side (Mower pulls to one side; no page yet).

## How to confirm

1. Check the belt hasn't fallen off and sits correctly and snugly around every pulley (and inside the belt guards on a lawn tractor).
2. Look along the belt for wear: worn or torn edges, cracks, fraying, a shiny glazed look, or stretching.
3. Signs of slipping: a shiny belt with no ridges, lots of slack between pulleys, or more than 1/2" of deflection.

## Fix

- A belt that slipped out of place but is in good shape: reinstall it securely around the pulleys.
- A belt that is worn, frayed, cracked, glazed, stretched or broken: replace it. Several Sources say to replace it at any sign of wear.
- If the belt fell off, check the tensioner pulley ([Worn pulley or idler bearing](worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing.md)) and the spring ([Missing idler arm spring](missing-idler-arm-spring.md)), and on a lawn tractor make sure the belt guards aren't bent or out of place.

No Source gives belt replacement steps for the drive belt; one Source recommends a repair shop for a transmission drive belt.

## Brand notes

- **Husqvarna:** on zero-turns, a worn pump drive belt slips and the mower moves slowly; on lawn tractors, check the motion drive belt is routed correctly, seated and inside the belt guards.
- **Ariens and John Deere zero-turns:** the hydraulic pump is driven by a V-belt off the engine; loose or off the pulley means no movement.
- **Cub Cadet:** a belt that can't grip the pulley won't drive forward or reverse.
- Craftsman, Exmark, Ferris, Hustler, Kubota, Simplicity, Spartan, Toro, Troy-Bilt and Walker articles give the same check-and-replace advice with nothing brand-specific.

## Tips & life hacks

- How long a belt lasts: one Source says replace the ground drive belt yearly; another says a drive belt lasts three to four years under ideal conditions (sooner with heavy use or poor care) and should be replaced within four years even with no other signs.
- A belt that keeps breaking often points to something else wrong, such as too many clippings in the deck or an oil leak.
- Check the tension springs and clean dirt, mud and debris off the pulleys.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** replace the ground drive belt yearly ([S24](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/what-are-the-riding-mower-parts-that-require-replacement-most-often.md), manufacturer) vs a drive belt lasts three to four years ([S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/this-is-how-long-a-mower-drive-belt-should-last.md), expert-blog).
