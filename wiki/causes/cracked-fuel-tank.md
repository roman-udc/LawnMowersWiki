# Cracked fuel tank

## Summary

Most mower fuel tanks today are high-density polyethylene (plastic). They resist fuel well, but their molded seams can separate over time, and they can crack with age or from an impact. Older mowers, and some smaller engines, have metal tanks that rust, and a rust spot can turn into a small hole. Either way gas leaks from the tank itself. The usual fix is to replace the tank; a rust hole in a metal tank can sometimes be patched when no replacement is made any more.

## Before you start

- Is the tank plastic (high-density polyethylene) or metal? A plastic tank leaks at the seams; a metal tank leaks through rust holes.
- For an older mower with a metal tank, check whether a replacement tank is still available.

Sources: [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S13](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/honda-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S20](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/riding-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S32](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/zero-turn-mower-is-leaking-fuel.md)

## Safety

- Gasoline and its fumes are flammable. Drain the tank into a container before you remove or patch it, wipe up spills, and keep sparks and flames away.

## How to confirm

1. Check the outside of the tank for leakage and damage.
2. On a plastic tank, check the molded seams.
3. On an aged metal tank, look for a small rust hole. With the tank empty, a flashlight helps to find cracks and holes.

## Fix

- Plastic tank leaking at a seam, or cracked: replace the tank. This is the best solution.
- Metal tank with a rust hole: replace it if a replacement is available. If the tank is no longer made, seal the hole with a petroleum-resistant epoxy such as TankWeld by J-B Weld.

> **⚠ Conflicting advice:** some Sources offer epoxy patching of a metal tank as an equal option to replacing it ([S25](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas-fixed.md), [S26](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/troy-bilt-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), [S15](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/husqvarna-lawn-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), expert-blog), while another prefers replacing because of the rust and fuel-system contamination, and patches only when the tank is discontinued ([S19](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/push-mower-is-leaking-gas.md), expert-blog).

## Brand notes

- High-density polyethylene tanks: Ariens, Bad Boy (most likely), Ferris zero-turns and walk-behinds, Gravely, Hustler, Kubota, SCAG, Simplicity, Spartan, Exmark, and current John Deere and Snapper.
- Metal tanks: older Snapper, Toro and Craftsman mowers, and some Honda and Troy-Bilt mowers (smaller engines may still use metal). Replacement metal tanks for older Snapper mowers are hard to find.
