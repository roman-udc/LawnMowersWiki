# Bad recoil starter

## Summary

Push mowers and walk-behinds without electric start are started by pulling the rope of a recoil starter. Over time the rope comes off the pulley (it is no longer wound on, or won't retract), or the pulley, spring or clips break. Then pulling the rope won't start the engine. Fix difficulty: easy to moderate (restring, swap a spring, or fit a new recoil assembly).

## Before you start

- Is it a pull-start mower? Riding mowers and push mowers with electric start don't use a recoil to start.
- Is the rope hard to pull or stuck? Check the blade first: the blade is attached to the crankshaft, so a blade jammed with grass or debris stops the starter from turning ([S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-replacement.md)).

## Symptoms it produces

- [Engine cranks but won't start](../problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md): pulling the rope doesn't start the engine. The rope has come unwound or won't retract, or the starter is broken inside.
- A starter rope that is hard to pull or stuck (blade bound by debris, or a bound recoil spring).

## How to confirm

1. Clean old grass from the blade and the underside of the deck so the blade turns freely.
2. If the blade is clear, remove the recoil housing and inspect it: rope off the pulley, broken pulley, loose, bound or missing spring, broken clips.

Sources: [S14](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/lawn-mower-replacement.md)

## Fix

- If the rope has only come off, restring it.
- If the spring is loose, missing or broken, try replacing the spring and restringing.
- If the clips or the pulley are damaged, replace the whole recoil assembly.
- Price the individual parts against a complete recoil assembly first. Replacing the assembly can be cheaper than repairing it.

## Parts & fitment

- **Recoil starter assembly:** match the engine. A complete assembly includes the rope, pulley, spring and clips.
- **Starter rope / recoil spring:** used on their own for a restring or spring replacement.
