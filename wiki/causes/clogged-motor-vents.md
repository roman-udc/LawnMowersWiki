# Clogged motor vents

## Summary

An electric mower's vents let air cool the motor. Grass and debris caught in them block that air, so the motor and battery run hot. Most electric mowers won't turn on when a vent is blocked, and an overheating mower can shut down mid-mowing. This applies to battery and corded electric mowers. The fix is easy: clean the vents carefully, without liquids.

## Before you start

- Turn the mower off and let it cool; on a corded mower, unplug the cord.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mowers-dangers-and-safety-guide.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Battery mower won't start](../problems/battery-mower-wont-start.md): the mower won't turn on with a vent blocked.
- [Engine overheats](../problems/engine-overheats.md): the electric motor overheats and the mower can shut down; it can potentially catch fire.

## How to confirm

Look at the air vents for grass, dirt and debris once the mower has cooled.

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mowers-dangers-and-safety-guide.md)

## Fix

Wipe the vents with a clean, dry cloth. Don't use liquids: they break down the motor's lubrication. If a rag doesn't clear them, use a can of compressed air (or an air compressor) to dislodge the debris. Be very careful not to damage the motor while cleaning.
