# Loose steering linkage

## Summary

A riding mower's steering runs from the steering wheel through the steering shaft and sector gear to the tie rods and front wheels, with bushings, washers and gears along the way. When these parts wear or loosen, the steering wheel has too much play, the wheels don't turn with the wheel, steering slips when turning, or the mower won't steer one way or the other. The fix is to find the worn or damaged part and replace it. Fix difficulty: moderate (finalizer judgement; the Sources give none).

## Before you start

- Is the play in the steering wheel itself, or do the front wheels wobble, point outward or not line up? Wheel and axle trouble is on [Bad front axle](bad-front-axle.md).

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/loose-steering.ridtr09.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Loose steering](../problems/loose-steering.md): too much play in the steering wheel, wheels that don't turn with the wheel, steering that slips when turning.
- [Mower pulls to one side](../problems/mower-pulls-to-one-side.md): worn bushings, washers or gears can stop a riding mower steering one way or the other.
- [Mower is vibrating](../problems/mower-is-vibrating.md): loose linkages and worn bushings add vibration.

## How to confirm

Check the tie rods, steering shaft and sector gear assembly, and the bushings, washers and gears in the steering system, for wear, looseness or damage.

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/loose-steering.ridtr09.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-lawn-mower-problems-fixed.md)

## Fix

- Replace the parts that have failed.
- If the steering is loose or the wheels don't turn with the steering wheel, the tie rod may need replacing.
- If the steering is loose or slips when you turn the wheel, the complete steering kit may need replacing.

Sources: [S4](../../raw/Articles/searspartsdirect.com/loose-steering.ridtr09.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-lawn-mower-problems-fixed.md), [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/common-riding-lawn-mower-problems.md)
