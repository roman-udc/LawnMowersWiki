# Clogged blower tubes or inlet

## Summary

On a riding mower or zero-turn bagger, air carries the clippings from the deck up the blower tubes into the hopper and bags, and leaves through the inlet screen. Old grass, sticks, leaves and dirt can plug the blower tubes, the inlet tube or the inlet screen. The inlet screen is the part most people forget: it sits on the hidden side of the hopper cover and fills with clippings out of sight. Any of these blockages kills the air draft, and the bags won't fill. Fix difficulty: easy.

## How to confirm

1. Remove the blower tubes and look down each one for old grass, sticks, leaves and dirt.
2. Take the bags or catchers out of the hopper and check the inlet tube, a common buildup spot.
3. Check the inlet screen on the hidden side of the hopper cover.

## Fix

1. Scrape the tubes clean inside, then spray the inside with silicone spray before refitting so grass doesn't stick.
2. Clean the inlet tube and refit the bags.
3. Clean the inlet screen by hand, with a wire brush for tough buildup, then spray it with silicone and let it dry before bagging.
