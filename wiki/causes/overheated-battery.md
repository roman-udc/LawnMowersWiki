# Overheated battery

## Summary

Battery mowers have a protection system that shuts the mower off when the battery (or the mower's circuits) gets excessively hot, especially in hot conditions. The mower then won't start until the battery cools. Clogged vents are a common reason the battery and motor get hot. The fix is easy: let it cool, clean the vents, and keep the battery and charger out of extreme heat.

## Before you start

- Has the mower been working hard in hot weather, or sitting in direct sun?

Sources: [S1](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/kobalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md), [S2](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/ego-mower-starts-then-stops.md)

## Symptoms it produces

- [Battery mower won't start](../problems/battery-mower-wont-start.md): the mower shuts down and won't start while the battery is hot.
- [Engine starts then dies](../problems/engine-starts-then-dies.md): the mower runs, then shuts off as part of its safety system when the battery or circuits get excessively hot.

## How to confirm

Feel whether the battery is hot, and check the mower's vents for grass and debris. Some chargers also show a too-hot or too-cold light (see [Bad charger](bad-charger.md)).

Sources: [S3](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/electric-lawn-mower-wont-start.md), [S11](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/toro-electric-mower-wont-start.md), [S5](../../raw/Articles/poweredoutdoors.com/dewalt-electric-mower-wont-start.md)

## Fix

Let the battery and mower cool before restarting, out of direct sunlight. Clean the mower's vents ([Clogged motor vents](clogged-motor-vents.md)). Keep the battery and charger out of extreme heat or cold.
