# Cracked primer bulb

## Summary

Some small mowers have a rubber primer bulb, often on the side of the air filter. Pressing it moves fuel to the carburetor. With age the bulb gets dry and brittle and cracks, so it seeps gas. It can also leak when it is overfilled with fuel from pressing it, or where it connects to the fuel line. Replacing the bulb is relatively easy.

> **Safety:** leaking gasoline is flammable. Replace a seeping bulb before running the mower, and wipe up spilled fuel.

## Before you start

- Does the mower have a primer bulb at all? Mostly smaller (push) mowers do.
- Does it still work when you press it, or does it seep gas?

- [Mower is leaking gas](../problems/mower-is-leaking-gas.md): fuel seeps from the bulb itself, from a cracked bulb, or where the bulb meets the fuel line.

## How to confirm

- Look at the rubber: dry, brittle or starting to crack means replace it.
- Check where the bulb attaches to the fuel hose; a poor fit leaks there.
- Check for gas around the bulb after pressing it; overfilling it can make it leak.
