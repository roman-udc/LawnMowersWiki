# Index

## Problems

- [Engine cranks but won't start](problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md) — engine spins on the starter or recoil but never fires: air, fuel, spark or compression is missing. - [Engine won't crank or turn over](problems/engine-wont-crank.md) — turn the key and the engine doesn't spin: nothing happens, or only a click or hum; usually the starter isn't getting power (battery, cables, ground, fuse, switches, solenoid), then the starter motor. - [Mower cuts uneven](problems/uneven-cut.md) — strips of uncut grass, stair-step ridges, scalped spots or brown, ragged tips: the deck isn't level (tires, deck, shell), the blades or deck parts are worn or damaged, or the mower is run too fast or below full throttle. ## Causes

- [Clogged air filter](causes/clogged-air-filter.md) — air filter plugged with dirt starves the engine of air; light-test, clean or replace (easy).
- [Fouled spark plug](causes/fouled-spark-plug.md) — dirty, wet, burnt or cracked plug gives weak or no spark; inspect, then clean or replace (easy).
- [Old fuel](causes/old-fuel.md) — gas older than about 30 days leaves varnish and absorbs water; drain, refill with fresh stabilized gas (easy).
- [Clogged fuel filter](causes/clogged-fuel-filter.md) — filter not changed yearly plugs and starves the carburetor; replace with the flow arrow toward the carburetor (easy).
- [Clogged fuel line](causes/clogged-fuel-line.md) — kinked or gummed line restricts fuel; flow-test each section, clear or replace the line (easy).
- [Bad fuel pump](causes/bad-fuel-pump.md) — failed vacuum pump gives no steady or pulsing flow; EFI pumps are checked with a pressure gauge (moderate).
- [Dirty carburetor](causes/dirty-carburetor.md) — varnish plugs jets and passages or sticks the float; confirm good fuel flow, then clean, rebuild or replace (moderate).
- [Wrong choke setting](causes/wrong-choke-setting.md) — choke off when cold or on when warm, or a stuck plate or worn cable; set, free or adjust it (easy).
- [Plugged fuel cap vent](causes/plugged-fuel-cap-vent.md) — plugged cap vent holds fuel in the tank; starts with the cap loosened; clean or replace the cap (easy).
- [Low compression](causes/low-compression.md) — valves, rings or cylinder wall leak compression (under about 40 psi); confirm with a gauge or leak-down test, send to a technician (pro).
- [Bad ignition coil](causes/bad-ignition-coil.md) — no spark with a good plug, or a damaged plug wire; replace the coil and set the air gap (moderate).
- [Sheared flywheel key](causes/sheared-flywheel-key.md) — blade strike shears the key and throws off spark timing; replace it with a flywheel puller (moderate).
- [Disconnected fuel solenoid](causes/disconnected-fuel-solenoid.md) — riding mowers: unplugged fuel solenoid on the carburetor stays closed; reconnect the harness (easy).
- [Empty fuel tank](causes/empty-fuel-tank.md) — no or too little gas, a broken fuel gauge or a leak; the most often skipped check; add fresh fuel (easy).
- [Fuel valve off](causes/fuel-valve-off.md) — fuel shut-off valve under the tank left closed after storage or transport; open it (easy).
- [Incorrect starting procedure](causes/incorrect-starting-procedure.md) — safety interlocks and choke need the right starting steps (bail lever, brake, PTO off, seat, key); follow the operator's manual (easy).
- [Bad recoil starter](causes/bad-recoil-starter.md) — pull-start rope off the pulley or won't retract, broken pulley, spring or clips; restring, repair or replace the assembly (moderate).
- [Wet electrical components](causes/wet-electrical-components.md) — rain or damp storage wets or corrodes switches, plug wires and wiring; dry them, replace corroded or chewed wiring (easy).
- [Air in the fuel system](causes/air-in-fuel-system.md) — diesel mowers: trapped air in the fuel system hurts starting and running; bleed it out (easy).
- [Bad glow plugs](causes/bad-glow-plugs.md) — diesel mowers: a glow plug that no longer heats leaves a cold engine unable to start; test and replace (moderate).
- [Weak or dead battery](causes/weak-or-dead-battery.md) — 12-volt battery too weak to power the solenoid and starter: nothing happens or just a click; test for 12.7 V, charge, jump or replace (easy).
- [Loose or corroded battery cables](causes/loose-or-corroded-battery-cables.md) — vibration loosens cables and moisture corrodes terminals, so the starter gets no power; tighten, clean or replace (easy).
- [Bad starter solenoid](causes/bad-starter-solenoid.md) — the switch that feeds the starter fails: click or hum and no cranking, or a hot, smoking wire; check feed and ground, bypass-test, replace (easy to moderate).
- [Bad safety switch](causes/bad-safety-switch.md) — a failed seat, brake, PTO or bail-lever switch won't sense the condition, so the mower won't crank or shuts off; multimeter-test and replace, never bypass (easy to moderate).
- [Bad starter motor](causes/bad-starter-motor.md) — solenoid clicks but the starter doesn't run, or it spins without turning the engine; suspect it last, then replace or rebuild (moderate).
- [Bad ignition switch](causes/bad-ignition-switch.md) — key turned and nothing happens, or the engine won't stop; multimeter-test the terminals and replace (easy).
- [Bad wiring or ground](causes/bad-wiring-or-ground.md) — loose, corroded, chewed or shorted wires and bad grounds cut power to the solenoid and starter or blow fuses; clean, repair or replace (easy to moderate).
- [Blown fuse](causes/blown-fuse.md) — a short or overload blows the fuse and cuts power to the ignition circuit, blade clutch or charging; replace it and find the short if it blows again (easy).
- [Faulty charging system](causes/faulty-charging-system.md) — stator, alternator or regulator stops recharging the battery, so it keeps going flat; test output, then have a mechanic find the fault (pro).
- [Hydrolocked engine](causes/hydrolocked-engine.md) — too much oil fills the cylinder so the engine won't turn with the rope or starter; stop cranking, drain to the full mark (moderate).
- [Seized engine](causes/seized-engine.md) — a locked-up engine can't be turned by the starter; the key gives only a click; send to a technician (pro).
- [Dirty cooling fins](causes/dirty-cooling-fins.md) — fins packed with dirt, oil or grass, or broken, so the engine overheats and shuts down; clean them (easy).
- [Dirty fuel tank](causes/dirty-fuel-tank.md) — debris, rust or old gas inside a metal tank after long storage; drain and clean the tank (easy).
- [Overfilled oil](causes/overfilled-oil.md) — oil above the full mark raises crankcase pressure, smokes, fouls the plug or hydrolocks the engine; drain the excess (easy).
- [Wrong fuel](causes/wrong-fuel.md) — gas with too much ethanol, low octane, diesel, or straight gas in a 2-cycle engine; drain and refill with the right fuel (easy).
- [Wrong oil or low oil](causes/wrong-oil-or-low-oil.md) — too little oil or the wrong grade causes friction and heat until the engine shuts down; correct the oil (easy if caught early).
- [Dull or bent blade](causes/dull-or-bent-blade.md) — dull, worn, chipped or bent blade tears grass (brown tips) and leaves strips; tip-to-ground check over 1/8 inch means replace; sharpen or replace, never straighten (moderate).
- [Mowing too fast](causes/mowing-too-fast.md) — ground speed too high for thick, tall or wet grass pushes it over and leaves alternating stripes; slow down (easy).
- [Wrong tire pressure](causes/wrong-tire-pressure.md) — low or uneven tire pressure tilts the deck and gives a stair-step cut; check first, set to spec and equal left and right (easy).
- [Wrong engine speed](causes/wrong-engine-speed.md) — engine not at full throttle, so the blades spin too slowly to cut cleanly; mow at full throttle (easy).
- [Unlevel deck](causes/unlevel-deck.md) — deck out of level side to side or front to back gives ridges and scalping; measure the four corners and level it (moderate).
- [Blade installed upside down](causes/blade-installed-upside-down.md) — blade sails pointing down instead of up toward the deck; reinstall with the correct hardware (moderate).
- [Bent deck or deck shell](causes/bent-deck-or-deck-shell.md) — damaged or bent deck shell gives an uneven cut; straighten if possible, else replace the shell or deck (pro).
- [Incorrect path overlap](causes/incorrect-path-overlap.md) — passes that don't overlap leave strips of uncut grass; overlap each pass (easy).
- [Debris on deck or pulleys](causes/debris-on-deck-or-pulleys.md) — grass packed under the deck or around pulleys blocks airflow and loads the engine; scrape and clean the deck (easy).
- [Wrong cutting height](causes/wrong-cutting-height.md) — wheels or deck set to different or too-low heights; set every height adjuster the same (easy).
- [Bad tire or wheel](causes/bad-tire.md) — damaged, loose, flat or mismatched wheel stops the deck sitting level; repair or replace it (easy to moderate).
- [Bad blade adapter or loose blade](causes/bad-blade-adapter-or-loose-blade.md) — loose blade, worn adapter, or wrong-size or aftermarket blade; tighten or replace with the correct blade (moderate).
- [Bad clutch cable or linkage](causes/bad-clutch-cable-or-linkage.md) — worn or broken blade clutch cable, lever, spring, bushings or linkage on a manual-engagement mower; blades won't engage; replace the worn parts.
- [Bad PTO clutch](causes/bad-pto-clutch.md) — failing electric blade clutch (bearing) or worn manual clutch bushings and linkage; vibration, blades won't engage; replace the clutch.
- [Bad PTO switch](causes/bad-pto-switch.md) — PTO knob no longer passes battery voltage to the electric clutch; blades won't turn on; check continuity and replace.
- [Bent crankshaft](causes/bent-crankshaft.md) — push mower blade strike bends the crankshaft; vibration and bad cut; engine teardown or new mower (pro).
- [Bent spindle or bad spindle bearing](causes/bent-spindle-or-bad-spindle-bearing.md) — blade rocks or knocks; vibration and step cut; replace the bearing or spindle housing (moderate).
- [Debris wrapped around blade spindle](causes/debris-wrapped-around-blade-spindle.md) — string, wire or weeds wound on the blade shaft shake the deck; remove it and check the spindle (easy).
- [Engine mount loose](causes/engine-mount-loose.md) — loose, worn or missing engine mounting bolts and other hardware; strong vibration; tighten or replace (easy).
- [Worn or stretched belt](causes/worn-or-stretched-belt.md) — cracked, glazed, stretched or loose deck belt; vibration, slipping blades, bad cut; replace the belt.
- [Worn pulley or idler bearing](causes/worn-pulley-or-idler-bearing.md) — deck or idler pulley restricted, noisy or wobbling; vibration, bad cut, belt trouble; replace the pulley.
- [Worn drive belt](causes/worn-drive-belt.md) — pump or ground drive belt worn, slipping or thrown: mower slow, weak or won't move; reseat or replace (moderate).
- [Bypass valve open](causes/bypass-valve-open.md) — drive release (bypass) levers or transaxle freewheel left open after pushing: mower free-rolls but won't drive; return levers to operating (easy).
- [Low hydraulic oil](causes/low-hydraulic-oil.md) — hydraulic oil low or old: mower slow, weak or won't move; top up or change the oil (easy to moderate).
- [Missing idler arm spring](causes/missing-idler-arm-spring.md) — idler spring broken, stretched or gone: drive belt slack, mower won't move; deck bracket wear causes vibration; replace (easy).
- [Hot hydraulic fluid](causes/hot-hydraulic-fluid.md) — runs fine cold, weak or stops when hot: let it cool, then dealer check for transmission damage (pro).
- [Air in hydraulic system](causes/air-in-hydraulic-system.md) — air after an oil change or rough-ground driving: mower won't reach normal speed; purge the system (easy).
- [Missing axle key](causes/missing-axle-key.md) — lawn tractor key fell out after a rear tire change: wheel won't turn; refit the key (easy).
- [Bad hydrostatic transmission](causes/bad-hydrostatic-transmission.md) — failed or leaking pump or wheel motor: weak, one side slow; dealer replaces (pro).
- [Bad head gasket](causes/bad-head-gasket.md) — a failed engine gasket (Sources rarely say which) leaks oil onto the hot muffler, which smokes; a bad head gasket also costs compression and power (moderate to pro).
- [Bad valve train](causes/bad-valve-train.md) — a valve burnt by overheating; red-hot muffler, oil on the plug; confirmed by head removal or leak-down test, mechanic cuts valve and seat (pro).
- [Oil in air filter or tipped mower](causes/oil-in-air-filter-or-tipped-mower.md) — push mower tipped or used on a slope runs oil into the air filter and carburetor; replace filter, clean carburetor (easy).
- [Rich fuel mixture](causes/rich-fuel-mixture.md) — more fuel than air gives black smoke; clear the air filter or other air restriction, clean a rich carburetor (easy).
- [Spilled oil on engine](causes/spilled-oil-on-engine.md) — oil dripped on the engine or muffler during an oil change burns off as smoke; wipe it off (easy).
- [Worn piston rings or valve guides](causes/worn-piston-rings-or-valve-guides.md) — bad rings or a scored cylinder let oil burn in the combustion chamber; oil on the plug; engine teardown or replacement (pro).
- [Bad gas cap seal](causes/bad-gas-cap-seal.md) — dried or failed seal lets gas splash out around the cap; often only a smell; replace the cap or its seal (easy).
- [Cracked fuel tank](causes/cracked-fuel-tank.md) — poly tank leaks at the seams or metal tank rusts through; replace the tank, patch a rust hole only if needed (moderate).
- [Cracked fuel line](causes/cracked-fuel-line.md) — dry, cracked, kinked or punctured rubber fuel line leaks; replace the line and clamps (easy).
- [Bad shutoff valve](causes/bad-shutoff-valve.md) — metal or plastic fuel shut-off valve leaks with age; replace it (easy).
- [Bad carburetor gasket or needle and seat](causes/bad-carburetor-gasket-or-needle-and-seat.md) — dry bowl gasket, or stuck float or float needle overflows fuel; replace the gasket, clean or rebuild the carburetor (moderate).
- [Cracked fuel filter](causes/cracked-fuel-filter.md) — old fuel softens the plastic filter housing until it cracks or leaks; replace the filter (easy).
- [Cracked primer bulb](causes/cracked-primer-bulb.md) — cracked rubber primer bulb or loose hose behind it leaks gas; replace the bulb (easy).
- [Overfilled fuel tank](causes/overfilled-fuel-tank.md) — tank filled to the brim spills or vents as fuel expands in heat; fill only to the bottom of the fill neck (easy).
- [Bad governor](causes/bad-governor.md) — misadjusted or faulty governor can't raise the throttle fast enough when the blades engage, so the engine bogs; no repair steps in the Sources (not rated).
- [Cutting wet or tall grass](causes/cutting-wet-or-tall-grass.md) — wet, long or thick grass overloads the engine and clumps under the deck; mow dry, mow often, double-cut tall grass, slow down (easy).
- [Water in fuel](causes/water-in-fuel.md) — rain through the cap vent or ethanol separating in storage puts water at the bottom of the tank; sputters, runs rough or won't start; drain, replace the filter, refill with fresh gas and additive (easy).
- [Bad cooling shroud](causes/bad-cooling-shroud.md) — engine guard (shield) loose, out of place or missing, or debris under the shroud, so cooling air escapes; reposition, tighten or replace the guard, clean under the shroud (easy).
- [Bad engine cooling fan](causes/bad-engine-cooling-fan.md) — riding mower fan broken or clogged can't draw air over the engine; clear debris, replace a cracked or stuck fan (not rated).
- [Bad drive control linkage or damper](causes/bad-drive-control-linkage-or-damper.md) — worn or loose zero-turn steering damper, tracking out of adjustment, stretched self-propelled drive cable or mis-adjusted neutral: mower jerks or pulls to one side, or creeps (easy to moderate).
- [Brake hung up](causes/brake-hung-up.md) — brake dragging on one drive wheel (pulls to one side) or parking brake left on (won't move); spin each raised wheel with the hydros bypassed.
- [Loose or seized wheel bearing](causes/loose-or-seized-wheel-bearing.md) — worn or seized wheel bearing or binding axle makes a walk-behind drag to one side; replace the wheel.
- [Unbalanced load](causes/unbalanced-load.md) — trailer, grass catcher or attachment pulls the mower to one side; center the load or remove it (easy).
- [Bad belt tensioner spring](causes/bad-belt-tensioner-spring.md) — deck tensioner spring stretched, broken or missing, or idler bracket hole or bushing worn: belt loosens and vibrates off, deck shakes; replace spring or bracket, grease bushing (easy).
- [Belt guard or keeper missing](causes/belt-guard-or-keeper-missing.md) — belt keeper rod bent toward the pulley, or a bracket the belt rubs: belt slips off or shreds; bend back to 1/8"-1/4" or replace (easy).
- [Oil or rust on belt](causes/oil-or-rust-on-belt.md) — oil leak makes the belt slip and swell; rust dries and cracks it; fix the leak or rust, then a new belt (moderate).
- [Wrong belt](causes/wrong-belt.md) — aftermarket or non-matching belt wears fast, shreds or squeals; fit the correct (OEM) belt by part number (easy).
- [Bad alternator](causes/bad-alternator.md) — alternator (stator coils under the flywheel) doesn't recharge the battery: charges on a charger but drains while running; under 13-14 V running; replace, usually by a mechanic (pro).
- [Parasitic battery drain](causes/parasitic-battery-drain.md) — key left on with a dash oil light or a 12-volt accessory plugged in flattens the battery; charge it and switch off (easy).
- [Bad battery](causes/bad-battery.md) — battery mower pack failed (old, extreme temperatures, mechanical): charge doesn't last, charges slowly, gets very hot, or wrong size; replace with the compatible pack (easy).
- [Bad charger](causes/bad-charger.md) — battery mower charger not plugged in fully, wrong outlet or plug, pack not on the contacts, or damaged charger or cord; check the charger lights (easy).
- [Bad motor](causes/bad-motor.md) — electric mower motor or start switch damaged, or bent motor shaft: won't start, stops, or blade wobbles; repair shop or service center (pro).
- [Battery not seated](causes/battery-not-seated.md) — battery mower pack not latched or contacts dirty: clean and slide it in until it clicks (easy).
- [Clogged motor vents](causes/clogged-motor-vents.md) — grass in an electric mower's motor vents: overheats, won't turn on; wipe dry or use compressed air, no liquids (easy).
- [Overheated battery](causes/overheated-battery.md) — battery mower shuts down and won't restart while the battery is hot; let it cool, clean the vents (easy).
- [Safety key missing](causes/safety-key-missing.md) — battery mower safety key out or not fully in, or handle and side rails not locked: mower won't start (easy).
- [Clogged muffler or spark arrestor](causes/clogged-muffler-or-spark-arrestor.md) — the Sources describe a cracked, broken or loose muffler (baffles rattling inside), not a clogged one: loud exhaust or metal rattle; repair or replace the muffler (easy).

## Procedures

- [Replace the spark plug](procedures/replace-the-spark-plug.md) — remove the old plug, gap the new one to spec, tighten firmly and reconnect the wire (easy, 15 minutes or less).
- [Clean or replace the air filter](procedures/clean-or-replace-the-air-filter.md) — light-test a paper filter, wash and dry a foam one, replace when dirty, torn or brittle (easy, 15 minutes or less).
- [Clean the carburetor](procedures/clean-the-carburetor.md) — remove the carburetor, clean the bowl, jets and passages with carburetor cleaner, reinstall (moderate, time not stated).
- [Rebuild the carburetor](procedures/rebuild-the-carburetor.md) — fit a rebuild kit: new gaskets, needle and seat, bowl gasket (moderate, 60 minutes or less).
- [Replace the carburetor](procedures/replace-the-carburetor.md) — swap in a new carburetor matched to the engine model and spec number (moderate, 45 minutes or less).
- [Replace the flywheel key](procedures/replace-the-flywheel-key.md) — pull the flywheel with a puller, fit a new key, torque the flywheel nut (moderate, 60 minutes or less).
- [Replace the fuel filter](procedures/replace-the-fuel-filter.md) — clamp the line, swap the filter with the flow arrow toward the carburetor (easy, 15 minutes or less).
- [Replace the fuel line](procedures/replace-the-fuel-line.md) — clamp off fuel, swap the line and clamps, check for leaks (easy, time not stated).
- [Replace the ignition coil](procedures/replace-the-ignition-coil.md) — swap the coil and set the air gap to the flywheel (moderate, 30 minutes or less).
- [Start a mower with old gas](procedures/start-a-mower-with-old-gas.md) — drain old gas, refill fresh with a stabilizer, run it through the system (easy, time not stated).
- [Start a zero-turn mower](procedures/start-a-zero-turn-mower.md) — the starting steps: seat, brake, PTO off, levers out, choke, key (easy, a few minutes).
- [Test the ignition coil](procedures/test-the-ignition-coil.md) — spark-test with a tester, then check the coil (easy, time not stated).
- [Tune up the engine](procedures/tune-up.md) — yearly service: oil and oil filter, air filter, spark plug, fuel filter (easy, about 2 hours dealer labor).
- [Test the battery](procedures/test-the-battery.md) — read the battery with a multimeter: 12.7 V is full, under that charge it (easy, time not stated).
- [Charge the battery](procedures/charge-the-battery.md) — slow-charge a mower battery at low amps, or trickle-charge it (easy, 30 minutes to 2 hours, up to 24 hours on a trickle charger).
- [Jump-start the battery](procedures/jump-start-the-battery.md) — jump a 12-volt mower battery from a car battery with the car engine off, then recharge (easy, time not stated).
- [Test the starter solenoid](procedures/test-the-starter-solenoid.md) — with a charged battery, bypass-test and voltage-test the solenoid (moderate, time not stated).
- [Replace the starter solenoid](procedures/replace-the-starter-solenoid.md) — disconnect the battery, move the wires to a new solenoid, remount (moderate, 30 minutes or less).
- [Replace the starter motor](procedures/replace-the-starter-motor.md) — remove the blower housing and swap the starter on a riding mower (moderate, 30 minutes or less).
- [Replace the ignition switch](procedures/replace-the-ignition-switch.md) — unplug the harness, release the tabs and swap the key switch (easy, 15 minutes or less).
- [Reset the ignition code](procedures/reset-the-ignition-code.md) — change or reset the code on a keyless Smart Switch ignition (easy, time not stated).
- [Replace the seat switch](procedures/replace-the-seat-switch.md) — test the seat switch with a multimeter and swap it under the seat (easy, 15 minutes or less).
- [Test and replace a fuse](procedures/test-and-replace-a-fuse.md) — check a fuse by eye and with a meter, replace it with the same amperage, trace the short if it blows again (easy, time not stated).
- [Read a wiring schematic](procedures/read-a-wiring-schematic.md) — find and follow your model's wiring diagram to trace the starting circuit (moderate, time not stated).
- [Change the engine oil](procedures/change-the-engine-oil.md) — drain the old oil, replace the filter if fitted, refill to the full mark with the right grade (easy).
- [Clean the cooling fins](procedures/clean-the-cooling-fins.md) — remove the engine shroud and clear dirt and grass from the fins (easy).
- [Clean the gas tank](procedures/clean-the-gas-tank.md) — drain, remove and clean a dirty or rusty metal gas tank (easy).
- [Drain excess oil](procedures/drain-excess-oil.md) — remove oil down to the full mark with an evacuator, baster or the drain plug (easy).
- [Level the deck](procedures/level-the-deck.md) — set tire pressures, measure the deck at four corners and adjust side to side and front to back per the manual (moderate).
- [Measure a mower blade](procedures/measure-a-mower-blade.md) — check a blade for a bend (tip-to-ground, over 1/8 inch means replace) and measure its diagonal length and center hole to order the right replacement (easy).
- [Repair a flat tire](procedures/repair-a-flat-tire.md) — find a tread leak, inject tire sealant through the valve stem and reinflate; sidewall damage means replace the tire (easy).
- [Replace a tire](procedures/replace-a-tire.md) — raise the mower, remove the wheel, break the bead and fit a new tire on the rim, then reinstall (moderate).
- [Change the hydraulic oil](procedures/change-the-hydraulic-oil.md) — drain the hydraulic oil, replace filters where fitted, refill and purge air (moderate, time not stated).
- [Purge the hydraulic system](procedures/purge-the-hydraulic-system.md) — bleed air from the hydrostatic system by cycling the drive with the bypass open, then closed, and recheck the level (easy, time not stated).
- [Sharpen and replace the blades](procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades.md) — remove the blades safely, sharpen and balance or replace them, reinstall sails up and torque the bolt (moderate).
- [Replace the idler pulley](procedures/replace-the-idler-pulley.md) — release belt tension, unbolt the worn idler or deck pulley, fit the new one and check tension.
- [Replace the spindle assembly](procedures/replace-the-spindle-assembly.md) — remove the deck belt, blade and spindle housing, fit the new assembly and torque it (moderate).
- [Test the PTO clutch](procedures/test-the-pto-clutch.md) — check an electric blade clutch with a multimeter.
- [Check compression](procedures/check-compression.md) — gauge in the plug hole, spin the engine with the starter; over 40 psi expected, under 40 is a compression problem; leak-down test for ACR engines (moderate, time not stated).
- [Adjust the tracking](procedures/adjust-the-tracking.md) — zero-turn or stand-on: adjust the speed adjustment bolt so both drive tires turn at the same speed (moderate, time not stated).
- [Adjust the neutral control](procedures/adjust-the-neutral-control.md) — stop a zero-turn or automatic lawn tractor creeping in neutral by adjusting the neutral control or motion control lever (moderate, time not stated).
- [Replace the deck belt](procedures/replace-the-deck-belt.md) — push mower drive belt from under the deck; riding mower deck removal, routing and refit; zero-turn belt covers and self-tensioning spring (moderate, about 30-45 minutes push, about an hour riding).
