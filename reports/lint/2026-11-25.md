# Lint report 2026-11-25: superseded-claims date pass

Scope: ticket 40. A targeted pass, not a full lint. It covers every `⚠ Conflicting advice` callout in `wiki/` (65 callouts on 47 pages) and the six highest-Source Problems (engine-cranks-but-wont-start, engine-wont-crank, engine-starts-then-dies, uneven-cut, mower-is-vibrating, mower-is-smoking).

Method: for each callout, read the `published` and `modified` dates of every Source it cites from its raw frontmatter, and the Trust tier (searspartsdirect.com = manufacturer, poweredoutdoors.com = expert-blog). On the top Problems, scan dated or numeric claims (ages, intervals, voltages, measurements) and trace them to the callouts that cover the same advice. Per the lint rules, a newer Source of a lower tier doesn't win automatically. Pages are not edited; the call is the maintainer's.

Dating caveats: every expert-blog Source was published 2021-05 to 2023-06, and nearly all were modified 2023-05 to 2023-06 (a site-wide refresh), so dates rarely separate expert-blog Sources. Manufacturer Sources range from 2013 to 2026. Seven of them have `published: null`. Those are marked "undated" and not used to rank.

## Superseded claims (needs review)

Strongest first.

1. **Blade replacement interval.** [causes/dull-or-bent-blade.md](../../wiki/causes/dull-or-bent-blade.md) (callout at line 127) and [procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades.md](../../wiki/procedures/sharpen-and-replace-the-blades.md) (line 34). "Replace the blades every season" rests on older manufacturer Sources: why-does-my-lawn-tractor-cut-unevenly (S122, 2019-06-01) on the Cause and S4 (2022-06-01) on the Procedure. A newer manufacturer Source, how-to-measure-a-mower-blade (S3, 2025-04-11), says most blades need replacing every 1–3 seasons. Same tier, newer: the yearly rule looks superseded. **Proposed fix:** lead with the 1–3 seasons figure, keep the yearly rule as the older manufacturer advice, and add the dates to the callout. The Cause's callout should also cite the 2025 Source.

2. **Paper air filter: clean or replace.** [causes/clogged-air-filter.md](../../wiki/causes/clogged-air-filter.md) (line 107). "Replace it if it is dirty" comes from a 2017-05-01 manufacturer video (S7). The newer manufacturer article how-to-clean-the-air-filter-in-your-lawn-tractor (S9, 2025-04-02) says tap it out and replace it only if it is still dirty, which agrees with the expert-blog side. **Proposed fix:** note that the newer manufacturer Source moved to tap-then-replace, and narrow the conflict to the 2017 video alone.

3. **Ignition coil difficulty.** [procedures/replace-the-ignition-coil.md](../../wiki/procedures/replace-the-ignition-coil.md) (line 71). The difficulty-3 rating is from a 2015-01-01 manufacturer guide (S2). A 2023-02-28 manufacturer article (S1) calls the job easy. Both are manufacturer, and the newer one is easier. The page's "moderate" is a fair middle, but the dates aren't stated. **Proposed fix:** add the dates to the callout. Keep "moderate" unless the maintainer prefers the newer "easy".

4. **How fast gas goes bad.** [causes/old-fuel.md](../../wiki/causes/old-fuel.md) (line 33). "About 30 days" is backed by the expert-blog Sources and one manufacturer Source, how-to-keep-lawn-mower-gas-from-going-bad (S10, 2021-03-25). The newer manufacturer Source why-your-mower-loses-power-when-the-blades-are-engaged (S195, 2025-07-21) says gas "more than a month or two old" may be the problem. The "over 6 months" Source (S194) is undated. This is a loosening, not a reversal, and S195 is a passing remark in a power-loss article. Treat it as weak. The 30-day figure is repeated on problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md (line 108) and problems/mower-is-smoking.md (line 116). **Proposed fix:** leave the 30-day lead and add S195's date to the callout. No change on the Problems.

5. **Starter fluid vs carburetor cleaner.** [causes/dirty-carburetor.md](../../wiki/causes/dirty-carburetor.md) (line 90), [problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md](../../wiki/problems/engine-cranks-but-wont-start.md) (line 118), [procedures/start-a-mower-with-old-gas.md](../../wiki/procedures/start-a-mower-with-old-gas.md) (line 75). The only starter-fluid Source is a 2017-05-01 manufacturer video (S7). Every carburetor-cleaner Source is a newer expert blog (2022–2023), and no newer manufacturer Source repeats the starter-fluid advice. Newer but lower tier, so this is not superseded by the rules. **Proposed fix:** add the dates to the callouts ("2017 manufacturer video vs 2022–2023 expert blogs") and leave the call to the maintainer.

6. **Battery "charged" threshold.** [causes/weak-or-dead-battery.md](../../wiki/causes/weak-or-dead-battery.md) (line 73) and [procedures/test-the-battery.md](../../wiki/procedures/test-the-battery.md) (line 63). "More than 12 volts is good" is from 2017-04-01 manufacturer Sources. The 12.7 V figure is from 2021–2023 expert blogs and is what problems/engine-wont-crank.md uses (lines 79, 92, 109). Newer but lower tier, and the two thresholds answer different questions (good vs fully charged). **Proposed fix:** none needed beyond optional dates in the callouts.
